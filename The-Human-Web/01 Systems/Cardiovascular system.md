# Cardiovascular system

## Core role

The heart and vessels deliver oxygen, nutrients, hormones, immune cells, and heat while removing carbon dioxide and metabolic products. The system depends on pump function, rhythm, vascular tone, blood volume, blood composition, kidney regulation, autonomic control, and tissue demand.

## Functional modules

- **Pump function:** the heart fills and ejects blood to pulmonary and systemic circulation.
- **Electrical conduction:** rhythm and rate coordinate contraction and respond to autonomic, electrolyte, thyroid, medication, and stimulant inputs.
- **Vascular tone and pressure:** arteries, veins, capillaries, kidneys, hormones, and autonomic reflexes regulate flow and blood pressure.
- **Microcirculation:** capillary exchange delivers oxygen and nutrients and removes waste at tissue level.
- **Blood composition:** hemoglobin, plasma volume, clotting factors, immune cells, glucose, and electrolytes shape cardiovascular load and delivery.
- **Demand matching:** exertion, fever, stress, anemia, thyroid changes, dehydration, and inflammation can alter heart rate and circulation needs.

## System cross-talk

- The [[01 Systems/Respiratory system|Respiratory system]] loads blood with oxygen and removes carbon dioxide.
- The [[01 Systems/Hematologic system|Hematologic system]] determines oxygen-carrying capacity and clotting context.
- The [[01 Systems/Renal system|Renal system]] regulates fluid volume, pressure, electrolytes, and erythropoietin signaling.
- The [[01 Systems/Endocrine system|Endocrine system]] affects heart rate, vascular tone, glucose, thyroid state, and adrenal response.
- The [[01 Systems/Nervous system|Nervous system]] regulates rate, pressure, and vascular tone through autonomic pathways.

## What strain can look like

- Palpitations, fatigue, dizziness, exercise intolerance, cold intolerance, shortness of breath, chest discomfort, swelling, fainting, or changes in pulse and blood pressure.
- Symptoms that vary with position, exertion, meals, dehydration, fever, anemia, caffeine, stimulant exposure, thyroid status, medication, or stress.

## Discriminating questions

- Is the main issue rhythm, pump function, vascular tone, blood volume, blood oxygen-carrying capacity, or tissue demand?
- Are symptoms exertional, positional, episodic, meal-linked, medication-linked, stimulant-linked, or associated with infection or fever?
- Are there red flags: chest pain, fainting, severe shortness of breath, stroke signs, new severe weakness, or rapid deterioration?
- What evidence would distinguish anxiety/autonomic arousal, anemia, thyroid change, dysglycemia, dehydration, medication effect, or primary heart disease?

> [!danger] Cardiovascular red flags
> Chest pain, severe shortness of breath, fainting, palpitations with weakness, signs of stroke, or rapidly worsening symptoms require urgent assessment.

> [!evidence] Boundary note
> A body system is a navigation layer. Symptoms usually arise from interactions among systems, exposures, life stage, medications, and context.
