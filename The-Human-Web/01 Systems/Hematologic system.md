# Hematologic system

## Core role

Blood formation, oxygen carriage, clotting, immune-cell transport, plasma transport, and repair signaling connect nutrition, marrow, kidneys, inflammation, circulation, and tissue oxygen demand.

## Functional modules

- **Red blood cell production:** marrow production depends on iron, B12, folate, kidney erythropoietin signaling, inflammation, and marrow health.
- **Oxygen transport:** hemoglobin carries oxygen from lungs to tissues and returns carbon dioxide partly through blood chemistry.
- **White blood cell function:** immune cells circulate, respond to infection or inflammation, and return information from tissues.
- **Platelets and clotting:** clot formation and bleeding control depend on platelets, clotting proteins, liver function, medications, and vessel integrity.
- **Plasma transport:** blood carries hormones, nutrients, proteins, immune mediators, waste products, and medications.
- **Measurement surface:** CBC, ferritin, B12, folate, inflammatory markers, kidney markers, and coagulation tests can reflect several systems at once.

## System cross-talk

- The [[01 Systems/Renal system|Renal system]] influences red blood cell production, fluid balance, and electrolyte context.
- The [[01 Systems/Hepatic and biliary system|Hepatic and biliary system]] contributes clotting proteins, plasma proteins, bile-dependent nutrient absorption, and metabolism.
- The [[01 Systems/Digestive system|Digestive system]] affects iron, B12, folate, and blood-loss evaluation.
- The [[01 Systems/Respiratory system|Respiratory system]] loads blood with oxygen.
- The [[01 Systems/Immune system|Immune system]] shapes white blood cell patterns and inflammation-related anemia.

## What strain can look like

- Fatigue, palpitations, cold intolerance, dizziness, shortness of breath on exertion, unusual bruising or bleeding, infection susceptibility, pallor, or nonspecific cognitive strain.
- Lab patterns involving hemoglobin, indices, white blood cells, platelets, ferritin, B12, folate, inflammation, kidney function, or liver function.

## Discriminating questions

- Is the issue red cell production, blood loss, hemolysis, oxygen loading, inflammation, marrow production, clotting, or immune-cell activity?
- Do symptoms line up with bleeding, diet, absorption, menstrual history, kidney disease, inflammation, infection, medication, pregnancy, or recent illness?
- Is ferritin low, high with inflammation, or uninterpretable without context?
- What evidence would separate iron deficiency, B12/folate deficiency, inflammation, kidney-related anemia, medication effects, or marrow disease?

> [!danger] Hematologic red flags
> Uncontrolled bleeding, black or bloody stool with weakness, fainting, chest pain, severe shortness of breath, or rapidly worsening symptoms require urgent assessment.

> [!evidence] Boundary note
> A body system is a navigation layer. Symptoms usually arise from interactions among systems, exposures, life stage, medications, and context.
