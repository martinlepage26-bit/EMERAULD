# Reproductive system

## Core role

Reproductive function reflects coordinated endocrine signalling, gonadal function, pituitary and adrenal inputs, energy availability, vascular health, inflammation, stress physiology, medication exposures, and life-stage context.

## Functional modules

- **Hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal signaling:** brain and pituitary signals coordinate ovarian or testicular hormone production and gamete development.
- **Gonadal hormone production:** ovaries and testes produce hormones that affect reproduction, bone, mood, skin, hair, energy, and metabolism.
- **Cycle and ovulation context:** menstrual cycle patterns can reflect reproductive, endocrine, metabolic, thyroid, stress, pregnancy, and medication factors.
- **Sperm production context:** sperm production depends on testicular function, hormones, temperature, illness, medications, toxins, and time.
- **Metabolic and adipose signaling:** insulin resistance, energy deficit, weight change, and adipose signaling can affect reproductive axes.
- **Life-stage transitions:** puberty, pregnancy, postpartum state, perimenopause, menopause, aging, and gender-affirming hormones change interpretation.

## System cross-talk

- The [[01 Systems/Endocrine system|Endocrine system]] coordinates reproductive hormones, thyroid, adrenal, pituitary, glucose, and adipose signaling.
- The [[01 Systems/Cardiovascular system|Cardiovascular system]] supports tissue perfusion and pregnancy-related hemodynamic change.
- The [[01 Systems/Immune system|Immune system]] participates in implantation, tissue remodeling, inflammation, and some reproductive disorders.
- The [[01 Systems/Nervous system|Nervous system]] influences stress physiology, sexual function, pain, and hypothalamic signaling.
- The [[01 Systems/Integumentary system|Integumentary system]] can show androgen, thyroid, and nutritional effects through hair and skin.

## What strain can look like

- Cycle irregularity, fertility concerns, libido change, hair changes, sleep disturbance, fatigue, pelvic pain, hot flashes, acne, androgen-related changes, or pregnancy/postpartum shifts.
- Symptoms that vary with cycle phase, pregnancy, postpartum state, menopause, hormonal medications, thyroid state, insulin resistance, stress, sleep, illness, or weight change.

## Discriminating questions

- Is the suspected issue ovulation, androgen excess, thyroid signaling, prolactin/pituitary signaling, adrenal hormones, insulin resistance, pregnancy, menopause, medication, or structural disease?
- What is the life-stage context, cycle pattern, pregnancy possibility, medication/hormone exposure, and metabolic context?
- Are there red flags such as severe pelvic pain, heavy bleeding, pregnancy-related symptoms, fever, sudden testicular pain, or rapidly worsening illness?
- What evidence separates PCOS, thyroid disease, normal life-stage transition, medication effects, stress/energy deficit, pregnancy, or inflammatory/structural disease?

> [!evidence] Boundary note
> A body system is a navigation layer. Symptoms usually arise from interactions among systems, exposures, life stage, medications, and context.
