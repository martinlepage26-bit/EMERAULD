# Hepatic and biliary system

## Core role

The liver processes nutrients and medicines, stores and redistributes fuel, produces bile, synthesizes many circulating proteins, coordinates lipid and glucose handling, and participates in immune-inflammatory signaling.

## Functional modules

- **Nutrient processing:** absorbed sugars, amino acids, lipids, vitamins, and minerals pass through liver metabolism and storage.
- **Bile production and flow:** bile supports fat digestion and absorption, and connects liver, gallbladder, bile ducts, and small intestine.
- **Glucose and lipid regulation:** the liver stores glycogen, releases glucose, and handles triglyceride and cholesterol metabolism.
- **Protein synthesis:** albumin, clotting factors, carrier proteins, and other plasma proteins depend partly on liver function.
- **Detoxification and metabolism:** medications, alcohol, hormones, and endogenous waste products are processed through liver pathways.
- **Inflammation and repair:** the liver responds to infection, metabolic stress, immune activity, and injury.

## System cross-talk

- The [[01 Systems/Digestive system|Digestive system]] delivers absorbed nutrients and receives bile.
- The [[01 Systems/Endocrine system|Endocrine system]] coordinates insulin, glucagon, thyroid, cortisol, and reproductive hormone effects.
- The [[01 Systems/Hematologic system|Hematologic system]] depends on liver-derived clotting proteins and iron/inflammation context.
- The [[01 Systems/Immune system|Immune system]] participates in inflammatory liver injury and repair.
- The [[01 Systems/Cardiovascular system|Cardiovascular system]] delivers portal and systemic blood flow that shapes liver processing.

## What strain can look like

- Fatigue, digestive symptoms, altered stool with bile-related issues, itching or jaundice in some contexts, abnormal liver enzymes, metabolic dysfunction, or altered clotting/protein markers.
- Symptoms and labs that shift with alcohol, medications, supplements, viral illness, metabolic risk, rapid weight change, gallbladder disease, or inflammatory disease.

## Discriminating questions

- Is the suspected issue hepatocyte injury, bile flow, gallbladder emptying, metabolic fat accumulation, medication toxicity, alcohol, viral disease, autoimmune disease, or downstream digestion?
- Are liver enzymes, bilirubin, albumin, platelet count, clotting tests, imaging, metabolic markers, and medication/supplement history available?
- Are there red flags such as jaundice, severe right upper abdominal pain, fever, confusion, vomiting, bleeding, or rapidly worsening illness?
- What evidence separates liver disease from digestive intolerance, endocrine/metabolic disease, anemia, medication effects, or nonspecific fatigue?

> [!evidence] Boundary note
> A body system is a navigation layer. Symptoms usually arise from interactions among systems, exposures, life stage, medications, and context.
