# Integumentary system

## Core role

Skin, hair, nails, sweat glands, sebaceous glands, sensory endings, immune cells, vessels, and connective tissues provide barrier, sensory, immune, thermoregulatory, wound-repair, communication, and vitamin D-related functions.

## Functional modules

- **Barrier protection:** skin limits water loss and protects against mechanical, microbial, chemical, and ultraviolet exposures.
- **Sensation and pain:** cutaneous nerves detect touch, temperature, pain, itch, pressure, and vibration.
- **Thermoregulation:** sweating, blood vessel tone, insulation, and behavior help regulate body temperature.
- **Immune surveillance and inflammation:** skin participates in allergy, infection defense, autoimmune conditions, wound repair, and inflammatory signaling.
- **Hair and nail growth:** growth cycles reflect local skin biology, hormones, thyroid signaling, nutrition, illness, stress, medications, and inflammation.
- **Visible systemic interface:** skin, hair, and nails may show clues from endocrine, nutrient, vascular, immune, or medication patterns.

## System cross-talk

- The [[01 Systems/Immune system|Immune system]] shapes inflammation, infection response, allergy, and wound healing.
- The [[01 Systems/Nervous system|Nervous system]] contributes sensation, itch, pain, sweating, and vascular tone.
- The [[01 Systems/Endocrine system|Endocrine system]] affects sweating, temperature tolerance, hair growth, skin texture, and androgen patterns.
- The [[01 Systems/Cardiovascular system|Cardiovascular system]] supplies skin perfusion and temperature regulation.
- The [[01 Systems/Digestive system|Digestive system]] and [[01 Systems/Hematologic system|Hematologic system]] can surface through nutrient status, absorption, anemia, and inflammatory markers.

## What strain can look like

- Hair loss, rash, itch, dryness, sweating changes, wounds that heal slowly, color change, cold intolerance, sensory changes, brittle nails, or inflammation.
- Symptoms that vary with exposure, season, stress, illness, thyroid state, iron status, medications, allergens, irritants, infections, or hormonal shifts.

## Discriminating questions

- Is the finding local skin disease, infection, allergy/irritant exposure, immune inflammation, vascular change, nerve sensation, endocrine shift, nutrient issue, medication effect, or hair-cycle change?
- Is onset sudden or gradual, localized or diffuse, itchy or painful, scaly or smooth, symmetrical or patchy, linked to exposure or illness?
- Are there red flags: rapidly spreading rash, fever, severe pain, mucosal involvement, facial/lip swelling, breathing symptoms, or signs of infection?
- What evidence separates thyroid-related hair/skin change from iron deficiency, androgen pattern, autoimmune disease, medication, stress shedding, or local dermatologic disease?

> [!evidence] Boundary note
> A body system is a navigation layer. Symptoms usually arise from interactions among systems, exposures, life stage, medications, and context.
