# Magnesium

## Established roles

Cofactor for hundreds of reactions involving energy, nucleic acids, muscle, nerves, glucose regulation, and blood pressure.

## Interpretation caution

Serum magnesium does not capture all body stores. Kidney function, medications, gastrointestinal loss, and supplement form affect the clinical picture. Excess supplemental magnesium can be unsafe in impaired kidney function.

> [!safety] Supplement boundary
> A biological role does not prove that supplementation will improve a symptom. Check dose, formulation, upper limits, kidney and liver function, pregnancy, medications, and laboratory context with a qualified professional.
