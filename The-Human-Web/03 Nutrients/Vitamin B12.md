# Vitamin B12

## Established roles

DNA synthesis, red blood cell formation, and neurological function.

## Interpretation caution

Absorption requires several digestive steps. Neurological manifestations can occur, and folate can correct some hematologic findings without correcting B12-related neurological injury.

> [!safety] Supplement boundary
> A biological role does not prove that supplementation will improve a symptom. Check dose, formulation, upper limits, kidney and liver function, pregnancy, medications, and laboratory context with a qualified professional.
