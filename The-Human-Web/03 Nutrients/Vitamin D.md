# Vitamin D

## Established roles

Calcium and phosphate regulation, bone health, and roles in immune and cellular function.

## Interpretation caution

Vitamin D status, activation, intake, sun exposure, absorption, kidney function, and medications can interact. Nonspecific symptoms do not establish deficiency.

> [!safety] Supplement boundary
> A biological role does not prove that supplementation will improve a symptom. Check dose, formulation, upper limits, kidney and liver function, pregnancy, medications, and laboratory context with a qualified professional.
