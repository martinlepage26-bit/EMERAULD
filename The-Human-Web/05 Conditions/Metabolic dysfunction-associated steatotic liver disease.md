# Metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease

## Clinical orientation

MASLD involves excess liver fat in a metabolic-risk context and spans a range from steatosis to inflammatory and fibrotic disease.

## Preserved nuance

Symptoms may be absent or nonspecific. Evaluation concerns metabolic risk, alcohol and medication context, liver injury, fibrosis risk, and alternative causes.

> This node supports informed questions. Diagnosis and treatment require appropriate history, examination, testing, and professional interpretation.
