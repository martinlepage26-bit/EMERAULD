# Irritable bowel syndrome

## Clinical orientation

IBS is a group of symptoms involving recurrent abdominal pain and altered bowel movements without visible structural damage identified in the digestive tract.

## Preserved nuance

IBS is not interchangeable with every digestive symptom. Alarm features, age, course, family history, inflammation, infection, and other disorders can change evaluation.

> [!safety] Diagnostic boundary
> This node supports informed questions. Diagnosis and treatment require appropriate history, examination, testing, and professional interpretation.
