# Iron deficiency anemia

## Clinical orientation

Iron deficiency anemia is anemia attributable to insufficient iron for hemoglobin production. Causes can include blood loss, inadequate intake, increased requirements, or impaired absorption.

## Preserved nuance

Ferritin, hemoglobin, indices, inflammation, age, sex, menstrual history, pregnancy, gastrointestinal symptoms, and bleeding risk may all affect evaluation.
