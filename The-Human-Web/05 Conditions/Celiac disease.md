# Celiac disease

## Clinical orientation

Celiac disease is a chronic immune-mediated disorder triggered by gluten that damages the small intestine.

## Preserved nuance

Do not remove gluten before indicated diagnostic testing without discussing it with a clinician, since dietary change can affect test results.

> [!safety] Diagnostic boundary
> This node supports informed questions. Diagnosis and treatment require appropriate history, examination, testing, and professional interpretation.
