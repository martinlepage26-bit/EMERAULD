# Type 2 diabetes

## Clinical orientation

Type 2 diabetes involves high blood glucose arising through insulin resistance and progressive impairment of insulin secretion.

## Preserved nuance

Diagnosis uses validated testing. Symptoms can be absent. Cardiovascular, kidney, eye, and nerve risk make longitudinal care important.

> [!safety] Diagnostic boundary
> This node supports informed questions. Diagnosis and treatment require appropriate history, examination, testing, and professional interpretation.
