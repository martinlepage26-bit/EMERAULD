# PCOS

## Clinical orientation

Polycystic ovary syndrome is a heterogeneous endocrine and reproductive condition. Hyperandrogenism, ovulatory dysfunction, and metabolic features can overlap in different patterns.

## Preserved nuance

Symptoms and morphology do not map one-to-one. Diagnostic criteria, age, medications, pregnancy goals, metabolic risk, and alternative endocrine causes require clinical review.

> [!safety] Diagnostic boundary
> This node supports informed questions. Diagnosis and treatment require appropriate history, examination, testing, and professional interpretation.
