# Autonomic dysregulation

## Pattern definition

A pattern involving altered regulation of heart rate, blood pressure, digestion, sweating, temperature, and arousal.

## Tension to preserve

Autonomic symptoms can arise from many conditions, medications, hydration changes, deconditioning, acute illness, and primary autonomic disorders. Do not reduce them to stress alone.
