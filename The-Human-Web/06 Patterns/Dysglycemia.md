# Dysglycemia

## Pattern definition

A pattern of abnormal glucose regulation. It is broader than a momentary subjective “sugar crash” and requires defined measurements and context.

## Tension to preserve

Meal timing, illness, medication, sleep, activity, alcohol, endocrine disease, and measurement conditions can all alter glucose patterns.
