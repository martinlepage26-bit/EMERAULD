## Source hierarchy

For clinical claims, prefer current guidelines and government or professional evidence reviews. Use systematic reviews to clarify contested areas. Use primary studies for narrow questions. Use commercial wellness pages only as leads, never as the final evidentiary anchor.

## Review record

The links and central claims in release 1.0 were reviewed on 2026-07-15. The vault does not reproduce whole source texts. It records short synthesis statements and direct links for verification.

The organ and tissue expansion added NINDS, NHLBI, NIAMS, NICHD, NIDDK, MedlinePlus, and NCBI Bookshelf source anchors on 2026-07-15.

Some government health pages state that they are not updated continuously. Before publication, clinical use, or productization, recheck the linked source and prefer the most current guideline or professional review available.
