# Clinical safety and red flags

> [!danger] Emergency boundary
> Call 9-1-1 or seek emergency care for severe breathing difficulty, severe or persistent chest pain, sudden one-sided weakness or numbness, new trouble speaking, fainting with concerning symptoms, severe sudden abdominal pain, uncontrolled bleeding, black or bloody stool with weakness, severe confusion, seizure, or a rapidly worsening condition. This list is not exhaustive.

For a non-urgent health concern in Québec, Info-Santé 811 provides nurse consultation. A pharmacist can review medication, supplement, and herb interactions.

## Do not use the map to

- delay emergency assessment
- stop, start, or change a prescription
- replace diagnostic testing with symptom matching
- infer a deficiency from one nonspecific symptom
- combine herbs and medications without an interaction review
- assume “natural” means safe
