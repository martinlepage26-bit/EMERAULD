## The epistemic rule

The layers can inform one another without validating one another. A coaching model is not neuroscience. A physiological analogy is not political evidence. A verified business relationship is not proof of a coordinated conspiracy. A rhetorical exaggeration can still point toward a real structural concern.

> [!hypothesis] Productive tension
> The atlas preserves both the critique of concentrated AI power and the possibility that AI can scaffold rather than replace human thought. The question becomes what design, ownership, practice, and governance preserve agency.
