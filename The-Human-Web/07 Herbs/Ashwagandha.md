# Ashwagandha

*Withania somnifera*

## What the evidence can support

Research has examined stress, sleep, and other outcomes, but studies are limited and product-specific.

## Connected systems

- [[01 Systems/Endocrine system|Endocrine system]]
- [[01 Systems/Nervous system|Nervous system]]

> [!danger] Safety and interactions
> Avoid assuming it is a neutral “adaptogen.” Thyroid effects, liver injury reports, pregnancy, autoimmune disease, surgery, and medication interactions require caution.

## Capture for a real product

- Exact label and brand
- Natural Product Number if sold in Canada
- Plant part and extract ratio
- Dose, route, frequency, and start date
- All medications and supplements
- Observed effects and adverse events
