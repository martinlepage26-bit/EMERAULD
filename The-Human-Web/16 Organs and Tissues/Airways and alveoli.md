# Airways and alveoli

## Core role

Airways move air into and out of the lungs. Alveoli are the tiny air sacs where oxygen enters blood and carbon dioxide leaves blood. Their function depends on airflow, surface area, blood flow, inflammation, and pressure mechanics.
