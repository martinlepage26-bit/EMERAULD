# Adipose tissue

## Core role

Adipose tissue stores energy, cushions organs, insulates the body, and acts as an endocrine and inflammatory signaling tissue. It is linked to insulin sensitivity, lipid metabolism, reproductive signaling, and inflammatory tone.
