# Peripheral nerves

## Core role

Peripheral nerves carry sensory, motor, and autonomic signals between the central nervous system and the limbs, skin, organs, and glands. They are vulnerable to compression, trauma, metabolic disease, nutrient deficiency, toxins, medications, and inflammatory processes.
