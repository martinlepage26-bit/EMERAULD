# Publication checklist

- [ ] Every direct quote matches a recording or transcript.
- [ ] Dates and offices are current.
- [ ] Funding, employment, mentorship, ownership, and control are not conflated.
- [ ] Intent claims have evidence beyond structural proximity.
- [ ] Market-loss figures specify companies, instruments, dates, and calculation.
- [ ] Allegations about war, death, criminality, or coordinated harm have primary evidence and precise attribution.
- [ ] Rhetorical labels are identified as judgments.
- [ ] Uncertainty and counterevidence remain visible.
- [ ] The argument still works after unsupported claims are removed.
