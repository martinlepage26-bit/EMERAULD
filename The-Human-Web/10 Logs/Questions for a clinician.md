## Questions

1. Which common causes fit this pattern, and which do not?
2. Are any medications, supplements, or interactions plausible contributors?
3. What findings would make this urgent?
4. Would examination or testing help distinguish the leading possibilities?
5. Should anything be avoided before testing?

## Bring

- Medication and supplement list with doses
- Product labels for herbs or natural health products
- Short symptom timeline
- Relevant family and personal history
- Existing results with dates and reference ranges
