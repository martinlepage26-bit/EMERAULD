# Intelligence as a utility

## The record shows

At BlackRock’s March 2026 Infrastructure Summit, Sam Altman described a future in which intelligence is available like electricity or water and purchased through metered use. OpenAI’s policy publications also compare advanced AI infrastructure with earlier general-purpose infrastructures.
