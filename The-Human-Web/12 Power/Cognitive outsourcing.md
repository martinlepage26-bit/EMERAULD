# Cognitive outsourcing

## The record shows

Generative AI can reduce effort for some knowledge tasks. A 2025 study of 319 knowledge workers found that confidence in AI and task-specific self-confidence predicted when users reported critical-thinking effort.

> [!evidence] Evidentiary tension
> The record does not show that generative AI inevitably “thinks for us” or systematically dumbs entire populations. Outcomes depend on task design, user practice, education, incentives, and whether AI replaces or scaffolds reasoning.
