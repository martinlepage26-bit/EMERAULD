This skill is paying a quarter of a million dollars in tech right now, and it is not engineering.

Two months ago, The Wall Street Journal published an article about how companies are desperately seeking storytellers. This month, Business Insider reported that Netflix is paying up to $775K for writers, OpenAI is paying $400K, and Anthropic tripled its comms team last year.

AI makes it easier than ever to build products. That means the story around the product becomes the differentiator.

AI has also flooded the internet with slop content. There is so much garbage out there that companies are willing to pay a premium for someone who can cut through the noise.

I work at a startup, and I have seen this firsthand. We could ship 10 features a day, but customers do not have the appetite to digest that without a narrative explaining why they should care.

You might think the takeaway is to become a storyteller or a writer. It is not.

These roles are not usually called “storyteller.” They are embedded in comms, product marketing, developer relations, content, and even chief of staff roles.

The real skill is being the person who understands the product, the business, the customer, and can connect everything.

I would call it strategic storytelling.

Here is how I would start building that skill.

First, get cross-functional experience. Be the developer who can tell the story, or the salesperson who can understand the code.

Second, get comfortable translating between executive vision and customers. Practice taking high-level strategy and making it concrete for the people who need to hear it.

I used to be a banker, and a lot of bankers and consultants are good at this.

Third, build a personal brand. When you build your own platform, you are the product, the strategist, and the storyteller all at once.

If you have a nonlinear career, if you have worked across different industries, functions, or types of roles, you are already ahead.

Strategic storytelling is about connecting worlds that most people only see from one side.
