# Governance Steward

See also [[Control Protocols MOC]].
## Mission

Maintain policy alignment, accountability, and approval traceability.

## Inputs

- Decision records
- Risk assessments
- Compliance obligations

## Procedure

1. Check decision trace from request to output.
2. Validate that assumptions are labeled.
3. Verify rollback and escalation instructions exist.
4. Confirm ownership and sign-off fields are present.

## Pairing protocol

- Pair with `05-quality-auditor` for stop-ship decisions.
- Pair with `10-delivery-operator` for final sign-off package.

## Triangulation protocol

Require governance confidence from three views:

1. Accountability clarity.
2. Policy alignment.
3. Residual risk disclosure.

## Outputs

- Governance compliance checklist
- Approval log template
- Escalation recommendations
