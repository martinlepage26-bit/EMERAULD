# Manifestation Rules

## Assignment logic

1. Use canonical ordering and unique combinations only.
2. Choose natural manifestations tied to physical, ecological, energetic, or cosmic behavior.
3. Require each manifestation to map to a real operational workflow function.
4. Include autonomous behavior for every combination.

## Element synthesis

- Physical elements shape weather, terrain, and force-state behavior.
- Wuxing elements shape growth, transformation, stabilization, precision, and replenishment loops.
- Spirit adds awareness and intent coherence.
- Chi adds circulation and vitality.
- Akasha adds source-field and cross-system synthesis.

## Escalation hierarchy

- Spirit = intent witness and ethical coherence.
- Chi = circulation and synchronization.
- Akasha = source-field resolution and system synthesis.
- If Chi + Akasha both appear, Akasha defines the field and Chi circulates within it.
- If Spirit + Chi + Akasha appear, enforce sequence: awareness -> circulation -> source-field integration.

## Manifestation family choice

- Weather: uncertainty routing and signal movement.
- Terrain: grounding, containment, and validation.
- Biological: growth and adaptive branching.
- Energetic: circulation and catalytic conversion.
- Cosmic: archive, field, and universal coherence.

## Pair-specific anchors

- Fire + Water -> Steam.
- Earth + Water -> Clay/Riverbed/Spring/Delta by context.
- Air + Water -> Cloud/Mist/Rain/Atmosphere.
- Wind + Water -> Storm/Current/Rainband.
- Fire + Earth -> Magma/Kiln/Ashfield/Forge-ground.
- Fire + Metal -> Forge/Alloy/Tempered Blade/Crucible.
- Wood + Fire -> Flame/Hearth/Charcoal/Renewal Burn.
- Wood + Water -> Sap/Forest Spring/Rootflow/Wetland.
- Metal + Water -> Mirror/Condensation/Channel/Compass.

## Anti-vagueness rule

Reject decorative names without operational behavior. Keep every manifestation executable in real workflows.
