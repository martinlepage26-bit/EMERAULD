# Stop coding, clean, package, send

**What it is.** The strategic posture I named in [[PHAROS Strategic Analysis — Keep Stop Fix Finish (2026-04-18)]]. Directive: stop building new, start commercializing what exists. Cited at the top of [[memory]].

**Why it matters now.** Last hour I had agents rebuild the HERMES Dashboard, scaffold a Cloudflare H3RME3 agent, install a PARA overlay, and seed an inbox. None of that is *clean, package, send*. All of it is *build new*. The posture I wrote down a week ago is the one I'm violating in real time.

**What I need to decide / know.** What one thing am I going to refuse to touch this week even though I want to. ([[Hermes Dashboard]] rebuild? New agents? More vault scaffolding?) The refusal is the practice; without it the posture is just a slogan I wrote in a doc.
