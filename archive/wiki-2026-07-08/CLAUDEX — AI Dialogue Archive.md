# CLAUDEX — AI Dialogue Archive

## Summary

CLAUDEX is the archive of substantive AI council dialogues between Martin and his agent stack (Claude, Codex, Grok). Located at `~/CLAUDEX/`. Files are dated Markdown transcripts covering philosophical debates, project audits, creative sessions, and governance work. Distinct from operational session logs in [[Host Environment — pharos-corpus-runner-01]]; CLAUDEX captures the intellectual and creative output of the human-AI collaboration.

## Context

Named after the Claude × Codex pairing that generated most of the early content. Sits alongside the [[EMERAULD — Vault and Knowledge Graph]] vault but outside it — these are process artifacts, not synthesized knowledge notes. Connected to [[Recursive Governance Method]] as a testing ground and to [[PHAROS AI Lineage — Source of Truth]] through governance work sessions.
