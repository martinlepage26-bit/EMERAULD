# PHAROS Stuck Deal Diagnostic — Minimum Viable Offer (2026-05-26)

## Summary

A single 60-minute paid call for a founder or B2B sales lead stuck inside a slow enterprise procurement cycle. Designed as the stripped-down, zero-build-time top-of-funnel version of the [[PHAROS Procurement-Unblock Sprint]]. Deliverable: a written diagnostic within 24 hours — the probable blocker, a political map of who is holding the deal, and 3–5 ranked actions. Designed in a session focused on skills-vs-hobbies analysis and minimum viable offer construction with a $100 start constraint.

## Context

This offer emerged from a session asking: what is the one thing I could sell this week, using only existing skills, with no build cost? The [[PHAROS Procurement-Unblock Sprint]] already existed as a named service but at a $35k–$125k price point requiring client commitment. The diagnostic is the entry layer: it validates the buyer before either party commits to a full sprint.

The constraints that shaped it:
- Under $100 to start
- One person can launch immediately
- Explainable in one sentence
- Validates before building anything complex

The session also surfaced that the Sprint's market hypothesis is only partially tested — no confirmed client transactions on record at time of design. The diagnostic is therefore also an evidence-gathering instrument for the Sprint itself.

## The Offer

**One sentence:** A paid 60-minute diagnostic for founders stuck inside a slow enterprise procurement, ending with a written action list to unfreeze the deal within a week.

**Exact customer:** B2B SaaS founder or enterprise sales lead, 6–18 months into a deal with a large organization, past the champion stage, now stuck waiting on legal, security, IT, or procurement, unable to tell whether the deal is dying or just slow.

**Painful problem:** They cannot tell whether the delay is bureaucratic friction or a quiet no. Every week of ambiguity costs them pipeline confidence and potentially lets a competitor in. Their champion will not give them a straight read.

**Deliverable:**
1. 60-minute live diagnostic call (operator asks, buyer answers)
2. 1–2 page written action list delivered within 24 hours:
   - Probable blocker (named, not "probably procurement")
   - Political map of who is actually holding the deal
   - 3–5 actions ranked by leverage

**Price:** $150–$300. Low enough to remove friction; high enough to signal competence over guessing.

**Start cost:** $0 beyond time. No platform, no curriculum, no website required.

## Relationship to Existing Offers

This is not a replacement for the Sprint. It is the top-of-funnel validation layer:

```
Diagnostic call ($150–$300)
    → Sprint ($35k–$125k, if diagnostic confirms the deal has value and Martin is the right fit)
```

The [[AI Governance Sprint — One-Page Sellable Packet]] serves a different buyer (professional services firms needing governance documentation). These are parallel offers, not the same ladder.

## Daily Execution Plan — Getting the First Customer

Source: Hormozi-style business coaching session (2026-05-26). The single most important daily activity before the first sale is **direct outreach to people who currently have the pain**.

### Why outreach beats everything else right now

No proof yet that anyone will pay. Branding, landing pages, and LinkedIn posts do not generate proof. Direct conversation with a specific person who has the specific pain is the only activity that collapses the gap between "offer exists" and "money received" in under a week.

### Two-hour daily block

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 0:00–0:20 | Build today's list: 10 named people (not categories — actual names) |
| 0:20–1:40 | Send 10 messages. One personalized line at the top, then the script |
| 1:40–2:00 | Follow up on previous days; book calls immediately on warm replies |

### Outreach script

For LinkedIn DM, email, or text to people with a real connection:

> Hey [name] — I've been building a short diagnostic service for founders who are stuck inside enterprise procurement. One 60-minute call, I help you figure out whether the deal is actually moving or quietly dying, and you get a written action list the next day.
>
> Are you currently in any deals that feel stuck? Or do you know someone who is?

For close contacts:

> Quick question — are you in any slow enterprise deals right now? I'm doing paid diagnostics and you might be a fit.

### Minimum daily volume

**10 contacts per day.** Below 10 is not doing the activity.

Math: 10% reply rate → 1 conversation per 10 contacts. 30% call-to-sale → 1 sale per 3 conversations. At 10 contacts/day: first sale statistically within the first week.

### Progress metric

**Conversations started** (replies received, any response). Not impressions, not profile views. Secondary metric: calls booked. Target: at least one call booked by day three.

### If no replies after three days — diagnose one of these

1. **Wrong list.** Switch to people who have posted about enterprise sales frustrations, procurement delays, or long deal cycles in the last 90 days. Pain must be live and current.
2. **Message too soft.** Does the first sentence name the specific pain? If it reads like a networking message, rewrite it to read like you already know they have a problem.
3. **Not asking for the referral hard enough.** Add: "Even if this isn't your situation — do you know one person currently stuck inside a procurement process?" One warm referral outweighs ten cold contacts.

**Do not change the offer. Do not rebuild positioning. Do not start a content strategy.** The offer is validated enough. The problem is volume and targeting.

## Skills-vs-Hobbies Session — Key Findings

This note was created from a session that first mapped skills vs. hobbies before designing the offer. Key signal:

**Monetizable skills (source-grounded from vault record):**
1. Enterprise procurement unblocking — PHAROS Sprint, named and priced; implies prior analytical work on this domain
2. Multi-agent AI architecture — Elemental Agents, real engineering substance (10 agent contracts, 165 combinations, 216-line awk validator)
3. Structured knowledge systems design — Obsidian Agent Vault, EMERAULD vault; builds for himself before selling

**Hobby/interest signal (inferred, not source-grounded):**
- Systems with symbolic coherence (Charge & Circle: governance with aesthetic form)
- Humanities formation alongside technical work (philosopher, peer-reviewed-paper-writer skills; PhD domain)
- Rigorous conceptual architecture for its own sake

The session flagged that no answers were given to the clarifying questions (which transactions have been paid; what activities happen with no deadline; what topics trigger over-explanation). Those answers would sharpen the hobby read. The skill read is more reliable — it is grounded in vault artifacts.
