# Vitamin B12 deficiency

## Clinical orientation

Vitamin B12 deficiency can affect blood formation and the nervous system. Dietary intake and multiple absorption steps are relevant.

## Preserved nuance

Neurological features may occur with or without obvious anemia. Folate exposure can complicate recognition of hematologic signs.
