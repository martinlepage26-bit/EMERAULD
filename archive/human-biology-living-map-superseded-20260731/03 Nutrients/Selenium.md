# Selenium

## Established roles

Component of selenoproteins involved in redox biology and thyroid hormone metabolism.

## Interpretation caution

Deficiency and excess can both be harmful. Evidence for routine selenium supplementation in thyroid disease is condition-specific and not a general treatment rule.

> [!safety] Supplement boundary
> A biological role does not prove that supplementation will improve a symptom. Check dose, formulation, upper limits, kidney and liver function, pregnancy, medications, and laboratory context with a qualified professional.
