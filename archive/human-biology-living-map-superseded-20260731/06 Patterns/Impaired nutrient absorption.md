# Impaired nutrient absorption

## Pattern definition

A cross-system pattern in which nutrients are not adequately transferred from the digestive tract into circulation.

## Tension to preserve

Possible contributors include mucosal disease, pancreatic or bile-related problems, surgery, medications, infection, and rapid transit. The affected nutrients depend on location and mechanism.
