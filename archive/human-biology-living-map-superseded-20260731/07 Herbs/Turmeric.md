# Turmeric

*Curcuma longa*

## What the evidence can support

Curcumin and turmeric products have been studied for several conditions, with variable formulations and bioavailability.

## Connected systems

- [[01 Systems/Immune system|Immune system]]

> [!danger] Safety and interactions
> Some highly bioavailable products have been linked to liver injury. Gallbladder issues, bleeding risk, pregnancy, and medication interactions warrant review.

## Capture for a real product

- Exact label and brand
- Natural Product Number if sold in Canada
- Plant part and extract ratio
- Dose, route, frequency, and start date
- All medications and supplements
- Observed effects and adverse events
