# Herbs and botanicals

> [!danger] Interaction-first rule
> Herbs are pharmacologically active exposures. Check the exact product, dose, route, pregnancy status, kidney and liver function, surgery timing, allergies, and all medicines with a pharmacist or qualified clinician.
