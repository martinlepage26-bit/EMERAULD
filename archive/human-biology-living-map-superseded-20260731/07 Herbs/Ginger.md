# Ginger

*Zingiber officinale*

## What the evidence can support

Studied most often for nausea and vomiting in specific contexts. Evidence and dosing are context-dependent.

## Connected systems

- [[01 Systems/Digestive system|Digestive system]]

> [!danger] Safety and interactions
> May cause gastrointestinal effects and may interact with medicines. Pregnancy, bleeding risk, gallbladder disease, and planned surgery warrant professional review.
