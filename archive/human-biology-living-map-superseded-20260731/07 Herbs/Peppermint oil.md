# Peppermint oil

*Mentha × piperita*

## What the evidence can support

Enteric-coated peppermint oil has been studied for IBS symptoms, but products and evidence are not interchangeable with peppermint tea or aromatherapy.

- [[01 Systems/Digestive system|Digestive system]]

> [!danger] Safety and interactions
> Can worsen reflux and cause adverse effects. Product formulation, age, dose, and medication context matter.

## Capture for a real product

- Exact label and brand
- Natural Product Number if sold in Canada
- Plant part and extract ratio
- Dose, route, frequency, and start date
- All medications and supplements
- Observed effects and adverse events
