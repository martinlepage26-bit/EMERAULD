The smallest “building block” is not matter, not information, not energy, not even a symbol. It is a difference that can be re-entered as a rule.

That is the hidden connection between DRT7, EML, and lily of the valley.

1. The object is never first

A molecule is not a smell until it meets a receptor.
A protein is not an enzyme until it constrains a reaction.
A number is not mathematics until it can be transformed.
A flower is not symbolic until it enters memory, danger, love, ritual, or myth.

So the basic unit is not:

“thing”

It is:

thing-in-relation

Even deeper:

a relation that preserves enough structure to repeat

That is why this feels profound. You are sensing that the “atom” of reality is not an atom. It is a mapping.

2. DRT7: the protein becomes a relation to DNA

The DRT7 result is striking because it moves templatehood away from the usual nucleic-acid chain and toward protein structure. The bioRxiv preprint reports that DRT7 has reverse-transcriptase and primase-polymerase domains, provides broad-spectrum anti-phage immunity, and, when activated, synthesizes long protein-primed, palindromic poly(A)/poly(T)-rich DNA.

The deep point is not simply “protein makes DNA.”

The deep point is:

form becomes instruction

Usually, biological information is imagined as linear:
DNA sequence → RNA sequence → protein sequence.

But here, the relation is more spatial and topological:

folded protein → constrained nucleotide addition → DNA trace

The protein is not merely an actor. It becomes a template-like field. Its shape limits what can happen next. That is a more primitive kind of information: not “message,” but constraint.

So DRT7 says: before information is text, information is permitted continuation.

3. EML: one relation unfolds mathematics

The EML operator has the same metaphysical shape.

eml(x,y)=e
x
−ln(y)

The arXiv paper claims that this single binary operator, together with the constant 1, can generate the usual scientific-calculator functions, including arithmetic, logarithms, exponentials, trigonometric functions, and constants like e, π, and i. It also describes EML expressions as binary trees generated from a grammar of the form S→1∣eml(S,S).

That is enormous conceptually.

It says mathematics may not need many primitive operations. Maybe a rich world can unfold from one repeatable relation:

take two things, relate them under one asymmetric rule, then feed the result back in.

This is recursion.
This is generation.
This is world-building.

EML is not merely a function. It is a seed-relation.

4. Lily of the valley: relation becomes beauty and poison

Lily of the valley is the poetic third term because it shows that the same relational principle does not stay abstract.

A flower is beautiful, but its beauty is not inside the flower alone. It appears through relation: volatile molecule ↔ receptor, form ↔ eye, memory ↔ symbol.

It is also dangerous. Lily of the valley contains cardiac glycosides; convallatoxin is described as a principal toxic cardiac glycoside, and toxicology sources connect its cardiotoxicity to inhibition of Na+/K+-ATPase.

So the same flower is:

scent to the nose
poison to the heart
symbol to the mind
nectar-sign to insects
seasonal marker to culture

Its “identity” is not singular. It is a bundle of relational activations.

Lily of the valley is not one meaning.
It is a relational prism.

5. The true primitive: not substance, but interface

Now combine the three.

DRT7: protein structure interfaces with nucleotide synthesis.
EML: one operator interfaces two inputs into a generative tree.
Lily of the valley: one plant interfaces with receptor, heart, memory, and symbol.

The shared primitive is:

interface

An interface is where one domain becomes legible to another.

Protein becomes legible to DNA synthesis.
Numbers become legible to functions.
Molecules become legible to bodies.
Flowers become legible to memory.

The interface is smaller than the object because the object only becomes functional through it.

6. The deepest formulation

A thing does not become real by existing alone.

It becomes real when it can make a difference to another thing.

So the smallest building block is:

difference-with-address

A difference must land somewhere.
A molecule must bind.
A symbol must be interpreted.
A protein shape must be read by a catalytic pocket.
An operator must accept inputs.
A flower must enter a nervous system, an ecosystem, or a story.

No address, no effect.
No effect, no meaning.
No repeatable effect, no world.

7. Template, operator, receptor are the same archetype

These three words look different, but they are cousins.

A template says: continue according to me.
An operator says: transform through me.
A receptor says: become signal in me.

Template = relation as copying.
Operator = relation as transformation.
Receptor = relation as recognition.

DRT7 is template.
EML is operator.
Lily of the valley is receptor-field.

All three are modes of the same deeper structure:

a relation that turns contact into consequence

8. Biology’s deeper dogma

The Central Dogma is about direction of sequence information.

But beneath that is an older principle:

life is made of constraints that preserve themselves by transforming other constraints.

DNA constrains RNA.
RNA constrains protein.
Protein constrains chemistry.
Chemistry constrains metabolism.
Metabolism constrains survival.
Survival constrains which DNA continues.

So even the Central Dogma is not merely a chain. It is a loop of constraints, with sequence as one powerful form of relation.

DRT7 feels shocking because it exposes a layer beneath the classroom rule:

before code, there is templating;
before templating, there is shape;
before shape, there is relation.

9. The lily is the warning

Lily of the valley says: do not confuse gentleness with harmlessness.

The same delicate bell-shaped flower can be fragrance, ornament, poison, medicine-image, fairy-symbol, funeral-symbol, spring-symbol.

This matters because relational reality is not morally simple.

A relation can heal or kill.
A template can defend a bacterium by sacrificing a cell.
An operator can generate mathematics or chaos.
A flower can become love in one body and arrhythmia in another.

Meaning is not intrinsic goodness.
Meaning is directed power.

10. The final depth

The smallest building block is not a particle.

It is not even information.

It is:

a repeatable asymmetry that lets one thing become the condition for another thing.

That is the seed of biology.
That is the seed of mathematics.
That is the seed of perception.
That is the seed of symbol.

DRT7 says: a protein can become a condition for DNA.
EML says: one binary relation can become a condition for mathematics.
Lily of the valley says: one flower can become a condition for scent, poison, beauty, memory, and myth.

So the hidden sentence beneath all three is:

Reality is not built from things. Reality is built from relations stable enough to be repeated, folded, read, and transformed.

And the deepest version:

A world begins when difference learns how to return.
