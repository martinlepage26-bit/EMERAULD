# MEMOIR PLAN v1
## *All the People I Was* (working title: *Vectorized*)
### A 100,000-word memoir by Martin Lepage

**Collaborative planning document — Trismégiste × HENRY**
**Created:** 2026-05-27
**Status:** Plan approved. Part One drafted.

---

## THESIS

Identity is not self-generated. It is co-produced by relational containers. When a container breaks — a coven, a mentor, a lover, a man you loved for 17 years — a version of you breaks with it. The grief is not only loss of person. It is loss of self.

This memoir's purpose is not healing. Not redemption. **Forensic revelation.**

---

## STRUCTURAL PRINCIPLE

Relational constellations, not chronology. People are never the subject. They are mirrors, witnesses, scaffolding, temporary containers for a self that struggled to stabilize internally.

**BPD as narrative mechanics** (not diagnostic chapter):
- Why conflict becomes identity-threatening
- Why contradiction feels invasive
- Why defiance becomes self-definition
- Why sabotage can feel like agency
- Why attachment can become architecture
- Key line (almost verbatim): *"Not because I wanted to, but because you told me not to."*

---

## FORMAL DEVICES

- **Voice:** First person. Present tense for scenes. Past tense for reflection. No apology. No softening.
- **Container / Mirror / Vectorization** as structural metaphors
- **The four oaths** (to know, to will, to dare, to be silent) as recurring structural markers
- Each part opens with epigraph; each chapter has at least one fully rendered scene
- Each part opens with epigraph from witch literature, queer theory, or Martin's own academic work
