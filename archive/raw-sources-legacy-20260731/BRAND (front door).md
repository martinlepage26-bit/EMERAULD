## The two engines (your internal method, made explicit)

### AurorAI — Legibility Layer (document-to-evidence)

**Purpose:** sorts messy client documents into legible, governable outputs.
**Outputs (examples):**

* Draft AI system / use-case inventory from scattered sources
* Evidence map: what exists, what’s missing, what’s stale
* Crosswalks (vendor claims ↔ required controls ↔ evidence)

**Promise:** reduces document chaos; increases traceability.
**Does not claim:** compliance certification, legal advice, automated approvals.

### CompassAI — Governance Engine + Deliverable Maker

**Purpose:** turns your governance method into repeatable routing: classify → obligations → gates → artifacts → monitoring.
**Outputs (examples):**

* Risk classification decisions + rationale logs
* Deliverable bundles (policies, registers, checklists, system/model cards)
* “Inspection-ready packet” builder (board/procurement/audit view)

**Promise:** standardizes governance logic and deliverables.
**Does not claim:** replaces accountable humans, sets risk appetite, guarantees compliance.

## The pipeline (overview)

**Inputs → AurorAI → Evidence Map → CompassAI → Gates + Artifacts → Monitoring + Response**

You get a system where a board member can follow the logic, procurement can see the control implications, and scrutiny can reconstruct what happened.

---

## Step 0 — Scope and boundaries

We establish:

* What counts as an “AI system” in your context (models, rules, automations, vendors)
* System boundaries, owners, and decision authorities
* Where AI touches sensitive domains (rights/benefits, employment, credit, safety, security)

**Output:** system boundary map + ownership draft.

---

## Step 1 — AurorAI: Document-to-evidence legibility

We ingest what you already have (policies, vendor decks, tickets, product docs, audits, security artifacts). AurorAI turns it into:

* Inventory draft (systems/use cases/vendors)
* Evidence map (present / missing / stale)
* Traceability crosswalks (claims ↔ controls ↔ evidence)

**Output:** inventory v1 + evidence map + crosswalks.

---

## Step 2 — CompassAI: Classification and routed obligations

We classify use cases by risk and impact, and record **rationale**:

* Risk factors and thresholds (your method + your risk appetite inputs)
* Required obligations by class (testing, documentation, approvals, monitoring)
* Ownership and sign-off path

**Output:** classification decisions + rationale logs + obligation routing.

---

## Step 3 — Gates: constraints embedded into delivery

We embed controls into workflow gates so governance is enforced where it matters:

* **Intake gate:** what must exist before build
* **Build gate:** tests, data/labeling constraints, evaluation requirements
* **Deployment gate:** sign-off packet, documentation standard, rollback readiness
* **Change gate:** what triggers re-review (model update, vendor change, new domain)

**Output:** gate checklists + sign-off packet templates + evidence standards.

---

## Step 4 — Artifacts: inspection-ready by design

We generate a consistent deliverable set:

* System/model cards (evidence artifacts, not marketing)
* Registers and checklists
* Vendor AI evaluation packet (procurement-ready)
* Operating model (RACI, cadence, decision forum)

**Output:** a governed artifact library + an indexed “inspection-ready packet.”

---

## Step 5 — Monitoring and response (keep it real)

Governance fails when it goes stale. We install the refresh loop:

* KPI/KRI definitions (drift, harm signals, thresholds)
* Quarterly refresh packets
* AI incident response playbook + tabletop drills
* Post-incident “patch notes” to update controls and gates

**Output:** monitoring plan + response playbook + refresh cadence.

---

**I do:** design governance routing, implement gates, standardize evidence artifacts, improve traceability and reconstructability.
**I don’t:** certify compliance, replace accountable decision-makers, set your risk appetite, or provide legal advice.
