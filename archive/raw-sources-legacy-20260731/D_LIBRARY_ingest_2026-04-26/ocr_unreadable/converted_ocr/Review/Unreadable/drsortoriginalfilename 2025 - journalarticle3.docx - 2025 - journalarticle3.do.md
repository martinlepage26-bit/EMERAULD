**Editorial Board***Departures in Critical Qualitative Research*University of California Press

Dear Editors,

Please find enclosed my submission, “Stuttering in the Institution: On Queer Knowing, Academic Containment, and Refusal,” for consideration in *Departures in Critical Qualitative Research*.

This essay offers a critical autoethnographic reflection on the epistemic, affective, and institutional barriers I encountered as a queer, stuttering scholar working on contemporary Paganism. Grounded in queer-crip theory, the piece frames stuttering not as communicative failure, but as epistemic resistance, a refusal to conform to the fluency, polish, and speed that define academic legibility.

Drawing on the work of Ahmed, Baril, Price, and others, I explore how the university manages difference through symbolic containment, and how lived voice can become method. The manuscript is rigorously theorized and methodologically anchored, combining personal narrative with conceptual intervention. A full Methods and Framework section is included.

*Departures*’ commitment to formal experimentation and institutional critique makes it an ideal home for this work. The manuscript is approximately 7129 words, formatted in APA 7, and includes an abstract and five keywords. It is not under consideration elsewhere.

Thank you for your time and care in reviewing this submission.
