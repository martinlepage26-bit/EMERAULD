Martin Lepage, PhD

*Slayage*

Bio

Martin Lepage is a Montréal-based scholar of media, gender, and contemporary religion. His research examines the intersections of queer theory, postfeminist media cultures, ritual studies, and digital aesthetics, with particular attention to questions of embodiment, seriality, and mediated subjectivity. He holds a PhD in Social Sciences and has published on neo-Paganism, digital ritualization, and queer methodological interventions in popular culture analysis. His current work investigates the aesthetic politics of televisual iconography and the epistemologies of hybrid academic-creative scholarship.
