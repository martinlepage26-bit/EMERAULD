OBJECT : Application to Assistant editor position

Dear Professor Boisvert,

I would like to be considered for the current open position as Assistant editor for the journal
Sciences Religieuses/Studies in Religion within the Canadian Corporation for Studies in
Religion (CCSR). As you know, I received a Ph.D. in Religious studies at Université du
Québec à Montréal in 2017, and I am convinced that my research experience, as well as
my interest in the different aspects of social scientific publication have adequately prepared
me for this position.

Since 2011, I have been actively involved in conversations with students and scholars from
many universities; through organization and participation in our annual Young researchers
conference; from Université de Montréal, with Groupe de Recherche Diversité Urbaine
(GRDU); from Université Laval, having been a part of conferences within ACFAS and
others; and from around the world, contributing to the Religious Studies Project, a project
sponsored by the British Association for the Study of Religion (BASR); to online networks
of researchers specialized in Pagan studies and Ritual studies; with the International
Society for the Study of Religion (ISSR), and the American Academy of Religion (AAR).
Consequently, my research was published, both in French and in English, often after
contributing to such conferences. This led to several peer-reviewed articles and book

chapters, in addition to book reviews, at Presses de l’Université Laval, Routledge, Sciences
religieuses/Studies in religion, Anthropologica, Religiologiques, among others.

More closely related to the position as assistant editor, I have been acting, since 2016, as a
reviewer for journals such as Social Compass and Lithuanian Journal of Anthropology,
and, since 2018, as director for an upcoming thematic issue of Religiologiques on
alternative religious practices among LGBTQ people. During my time with the Religious
Studies Project, between 2014 and 2016, I acted as assistant editor, working on podcast
interviews, creating content and responses, as well as editing the website. Furthermore, I
have a great deal of experience in revision and translation, having served as a research
assistant and worked on publishing results for projects on sexuality, ageing and
masculinities, on gendered school rites, on youth and sexuality in Quebec, on marginalized
populations in France, and on religious diversity in Montreal. It would finally very much
be a pleasure and a passion to actively participate in the stimulating and important scientific
community that is the CCSR. Since I have no other strict professional commitment at the
moment, I would be available, as requested, to fill this position during the next three years.

You will shortly receive a recommendation letter from Roxanne Marcotte, professor in the
Département de sciences des religions at UQAM, but be assured that I will be happy to
meet with you any time you deem appropriate to answer your questions about my academic
path and my professional endeavors.
Thank you greatly for your consideration,

Martin Lepage, Ph.D.
