Dear Martin:
We are pleased to offer you a conditional offer of Full-Time employment as a Agent with MMCC Solutions
Canada (hereinafter known as Teleperformance Canada). This offer of employment is subject to the
Conditions set forth in paragraph 9 below. Once you have satisfied the Conditions, we will contact you to
discuss your start date (the “Effective Date”). This letter agreement (the “Agreement”) shall set forth our
mutual understanding regarding your employment with Teleperformance Canada pursuant to the following
terms and conditions (the receipt and sufficiency of which is acknowledged).

1.

POSITION AND TERM

Your employment with Teleperformance will commence on the Effective Date and will continue indefinitely
until it is terminated in accordance with the terms set forth in this Agreement.
You shall perform those duties and responsibilities necessary or incidental to perform the functions of a call
centre agent. You will report to an Operations Supervisor of Teleperformance Canada. Your assignments,
duties, responsibilities, schedule, title and reporting arrangements may change from time to time at the sole
discretion of Teleperformance. You agree that any changes which may occur pursuant to this paragraph will
not affect or change any other part in this Agreement.
2.

PROBATIONARY PERIOD

The first ninety (90) calendar days of your employment shall be considered a Probationary Period. If
Teleperformance Canada terminates your employment within the ninety (90) calendar day Probationary
Period, you will not be entitled to receive any notice of termination or pay in lieu thereof.
3.

POLICIES

It is a term and condition of your employment with Teleperformance Canada that you agree to comply with
the policies and procedures of Teleperformance Canada, including but not limited to those set out in
Teleperformance Canada Policies and Procedures Guidelines Handbook, as may be amended from time to
time by Teleperformance Canada at its absolute discretion. Violation of Teleperformance Canada’s Policy
and Procedure Handbook and any other policies and procedures of Teleperformance Canada may result in
discipline up to and including termination.
It is understood that any of the changes which may occur pursuant to this paragraph will not affect or change
any other part of this Agreement.
4.

HOURS OF WORK

You may be scheduled to work up to forty-eight (48) hours in a week, as required by business demands.
These hours of work may be amended from time to time and your schedule may vary, depending on the
needs of the business.
Due to the nature of our business you may be requested to work in excess of forty-eight (48) hours in a given
week. Teleperformance has received approval from the Director of the Ministry of Labour to schedule hours
in excess of the maximum weekly limit of forty-eight (48) hours as set out in the Employment Standards Act,
2000 (“ESA”). If you choose to work hours in excess to the maximum weekly limit of forty-eight (48) hours,
you may agree in writing by completing the Teleperformance written agreement for excess hours of work
[Written Agreement for Excess Hours of Work – Schedule A], provided to you in your orientation package.
If you do not want to work more hours, you do not have to sign [Written Agreement for Excess Hours of
Work - Schedule A].

The nature of our business requires that employees are flexible and able to adapt to different schedules.
Therefore, we require our employees to be flexible to scheduling based on the needs of our business.
Teleperformance reserves the right to change the process for how scheduling is completed at any time. From
time to time due to unforeseen circumstances with scheduling and volume forecasts, voluntary time off
and/or mandatory time and/or temporary layoff under the ESA may occur.
You will be eligible for overtime compensation pursuant to the overtime provisions in the ESA, as amended
from time to time.
It is understood that any of the changes which may occur pursuant to this paragraph will not affect or change
any other part of this Agreement.
5.

COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

You will receive an hourly rate of $12.75 (“Hourly Wages”), as well as any other applicable shift premium,
bonus and/or language premiums (“ Other Payments”) which may apply to the program with which you are
affiliated. You will be paid semi-monthly (on the 7th and 22nd of the month) by direct deposit only (less
deductions required by law). If you are transferred to another program either voluntary or due to program
closure or volume decrease, your hourly wages and entitlement to other payments, if any, may change to
reflect the specifications of the program to which you are transferring.

You will be entitled, to the extent that you are eligible, to participate in the employee benefit plans and
programs which may be available to employees of Teleperformance, from time to time. Benefit coverage is
subject to the terms and conditions of the applicable benefit plan and policy, and may be reduced, modified
or cancelled by Teleperformance Canada at any time.

Vacation accruals begins on your first day of training. You will accrue vacation at a rate of 0.83 days per
month, for a maximum of ten (10) vacation days per calendar year. Please refer to the Teleperformance
Vacation Policy in the Teleperformance Canada, Policies and Procedures Guidelines for more details.
It is understood that any of the changes which may occur pursuant to this paragraph will not affect or change
any other part of this Agreement.
6.

PUBLIC/STATUTORY HOLIDAY

You may be required to work on Canadian statutory holidays, in the event you do work on a statutory
holiday, you will be compensated for working such day in accordance with the ESA.

7.

TERMINATION OF EMPLOYMENT

By Employee
You may resign from your employment at any time upon giving Teleperformance Canada at least two (2)
weeks’ advance written notice. In the event that you resign your employment, you shall only receive
payment of: (i) unpaid Hourly Wages and Other Payments, if any, up to the date of resignation; and (ii)
accrued vacation pay, if any, that shall have been earned by you prior to the date of resignation but not yet
paid.
With Cause
Teleperformance has the right, at any time and without notice, to terminate your employment under this
Agreement for Cause. In the event that Teleperformance terminates your employment for Cause, you shall
only receive payment of: (i) unpaid Hourly Wages and Other Payments, if any, up to the date of termination;
and (ii) accrued vacation pay, if any, that shall have been earned by you prior to the date of termination but
not yet paid.
“Cause” means any act or omission by you that would in law permit Teleperformance Canada to, without
notice or payment in lieu of notice, terminate your employment.
Without Cause
In the absence of Cause, Teleperformance may terminate your employment, at any time, at its sole discretion
and for any reason whatsoever, and in such event Teleperformance’s sole obligations shall be: (i) to provide
you with the minimum notice of termination, or pay in lieu thereof, and severance pay, if any, as may be
required pursuant to the ESA, as amended from time to time; (ii) to pay to you any portion of unpaid Hourly
Wages and Other Payments, if any, up to the date of termination; (iii) to pay to you any accrued vacation pay,
if any, that shall have been earned by you prior to the date of termination but not yet paid and any vacation
pay owing for the statutory notice period under the ESA only; and (iv) to provide for your continued
participation in Teleperformance’s group benefit plans in which you participated immediately prior to the
date of termination, for such time as required under the ESA, as amended from time to time.

You understand and agree that the notice and severance requirements contained in this clause shall continue
to apply at any time in the future, regardless of the duration of your employment, and despite any changes
that may occur in your compensation, job functions, responsibilities or title, so long as you continue to be
employed by Teleperformance. You further agree that the notice and/or payments required under the ESA,
as amended from time to time, and other entitlements outlined in paragraph 7(c) fully satisfies
Teleperformance Canada’s obligations to you relating to your termination of employment, and that you are
not entitled to additional pay or notice of termination under statute, common law, civil law or contract.
8.

CONFIDENTIALITY

During the course of your employment, you may have access to information that is privileged and
confidential. As a condition of your employment with Teleperformance, you will be required to sign our
Confidentiality Agreement attached hereto as Schedule “B”, and the execution of and adherence to the
Confidentiality Agreement is an express term of this Agreement. You may also be asked to sign a separate
Client Confidentiality agreement and other pertinent documents if so required by the client of the program
you are assigned to.
9.

This offer of employment is conditional upon the following:
Successfully completing and/or passing the following employment related checks: a Police Record/Criminal
Record Check; a Credit Check; and a Reference Check regarding educational qualifications, professional
qualifications, and your performance and/or behaviour standards at your current or former place of
employment. In order for Teleperformance to conduct such background checks, you agree to return to
Teleperformance a signed copy of the [Consent form name] attached hereto as Schedule “C”. There may be
times throughout your employment that you may be asked to provide an updated background check.
Successful completion of in-class training, which includes passing of all testing as directed by our clients. In
order to move to the production environment you must meet all requirements of knowledge, performance and
attendance during the training period. Due to business needs, training times and dates may be subject to
change. Attendance is mandatory for all training sessions. If any portion of the training cannot be completed,
approval must be obtained in advance from the appropriate site management. If you are absent from any part
of the training program, you must provide written documentation with reasonable cause.

GENERAL CONTRACT PROVISIONS

employment, for any reason, your representations, warranties and covenants contained herein shall survive
any such termination or cancellation and shall remain in full force and effect.
Severability – If any covenant or provision contained herein is determined to be void, invalid or

Entire Agreement – This Agreement constitutes the whole of the agreement between the parties. There are
no collateral representations, agreements or conditions not specifically set forth herein.
Non-Assignability – This Agreement or any part of this Agreement, is not assignable by you, and any benefit
derived under this Agreement is for your sole benefit and may not be assigned.
Amendments and Waivers – No supplement, modification, amendment or waiver of this Agreement shall
be binding unless executed in writing by you and Teleperformance. No waiver of any of the provisions of
this Agreement shall be deemed to constitute a waiver of any other provision (whether or not similar), nor
shall such waiver constitute a waiver or continuing waiver unless otherwise expressly provided in writing by
you and Teleperformance.
Governing Law – This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the
Province of Ontario and Canada, as applicable.
Independent Legal Advice – You acknowledge that you have read, understand and agree with all of the
provisions of this Agreement, and acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to obtain independent legal
advice with respect to it.

Anti-Bribery Policy
Teleperformance employees, consultants, independent contractors, agents and other business partners working on behalf
of Teleperformance, must always conduct business in accordance with the highest standards of ethical behavior and
honesty.

There are laws and regulations that govern Teleperformance’s operations and prohibit the payment of money or other
things of value in order to receive a commercial benefit or personal advantage. Canada has laws that make this type of
activity a criminal offence and such behavior, even without a specific law prohibiting it, is clearly inappropriate and shall
not be tolerated by the Teleperformance.

Teleperformance employees are prohibited from offering payments of money or anything else of value, whether directly
or indirectly, to a client, potential client, business associate, manager or anotheremployee unless previously disclosed to
and authorized by the Executive Leadership Team.

In addition, Teleperformance employees are prohibited from offering money or anything else of value in order to:

influence any act or decision;
cause the person to commit or omit any act in violation of his or her lawful duties;
secure any improper advantage; or
induce the person to use his or her influence to affect any act or decision

Violations of this Policy not only undermine Teleperformance’s core values and business purpose and potentially subject
Teleperformance to penalties and criminal penalties. Employees who violate this policy shall be subject to discipline, up
to and including termination of employment for cause.

Attendance and Punctuality Guidelines
It is recognized that a reasonable amount of absence and/or tardiness, due to bona fide sicknesses or emergency situations,
is sometimes beyond the control of an employee. On the other hand, Teleperformance is entitled to a reasonable degree of
regularity in the attendance of our employees. Unscheduled absences from work cause disruption to the work process and
place additional burdens on your fellow employees.

The scope of this guideline cannot anticipate all circumstances surrounding employee attendance – please see your
supervisor or HR Department for any clarifications you may require.

Paid Sick Time

It is up to an employer’s discretion to pay for “sick “days. Sick days do not roll over into the following calendar year nor
are they paid out.

Sick days requested after a resignation of employment has been accepted will not be paid out.
In order to be paid sick time standard documentation may be requested.

Once employees complete 6 months of service, all employees accrue 1 sick day per quarter. Unused sick days may be
rolled over into the next quarter. However, sick days do not roll over into the following calendar year nor are they paid out

Reporting of an Unscheduled Absence/Emergency Leave
If an employee is unable to report to work when scheduled, it is mandatory to contact their manager a minimum of two (2)
hours prior to the scheduled start time. If unable to speak to their manager directly, the employee is required to leave a
voice mail message on their manager’s extension so that arrangements can be made for appropriate coverage. The sick
line number will be provided to all employees during training.

Required Verification of Absence documentation

Teleperformance reserves the right to request written verification for any unscheduled absence. The employee, when
requested, must provide evidence that is “reasonable”. For medical absences, the standard Teleperformance Medical
Absence Form must be used as documentation. For absences related to an employee's family member, the verification
should include name and relationship of the person for which the leave is sought and that the absence is required because
of illness, injury, or medical emergency.

Written verification will be required after three (3) consecutive days or more of unscheduled absence, and after
three (3) non-consecutive days of unscheduled absence in a calendar year.
During training appropriate documentation is required for any and all absences. (please see Training
Attendance Guidelinebelow)
The use of a fraudulent documentation will not be tolerated and will result in immediate separation of
employment for cause.

Based on the history of attendance Teleperformance reserves the right to request documentation for any and all
absences at any time.
