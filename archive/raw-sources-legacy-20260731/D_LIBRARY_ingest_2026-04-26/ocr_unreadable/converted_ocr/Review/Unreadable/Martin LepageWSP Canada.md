Dear Hiring Committee,
I am writing to express my interest in the position of Head of Privacy, Artificial Intelligence
and Technology Law – Americas. This role strongly aligns with my professional background
and expertise at the intersection of AI governance, data protection, regulatory compliance,
and the practical integration of legal requirements into complex, technology-driven
organizations.
My experience has focused on advising organizations operating in rapidly evolving
regulatory environments, where privacy, responsible AI, and digital technologies require
governance frameworks that are both robust and operationally effective. I have developed a
strong ability to translate complex legal and regulatory obligations into clear, actionable
guidance that can be embedded into product design, business processes, and organizational
decision-making.
The regional scope of this role across Canada, the United States, Latin America, and the
Caribbean is particularly compelling. I am comfortable working across jurisdictions with
divergent legal regimes, ensuring consistency in global programs while adapting
implementation to local regulatory and operational realities. I am especially drawn to the
strategic dimensions of the position, including oversight of regional programs, leadership of
local privacy and AI governance stakeholders, risk assessments, and collaboration with
cross-functional teams at both operational and executive levels.
WSP’s commitment to embedding privacy, responsible AI, and technology governance as
enablers of trust, innovation, and long-term value creation closely reflects my own approach
to these issues. I would welcome the opportunity to contribute to advancing this vision across
the Americas.
Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to the possibility of further
discussion.
Sincerely,
