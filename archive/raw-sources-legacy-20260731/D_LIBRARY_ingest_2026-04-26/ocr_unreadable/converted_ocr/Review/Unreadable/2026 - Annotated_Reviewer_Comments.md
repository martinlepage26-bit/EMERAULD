Annotated Reviewer Comments
Manuscript
When Threat Affirms: Expanding Self-Affirmation Theory Through the Asian-White Multiracial
Experience​
Below, reviewer comments are inserted directly at relevant section breaks using bracketed annotations to
simulate margin comments (Word-compatible).
Overall Manuscript
Strong paper with a clear conceptual contribution. The framing of threat as generative rather than merely
something to be buffered is interesting and appropriate for JHP. Main issue is length and repetition rather
than substance.
Literature Review
The literature review is thorough and well integrated, particularly the engagement with self-affirmation
theory and multiracial identity research. Some synthesis sections could be tightened to avoid restating
well-established points.
Method (Collaborative Autoethnography)
The method is appropriate and well justified. Collaborative autoethnography is a good fit for the research
questions, and reflexivity is handled carefully. However, the manuscript should include an explicit
statement regarding IRB approval, exemption, or ethical oversight.
Findings
The findings are compelling and clearly written. Julie’s narrative is analytically productive rather than
merely descriptive. Consider trimming some illustrative passages that repeat the same analytic insight.
Discussion
The discussion effectively connects the findings to self-affirmation theory, but it is overly long. Core
arguments about multiplicity and threat are reiterated several times. Streamlining would significantly
strengthen the paper.
Scope and Claims
At times, the discussion edges toward broader theoretical generalization. This is a single-case collaborative
autoethnography; clearer boundary-setting around what the study can and cannot claim would improve
rigor.
Ethics
While ethical care is evident in practice, there is no explicit ethics or IRB statement. This needs to be added
for transparency, given that the data originated from a prior student research project.
Final Recommendation
Very close to publication-ready. Revise for concision, clarify scope, and add an ethics statement.
Recommended: Accept with Revisions.
