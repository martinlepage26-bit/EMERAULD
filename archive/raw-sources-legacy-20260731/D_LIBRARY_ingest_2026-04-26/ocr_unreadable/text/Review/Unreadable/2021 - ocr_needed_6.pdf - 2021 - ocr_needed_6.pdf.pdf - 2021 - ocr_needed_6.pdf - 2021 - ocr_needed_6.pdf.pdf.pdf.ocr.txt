| About the Author
Edward W. Said, one of the country’s most dis-
tinguished literary critics, is Parr Professor of Eng-
lish and Comparative Literature at Columbia Uni-
versity. He was Visiting Professor in Comparative
Literature at Harvard and a Fellow of the Center for

i Advanced Study in the Behavioral Sciences at Stan-
ford, and delivered the Gauss lectures in criticism
at Princeton in 1977. In 1976 his book Beginnings:

. Intention and Method won the first annual Lionel

Trilling Award given at Columbia University.
