# Oralité primaire et transmission des savoirs — Claude Gilles 2017 (Vodou haitien à Montréal-Nord)

## Summary
UQAM master’s thesis (maîtrise en communication) by Claude Gilles (March 2017) on **primary orality** and **knowledge transmission** in the practices of **Haitian Vodou in Montréal-Nord**. This is a high-value local field anchor that can complement the more “classic” Vodou ethnography spine (e.g., [[Voodoo — Alfred Métraux (1959) — Haitian Voodoo ethnography]]) with a Montreal diasporic context.

## Why it matters here
- **Montreal continuity:** bridges “Vodou as tradition” into a concrete Montreal site (Montréal-Nord) with contemporary transmission dynamics.
- **Method alignment:** intersects with the vault’s ethnography + oral transmission concerns (see [[Qualitative Handbook — Expanded Reference List]]).
- **Genealogy support:** strengthens the archive around the Vodou/Vodun cluster already present via Métraux + Tourreil.

## Key Ideas (Vault-usable)
- “Oralité primaire” as a framework for how knowledge is carried, stabilized, and legitimated without relying on text-first transmission.
- Diasporic practice as a *transmission problem* (how authority, memory, and correct practice survive relocation).
