# Anthropologie rituelle autour de la maladie et de la mort — Tourreil 2019 (Abomey, Bénin)

## Summary
Virginie Tourreil’s 2019 doctoral thesis (Université Paris-Saclay / UVSQ) studies illness and death rituals in a Vodun context (Plateau d’Abomey, Bénin). It expands the ritual/medicine/death reference surface available to [[Queer Sociology of Magic and Ritual]] and [[Ritual, Magic, and Institutional Authority]], and provides comparative material that can discipline claims made in the Haitian Vodou cluster (see [[Voodoo — Alfred Métraux (1959) — Haitian Voodoo ethnography]]).

## Context
This is a high-density academic artifact with stable identifiers (HAL, thesis metadata) that can be cited without relying on secondary summaries. It is a strong candidate for Henry’s master reference lists because it anchors a whole domain of ritual anthropology adjacent to the vault’s ritual authority and proof-regime work (see [[Social Compass Paper — Ritual Authority and Régime de Preuve]]).
