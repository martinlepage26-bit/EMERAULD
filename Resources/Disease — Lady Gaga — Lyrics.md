## Summary

Full lyrics to "Disease" by Lady Gaga (2025). A seduction-as-medicine conceit: the speaker positions herself as doctor/antidote to a partner's suffering. Connects to the enchantment-as-desire category in [[Inductive Literary Discourse Analysis — Witchcraft in Song Lyrics]] and sits alongside "Abracadabra" in the Gaga corpus analyzed there. Relevant to [[Media Studies and Pop Culture Analysis]].

## Context

Released 2025. The medical metaphor ("cure your disease," "antidote," "poison on the inside") frames erotic power as healing authority — a recurring Gaga move of inverting vulnerability and control. Source clipping: `Clippings/gaga gisease lyrics.md`.
