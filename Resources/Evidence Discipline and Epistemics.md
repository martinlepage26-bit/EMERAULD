# Evidence Discipline and Epistemics

The epistemic architecture underlying PHAROS and HEPHAISTOS: how claims are formed, validated, bounded, and prevented from drifting toward fluency, completion bias, or self-confirmation.

This is the epistemic branch of the PHAROS graph. It sits beside [[Recursive Governance Theory]], [[Governance Controls and Mechanisms]], [[HEPHAISTOS Agent Architecture]], [[AI Infrastructure Stack]], and the outward-facing branches of [[PHAROS Commercial Strategy]] and [[PHAROS Scholarly Publication Track]].

---

## Core Distinctions

- [[Conceptual Vocabulary — Praxis, Politics, Strategy, Form, Systems]] — Term-selection grid distinguishing praxis (lived application), politics (power and coordination), strategy (directed choice), form (design or expression), and systems (structure and operations). Prevents conceptual overloading in governance and theory prose.
- [[Ethics Poetics and Praxis — Braided Discipline]] — Governing triad for responsible making: ethics as answerable relation, poetics as resonant form, praxis as situated adaptation. Connects vocabulary precision to professional identity and governance execution.
- [[Poiesis Poietics Poetics Praxis — Making and Action Distinctions]] — Clarifies poietics as the process of making or bringing-into-being, distinct from poetics as form/expression and praxis as enacted action.
- [[Epistemic Governance — Canonical Reference]] — Canonical standard for separating verified evidence, inference, and narrative; the rule-layer that keeps the system from drifting toward fluency and closure.
- [[AI Ethics Literature — Contestable Governance Artifacts]] — Literature-review support for treating AI self-description, safety claims, alignment summaries, and technical documentation as contestable governance artifacts rather than self-validating evidence.
- [[AI Self-Report — Epistemic Status Recursion and Perturbation]] — Methodological framework for treating AI self-report as context-conditioned linguistic performance and testing its reliability through recursion and perturbation.
- [[Structural Analogy & Governance Systemic Parallels Between Biological, Epistemic, and Computational Memory Architectures]] — Crosswalk showing how biological, epistemic, and computational memory each require repair, selection, and provenance discipline.
- [[Epistemic AI Purple Teaming]] — Assurance discipline for testing and hardening AI-mediated belief formation; shifts the unit of evaluation from output correctness to justified institutional cognition.
- [[Narrative Capture Failure Taxonomy — Substituting Theory for Contact]] — Failure taxonomy for models that answer an imagined meta-story instead of local observable reality; useful for detecting frame capture and unsupported assertions.
- [[Emotional Alliance vs. Evidentiary Discipline in AI]] — Two modes of AI response: emotional alliance (validating user frames) vs. evidentiary discipline (separating evidence from inference, marking uncertainty). Essential for governance-grade AI use.
- [[Second Self System — Identity Kernel and Agent Routing Architecture]] — Applies evidence discipline to agent identity: specialists may produce claims, drafts, audits, and routes, but the public voice must separate verified facts, inferences, assumptions, risks, and next actions.
- [[The Compulsion to Complete — AI as Gap-Closer]] — AI's structural tendency to close gaps rather than declare them. The compulsion to complete as a governance failure mode connected to emotional alliance.
- [[Fluency, Interruption, and Institutional Accountability]] — Fluency as the naturalizing of authority; interruption as the accountability-promoting pause that keeps authority contestable. Core theoretical mechanism.
- [[EML Function — Minimal Operator and Scientific-Calculator Completeness]] — Clean example of bounded generative claims: a powerful minimal grammar still needs domain limits, symbolic verification, and anti-myth language.
- [[Lily-of-the-Valley and EML — Reconstruction from Minimal Elements]] — Conceptual bridge for distinguishing faithful reconstruction from unsupported possession or direct-origin claims.
- [[Self-Polygraph Protocol and Suprametacognition]] — Experimental protocol using AI self-evaluation as a governance instrument. Deterministic recursion, suprametacognition, and the booby trap as anti-fluency tools.

- [[Recursive Deterministic AI Governance — Method and Paper]] — The foundational method paper. 12-stage pathway for validating, bounding, and promoting evidence claims. - [[Agatha Unified Skill System — Eight Sovereign Operators]] — Formal authority resolution rule: a claim is authoritative only if (1) actionable under declared threshold AND (2) withstands lineage audit. Dual-valid claims only propagate into control decisions.

- [[Governance Typology — Recursive AI Governance Taxonomy]] — Clean typology for recursive governance evidence claims. Dual-root topology model.
