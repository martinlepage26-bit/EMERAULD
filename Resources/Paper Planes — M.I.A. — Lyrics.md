# Paper Planes — M.I.A. — Lyrics

## Summary

Full lyrics (one line redacted; see source clipping for complete text) to "Paper Planes" by M.I.A., from the album *Kala* (2008). A key text in postcolonial pop: immigration, hustle economics, and third-world/first-world friction delivered through ironic gunshot/cash-register hooks. Connects to [[Media Studies and Pop Culture Analysis]] and the song corpus in [[Inductive Literary Discourse Analysis — Witchcraft in Song Lyrics]].

## Context

"Paper Planes" samples The Clash's "Straight to Hell" and became M.I.A.'s breakthrough single. The lyrical content — visa fraud, hustling, Robin Hood redistribution — enacts the diasporic subject speaking back to border regimes. Relevant to any analysis of pop music as political speech.
