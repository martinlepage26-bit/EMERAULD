# The Data Gaze — Beer (Capitalism, Power, Perception)

## Summary
*The Data Gaze: Capitalism, Power and Perception* (David Beer, 2019) is framed (in the captured review) as an extension of Beer’s earlier *Metric Power* toward big data: how “lines of sight” and predictive visualization materialize futures, how “data imaginaries” generate a **data rationality** (“to think only within the bounds of data”), and how black-boxed infrastructures translate quantification into governance leverage. Sits adjacent to [[Privacy as Contextual Integrity — Nissenbaum 2004 (Public Surveillance)|Nissenbaum’s contextual integrity]] (what flows feel wrong even when nominally permitted) and to [[Governance by Denial|governance-by-denial]] (what institutions can recognize and sanction once data-rationality has set the recognition frame).

## Provenance note
The captured artifact is a **draft book review** (Konstantinos Kerasovitis, 2019) in *Information, Communication & Society*, not the full book. Use as a routing/anchor note; verify claims against the book before quoting as Beer’s positions — flagged in [[D Library — Genealogy Flags and Cleanup Leads (2026-04-26)|the D-Library genealogy flags]].

## Key Ideas (vault-usable, from the review)
- **Archon / archive gatekeeping:** big data hinges on who controls access, interrogation, and the interpretive pipeline (data is never “raw”) — the same point [[Automating Inequality — Eubanks (Digital Poorhouse, profiling and punishment)|Eubanks]] makes about welfare-state classification.
- **Data imaginary:** what big data is *presented* as doing vs what it actually does; this gap shapes organizational adoption — a [[Power in International Politics — Barnett & Duvall 2005 (Taxonomy)|productive-power]] effect at the level of the data-rationality itself.
- **Obligation to predict:** predictive systems are framed as error-free and urgent (“use this or fall behind”), converting adoption into necessity — companion to [[The Compulsion to Complete — AI as Gap-Closer]] at the institutional scale.
- **Black-box friendliness:** anthropomorphized algorithmic imaginary (“smart, learns, does the thinking”) masks technical opacity — the same pattern audited in [[AI Self-Report — Epistemic Status Recursion and Perturbation]] and resisted by the [[PHAROS Method — Technical Reference|PHAROS interrogability requirement]].
- **Self-validating futures:** prediction steers action → action makes prediction “true” → expands data frontiers (datafication limits push outward); a structural cousin to the [[AI Iterative Loop — Frame Capture and Recursive Validation Failure|recursive validation failure]] mode at societal scale.
