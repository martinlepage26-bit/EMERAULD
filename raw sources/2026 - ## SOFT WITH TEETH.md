## SOFT WITH TEETH

**Verse 1**
Some smiles land like a dare in gloss
Sweet on top, but it counts the cost
“Take your time” said with one eye sharp
Like the clock got a hand in the dark

A “hey” turns quick into “where you been”
A hush turns into a sudden scene
Care comes cute, then checks the door
Like quiet is crime to be “sure”

**Pre-Chorus**
Praise when bright, tense when slow
That’s how a room learns what it owes
Little joke, little sting
Love shouldn’t need a ring to swing

**Chorus**
Soft with teeth, that’s the trap
Velvet voice with a snap
Warm until the air turns wide
Then the cold comes from inside
Kiss, then count, then blame
Call it “truth,” call it “same”
If a pause turns into court
That’s not love, that’s a sport

**Verse 2**
Some nights shine, then shift on a dime
Gold on skin, then a cut in time
Not fear, not doom, just that thin chill
Like the world goes quiet and the body goes still

Big talk dreams with no safe place
Hope on fast, but it can’t keep pace
Solitude loud, no peace in it
Ambition lit, but it won’t quite sit

Bright gets loved, shade gets trimmed
Soft gets asked to stay slim
“Not yet” heard as “never”
Then the grip gets clever

**Pre-Chorus**
A line gets drawn, it gets called a wall
A breath gets taken, it gets called a fall
Funny how “care” can turn mean
With a smile so clean

**Chorus**
Soft with teeth, that’s the trap
Velvet voice with a snap
Warm until the air turns wide
Then the cold comes from inside
Kiss, then count, then blame
Call it “truth,” call it “same”
If a pause turns into court
That’s not love, that’s a sport

**Bridge (big vocal, cheeky bite)**
Say “be real,” mean “be easy”
Say “be close,” mean “don’t leave me”
Say “no rules,” then keep score
Say “just us,” then want more

Not every want gets a key
Not every doubt gets to be
a hand in the sleeve, a thumb on the scale
a pretty push that can’t fail

**Final Chorus (lift, chantable)**
Soft with teeth, cut so sweet
Sugar jaw, pretty heat
Warm until the air turns wide
Then the cold comes from inside
Kiss, then count, then blame
Call it “love,” call it “same”
If a pause turns into court
That’s not love, that’s a sport

**Outro (warm, small, haunted)**
Soft can stay soft.
When it’s safe.
When it’s not
it shows its teeth.
