Right to Explanation (Art. 13–15): Users can demand visibility into automated decisions (e.g., AI-driven profiling).
Right to Be Forgotten (Art. 17): Individuals can request invisibility (e.g., delisting search results).
Data Portability (Art. 20): Users can access and transfer their data (e.g., social media archives).

EU Digital Services Act (DSA): Platforms must explain content removal (e.g., why a post was taken down) and provide appeals processes.
Germany’s NetzDG: Social media must remove illegal content (e.g., hate speech) within 24 hours or face fines.
Australia’s Mis/Disinformation Code: Platforms must label or remove false content (e.g., deepfakes).
