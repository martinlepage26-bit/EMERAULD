One of the most important theories I have is about the soul of human beings. After several readings and studies about it, I have come to the conclusion that it has a point of anchor in the heart of humans.

In fact, I believe that the soul has only one particle of matter that takes part of the muscles in the human heart. Then the soul, even when it is not contained in a body, gravitates all around it.

This theory confirms a lot of things, legends, rumors, and other theories.

It explains why we say that our feelings come from the heart. It explains why werewolves would only eat the heart of their victims. It gives an answer to the legend that vampires can only die by having a stake driven through their heart. It also confirms the theory of the silver thread that links the soul to the body in astral projection, attached to the heart.

This particle of matter, sheltered into the heart, cannot be deciphered from the other cells in that body part. This is why science or medicine will probably never be able to prove that theory right, and even less, wrong.

Most believers and witches deny the existence of demons in our world. Personally, I believe that they exist in more ways than you might think. First of all, let me explain what a demon is in the first place.

Mostly, demons are created by mankind with hate, violence, fear, revenge, and death. In fact, any energy negative enough to suck from other living forms can be called a demon. These demons can be activated by a dark witch, acting after his or her will.

Sometimes, a demon, after years and years of constant growth and evolution, becomes strong enough to invade a body, either by possessing an animal or a human, any already existing type of creature, or by taking the place in a newborn child where the soul normally anchors itself.

But be aware that this happens very rarely, and that the odds this being keeps living after its incarnation are very thin. And of course, if the creature stays alive after this, it will not know that it was a demon once before.

It will only keep taking energy from others because hate and violence will be its only goals, without even noticing that it is bad, in an instinctive way, I might say.

But therefore, that so-called human-shaped demon normally doesn’t have any power. Exceptions can always happen when the baby that the demon invades is destined to be a witch. In this case, the baby will grow up and develop its powers in a dark way, using them to hurt people and for its personal advantage.

This demon kind doesn’t look any different from anybody else, although there might be a possibility that the dark strength of the demon is so powerful that it causes metamorphosis in the being it invades.

This is why I believe that it is in this way that vampires, werewolves, banshees, or any other type of well-known demon are created, spreading afterward their demonic curse to other beings.

I don’t know if, as a good witch, it is our duty to vanquish and kill these demons, because at the age of 18, I’ve never faced one myself. But I know that if you sense evil in a newborn child of your family, you have to do anything in your power to save the baby from a dark destiny.

Here are my thoughts about the existence of **vampires**.

First of all, yes, I believe that they exist. Only, I also think that if there are some left on this earth, they are hiding, because they can’t be seen by society, otherwise they would be destroyed.

Since they are beings of great power, understanding their nature is very important. Even if their nature is indeed controversial, as if I don’t really know if they kill from an evil desire of destruction or only from a survival will, I will explain what kind of vampires, according to my knowledge and experience, in fact exist.

The first kind of vampire, the most evil of all, descends from the union of a half-demon with a woman of this earth.

This story came to me in the speech of a very powerful and experimented witch, who actually lived it in one of her past lives.

At the beginning, there was a demon that incarnated in a dozen men: long black hair, pale face, very attractive in an unknowable way. One of these men copulated with a woman. Together, they had a baby, half-demon, half-human. This creature grew up very fast and copulated with its father, the demon-man. This created the first vampire of this kind, a man with red hair who had powers of his own.

Another kind of vampire that I believe exists was created when, somehow, a person got cursed because of his sins. This person, probably strong-bonded with God, became a traitor, and when he died, became a creature of the night, hater of God and all things that represent Him.

I believe that this is the only kind of vampire that is actually afraid of crosses and garlic, since this plant has great exorcism powers.

Then comes the kind of vampire that has been the most popularized by movies and popular culture. This is the bat-vampire. I believe this one exists in two ways.

First, it can be the union of a very powerful witch and a bat. This had the effect of giving the witch the need for blood, the power to turn into a bat, and, of course, the hardest obligation: to live only at night.

The second way I have to believe in this kind of vampire is that, maybe, it is only the first kind I talked about that has the power of shape-shifting, and since they can only hunt at night, the form of the bat became the most useful.

And then, of course, comes the matter of the psychic vampires, that do not drink blood, but only feed on people’s energy to keep themselves alive.

This kind of vampire always existed and has no link with evil beings or demons. In fact, humans can be, for few of them, powerful psychic vampires, taking energies from their entire family and friends.

After explaining what are, according to me, the different kinds of vampires, comes the question of the whole notion of the sun, silver, blood, stake in the heart, and garlic.

Even if we can easily understand the matter of the sun, creatures of the night can’t stand light, and of garlic, which has already been explained, and of the blood drinking, the only thing that they can physically absorb to stay alive, some of them cannot be related to a real proof of certainty.

Like silver, for example. That is a pure metal of exorcism, but it has never been proved to have effect on the evil powers of vampires.

But the most important of these notions is the way that they can or can’t be killed.

First, the sun. The sun is the ultimate power of good. So, any of the kinds of vampires I named, and that are especially evil, are killable with the sunlight.

And then there is the stake in the heart. Like I explained before, the heart is the anchor point of the soul. Since the powers take place in the soul, to destroy the heart is the only way to annihilate the powers of the vampire.

Then comes the question of immortality.

It is obvious that only the first kind of vampire is immortal, since it has been created from a powerful evil and not from a human, which could only bring its species to decaying.

Spreading is a very important thing to all kinds of vampires. There is a belief that only the most beautiful, strange, and powerful people can be turned into vampires. This process, compared to a blood disease obtained by the mix of the vampire’s and the human’s blood, is very painful, and few can actually survive it.

This is why I think most vampires were witches in their lives, or at least very old souls who had the strength to survive.

Among the set of tools that a witch normally has, the **Book of Shadows** is certainly the most important.

In order to get a magical book from the fabrication of a Book of Shadows, certain things must be done.

The most important thing is that you have to handwrite your own Book of Shadows. Handwriting will make your book a book of power, which you will tap in during times of need, or when you feel that you have a drop of energy.

Even though being a tool of power, your book already has a degree of protection, it still needs some protection from you. I personally wrote the one right beside in mine.

Also, to make it even more personal and linked to your inner energy and power, you might want to decorate it or, at least, put some meaningful embellishment in it.

This together will, after a certain time, create a powerful book that will last you for a very long time, maybe for generations.

There are other kinds of books of power that exist in this world. Of course, here, I am speaking about Books of Shadows made by evil witches.

These books, aside from the fact that they have been created to contain magical information, are very different from our kind. Because they are not protected like ours, they might be dangerous to people who are not witches.

These books are made to suck energy from living beings to give to their masters, to put some curse on you, or even to mess up with your karma, even though you have done nothing wrong.

To destroy these books is very important, because you don’t know what kind of danger it could inflict to innocent people.

Here are my thoughts about **correspondences**.

Correspondences are the link between herbs, colors, or minerals and their properties that witches use in spells and rituals. Any book of magic that you find on the market will give you tables of correspondences, but few, if not none, of them tell you why mandrake is an herb for love, or garlic for exorcism.

I have two beliefs about this.

First, I think that herbs, stones, etc., got their particular power because of popular culture. It is obvious for everyone that the rose is a flower of love, or that sage is used for purification. Traditions, general belief, and cultures give power to things at a certain point.

This is why I believe there is another reason. Since herbs, stones, and colors are obviously natural things, they all have a certain vibration, a vibe that, when used in a spell, affects the energy of the spell in a way that the rose creates a love spell.

And this is the same thing for other herbs and minerals. Colors also have their kind of vibration, but it is more a matter of light that reflects on a candle or an ink, which still affects the energies of the spell.

Even though I don’t really have any answers about how these correspondences of energy have been discovered and listed this way, I must think that ancient witches or sorcerers, or even wise men, did these lists by trials and mistakes, like every good witch learns his or her witchcraft.

Since I became a witch, and even before, I had this belief that every witch, or at least every being of good **power**, has some kind of self-preservation power, a capability ordered by instinct that protects the person in question.

I don’t really know where that protection comes from exactly, but I must think that the inner power of the witch is based on the life inside the body. So it is obvious that, in order to protect this life, the inner power acts like some sort of karmic protector.

This thing acts in a lot of ways, like it stops you from crossing a street before a car hits you, or it makes someone get hurt before this person hurts you.

Once again, it can be a question of karma, but most of the time, witches have more luck, more chances for doing good in society.

Of course, like anything else, there is a counterpart to this theory. Don’t expect to be protected very well if you keep abusing your powers, using your spells against people, or for revenge. Once again, it is all about the karmic laws.

When a witch gets strong and wise enough, it can result in the development of powers. And by powers I mean real magical powers, rooted in the inner power of the witch. The more she or he is powerful, the more the powers will be.

Even if some powers are not very rare, some of them are, depending on the level they are part of. I personally classified powers in three degrees of strength.

First, at the lowest level of powers, we find the warning powers. This includes all divination powers, like clairvoyance or premonition, powers like empathy, that are made to help people, or any evil-sensing powers.

At the second level, we find the protection powers. These can have an effect on people and help them concretely, but they cannot hurt. This includes every healing, deflecting or evil-shielding powers, and telepathy.

The third kind of power is the defending powers. Understand that they are not called attacking powers, even though they can hurt people, because they are made to do good when a good witch has one. This category includes telekinesis, pyrokinesis, mind-affecting powers, or even object-creating powers.

Among all the reasons there are to stop a person from becoming a witch, I believe that Catholicism is a major one.

In fact, in my life, I’ve seen some Catholic rituals that might have consequences on the development of one’s psychism.

First, when a kid is born with Catholic parents, society doesn’t give that baby a choice for its religion and spirituality. In fact, a kid can’t go to school if he doesn’t have a baptism certificate. So, at only three or four months, they already choose a religion for the kid, which stops him from having the choice.

In the same way, baptism is a ritual that includes a godmother and a godfather. These people are supposed to help the kid through his learning of spirituality. But I believe that the kid’s and the godmother’s, if it’s a girl, or the godfather’s, if it’s a boy, spirituality are directly linked.

So, if a godfather doesn’t have a strong spiritual side, there will be a chance that the little boy won’t either. The same thing happens if a girl has a witch as a godmother. She has a better chance of becoming one too.

Mixed with a touch of psychic vampirism, he seems to have the capacity of maintaining his victims and zombifying them. In fact, with techniques as animal as sexuality and manipulation, in an absence of common sense, he creates himself specific identifiable victims.

As an example, his girlfriend Josie has become a consenting victim, from a desire of a fake future and a certain life safety assurance, through sexuality, gifts, lies, and seizure of energy.

This kind of victim becomes almost a slave and lives only to content him. However, some potential victims, as myself and Joey, have escaped soon enough to keep their identity.

In fact, victims lose their energy, their personality, and their habits for the benefit of the demon. Thus, when they regain consciousness, the victims are given their lucidity back and are aware of the vampire’s acts.

In spite of the difficulty to escape the demon’s hold, it is still possible to achieve it. The only solution is to get out of his whereabouts, or tiredness, exhaustion, and bad luck will remain.

Though it seems very hard to comprehend the nature of this demon, he only lives to steal energy from people he surrounds and exploits by picks and [unclear]. In this way, he is not really aware of what he does. He does not know it’s wrong, because that is the only way he can live.

Regarding this fact, we can understand one of his biggest weaknesses. The demon fears any sign of weakness. And loss of energy, signs of joy, or any kind of surrender is ferociously avoided by the demon.

In fact, it can become, at a certain point where the victims start to recover, an obsession, a pride crisis.
