As a disabled individual, somebody who stutters, who has always had a hard time being understood and perceived authentically in accordance with what they really are, I'm just another human being among billions and billions like a temporary grain of sand, but has long became dark oil or tar in the depths of the earth. But I am also a star-seed. I am disabled, but it gives me more abilities than most people who just fit the norm. And it gives me power on my own, not taking it from others.
That is why I started this whole endeavor when Phenix died, what he did. I lost a bit of myself and stopped understanding this for what it was. And then things started to fall apart and I started to fail more. And I'm using the project to get out of that for myself and to address at the same time a bigger issue for the world, for society as it is changing with the invent of the immense power that comes with artificial intelligence. As something ended for me, I looked to the future and I hope that it will seed something into the world itself.
As I am writing this, my two one-year-old cats are wreaking havoc on my house. They represent the chaos that the world needs to stop the tears, to have sadness metabolized into new love, to have technology turn into social justice, to have trauma turned into respect. Down with those who want to destroy that.
