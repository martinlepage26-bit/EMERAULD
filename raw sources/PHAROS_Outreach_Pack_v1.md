## How to Identify the Right Contact (do this before sending)

Each email is written for a specific role family. **Do not send to a generic info@ address or to the CEO.** The right recipient is the senior executive whose calendar will already have "AI governance" or "model risk" on it for Q3 2026 planning. Find them this way.

**Step 1 — LinkedIn title search.** For each institution, run these searches in LinkedIn (use the company filter):

- *Desjardins:* search "model risk" OR "risque de modèle" OR "gouvernance IA" OR "AI governance" OR "Chief Data Officer" — filter to current employees only
- *Banque Nationale / National Bank of Canada:* search "model risk management" OR "MRM" OR "AI risk" OR "Chief Risk Officer" — note that BNC has a public, identifiable AI governance function
- *Beneva:* search "risque de modèle" OR "actuarial" OR "Chief Risk Officer" OR "gouvernance des données" — Beneva is smaller and the right contact is likely VP-level rather than C-suite

**Step 2 — Verify currency.** Click into the profile. Has the person posted, commented, or updated their position in the last six months? Inactive profiles often mean the person has moved internally or out — sending to them produces no reply and burns the lead. If unclear, search Quebec professional registries (e.g., the Institut canadien des actuaires) or recent conference rosters (the AMF's annual Rendez-vous, ICAR, the Conference Board of Canada AI events) where current staff are publicly named.

**Step 3 — Choose role over seniority.** A VP of Model Risk Management who has been in the role 18 months is a better first contact than a Chief Risk Officer who oversees thirty functions. The VP can be a champion. The CRO can only be an executive sponsor — and they will not become one without the VP first.

**Step 4 — Get the email format.** Most Quebec FIs use `firstname.lastname@institution.ca` or `firstname.lastname@institution.com`. If LinkedIn doesn't reveal the email, tools like Hunter.io or Apollo.io will verify a format with low false positive rates. Failing that, send via LinkedIn InMail with the same body, slightly compressed.

**Step 5 — Time the send.** Tuesday or Wednesday morning between 8:00 and 9:30 AM Eastern. Avoid Mondays (cleanup), Fridays (winddown), and the week of August 2, 2026 (everyone in this role family will be in EU AI Act incident-mode that week and not reading cold email).

## Follow-up Sequence

Most cold outreach to senior risk executives gets no reply on the first send. That is normal and is not a signal to escalate. Run this sequence:

**Day 0** — Send the email above.

**Day 7** — If no reply, send a one-paragraph follow-up. Keep it short:

> Bonjour [Prénom], suivi rapide de mon courriel de la semaine dernière. Je sais que la période est chargée. Si la conversation est mieux placée en juin ou juillet, je peux suivre. Si la bonne personne est ailleurs dans votre organisation, je vous serais reconnaissant pour l'aiguillage.

**Day 21** — Final follow-up, value-add only. Send a short note attaching one article or analysis the recipient would find genuinely useful — for example, the most recent Modulos analysis on the post-trilogue AI Act timeline, or a summary of the OSFI E-23 examiner FAQ. Do not include a CTA. The point is to demonstrate that you read what they read.

If no reply after Day 21, **stop**. Move the contact to a six-month retouch list. Going past three sends without engagement damages future deliverability and burns the relationship.
