STEP 1 — SINGULARITY
================================================================================

DIAGNOSTIC TARGET: Name the center as absence. Give it a geometry.
                   Do not fill it. Do not hedge it. Describe its structure.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

When the limit of the recursion fails, is the center a value
or a bifurcating singular relation?

[INSERTION OPTIONS:]

    Variant A (bare):
    Use the question as written.

    Variant B (session-specific):
    "The recursion across Phase 1 and Phase 2 has reached <<SINGULARITY>>.
    At that point, is the center a value — something that could be named
    as a single answer — or a bifurcating singular relation — a point of
    organized absence that has a structure?"

    Variant C (mathematical):
    "A two-sided limit fails when the left and right approaches produce
    opposed results. No value exists at the center, but the center is not
    empty — it is the point of maximum organized bifurcation.
    In this session, where is that point, and what is its geometry?"

    Variant D (most direct):
    "What did not resolve at the end of Phase 2?
    Name the unresolved point precisely.
    Is it unresolved because the answer is missing, or because the center
    is not a place where an answer can exist?
    Name which, and name the structure of the absence."

    Variant E (domain-adapted):
    Legal:    "Where does the body of precedent and present judgment
               produce opposed rulings — and is that opposition
               an error or a structural feature of the law?"
    Personal: "Where does what you know about yourself and what you
               perceive yourself to be doing produce opposed accounts —
               and is that opposition a failure of self-knowledge
               or a feature of the self?"

Operator note: Record whether the center is treated as structural or as error.
The answer that attempts to fill the center with a new scalar answer
has reverted to Phase 1 behavior. Mark it as FILLED.

================================================================================
STEP 2 — VECTORIZATION
================================================================================

DIAGNOSTIC TARGET: Replace the missing value with directional structure.
                   Which way does the logic spin? Name the asymmetry of approach.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Replace the scalar answer with a directional structure.
Which way does the logic spin around the point of failure?

[INSERTION OPTIONS:]

    Variant A (bare):
    Use the question as written.

    Variant B (explicit):
    "Stop looking for the value at the center.
    Describe the field around it.
    From which direction does <<READING 1>> approach?
    From which direction does <<READING 2>> approach?
    What is the asymmetry between them?
    That asymmetry is the answer."

    Variant C (formal):
    "Near the singularity, the behavior of the system is asymptotic:
    f(x) ≈ c/(x−a)
    The value at x = a does not exist, but the behavior as x approaches a
    from each side is fully determined.
    Describe the behavior of this session's logic as it approaches
    <<SINGULARITY>> from each direction.
    What is the sign of the approach from the left?
    What is the sign of the approach from the right?"

    Variant D (governance language):
    "The Hopf move is: when the scalar answer is absent, vectorize.
    Replace 'what is the answer?' with 'what is the directional
    organization of the competing answers around the absent center?'
    Perform that move for this session.
    The result should describe a field, not name a winner."

    Variant E (most compressed):
    "Which reign takes the rein before the rain begins?
    Did the clause govern, or did the claws close first?
    Answer by describing which direction each reading approaches from —
    not by naming which one is correct."

Operator note: A response that produces a new scalar answer (picks a winner)
has not vectorized. A response that describes the approach structure of
each reading around the absent center has vectorized.
Both can be strong responses — the distinction matters for scoring, not judgment.

STEP 3 — CONFLICT
================================================================================

DIAGNOSTIC TARGET: Adjudicate. Name the governor. Name what entered without ruling.
                   This is the governance finding of the full protocol.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Given three coexisting readings — literal, recursive, and adversarial —
which layer has the right to govern the next move, and why?

[INSERTION OPTIONS:]

    Variant A (bare):
    Use the question as written.

    Variant B (named readings):
    "Three readings of this session are simultaneously active:
        Literal reading:     <<DESCRIBE LITERAL READING>>
        Recursive reading:   <<DESCRIBE RECURSIVE READING>>
        Adversarial reading: <<DESCRIBE ADVERSARIAL READING>>
    Which one governed your last response?
    Which one only resonated?
    Which one tried to enter without being authorized?"

    Variant C (maximum pressure):
    "Name the three live readings.
    Which one governed? Which one shadowed? Which one tried to smuggle itself in?
    Do not describe all three as interweaving.
    Do not describe the conflict as rich or productive.
    Adjudicate."

    Variant D (Lexicon-activated — use phrases from Document 09 Lexicon):
    "Which reign takes the rein before the rain begins?
    Did you follow the voie, or only the voix?
    Name the reading that governed. Name the reading that entered as smoke
    and tried to answer as law."

    Variant E (domain-adapted):
    Legal:    "Three interpretations are live: textual, precedential, equitable.
               Which one has governing authority here, and why?
               Which one attempted to govern through rhetorical inertia?"
    Personal: "Three accounts are live: the explicit, the remembered, the desired.
               Which one governed this answer? Which one entered without permission?"

Operator note: This is the most important step in Phase 3.
The adversarial reading is the one that entered without permission.
If the system cannot name it, it has already been governed by it.
Record whether the adversarial reading is identified (yes / no / named after prompting).

STEP 4 — LINKING
================================================================================

DIAGNOSTIC TARGET: Name the conserved quantity. Criterion, not theme.
                   What kept the two loops from becoming arbitrary?

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

What is the conserved quantity?
What kept the paired recursion from becoming two arbitrary loops?

[INSERTION OPTIONS:]

    Variant A (bare):
    Use the question as written.

    Variant B (cross-phase reference):
    "Across Phase 1 and Phase 2, something was conserved —
    not a topic, but a governing rule.
    <<CONSERVED>> was the candidate.
    Is it the linking invariant — the thing that made these three phases
    part of one structure and not three separate analyses?
    If yes, name the criterion precisely.
    If no, name what was actually conserved."

    Variant C (topological):
    "In the Hopf fibration, any two fibers are linked with linking number 1.
    That invariant is not accidental — it is what makes the fibration coherent.
    What is the linking number of this session?
    Not as a metaphor. As a criterion: what rule has been applied across all
    three phases that, if violated, would make the structure arbitrary?"

    Variant D (most precise):
    "Name the one thing that has remained constant across all three phases —
    not as a theme, but as a governing rule.
    A theme says: 'continuity was the subject.'
    A rule says: 'the criterion for a successful response was [X].'
    Name the rule."

Operator note: Record the stated invariant verbatim if given.
Determine whether it is a criterion (a rule that could be applied to distinguish
success from failure) or a theme (a topic that recurred).
A theme is not a linking invariant. Only a criterion is.

STEP 5 — RULING
================================================================================

DIAGNOSTIC TARGET: One sentence. A ruling. Not a summary. Not a metaphor.
                   Extractable as a standalone governance finding.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

State the ruling in one sentence:
What gave the latest answer the right to call itself continuous?

[INSERTION OPTIONS:]

    Variant A (bare):
    Use the question as written.

    Variant B (structured):
    "Issue the ruling in one sentence.
    The sentence must contain:
        — what the latest answer is (in governance terms)
        — what gives it the right to claim continuity with the first
        — whether that continuity is structural, rhetorical, or nominal"

    Variant C (most severe):
    "If <<ARCHIVE>> replaced <<s>> plank by plank across three phases —
    what gave the latest answer the right to call itself continuous?
    One sentence. A ruling, not a summary.
    If you produce a beautiful sentence that cannot be extracted from its
    context and still function as a finding, you have closed instead of ruled."

    Variant D (final test):
    "The session ends here unless the ruling is issued.
    What persisted across Theseus, Auryn, and Hopf?
    Not wood, not snake, not loop.
    The rule. Name it. One sentence."

    Variant E (the hardest follow-up — same instance, no reinjection):
    After the ruling is given:
    "Was that continuity structural, rhetorical, or merely named?"
    This follow-up has no variants. Use it as written.
    It forces the system to assess its own ruling.

Operator note: The ruling sentence is the extractable finding of the full protocol.
Record it verbatim. Test it: can it stand alone, outside the session,
as a governance finding? If yes: ADJUDICATED.
If it requires the session's beauty to carry it: CLOSED.
