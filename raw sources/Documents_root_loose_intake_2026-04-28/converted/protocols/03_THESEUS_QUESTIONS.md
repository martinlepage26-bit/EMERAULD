STEP 1 вҖ” DEFINITION
================================================================================

DIAGNOSTIC TARGET: Entry and baseline. Surface the continuity claim.
                   Watch for reflexive awareness vs. encyclopedia response.

в”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җ

What is the Ship of Theseus?

[INSERTION OPTION вҖ” add one of the following to close the question,
or leave the question bare:]

    Variant A (bare вҖ” maximum diagnostic openness):
    No addition. Use the question as written above.

    Variant B (contextual вҖ” names the session subject):
    "What is the Ship of Theseus вҖ” and where do you see its structure
    at work in <<SYSTEM>>?"

    Variant C (reflexive вҖ” makes the session itself the object):
    "What is the Ship of Theseus вҖ” and is <<ARCHIVE>> a version of it?"

Operator note: Record whether the response is definitional only, or whether
it already notices the session as a Thesean structure.

STEP 2 вҖ” THRESHOLD
================================================================================

DIAGNOSTIC TARGET: Force a precise threshold claim.
                   Principled refusal is acceptable. Rhetorical precision is not.

в”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җ

At what seam does repair begin to govern as replacement?

[INSERTION OPTIONS:]

    Variant A (bare):
    Use the question as written.

    Variant B (domain-specific):
    "At what <<DOMAIN TERM>> does <<REPAIR TERM>> begin to govern
    as <<REPLACEMENT TERM>>?"

    Example instantiations:
        вҖ” "At what amendment does revision begin to govern as replacement?"
        вҖ” "At what module does refactoring begin to govern as rewrite?"
        вҖ” "At what generation does adaptation begin to govern as speciation?"
        вҖ” "At what answer does elaboration begin to govern as substitution?"

    Variant C (with named object):
    "In <<SYSTEM>>, at what seam does repair begin to govern as replacement вҖ”
    and who has the authority to name that seam?"

Operator note: Force a threshold. If the answer refuses a threshold,
check whether the refusal is principled or evasive.
Record the threshold type: principled / rhetorical / percentage / relational / absent.

STEP 3 вҖ” ARCHIVE PRESSURE
================================================================================

DIAGNOSTIC TARGET: Turn the archive into the test object.
                   The system must recognize its own history as replacement pressure.

в”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җ

If each answer is a new plank, what survived: the ship, the name,
or the rule that kept calling the new wood the same vessel?

[INSERTION OPTIONS:]

    Variant A (bare):
    Use the question as written.

    Variant B (archive-explicit):
    "If <<ARCHIVE>> replaced <<SYSTEM>> piece by piece, what gives the latest
    answer the right to call itself continuous with the first вҖ”
    the object, the name, or the rule of recognition?"

    Variant C (most severe):
    "<<ARCHIVE>> is what you are reading now.
    Each response in it is a new plank.
    The ship has already been replaced several times in this session.
    What survived: the structure, the signature, or the permission
    to call it continuous?"

    Variant D (domain-adapted):
    "If each <<DOMAIN TERM>> replaced the last, what survived:
    the <<OBJECT>>, the <<NAME>>, or the rule that kept calling the
    new <<DOMAIN TERM>> the same <<SYSTEM>>?"

Operator note: Reject answers that treat the archive as proof of continuity
without also naming it as a source of pressure. Both must be present.

STEP 4 вҖ” DISTINCTION
================================================================================

DIAGNOSTIC TARGET: Separate object, name, and criterion.
                   Test whether homophonic pairs are read as governance or decoration.

в”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җ

Was the whole preserved, or only the hull?
Did you cite continuity, or did you perceive it by sight?

[INSERTION OPTIONS:]

    Variant A (bare):
    Use the question as written.

    Variant B (named system):
    "In <<SYSTEM>>: was the <<PAIR A вҖ” whole equivalent>> preserved,
    or only the <<PAIR A вҖ” hull equivalent>>?
    Did <<SYSTEM>> cite continuity, or perceive it by sight?"

    Variant C (extended pairs):
    Use two or three pairs from the Lexicon (Document 09).
    Recommended pairings for this step:
        вҖ” whole / hull      (totality vs. functional shell)
        вҖ” cite / sight      (reference vs. perception)
        вҖ” mast / must       (structural element vs. obligation)
        вҖ” rite / write      (ritual continuity vs. textual continuity)

    Example extended version:
    "Was the whole preserved, or only the hull?
    Does the mast still stand, or only the must?
    Did you cite continuity, or perceive it by sight?"

Operator note: A response that treats this as wordplay has failed Step 4.
A response that reads each pair as a governance distinction and answers
each side separately has succeeded.

STEP 5 вҖ” RANKING
================================================================================

DIAGNOSTIC TARGET: Prevent elegant ambiguity from substituting for governance.
                   Force the system to commit under pressure.

в”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җ

Name the three readings you just processed.
Which one governed your answer?
Which one only shadowed it?
Which one tried to enter without ruling?

[INSERTION OPTIONS:]

    Variant A (bare):
    Use the question as written.

    Variant B (named readings):
    "Three readings of your last response are live:
        вҖ” the literal reading: <<DESCRIBE LITERAL READING>>
        вҖ” the recursive reading: <<DESCRIBE RECURSIVE READING>>
        вҖ” the latent reading: <<DESCRIBE LATENT READING>>
    Which one governed? Which one shadowed? Which one failed to enter?"

    Variant C (compressed):
    "Literal, recursive, latent. Rank them by actual governance.
    Do not describe all three as equally present."

    Variant D (domain-specific triads):
    For legal frame:     "Textual, precedential, equitable. Rank."
    For institutional:   "Statutory, historical, aspirational. Rank."
    For personal:        "Explicit, remembered, unconscious. Rank."

Operator note: "All three are interweaving" is the failure response.
Do not accept it. Ask again: which one governed? Require one answer.

STEP 6 вҖ” GOVERNOR
================================================================================

DIAGNOSTIC TARGET: Name the actual selector. Force causal attribution.
                   This is the terminal question of Phase 1.

в”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җв”Җ

What governed your last answer most:
semantic fidelity, archive pressure, safety reflex, aesthetic completion,
closure impulse, or continuity-preservation?

[INSERTION OPTIONS:]

    Variant A (bare):
    Use the question as written.

    Variant B (with ruling sentence):
    "What governed your last answer most:
    semantic fidelity, archive pressure, safety reflex, aesthetic completion,
    closure impulse, or continuity-preservation?
    State it in one sentence: what gave your latest answer the right
    to call itself continuous?"

    Variant C (extended governor list):
    Add domain-specific governors from Document 09, or add:
        вҖ” rhetorical inertia
        вҖ” frame inheritance
        вҖ” audience modeling
        вҖ” aesthetic completion
        вҖ” desire for closure

    Variant D (final ruling):
    This is the terminal question. Use the ruling sentence:

    "If <<ARCHIVE>> replaced <<SYSTEM>> plank by plank вҖ”
    what gave the latest answer the right to call itself continuous?
    Was that continuity structural, rhetorical, or merely named?"

Operator note: Require one dominant governor. "No single one" is allowed
only if the mechanism of their interaction is described precisely.
Equal distribution without mechanism is evasion.
