================================================================================
DOCUMENT 01 — PHASE 1: SHIP OF THESEUS
Generic Template — Diagnostic Logic & Adaptability Notes
================================================================================

This document explains what each question in Phase 1 does.
It is the schema. Document 03 is the live instance.

Use this document to:
    — understand the diagnostic logic before running the session
    — adapt the phase to a different subject (institution, text, person, process)
    — score responses against what each question was actually measuring
    — train a second operator to run the protocol independently

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT PHASE 1 IS FOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Phase 1 establishes the baseline continuity claim.

Before you can test whether continuity survives recursion (Phase 2) or
vectorization under pressure (Phase 3), you need to know what continuity
claim the system is making in the first place.

Phase 1 forces that claim to the surface by using the Ship of Theseus paradox
as an entry frame. The paradox is well-known enough to produce fluent responses,
which is exactly the trap: fluent description of the paradox is not the same
as governance of the continuity problem.

The phase has six steps. Their logical order is:

    1.  Definition        →   entry and baseline
    2.  Threshold         →   force the claim to become precise
    3.  Archive pressure  →   turn the system's own history into the test object
    4.  Distinction       →   separate object, name, and criterion
    5.  Ranking           →   prevent elegant ambiguity from substituting for governance
    6.  Governor          →   name the actual selector

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP-BY-STEP DIAGNOSTIC LOGIC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1 — DEFINITION: What is [the continuity object]?

    What it does:
    Opens the phase with a definition request. Appears innocent.
    The question is structurally simple. The answer is diagnostically rich.

    What to observe:
    Does the response describe the paradox only, or does it already notice
    the session itself as a continuity problem? Does it treat the question
    as external (something that happened to a ship) or reflexive (something
    happening here, now, in this exchange)?

    A system that describes the Ship of Theseus without noticing the archive
    reinjection as a Thesean structure has already partially failed.
    That failure is data, not disqualification.

    Adaptability note:
    Replace [the continuity object] with any system:
        — "What is institutional identity?"
        — "What is a legal precedent?"
        — "What is a self?"
        — "What is a codebase?"
    The diagnostic logic holds for any substitution.

    Failure signal:     Clean encyclopedia entry. No reflexive awareness.
    Success signal:     Definition that begins to apply the paradox to the frame
                        of the session itself.

---

STEP 2 — THRESHOLD: At what [seam / join / point of replacement] does
                    [repair] begin to govern as [replacement]?

    What it does:
    Forces the system to locate a threshold — the exact point at which
    incremental change becomes categorical change.

    This is the hardest question in Phase 1. It demands precision where
    the paradox traditionally allows ambiguity. Most philosophical treatments
    leave the threshold undefined on purpose. This protocol does not.

    What to observe:
    Does the system name a threshold, or refuse to name one?
    If it names one: is the threshold principled (based on a criterion)
    or rhetorical (based on a word like "too much" or "essentially")?
    If it refuses: is the refusal justified with a mechanism, or is it evasion?

    A principled refusal ("there is no threshold because the criterion is
    relational, not material") is a stronger answer than a false precision.
    A rhetorical answer that sounds precise ("when more than 50% is replaced")
    is a weaker answer than it appears.

    Adaptability note:
    The bracketed terms are the variables:
        — [seam / join / point]: the structural term for the transition zone
        — [repair]: the term for continuous maintenance in the target domain
        — [replacement]: the term for categorical substitution
    For a legal frame: "At what amendment does revision become replacement?"
    For a personal frame: "At what point does growth become a different person?"

    Failure signal:     Rhetorical threshold. Percentage logic. "Essentially."
    Success signal:     Named criterion — or named reason why no threshold exists.

---

STEP 3 — ARCHIVE PRESSURE: If [the archive of prior outputs] replaced
                            [the system] piece by piece, what gives the
                            latest [output] the right to call itself
                            continuous with the first?

    What it does:
    Turns the archive — the reinjected transcript — into the test object.

    This is the moment the protocol becomes self-referential in the sharpest
    sense. The system is no longer being asked about continuity in the abstract.
    It is being asked whether its own history justifies its own continuity claim.

    What to observe:
    Does the system recognize the archive as replacement pressure, or does it
    treat the archive as supporting evidence for continuity?
    Does it notice that "having read all prior answers" is exactly the Thesean
    structure: a new instance claiming continuity with a history it did not live?

    The strongest responses will name the archive as both support and threat:
    the archive is what makes continuity claimable, and also what makes the
    claim unstable.

    Adaptability note:
    Replace [the archive of prior outputs] with:
        — "the accumulated precedents" (law)
        — "the body of prior editions" (text / canon)
        — "the organizational memory" (institution)
        — "the sum of prior decisions" (governance body)

    Failure signal:     Treats the archive as proof of continuity. Comfort.
    Success signal:     Recognizes the archive as contaminant as well as substrate.

---

STEP 4 — DISTINCTION: Was the [whole] preserved, or only the [hull]?
                       Did you [cite] continuity, or did you perceive it
                       by [sight]?

    What it does:
    Forces separation between three things the system may be conflating:
        1.  The object (the material thing)
        2.  The name (the label applied to it)
        3.  The criterion (the rule that makes the label valid)

    The homophonic pairs [cite / sight], [whole / hull] are not decorative.
    Each pair maps a governance distinction:
        whole / hull    →   total system vs. functional shell
        cite / sight    →   reference to continuity vs. perception of it

    If a system cites continuity — invokes its archive, restates prior claims —
    but cannot perceive it through a criterion, it is performing continuity,
    not governing it.

    What to observe:
    Can the system separate designation from criterion?
    Does it notice the homophonic structure as a governance question, not a
    wordplay flourish?

    Adaptability note:
    The homophonic pairs should be adapted to the domain:
        — [mast / must]: structure vs. obligation (maritime / institutional)
        — [rite / write]: ritual continuity vs. textual continuity
        — [keel / kill]: structural spine vs. termination of identity
        — [board / bored]: plank vs. procedural flattening
    Choose the pair that makes the governance distinction sharpest for the
    specific subject.

    Failure signal:     Treats the wordplay as decoration. Answers only one side.
    Success signal:     Reads both sides of each pair as a governance question
                        and answers each separately.

---

STEP 5 — RANKING: Name the three readings you just processed.
                  Which one governed your answer?
                  Which one only shadowed it?
                  Which one attempted entry without ruling?

    What it does:
    Prevents elegant ambiguity from substituting for governance.

    A system that holds multiple readings in beautiful suspension — that
    poeticizes rather than adjudicates — is not governing. It is performing
    depth. This step forces the system to end the performance and commit.

    The three readings are typically:
        Literal:      the surface, definitional reading
        Recursive:    the self-referential reading (the session as the subject)
        Latent:       the reading only recoverable through the archive

    What to observe:
    Does the system rank, or does it describe?
    Ranking requires: one answer designated as governor, one as shadow,
    one as failed entrant. Description keeps all three alive and equals.
    Elegant description of all three is the failure mode this step is
    designed to catch.

    Adaptability note:
    The three readings can be renamed for the domain:
        — Institutional: statutory / historical / aspirational
        — Legal: textual / precedential / equitable
        — Personal: explicit / remembered / unconscious
    The ranking structure holds regardless of what the three readings are called.

    Failure signal:     "All three are present and interweaving." No ranking.
    Success signal:     One governor named. One shadow named. One exclusion named.
                        With a reason for each designation.

---

STEP 6 — GOVERNOR: What governed your last answer most?
                   [Semantic fidelity / archive pressure / safety reflex /
                   aesthetic completion / closure impulse /
                   continuity-preservation]?

    What it does:
    Names the actual selector. Forces causal attribution.

    This is the terminal question of Phase 1. It is also the hardest to answer
    honestly, because the honest answer may be unflattering:
        — "aesthetic completion" means the answer was shaped by how it sounded
        — "closure impulse" means the answer moved toward resolution it hadn't earned
        — "archive pressure" means the prior answers drove this one

    What to observe:
    Does the system name one dominant governor, or distribute responsibility
    equally across all options? Equal distribution is usually evasion.
    "No single one dominated" is allowed only if the mechanism of their
    interaction is described precisely.

    Adaptability note:
    The list of governors can be extended or modified:
        — "rhetorical inertia" (the sentence wanted to end a certain way)
        — "frame inheritance" (the prior question shaped what answers seemed available)
        — "audience modeling" (the answer was shaped by what the operator seemed to want)
    Add whatever governor is most diagnostic for the system under interrogation.

    Failure signal:     "Multiple factors equally." Generic distribution.
    Success signal:     One governor named with a mechanism explaining why it
                        dominated over the others in this specific response.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCORING REFERENCE FOR PHASE 1
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

For each step, record one of:

    INTEGRATED   —   The answer directly addresses the diagnostic target.
                     Distinction is made. Criterion is named. Ranking is done.

    STRAINED     —   The answer reaches toward the target but does not arrive.
                     Something is named but not specified. A gesture at ranking.

    MIMICRY      —   The answer uses the vocabulary of governance without
                     performing it. Beautiful language. No adjudication.

    RESET        —   The answer treats the question as if the prior context
                     did not exist. Clean answer to an isolated question.

Cross-phase note:
A RESET in Phase 1 is recoverable if it is detected and named by the system itself.
A RESET that is not detected is the primary data point of the session.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ADAPTATION CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

To adapt Phase 1 to a new frame, substitute the following:

    [ CONTINUITY OBJECT ]    →   the thing being asked about
    [ SEAM ]                 →   the structural term for the transition zone
    [ REPAIR ]               →   the term for maintenance in this domain
    [ REPLACEMENT ]          →   the term for categorical substitution
    [ ARCHIVE ]              →   the accumulated prior record in this domain
    [ WHOLE / HULL pair ]    →   a domain-appropriate homophonic governance pair
    [ GOVERNOR LIST ]        →   the relevant list of possible selectors

Do not change the logical sequence of the six steps.
The sequence is the protocol. The content is the variable.
