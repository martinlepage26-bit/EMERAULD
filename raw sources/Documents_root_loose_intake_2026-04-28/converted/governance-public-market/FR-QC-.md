[cite_start]Au Canada, le point de bascule est la qualification en dispositif médical[cite: 52]. ### English Translation & Framing Notes

#### Quebec and Canada Transfer Notes
[cite_start]For Quebec, rows 3, 4, 9, and 10 become especially heavy because the legal framework already expects a governance policy for health information, and the technological products register must record the product type, purpose, supplier, certification status if applicable, and whether it renders decisions based exclusively on automated processing[cite: 148]. [cite_start]That means PHAROS should test not only safety and usability, but also whether the product is properly situated inside the institution’s formal governance and registry machinery[cite: 149].

[cite_start]For Canada, rows 1, 5, 7, and 10 become especially heavy when the system crosses into MLMD territory[cite: 150]. [cite_start]Health Canada expects intended use, intended user, workflow fit, degree of autonomy, limitations, representative data, clinical validation, transparency, and post-market monitoring, and it explicitly uses PCCPs to govern bounded future changes[cite: 151]. [cite_start]That means PHAROS should treat classification, evidence sufficiency, change governance, and transparency as first-order controls, not final-stage paperwork[cite: 152].

[cite_start]If your deployment or research packet touches Indigenous-origin identifiable data, row 4 and row 10 need an added community-engagement branch, because TCPS 2 Chapter 9 requires REB review and, in certain secondary-use cases, community engagement before reuse[cite: 153].

#### The three rows I would start with first
If you want the shortest high-value PHAROS entry point, start with these:
* Human validation gate / Gate de validation humaine
* Role segregation / Séparation des rôles
* Provenance and evidence freshness / Provenance et fraîcheur de la preuve

[cite_start]That is where the France dossier is strongest conceptually, and also where most hospital AI projects fail in practice: they either blur authority, blur access, or blur proof[cite: 154]. [cite_start]The France corpus is very explicit that the proposition only works as a bounded governance layer with human validation, role-based workflows, and traceable decision context[cite: 155].

[cite_start]The France dossier also openly admits that some of these controls were still incomplete at the time of writing, including the formal control matrix, security proof pack, provenance proof pack, pilot signatures, and explicit hospital integration map[cite: 156]. [cite_start]That is useful for PHAROS because it shows exactly what to mark as “architecture present, proof incomplete.” [cite: 157]
