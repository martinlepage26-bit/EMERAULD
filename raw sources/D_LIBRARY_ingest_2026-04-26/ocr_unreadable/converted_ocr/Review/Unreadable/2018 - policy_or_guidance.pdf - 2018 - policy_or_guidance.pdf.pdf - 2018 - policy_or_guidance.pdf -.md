AVAILABLE POSITIONS:
Posting Date:

March 22, 2018

Job Title:

Baker Post-Doctoral Fellow

Department:

School of Religion, Queen’s University

Description of Area
or Topic of
Research:

Religion and Material Culture. We are seeking a social scientist who
specializes in the material nature of religion as lived in the contemporary
world.
Some examples might include: religion and any form of material culture,
religion and objects or material(s), religious spaces, pictorial or material
representations of the sacred, issues of religion and embodiment, such as
religious pilgrimage or religious tourism; curating and experiencing religion,
spirituality, supernatural ontologies/worldviews in exhibitionary institutions
like the museum, gallery, etc.; working with religious studies theory and
method in the "material turn.”

Candidates must have a demonstrated ability to teach undergraduate
courses in their area of specialty. Knowledge of a specific religious tradition
would be considered an asset.

Supervision and
Academic Unit:

Pamela Dickey Young, School of Religion

Remuneration:

$34, 600. The incumbent will be separately appointed and compensated as a
Term Adjunct to teach a .5 credit (3 units) University course per academic year.
The Term Adjunct Appointment shall be governed by the Queen’s-QUFA
collective agreement.

Start Date and
Duration of
Appointment:

July 1, 2018-June 30, 2020

Required
Qualifications:

Requirements for the PhD must be completed by July 1, 2018 and not earlier
than July 1, 2013.
