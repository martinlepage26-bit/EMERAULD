I am Glammy Lee, I am a Hairdresser and Creative director. I am Omnidirectional in my artistic endeavours. My first love has
always been fashion design, I pursued hair design as a way to stay creative whilst supporting myself and exploring my other
creative interests. My whole life I have designed, starting at age 3 with a Barbie always in hand, wearing something I created. I
received my fist sewing machine at a young age, Barbie as my muse. I have since continued tailor , and alter my looks and
pieces to stand out from the crowd. I breathe fashion and miss the days of camp, performative elegance, drama on the
catwalk, and supermodel struts. Growing up I was inspired by the likes of Thierry Mugler, Gianni Versace, David Lachapelle,
Jean-Paul Gaultier, Alexander McQueen, Jerry Hall, Naomi Campbell, Heatherette, Madonna and the Spice Girls. I love those
who live for attention and put on a show. Presentation is so important and I am here to put the “show” back in “fashion show”.
I may sound hyper confident, and that’s true, only because I have dreamed and planned for this moment my entire life and
know I am ready to execute this vision impeccably, and to create an inclusive fun and immensely expressive presentation for
this project and future events.

Hair-Play is melding hair and design through both abstract and simple concepts. Pushing limits of what hair, wigs, clubwear,
costuming, innovative, and functional fashion can be. Motifs of play, with toy-like accessories with interactive elements, as
many pieces have extra features and displays. Each piece tells a different story, blending techniques of hairstyling, mixed
medium artwork, up-cycling, technology, sculpture, cultural hair diversity and referencing iconic moments in history and
creating some new ones. The collection composed of 18 looks will be featured in Fashion Art Toronto's 2022 Spring/Summer
showings, then will be displayed in the WIP gallery in Montreal. Additional works will be included to create interactive and
immersive experience featuring wig pieces, hair art, and sculptures. All pieces will be archived and available for rental for
photoshoots, events and performance, and auctioned off at a later period this year. Themes will be centred around futurism,
playfulness, queer reference, cultural diversity, and couture. Inclusiveness is a major staple throughout my brand, an inviting

Art for me has always been about risk taking, like most of life in general. As the loudest and most queer confident person I
know, my life has been a series of risks I take daily just to live up to my authentic self. Moving around a lot as a child, I
experienced something quite difficult : understanding how the world views you. A cold reality for a black gay and hyper
expressive child, as people often fear what they don't understand. It was always difficult for me to find spaces I felt where I
belonged, so I simply created my own. My imagination still runs just as wildly as it did when I was a child. Lucky for me I figured
out a way to make a living from it: to create not only safe spaces but inspirational worlds, where everyone is invited.

After years of working in hair salons with Eurocentric beauty standards, aggressive and competitive barbershops, I decided to
open my home studio space to cater to those who needed a safe space providing accessible hair services. In turn, rather than
doing simple maintenance as I did for years as a hairdresser, I am working to create wild and colourful stylings to my loyal
clientele, 90% of which is LGBTQIA+ and BIPOC. Creating this collection and this gallery exhibition will play a crucial part in
expanding my brand and providing me with the tools to finally create on a larger scale and really show what I am capable of.
From laser-cutting my own stencils to create patterns on hair, to confectioning battery-operated light-up wigs, I am always
looking for ways to push my art's boundaries. This collection will push that even further by manipulating synthetic hair and
aspects of technology to sculpt fashion and enjoy the play of hair.
