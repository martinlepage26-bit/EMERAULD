AMERICAN HORROR STORY: V

Written by

Martin Lepage

martinlepage26@me.com

FADE IN:

EXT. LOS ANGELES - NIGHT - 1995

Darkness cloaks the Hollywood Hills. Thunder rumbles. Lightning flashes reveal a sprawling mansion on the edge of Mulholland Drive.

INT. JK MANSION'S HOUSE - LIVING ROOM - NIGHT

Broken glass. Blood on the floor. A SCREAM cuts through the air.

JK MANSION (40s, striking, muscled, shirtless) stands frozen, eyes wide. His hands shake. His wife, LISA (30s), is DEAD on the floor. A child’s SHOE lies nearby.

JK

(whispers)

No...

Footsteps. POLICE burst in, guns raised.

OFFICER

Hands where we can see them!

JK drops to his knees, stunned.

SMASH TO BLACK:

TITLE CARD: AMERICAN HORROR STORY: V

INT. PATRICK'S APARTMENT - NIGHT - PRESENT DAY

Stacks of books, takeout containers, glowing laptop screen. PATRICK (late 30s, scruffy, intense) watches a trashy reality show on mute: \*THE VAN VEINEM SHOW.\*

The five sisters—VIOLA, RONNA, VANESSA, VICTORIA, and VIOLET—laugh in glittering gowns.

Patrick scribbles on a legal pad: “The Pact. Blood. Fame. Who pays?”

PATRICK

(to himself)

What if it’s all true...

INT. COFFEE SHOP - DAY

Patrick meets his AGENT (40s, sharp, distracted). He pitches, nervously.

PATRICK

It’s not just a show. It’s \*the\* show. Think O.J. trial meets Faustian horror. JK Mansion, the Van Veinem sisters, the rumors... What if the murders weren’t just human?

AGENT

You’re talking demons?

PATRICK

Deals. Sacrifices. Power. And the sisters—they’re not who we think they are.

AGENT

You write it, I’ll shop it.

Patrick exhales, exhilarated and terrified.

INT. PATRICK’S APARTMENT - NIGHT

Candles flicker. A rough outline for a pilot on his laptop: “Ⅴ”.

He begins typing: "1995. A storm. A pact. A murder."

The shadows behind him shift. He doesn’t notice.

MONTAGE:

-- Patrick watching Van Veinem reruns.

-- Tapping through tabloids with headlines: “Victoria’s Hidden Past”, “The Violet Veil: Hollywood’s Most Private Sister.”

-- Typing furiously. Drinking. Whispered voices in the background.

INT. VAN VEINEM COMPOUND - DAY (FLASHBACK)

1995. Vita Van Veinem (50s, formidable) watches news of Mansion’s arrest. She turns to her husband, VICK (40s, clean-cut, cold).

VITA

Do what you have to do.

VICK

You know the cost.

They lock eyes. Violet (14) watches from the staircase, unseen.

INT. PATRICK’S APARTMENT - LATER

He wakes from a nightmare. Sweating. The word “VICTORIA” is carved into the condensation on the window.

INT. ARCHIVE LIBRARY - DAY

Patrick digs through old court transcripts. Finds a photo: Mansion, Vick Van Veinem, and... a young girl in the background.

PATRICK

Victoria?

He pulls out a folder marked “SUPPRESSED EVIDENCE.”

INT. DREAM SEQUENCE - UNKNOWN SPACE

Darkness. Five sisters in white gowns dripping blood. They chant.

VIOLET (V.O.)

You opened the door, Patrick. Now we’ll never leave.

INT. PATRICK’S APARTMENT - NIGHT

Patrick types the final line of his script’s first act: “This is not fiction.”

He hits SAVE. The lights flicker.

EXT. HOLLYWOOD - NIGHT

High above the city, Victoria Van Veinem steps out of a black SUV. A flicker of something dark follows her.

INT. PATRICK'S APARTMENT - SAME TIME

Patrick looks up. His TV turns on by itself. Static. Then a brief, distorted clip of the murder scene from 1995.

LISA (on screen)

Don’t believe them.

SMASH TO BLACK.
