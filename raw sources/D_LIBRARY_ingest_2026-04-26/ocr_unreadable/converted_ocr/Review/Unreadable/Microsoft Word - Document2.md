Re: Application for Executive Director Position
Dear Members of the Hiring Committee,
I am applying for the position of Executive Director at ACCM with more than fifteen years
of combined experience in clinical research coordination, nonprofit governance, and
community engagement. My career has consistently focused on HIV and sexual health, and
I bring both the executive skills to secure and manage funding and the community trust
needed to ensure programs reflect the realities of people living with HIV and hepatitis C.
At Clinique médicale L’Actuel, I currently coordinate multiple HIV and sexual health
studies, managing budgets, participant recruitment, compliance, and interdisciplinary
collaboration. A key part of my role involves drafting and revising grant proposals for
pharmaceutical and public-health funders, aligning research priorities with funder
expectations while ensuring projects remain community-centered. I have successfully
contributed to proposals that secured investments in HIV prevention and quality-of-life
research, giving me a strong understanding of donor cultivation, institutional partnerships,
and outcome-driven reporting. These skills translate directly to the fundraising and
financial sustainability priorities of ACCM.
In parallel, my academic and community contributions have sharpened my governance and
leadership capacities. As a member of RÉZO’s Community Engagement Committee, I
represented LGBTQ+ communities in the design of harm reduction strategies, balancing
grassroots needs with policy frameworks. My PhD research on queer resilience in
Montreal’s communities trained me to manage large-scale projects, analyze complex data,
and communicate results effectively to diverse audiences. As a peer reviewer for
international journals and independent scholar, I have also built the ability to present ideas
persuasively to funders, boards, and stakeholders.
What distinguishes my candidacy is my ability to combine executive discipline with
activist engagement. I know firsthand the importance of sustainable funding streams,
whether through grants, government partnerships, or donor campaigns, and I have the
experience to deliver them. At the same time, my leadership style remains collaborative

and transparent, empowering staff and volunteers while keeping people living with HIV
and hepatitis C at the center of decision-making.
I am inspired by ACCM’s unique position as Montreal’s only English-language HIV and
hepatitis C service organization, and I am eager to bring my expertise in fundraising,
governance, and strategic planning to secure its future. I would welcome the opportunity
to discuss how my leadership can strengthen ACCM’s financial base, broaden its advocacy
impact, and deepen its community reach.
Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,

Martin Lepage, PhD
