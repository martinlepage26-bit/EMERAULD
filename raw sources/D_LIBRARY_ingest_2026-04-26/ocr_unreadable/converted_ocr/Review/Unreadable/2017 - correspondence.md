Montreal, May 11th 2017

RE: In support of Martin Lepage’s application for an part-time Faculty position

Dear Sir, Madam,

I have known Martin Lepage since my arrival in 2013 at the Department of Religious Studies at
the Université du Québec à Montréal where Martin was a doctoral student.
Over the years, I could observe that Martin was a highly esteemed and dynamic member of the
intellectual and social life of the department: extremely committed, positive, and generous of his
ideas and of his time, Martin has given brilliant lectures at many courses taught in our
department, as teaching assistant and as a guest speaker, where he has been highly appreciated
by the students and the professors. But Martin’s activities have extended well beyond our
department: he has been a key research assistant in several interesting projects in gender studies
at UQAM and other Quebec universities, he has given an impressive number of papers at
international and national conferences, and he has published and is publishing a number of
extremely interesting articles on the pagan community of Montreal, with a focus on gender
performance, ritual construction of identity, and queer religiosity.
Within the span of four years, I saw him grow academically, and extend the network of his
collaborations well outside Canada, reaching prestigious European journals and colleagues. If I
was asked to characterize in a few key words his activity of these last four years, I would say:
dynamism, enthusiasm, determination, positivity, as well as brilliant, and rigorous hard work.
I had the opportunity to hear Martin giving a lecture on ‘Gendered Pagan Rituality and the
Queer Goddess of Love and Sex’ at the American Academy of Religion conference held at
McGill University in 2015. I was struck by the finesse of his arguments and the easy, fluid way
