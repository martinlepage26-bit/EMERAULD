Martin Lepage is a Montréal-based scholar whose work engages feminist media studies, ritual studies, and cultural sociology. His research focuses on power, legitimacy, and gender in popular culture, with sustained attention to witchcraft, postfeminism, ritual practice, and narrative authority in television and film. He has an extensive record of academic and interdisciplinary research spanning ethnography, media analysis, and theoretical inquiry.
