Québec, October 18th, 2017

OBJECT : Support for the Candidature of Martin Lepage

Dear Professor Morrison,

This reference letter is for Martin Lepage’s application for a position in Social Scientific
Study of Religion at Middlebury College. I wish to share the experience I have had with Martin
over the last years.
I met Martin during the 2014 ACFAS (a major Canadian francophone scientific
conference) on a panel I organized on innovation and creativity surrounding rituals as social
practices. This conference was held at Concordia University (Montréal) and ultimately led to a
publication with Université Laval Press entitled La fabrication des rites (2015). When I heard
him give his paper during the ACFAS conference, I found his research to be quite relevant and
most accurate in the ways it combines multiple disciplines and area studies: religious studies,
ritual studies, performance studies, gender studies, as well as socio-anthropological
methodologies. I also thought that he was a very efficient speaker who engages fluidly with
audiences from different disciplines. He has shown this ability once more during another
international conference I organized in October of last year at Université Laval (Québec), on the
thematics of rites and identities. He also has shown a great ease at networking with multiple
prominent scholars in social studies with whom I’ve been collaborating for years.
Even though he has not taught a class of his own yet, he was hired in September 2016 by
the University of Sudbury to teach in Religious studies (four course loads that were
unfortunately cancelled), and he has worked extensively as a teacher’s assistant for more than
five years in his department at Université du Québec at Montréal. He was also involved in other
spheres of academic life and community (student conference, journal evaluator, editor, etc.),
1

which makes me confident that he will manage this position with great ease and that he will be a
dedicated colleague.
In the last years, I have also worked with Martin on different research endeavors and
scientific applications. As such, he has developed an impressive expertise on fields interested in
social norms, identities, religious practices and gender performance, as well as the intersection
of epistemologies and methodologies surrounding marginalized communities and individuals.
This expertise is well expressed in his excellent doctoral dissertation, which I recently and
officially evaluated has member of the jury of his thesis, as well as in the many research projects
he has participated in over the years.
In hopes the committee will take Dr. Lepage’s application into great consideration, as I
am convinced his perspective on research and his rigorous contribution to academic life would
bring a fresh addition to the Department of Religion at Middlebury College. Should there be any
more questions about this application, please feel free to contact me at
denis.jeffrey@fse.ulaval.ca.

With my best regards,

Denis Jeffrey, full professor
Faculté des sciences de l’éducation
Université Laval, Québec, Canada
Denis.Jeffrey@fse.ulaval.ca
