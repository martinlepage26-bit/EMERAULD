## Your recurring leak points (corpus-wide)
These are not “errors.” They are the places where readers lose traction.
### 1) Abstraction spikes without anchors
When concept-nouns stack (norme, pouvoir, agentivité, reconnaissance, performativité)
without a concrete micro-instance, your best readers follow, but average reviewers call it “too
theoretical.”
You *do* anchor beautifully when you choose to, for example the ritual/norm explanation that
drops into concrete gestures and everyday examples.
So the algorithm should enforce: **one anchor per paragraph** in high-density sections.
### 2) Claim arrives late
Your paragraphs often build a strong runway, but the “knife sentence” lands later than it
needs to. Your thesis roadmap sections show you can be extremely direct when you want to
be.
So the optimizer will pull the central claim forward without cutting the spiral.
### 3) Redundancy vs recursion confusion
You have productive recursion, where the second pass adds stakes or mechanism. You also
sometimes have repeat passes that restate without adding a new lever. The algorithm will
distinguish them.
