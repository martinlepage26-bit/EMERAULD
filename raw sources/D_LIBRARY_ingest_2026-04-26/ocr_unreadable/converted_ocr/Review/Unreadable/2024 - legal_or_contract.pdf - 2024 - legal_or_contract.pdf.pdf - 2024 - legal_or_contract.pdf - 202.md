CALL FOR CHAPTERS
INFORMATION AND SUBMISSION GUIDELINES FOR THE BOOK

SPACE, PHILOSOPHY AND ETHICS

Editor Dr Bill Anderson
May 2023
The purpose of these chapter information and submission guidelines is to set you up with
what
you
need
to
prepare
and
submit
your
manuscript
to
bill.anderson@concordia.ab.ca.
Any deviations of the guidelines will result in an immediate return of the chapter in order
to bring it into compliance for re-submission.
0) English Language Only. For space science people, this book is a platform to discuss
the subconscious philosophical and ethical implications of their research that have
been in the back of their minds while researching. For philosophers and nonspecialists, it is an opportunity to learn together and struggle to find solutions for the
ethical quandaries that space science, exploration and technology present to
humanity. For those who feel uncomfortable writing outside their normal science
discourse, editorial assistance will be provided. Inversely, for those outside the
science community, scientific and technological assistance will be provided.
1) THEME of Space, Philosophy and Ethics may be approached interdisciplinarily from
the Sciences, Engineering, Technology, Humanities, Social Sciences, Theology,
Philosophy, Film, Ethics, Science Fiction and Pop Culture; though this list is not
intended to be exhaustive nor restrictive. Some specific ideas for exploration are as
follows.
❖ Space, Money and Ethics
❖ Who Owns Space?
❖ Space Ethics in Pop Culture
❖ The Meaning of Space Exploration
❖ The Teleos (Purpose) for Space Exploration
❖ Theology of Space
❖ Search for Extraterrestrial Life
❖ Space Ethics in Film
❖ Artificial Intelligence and Ethical Use in Space
❖ Safe Space of Video Games for the Ethical Implications of Space Exploration
❖ Theological Implications of Extraterrestrial Life
❖ The Relationship between Physics, Metaphysics and Ethics

2) Deadline for Chapter Submission 31st December 2023
3) Style and Format
3.1 Must conform rigorously to the Chicago Manual of Style 17th Edition
3.2 Margins
3.2.1 Top, Bottom and Right = 1 Inch
3.2.2 Left = 1.3 Inches
3.2.3 Mirror Margins
3.3 Justified Margins All Around
3.4 Main Text Font = Times New Roman 11
3.5 Double Spaced
3.6 NB FOOTNOTES ONLY for referencing
3.7 Footnote Format
3.7.1 Font = Times New Roman 10
3.7.2 NO TAB but one space after numeral
3.7.3 Single Spaced
3.8 Bibliography at the end of chapter
4) Word Count 5,000-8,000 Words
4.1 May be less with the minimum being 2,500 Words
4.2 Could go higher under certain circumstances
4.3 But try to stick to these limits
5) Block Quotations Used for 35 Words to a Maximum of 50 Words
6) Direct Quotations
6.1 Cannot exceed 50 words in a block or in-body quotation
6.2 Cannot exceed 300 words total of a single reference in the whole chapter
6.3 Summarize or paraphrase the essential idea or ideas (with due citation along
the way) and then use a direct quotation to pull it altogether or punctuate the
point(s).
7) Word Processing Version
7.1 Microsoft Word
7.2 Do not PDF your chapter
7.2.1 Need to allow the Editor and Reviewer(s) to open it up and comment
7.2.2 Under no circumstances will your written work be used elsewhere for other
purposes
7.3 Microsoft Word INDEXING TOOL
7.3.1 To be used when you receive the Editor’s Draft in May 2024
7.3.2 Do not index your original chapter until this stage
8) Avoid Photos, Images, Illustrations and Charts If Possible
8.0 If you include them . . . .
8.2 Must comply with VP Guidelines (attached)
8.2 Must have written consent by other publishers, photographers and artists
9) Do Not Use Electronic Formatting for Citations for Footnotes and Bibliography
10) Do Not Use Headers or Footers

11) Use 1st Person “I/We” (latter only if co-authored)
12) Timeline
12.0 Should expect ebb and flow to the submission, editing and revision process . . . .
12.1 Submit chapter by 31st December 2023 OR EARLIER if possible
12.2 Will receive a “Receipt of Chapter” email to confirm its been received by the
Editor
12.3 Expect a response and or editorial revisions by 28th February 2024
12.4 At that time, you will be required to put the chapter into “House Format” (which
has a few minor variations from the Chicago Style) and a template provided
12.5 Re-Submit revised chapter by 31st March 2024
12.6 Contract including one free copy of the book will be sent to you after your
revisions are approved
12.7 Editor’s Draft will be sent to you some time in May 2024
12.8 Author should be available during May-July 2024 by email to respond to
questions and provide revisions as the book is pulled together . . . .
12.9 Deadline for final submission of completed book to VP 30th June 2024
13) Philosophy of Editing
13.0 Minimalist Approach If Possible
13.1 Presume that the authors are the experts in their field and for their written work
13.2 Therefore, I will not change style, content, words, grammar, syntax or
punctuation unless absolutely necessary
13.3 Hands-On Where Necessary eg will provide all the help necessary for those
authors whose first language is not English
13.4 For those who feel uncomfortable writing outside their normal science
discourse, editorial assistance will be provided.
13.5 Inversely, for those outside the science community, scientific and technological
assistance will be provided.
14) Editing Process
14.1 Revise your chapter as per the Review(s)’ Comments in the Word Review Tool in
the margins
14.2 Return your revised chapter in House Format and without the Comments in it
14.3 Editor’s Draft will then be sent to you some time in May 2024
14.4 Editor’s Final Draft will then be sent to you and you should INDEX it according to
VP Guidelines at that time
14.5 VP Proofs will then be sent to you some time in July 2024
NB Submitting a chapter for review is NOT a guarantee that it will be accepted for
publication. Each chapter will be sent out for double-blind review to scholars with
expertise on the particular topic under consideration.
