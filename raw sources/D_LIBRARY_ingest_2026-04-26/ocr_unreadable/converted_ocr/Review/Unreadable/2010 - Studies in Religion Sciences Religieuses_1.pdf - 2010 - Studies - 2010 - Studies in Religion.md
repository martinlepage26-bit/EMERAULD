Contemporary Paganism and gender in Montreal

Wicca is the most influential Pagan tradition in the province of Québec and in the city
of Montréal, but it is certainly not the only one (Gagnon, 2003; Charbonneau, 2008;
Roberts, 2009). What I have witnessed in Montreal, however, is a far less structured
form of Wicca than what can be seen in the United States or in other parts of Canada
such as, for example, the Wiccan Church of Canada in Toronto (Marron, 1989). During
my four years of studies on the field, I have heard of a maximum number of four or five
covens in the “official” hierarchical and initiatory sense, with a high priest and a high
