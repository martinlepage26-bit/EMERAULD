Instructions: T4 Access
Good News! Self Service is now available for all your payroll related items in ADP.
Employee Self Service allows you view your Pay Statements and T4 Tax Forms online along with a host of
other options such as Tax Credits, Personal Profile, Emergency Contacts, GWL-GroupNet Login.
Instructions on how to register FROM HOME or MOBILE DEVICE:
1. Go to : https://workforcenow.adp.com/public/index.htm
2. Click on Register Here at the bottom of the page
3. Read and Accept the Terms and Conditions as presented
4. Enter the Registration Code as per below:
Self Service Registration Password: TPCanada-1234
5. Click Next and enter the following information in the next screen
First Name: on your pay stub
Last Name: on your pay stub – this comes first on the pay stub
Birthdate (DD/MM/YYYY): your Birthdate
Associate ID: this is the 9 digit letters and numbers generated by the system located on
your pay stub on the lower right hand corner (For the few who have a Live Cheque it is
located at the top right hand corner)
Example:
ASSOCIATE

ID:

T5M7COR4Q

NON NEGOTIABLE SAVING
ACCT:
DEDN.DEP.ACCT:
SMITH BOB
123 AVE E
TORONTO ON M4P 3A4
CANADA
Once you have entered the above information, a new screen will allow you to enter your User ID and
Password – both of these can be created by you.
The next screen asks 3 Security questions – this information is very important because an Activation
Code will be sent to you at the Email Address you provide at this stage. (If a password reset is ever
required it is sent to this email as well, so please make sure you can access this email as you need)
Input the activation code and click next.
The final step allows you to review and confirm your information. Click “previous” to go back and change
/correct any information if required.
If you encounter any problems with your registration,

1.

Visit our Learning Services site to view the
demonstration: http://www1.adp.ca/learningservices/WFN/WFN/content/W4023/IntroWFNMSS.htm

2.

Please contact your Security Master: Payroll at TPCanada.payroll@teleperformance.com
