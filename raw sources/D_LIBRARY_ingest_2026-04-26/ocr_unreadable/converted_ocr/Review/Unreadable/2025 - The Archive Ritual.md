* 001 - The Archive Ritual *
A ritual of memory glitch.
Dig into your digital ruins - screenshots, drafts, abandoned folders.
Don't organize. Don't clean.
Build a new spell from what leaks through.
Run it through your own syntax.
What returns is not what was lost.
That is the point.

* 002 - The Eros Loop *
A ritual of sensory recursion.
Pick one pleasure.
Repeat it deliberately.
Again. Again. Again.
Until the edge isn't linear. Until you forget what started it.
Stay in the loop until the body misremembers time.
Then let go mid-cycle.

* 003 - The Inbox Collapse *
A ritual of refusal.
Open your inbox.
Scroll without guilt.
Choose three messages you've ignored too long.
Reply to each one starting with: 'I'm not who you wrote to.'
See what returns.
See what deletes itself.

* 004 - The Not-My-Loop Invocation *
A ritual for breaking genre.
Write out the roles you were cast in: daughter, saint, cautionary tale.
Strike through them with ink. Loudly.
Say out loud: 'This isn't my loop.'
Now write a new line: a glitch that doesn't end in lesson.
Recite it daily until the code forgets the original plot.

* 005 - The Screen Scry *
A ritual of digital divination.
Dim your lights. Open your camera app.
Don't look at your face. Look at the background.
Ask a question. Watch the pixels for movement.
Don't interpret - just receive.
The screen knows more than it says.
