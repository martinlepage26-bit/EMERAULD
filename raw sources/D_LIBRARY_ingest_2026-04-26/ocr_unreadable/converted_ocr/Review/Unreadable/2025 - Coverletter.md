Dear Mr Wissoker,
I am pleased to submit for your consideration my book proposal for The Scythe Already in
Motion: Buffy, Queer Ritual, and the Politics of Glitch. This manuscript is a hybrid of
ethnography, queer theory, and media studies, grounded in four years of fieldwork
(2013–2017) with queer and trans ritual communities in Montreal and online. Using Buffy the
Vampire Slayer as both archive and method, it examines how pop‑cultural fragments circulate
as active ritual tools, altering the structure and politics of contemporary queer spiritual
practice.
The book is complete at approximately 250 pages (65,000 words). I anticipate finalizing
copy‑edits within two months of acceptance. I believe it would be a strong fit for Duke
University Press’s Perverse Modernities or Sign, Storage, Transmission series, given its
interdisciplinary focus and its commitment to blending high‑level theoretical analysis with
grounded ethnographic narrative.
Enclosed please find:
●​ A curriculum vitae
●​ A full prospectus with chapter outlines
●​ One sample chapter (Chapter One: [Willow Rosenberg: Recursion and Saturated
Grief])
●​ A second sample chapter (Chapter Four: [Harmony Kendall: Fugitive Belonging and
the Camp of Double Life])
I would be honored for Duke University Press to consider this work for publication and
welcome the opportunity to discuss its fit with your list.
Warm regards,​
Martin Lepage, PhD
