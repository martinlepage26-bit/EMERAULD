Subject: Revised Submission – Authority Without Ethics: Ritual Efficacy and Gendered

Dear Editors,
I am pleased to submit the revised version of my article, now titled “Authority Without
Ethics: Ritual Efficacy and Gendered Power in The Love Witch,” for your consideration in
Ritual, Magic, and Witchcraft.
This version reflects substantial revisions undertaken to clarify the article’s core argument,
deepen its engagement with ritual theory, and better situate its intervention within current
interdisciplinary scholarship. Specifically, I have expanded the analysis of ritual as a
procedural system, refined the integration of film and religious studies literature, and made
stylistic adjustments to increase clarity and readability.
I have also ensured that the manuscript aligns with the Chicago Manual of Style and RMW’s
formatting requirements. The article now includes an updated abstract, complete endnotes,
and a revised works cited list.
Please note that while the footnotes in the document were created automatically, I was unable
to convert them into endnotes due to software limitations. To maintain proper formatting, I
have manually created a complete Chicago-style endnotes section at the end of the document.
Thank you for the opportunity to revise and resubmit. I remain deeply appreciative of the
thoughtful feedback and the valuable space your journal offers for work at the intersection of
media, ritual, and power.
Sincerely,
Martin Lepage
