Five Andrew W. Mellon Postdoctoral Fellowships in the Humanities are available for
the 2018-2019 academic year on the general theme of STUFF. Open to untenured
junior scholars holding a PhD (the only terminal degree eligible) who are no more than
eight years out of their doctorate (received between December 2009 and December
2017). Research proposals from all humanistic disciplines and allied areas (e.g.,
anthropology, history of science) are eligible, except for educational curriculum-
building and the performing arts (scholars of performing arts are eligible). Fellows
teach one undergraduate course in either the fall or the spring semester during the year
in addition to conducting their research (must be in residence during fellowship year:
August 1 - May 31). Stipend: $54,590 plus single-coverage health insurance and a
$3,000 research fund.
