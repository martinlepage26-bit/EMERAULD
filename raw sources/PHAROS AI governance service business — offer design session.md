 For companies already using AI but lacking basic controls, policy, risk documentation, or client-facing trust materials.

AI governance is becoming a real buying problem. The EU AI Act applies progressively, with AI literacy and prohibited-practice provisions already applicable from February 2, 2025, GPAI obligations from August 2, 2025, and many high-risk and transparency obligations scheduled from August 2, 2026. ([AI Act Service Desk](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/eu-ai-act-implementation-timeline?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Timeline for the Implementation of the EU AI Act | AI Act Service Desk")) NIST’s AI RMF gives a practical voluntary structure around Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage, and its playbook gives implementation actions organizations can use. ([NIST](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework?utm_source=chatgpt.com "AI Risk Management Framework | NIST")) In Canada, Bill C-27/AIDA did not become law in the 44th Parliament, but privacy, human rights, sectoral, and Quebec Law 25 style obligations still create governance pressure for businesses using AI. ([Parlement du Canada](https://www.parl.ca/legisinfo/en/bill/44-1/c-27?utm_source=chatgpt.com "C-27 (44-1) - LEGISinfo - Parliament of Canada"))

The strongest freelance offer:

**AI Trust Sprint for Small Businesses Using AI**

You help small businesses, agencies, consultants, SaaS founders, and online creators turn messy AI usage into a clean, defensible trust system.

The painful problem: they are using ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, AI writing tools, automation tools, customer-data workflows, or AI-generated content without knowing what risks they are creating. They may not need a lawyer or full compliance program yet. They need a simple, credible, client-ready AI usage policy, risk register, disclosure language, vendor checklist, and internal operating rules.

Positioning:

“I help small businesses and creator-led companies use AI without looking reckless to clients, partners, platforms, or regulators.”

You do not need a big brand. You need a sharp point of view, sample artifacts, and a repeatable diagnostic. Position yourself as an operator who translates AI governance into business-ready decisions, not as an abstract ethics consultant.

Your authority stack:

“Trust and AI Governance operator.”  
“Builds practical AI policies, risk controls, and client-facing trust materials.”  
“Uses NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act readiness logic, and privacy-by-design principles as source frameworks.”  
“Designed for small teams that need clear action, not a 90-page compliance report.”

Pricing tiers:

**Tier 1: AI Use Policy Kit, $750 to $1,500**

For solo creators, consultants, coaches, agencies, and freelancers.

Deliverables: 1 AI usage policy, 1 client-facing AI disclosure, 1 internal do/don’t checklist, 1 data handling guide, 1 review call or Loom walkthrough.

**Tier 2: AI Trust Sprint, $2,500 to $5,000**

For small businesses with teams, clients, or recurring AI workflows.

Deliverables: AI use inventory, risk register, policy pack, vendor/tool review, disclosure language, staff AI rules, lightweight governance roadmap, leadership summary.

**Tier 3: AI Governance Retainer, $2,000 to $6,000 per month**

For companies deploying AI into operations, customer service, content, HR, marketing, analytics, or product workflows.

Deliverables: monthly risk reviews, vendor assessments, policy updates, incident response support, client/security questionnaire support, staff enablement, AI governance documentation upkeep.

Premium day-rate offer:

**$1,000 AI Trust Day**

Audience: agencies, creator businesses, B2B consultants, small SaaS firms, fractional executives, and service businesses using AI in client work.

Promise: “In one day, I’ll turn your AI usage into a client-ready trust system.”

Prep system: intake form, tool inventory, data types handled, client-facing AI use cases, current policies, examples of AI-generated outputs, business risks, and any client objections they have received.

Delivery flow:

Morning: map their AI use cases and classify risks.

Midday: draft their AI usage policy, client disclosure, staff checklist, and risky-use prohibitions.

Afternoon: create a tool/vendor register, risk register, escalation rules, and a 30-day remediation plan.

End of day: send a polished trust pack plus a 10-minute walkthrough video.

Follow-up: offer a $2,000/month maintenance retainer or a $3,500 implementation sprint.

Cold outreach message:

Hi [Name],

I noticed you’re using AI in your content/client work, which is smart, but most small teams are missing the basic trust layer around it: what data can go into AI tools, what gets reviewed by a human, what clients are told, and what happens if something goes wrong.

I help small businesses create a simple AI Trust Pack: usage policy, client disclosure, risk checklist, and internal rules your team can actually follow.

It is not a legal memo or a giant compliance project. It is a practical one-day cleanup that makes your AI use look intentional, professional, and defensible.

Would it be useful if I sent over a quick example of what that pack includes?

Best,  
Martin

DM version:

Noticed you’re using AI in your business, which is smart. One gap I see with a lot of small teams is that they don’t have clear rules for client disclosure, data handling, human review, or risky AI use.

I build lightweight AI Trust Packs for small businesses: policy, disclosure language, internal checklist, and risk register. Practical, not legal-theory heavy.

Want me to send a quick example of what one looks like?

How ChatGPT does 70% of the work:

Use ChatGPT for first-pass drafting, synthesis, checklists, intake analysis, and client-specific customization. You should personally own judgment, risk calls, source interpretation, client communication, and final quality control.

Workflow:

First, feed ChatGPT the client intake and ask it to produce an AI use inventory. Then ask it to classify use cases by risk: low, medium, high, and unacceptable. Next, generate a policy draft based on their actual use cases. Then create client-facing disclosure language in plain English. After that, generate a risk register with controls, owner, severity, likelihood, and next action. Finally, ask ChatGPT to produce a short executive summary and implementation checklist.

Core delivery prompt:

“Act as my AI governance analyst. Based on this client intake, create: 1. AI use inventory, 2. risk classification, 3. top 10 governance gaps, 4. draft AI usage policy, 5. client-facing AI disclosure, 6. internal checklist, 7. 30-day remediation plan. Use practical language for a small business. Flag assumptions separately from verified information. Do not invent legal obligations.”

Seven-day paid challenge:

**Challenge name:** AI Trust in 7 Days

Price: $47 to $97.

Audience: small business owners, creators, consultants, agencies, and coaches using AI in public or client-facing work.

Transformation: from “we use AI casually and hope nothing goes wrong” to “we have a clear AI policy, disclosure, checklist, and risk map.”

Day 1: Map every AI tool and use case.

Day 2: Decide what data can and cannot go into AI tools.

Day 3: Create human review rules.

Day 4: Write client-facing AI disclosure language.

Day 5: Build a simple AI risk register.

Day 6: Create team or contractor rules.

Day 7: Publish the AI Trust Pack and implementation checklist.

Fastest free-traffic strategy: post daily on LinkedIn and X around “AI mistakes small businesses are making,” DM people who engage, comment on agency/creator posts about AI, and offer a free “AI trust gap checklist” as the entry point.

30-day cash plan aiming at $10,000:

The math is simple: sell four $2,500 AI Trust Sprints, ten $1,000 AI Trust Days, or a blended path such as two $2,500 sprints plus five $1,000 days.

Days 1 to 3: Build the offer, one-page landing page, intake form, Stripe payment link, sample AI Trust Pack, and outreach list of 150 prospects.

Days 4 to 10: Publish one strong post per day, send 20 targeted DMs/emails per day, and offer 10 free mini-audits. Goal: book 5 to 10 diagnostic conversations by async Loom or email, not sales calls.

Days 11 to 17: Sell the $1,000 AI Trust Day as the fast, low-friction entry offer. Deliver the first clients manually, capture objections, improve templates, and ask for testimonials.

Days 18 to 24: Raise the main offer to $2,500 for a full AI Trust Sprint. Use before/after examples: messy AI use inventory becomes polished trust pack.

Days 25 to 30: Convert satisfied buyers into retainers. Offer “monthly AI governance maintenance” at $2,000/month.

What to automate: intake forms, Stripe checkout, scheduling, Notion or Google Docs templates, first-pass policy drafts, risk-register generation, outreach personalization, follow-up messages, and client summary reports.

What not to automate: final risk judgment, client-specific recommendations, legal boundary statements, sensitive data handling decisions, and anything that implies certified legal advice.

Three online service offers you can launch immediately:

**1. AI Trust Pack for Creators and Agencies**

Price: $750 to $1,500.

For: creators, consultants, coaches, social media agencies, ghostwriters, and AI-assisted service providers.

Find clients: LinkedIn, X, YouTube creators, newsletter operators, agencies advertising AI-assisted work.

Deliver with AI: use ChatGPT to analyze workflows, draft disclosure language, generate internal checklists, and tailor templates.

Scale to $10K/month: sell 8 to 12 packs monthly, then upsell implementation or review retainers.

**2. AI Vendor and Tool Risk Review**

Price: $1,500 to $3,500.

For: small businesses adopting AI tools for CRM, marketing, HR, customer support, analytics, or content.

Find clients: companies posting about “AI automation,” “AI stack,” “new AI workflow,” or hiring AI consultants.

Deliver with AI: use ChatGPT to summarize vendor terms, build a risk table, compare data-handling concerns, and produce buyer recommendations. You still verify source text and avoid legal overclaiming.

Scale to $10K/month: four reviews at $2,500 each.

**3. AI Governance Starter System**

Price: $5,000 to $10,000.

For: funded startups, small SaaS companies, professional services firms, and agencies selling into regulated or enterprise clients.

Find clients: founders discussing enterprise sales, procurement friction, security questionnaires, AI features, or compliance blockers.

Deliver with AI: generate policy drafts, use-case maps, control checklists, staff training content, risk-register drafts, and client FAQ materials.

Scale to $10K/month: one to two projects monthly, plus maintenance retainers.

The strongest first move: launch the $1,000 AI Trust Day, not the 30-day $10K fantasy. It is specific, urgent, easy to explain, and credible from your background. Your wedge is not “AI makes money.” Your wedge is “AI creates trust debt, and I clean it up fast.”
