# Kairos: the loop pipeline platform (LPP)

Kairos is one engine with six stages, whatever the channel:

**Plan** (what, when, for whom) -> **Draft** (several options in the house voice) -> **Approve** (a person, or automatically within set rules) -> **Act** (send, publish, remind, behind safety checks) -> **Sort what comes back** (draft the routine, escalate the important) -> **Learn** (shift effort toward what works).

Social media is the showcase angle because it is built end to end. Every other angle is the same engine with a different connector and preset.

| Angle | What the loop plans | What goes out | What comes back and gets sorted | What it learns | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Social media | 14-day posting calendar | Posts on X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Threads | Comments, DMs, leads | Topics that perform | Built |
| Customer service | Nothing (reactive) | Replies to routine messages | Email, forms, DMs, reviews | Replies that resolve | Partly built (social inbox only) |
| Reviews | Review request after each job | Requests, review replies | Google/Facebook reviews; 1-3 stars to a person | Best moment to ask | Build needed |
| Quotes (B2B) | Follow-up plan per quote | Follow-up emails | Ready to buy, objection, not now, lost | Timing and tone that get answers | Build needed |
| Invoices (recouvrement) | Reminder plan per invoice | Reminders, firmer within limits | Paid, promise, wrong contact, dispute (hand-off to Aequitas) | Reminders that get paid | Build needed |
| Maintenance / rebooking | Due date per customer | Reminders | Bookings, not now | Best lead time | Build needed |
| Multi-location networks | Calendar per location | Local posts, one brand voice | Reviews and messages per location | What works locally | Extension of social |
| Law 25 requests | Deadline per request | Acknowledgements and answers, always human-approved | Requests from every channel | Nothing, by design | Build needed |
| Email newsletters | Monthly calendar | Newsletters, anti-spam consent as a gate | Replies, unsubscribes | Subjects that get opened | Build needed |
| Website / SEO | Articles by topic | Blog articles | None | Topics that bring visits | Build needed |
| Recruiting | Job ad calendar | Job posts | Applications, sorted but never filtered automatically | Channels that deliver | Build needed, legal caution |
| Ads | Variants to test | Ads within a hard spend cap | Performance | Winning variants | Build needed |

**Constant across every row:** approval first, per-account safety checks, the house voice, escalation of anything important, the audit log, and learning. Only the connector (email, Google, accounting, CRM) and the preset change.

**Kairos and Aequitas.** When the loop meets a real disagreement (an invoice dispute, a formal complaint), it stamps it "POUR VOUS" and can hand the case to Aequitas, the separate dispute-resolution product. Kairos runs the everyday flow; Aequitas runs fair, documented resolution.

**Sales-page honesty.** The five corporate pages in `docs/sales/` describe angles marked "Build needed" above as pilot offers. Only the social angle and the social inbox exist in code today.
