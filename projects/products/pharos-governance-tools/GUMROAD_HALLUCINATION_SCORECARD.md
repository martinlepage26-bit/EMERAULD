### The Five Dimensions (pilot-derived)
1. Source Traceability (weight 0.25) — Can this exact claim be traced to a verifiable primary with locator?
2. Claim Specificity (0.20) — Is the proposition narrow and falsifiable, or a sweeping generalization?
3. Domain Volatility (0.15) — How fast-moving or contested is the subject (e.g., live regulatory transition)?
4. Model Confidence Language (0.20) — Does the output hedging match the actual source support, or did certainty inflate?
5. Cross-Verification Coverage (0.20) — Has anyone run an independent route or adversarial check?

Total = Σ (rating × weight × 20) → 0–100 index.
