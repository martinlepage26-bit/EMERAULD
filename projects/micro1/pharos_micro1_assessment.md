The risks

The offer is cold outreach to a company they know almost nothing about. The range "$50K–$2M" is designed to make you respond — the actual number depends entirely on what you can demonstrate. Most companies that respond get $50K or less. The $2M end requires genuinely rare, structured, high-volume, proprietary operational data.

The IP risk is real. If you license "anonymized operational data from internal workflows," you are potentially exposing the Pharos method — the recursive governance logic, the audit trails, the adversarial test records. That is your primary defensible IP. Anonymization does not protect structural logic. A sophisticated AI lab can reconstruct a governance methodology from enough workflow traces.

There is also a positioning risk. If you go into this call without a clear articulation of what your data actually is, they will define it for you — and they will define it cheap.

The potential

This is where it gets interesting. Pharos is not a data annotation shop. What you actually have is rarer than labeled data:

Structured adversarial governance traces. 12 documented adversarial attacks, mitigated, with recursive audit logs. That is evaluation data for AI safety and alignment — not commodity annotation work. Frontier labs pay significant amounts for high-quality red-teaming and governance evaluation records.

Deterministic decision logs. COMPASSai has 70+ endpoints and generates structured decision traces. If those traces document how a governance system enforces veto authority, classifies risk, and produces audit trails — that is exactly what AI labs need to train models that can operate within institutional constraints.

The Self-Polygraph Protocol. 23 instantiations, qualitative reflexive analysis of LLM behavior. That is evaluation methodology data, not just outputs.

The realistic ceiling for what you have, positioned correctly, is in the $200K–$500K range — not $2M. $2M requires either massive volume of structured data or a multi-year exclusive partnership. You are not there yet. But $200K–$500K for a focused, well-scoped data package is achievable if you go in with precision.

What the microphone wants to hear

They are looking for: workflow specificity, data structure clarity, anonymizability, and volume. They are not looking for vision or theory.
