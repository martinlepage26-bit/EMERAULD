### Executive Summary

Pharos AI operates two deterministic, expert-governed data production pipelines that generate exactly the category of structured evaluation and decision traces that frontier AI labs need for training safer, more constrained models:

- **Governance Evaluation Data**: How institutional and regulatory AI systems make (or fail to make) decisions under explicit constraints — with full human override rationale, claim-boundary enforcement, and audit trails.
- **Professional Domain Evaluation Data**: How expert systems in accounting, tax, and legal domains retrieve, extract, route, cite, and safely bound claims — including intent normalization, source-authority scoring, and overclaim prevention.

We are not selling an archive of past data. We are selling a **repeatable, QA-controlled production operation** that can generate this data to spec, on demand, in any governance or professional domain you prioritize.

We propose starting with a small, paid Phase 1 pilot so you can validate quality, format, and process before any larger commitment. This is the same model that allowed specialized data operations (e.g. TELUS Digital red-teaming) to win meaningful contracts with frontier labs.

### What Makes This Different (and Valuable)

- **Deterministic production, not ad-hoc annotation**. Same process, same controls, same output format every time. Quality is a structural property, not a sampling problem.
- **Expert reasoning layer**. The human correction + rationale field is written by a PhD-level governance specialist. This is the part RLHF and evaluation teams at Anthropic, OpenAI, etc. pay the highest per-record premium for.
- **Claim-boundary enforcement built in**. Every record documents not just "what was wrong" but "what institutional or professional rule made the correction necessary."
- **Two complementary categories from one operator**. Governance workflow traces + professional (accounting/legal) domain traces. You can buy one, both, or phased.
