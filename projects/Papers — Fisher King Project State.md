## Core Question
How do I turn the paper corpus from a brilliant, sprawling publication field into a sequenced submission machine with one next paper, one next blocker, and one next venue decision?

## Why It Matters Now
The papers validate PHAROS, preserve the intellectual lineage, and create public authority. The wound is sequence collapse: many near-ready, major-work, and method-critical manuscripts compete for attention at once. HENRY's discipline applies here: claim-evidence traceability, venue discipline, and Reviewer-2 gates before further expansion.

## Current Direction
Heal. Treat the publication track as a queue, not a cloud. Pick one primary submission lane and one secondary quick win.
