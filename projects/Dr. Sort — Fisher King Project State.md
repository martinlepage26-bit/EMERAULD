# Dr. Sort — Fisher King Project State

## Status
dormant

## Core Question
What is Dr. Sort now: a product to revive, an engineering archive to preserve, or a methodological failure case that should stay as evidence for why PHAROS is governance-as-method rather than governance-as-automation?

## Why It Matters Now
Dr. Sort is not minor. It is the operational case where LOTUS tried to become automated archive governance and failed in instructive ways. The vault already documents three or four codebases, false-date extraction, duplicate proliferation, classifier limits, and the Khaibit/shadow boundary. The wound is deciding whether to revive engineering or preserve it as a phase-transition artifact.

## Current Direction
Declare dormant as a product, active as evidence. Do not rebuild Dr. Sort until the specific product reason is stronger than the methodological lesson already extracted from its failure.
