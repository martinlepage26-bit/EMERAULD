## Linking Asymmetries (High Impact)

### 1. Creative Work → Governance/Method Bridges (One-Direction)

**Issue:** Novels and creative writing explicitly link to governance concepts, but governance MOC doesn't link back.

**Examples:**

| Creative Work | Links To | Reverse Link | Status |
|---|---|---|---|

**Impact:** Readers of governance notes won't discover the novelistic articulation of those concepts. Readers of creative work won't find formal method documentation.

**Recommended bridges:**

- Add **"Governance-Informed Creative Work"** section to Writing MOC

### 3. Method → Practical Application Gap

**Issue:** The PHAROS method papers (First Method Paper, Recursive Deterministic AI Governance, RECURSO, Loop Papers) are theoretically robust but lack explicit links to:
- Client/practitioner guidance (Runbook SOP exists but is weakly linked)
- Case studies beyond the paper's own storyboard
- Implementation checklists or decision trees

**Examples of missing bridges:**
- [[PHAROS Runbook SOP]] should be more tightly linked from method papers
- [[PHAROS Method — Technical Reference]] is a good bridge but underlinked from governance MOC (only 1 forward link from MOC index)
- No explicit connection from method papers to [[InfraFabric Architecture]] (the infrastructure layer that embodies the method)

**Impact:** Someone reading the method papers can't easily find "here's how to actually do this."

**Recommended fix:** Add **"From Theory to Practice"** section in Governance MOC linking method papers directly to runbook, technical reference, and infrafabric architecture.
