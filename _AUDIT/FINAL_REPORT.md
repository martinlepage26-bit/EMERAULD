# FINAL REPORT

## Outcome

The EMERAULD reliability audit and minimal reorganization pass is complete.

This pass did **not** attempt a mass cleanup. It established a clearer human-first navigation layer on top of the existing hybrid vault while preserving the operational, generated, raw, and historical surfaces in place.

## What changed

- `wiki/Home.md` is now explicitly the canonical human home.
- `Welcome.md` is now a thin orientation wrapper instead of a competing entry surface.
- `index.md` now functions as the filesystem and operational catalog instead of a second conceptual home.
- `_INDEXES/CRITICAL_KNOWLEDGE_INDEX.md` now provides a short high-value retrieval surface.
- `Areas/Writing/Writing and Novels MOC.md` was repaired from a noisy residual hub into a usable writing map.
- `_AUDIT/` now contains the decision record, inventory, assessment, candidate lists, and rollback guidance.
- `_QUARANTINE/README.md` establishes the quarantine boundary without deleting anything.

## What did not change

- no embedded repo structure changed
- no broad link-repair campaign ran
- no mass rename pass ran
- no archive move batch ran
- no quarantine move batch ran
- no deletions were executed

## Retrieval improvement achieved

- one canonical conceptual home instead of three competing starts
- active PHAROS, writing, personal/project, Lavoie, memory, and tracker surfaces are now reachable from `Home` within one or two clicks
- high-value notes now have a compact dedicated index
- operational noise is demoted by navigation rather than by risky movement

## Remaining structural debt

- full-vault unresolved links remain high
- freshness metadata remains distorted by old bulk `updated` stamping
- `projects/`, `graphify-out/`, and `raw sources/` still dominate the vault by volume even though they no longer dominate the entry path
- several legacy generated directory MOCs remain in the vault as searchable residue

## Verdict

EMERAULD remains a hybrid vault. After this pass, it is materially easier to enter as a knowledge system without pretending the operational and generated material is unimportant or disposable.
