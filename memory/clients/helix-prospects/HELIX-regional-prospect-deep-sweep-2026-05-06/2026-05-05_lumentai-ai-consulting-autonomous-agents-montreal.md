About LumentAI

## A boutique firm obsessed with practical AI

Founded in Montreal, Quebec, LumentAI is a two-person senior team — developers and data scientists who care about one thing: building AI that actually works in production. You work directly with the people building your system, not junior associates. We don't sell hype — we deliver systems.

We specialize in agentic AI, large language models, and intelligent automation. Based in Canada, we understand data sovereignty and build systems that respect your compliance requirements from day one. We serve clients across Canada and work in both English and French.

#### Outcome-focused

We measure success by the value our AI creates for your business, not by lines of code.

#### Collaborative by default

We embed with your team, align on goals early, and iterate fast with constant feedback.

#### Production-ready

We build with reliability and scalability in mind — tested, monitored, and ready to run.
