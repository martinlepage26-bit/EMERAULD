# About ORAH

## Our Vision

To make Ottawa a global leader in responsible AI development, governance, and advocacy.

Our Purpose

## Our Mission

ORAH seeks to drive the future of ethical AI through education, advocacy, and cross-sector collaboration to advance transparency, accountability, and policy while uniting a community committed to responsible AI solutions and practices in the AI ecosystem.

Our Story

## About the Initiative

AI is transforming innovation across sectors and everyday life but presents a pressing challenge in ethics, governance, privacy, and data protection. As adoption accelerates, embedding responsibility into AI development is no longer optional, it is essential to protect against potential harm while unlocking its full benefits.

The Ottawa Responsible AI Hub (ORAH), a non-profit initiative, provides a platform to advance dialogue, education, advocacy, and collaboration on responsible AI. By bringing together professionals, policymakers, researchers, civil society, and the public, ORAH is uniquely positioned to lead this movement by building a community committed to responsible AI.

Our Home

## Why Ottawa?

Ottawa is Canada's tech hub, home to over 1,700 technology companies, research institutions, and policy think tanks. The city's proximity to federal government bodies and institutions like the National Research Council (NRC), Shopify, and the University of Ottawa's AI + Society Initiative positions it uniquely to lead in responsible AI and digital ethics.
