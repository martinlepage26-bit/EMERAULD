### Product Overview

Reduce manual review and research across your file with an AI assistant built into your accounting workflows. Caseware AiDA pulls key details from documents, explains analytics and answers technical questions in context with source links for easy verification and private sessions. 

‍

### Features

Linked Document Summaries

Summarize a PDF or ask AiDA to pull key terms. Each point links to its text in the PDF so staff and reviewers can confirm evidence quickly before writing conclusions.

Two Model Modes

Choose AiDA Fast for quick extraction, like lease terms, or switch to AiDA Enhanced for deeper accounting and auditing questions when you need more reasoning and detail.

Analytical Review Assistant

Ask AiDA to explain charts and analytics, flagging trends and anomalies. Copy the narrative into workpapers to support analytical review and risk assessment follow-up.

Contextual Guidance

Ask questions in your checklist or document. AiDA returns standards guidance for the step you are on, helping staff apply your methodology consistently with less research.

### How to get started

  1. **Set Up**   
Turn on AiDA for your firm users. 

  2. **Open**   
Open a checklist, workpaper or doc. 

  3. **Ask**   
Prompt AiDA or click Summarize. 

  4. **Verify**   
Use links to confirm key points. 

  5. **Document**   
Paste AiDA’s response into the file.
