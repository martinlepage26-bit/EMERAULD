This is for:

In this article

  * Amazon Bedrock (AWS)

Coveo uses third-party AI models, including large language models (LLMs), to power AI services within the [Coveo Platform](https://docs.coveo.com/en/186/), such as [Relevance Generative Answering (RGA)](https://docs.coveo.com/en/nbtb6010/). Your use of the AI services must comply with the applicable third-party terms listed below, including any usage restrictions and supported regions.

## Microsoft Azure OpenAI Service (OpenAI model families)

Coveo may use OpenAI model families provided through Microsoft Azure OpenAI Service. Applicable terms include [Microsoft Acceptable Use Policy](https://www.microsoft.com/licensing/terms/product/ForOnlineServices/all).

## Amazon Bedrock (AWS)

Coveo may use Amazon Bedrock to access foundation model families, including:

  * **AWS Titan**. Applicable terms include [AWS Responsible AI Policy](https://aws.amazon.com/ai/responsible-ai/policy/) and [AWS Acceptable Use Policy](https://aws.amazon.com/aup/).

  * **AWS Nova**. Applicable terms include [AWS Responsible AI Policy](https://aws.amazon.com/ai/responsible-ai/policy/) and [AWS Acceptable Use Policy](https://aws.amazon.com/aup/).

  * **Anthropic Claude**. Applicable terms include Anthropicâs [Usage Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/aup) and its [supported countries and regions](https://www.anthropic.com/supported-countries).

  * **Meta Llama**. Applicable policies include Metaâs use policies for LLama model families:
