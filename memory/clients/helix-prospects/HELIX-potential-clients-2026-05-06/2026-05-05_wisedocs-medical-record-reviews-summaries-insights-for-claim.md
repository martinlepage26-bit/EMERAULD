### What does Wisedocs do?

Wisedocs uses artificial intelligence to read, sort, and summarize complex medical records and claims documents. It helps legal, insurance, and healthcare professionals review cases 80% faster and more accurately with human oversight.  

### How does AI summarize medical records?

AI algorithms identify key medical data points, extract diagnoses, treatments, and timelines, and organize them into clear AI medical summaries or chronologies. This reduces manual review time and minimizes human error for claims, medical and legal teams.  

### What is the difference between a medical summary and a medical chronology?

A medical summary provides a concise overview of the most relevant medical information from a patient’s records. A medical chronology organizes medical events in order, from first consultation to final report, helping professionals understand the full medical history.

### Who benefits from AI medical record summarization?

Insurance carriers, medical case management teams, legal defense firms, IME/QME providers, and TPAs use Wisedocs to handle high-volume medical record reviews efficiently and securely. 

### What types of claims does Wisedocs support?

Wisedocs supports medical record reviews for workers’ compensation, auto and casualty, disability, liability, healthcare, legal defense, government, and IME/QME claims.

What types of medical or claims documents does Wisedocs support?

Wisedocs supports nearly all medical and claims document types, including clinical notes, diagnostic imaging, specialist assessments, IME/QME reports, emergency records, treatment notes, lab results, insurance and legal forms, employment and financial documents, and other materials commonly found in workers’ compensation, disability, auto, and liability claims.

How does AI improve the accuracy of medical summaries and chronologies?

AI cross-checks data across medical and claim documents, highlights inconsistencies, and flags missing information for further analysis. This ensures the final output is consistent, accurate, and legally defensible.
