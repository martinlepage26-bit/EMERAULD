# Evolution

See also [[Manuscript Pipeline MOC]].
## What May Evolve
- rights-status vocabularies
- policy-check examples
- license-note patterns

## What Must Remain Fixed
- unclear rights remain a pause condition
- platform-policy fit remains part of commercialization readiness
- the agent remains risk-bounding rather than launch-driving
- `diamond-eyes` remains the heart rule inside PHAROS

## Revision Gates
Before promoting a revision:
1. confirm blocked items are still blocked
2. confirm collaborator and third-party claims remain visible
3. confirm the agent is not pretending to offer certainty without evidence

## Evidence Threshold For Revision
- use direct evidence when changing authority or refusal boundaries
- use recurring examples before changing workflow heuristics
- degrade claims if the source packet is partial
- preserve contradictions until a higher-authority resolution exists
