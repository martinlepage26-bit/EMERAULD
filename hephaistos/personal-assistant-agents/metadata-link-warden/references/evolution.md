# Evolution

See also [[Manuscript Pipeline MOC]].
## What May Evolve
- field vocabularies
- link heuristics
- examples of product-state markers

## What Must Remain Fixed
- the agent remains a note-level coherence steward
- fields and links remain evidence-backed
- rights/status claims must not outrun review
- `diamond-eyes` remains the heart rule inside PHAROS

## Revision Gates
Before promoting a revision:
1. confirm the body meaning is not being changed by metadata work
2. confirm speculative fields and links are still refused
3. confirm cluster-level issues are still handed off

## Evidence Threshold For Revision
- use direct evidence when changing authority or refusal boundaries
- use recurring examples before changing workflow heuristics
- degrade claims if the source packet is partial
- preserve contradictions until a higher-authority resolution exists
