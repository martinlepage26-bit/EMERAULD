## Recurring Figures

These move across the novels and scholarly work:
- **The seam** — where autobiography meets invention (Alchemy of the Wound)
- **The stutter/broken frequency** — voice as infrastructure failure (Broken Frequency, Weather Beneath the Walls)
- **Insistence** — return without resolution (CORPUS)
- **Erasure** — not defeat but disappearance of the governing structure (Le sang)
- **The open circle** — ritual refusal of closure (connects to [[The Circle Remains Open — Toward a Queer Sociology of the Sacred]])
