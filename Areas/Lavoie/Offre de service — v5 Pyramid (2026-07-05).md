# Offre de service — v5 Pyramid (2026-07-05)

> For future Claude: the current Groupe Lavoie offer is v5.0 (2026-07-05) at `~/Lavoie/dossier-v6/offre-de-service-v5.html`, published at offre-lavoie.pharos-ai.ca via the `~/Lavoie/offre-lavoie-domain-proxy/` Cloudflare Worker. The v4 markdown lineage is archived (`~/Lavoie/_archive/cleanup-2026-07-08/`). Every figure is a **base de travail, never a final quote**, until the [[Areas/Lavoie/Contrat PHAROS x Groupe Lavoie — v5 Signature Track (2026-07-08)|v5 contract]] is signed.

## Summary

Pyramidal three-tier offer: each phase includes the previous, and no higher tier is built before its foundation. Offre 1 Fondations (hosting + security in Montréal, operational + financial dashboard with margin **per division**, field time-tracking M4 as a distinct scope line, Barrio materials pricing). Offre 2 Opérations (end-to-end plumbing chain, call triage, dispatch, conversation scripts validated by Israël before deployment, human escalation out-of-script, calibration on real communications, basic tenant portal). Offre 3 Intelligence générale (general orchestrator, advanced GVI: vacant-unit detection, market publication, suggested pricing, lease-date rights checks, external APIs). Delivery surfaces connect to [[Areas/Lavoie/Contremaître — Groupe Lavoie Field-Operations Platform|Contremaître]].

## Context

Working anchors (base de travail): Offre 3 ~14 500 $ CAD; full Fondations → orchestrator ~29 500 $ CAD; recurring 1 500 $ CAD/mois from signature (decided 2026-07-08; X/Y/Z service-definition blanks still open). Setup fee distinct from monthly; prior-phase payments credited toward the next. Positioning line held in every client artifact: *"Pharos AI rend Lavoie plus indépendant, pas plus dépendant."*

Never promised as full automation: legal decisions, sensitive financial decisions, final validations requiring human judgment, out-of-script cases in early phases, instant company-wide generalization without a learning phase.

## Details

The four divisions the offer serves, with priority AI per division: SOS Plomberie (dispatch IA, urgence SEO), Excavations Lavoie (automated expertise reports — the bottleneck the engagement exists to remove, since Martin currently writes every expertise report by hand), GVI with Émile on dispatch (tenant dispatch, ticketing), Clôtures Israel Concept (metric quote tool). The profitability dashboard must show margin per division, not just per technician or territory. The A1–A5 access gates and the 25-question diagnostic questionnaire advance in parallel; neither blocks the other (statuses live in `~/TASKS.md`, client-facing wording in `~/Lavoie/MESSAGE_SUIVI_PATRICIA.md`).
