# AI Governance Offer Ladder - Montreal Quebec 90-Day Revenue Plan

Built from Martin's actual stack: Python/FastAPI backend, React/Expo frontend experience, Claude API access, Cloudflare, and the existing agent architecture (Argus, Hephaistos, Queen Keyport, Hermes). Goal: first CAD 5K-10K in 60-90 days, with recurring revenue preferred.

## Ranked Offers

| Rank | Offer | Who pays and why | What to build vs. what already exists | First revenue | Biggest risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AI Governance Sprint / Trust Pack | Small professional firms, agencies, and creator-led businesses that need a defensible AI policy, disclosure, and risk pack fast | Build intake, risk inventory, policy/disclosure templates, and an export pack; reuse Claude and the agent stack for drafting | 1-3 weeks | Too generic unless anchored to one niche |
| 2 | Law 25 / EFVP Copilot | Quebec privacy leads and IT project owners who need EFVPs and cross-border/privacy documentation without hand-building everything | Build the questionnaire, clause library, and DOCX/PDF generator; existing templates become inputs | 2-4 weeks | Legal accuracy and human-review discipline |
| 3 | Vendor AI Due-Diligence Desk | Procurement, legal, and compliance teams buying AI tools and needing faster comparisons of vendor claims vs. evidence | Build upload, parsing, scoring, and review UI; reuse extraction and drafting agents | 4-6 weeks | Long procurement cycles |
| 4 | AI Change Radar Retainer | CEOs, GCs, and privacy leads who need to know what changed in AI/privacy/compliance without tracking it themselves | Build monitoring, summarization, and report generation; agents do the scan, the offer sells the decision support | 1-2 weeks | It looks like content unless it clearly changes decisions |
| 5 | Internal AI Use Register | CIO, privacy, or HR teams trying to stop shadow AI and create an actual control point | Build workflow app, attestation flow, and admin dashboard; reuse agents for draft notices and review summaries | 4-8 weeks | Adoption friction if it is not embedded in workflow |

## Build Order

- Lead with #1 for the first paid entry offer.
- Use #2 and #3 as higher-trust add-ons for regulated clients.
- Use #4 as the recurring retainer layer.
- Keep #5 as an internal-control workflow for larger buyers, not the first sale.
