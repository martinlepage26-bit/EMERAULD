# Consented Frame — Ethics and Wisdom Gate

## Summary

**Consented Frame** is the governance *principle* (and promotion gate) that checks whether an artifact, decision, or routing step is not only defensible, but also **wise and caring** — i.e., aligned with genuine human flourishing rather than procedural theater.

This principle was previously referred to as the **Diamond-Eyes gate** in the governance architecture. The *skill* named **Diamond-Eyes** remains a separate, operational refinement procedure for artifacts (see [[Diamond-Eyes — Aesthetic Refinement Skill]]).

## What the gate asks

- Are we inside a frame that the affected humans could rationally consent to?
- Does the outcome preserve genuine flourishing, not just formal compliance?
- If the move is technically correct, is it still *wise* in context?

## Notes

- This is a naming change of the governance principle only. The skill name stays the same.
- Where older notes mention “Diamond-Eyes gate” as a governance gate, they should be read as **Consented Frame**.
