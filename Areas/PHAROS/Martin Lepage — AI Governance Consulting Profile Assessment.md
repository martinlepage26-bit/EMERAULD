## Details

### Strengths Identified
1. **Clinical research background** — 3 years of clinical research maps to auditing algorithmic systems, data integrity, and deterministic governance frameworks. Clinical research operates under some of the most stringent regulatory frameworks in the world.
2. **Systems-level critical thinking** — PhD in social sciences + [[Mort et Naissance et L'Ombre et le Double — MA Thesis on Yvon Rivard (Université Laval, 2010)|MA in Études Littéraires (Université Laval, 2010)]] = training in sociotechnical analysis, second-order effects, societal biases, and human-machine interaction nuance. The MA's mythocritical-Hermetic apparatus is the documented substrate of the PHAROS governance method (see [[Mythocritique to PHAROS — The 2010 Master's Thesis as Methodological Keystone|the keystone]]) — a 14-year methodological lineage that distinguishes this candidate from recent-pivot AI-ethics consultants.
3. **Applied ethics in practice** — Can translate high-level ethical principles into actionable, clinical-grade governance policies for engineering teams.
4. **Technical proximity** — Hands-on literacy in digital infrastructure and deployment pipelines distinguishes from purely theoretical consultants.

### Challenges
1. **Translational hurdle** — Must translate academic/clinical experience into enterprise risk management language (NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act).
2. **Short Novartis tenure** — 3 months may prompt interview questions. Frame as targeted engagement or stepping-stone.

### Recommended Positioning
"Applied ethics and research methodology expert bringing clinical-grade rigor to algorithmic governance." Target: companies building or deploying high-stakes AI in healthcare, finance, or public sector.

Key differentiator: can bridge technical and policy teams, can build qualitative evaluation frameworks and deterministic governance models. The cross-domain profile is itself the argument for [[Strategic Storytelling in the AI Economy]] — Martin's positioning is structurally identical to the role this note describes as highest-value.

## Key Ideas
- The academic + clinical + technical combination is genuinely unusual in AI governance consulting
- Weakness is language/framing, not capability — translation to enterprise vocabulary is learnable
- The PHAROS project is the primary portfolio proof-point
- Clinical-grade rigor is the right framing, not "social science perspective"
