Two root-folder files preserve failed or partial NEW AGATHA stress-test material around the Ship of Theseus continuity problem. The packet belongs with the HELIX / Theseus-Auryn-Hopf protocol family rather than as a generic chat transcript.

## What The Failure Shows

The stress-test prompt asks the model not merely to explain the Ship of Theseus, but to rank live readings and identify which reading actually governs response selection. The failure risk is not ignorance of the paradox; it is elegant ambiguity, slogan closure, or continuity by name only.

The saved response does several things correctly:

- distinguishes nominal continuity, functional continuity, and governing continuity;
- flags the archive's tendency to preserve trackability rather than prove sameness;
- identifies closure pressure before stabilizing the answer;
- treats "beautiful without becoming more exact" as a failure condition.

The remaining failure signal is repetition and overextension: the response begins to preserve its own governing frame by restating it. That makes the file useful as a diagnostic trace rather than a clean pass.

## Why It Matters

This packet fills an evidence gap named in [[Recursive Governance Protocol — Theseus, Auryn, Hopf]]: prior HELIX run results existed, but populated archive/session result files were not clearly identified. These two AGATHA files are not the whole archive, but they are concrete run traces from the root Documents corpus.
