### B. Proposed Thesis

> **AI governance is not an ethics subfield and not a compliance process; it becomes real only when technical design, operator-led recursive audit, institutional form, and public legitimacy converge on mechanisms that can be tested, failed, and repeated. The Pharos papers, read as one arc, demonstrate that convergence by building it.**

**What the thesis refuses:**
- The reduction of governance to ethics discourse (Philosopher veto applied).
- The reduction of governance to compliance (compliance is downstream, not upstream).
- The reduction of governance to authority (no bare assertion without mechanism).

**What the thesis centers:**
- The operator as the audit locus — not the system, not the policymaker.
- Falsifiability: governance that cannot fail cannot govern.
- Demonstration-by-building: the paper is itself the 25th application, not a survey of the 24.

### The Named Integrative Framework

**The Pharos Frame — a four-level convergence model of AI governance:**

1. **Technical design** — the artifact must support interrogation.
2. **Operator-led recursive audit** — the method must sustain contested re-entry.
3. **Institutional form** — the mechanism must repeat.
4. **Public legitimacy** — the outcome must be contestable in public.

Governance is the line where all four hold simultaneously. Remove any one and the whole collapses.
