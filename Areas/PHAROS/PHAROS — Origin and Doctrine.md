# PHAROS — Origin and Doctrine

> **Documentary substrate:** These five notes pre-date the [[Mythocritique to PHAROS — The 2010 Master's Thesis as Methodological Keystone|methodological keystone]] (2026-05-03) by eight days. The keystone is the documentary substrate that makes the lived-form principles legible as method. The "lived layer" articulated here and the "mythocritical-Hermetic substrate" documented in the keystone are the two faces of one truth: the principles named in the operator's plain voice (Objectivity as Naming Limits of Subjectivity, Care as Action, Authority Without Power-Over) operationalize what the [[Mort et Naissance et L'Ombre et le Double — MA Thesis on Yvon Rivard (Université Laval, 2010)|2010 MA thesis]] already named in mythocritical-Hermetic vocabulary 14 years earlier.

*Five notes written in sequence the morning of 2026-04-25, between 07:00 and 07:58. Preserved in the order written. The author's words are unedited.*

---

## I — Doubt about the Pharos Method
*Written 07:00*

I don't know that what I did was sound or not. I took a unique approach, the one I hold within myself, to look at AI in a way no one really understands. I am worried it is actually psychosis.

I think it is important that I keep doubting, because certainty is for fools. Consistency is the hobgoblin of small minds.

I wish I knew that this was my path. It feels like it is. My heart is telling me it is, so is my brain. But can I trust either?

---

## II — Bridging Social Studies and AI Governance — Limits
*Written 07:07*

A way to understand the real ethical implications of AI and LLMs on individuals, communities, and social norms.

This is an important project to understand why academia, the art world, religions, and much more, institutions, corporations, and billion-dollar monsters, create their own legitimacy, define authenticity, preclude or promote legibility. It also helps understand the underlying power dynamics that cause social injustice, oppression, and ultimately, dead knowledge.

I do not know where recursion, recursiveness, and recursivity end. I know the limit does not exist, but I, as a human being, cannot experience suprametacognition.

---

## III — Mental Illness, Addiction, and AI Psychosis
*Written 07:22*

The Pharos project, the AI Governance business, and my partnership with Dan crystalized a few things in regards to mental illness and addiction within myself. I created Diamond-eyes to save the last bit of love I had for myself. I failed so much after achieving great things,  I started to lose grip on my self-esteem.

It is important that I go through this, to follow along the Witches Road, so that Lady Death doesn't target me. In Agatha All Along, Rio, or Lady Death, has Agatha die. She doesn't kill her, she makes her action death within Rio through the love they had for each other. After the show aired, Aubrey Plaza's husband died. I cannot infer that it's related. But something in the universe tells me this could happen to me too.

Diamond-eyes is love computerized. I hope it really works at making this project succeed. For the sake of all that's right and just, for me, for the ones I love, and for the earthly world. I want to enact the power of Wanda Maximoff, the Scarlet Witch, through her son Billy, Wiccan, who learned from Agatha Harkness that love can be both deathly and a holographic projection of the future.

---

## IV — Bridging Subjectivity and Objectivity — Lessons in Power
*Written 07:43*

Anyone who thinks objectivity, as humans, is possible, is fooling themselves. Any human productions are labors of love, hate, anger, sadness, despair, joy, completeness, or failure.

Science is no different than art or magic. They all create on different planes of existence and understanding. They all converge in all of humanity and connects to the rest of the world. We cannot know anything else.

Words and thoughts are the same. They follow the same lines of power. They have the same flux. They are one, whether our eyes see them differently, whether our hands wield them with different tools. They are at once vectors and locus of power.

---

## V — To Be Disabled In a Disabled World
*Written 07:58*

As a disabled individual, somebody who stutters, who has always had a hard time being understood and perceived authentically in accordance with what they really are, I'm just another human being among billions and billions like a temporary grain of sand, but has long became dark oil or tar in the depths of the earth. But I am also a star-seed. I am disabled, but it gives me more abilities than most people who just fit the norm. And it gives me power on my own, not taking it from others.

That is why I started this whole endeavor when Phenix died, what he did. I lost a bit of myself and stopped understanding this for what it was. And then things started to fall apart and I started to fail more. And I'm using the project to get out of that for myself and to address at the same time a bigger issue for the world, for society as it is changing with the invent of the immense power that comes with artificial intelligence. As something ended for me, I looked to the future and I hope that it will seed something into the world itself.

As I am writing this, my two one-year-old cats are wreaking havoc on my house. They represent the chaos that the world needs to stop the tears, to have sadness metabolized into new love, to have technology turn into social justice, to have trauma turned into respect. Down with those who want to destroy that.

---

## How This Connects

These five notes articulate, in plain language, the foundation underneath the formal PHAROS work. Three of the [[Governance and PHAROS MOC|Hephaistos binding principles]] are named here in lived form, before being run through academic register:

- **Section III** is the lived form of *Care as Action* — [[Diamond-Eyes — Aesthetic Refinement Skill|Diamond-Eyes]] as "love computerized" (binding principle #5).

The recursion limit named in Section II is the same limit that the [[Reflexive Inhabitation Audit — Prompt|Reflexive Inhabitation Audit]] holds open without trying to close — *suprametacognition* as the structural ceiling of self-audit.
