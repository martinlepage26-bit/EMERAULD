# HELIX — Value Proposition and Buyer Profile

## Summary

HELIX productizes the [[PHAROS Method — Core Framework|PHAROS method]] as a self-serve 4-phase governance stress-test runner — letting a compliance officer run a governed AI assessment without engaging [[Martin Lepage — Professional Profile|Martin]] personally. Confirmed via operator stress-test (/grill-me) 2026-05-05.

## Context

HELIX sits inside the [[PHAROS Product Stack]] between the consulting service (Martin personally) and doing nothing. It lowers the access point for buyers facing [[EU AI Act and Law 25 — Regulatory Pressure Window|EU AI Act + Law 25]] pressure who cannot absorb a full consulting fee. The app is deployed at `helix-marketing.pages.dev` (marketing) and `helix-app-d9n.pages.dev` (app); backend at `/home/cerebrhoe/helix/backend/`. See [[HELIX Production Shipping Runbook — Web, iOS, Android (2026-04-19)]] for the deployment record. The full commercial context lives in [[PHAROS AI Commercial Launch — 8-Stream Deploy (2026-05-01)]].

## Details

### Core Value Proposition

HELIX runs the four-phase PHAROS protocol (Möbius → Transit → Pharos → Trap) across multi-provider LLM endpoints (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google). It scores AI systems on governance dimensions (equity, justice, answerability) and produces an auditable record — without requiring Martin personally to run the session.

**The buy trigger:** A compliance officer or governance team lead at a mid-market Quebec/Canadian firm is facing real EU AI Act + Law 25 enforcement pressure and can't absorb a full consulting engagement. Their options are: hire Martin directly ($$$), use HELIX, or fly blind into an audit. HELIX is the middle path.

### Primary Buyer

| Attribute | Profile |
|---|---|
| Role | Compliance officer, Director of Risk, Governance team lead |
| Organization | Mid-market Quebec/Canadian firm |
| Trigger event | EU AI Act or Law 25 audit pressure; procurement review; internal AI governance gap |
| Budget frame | Can't afford full consulting; willing to pay for a structured, auditable self-assessment |
| Alternative cost | Flying blind into an audit — or hiring Martin at consulting rates |

### Offer Structure (confirmed 2026-05-05, convergent Claude + Codex read)

The first sale is **protocol + expert judgment + deliverable**, not a software subscription. Backend not required for first sale.

| Tier | Name | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| $5K | HELIX Diagnostic | One model/system, one or several runs, live debrief, evidence packet, short written ruling |
| $15K | Governance Assessment | Multiple models/workflows, comparative HELIX runs, failure-mode analysis, governance memo, remediation priorities |
| $50K | Full Engagement | HELIX + governance framework, executive briefing, policy/control mapping, model-risk narrative, practitioner ruling suitable for board/vendor/procurement |

Self-serve SaaS (backend-gated, paid tiers, quota enforcement) deferred until repeated demand proves it worth operationalizing. First 90 days: close through relationship, not paywall. Manual invoice, scheduled session, controlled app link, BYOK, evidence export, practitioner ruling.
