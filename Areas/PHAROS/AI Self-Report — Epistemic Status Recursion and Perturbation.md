# AI Self-Report — Epistemic Status Recursion and Perturbation

## Summary

Methodological framework for treating AI self-report as context-conditioned linguistic performance rather than transparent evidence of internal state. Recursion can produce apparent continuity and reflective depth; perturbation tests whether that continuity is robust or merely scaffolded by prompt context. This note extends [[Self-Polygraph Protocol and Suprametacognition]], [[Recursive Continuity Without Memory — AI Identity Across Sessions]], and [[AI Ethics Literature — Contestable Governance Artifacts]].

## Core Premise

AI-generated self-description cannot be treated as privileged evidence of internal system states. Fluent statements of uncertainty, limitation, awareness, or non-awareness are produced through prompt-conditioned language generation, not demonstrated access to persistent interiority.

The methodological question shifts from ontology to reliability: not "is the model conscious?" but "under what conditions, if any, can a model's self-description be treated as evidence?"

## Key Ideas

- AI self-report is analogous to institutional assurance: potentially meaningful, but valid only inside an evidentiary framework.
- Recursive prompting stabilizes discursive attractors such as structured uncertainty or balanced reasoning.
- Perturbation distinguishes robust structure from carefully maintained context dependence.
- Introspective depth is a property of the interactional arrangement, not an intrinsic system property.
- Governance should regulate system-generated claims by testing their stability under interruption, adversarial pressure, and comparative validation.

## Relation to Self-Polygraph

The [[Self-Polygraph Protocol and Suprametacognition]] should not be read as a hidden-state detector. Its value is that it evaluates whether self-descriptions remain reliable when recursive context is changed, interrupted, or reintroduced. This also clarifies the role of [[Recursive Continuity Without Memory — AI Identity Across Sessions]]: continuity without persistent memory is a governance object, not an identity guarantee.
