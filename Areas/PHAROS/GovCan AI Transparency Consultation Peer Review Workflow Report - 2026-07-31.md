## Draft Review

Top blockers found:

1. The strongest recommendations were present but dispersed. A consultation reader should see the requested measures early and in a compact form.
2. Responsibility allocation across developers, deployers, platforms, procurers, and regulators was sound but scattered across topic sections.
3. Several paragraphs repeated the same "not sufficient" and "usable evidence" argument. Repetition weakened force without adding new policy content.
4. Internal drafting notes and vault source references made the file unsuitable as a public-facing submission.
5. The draft could answer the consultation prompts more directly by making the five consultation areas visible as organizing units and by adding an explicit implementation path.

## Revision Plan Applied

1. Preserved the governing claim: transparency must answer what the recipient can do with the information.
2. Added a compact executive recommendation block.
3. Added a value-chain responsibility section to consolidate who should disclose what and to whom.
4. Kept the five ISED areas as explicit section headings.
5. Tightened repetitive claims while preserving the draft's main phrases and policy architecture.
6. Removed internal drafting sources from the submit-ready copy.
7. Kept proportionality, SME support, protected regulator access, procurement leverage, and future-proofing as cross-cutting requirements.
