# Authority Without Power-Over

## Summary
PHAROS binding principle #6 from the [[HEPHAISTOS Agent Architecture|HEPHAISTOS doctrine]]: **authority is stewardship, not dominion.** Authority that operates without power-over does not extract submission from others to function. The lived formulation: *"power on my own, not taking it from others."* **Authority without extractive dependency** — core concept for [[Authority, Legitimacy, and Post-Sovereignty]], connecting to [[Care, Ethics, and Governance]] (care as answerability), [[Consent and Boundary Frameworks]] (boundary-setting as structural counterpart), and [[Governance Controls and Mechanisms]] (control design without coercion).

## Context
Named in the binding-principles list of `/home/cerebrhoe/AGENTS.md` (HEPHAISTOS Governance Harness Global Invariants). The principle articulates a structural distinction the rest of the corpus operationalizes: authority that depends on power-over is unstable when the dominated refuse compliance; authority that does not depend on power-over does not have this dependency. Equity-promoting equality, not domination disguised as governance.

The lived ground of this principle is documented in [[PHAROS — Origin and Doctrine]] Section V, where Martin formulates it as the operating posture that came after the originating event. Connected to [[Consent, Fraud, and Boundary Protocols]] — refusing to take power from others is the structural counterpart to refusing power taken from oneself.

## Where the principle binds

- **HEPHAISTOS** scope authority: scope-setting that does not silence other voices
- **Queen Keyport** governance authority: refusal conditions that do not punish below the threshold
- **Hermes** routing authority: coordination that does not coerce
- **Diamond-Eyes** gate: aesthetic refinement that does not shame
- **Care as Action** (binding principle #5) is the affective expression of this principle

## Distinction from related principles

Authority Without Power-Over is not the same as:
- **No authority** — anti-authoritarianism dissolves into incoherence; this principle requires authority, just not power-over
- **Soft authority** — politeness theatre that keeps power-over while disguising it; the principle is structural, not stylistic
- **Consensual authority** — consent can be manufactured under unequal conditions; the principle is about the form of authority, not procedural consent alone

## Insights

- The principle is testable: can the authority continue to function if those it claims to govern simply refuse compliance? If the answer requires escalating coercion, the authority depends on power-over.
- The principle is the structural ground beneath much of the [[Refusing Fixity — Aesthetic Governance and Fugitive Authorship|fugitive-authorship work]]: refusing fixity refuses an authority structure that requires submission to a fixed identity.
- The Buffy paper [[For Her Alone to Wield — Buffy Power as Infrastructure]] reads "power as infrastructure" through this principle: redistributable power is power that does not function via power-over.
