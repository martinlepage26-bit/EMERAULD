## Signal 1 — Healthcare AI Governance Gap (High Relevance)

**Source:** [[raw/Clippings/AI is speeding into healthcare. Who should regulate it.md]] (Harvard Gazette, I. Glenn Cohen, 2026-01-12)

**Verified claims:**
- Most medical AI in the US is never reviewed by a federal or state regulator.
- The Joint Commission and Coalition for Health AI issued guidance in Sept 2025, but compliance burden falls on individual facilities.
- Small hospital systems face prohibitive cost barriers to internal AI review.
- The "race dynamic" — startup timelines, national competition — creates structural pressure to deprioritize ethics.
- Consumer-facing medical AI (e.g., mental health chatbots) has no internal hospital review layer at all.

**PHAROS signal:** This maps directly to [[PHAROS Commercial Strategy]] positioning. The gap Cohen describes — "we want standards, but FDA-level review is too slow and expensive" — is exactly the market PHAROS occupies. The Law 25 + AI Act 90-day window is a near-term, jurisdiction-specific version of this governance gap. The healthcare vertical is an adjacent market once the first governance sprint clients are converted.

**Inferred (provisional):** Quebec healthcare AI governance is likely even less structured than the US model Cohen describes. This is a market entry point, not a gap already filled.
