# PHAROS Procurement-Unblock Sprint

## Summary
The fastest monetization path for PHAROS expertise is a single 21-day, fixed-fee engagement for AI companies whose enterprise deals are stalled in procurement, audit, vendor review, or executive scrutiny. Eight closes at $125k = $1M. One offer, one channel. Connected to [[PHAROS Runbook SOP]] and [[PHAROS Company Registration and Security Incidents]]. Entry-layer validation instrument: [[PHAROS Stuck Deal Diagnostic — Minimum Viable Offer (2026-05-26)]] (60-min paid diagnostic, $150–$300, zero build cost).

## Context
This is the business strategy developed in conversation with an AI assistant acting as Blair Enns (Win Without Pitching). The core insight: stop selling "AI ethics" or "thought leadership." Sell "we get stalled enterprise AI revenue unstuck." That is the shortest path from actual authority to cash.

---

## The Offer

**Name**: PHAROS Procurement-Unblock Sprint

**Client**: AI companies with enterprise revenue stalled by procurement, audit, vendor review, or executive scrutiny

**Promise**: In 21 days, produce the governance dossier, decision-rights map, evidence pack, and executive response materials needed to move the deal forward

**Pricing ladder**:
- First 2 clients: $35k–$50k, fixed fee, 50% upfront
- After 2 wins: raise to $75k
- After 4 wins: raise to $125k

**Revenue target**: 8 closes at $125k = $1M

---

## One Channel Only

Warm email from **consult@pharos-ai.ca** to people who already trust you and can introduce you to founders or operators with live deal pressure.

- No website rebuild
- No LinkedIn posting
- No ads
- No free audits
- No custom proposals before fit is confirmed

Boundary note: [[PHAROS LinkedIn April 2026 Publishing Routine]] is useful background for public positioning, but it does not change this sprint's first-channel rule. The sprint still starts with warm introductions; LinkedIn remains a later public-surface amplifier, not the P0 sales channel.

**Email template**:

> Subject: quick introduction?
>
> I've narrowed PHAROS to one offer: a 21-day sprint for AI companies whose enterprise deals are stalled in procurement, audit, or executive review.
>
> If someone in your network is dealing with that right now, would you be willing to introduce us? I can tell them quickly if I'm the right fit.
>
> Martin

## 90-Day Blair Enns Action Plan (< $100 upfront)

### Days 1–7
- Write one-page brief: headline = "We help AI companies get stalled enterprise deals unstuck"
- Include only 5 things: who it is for, what problem you solve, what gets delivered, timeline, fixed fee
- Build a list of 50 people who already trust you and could introduce you to founders, operators, investors, or compliance leaders
- Send 10 warm emails a day asking for introductions, not work

### Days 8–14
- Take every intro call live on Zoom
- Do not pitch first; diagnose
- Ask only:
  1. What deal is stuck?
  2. What revenue is at risk?
  3. Who is blocking it?
  4. What documentation already exists?
  5. What deadline matters?
  6. Who owns the decision?
- Disqualify aggressively if there is no live deal, no deadline, or no economic buyer
- End qualified calls with a verbal recommendation for the sprint

### Days 15–30
- Make proposals verbally on the call, then confirm in a one-page letter
- Keep the proposal to: scope, timeline, fee, payment terms, and start date
- Require 50% upfront to reserve the start
- Targets: 30 warm asks sent / 10 introductions / 6 diagnostic calls / 3 qualified opportunities / 1 closed client

### Days 31–60
- Deliver the sprint with extreme responsiveness
- At kickoff: "If this works, I'll ask you for two introductions"
- Capture three assets from the work: short anonymized before/after case, one client quote, one list of objections you resolved
- Ask for the two introductions before final delivery, while the value is fresh

### Days 61–90
- Repeat the same process with referral momentum from client one
- Raise price after the second win
- Close clients two and three
- Remove any activity that does not directly create introductions, conversations, proposals, or delivery

### Budget
- One-page brief: $0
- CRM in Google Sheets or Notion: $0
- Zoom: $0
- Calendar tool: $0–$20
- E-sign tool: $0–$30
- **Total upfront: likely < $100**

### Daily Cadence
- 90 min: warm outreach and follow-up
- 90 min: intro calls or proposal follow-up
- Rest of day: client delivery or case-building

---

## Key Move

Do not try to build attention. Convert existing trust into introductions, and convert introductions into paid diagnostics for a single high-value problem.

---

## Insights

- The positioning shift from "AI ethics" to "unblocking stalled enterprise revenue" is critical. It converts an abstract academic credential into a concrete economic service
- Price anchoring through progressive raises (50k → 75k → 125k) creates urgency for early adopters and establishes scarcity without theatrical pricing games
- The referral-at-kickoff move ("if this works, I'll ask you for two introductions") is the compounding mechanism. It is planted while value is still being delivered
- This offer exists because the PHAROS Runbook SOP already provides the artifact structure — the sprint is essentially running the SOP for a client under time pressure

## Open Questions

- What is the back-pressure to run when there is no existing trusted network? (cold start problem)
- How does the offer evolve after 8 clients? Product, platform, or retainer?
- What happens if the enterprise deal is stalled for reasons outside PHAROS competence (e.g., pure vendor pricing, political blockers)?
