# HEPHAISTOS Agent Architecture

The operational layer that embodies PHAROS governance discipline at the session and system level: the three-agent stack, skill corpus, session model, communication infrastructure, and authority resolution.

This is the operational/agent branch of the PHAROS graph. It operationalizes [[Recursive Governance Theory]] and [[Evidence Discipline and Epistemics]], and it depends on the tooling and transport layer in [[AI Infrastructure Stack]].

**Methodological substrate:** The agent stack reads as Hermetic-Egyptian theogony when traced through the [[Mythocritique to PHAROS — The 2010 Master's Thesis as Methodological Keystone|2010 MA thesis substrate]]: HEPHAISTOS occupies the forger/Ptah slot; Queen Keyport occupies the Council that judges Thomas's *procès* (MA pp. 17–19); Hermes is Hermès himself; [[Trismégiste — Personal AI Assistant|Trismégiste]] is the explicit invocation from MA p. 50 (Hermès Thoth Trismégiste, "le Cosmos tout entier est un Livre cosmique"); HENRY is the *chroniqueur* (MA p. 17); Argus is functionally analogous to Thoth as scribe-auditor at the weighing of the heart; Gadget is the *explorateur* who tests the frontiers. Each agent's structural authority derives from its mythic role — see the keystone for the full table.
