## What This Bundle Demonstrates

This bundle applies the PHAROS method to itself. The gap-closure procedure follows the same logic as the RDAIG 12-stage pathway:
- Corpus formation: existing Section 6 materials
- Admissibility boundary: distinguishing found materials from produced materials
- Failure harvesting: treating the gaps as evidence that needed to be reconstructed, not discarded
- Determinism hardening: provenance metadata on all produced artifacts

The bundle's distinction between "found and normalized" and "produced post-audit" is the receipts discipline applied to the disclosure's own evidentiary chain.

In delta-closure terms, this bundle closes a specific evidence gap by naming the current evidence state, the target review state, the verification basis, the production/found-artifact distinction, and the remaining dependencies. It does not claim that all PHAROS evidence is complete.
