# Founder Agreement — PHAROS x Danny Stocker Perimeter Reset (2026-07-10)

> For future Claude: the July 2026 state of the founder question between Martin and Dan (Danny Stocker, InfraFabric side). It is **not a signed agreement and not a superseded charter** — it is an open decision with named forks, and it is currently the upstream blocker on the [[Areas/Lavoie/Contrat PHAROS x Groupe Lavoie — v6.x Résolutions Track (2026-07-10)|Lavoie contract v6.3]] (Annexe E cannot be finalized while asset ownership is open). Everything here is drafting state; a `⚑` in the asset registers means **draft-committed, unsigned**, not agreed. Registers on disk: `~/docs/governance/founder-asset-registers-2026-07-09.md`.

## Summary

A `/grill-me` pass over a ten-bucket outline (repos, domains and brands, infrastructure, client and project assets, data rights, model/prompt/agent assets, commercial rights, exit mechanics, public claims, evidence package) resolved the perimeter to one sentence from Martin: **Dan joins Pharos, his IP stays his; the Lavoie engagement sits in Pharos.** Everything downstream of that sentence is still open, and a `/tabula-rasa` pass then had to strip several assumptions that had already hardened into "facts" in my own working context.

## Context

This does not replace [[Areas/PHAROS/Founder Charter — Lepage and Stocker|the Founder Charter]] — the charter is a 2026-06 document with an equity split and ten reserved matters, and it is a *different instrument* from the agreement being drafted now. The relationship between the two is itself unresolved and should be treated as a live contradiction rather than quietly reconciled. Dan gave a draft to GPT, which returned a schedules-based review; whether that draft exists in a shareable form is unconfirmed.

> [!warning] Contradiction detected
> [[Areas/PHAROS/Founder Charter — Lepage and Stocker]] states a 55–60 / 40–45 equity split and a CEO/CGTO division. The July perimeter ("Dan joins Pharos, his IP stays his") is a different shape, and equity/vesting/deadlock has been asked four times without an answer. Do not cite either document as settled. Martin decides; agents flag.

### Asset registers (sections A–G, with `[?]` blanks)

Discoveries from the register pass: `infrafabric.io` nameservers point at one.com; `legipro.ca` is **unregistered** (~$15, worth registering after a whois check); `legipro.com` is parked at NameBright; 20 repos live under the `martinlepage26-bit` org; the local governance surface holds 67 skills, 27 agents and 71 canon files. Blanks still open: who pays for which account, the Forgejo inventory on mtl-01, and the InfraFabric perimeter itself.

### What tabula-rasa corrected (worth remembering as a failure mode)

Four things I had been treating as established were assumptions:

1. Annexe E treated as committed fact — it is **my unsigned draft**.
2. `infrafabric.io` assumed to be Dan's — unverified.
3. PHAROS assumed to be a sole proprietorship — inferred from a blank field, not from evidence.
4. "GPT leaked a draft" — unconfirmed.

The general lesson: in a long session, an assumption stated once gets cited as fact three turns later. The register legend needed the same correction (`⚑` = draft-committed, unsigned).
