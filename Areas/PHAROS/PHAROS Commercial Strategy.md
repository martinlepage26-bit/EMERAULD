# PHAROS Commercial Strategy

How the PHAROS governance method translates into a commercial practice: revenue paths, pricing, legal positioning, business development, and the 90-day sprint.

This is the commercial branch of the PHAROS graph. It packages the method, controls, and evidence discipline into buyer-facing offers and sits beside [[PHAROS Scholarly Publication Track]] as the outward-facing branch of the method.

**Methodological substrate:** [[Mythocritique to PHAROS — The 2010 Master's Thesis as Methodological Keystone]] documents the 14-year methodological lineage that distinguishes PHAROS from generic governance advisory. The substrate is itself a commercial differentiator: a method grounded in 2010 mythocritical/Hermetic apparatus operationalized through 2017 sociology of religion fieldwork into a 2024–2026 governance method is not the same product as a vendor's recent-pivot AI-ethics consultancy.
