# AI Governance Sprint — One-Page Sellable Packet

*Offer #1 from [[AI Governance Offer Ladder - Montreal Quebec 90-Day Revenue Plan]]. This is the first paid entry offer.*

---

## The Problem (for the buyer)

You're using AI tools in your business. Your clients, partners, or regulator will ask: **Is this documented? Is it governed? Can you show me your policy?**

Most firms don't have an answer. The ones that do are already closing deals the others aren't.

---

## What You Get

**A defensible AI governance pack in 1–3 weeks.**

Delivered as editable documents:

1. **AI Use Inventory** — a structured list of every AI tool in use: what it does, what data it touches, and what decisions it influences.
2. **Risk Register** — a one-page summary of your top 3–5 AI risks, with existing controls and gaps named.
3. **AI Use Policy** — a short, plain-language internal policy (1–2 pages) your team can actually follow.
4. **Client/Partner Disclosure** — a short statement you can include in contracts, proposals, or RFP responses.
5. **Session Report** — a bounded summary of findings, decisions made, and what's left for a second phase if needed.

---

## Who It's For

- **Professional service firms** (law, accounting, HR, marketing, consulting) using AI tools and facing client questions.
- **Agencies and studios** that need an AI disclosure for client contracts.
- **Creator-led businesses** building public trust with a documented AI policy.
- **Any organization** that received an RFP or contract clause asking about AI governance.
