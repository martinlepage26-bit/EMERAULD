# Brutal Isms — Art Grant Proposal

## Summary
Brutal Isms is a seven-month multidisciplinary studio project: sculpture, art film, performance, and documentation. A queer Black artist at the height of his powers building independent infrastructure and making work that answers to no one. Target: Canada Council Explore and Create (Artistic Creation component). Related thematically to [[Loop Papers and Recursive Governance]] — both are about excluded material returning under sovereign conditions.
2026-05-12 revision pack captured separately at [[Brutal Isms — Grant Revision Package (2026-05-12)]].

## Context
This is a Canada Council for the Arts grant proposal developed through iterative AI-assisted drafting. The Canada Council program formerly named "Research and Creation" now sits inside Artistic Creation under Explore and Create (as of March 2026). The proposal was refined for jury-facing language: clear methodology, concrete execution evidence, sharply articulated impact.

---

## Artistic Statement

**Core position**: "I know exactly why I make art. I am finally making it for that reason."

For over a decade: professional production at the highest level of the beauty and fashion industries — directing, building, designing, performing. Done brilliantly. At significant cost: filtering every instinct through what the client needed, what the brand allowed, what the industry could absorb.

This project ends that arrangement.

**The declaration**: Brutal Isms establishes an independent studio — a multidisciplinary laboratory equipped for sculpture, film, performance, and documentation — as the site of a deliberate return to first principles. A queer Black artist at the height of his powers, building a space that answers to no one, and making work from that position.

*"This is not a project born from desperation. It is born from arrival."*

*"The return is the protest. The studio is the site of it. The work is the proof."*

---

## Project Description

**Format**: Seven months. One space. Every discipline in active relation, without permission.

The studio is a laboratory. Testing how material, image, movement, and record behave under pressure — where they resist, where they sharpen, where they collapse into a single language.

### Four Disciplines

**Sculpture and Mixed-Media Construction**
Materials: concrete, wood, iron hardware — chains, mesh, chain-link fencing, nails — hair, linen, canvas, Vantablack.

These materials carry histories: iron as instrument of confinement, hair as site of racial commodification. Both reclaimed as building materials. Concrete acts as body. Fabric becomes skin. Metal is bone.

Forms will be rough, imperfect, unearthed — objects that reject the polished visual language of industries spent years serving. Some function as sculpture. Some as furniture. Some with no fixed category yet. All tested through proximity, performance, filming, and repeated physical use. Not static objects. Environments that can be inhabited, confronted, and transformed.

**Fashion and Art Film**
Write, direct, perform, and edit short films inside the studio, among the objects built, under conditions controlled entirely by the artist. Not content. Cinematic statements.

Films are not separate from the sculptural work — they are a method of inquiry inside it. Camera as tool for testing atmosphere, surface, gesture, and psychic charge. What sculpture holds in stillness, film pushes into sequence. What performance exposes in real time, film fixes, fractures, and reorders.

Shot on Laowa FX 38, GoPros, and iPhone rigs.

**Movement and Performance**
The studio gives the privacy required to develop movement-based work with precision. The body is not illustration. It is method. It is archive. It is witness.

Movement research unfolds in direct response to the built forms, materials, and emotional conditions generated in the space. Uses repetition, improvisation, and filmed study to identify a physical vocabulary that cannot emerge through design or language alone.

**Documentation as Practice**
Documentation is not secondary to this project. It is one of its engines.

Video, journalling, archival capture, process notes, and image studies form a parallel body of work recording the evolution of the practice in real time. This record allows self-analysis month by month: what materials recur, what formal tensions persist, what gestures return, what conditions unlock the strongest work. The documentation is mine first. It serves the research first.

## Artistic and Cultural Context

*"The materials of this project have been used against people like me. That is precisely why I am using them."*

**Lineage**: Kara Walker, El Anatsui, Nick Cave — artists who have taken loaded materials and inherited violence and transformed them into work with aesthetic and political force.

**What distinguishes Brutal Isms**: its grounding in live, embodied, self-directed studio research — sculpture, film, performance, and documentation pursued simultaneously, daily, in one purpose-built environment.

**The labor context**: In 2025, six months as the only Black employee at a salon subjected to psychological harassment, unpaid labour, and deliberate racial targeting. Reported to labour standards. Won. The legal victory was real. It did not alter the deeper condition: exploitation is normalized, and artists at the intersection of multiple marginalised identities are routinely expected to either assimilate or absorb the cost of refusing.

This project is not a response to that experience. It is a refusal to let it define the terms of creative life.

## Research Questions

1. What does my practice become when it is accountable only to artistic truth?
2. How do materials with histories of oppression transform in the hands of those they were used against?
3. What happens when sculpture, film, movement, and documentation are pursued as one continuous method rather than as separate outputs?
4. What is the relationship between documentation and creation when both are pursued simultaneously, privately, and without an audience in mind?
5. What becomes possible when an artist with professional-level skills across every discipline stops making work for everyone else?

These questions will not be solved in theory. They will be worked through in the studio, daily, across seven months, with structured monthly reflection built into the process.

## Dissemination and Community Impact

**Exhibition**: WIP Gallery, Montréal, opening December 4, 2025.

**Audience**: ~5,000 — process documentation shared as evidence of method, not content.

**Sector intervention**: autonomous infrastructure for queer Black artists; direct challenge to extractive authorship models in commercial industries. Brutal Isms reverses the flow: the same artist whose skills generate value inside commercial systems can also generate uncompromising cultural value outside them, under conditions he defines.

The studio infrastructure is designed to remain operational and self-sustaining beyond the grant period. Not a single output. An engine.

## Jury Rationale (AI-assisted, documented)

- **Artistic merit**: recast the studio as a laboratory and the four disciplines as one research methodology — rigorous investigation, not a fragmented list of activities
- **Feasibility**: professional background as evidence of existing parallel mastery — grant gives sovereign conditions to capacities already executed professionally
- **Impact**: dissemination pushed beyond personal healing into sector intervention — public value argument for queer Black autonomous infrastructure
- **Language**: cut hedging, reduced descriptive duplication, made sentence logic more sequential and forceful; GODMODE tone with more control, more structure, less jury doubt

## Insights

- This proposal contains the same structural argument as the PHAROS academic work, at the level of artistic practice: governance of production without external permission, documentation as method, and the question of what work becomes when accountable only to its own logic
- The labor conflict (racial harassment at the salon, won at labour standards) provides the moral stakes without letting those stakes become the entire rationale — it is positioned as context, not victimhood
- "The return is the protest" — language that belongs in the same register as "loop papers return." The studio is the governance architecture for the excluded self
- Canada Council explicit note: the application must always serve the art; commercial fashion/film language must never read as brand-facing production

## Open Questions
