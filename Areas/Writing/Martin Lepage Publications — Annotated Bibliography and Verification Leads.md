# Martin Lepage Publications — Annotated Bibliography and Verification Leads

## Summary

Externally oriented annotated bibliography of [[Martin Lepage — Professional Profile|Martin Lepage]]'s UQAM-affiliated publication corpus, centered on contemporary Paganism, Wicca/Reclaiming, ritual and performance, queer/LGBTQ identity, gender normativity, magic, and later media studies. This note complements [[Complete Paper List — Martin Lepage Corpus]] by separating public/validated publication records from internal draft, submission, and project-status records.

This note is the scholarly publication surface in the Martin tier split. For the public/professional surface, see [[Martin Lepage — Professional Profile]]; for the creative/compositional surface, see [[Writing and Novels MOC]].

Source status: synthesized from a user-supplied report preserved in [[2026-04-18_martin-lepage-uqam-annotated-bibliography-report]]. Citation markers, sandbox links, and dataset references in the raw report were not independently re-verified during this vault pass.

### Theses

- 2010 — *Mort et Naissance de Christophe Ulric et L'Ombre et le Double d'Yvon Rivard : lecture mythocritique d'une quête spirituelle*. M.A. thesis, Université Laval (études littéraires). 94 pages. Establishes the methodological substrate (mythocritique + Egyptian spiritual triad + Hermetic doctrine) that grounds the entire later corpus. See [[Mort et Naissance et L'Ombre et le Double — MA Thesis on Yvon Rivard (Université Laval, 2010)]] and [[Mythocritique to PHAROS — The 2010 Master's Thesis as Methodological Keystone]].
- 2017 — "Why be king, I'm already a queen!" : performances rituelles et négociations queer au sein de la communauté païenne de Montréal. Doctoral dissertation, UQAM. Open Archipel record and PDF reported. The PhD extends the MA's mythocritique with sociocritique applied to queer pagan ritual ethnography.
