# Agatha All Along Social Compass — Version Genealogy

## Summary
Version history of the Agatha All Along / Social Compass paper across 17 files. Key arc: French ethnographic article (v4.1) -> progressive revisions through v4.5 -> Social Compass reformatted versions (v5) -> full journal proposal. [[Agatha All Along — Wicca, Digital Mediatization, and Proof Regimes]] is the canonical wiki note.

## Context
Submitted to [[Social Compass Paper — Ritual Authority and Régime de Preuve|Social Compass]] (thematic issue dir. Olivier Servais and Lionel Obadia). Indexed in [[Media Studies and Pop Culture Analysis]] and [[Queer Sociology of Magic and Ritual]]. The "régimes de preuve" frame that arrives at v5sc2 is structurally the *ren*-discipline (proof through naming) named in [[Mythocritique to PHAROS — The 2010 Master's Thesis as Methodological Keystone|the keystone]] (MA p. 49). Part of [[Research and Papers MOC]] and [[Historical Academic Portfolio — Pre-PHAROS Scholarly Work]]. Companion: [[Social Compass Reviewer Response — Wicca and Agatha All Along]].

## Diff Summaries

### v4.1 to v4.3
Minor incremental edits. Article is stable in this range.

### v4.3 to v4.4
Major addition: 100-line change, +828 words. References section added; bibliographically complete for first time.

### v4.4 to v5sc2
Key conceptual arrival: abstract introduces "regimes de preuve" (proof regimes) -- how ritual authenticity is constructed across field sites and digital platforms.

### Social Compass proposal branch
SocialCompassWiccaAgatha_V3.docx (9,045 words) is a full rewrite for the journal thematic issue with title "De la Wicca a Agatha All Along."

### Reviewer responses
Two short documents (636 and 925 words) responding to originality, AI tool disclosure, and theoretical justification.

## Canonical Version
agatha_socialcompass_v5 (2).docx is the most developed working version. SocialCompassWiccaAgatha_V3.docx is the most complete for the journal submission.

## Key Insights from Version History
The article stabilizes early (v4.1-v4.3) then expands with references. The conceptual peak is "regimes de preuve" in v5sc2. The reviewer responses confirm a real journal submission process.
