## Diff Summaries

### v1 → v2
Major expansion (~88 changed lines). v1 is a short working draft; v2 is a complete academic manuscript with abstract, 3-site structural framework, and full argumentation. The argument shifts from describing the voodoo doll as "a durable mistake with institutional life" (v1) to a full theorization of colonial archival legibility.

### v2 → v3
757 changed lines — the largest single-step transformation. v3 appears to be a restructuring into a more outline/proposal format, with significant content cuts. Likely a reorganization pass.

### v3 → v4
Only 5 changed lines. v4 introduces the key theoretical synthesis (Mauss, Godbout, Weber) and the "deferred authority" concept in the abstract. These are significant conceptual additions despite minimal diff.

### v4/v5 → v6 → v7
Progressive expansion: v5 adds ~1,579 words over v4; v6 adds ~1,471 more; v7 is the largest at 12,126 words. Each layer adds scholarly apparatus, more ethnographic texture, and extended argumentation.

### Manuscript branch (vM1, vMR)
The manuscript files appear to be a parallel branch or a cleaned-up version for journal submission, with different titles. vMR represents a late-stage reformulation with a new title foregrounding the "curse/contest/conform/comply" framework.

## Canonical Version
**`VOODOO (7).docx`** is the longest and most complete working draft. The manuscript branch (`voodoo_doll_manuscript_revised.docx`) may represent the submission version with the revised title.

## Key Insights from Version History
The paper underwent a complete conceptual restructuring (v2→v3) before stabilizing into a theoretical framework centered on "deferred authority" and Mauss/Weber/Godbout. The two "Afterlife of Colonial Naming" files are unreadable (possibly corrupt or protected); they may be journal-formatted versions.
