## Scholarly paper pipeline (PEER-REVIEW)
`PEER-REVIEW/` is a staged **project operating system** for turning a large body of ideas, sources, notes, and draft fragments into a peer-reviewable scholarly article. It is not a one-pass generator. The default target is an 8,000-word social-sciences and adjacent-humanities article, and journal submission guidelines override every house default. The pipeline is worked one node per session, and every node closes with a handoff entry in `00_PROJECT_CONTROL/MODEL_HANDOFF_LOG.md` so no later session restarts the project from scratch.

Pipeline (nodes 00–15): intake and corpus formation, reference tree and field mapping, outline architect, outline review and citation assessment, abstract v1, deep research bundle, literature review, literature-review validation, conceptual framework, works-cited hardening, analysis and findings, discussion, conclusion, abstract revision after body, introduction (written last), final journal conformity.

Folders: `00_PROJECT_CONTROL` (brief, style rules, claim register, section plan, handoff log), `01_RAW_MATERIALS`, `02_SOURCE_FIELD`, `03_OUTLINE_AND_ABSTRACT`, `04_RESEARCH_BUNDLE`, `05_SECTION_DRAFTS`, `06_VALIDATION`, `07_FINAL_OUTPUTS`.

### Current paper — Queer Agents
*Queer Agents: Compulsory Coherence, Delegated Subjectivity, and the Design Politics of Personal AI Systems* (Martin Lepage). A theory-building intervention, with no empirical corpus, that applies queer and crip theory to the design assumptions of personal AI agents (persistent, memory-bearing systems that act as proxies for individuals in institutional contexts). It argues that the dominant design logic encodes **compulsory coherence**, the structural demand that users present as unified, continuous, institutionally legible selves, by analogy with Rich's compulsory heterosexuality and McRuer's compulsory able-bodiedness. Against this it proposes **queer agent infrastructure**: discontinuity as feature, illegibility as protection, refusal as function, and care as governance logic.

Four concepts: compulsory coherence, delegated subjectivity, queer agent infrastructure, care as governance logic (after Tronto). Three analytic domains: chronic illness (Kafer's crip time), gender transition, and precarious labour (Berlant's slow death). Core theoretical sources: Butler, Muñoz, Ahmed, Kafer, McRuer, Rich; critical-AI-studies candidates (Crawford, Noble, Benjamin) carried as `[SOURCE TO VERIFY]`. Target journal is TBD, with candidates *GLQ*, *differences*, *Signs*, *TSQ*, *Big Data & Society*, and *AI & Society*. This connects to ongoing work in [[Pagan and Queer Ritual Studies MOC]] and reuses the source [[Halberstam — The Queer Art of Failure (2011)]].

## Writing conventions
- Academic: no fabricated citations, no Wikipedia, conclusions analytical not lyrical
- Never begin sentences with "And"
- No em dashes
- Full original length preserved on revisions
- Scholarly pipeline (peer-review work): never invent sources, page numbers, publication details, quotations, or journal requirements; flag uncertainty inline with `[SOURCE TO VERIFY]`, `[PAGE NEEDED]`, `[CITATION NEEDED]`, `[DETAIL TO VERIFY]`; keep Discussion and Conclusion separate unless the journal requires otherwise; never flatten the author's voice into generic academic prose, since model fluency is not validation
- House default formatting (when no journal guidelines exist): Times New Roman 12 pt, single-spaced, in-text citations as (Author, Year:Page)
