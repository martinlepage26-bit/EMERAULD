# Les fées dansent sur le Mont-Royal — Lepage & Gauthier (Anthropologica)

## Summary
French-language paper text (Martin Lepage + François Gauthier) on **magic and queer negotiations** within Montreal neo-pagan communities, framed against contemporary religiosities, ritual practice, and critiques of disenchantment/modern reason. This is a key anchor for the vault’s “queer ritual authority” and “magic as negotiated practice” cluster.

## Why it matters here
- Gives Montreal-grounded empirical material for the vault’s ritual authority framework (proof regimes, authenticity, and negotiated practice).
- Reinforces the “disenchantment” bridge between Weberian frames and contemporary queer spiritualities in Montreal.

## Notes (vault-usable handles)
- Neo-pagan ritualities organized around nature-anchored representations and gendered archetypes; queer negotiation exposes the constructedness and normativity of gender frameworks inside practice.
- Critique of Christianity is often caricatural/reductive, coupled with a romantic critique of modern reason/disenchantment — a useful caution for governance writing about “modernity” as a straw target.
