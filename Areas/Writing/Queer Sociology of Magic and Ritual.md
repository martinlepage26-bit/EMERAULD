# Queer Sociology of Magic and Ritual

Martin's primary academic research domain: the sociological analysis of queer and pagan religious practice, ritual authority, authenticity, and the politics of magic. Spans from the 2006 dissertation-era paper through the 2026 revisions and Social Compass submissions.

This is the scholarly Martin surface for the queer-pagan corpus; the public/professional and creative/compositional surfaces are split elsewhere in the Martin bridge notes.

This is the scholarly branch of the ritual/magic subgraph. It sits beside [[Ritual, Magic, and Institutional Authority]] and returns to the same question from fieldwork, theory, and version genealogy instead of media analysis alone.

**Forward-feeding into PHAROS:** This domain is not historically closed. Per [[MASTER BIBLIOGRAPHY — Theoretical Genealogy of LOTUS]], the theoretical apparatus developed across this body of work (Bourdieu symbolic power, Foucault governmentality, Butler performativity, Mauss techniques du corps, Honneth recognition, Charles Taylor authenticity, Crenshaw/Bilge intersectionality, Halberstam queer art of failure) is the documented lineage of the [[LOTUS Model — Agency and Social Positioning|LOTUS vector model]] in PHAROS. Methodological isomorphism, not domain abandonment.

**Backward-rooting into mythocritique:** This domain extends a methodological substrate older than the PhD itself. The [[Mort et Naissance et L'Ombre et le Double — MA Thesis on Yvon Rivard (Université Laval, 2010)|2010 MA thesis at Université Laval]] established the mythocritical apparatus (Frye, Eliade, Greimas, Brunel, Durand) and the Egyptian/Hermetic frame (the spiritual triad *âme/double/ombre*; Hermès Trismégiste). The queer pagan PhD corpus is the *sociocritique extension* of that substrate — not its origin. See [[Mythocritique to PHAROS — The 2010 Master's Thesis as Methodological Keystone]] for the full architectural genealogy from MA-era mythocritique through PhD-era sociocritique to PHAROS.
