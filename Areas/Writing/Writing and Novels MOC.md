This is the hub for [[Martin Lepage — Professional Profile|Martin Lepage]]'s creative writing practice — novels, monographs, allegory, and speculative fiction published under his imprint [[HEXA Press — Publishing and Practice Initiative|HEX/ADECIMAL Press]]. The body of work is bilingual (English and French), genre-crossing, and structurally unified by a set of recurring preoccupations: the seam between autobiography and invention, queer forms of selfhood that refuse to cohere, the governance of voice and archive, and resolutions that insist on remaining open.

This note is the creative/compositional surface in the Martin tier split. For the public/professional surface, see [[Martin Lepage — Professional Profile]]; for the scholarly publication surface, see [[Martin Lepage Publications — Annotated Bibliography and Verification Leads]].

The creative work is not separate from the scholarly and governance work — it is the same inquiry in a different register. The epistemic moves in the novels (failure harvesting, declared gaps, insistence without closure) mirror those in the [[Recursive Deterministic AI Governance — Method and Paper|PHAROS methodology]]. The imprint, [[HEXA Press — Publishing and Practice Initiative|HEX/ADECIMAL Press]], is a publishing and practice initiative that holds both registers under one roof.

## Methodological Substrate

The creative work is not derivative of the scholarly work, nor is the scholarly work derivative of the creative — both are *textes-rites* in the lineage Yvon Rivard inaugurated for the operator at the MA level. The keystone (added 2026-05-03) makes this substrate explicit: the Egyptian spiritual triad, the Osirian death/rebirth structure, the Phoenix-as-rebirth, and the Name dialectic are present across all three registers (academic, creative, governance).

- [[Mythocritique to PHAROS — The 2010 Master's Thesis as Methodological Keystone]] — The architectural keystone establishing the novels-as-textes-rites lineage.
- [[Mort et Naissance et L'Ombre et le Double — MA Thesis on Yvon Rivard (Université Laval, 2010)]] — Primary source: the thesis on Rivard's two early novels.
- [[Pourquoi rêver encore — Lecture allégorique et métaphysique d'Yvon Rivard]] — *L'ombre / le silence / le souffle* — three formal elements that recur across the creative corpus.

## Novels in Progress and Published

### [[Alchemy of the Wound — Novel]]
Autofiction narrated through a child named "Marty." A cat bite opens the first seam in the world. The wound does not heal — it alchemizes, producing a "red seed": generative remainder rather than resolution. Published by HEX/ADECIMAL Press (ISBN 9798251826722). The clearest fictional statement of the seam concept across all of Martin's work.

### [[CORPUS ou le génie de l'insistance — Novel]]
French-language novel (2025). Two protagonists, Théone and Moriel, traverse competing levels of coherence rather than a conventional narrative arc. A concurrent world attempts to enter through a "brèche." Resolution is not confrontation but synchronization: "One does not advance — one synchronizes." Published by HEX/ADECIMAL Press (ISBN 9798275803334). The title names the governing ethic of the 2025 creative cycle: the genius of insistence.

### [[Le sang qui porte ton nom — Novel]]
French-language dark fantasy (2025). Five protagonists with psychic abilities investigate an apparent black sorcerer, only to discover the antagonist is a victim — consumed from within by an ancient entity called Elle. The queer charge of the Justin/Ulrich relationship is unambiguous. Resolution is erasure, not defeat: "not displaced, not defeated. Erased." Published by HEX/ADECIMAL Press (ISBN 9798274705103).

### [[The Broken Frequency of the Word — Novel]]
Novel (2025). Matisse, grieving their mother, encounters a mysterious hum and a pink-gold cat that appears at moments of transition. Resolution arrives not through the closure of grief but through shared frequency — two broken signals finding mutual resonance. Published by HEX/ADECIMAL Press. The most intimate of the 2025 novels and the most precise on what governance adjudication actually requires: not agreement, but the capacity to hear each other's signal through each other's noise.

### [[The Weather Beneath the Walls — Novel]]
Literary novel, approximately 45,700 words. First-person narrator Etienne Vachon, a Montreal sound archivist, returns to his Saguenay hometown after his mother's death and uncovers suppressed records from a speech therapy clinic. The climax is a live broadcast of those suppressed voices through the town's civic PA infrastructure. Sister Helene's line: "We used good intentions as insulation. That does not change the temperature of what happened." Completed manuscript; publication under HEX/ADECIMAL Press, status unconfirmed.

### [[The Palace Under Root — Allegory and the Keyport Novel]]
Two interconnected creative texts. The Keyport Domain of Crystal is a fantasy court novel in development, organized around misclassification and institutional drift in a royal archive. The companion text, "The Palace Under Root," is a short allegory casting the HEPHAISTOS governance architecture as a palace court, with Diamond Eyes as its most politically delicate figure. The governing question of both: "Find not where the line was written, but who made it usable."

## Monographs and Long-Form Work

### [[The Returning Light — Monograph]]
Philosophical-poetic monograph (2025). Deploys queer theory, ritual studies, and posthumanism as interlocking frameworks around the figure of what comes back — light from a star that may no longer exist. The central governance application: working with real effects whose causes cannot be verified by checking the source. The form enacts the argument, with poetic sections doing epistemic work that argument cannot. Published by HEX/ADECIMAL Press.

### [[Breath of the Astral Year — Astrology Monograph]]
Full astrology monograph structured around twelve competences of the year — one per zodiacal sign — reframed as civic and embodied capacities rather than personality types. Central argument: astrology functions as a shared temporal technology and civic grammar, particularly for queer communities whose institutional vocabularies of selfhood are hostile. Includes a critical analysis of Co-Star and The Pattern as platforms that reduce civic grammar to individual optimization metrics. Published by HEX/ADECIMAL Press.

### [[The Rooted Archive — Nine Chambers of Plant, Spirit, and Knowledge]]
Bilingual ethnobotany, folklore, and living-practice curriculum/manuscript project. The Desktop `Herbier.txt` copy is captured as provenance under the 2026-05-06 Desktop scan; the wiki note is the durable synthesis handle.

### [[Legitimacy Machines — Book Skeleton and Architecture]]
Skeleton and architecture for the book-length theoretical project *Legitimacy Machines, Altars of Control: Governing Wonder and Training Power Across Screen Worlds*. Seven chapters, each analyzing a screen object (Buffy, Avatar, Agatha All Along, The Love Witch, Disenchantment, Jurassic Park) as a legitimacy machine that trains audiences in governance norms through ritual repetition. In development; no press or ISBN confirmed. The companion introduction is documented in [[Ritual Machine Lab — Legitimacy Machines Introduction]].

### [[Legitimacy Machines Altars of Control — Full Book Architecture]]
The canonical "TRUE" version of the Legitimacy Machines book, including the full table of contents with all eight chapters. Notes a publication platform (hexadecimalmystic.substack.com). Adds two chapters not in the earlier skeleton: Chapter IV (What We Do in the Shadows — Immortality as Administrative Farce) and Chapter VII (Alien — The Corporate Womb and The Politics of Survival).

### [[La fréquence brisée du verbe — Roman]]
French-language novel (2025). Opens in the silence that falls when a refrigerator stops humming — the day the narrator finds his mother dead on the couch. "Ce silence deviendra sa langue maternelle." The French counterpart or original of [[The Broken Frequency of the Word — Novel]].
