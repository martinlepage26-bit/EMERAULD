# Complete Paper List — Martin Lepage Corpus

## Summary

A structured audit of the full scholarly corpus of [[Martin Lepage — Professional Profile]], comprising 30 distinct works organized across six phases and a parallel cultural studies track. All compositions are 2024–2026, with the single exception of the 2017 PhD thesis. This document is the canonical reference for submission status, venue targeting, and blocking tasks across the [[PHAROS Evidentiary Gap Closure Bundle]] and related publication efforts.

> **Correction (2026-05-03):** The corpus has **two** pre-2024 anchors, not one. The 2010 MA thesis at Université Laval (`Mort et Naissance de Christophe Ulric et L'Ombre et le Double d'Yvon Rivard : lecture mythocritique d'une quête spirituelle`) is the methodological substrate; see [[Mort et Naissance et L'Ombre et le Double — MA Thesis on Yvon Rivard (Université Laval, 2010)]] and the keystone [[Mythocritique to PHAROS — The 2010 Master's Thesis as Methodological Keystone]]. The 2017 PhD is the *sociocritique extension* of that substrate, not the origin. The "single exception" framing above predates the keystone work and is descriptively incomplete.

## Context

This list was produced as part of the [[Master Project Tracker — 2026]] workflow to resolve version confusion, mislabeling, and missing references across 69 raw source files. It sits at the intersection of the [[Governance and PHAROS MOC]] and the [[Research and Papers MOC]], giving both a verified count and actionable status for every work. The corpus connects directly to [[PHAROS AI Ethics Submission — Springer Draft]], [[Loop Papers and Recursive Governance]], [[First Method Paper — Recursive AI Governance as Executable Method]], and [[Recursive Deterministic AI Governance — Method and Paper]].

For the externally visible publication bibliography, including UQAM identity resolution, homonym exclusions, peer-reviewed articles/chapters, open PDFs, and acquisition gaps, see [[Martin Lepage Publications — Annotated Bibliography and Verification Leads]]. That note is a public-record cross-check; this note remains the internal pipeline/status audit.

## Details

The corpus is organized into phases, with a parallel Cultural Studies track.

**Phase 0 — Foundations**
Two peer-review articles and three governance/working documents. The Sealed Card Protocol (moderate-work, target: Material Religion or Journal of Ritual Studies) and Charging Objects (near-ready, target: Material Religion) are the two submission candidates. [[Martin Walks the Witches' Road — Corpus as Charge-Persistence|The Witches' Road]], the Alambic Scroll, and the Recursive Deterministic AI Governance working method statement are internal governance protocols, not peer-review submissions in current form.

**Phase 1 — First Method / Algorithmic Agentic AI**
Four active papers, all requiring major work. Zero references across all four; all share a blocking problem: Shermer 2009 agenticity register collapse requires remediation before submission. Targets include AI & Society, Minds and Machines, Big Data & Society, and Science, Technology, and Human Values. A 2013 archival version is preserved for lineage only.

**Phase 2 — Recursive Governance Under Constraint**
Two papers. "From AI Anxiety to Recursive Governance Under Constraint" (target: AI & Society) is now submitted and enrolled in Springer Nature In Review; Research Square preprint screening is pending the journal's quality check. The earlier disk-residue items were the pre-submission blockers in that run. "Governance by Denial" has two divergent manuscripts under the same title; the PDF (Harvard-cited, ~40 refs) is the submission candidate for Security Dialogue.

**Phase 3 — PHAROS Protocol Closure**
Two papers. The PHAROS architecture paper (target: AI and Ethics) has no single canonical clean version. The +0.0628 evidence-hardening constant and Möbius priority anchor are load-bearing but provenance-opaque — a research-integrity flag before any submission. The multi-model adversarial evaluation paper was mislabeled as a PHAROS method paper; it requires standalone venue assignment. An item labeled "AI Governance Constitution" in source files is actually the Unified Martin Voice Spec v4.0 — a mislabeling to note.

**Phase 4 — Continuity and Stress Tests**
Five papers plus one bounded. "The Wheel and the Watcher" is near-ready and is the coinage site of the term suprametacognition, retargeted to Philosophy & Technology. "Recursive AI Self-Report as an Evidentiary Problem" is closest-to-ready but held pending "From AI Anxiety" triage at AI & Society to avoid double submission.

**Phase 5 — Authority, Ethics, and Public Voice**
Two near-ready articles and one book manuscript. "Who's the Boob, Who's the Trap?" (~46,500 words) has zero inline citations — citation retrofit at book scale is the largest single blocking task in the entire corpus.

**Parallel Cultural Studies Corpus**
Seven papers, one chapter manuscript, and the 2017 PhD thesis. "Magic After Legitimacy: Witchcraft, Sisterhood, and the Work of Power in Charmed" is currently under peer review at Feminist Media Studies (Submission ID 262014844) — no other paper may be submitted there while it is in review. "Glitching the Sacred" is near-ready pending only a references section, making it the highest-leverage near-ready action in the cultural studies track.

## Key Ideas

- All composition dates are 2024–2026; year labels in source filenames are archiving artifacts or subject-matter reference dates.
- The PhD thesis ("Why Be King? I'm Already a Queen!") is the sole pre-2024 item; article extraction is recommended, not monograph conversion.
- Eight works are near-ready or in review; eight are moderate-work; seven are major-work; five are bounded/deferred/archival; one is a book.
- The "AI Governance Constitution" PDF is mislabeled and is actually the Unified Martin Voice Spec v4.0.
- Two papers share the title "Governance by Denial" but are different manuscripts.

## Insights

- The largest bottleneck across phases is zero-reference states — the agenticity register collapse in Phase 1 and the citation deficit in the book manuscript are the two dominant blocking conditions.
- Venue conflicts are active: no submissions to Feminist Media Studies while "Magic After Legitimacy" is in review; "For Her Alone to Wield" and "Every Hair a Battle Scar" must not go to the same venue simultaneously.
- The "Glitching the Sacred" bibliography is the single highest-leverage near-ready fix in the cultural studies corpus.
