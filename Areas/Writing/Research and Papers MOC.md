# Research and Papers MOC

## Summary

This map of content indexes Martin Lepage's scholarly work across six research areas: AI governance and the PHAROS recursive governance methodology; the method papers that formalize that methodology; the fluency, interruption, and institutional accountability framework; queer theory and media studies; disability and stuttering epistemology; and sociology of ritual, magic, and authority. The work is not organized as a series of separate projects. It constitutes a single governed intellectual arc running from [[Historical Academic Portfolio — Pre-PHAROS Scholarly Work]] through the current PHAROS governance publications, unified by the question of how authority is assembled, made legible, and contested — or denied contestation.
