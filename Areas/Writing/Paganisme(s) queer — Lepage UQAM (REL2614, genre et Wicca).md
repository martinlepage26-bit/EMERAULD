# Paganisme(s) queer — Lepage UQAM (REL2614, genre et Wicca)

See also [[Pagan and Queer Ritual Studies MOC]].
## Summary
Course presentation artifact by [[Martin Lepage — Professional Profile|Martin Lepage]] (UQÀM, Sciences des religions) outlining contemporary paganism, gender binarity in Wicca, and LGBTQ identities/“third gender” ritual frames. It strengthens the teaching-to-research continuity inside [[Queer Sociology of Magic and Ritual]] and [[Queer Theory Foundations]], corroborates the archive genealogy around the queer Pagan corpus (see [[Martin Lepage Publications — Annotated Bibliography and Verification Leads]]), and is part of [[Martin Lepage Professional Identity]] as a teaching artifact preceding the [[Why be king Im already a queen — Lepage 2017 (PhD thesis, UQAM)|2017 thesis]]. Connects to [[The Inner Goddess — Gender Normativity and Gay Identity in Wiccan Ritualities]] (the gender-binarity-in-Wicca thesis it teaches), [[Queering Neo-Pagan Magic — 2026 Paper]] (the active research surface for these themes), and the methodological substrate at [[Mythocritique to PHAROS — The 2010 Master's Thesis as Methodological Keystone]].

## Context
This deck is useful as a *concept scaffold* connecting definitional work (what “paganism” is as a modern category) to the internal ritual politics of Wicca and queer identification. It pairs naturally with the empirical/argument notes in [[Neo-Shamanism and Gender — Montreal Pagan Community]] and the long-arc hub [[Queering Neo-Pagan Magic — Identity Power and the Uses of Magic]].

## Key Ideas (as captured)
- Paganism as a modern constructed category (“revived religion”) with multiple lineages of meaning.
- Wicca emergence and founding myths (Gardner / Murray) as part of the category formation story.
- Gender duality in Wicca and the problem-space it creates for LGBTQ identity and ritual participation.
