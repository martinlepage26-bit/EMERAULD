# Mythocritique to PHAROS — The 2010 Master's Thesis as Methodological Keystone

## Summary

This note documents the methodological substrate that grounds [[LOTUS Model — Agency and Social Positioning|LOTUS]], [[Recursive Governance Theory|the PHAROS recursive method]], the EMERAULD vault architecture, the [[Trismégiste]]-as-operator-continuity convention, and the agent/skill ecosystem in [[Martin Lepage — Professional Profile|Martin Lepage's]] 2010 master's thesis at Université Laval: *Mort et Naissance de Christophe Ulric et L'Ombre et le Double d'Yvon Rivard : lecture mythocritique d'une quête spirituelle*. The thesis names — explicitly, with primary-source citations — the **Egyptian spiritual triad** (*âme/double/ombre*), the **Hermetic doctrine of cosmos-as-book** (Hermès Thoth Trismégiste, page 50), the **Osirian recursive death/rebirth structure** (page 81), and the **mythocritical method** (Frye/Eliade/Greimas/Brunel/Durand). The standing question of the thesis — ***Pourquoi rêver encore?*** — is the question PHAROS answers: governance survives administrative reduction by remaining a method, not an automation. This is a **descriptive/associational** claim about documentary lineage, not a causal claim about Martin's intellectual process. Related to [[MASTER BIBLIOGRAPHY — Theoretical Genealogy of LOTUS]], [[Dr. Sort Corpus Map — March 2026]], [[Pourquoi rêver encore — Lecture allégorique et métaphysique d'Yvon Rivard]], [[Bonded Intelligence Under Constraint — The LOTUS Processor Framework]], [[Governance and PHAROS MOC]], and [[Research and Papers MOC]].

## Details

### The MA thesis (2010, Université Laval) — what it does

**Subject:** A mythocritical reading of Yvon Rivard's first two novels — *Mort et Naissance de Christophe Ulric* (1976) and *L'Ombre et le Double* (1979) — as two volumes of a single allegorical-spiritual cycle. Both novels stage a quest (love in MN, origins in OD) that doubles as a quest of the soul.

**Method:** Mythocritique. From the conclusion (p. 83): *"Nous avons choisi de ne pas diriger notre analyse vers une sociocritique, qui ignorerait tout élément spirituel particulier à un seul auteur, ou vers une psychocritique, qui tend minutieusement à laisser de côté les sphères du divin, de l'invisible et de l'esprit comme semblent l'entendre les romans de Rivard."* Mythocritique was chosen by **explicit rejection** of two alternatives.

**Structural matrix:** *Le Livre des Morts des Anciens Égyptiens* (the Egyptian Book of the Dead) — proper title *"Livre de la sortie (de l'âme) au jour"* — provides the architecture (pp. 43–45). Death is not somber finality but provokes "une renaissance, une vie se poursuivant au niveau de l'esprit."

**Spiritual triad:** *âme / double / ombre* — *ba / ka / khaibit* in the Egyptian frame (pp. 46–48):

- ***Ba* (âme — soul)**: "anima = souffle de vie, = l'élément dynamique qui anime le corps." Soul as breath of life. Represented as anthropomorphic figure or bird with human head. Connected to blood as siège de la vie.
- ***Ka* (double — ethereal double)**: "la partie la plus résistante, la plus condensée." Compared explicitly to the German Doppelgänger. Guardian-angel role: "corriger les erreurs du jour; maintenir son 'jumeau' dans la ligne de son destin ou l'y ramener." Possible appearances include: *l'Homme de lumière, le Moi-lumière, le Guide personnel, l'Ange initiatique, le Témoin du Ciel, la Nature parfaite, Jumeau céleste*.
- ***Khaibit* (ombre — shadow)**: "René Guénon appelle l'ombre." Counterweight of the double. Substrat des passions animales. Plays the role of "moi de substitution." Mimics out-of-step the thoughts of the *moi*. **But also, paradoxically:** *shout* (Egyptian shadow term) is "une métaphore de protection." The shadow is not only negative — it participates in spiritual ascent.

Plus two further components beyond the triad (pp. 49–50):
- ***Ren*** (the Name): "[p]rononcer le nom c'était façonner par la voix son image spirituelle." To pronounce the name = to fashion the spiritual image through voice.
- ***Sekhem*** (Magical Will): the divine power conferred through the Name, taught by Hermès Thoth Trismégiste.

**Hermetic doctrine (page 50, named directly):**

> *"[L]e Livre des Morts s'inscrit dans le 'culte de Thoth [qui] donna naissance à la doctrine du Verbe divin, du Logos créateur et de la magie.' Cette doctrine d'**Hermès Thoth, dit Trismégiste**, en adéquation avec la composition spirituelle de l'être, constitue le sekhem, qui donne la Volonté magique à l'homme, par le pouvoir divin du Nom. Ainsi, dans la pensée spirituelle égyptienne, 'le Cosmos tout entier est un Livre cosmique, un Manuscrit, un gigantesque rouleau de Papyrus issu des mains des dieux. Cherchez donc à lire et à comprendre ce Livre ouvert et étalé devant vous, cette révélation des dieux,' tel était le message spirituel d'Hermès Trismégiste."*

The footnote (n. 112) cites the Kybalion principles — mentalism, correspondence, vibration, polarity, rhythm, cause-effect, gender — and notes their resemblance to "termes littéraires, musicaux voire mathématiques."

**The Osirian myth (page 81, naming the structural reference):**

> *"À l'image du **mythe osirien**, la triade spirituelle relate ce passage migratoire dans les récits de Mort et Naissance de Christophe Ulric et de L'Ombre et le Double."*

Death/dismemberment/restoration as recursive structure. The Osiris myth anchors Egyptian beliefs on the passage between life and death. The triad's work in the novels respects "la même finalité que le mythe égyptien, soit la continuité de la vie de l'esprit après la mort."

**Closing axiom (page 81, Yvon Rivard quoted):**

> *"seuls sont vivants ceux que 'la mort traque'."*

Only those whom death tracks are alive.

**Diamond-Eyes anticipation (page 84, Pierre Vadeboncoeur quoted on Rivard):**

> *"L'humanité dont il fait preuve donne la mesure de son caractère. […] La pensée d'un être n'est pas indépendante de la mesure de son âme."*

The thought of a being is not independent of the measure of the soul. (See also "Diamond-Eyes correspondence" below.)

### The recent "Pourquoi rêver encore?" essay revision (2024–2026)

The MA-era essay [[Pourquoi rêver encore — Lecture allégorique et métaphysique d'Yvon Rivard]] is, per the [[Pourquoi Rever Encore — Version Genealogy|version genealogy]], a 2024–2026 revisitation of the MA material. It states three formal elements of transfiguration in its abstract:

> *"le sujet traverse l'ombre, le silence et le souffle dans une dynamique de transfiguration"*

Each element maps onto something operational:

- ***L'ombre*** → LOTUS *Sh* (shadow remainder); the L99 Gap Declaration; the Khaibit-as-paradoxical-protection from p. 48
- ***Le silence*** → the L99 Gap as principle ("l'innommé est aussi l'innommable" — page 20 of the MA, OD: 14); page 78 of the MA: *"la création commence vraiment avec le silence"*
- ***Le souffle*** → the *ba* of page 46 (*"souffle de vie, = l'élément dynamique qui anime le corps"*); the voice/TTS work

The recent essay's methodological frame names "narratologie, mythocritique (Frye, Bachelard, Eliade) et la pensée du sujet situé (Haraway, Bertaux, Beauchemin)." This is the MA's apparatus + situated subjectivity (the only non-MA addition, drawn from PhD-era methodology). The standing question — *Pourquoi rêver encore?* — is preserved.

### The three operational layers

**1. LOTUS** — operationalization of the spiritual triad as agency-measurement vector.

| Egyptian element | LOTUS architecture |
|---|---|
| ***Ba* (Âme — soul, breath of life, dynamic principle)** | `L_base` — base LOTUS vector; the immanent capacity. The dimensions P (perceptual latitude) and R (regulatory bandwidth) are *ba*-functions: capacity for accurate perception and self-regulated breath. |
| ***Ka* (Double — relational manifested self under conditions, *jumeau céleste*, more complete and free than the *moi*)** | `L_eff = M*L_base - N*K + Q*G` — the effective state under constraint and scaffold. The *ka* is "more malleable than the *âme*" (p. 47); the L_eff is more malleable than L_base because constraints and scaffolds act on it. |
| ***Khaibit* (Ombre — shadow remainder, paradoxical protection, *moi de substitution*)** | `Sh` — the explicit "shadow remainder" dimension: "what cannot be fully captured by the other dimensions." The note [[LOTUS Model — Agency and Social Positioning]] originally listed as an "open question" the absence of a standalone note for Sh; the answer is the MA thesis. |

The constraint vector `K = [body, time, economic, relational, institutional, stigma, isolation, urgency]` and the scaffold vector `G = [relational, material, clinical, cultural, spiritual, organizational, interpretive]` are the **structural conditions of the spiritual quest** — the obstacles and supports between *moi* and *ka*, between manifested self and guardian double.

**2. PHAROS recursive method** — operationalization of the **Osirian recursive structure** as governance gates.

The Osiris myth (named on p. 81) is death-dismemberment-restoration. The PHAROS pipeline mirrors this:
- **Initial sequence** (corpus formation, claim instantiation) — Osiris alive
- **Body of recursive transformation passes** — dismemberment, evidence binding, adjudication; the claim is decomposed and gathered through the gates (p. 28's Greimas: "une séquence initiale et une séquence finale sur des plans de 'réalité' mythique différents que le corps du récit")
- **Final sequence** (promotion decision, consequence binding) — restoration on a new plane

The 6-step adjudication protocol structurally inherits the trial of Thomas before the Council in *L'Ombre et le Double* (pp. 17–19): claim defends itself before judgment; verdict "*Cet homme est déjà mort. Qu'il vive!*" gives life through acknowledgment of death. PHAROS gates are non-exceptionable for the same reason the Egyptian gate-formulas are non-exceptionable: each gate is initiatic, not procedural.

The Springer paper claim — *"AI cannot govern itself as sovereign authority"* — is consistent with the MA conclusion's rejection of psychocritique-style external observation: governance cannot be sovereign-from-outside; it must be initiatic-from-within, processional, gated.

The Yvon Rivard line cited on p. 81 — *"seuls sont vivants ceux que la mort traque"* — is the operating principle of governance-as-method-not-automation. To live is to be tracked by failure. PHAROS keeps governance alive by allowing failure-mode to track it.

**3. EMERAULD vault** — operationalization of the **Hermetic Cosmos-as-Book doctrine** (p. 50).

The vault is the Cosmic Manuscript. The wiki link names are the divine Names (*ren*, p. 49). The linking-rule — every note must participate in the graph through wiki links — is the operationalization of *ren*-discipline: "[p]rononcer le nom c'était façonner par la voix son image spirituelle." A note that is not linked is not pronounced; it is not part of the Cosmic Manuscript. [[Trismégiste]] as the operator-continuity layer is the explicit invocation of the Hermetic operator named on p. 50.

The vault's [[Inner Mind Eye]] doctrine — *act on stated values, never on inferred* — is the *ren*-discipline of p. 49: "Avoir oublié le Nom pendant le passage de la vie à la mort mettait en danger l'existence du défunt; connaître le Nom d'un esprit ou d'un dieu équivalait à les avoir à sa merci." Acting on the inferred is acting on a Name not pronounced.

### The Name dialectic (pages 79, 49)

The closing of *Mort et Naissance* (MN: 283), cited on p. 79:

> *"C'est alors qu'il entendit monter du fond de lui-même, du vide creusé en lui par la disparition du nom précieux, une voix qui n'était ni celle de Geneviève ni celle de Philémon, et qui se tut à l'instant même où la lumière effaçant les paupières formula son regard:*
>
> *Geneviève n'est plus, voici la mer."*

And the thesis's gloss (p. 79):

> *"C'est lorsqu'il perd le contrôle du nom qu'il laisse aller tout son être à la plénitude de la mer."*

The Name must be **both pronounced AND released**. Holding the Name without releasing it blocks plenitude. **This is the precise dialectic of the L99 Gap Declaration**: pronounce what can be pronounced, declare what cannot be named. PHAROS bounded-claims discipline operates this dialectic.

### Hermetic mentalism (page 80, OD: 187 cited)

> *"de divin que cette pensée en nous qui à chaque instant crée l'univers"*

Kybalion principle 1 — *The All is Mind; the Universe is Mental*. This is the principle that grounds why governance cannot be sovereign-authority-from-outside: the universe is created at every instant by thought; governance is thought-into-being through method, not observed-from-outside through detection.
