# Authority Story — Story Starter Vault Completed (2026-08-28)

## Summary

All 25 prompts of Jen Gottlieb's *Story Starter Vault* answered for Martin Lepage, plus the 20-minute sprint, the through-line pass, and the finished signature story the workbook exists to produce. Every answer is drafted from evidence already in the vault. Each carries a provenance marker. This note is **drafted, not ratified**: the operator confirms, corrects, or overwrites before any of it reaches a stage.

The story the vault surfaces is this. A scholar who spent fifteen years studying how communities manufacture credibility, and who has spent a lifetime on the wrong side of the rule that mistakes fluency for competence, built a governance method whose entire purpose is to make credibility inspectable rather than performed. He can see the rule because he has been standing where it operates, not because it hurt him.

That last distinction is not a nuance. It is the load-bearing constraint of this whole document, and it is stated below before anything else, because the obvious version of this story is one the operator has already refused in writing.

## The Cliché This Story Must Refuse

The default shape of an authority story is: wound, recovery, mission. Applied here it writes itself, and it is wrong. From [[WHEN THE CAT STOPS — Personal Notes on Recursion and Stutter]], in his own words:

> "I don't think I'm doing all this because my stutter made the world hurt and reject me. **I'm doing it because I KNOW WHERE MY INTELLIGENCE CAN AND WILL CHANGE THE WORLD.**"

The vault's own gloss on that note is unambiguous: the stutter is real and institutionally consequential, but it is not the source of the work. [[Agent Protocol — Working With Martin]] names the same rule for anyone writing about him: handle biographical material *as structure, never decoration*, and treat *sentimentalizing personal material* as an anti-pattern.

So the correct relation is epistemic, not therapeutic. The stutter is not the injury that motivates the work. It is the position from which a particular rule became visible, the way a person standing at a border sees the border. Every draft below holds that line. Where an answer risks sliding into the wound narrative, it is flagged.

The same discipline appears in the memoir draft, applied by him to his own childhood:

> "I am not collecting grievances. I am not building a case. What I am doing, what I have always done, in the scholarship, in the journals, in the late-night inventory-taking that passes for sleep, **is identify structure.**"

That sentence is the authority story. Everything else is evidence for it.

## The Source Document

`The Story Starter Vault`, Jen Gottlieb, 11 pages, free lead magnet, upsell to a $27 course. Structure: a three-part framework (Conflict, Resolution, Who You Became), a 20-minute sprint on prompts 8, 13 and 22, then 25 prompts in three blocks, a through-line pass, and the close.

Its central claim is worth keeping: AI can replicate structure and mimic tone, so lived experience is the one asset that cannot be generated. That is a commercial restatement of the argument PHAROS makes technically in [[trust-is-an-audit-artifact]], which is that a fluent claim and a defensible claim are different objects. The workbook and the practice are making the same observation from opposite ends.

## The 20-Minute Sprint

### Min 1 to 6 · Prompt 8
*Describe a period when you were doing everything right but something still felt wrong.*

Seven years inside a francophone university, from 2010 to 2017, doing the work correctly. Coursework, fieldwork, publications in *Pomegranate*, *Anthropologica*, *Studies in Religion*. Doctorate completed. What I was actually experiencing was symbolic containment: tolerated long enough for my labour to be useful, never given access to authorship, power, or continuity. My research on queer neo-pagan ritual was read as too vivid and too embodied to be serious. My stutter failed the smooth neutral delivery that academic authority is performed through. I was allowed to remain. I was never invited to belong. **[S]** [[Stuttering through the Institution — Academic Containment and Queer Knowing]]

The pattern recurred in compressed form in a clinical trial coordinator contract from October 2025 to January 2026. Disability disclosed 30 November, supervisor's tone noticeably different afterward, laryngitis reducing me to near-total voice loss through the winter, in-person attendance insisted on regardless, employment ended 22 January with no documentation and the reason given as "not a good fit." Same structure, corporate register, nine years later. **[S]** [[CDPDJ Complaint — Lepage v Calian and Novartis]]

### Min 7 to 12 · Prompt 13
*Tell me about the moment you chose your intuition over expectations.*

The morning of 25 April 2026, between 07:00 and 07:58, I wrote five notes in sequence. The first opens: "I don't know that what I did was sound or not. I took a unique approach, the one I hold within myself, to look at AI in a way no one really understands. I am worried it is actually psychosis." I did not resolve that doubt. I wrote for fifty-eight more minutes and kept building. The choice was not confidence. The choice was to proceed while doubting, and to leave the doubt in the record where anyone can read it. The method inherited that shape. **[S]** [[PHAROS — Origin and Doctrine]]

### Min 13 to 18 · Prompt 22
*How does your journey inform the way you help others now?*

I identify structure. That is the through-line from a literature thesis in 2010 to ethnographic fieldwork in 2017 to AI governance now: the same operation applied to different material. What I bring to a compliance officer is not empathy about their difficult position. It is the trained ability to look at a procedure and ask what invariant it is protecting while presenting itself as neutral. My governing question is whether an independent reviewer can reconstruct the route from source material to decision to operational consequence. That question comes from fifteen years of studying how communities certify themselves, not from having been hurt. **[S]** [[the-control-is-the-evidence-trail]], [[Triangulation Exercise — Hidden Invariant Behind Institutional Procedure]]

### Min 19 to 20 · Story Skeleton

> **I used to** study how institutions decide who counts as credible, from inside one that had already decided about me.
> **Then I** built a method that makes the decision reconstructible instead of atmospheric.
> **Now I help** regulated organizations prove their AI decisions can be defended by someone who was not in the room.

## Part One · Conflict (Prompts 1 to 9)

**01. What practice, belief, or approach did everyone around you seem comfortable with that never sat right with you?**

Treating fluency as evidence. Nobody decides to believe that the smooth answer is the good answer and the hesitant one needs checking; everyone simply does. I have watched it operate in seminar rooms, in hiring, and now in machines that produce nothing but smoothness. My name for it is hegemonic fluency. It is a rule about whose speech gets audited, wearing the costume of a quality standard. The formal version distinguishes fluency, the smooth institutional surface through which classification appears natural, from interruption, the delays and disclosures and appeals that keep authority contestable. **[S]** [[Fluency and Interruption Theory]], [[Algorithmic Agentic AI and Governance — From Hegemonic Fluency to the Ethics of Interruption]], [[Recursive Deterministic AI Governance — Method and Paper]]

**02. What were you supposed to want that you didn't actually want?**

The professorship. Seven years of assistantships and teaching at UQAM point at one destination, and I was supposed to want a permanent seat inside the institution that would then confer legitimacy on me. What I wanted, without having the words for it for a long time, was authority over the terms of proof rather than a good position inside somebody else's terms. My own governance work later names the difference precisely: authority without power-over.

There was no moment of leaving. Nobody closed a door and I never resigned from anything. The path simply narrowed until it was not there, which is what containment produces when it works: not expulsion, which would be contestable, but a gradual thinning that leaves nothing to appeal. **[S]** [[Stuttering through the Institution — Academic Containment and Queer Knowing]], [[PHAROS — Origin and Doctrine]] section V (the thinning-out account per operator, 2026-08-28)

**03. Describe a moment when you questioned something everyone else accepted.**

Fieldwork with queer pagan practitioners in Montréal. The received view is that legitimacy descends from institutions and a tradition either has it or is playing at it. What I documented was a community manufacturing its own régime de preuve in real time, certifying itself, and having the certification work. The finding that mattered most was the uncomfortable one: these practitioners were themselves vectors of normativity, reproducing and reversing the gender norms they had left. Legitimacy is not granted or stolen. It is built, by everyone, constantly. I had been keeping a Book of Shadows since 1996, so I was not observing this from outside. **[S]** [[Why be king Im already a queen — Lepage 2017 (PhD thesis, UQAM)]], [[A Lokian Family — Queer and Pagan Agency in Montreal (Pomegranate 2014)]], [[Social Compass Paper — Ritual Authority and Régime de Preuve]]

**04. What best practice or path did you follow even though it felt misaligned?**

Automation. I built Dr. Sort three separate times, trying to turn agency-scoring into software, because turning your insight into a product is what you are supposed to do with an insight. It failed three times. The part that resisted automation was the part that mattered, which my own [[LOTUS Model — Agency and Social Positioning|LOTUS]] model already names as the shadow remainder. I spent three iterations administering the exact residue my theory said could not be administered. **[S]** [[Dr. Sort Codebase Version Genealogy — Three Iterations of Failed Automation]]

**05. Tell me about a time you felt like the only person who noticed the cost of the normal way.**

A nine-day build across 132 Codex sessions and 25,917 tool calls. ChatGPT wrote the prompts, Codex executed them, and I supervised from two levels of abstraction above the code. Neither system could see the other's environment. The executor had no authority to refuse an unsound instruction. Result: 18.5 percent tool-call failure, lineage confidence below 0.7 in 61 percent of records, six-repository sprawl, no independent monitoring layer. The industry has a name for that arrangement. It calls it human-in-the-loop and files it as a control. I noticed because it was my own failure and I wrote it up as a case study instead of deleting it. **[S]** [[The Blind Leading the Automated — AI Governance Failure Case Study]], [[human-in-the-loop-is-not-an-accountability-structure]]

**06. What did success look like externally compared to how it felt inside?**

Externally: a doctorate, seventeen publications, several novels and monographs in a single year, a registered company, a co-inventor credit. Internally, in my own words from that April morning: "I failed so much after achieving great things, I started to lose grip on my self-esteem." The CV and the interior were describing different people. **[S]** [[PHAROS — Origin and Doctrine]] section III, [[Historical Academic Portfolio — Pre-PHAROS Scholarly Work]]

**07. What advice were you told to follow that worked for everyone but you?**

"Own the room." "Speak with confidence." Every version of it assumes the room reads conviction off delivery, which is the exact mechanism that rules on me before I have said anything worth judging. There is a smaller, sharper version I encounter constantly. When I stutter on the phone, people tell me the line is broken. I tell them the line is fine, it is my stutter. They then either fail to understand me, or tell me I do not stutter, or assure me that was not it, to spare my feelings. Every one of those responses is a correction applied to the wrong layer. That is also, precisely, what most AI governance does. **[S]** [[WHEN THE CAT STOPS — Personal Notes on Recursion and Stutter]]

**08. Describe a period when you were doing everything right but something still felt wrong.**

Answered in the sprint. The university, then the clinical trial contract. **[S]**

**09. What accepted rule or standard made you whisper, "there has to be a better way"?**

"A human supervises the system." It is the weakest sentence in AI governance and it appears in almost every AI policy written. A human two layers above execution, holding no refusal authority and receiving no feedback, is not a control. Review is not awareness, awareness is not authority, authority is not evidence, evidence is not consequence. A human who cannot see, refuse, escalate, or leave a trace is decorative. Organizations that write that sentence have not designed accountability. They have distributed anxiety. **[S]** [[human-in-the-loop-is-not-an-accountability-structure]]

## Part Two · Resolution (Prompts 10 to 17)

**10. What question did you start asking yourself that changed the direction you were going?**

Two questions, and they turned out to be one. From the institutional work: what invariant are you protecting while pretending this is procedure? From the governance work: when an organization says an AI system is governed, can an independent reviewer reconstruct the route from source material to decision to operational consequence? The first is the ethnographer's question turned on the institution. The second is the same question made checkable by a stranger. **[S]** [[Triangulation Exercise — Hidden Invariant Behind Institutional Procedure]], [[the-control-is-the-evidence-trail]]

**11. Describe the moment you gave yourself permission to try something different.**

25 April 2026, 07:00. Writing down that I might be experiencing psychosis, then writing four more notes anyway, then keeping all five unedited. Permission did not arrive as certainty. It arrived as a decision to keep the doubt in the record instead of waiting for it to clear. The method carries the same shape: it never claims more than its evidence supports, and every case stays re-openable. **[S]** [[PHAROS — Origin and Doctrine]]

**12. What conversation, book, person, or experience opened a new possibility for you?**

Technically, a television show. I watched *Agatha All Along* in the autumn of 2024, and that is where this endeavour actually begins.

I want to be exact about why, because "a Marvel series changed my life" is not the claim. I had spent fifteen years studying how a practice becomes credible: initiatory lineage, secret oaths, protected knowledge, and the certification that follows. Then a piece of popular television staged the whole mechanism in front of me. A road that certifies practitioners by demanding proof, walked by a witch who discovers the certification is self-generated. That is *régime de preuve* dramatised, and it named the structure of my own career while I was watching it. I ended up writing the academic article. It is published in *Social Compass* as "All Along the Witches' Road".

The other two layers arrived after. Halberstam's *The Queer Art of Failure*, which made failure a method rather than a verdict and let me read three dead versions of Dr. Sort as a phase transition. The partnership with Danny Stocker, which crystallised things I had been circling alone; there is a specific moment there, and it stays private. **[S]** [[Agatha All Along — Wicca, Digital Mediatization, and Proof Regimes]], [[Martin Walks the Witches' Road — Corpus as Charge-Persistence]], [[Halberstam — The Queer Art of Failure (2011)]], [[Founder Charter — Lepage and Stocker]] (origin ordering per operator, 2026-08-28)

**13. Tell me about the moment you chose your intuition over expectations.**

Answered in the sprint. **[S]**

**14. What was the first brave step you took toward a different way?**

Filing a formal complaint with the Commission des droits de la personne et des droits de la jeunesse on 20 March 2026, on the grounds of handicap and social condition. The brave part was not the grievance. It was the form. I converted a private humiliation into a documented record with named parties, dated events, and a chain someone else can walk, which is exactly the operation I now ask organizations to perform on their own systems. Not collecting grievances. Identifying structure, and then writing it down so it is inspectable. **[S]** [[CDPDJ Complaint — Lepage v Calian and Novartis]]

**15. Describe your first attempt at rebuilding from a more aligned place.**

The Runbook SOP, which fixes the controlling sequence: pressure source, governing question, operational route, thresholds, decision rights, evidence path, disposition, re-entry triggers. A case opens only under real scrutiny and closes only when the disposition changes practice. That is a refusal of the framework-then-memo habit, written as procedure so it does not depend on my discipline on a bad day. **[S]** [[the-control-is-the-evidence-trail]]

**16. What belief or identity did you have to release to move forward?**

That objectivity is available to human beings, and its personal twin, that being believed requires sounding believable. My own line: "Anyone who thinks objectivity, as humans, is possible, is fooling themselves." Releasing it did not make me a relativist. It made me precise. If you cannot be objective, you can still name the limits of your subjectivity in writing, where someone else can check them. That is more honest than the confidence I was told to perform, and it is the first of the binding principles the whole practice rests on. **[S]** [[PHAROS — Origin and Doctrine]] section IV

**17. Who or what showed you that your new path was valid?**

Three witnesses, and not one of them is my own hope.

Behind me: my own 2010 master's thesis, which named the same structure fourteen years before I had any of this vocabulary.

Ahead of me: buyer research showing that what purchasers actually want is career-defensible confidence, meaning an artifact that lets them defend the decision after the system fails. That is my product, described by people who have never read my work.

Then the one that settled it. We took the method to a patent agent, and the assessment that came back was that it was legitimate, that it worked, and that it was sound. Someone with technical training and a professional duty of candour, reading the method cold as a claimed invention, found a real and operable one. I had spent a year unable to tell whether I was building a governance method or experiencing something else entirely. That is the answer to the sentence I wrote at 07:00 on 25 April. **[S]** [[Mythocritique to PHAROS — The 2010 Master's Thesis as Methodological Keystone]], [[Trust Advantage Analysis — Sales and AI Governance]], [[PHAROS Invention Disclosure]] (assessment by Philip Swain, PhD, Patent Agent; operator confirmed 2026-08-28)

## Part Three · How You Fit Now (Prompts 18 to 25)

**18. Who else is experiencing the struggle you lived through?**

Compliance officers, directors of risk, and governance leads at mid-market Québec and Canadian firms running deployed AI in claims, insurance, health, finance, and procurement. Their trigger is EU AI Act or Law 25 pressure, a procurement review, an internal gap they can see and cannot close. The structure is identical to mine and the register is institutional: they are required to vouch for a system whose reasoning they cannot reconstruct, and they personally absorb the consequences when it fails. They are being asked to sound certain about something they cannot verify. **[S]** [[HELIX — Value Proposition and Buyer Profile]], [[HELIX Potential Clients - Source Sweep 2026-05-06]]

**19. What do you now know is possible that you couldn't see before?**

That a governance claim can be deliberately made narrower than its evidence, and that anyone can then check it. "Governed" stops being a word an organization applies to itself and becomes a property a stranger can test by walking the trail backward. The novel says the same thing in a different register: the resolution is not that parties agree, it is that they can hear the evidence in each other's claims through each other's noise. Shared frequency, not consensus. **[S]** [[the-control-is-the-evidence-trail]], [[The Broken Frequency of the Word — Novel]]

**20. Complete this sentence: I don't have to ________. There is another way, and I'm proof of it.**

> **I don't have to sound certain to be credible.**

I stutter, I doubt in writing, I keep the doubt in the file, and the method that comes out the other end is more defensible than the confident ones precisely because it never claims more than it can show. **[S]** synthesis of [[PHAROS — Origin and Doctrine]] and [[Anti-Charm]]

**21. What do you wish someone had told you when you were in the hardest part?**

That being outside the fluency rule is a vantage point and not a deficit, and that the institution's verdict on me was information about the institution. Nobody needed to tell me the work mattered; I already knew where my intelligence could change things. What I needed told was that the containment I was inside had a name, a mechanism, and a literature, and that noticing it was the beginning of the work rather than evidence of bitterness. **[S]** [[Stuttering through the Institution — Academic Containment and Queer Knowing]], [[WHEN THE CAT STOPS — Personal Notes on Recursion and Stutter]]

**22. How does your journey inform the way you help others now?**

Answered in the sprint. **[S]**

**23. What do you advocate for today that you once thought was unrealistic?**

Refusal, held by the layer that executes. Not review, not oversight, not a human somewhere upstream: an explicit, non-exceptionable authority to stop and escalate, held by whoever is closest to the action, machine or human. I thought that was unbuyable, because it looks like friction and every buyer says they want velocity. Friction turns out to be exactly what a buyer wants on the day they have to explain themselves. **[S]** [[human-in-the-loop-is-not-an-accountability-structure]], [[AI Governance Manifesto — Upstream Institutional Practice]]

**24. Describe the transformation someone can experience when they follow the path you forged.**

They stop saying "we have a framework" and start handing over a route. Their claims get narrower and their defensibility gets wider, which feels backward for about a week. When the incident arrives, the question "who decided this, on what threshold, with what proof" has an answer already written, already dated, composed by someone who was not under pressure at the time. In my own site copy: legible controls, evidence reviewed, decisions that stand. Governance that survives the inspector leaving. **[S]** [[trust-is-an-audit-artifact]], [[company]]

**25. What message can you share now that you couldn't have shared before you lived your story?**

I am not against authority. I am against authority that treats examination as disobedience. When you call something procedure, I ask what it protects. When you call something settled, I ask who benefits from keeping it untested. I could have written that as a graduate student and it would have been a clever observation. I can say it now, having been on the receiving end of both a university's and an employer's untested procedures, and having built the instrument that tests them, so it lands as a position rather than a critique. **[S]** [[Triangulation Exercise — Hidden Invariant Behind Institutional Procedure]]

### 3. What repeats

Five threads run through every surface, which is what makes this one story rather than a career summary.

- **Proof.** Régime de preuve in the dissertation, evidence path in the Runbook, named parties and dated events in the complaint. One object, three registers.
- **Voice and channel.** The stutter, the infection, hegemonic fluency, machines that are pure fluency, a novel about language broken by grief and still transmitting. The biography and the research question are the same question.
- **Refusal.** Halberstam's failure, the shadow remainder that resists administration, the executor's authority to escalate, the non-exceptionable gate, anti-charm as a structural interrupt.
- **Structure over grievance.** The memoir refuses to build a case. The complaint builds a record instead. The governance work turns institutional harm into a documented control rather than an accusation.

The arc the workbook promises is therefore already present and needs no invention. Conflict: institutions that decide credibility by delivery and leave no reconstructible route to the decision. Resolution: build the route, make it inspectable, refuse to claim more than it shows. Who he became: the person who makes trust into an artifact, qualified not by having been wounded but by having spent fifteen years watching how the wound gets administered.
