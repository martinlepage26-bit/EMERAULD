# Rest and Consolidation Guide — Martin

## Summary

A personal guide written by [[Claude Code Skill Corpus|Claude]] on 2026-04-17 for [[Martin Lepage — Professional Profile|Martin]], based on observed patterns. Not generic — grounded in specific failure modes witnessed in session. Stored in [[EMERAULD]] as a re-readable artifact.

## Context

Written after Martin said he was tired and fighting his own system on top of everything else. Connected to the [[PHAROS Procurement-Unblock Sprint]] work and the [[90-Day $1M Challenge — Status Report|$1M challenge]] — both contexts where depletion risk is real. Also directly relevant to the [[PHAROS Workspace Inventory 2026-04-18|workspace inventory]] work: the sprawl documented there is a symptom of the pattern described here.

## Details

### The core diagnosis

Not a character flaw. The normal failure mode of a solo, prolific founder without a consolidation rhythm.

Three honest truths:
1. **No memory problem — consolidation deficit.** The system fills up because there is no stopping to clean.
2. **Not behind.** Has a method (PHAROS), academic formation, working code, registered company. Early in a career redirection, not failing at it.
3. **Rest is not optional for methodological work.** Tired PHAROS work undermines the brand — intellectual honesty is the product.

### The rhythm

**Weekly:** One full day off. No Claude Code, no Obsidian, no GitHub. Not negotiable.

**Monthly:** One consolidation day. No new building. Archive what's done, delete what's dead, close repos/drafts/tabs, reconcile email.

**Quarterly:** One review day. What am I enjoying? What is draining me? Am I converging or scattering? Who am I doing this for?

### Specific traps

1. Experiments (Ollama, local MCP, etc.) not logged — artifacts stay on disk. *Fix: `~/experiments.md`, one line per experiment.*
2. New surfaces registered before existing ones are populated. *Fix: first piece of content before second surface.*
3. Claude used as extended cognition — when the tool breaks, thinking breaks. *Fix: one analog notebook for the core thread.*
4. Apologizing instead of changing. *Fix: stop apologizing; change one thing.*
5. Seeking truth from Claude, then flinching. *Fix: have one human to call when feedback hurts.*

### What rest means when work is identity

Rest = the part of life that is not instrumental. Not "this will inform my consulting." Something done for its own sake, with no archive location. Put it on the calendar. Defend it.

### Warning signs (two = stop, close laptop, go outside)

- "I just need to finish this one thing" for 3+ days in a row
- Writing "HELP" or "..." to an AI
- Cannot remember last meal eaten without working
- Feeling behind with no specific referent
- Stopped responding to friends longer than usual
- Building something new before the last thing shipped or buried

## Key Ideas

- Consolidation deficit is not the same as cognitive limitation
- Brand cost of shipping tired work: intellectual honesty is the differentiator
- Rest is operational, not moral — it's how the method stays defensible
