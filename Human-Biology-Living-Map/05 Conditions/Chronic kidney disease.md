# Chronic kidney disease

## Clinical orientation

CKD means kidney structure or function is abnormal over time and filtration or other kidney roles are impaired.

## Preserved nuance

CKD can alter anemia risk, mineral balance, vitamin D activation, fluid status, medication clearance, and supplement safety.
