# Iodine

## Established roles

Required raw material for thyroid hormone production.

- [[02 Pathways/Thyroid hormone synthesis and signalling|Thyroid hormone synthesis and signalling]]

## Condition links

- [[05 Conditions/Hypothyroidism|Hypothyroidism]]

## Interpretation caution

Both too little and too much iodine can disturb thyroid function in susceptible people. Iodine should not be treated as a universal thyroid remedy.

> [!safety] Supplement boundary
> A biological role does not prove that supplementation will improve a symptom. Check dose, formulation, upper limits, kidney and liver function, pregnancy, medications, and laboratory context with a qualified professional.
