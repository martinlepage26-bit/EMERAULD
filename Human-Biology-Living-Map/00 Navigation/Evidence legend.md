# Evidence legend

| Label | Meaning | Appropriate wording |
|---|---|---|
| Established | Reproduced clinical or physiological relationship supported by authoritative reviews or guidelines | “is required for,” “is a recognized feature of” |
| Supported association | Human evidence supports an association, but direction or individual causation may remain uncertain | “is associated with,” “can accompany” |
| Plausible mechanism | Mechanistically coherent with incomplete or indirect clinical confirmation | “may contribute to,” “could connect through” |
| Exploratory | Early, mixed, or context-dependent evidence | “has been proposed,” “evidence is mixed” |
| Personal observation | A dated observation about one person | “occurred after,” never “was caused by” |
| Safety flag | Potential for urgent harm, contraindication, toxicity, or interaction | “requires prompt review,” “do not combine without checking” |

## Evidence rules

- A biochemical role does not prove that supplementation improves a symptom.
- A deficiency symptom list does not diagnose deficiency.
- A normal single test does not always close a pathway, but neither does a symptom override testing.
- Association, mechanism, diagnosis, and treatment efficacy are different claim types.
- Reviews can become outdated. Each note has `last_reviewed` and `review_due` fields.
