# St Johns wort

*Hypericum perforatum*

## What the evidence can support

Evidence supports some use in mild to moderate depression, but this does not make it appropriate for self-treatment or for other symptom clusters.

## Connected systems

- [[01 Systems/Nervous system|Nervous system]]
- [[01 Systems/Hepatic and biliary system|Hepatic and biliary system]]

> [!danger] Safety and interactions
> High interaction risk. It can reduce the effectiveness of many medicines and can interact dangerously with serotonergic drugs. Medication review is essential.
