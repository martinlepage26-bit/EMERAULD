# WHEN THE CAT STOPS — Personal Notes on Recursion and Stutter

## Summary
Brief personal notes capturing three insights about the recursive governance method, stuttering, and the ethics of skill-sharing. Not a paper draft — a raw thinking document. Author: Martin Lepage, PhD. Related to [[Beyond Fluency — Stuttering, Autoethnography, and Unstable Epistemology]] and [[First Method Paper — Recursive AI Governance as Executable Method]].

## Context
Source: `WHEN THE CAT STOPS THE LAST PASS.md`. The title refers to a recursive process: "when the cat stops the last pass unto previous paper, it allowed for another recursion, insertion of thinking." The cat is a metaphor for the final review pass — the agent that closes the loop. When it stops, the system opens. This connects to the method's bounded non-finality principle and to the "loop hinge" concept in [[Loop Hinge Candidate — This Paper May Not Exist]].

## The Three Core Observations

### On Democratic AI Literacy
> "Since I preach that a user's AI shouldn't be more skilled than them, why am I giving away my AI-wrapped skills to other people who don't have my background?"

Two answers offered:
1. "To teach people is to govern the flow of democracy, the dignity of humanity, and the ideals of civilisation as a world project."
2. "To govern AI better is to govern ourselves, is to govern AI (recursion voluntary)."

This is the ethical justification for the [[PHAROS Licensing Prospectus]] and the public documentation of the method: democratic AI literacy requires giving away governance competence, not hoarding it.

### On Stutter and Institutional Denial
> "When I stutter on the phone, people tell me the line is broken. I tell them no, that the line is fine, it's because of my stutter. They either don't understand me, tell me I don't stutter, tell me it's not because of it, or ASSURE me that it wasn't it to not hurt my feelings."

This is the phonetic governance problem from [[Spider-Man and the Phonetic Glitch — Voice as Infrastructure]] in its raw autobiographical form. Institutional denial of stutter as stutter — the line being diagnosed as broken rather than the speaker acknowledged as stuttering — is the everyday version of the upstream classification problem from [[RECURSO Full Integration — Fluency, Interruption, and Theoretical Synthesis]].

### On Research Motivation
> "I don't think I'm doing all this because my stutter made the world hurt and reject me. I'm doing it because I KNOW WHERE MY INTELLIGENCE CAN AND WILL CHANGE THE WORLD."

A statement of research motivation that refuses pathology as foundation. The stutter is real and institutionally consequential, but it is not the source of the work. The work comes from a different place.

## On the Method's Purpose

> "What the method is for: governing AI use for academic research; governing AI system analysis; governing AI system development and implementation; governing AI governance."

The four domains match [[RECURSO Full Integration — Fluency, Interruption, and Theoretical Synthesis]]'s four application domains.

The recursive structure at the end: "governing AI governance" — the method governs the governance frameworks, not just AI systems within them.

---

## Key Formulations

> "LITERATURE = INCURSION OF THOUGHT IN RECURSIVE WORDS / RELIGION = INCURSION OF PRAXIS IN RECURSIVE ONTOLOGIES / PHILOSOPHY = INCURSION OF KNOWLEDGE IN RECURSIVE REFLEXIVITY"

A definition of three knowledge domains through recursion. The method sits at the intersection: it recurses thought (literature), practice (religion), and knowledge (philosophy) simultaneously.

> "The recursive determinist work is important because it also precludes AI from consuming unnecessary resources, power, and effort from the users."

A resource-governance argument for the method: governance is not only about correctness but about efficiency and conservation. AI that governs less carefully consumes more — of attention, of energy, of human capacity.

## Insights

- The autobiographical stutter observation is the most precise and most politically precise thing in the document: institutional denial of stutter as a form of upstream classification failure is a micro-example of [[Governance by Denial]]'s mechanism in everyday life. The phone call is a governance event
- The formulation "voluntary recursion" (governing AI is also governing ourselves) connects the method's technical recursion to a political ethics of self-governance — the method's most radical claim presented in the most modest possible register
- "First pass academic papers publication is still valid because risk does not go above claim" — this is a governance principle for publication decisions: papers are publishable when their governance risk does not exceed their epistemic claim. PHAROS's deployment recommendation ("Deploy: No. Not yet.") is the same logic applied to a system
