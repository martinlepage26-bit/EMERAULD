# Skill Domain — Governance and Method

See also [[Manuscript Pipeline MOC]].
See also [[Control Protocols MOC]].
See also [[Governance and PHAROS MOC]].
## Summary

The Governance and Method domain contains the analytical and procedural core of [[Martin Lepage — Authored Skills|Martin's signature methodology]]: the skills that define what counts as evidence, how authority is traced, how power is mapped, and how governance artifacts are promoted or refused. These skills operationalize the [[PHAROS Method Map|PHAROS method]] and the [[Recursive Governance Theory|recursive governance framework]] across all project surfaces. This domain is the highest-governance cluster in the [[Skill Ecosystem — Professional Capability Registry]] and feeds directly into the [[Skill Domain — Skill Architecture]] meta-layer and the [[Skill Domain — Research and Scholarly Work]] research layer.

Adjacent enforcement surface: [[Skill Domain — Security and Compliance]] (privacy/compliance review, refusal conditions, threat models, red-team validation) when governance decisions touch regulated or adversarial domains.
Adjacent enforcement surface: [[Skill Domain — Security and Compliance]] (privacy/compliance review, red-team/adversarial testing, ownership mapping).
