# PHAROS Outreach Pack — Q2 2026 Tier 1 Quebec Targets

## Summary

Operational outreach pack for the first three PHAROS cold outreach conversations in Quebec, targeting Mouvement Desjardins, Banque Nationale du Canada, and Beneva. Prepared May 1, 2026. Objective: convert each to a 30-minute discovery call within four weeks. Contains: contact identification steps (LinkedIn title search + currency verification), cold email templates, and institution-specific targeting notes. Companion to [[PHAROS External Proof Packet — Procurement-Unblock 2026-04-28]] and part of the [[PHAROS Procurement-Unblock Sprint]] strategy.

## Context

This pack is the operational Day 0 instrument for the PHAROS commercial launch outreach. It was prepared as part of the 8-stream PHAROS commercial launch (see [[PHAROS AI Commercial Launch — 8-Stream Deploy (2026-05-01)]]). Outreach was pending Calendly verification and production domain confirmation as of 2026-05-01. The three targets are Quebec's largest financial cooperative (Desjardins), second-largest bank (BNC), and a major insurer (Beneva).

The institutional logic: Quebec financial institutions face Law 25 (in force), OSFI E-23 (examination expectations now), and potential EU AI Act extraterritorial exposure. Senior risk and governance executives will already have AI governance on their Q3 2026 planning calendars.

## Details

### How to Identify the Right Contact

**Do not send to generic info@ or to the CEO.** The right recipient is the senior executive whose calendar will already have "AI governance" or "model risk" in Q3 2026 planning.

**Step 1 — LinkedIn title search:**
- **Mouvement Desjardins:** "model risk" OR "risque de modèle" OR "gouvernance IA" OR "AI governance" OR "Chief Data Officer" (company filter, current employees only)
- **Banque Nationale / National Bank of Canada:** "model risk management" OR "MRM" OR "AI risk" OR "Chief Risk Officer" — note: BNC has a public, identifiable AI governance function
- **Beneva:** "risque de modèle" OR "actuarial" OR "Chief Risk Officer" OR "gouvernance des données" — smaller, right contact likely VP-level rather than C-suite

**Step 2 — Verify currency:** Has the person posted, commented, or updated their position in the last 6 months? Inactive profiles often indicate internal moves. If unclear, check Quebec professional registries (Institut canadien des actuaires) or recent conference rosters (AMF annual Rendez-vous, ICAR, Conference Board Canada AI events).

### Institution Notes

**Mouvement Desjardins:**
- Quebec's largest financial cooperative, federal AI regulation exempt (not a federal bank), fully subject to Law 25
- Cooperative governance model means procurement decisions involve both board and management layers
- Primary contact: model risk or AI governance function; check the *Fédération des caisses Desjardins* executive team

**Banque Nationale du Canada:**
- Second-largest bank, federally regulated → OSFI E-23 applies now
- Publicly visible AI/ML function (mentioned on BNC's public communications)
- Primary contact: Head of Model Risk Management or VP/SVP equivalent

**Beneva:**
- Quebec insurer (Desjardins Life + La Capitale merger), subject to Law 25 and AMF oversight
- Smaller C-suite, so VP-level is the right level rather than C-suite direct
- Actuarial function is the likely AI governance adjacent entry point

### Conversion Target

- 2-4% of qualified visitors to discovery call
- 30-50% of discovery calls to signed engagement
- Engagement: $18,000 CAD Diagnostic (4-week) or higher tier

### Blocking Prerequisites (at time of preparation)

- Calendly event verification (`calendly.com/pharos-ai/scoping` — WSL DNS issue may mask actual availability)
- Production domain confirmation (`pharos-ai.ca` resolving from external networks)
