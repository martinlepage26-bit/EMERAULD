## Context

This note hardens the “locked spec” execution system by making the success condition *painfully concrete and non-gameable*, so proof assets and funnel metrics stay clean. It is the evidentiary discipline layer for the [[PHAROS Procurement-Unblock Sprint]] offer and shares the same receipts-first logic as [[Trust Advantage Analysis — Sales and AI Governance]]. The “Argus Stress Test” in the title reflects the [[Governance Stress-Test Protocols — Index]] adversarial audit stance applied to commercial proof. Positioned within the [[PHAROS Commercial Strategy]] thread.

## Locked spec (as used in the loop)

> I help solo founders who’ve been building for 3+ months with $0 revenue land their first paying client in 14 days by validating and selling a simple service offer (no product), via a 2-week hands-on intensive with direct feedback, using Twitter/X by engaging with founders stuck at $0 and posting teardown threads.

## Failure modes (what breaks in reality)

1. **“Committed” is gameable** (LOIs, “I’m in,” vague promises ≠ money).
2. **“Client” is undefined** (friend/family support payments, barter, existing employer, charity payments poison proof).
3. **Scope can balloon** ($300 becomes 15+ hours of unpaid labor + token payment → destroys economics and causes churn).
4. **14-day procurement mismatch** (real buyers can be slow to pay even when serious).
5. **Refund/chargeback ambiguity** (a “win” that reverses later corrupts proof + metrics).

## Hardened definition of success (drop-in language)

Use verbatim:

**Definition of success (Day 14):**
- At least one *new, arms-length buyer* (not friend/family) has **paid** (money received) **$300+ USD total** for a **clearly scoped service deliverable**, with a **scheduled delivery date**.

**Payment rule:**
- Payment must be **cash collected** (deposit or paid invoice). Verbal agreements, LOIs, “committed to pay later,” and “exposure” do not count.

**Anti-scope-creep rule:**
- The $300+ engagement must be scoped to **≤ 2 hours synchronous OR ≤ 1 day async work** (unless explicitly upsold after payment).

**Proof artifacts required (for case studies + credibility):**
- Receipt/invoice screenshot
- 3-bullet scope
- Graduation-call quote/testimonial snippet

## Metrics hygiene (so diagnostics work)

Track two distinct conversion events:
- **Booked (paid):** money collected (deposit or full payment).
- **Delivered (completed):** deliverable shipped + usable testimonial.

Do not merge these; if you do, the funnel becomes impossible to diagnose.
