## Diff Summaries

### v1 to v2
90-line diff. Key changes: subtitle from "Field Manual For The Body" to "A Novel"; copyright page simplified (ISBN removed from v2); a typo fixed ("diIt" -> "It"); section break changed ("@" -> "* * *"). The revision is editorial/production cleanup.

### v2 to v3/v4 (2026 versions)
The 2026 text versions are slightly longer (+186 and +227 words vs v2). These may represent a 2026 revision cycle.

### v4 to v5 (thriller pass)
The thriller pass reduces word count by 449 words -- deliberate pacing cuts to sharpen the thriller genre elements.

## Canonical Version
2026 - Alchemy of the Wound_1.txt (57,153 words) is the latest version. The thriller pass is a genre-specific variant, not necessarily the final submission copy.

## Key Insights from Version History
The shift from "Field Manual For The Body" to "A Novel" as subtitle is a significant reframing -- moving from a hybrid/instructional framing to a straightforward genre declaration. The thriller pass suggests active development of the manuscript for a commercial submission track.
