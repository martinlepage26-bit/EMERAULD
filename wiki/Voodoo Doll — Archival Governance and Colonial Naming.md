# Voodoo Doll — Archival Governance and Colonial Naming

## Summary
Academic article arguing that the "voodoo doll" is not an authentic object-category of Haitian Vodou but a **bounded colonial archival artefact** — a classificatory fiction made institutionally durable through imperial discourse, ethnographic misrecognition, museum cataloguing, tourism, popular media, and metadata systems. Author: Martin Lepage, PhD. Full title: "The Afterlife of Colonial Naming: The 'Voodoo Doll' as Archival Problem." Central argument: the problem is not misinformation but **archival governance** — how a historically contingent colonial label becomes administratively stable and publicly authoritative. **Deferred authority through institutional repetition:** the same mechanism by which efficient portable labels outcompete accurate relational descriptions in cataloguing systems, hardening them into governance infrastructure. Repair requires contested-term protocols, provenance-aware description, and community-informed reclassification. Related to [[Sealed Card Protocol — Legitimacy, Glitch, and Charging]], [[Governance by Denial]], [[Authority, Legitimacy, and Post-Sovereignty]], and [[Historical Academic Portfolio — Pre-PHAROS Scholarly Work]].

## The Bounded Colonial Archival Artefact

The paper's core analytical category, with three mutually reinforcing senses:

1. **Semantic boundedness** — heterogeneous Afro-diasporic practices (Haitian Vodou, Louisiana Voodoo, New World Afro-diasporic spiritual practices) compressed into one portable, visually legible stereotype

2. **Material-institutional boundedness** — the term is embedded in archives, museum catalogues, accession systems, metadata fields, exhibition labels. Once in the catalogue, it becomes searchable, citable, interoperable — it functions as infrastructure

3. **Political boundedness** — racialized regimes of legibility make Black religious life intelligible through scripts of danger, primitivism, and superstition. The "voodoo doll" label is not neutral classification — it is a technology for governing how these practices are permitted to be known

---

## Deferred Authority

The mechanism by which historically contingent labels become authoritative:

**Deferred authority** = later uptake retroactively validates earlier naming. Repetition substitutes for proof.

- The colonial ethnographer names the object
- The museum inherits the name with the object
- The catalogue makes the name searchable
- The search result makes the name cite-able
- The citation makes the name authoritative

At each stage, the name's authority is *deferred to* — assumed rather than verified. The governance failure is not that anyone chose to lie, but that the system is designed to reward continuity over correction.

**The three final sentences of the paper:**
> "A name can become a shelf. A shelf can become a search result. A search result can become public memory."

---

## Portability as a Logistical Advantage

Why the "voodoo doll" label persists despite being inaccurate:

**Portability** = semantic efficiency that outcompetes accurate but relational descriptions in high-throughput public environments.

- Short, visual, pre-interpreted
- Requires no contextual knowledge to deploy
- Cross-cultural legibility: understood (incorrectly) by audiences with no knowledge of Haitian Vodou
- Easy to catalogue, tag, and retrieve

The stereotype's efficiency is its governance power. Accurate descriptions (specifying the lwa, the practitioner tradition, the ritual context) require background knowledge that most catalogue users do not have. The label wins not because it is true but because it is efficient.

This is the archival version of the fluency argument in [[Fluency, Interruption, and Institutional Accountability]]: the smooth, efficient, legacy-compatible classification outcompetes the slower, more contextual, more accurate one.

## Practitioner Counter-Description

The paper's fourth finding: practitioner counter-description reopens jurisdiction over meaning.

**The Robi material**: A practitioner (identified as Robi) reframes the object:
- Needle = index, not weapon — pointing toward a relation, not inflicting harm
- Shelf of figures = archive of care, not menace display
- The object charts a network of obligations and relations rather than enacting malicious power

This vernacular counter-theory is not an origin proof (it does not establish that "real" Vodou practices look like this). It is a **counter-description** that demonstrates that the "voodoo doll" narrative is a claim about meaning, not a report of meaning — and that practitioners have their own claims.

## Theoretical Synthesis

The paper develops an original synthesis connecting three bodies of theory:

**Mauss** (*Sociologie et anthropologie*): labels circulate through social obligations to give, receive, and return. Classificatory language behaves like a socially charged gift — once given and received, it is difficult to refuse without social cost.

**Godbout & Caillé** (1992): even modern institutions make goods serve ties. Inherited terms persist through professional loyalty, citation obligation, institutional recognition — the gift economy of academic knowledge.

**Weber** (legal-rational authority): terms harden once embedded in rule-bound systems that privilege procedural regularity, stable categories, and administrative consistency. The archive is a legal-rational authority structure; the "voodoo doll" label is an administrative fact within it.

**Foucault**: discourse, ordering, institutional regimes of truth. The "voodoo doll" is a discursive object made legible by institutions, not by practice. Its power is not inherent — it is produced by the governance apparatus.

**Favret-Saada** (witchcraft ethnography): there is no innocent external vantage. To describe an artefact as a "voodoo doll" occupies a position within a chain of utterance that reactivates classificatory violence. Method requires reflexivity as discipline, not just as posture.

## Archival Repair

The paper's concluding policy argument: repair requires more than denial or pluralization.

**Two insufficient responses refused**:
1. Simple denial: "the voodoo doll doesn't exist in Vodou" — accurate but produces its own flattening (denying all figural practice)
2. Simple pluralization: "there are many objects like this" — accepts the stereotype's frame by treating it as a category to be populated rather than dismantled

**What repair requires**:
- Contested-term protocols in archival systems — explicit flags that mark historically contingent labels as such
- Plural vocabularies in catalogue fields — practitioner terminology alongside academic/colonial terminology
- Provenance-aware description — who named this, in what context, with what authority
- Community-informed reclassification — practitioner communities have epistemic standing to reclassify objects in their traditions

This archival repair framework connects to the [[PHAROS Invention Disclosure]] classification governance architecture: the same principle applies to AI systems that inherit and replicate colonial or otherwise harmful classifications from training data.

## Methodological Commitment

The paper's methodological note: quotation marks around "voodoo doll" throughout the text are a disciplinary refusal of naturalization, not a cosmetic choice. Using the term without quotation marks treats it as a stable reference; the quotation marks mark it as a contested, historically contingent claim.

This is the same methodological position as the [[Agatha All Along — Wicca, Digital Mediatization, and Proof Regimes]] paper's refusal to conflate "figure of the witch" and "witch identity" — both papers insist that the category is the object of analysis, not the tool of analysis.

## Insights

- The "deferred authority" concept is one of the sharpest original contributions in the archive: it names the specific mechanism by which false information becomes institutionally true — not through direct assertion but through accumulation of uses that each defer to the authority of prior uses. This is directly applicable to AI training data governance: a model trained on archived metadata inherits deferred authority without any individual actor choosing to endorse the original false classification
- The portability argument explains why accuracy loses to stereotype in high-throughput environments — which is precisely the condition of AI training data ingestion: vast datasets, minimal per-item contextual review, efficiency incentives that favor consistent labeling over accurate labeling
- The Mauss synthesis is methodologically unusual: connecting gift economy theory to archival governance via the claim that institutional labels are socially obligated gifts — received, held, and difficult to return. This is a governance claim, not a cultural studies one
