### Key Definitions (Output 4)
- **Recursive**: repeated rendering, comparison, revision, and re-entry of bounded materials
- **Deterministic**: property of the governing pathway, not output identity
- **Governance**: workflow-embedded constraint design across intake → classification → transformation → approval → deployment → monitoring → audit
- **Agency**: capacity of AI outputs to shape decisions beyond the model boundary
- **Bounded corpus**: deliberately delimited materials under declared provenance/epistemic rules
- **Control extraction**: translating patterns of stability/drift into testable controls
- **Workflow binding**: attaching extracted control to real operational interface
- **Failure doctrine**: uneven oversight, forgotten branches, consent ambiguity treated as evidence

### Distinction from Meyman (Recursive Cognition Framework)
1. Meyman: recursive cognition as language-thought and epistemic architecture → RDAIG: recursive use as governance-control pipeline on bounded corpus
2. Meyman: linguistic precision, self-validation, authority-retention → RDAIG: comparative rendering, recursive return under stop rules, control extraction, workflow binding
3. Meyman: determinism as epistemic architecture → RDAIG: determinism as reconstructable governance decisions tied to actors, evidence artifacts, review intervals, auditability
