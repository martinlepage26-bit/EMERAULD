# MASTER PACK — D Drive Archive Map

## Summary

The MASTER PACK is the primary archive on `D:\` (`/mnt/d/MASTER PACK/`) containing Martin Lepage's research papers, skill corpus, governance test materials, and PHAROS lineage documents. This is the authoritative source for the [[AI Society Manuscript — From AI Anxiety to Recursive Governance]] paper line and the [[HEPHAISTOS]] skill ecosystem.

## Structure

### Paper Archive
- **`APEX_PAPERS_COMMON/`** — 10 numbered paper folders (the APEX paper line). See [[APEX Papers — Research Archive Map]] for detail.
  - 01: Recursive Deterministic AI Governance
  - 02: Loop Hinge / Spider-Man
  - 03: Afterlife of Colonial Naming (Voodoo)
  - 04: First Method Paper (Algorithmic Agentic AI, Fluency, Interruption)
  - 05: From Recursive Production to Governable Method (PHAROS program)
  - 06: The Self-Polygraph Protocol

  - 10: Governance by Denial
