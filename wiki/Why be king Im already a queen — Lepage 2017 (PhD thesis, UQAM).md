# Why be king Im already a queen — Lepage 2017 (PhD thesis, UQAM)

## Summary
Martin Lepage’s 2017 PhD thesis (UQAM, sciences des religions) studies **ritual performances and queer negotiations** inside Montreal’s neo-pagan community, with sustained attention to Wicca and Reclaiming Witchcraft. The thesis frames ritual as a site where gender/sexual identity, recognition, and authenticity are actively produced, contested, and stabilized through practice. The PhD's sociocritical method is the *extension* of the mythocritique substrate established seven years earlier in the [[Mort et Naissance et L'Ombre et le Double — MA Thesis on Yvon Rivard (Université Laval, 2010)|2010 MA thesis at Université Laval]] — see [[Mythocritique to PHAROS — The 2010 Master's Thesis as Methodological Keystone]] for the documented genealogy from MA-era mythocritique through PhD-era sociocritique to PHAROS. Foundational reference for [[Queer Sociology of Magic and Ritual]], [[Queer Theory Foundations]], and [[Martin Lepage Professional Identity]] (the doctoral anchor of the queer-pagan corpus). Connected to [[Martin Lepage Publications — Annotated Bibliography and Verification Leads]] and [[Historical Academic Portfolio — Pre-PHAROS Scholarly Work]].
