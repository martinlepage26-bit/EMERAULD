# Novel and Creative Corpus

Martin's fiction and creative nonfiction projects: the novel manuscripts, the book projects under HEX/ADECIMAL Press, and the creative works that run alongside the academic corpus.

---

## Methodological Substrate

The novels are not separate from the scholarly work — they are *textes-rites* in the lineage Yvon Rivard inaugurated for the operator at the MA level (Université Laval, 2010). Each novel carries the same theoretical charge as the academic apparatus: Phoenix-as-rebirth (alchemical transmutation), soul/double/shadow (the Egyptian spiritual triad), the Name dialectic (pronounce and release), the Witches' Road logic of charge-persistence-through-recomposition.

- [[Mythocritique to PHAROS — The 2010 Master's Thesis as Methodological Keystone]] — Establishes novels-as-textes-rites in the Rivard lineage.
- [[Mort et Naissance et L'Ombre et le Double — MA Thesis on Yvon Rivard (Université Laval, 2010)]] — The thesis on Rivard's first two novels; primary source for the textes-rites concept.
- [[Pourquoi rêver encore — Lecture allégorique et métaphysique d'Yvon Rivard]] — Recent essay revisitation: *l'ombre / le silence / le souffle* as the three formal elements that recur across the creative corpus.
- [[Narrative and Method Integration — Creative Articulations of Governance]] — Bridge between method and narrative.
