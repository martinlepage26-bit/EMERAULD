# PHAROS Skill Corpus Change Genealogy — 2026-05-06

## Summary

This note records when key skills were added, moved, mirrored, retired, renamed,
or superseded in relation to the development of PHAROS and the HEPHAISTOS
three-agent governance architecture.

It connects the skill corpus to PHAROS as a project history: not just what skills
exist, but how their authority changed as the system matured.

The closure discipline for this note is [[Delta Closure Frame — Conditions, Actors, Constraints]]: skill changes are not normalized merely by listing them, but by specifying what exists now, what used to carry authority, what superseded it, who/what routes it, and what lineage constraints remain.

## Reading Rules

- **Filesystem dates** show current disk state and are useful evidence, but they
  are not always the full origin story.
- **Vault notes and memory logs** carry the higher-level genealogy: why a skill
  moved, what authority it gained or lost, and how PHAROS routing changed.

## Governance Interpretation

The skill corpus developed in phases:

- **Capacity phase:** implementation, research, writing, and deployment skills
  accumulated.
- **Authority phase:** HEPHAISTOS, Queen Keyport, Hermes, right-arms, and
  Diamond-Eyes gave the skills a governance structure.
- **Topology phase:** Codex/Claude sync changed the corpus from role-separated
  to mirrored/superset tooling.
- **Control phase:** phantom skills, aliases, retired skills, and superseded
  routes were named so PHAROS could separate live authority from lineage.
- **Genealogy phase:** the current vault records counts, moves, retirements, and
  mirrors as project history rather than treating them as cleanup noise.
