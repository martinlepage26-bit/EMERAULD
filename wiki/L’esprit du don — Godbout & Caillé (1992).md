# L’esprit du don — Godbout & Caillé (1992)

## Summary
Godbout & Caillé argue that gift logics persist far beyond “holiday gifts” and charity: giving circulates through families, organizations, and markets, and cannot be reduced to market self-interest or state coercion. In this vault it is a modern complement to [[The Gift — Mauss (Obligation, Reciprocity, Hau)]] and supports the institution-level analysis in [[Voodoo Doll — Archival Governance and Colonial Naming]]. Reference for [[Care, Ethics, and Governance]] (gift logic as a third regime beyond market and state) and [[Ritual, Magic, and Institutional Authority]] (obligation as institutional form).

## Context
This text is explicitly part of the Mauss/Godbout/Weber synthesis tracked in [[Voodoo Doll as Archival Problem — Version Genealogy]]. It matters here because it provides a bridge from “archaic exchange” (Mauss) to modern organizational gift forms and the ways institutions naturalize obligation.

## Key Ideas (Vault-usable)
- The gift persists as a social form across domains (family, organizations, art markets, mutual aid).
- “Market or State” is an incomplete ontology for social relations; gift logics are a third persistent regime.
