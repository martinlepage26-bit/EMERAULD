# MASTER BIBLIOGRAPHY — Theoretical Genealogy of LOTUS

> **CORRECTION NOTICE (2026-05-03):** This note's theorist-mapping table (Bourdieu → S, Foucault → K, Charles Taylor → C, Honneth → S, Crenshaw → multi-dim, Halberstam → Sh, etc.) reflects **PhD-era citations, not the MA-era substrate**. The deeper genealogy of LOTUS runs through the **2010 master's thesis at Université Laval** (mythocritique on Yvon Rivard), whose bibliography names a different corpus entirely: Frye, Eliade, Greimas, Brunel, Durand, Bachelard, Bernard, Kolpaktchy, Rachet, Vivenza/Guénon, Riffard. The Egyptian spiritual triad (*âme/double/ombre*) — not Bourdieu/Foucault/Butler — is the direct ancestor of LOTUS dimensions. **For the corrected substrate analysis, see [[Mythocritique to PHAROS — The 2010 Master's Thesis as Methodological Keystone]] and [[Mort et Naissance et L'Ombre et le Double — MA Thesis on Yvon Rivard (Université Laval, 2010)]].** The PhD-era theorist mapping below remains valid as an analysis of how the operator's *PhD-era* extension of the apparatus shows up in the layered working bibliography file, but the LOTUS architecture's deeper structural source is Egyptian/Hermetic, not French queer/post-structuralist.

## Summary

Documentary analysis of `MASTER BIBLIOGRAPHY.txt` (997 lines) — the layered, pre-merge bibliography that serves as Martin Lepage's working cross-reference file for [[Dr. Sort and LOTUS Ownership Decision — March 2026|Dr. Sort]] and, materially, as a **theoretical receipt for the PhD-era extension of the LOTUS substrate**. The file makes traceable a claim already named in passing in [[Bonded Intelligence Under Constraint — The LOTUS Processor Framework]] but never sourced: that the [[LOTUS Model — Agency and Social Positioning|LOTUS vector model]] inherits theoretical scaffolding from Martin's PhD-era ethnographic work on queer pagan agency in Montreal. **The deeper substrate (the MA-era mythocritical/Egyptian/Hermetic apparatus) is documented separately in [[Mythocritique to PHAROS — The 2010 Master's Thesis as Methodological Keystone]].** This note covers the PhD-era extension layer; the keystone covers the MA-era substrate. Related to [[Governance and PHAROS MOC]], [[LOTUS Model and Agency]], and [[Historical Academic Portfolio — Pre-PHAROS Scholarly Work]].

## Context

Raw source: `raw sources/MASTER BIBLIOGRAPHY — pre-merge layered corpus.txt`, recovered 2026-05-03 from `/home/cerebrhoe/dr-sort/pdf_sorter_v1/`. This makes the file simultaneously a working reference list, a theoretical scaffold, and an operational input to a tool Martin built that maps Martin's own corpus.

## Trace 2 — Theoretical Apparatus Mapping (PhD → LOTUS)

The PhD-era theoretical framework, reconstructable from Layer 5 of the bibliography, maps onto LOTUS's six-dimensional vector with high specificity. Each mapping has direct citation evidence:

| LOTUS dimension | PhD theoretical frame | Bibliography evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **P** — Perceptual latitude | Bourdieu's *misrecognition* + Goffman's frame analysis | Bourdieu 1982 (line 388, also 40); Goffman 1959 (line 514) |
| **R** — Regulatory bandwidth | Foucault's governmentality + Mauss techniques du corps | Foucault 1975, 1976 (lines 481–482, also 256, 852); Mauss 1968 (line 70, also 637, 964) |

The **Constraint vector** `K = [body, time, economic, relational, institutional, stigma, isolation, urgency]` maps directly onto the PhD-era list of marginalization axes catalogued across Layer 5: body (Foucault, Mauss techniques du corps), institutional (Foucault *Surveiller et punir*), stigma (Goffman, Sedgwick), relational (queer pagan community ethnography).

The **Scaffold vector** `G = [relational, material, clinical, cultural, spiritual, organizational, interpretive]` maps onto the PhD-era list of restoring forces: relational (Reclaiming community ethnography, Salomonsen 2002), spiritual (Starhawk 1979, 1990), interpretive (hermeneutic ritual studies — Schechner, Turner, Bell).

## Methodological Isomorphism Claim

The bibliography supports the following descriptive claim with multi-source confirmation:

> LOTUS's six dimensions, constraint vector, and scaffold vector are not generated from AI governance theory. They are operationalizations of the theoretical apparatus Martin developed across his ethnographic and theoretical work on queer/pagan agency under heteronormative institutional constraint (1995–2018), translated into a computational form (2024–2026) where the constraining institutions are AI platforms and governance regimes.

**Triangulation:**
- **Trace A (bibliography):** explicit theorist-by-theorist mapping above
- **Trace B (vault):** [[Bonded Intelligence Under Constraint — The LOTUS Processor Framework]] already names "long-standing anti-individualism … from queer Pagan community studies through AI governance" (line 36 of that note)
- **Trace C (corpus):** Continuous publication record in bibliography — Lepage 2013, 2015, 2017a, 2017b, 2018 — using identical theoretical vocabulary that surfaces in LOTUS specifications

**Bounded conclusion:** This is a *descriptive/associational* claim about documentary lineage. It is not a *causal* claim about Martin's intellectual process — that would require authorial attestation. The bibliography proves the lineage; only Martin can confirm the intent.
