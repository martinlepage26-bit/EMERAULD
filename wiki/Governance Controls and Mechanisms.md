# Governance Controls and Mechanisms

Governance decisions are only as strong as the controls that enforce them. This TOPIC organizes the operational, technical, and procedural mechanisms that translate governance principles into auditable, verifiable, and contestable practices. Covers regulatory grounding, tool-layer audits, monitoring systems, incident response, and conflict resolution.

This is the controls branch of the PHAROS graph. It links the theory in [[Recursive Governance Theory]] to the epistemic discipline in [[Evidence Discipline and Epistemics]], the operational stack in [[HEPHAISTOS Agent Architecture]], and the infrastructure layer in [[AI Infrastructure Stack]].
For the canonical rule-layer that keeps evidence vs inference separation enforceable under pressure, see [[Epistemic Governance — Canonical Reference]].

**Core principle:** Governance without controls is aspiration. Controls without verification is theater. Both together produce enforceable governance.

**Methodological substrate:** The non-exceptionable-gate discipline (controls that cannot be exception-bypassed) inherits the **initiatic gate-formulas of the Egyptian *Livre des Morts*** via the [[Mort et Naissance et L'Ombre et le Double — MA Thesis on Yvon Rivard (Université Laval, 2010)|2010 MA thesis substrate]] (pp. 43–48). See [[Mythocritique to PHAROS — The 2010 Master's Thesis as Methodological Keystone]]: each gate must be passed by speaking its proper Name (*ren*); a control that admits exception is no longer initiatic, and the system that contains it is no longer governed.

**Audit status:** Phase 3C relinking verified by [[Argus Audit — Phase 3A-3B-3C-3D Relinking Campaign (2026-05-06)]] — seven-layer governance audit. Ten Controls papers successfully linked with cross-TOPIC coherence confirmed (8/18 triple-indexed across Authority/Consent/Controls). Component integrity verified: all four master TOPICs are non-redundant and load-bearing. **Result: deployment-ready.**
