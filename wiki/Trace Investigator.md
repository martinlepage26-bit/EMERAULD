# Trace Investigator

## Summary

Trace Investigator is the vault-facing note for the `trace-investigator` skill. It tracks how authority, accountability, definitions, exceptions, and monitoring signals move across mixed document packs, with explicit separation between direct evidence, supported inference, and speculation.

## What it is for

- Comparing policies, checklists, appendices, emails, dashboards, SOPs, charters, and mixed governance artifacts
- Tracing who defines, who decides, who inherits, and who disappears
- Separating formal revision from informal norm change
- Flagging where monitoring or exception handling softens, narrows, or widens governance meaning
