# AI Governance Public Statement and Market Impact Pack

Root Documents contained a small governance-public-positioning packet: short and long public statements, a market-impact analysis for governed multi-agent infrastructure, a French/Quebec governance extraction file, an AI & Society title-page ODT, and several AI governance reference PDFs.

## Public Positioning

The two public-statement drafts frame AI governance as serious, transparent, accountable, and commercially enabling rather than innovation-blocking. The stronger version is the longer draft because it moves beyond principle into concrete buyer language: oversight, human review, risk monitoring, compliance, decision traceability, and resilience under scrutiny.

## Market Thesis

The `if-gov-ai-2027-2028.md` file argues that multi-agent AI will split into two markets:

- systems that can prove governance through audit trails, external evidence surfaces, controls, and bounded claims;
- systems that cannot prove governance and will be blocked by regulated procurement.

The key move is commercial: governance infrastructure becomes the enterprise adoption layer for agentic systems in healthcare, finance, legal, infrastructure, and public-sector contexts.

## Submission Support

It belongs with [[AI Society Manuscript — From AI Anxiety to Recursive Governance]] as submission apparatus, not as a standalone paper.
