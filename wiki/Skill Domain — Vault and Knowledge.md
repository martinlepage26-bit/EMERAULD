# Skill Domain — Vault and Knowledge

## Summary

The Vault and Knowledge domain contains the skills that maintain, query, and evolve [[EMERAULD — Vault and Knowledge Graph|EMERAULD]] as a live knowledge graph: retrieval, graph hygiene, provenance tracing, memory system design, and comprehensive synthesis sweeps. These skills are the operational backbone of Trismégiste's continuity work and sit at the intersection of [[Martin Lepage — Authored Skills|Martin's authored skill set]] and the [[Skill Ecosystem — Professional Capability Registry]]. The vault cluster — Ask Vault, genealogy-loupe, boil-the-ocean, tabula-rasa, Vault Graph Hygiene — is the identity layer that makes EMERAULD self-sustaining across sessions. Related domains include [[Skill Domain — Skill Architecture]] (meta-layer for skill maintenance) and [[Skill Domain — AI and LLM Tools]] (vector store and search tooling).
