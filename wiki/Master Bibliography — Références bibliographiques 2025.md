# Master Bibliography — Références bibliographiques 2025

## Summary

Comprehensive bibliography document (2025) by [[Martin Lepage — Professional Profile|Martin Lepage]] compiling references across the full research corpus — neo-Pagan/Wicca studies, queer theory, digital religion, popular culture analysis, and AI governance. Serves as the master reference list for the [[Legitimacy Machines — Book Skeleton and Architecture|Legitimacy Machines]] book project and associated papers. Part of [[Research and Papers MOC]].

## Context

This bibliography sits at the intersection of all research streams in [[Martin Lepage — Professional Profile|Martin Lepage]]'s corpus. It references sources used across the Pagan ethnography papers (from [[The Inner Goddess — Gender Normativity and Gay Identity in Wiccan Ritualities]] through [[Queer Neo-Pagan Spiritualities and the Question of Authenticity in Wicca]]), the popular culture analyses ([[Agatha All Along — Wicca, Digital Mediatization, and Proof Regimes]], [[For Her Alone to Wield — Buffy Power as Infrastructure]], [[Avatar — Lecture queer du pouvoir incarné]]), and the governance work ([[PHAROS Recalibration — Unified Governance Architecture]]). The 2025 date places this as a late-corpus compilation, likely assembled for the book or the Social Compass submission.

- The bibliography confirms the interdisciplinary range of the corpus: religious studies, queer theory, media studies, and AI governance are all represented
