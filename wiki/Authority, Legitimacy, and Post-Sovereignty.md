# Authority, Legitimacy, and Post-Sovereignty

Authority operates through legitimacy — the mechanisms by which power becomes believable, contestable, or invisible. When those mechanisms break, what forms of authority remain possible? This TOPIC organizes papers exploring authority after institutional legitimacy collapses, the construction and contestation of legitimacy, and governance in post-sovereign conditions.

This is the authority/legitimacy branch of the PHAROS governance-values graph. It sits beside [[Care, Ethics, and Governance]] and [[Consent and Boundary Frameworks]] as the legitimacy leg of the subgraph, and it links back to [[Governance and PHAROS MOC]].

**Core question:** How is authority assembled, made legible, and contested — or denied contestation?

**Methodological substrate:** The post-sovereign reading is grounded in the [[Mythocritique to PHAROS — The 2010 Master's Thesis as Methodological Keystone|2010 MA thesis substrate]]: Hermetic mentalism (Kybalion principle 1, MA p. 80, OD: 187 — *"de divin que cette pensée en nous qui à chaque instant crée l'univers"*) grounds why governance cannot be sovereign-authority-from-outside; the universe is created at every instant by thought; authority must be initiatic-from-within, not external-warrant. The PHAROS Springer claim *"AI cannot govern itself as sovereign authority"* derives from this substrate.

**Audit status:** Phase 3A relinking verified by [[Argus Audit — Phase 3A-3B-3C-3D Relinking Campaign (2026-05-06)]] — seven-layer governance audit. Eight Authority papers successfully linked with cross-TOPIC coherence confirmed and zero authority drift detected. **Result: deployment-ready.**

## Authority After Legitimacy Collapse

When the institutions that once grounded authority are no longer believable, five models of authority become visible: abdication, administrative sovereignty, care without claim, glitch sovereignty, and relational authority without resolution.

- [[Authority After Legitimacy — Disenchantment and Queer Political Theory]] — Analysis of *Disenchantment* as a queer political text showing five models of authority after institutional legitimacy collapses. Abdication (Oona), administrative sovereignty (Archdruidess), care without claim (Ursula), glitch sovereignty (Bean), relational authority without resolution (Mora).
- [[Governance by Denial]] — Political theory of democratic hollowing: how institutions produce formal rights without access. Diagnoses the political conditions that make recursive governance necessary. The breakdown of institutional legitimacy as prerequisite for the method.

## Legitimacy Machines

How institutions assemble legitimacy through narrative, repetition, and ritual. The concept of a "ritual machine" — a narrative system that produces authority and becomes invisible as it succeeds.

- [[Ritual Machine Lab — Legitimacy Machines Introduction]] — Book introduction defining the **ritual machine** concept: narrative systems producing authority through repetition, becoming invisible through success. Opens into Buffy analysis.
- [[Legitimacy Machines — Book Skeleton and Architecture]] — Monograph in progress: *Legitimacy Machines, Altars of Control: Governing Wonder and Training Power Across Screen Worlds*. Seven screen objects analyzed as ritual machines. Three movements: enclosure and its failures, redistribution without infrastructure, post-sovereign polity.

## Authority Without Ethics

What happens when authority operates without the care (answerability) that makes it governable.

- [[Authority Without Ethics — The Love Witch]] — Analysis of ritual power and seduction in *The Love Witch* (2016). Authority as charm rather than justification. Chapter 5 of *Legitimacy Machines*. Demonstrates the failure mode when authority refuses answerability.
- [[The Familiars of Hollywood — Queerness, Whiteness, and Representation]] — *What We Do in the Shadows* as allegory for Hollywood's vampiric economy of visibility. Colin Robinson (whiteness as banal extractive power); Guillermo (queer Latinx servitude). Authority through consumption rather than legitimacy.
- [[Authority Without Power-Over]] — **Binding principle #6** of [[HEPHAISTOS Agent Architecture]]: authority is stewardship, not dominion. Authority that functions without extracting submission; governance without power-over as structural alternative to domination.

## Institutional Authority and Power

How power is exercised through institutional systems; how authority becomes naturalized and invisible.

- [[For Her Alone to Wield — Buffy Power as Infrastructure]] — Slayer power as distributed, contestable infrastructure rather than individual property. Enclosure → redistribution → fragmentation framework for authority.
- [[Transmutation and the Body — Queer Ethics in Fullmetal Alchemist]] — *FMA* as state theology. How authority naturalizes itself through bodily discipline and equivalent exchange.
- [[Avatar — Lecture queer du pouvoir incarné]] — Queer reading of *Avatar* analyzing the body as a site of curriculum and authority training. Chapter III of *Legitimacy Machines*.

## Post-Sovereign Polity and Governance

Governance in conditions where state sovereignty is fragmented, challenged, or explicitly refused.

- [[Unnatural Selection — Jurassic Park and the Posthuman Commons]] — Queer posthumanist reading arguing the law cannot protect what it cannot name. Governance systems must include provisions for entities resisting classification.
- [[Still Running — Willow, Anya, and Queer Ritual Infrastructure]] — Willow and Anya as posthuman ritual technologies. Governance through procedure rather than sovereign command.
- [[The Scythe Already in Motion — Buffy, Queer Ritual, and the Politics of Glitch]] — Post-canonical Buffy fragments in queer/trans magical practice. Glitch as media fragments becoming operational ritual technologies. Governance outside official channels.
- [[Agent Orchestration — PHAROS Launch as Governed Multi-Agent Execution]] — Three-agent stack (HEPHAISTOS/Queen Keyport/Hermes) as **distributed authority without sovereignty**. Scope → governance → routing as non-hierarchical authority distribution. Live operational case.
- [[Algorithmic Agentic AI and Governance — From Hegemonic Fluency to the Ethics of Interruption]] — Algorithmic authority as fluency (naturalizing systems as invisible). Ethics of interruption as contested authority. Governance depends on preserving contestation, not perfecting smoothness.

## Authority and Legibility

How authority becomes legible or illegible; how visibility and representation shape governance.

- [[Sealed Card Protocol — Legitimacy, Glitch, and Charging]] — Academic manuscript analyzing AI authenticity controversies through five concepts: glitch, seam, charging, legibility, and legitimacy. Authority requires legibility.
- [[On Phantom Publics, Clusters, and Collectives — Petersmann and Van Den Meerssche 2024]] — Algorithmic clustering as subject-making. How governance creates subjects that lack ordinary recognition pathways, shifting evidence discipline from output fairness to subject-making conditions.
- [[Spider-Man and the Phonetic Glitch — Voice as Infrastructure]] — Phonetic legitimacy as governance mechanism. Voice as infrastructure of authority.
- [[Voodoo Doll — Archival Governance and Colonial Naming]] — **Deferred authority** through institutional repetition: colonial labels harden into archival legitimacy. Naming-as-infrastructure; how efficient portable labels outcompete accurate but relational descriptions in cataloguing systems. Practitioner counter-description reopens jurisdiction over meaning.

- [[Ritual Machine Lab — Legitimacy Machines Introduction]] — The ritual machine only functions when *believed*. Its breakdown (refusal) is resistance.
