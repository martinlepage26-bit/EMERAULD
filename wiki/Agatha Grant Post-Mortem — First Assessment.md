# Agatha Grant Post-Mortem — First Assessment

## Summary

Two raw AI-session capture files from the `1stAgatha post-mortem/` folder document two distinct but concurrent threads in Martin Lepage's life during the Agatha agent period: a proposed art grant project (WHEELS OF WILL) and a Calian employment contract review. These are not formal post-mortem documents; they are unedited ChatGPT conversation exports capturing live deliberation. They sit in the archive as first-pass evidence of the Agatha agent's operational scope during an early session. Related to the broader [[Agatha All Along — Wicca, Digital Mediatization, and Proof Regimes]] research line.

## Context

The `1stAgatha post-mortem/` folder is part of the [[MASTER PACK — D Drive Archive Map]] archive. The two files represent the outputs of what appears to be one of the first sessions in which an AI agent (Agatha) was used for dual creative-professional work. The Agatha agent's context at this stage combined academic and arts-practice work with employment documentation — a pattern consistent with the broader [[AI Personas — Agatha, DOTTIE, and MOBI]] multiplex described elsewhere in the vault.

## File 1: `1stAgatah-grant-ST.txt` — WHEELS OF WILL Art Grant

### Content

A ChatGPT session in which Martin describes the WHEELS OF WILL photographic art project and requests a formal grant draft. The project:

- **Title**: WHEELS OF WILL
- **Discipline**: Interdisciplinary / Visual Art / Research-Creation
- **Core question**: Can a viewer distinguish an authentic ritual Pagan artifact from a staged prop? Has digital commodification supplanted spiritual substance?
- **Method**: For each of 8 Sabbats and 13 Esbats (21 festival cycles), 8 photographs of the same artifact — one post-ritual, others staged — will challenge the viewer's visual literacy
- **Artifacts**: apple skulls, witch bottles, Yule logs, maypoles, tarot spreads, ritual knives (athame, boline), clay love-spell figures, incense and powders
- **Educational component**: digital zine, open studio visits, metalworking courses at Les Forges de Montréal
- **Budget**: ~$11,000 (Canada Council for the Arts / CALQ targeting)
- **Duration**: One year (13 moons, 8 sabbats)

### Scholarly grounding

The project draws directly from Martin's doctoral thesis on ritual performativity and gender identity among LGBTQ Pagans in Montreal and Ontario — see [[Agatha All Along — Wicca, Digital Mediatization, and Proof Regimes]] for the academic context. The grant treats this line of inquiry as a pivot from academic research to artistic interpretation, allowing symbolic and performative registers to take precedence over scholarly argument.

The central research question — whether digital image saturation has displaced space for authentic material culture — maps onto the proof-regime analysis in [[Agatha All Along — Wicca, Digital Mediatization, and Proof Regimes]], specifically the question of how digital mediatization transforms what counts as evidence of spiritual authenticity.

### Assessment

The grant draft is competent and field-appropriate for Canada Council / CALQ submission. The conceptual framing is strong; the budget is plausible. This file captures Martin's intent at the ideation and early-draft stage, not a final submission artifact.

## File 2: `AgathaetCalian.txt` — Calian Employment Contract Review

### Content

A ChatGPT session in which Martin asks for a section-by-section French-language review of a Calian/Taliyan Clinical Research Coordinator employment contract (`Calian CRO – Employment Agreement – Lepage, Martin SD27OCT2025.docx`). The session establishes:

- **Role**: Clinical (Research) Coordinator at Calian/Taliyan
- **Request**: Full analysis in French, section by section, with commentary on implications, risks, and negotiating points
- **Context**: Martin identifies this as "mon nouveau contrat" — a live document at the signature stage

### Assessment

This file documents a simultaneous employment decision running in parallel with the WHEELS OF WILL creative work. It is relevant to the [[CDPDJ Complaint — Lepage v Calian and Novartis]] file in the vault: this contract appears to be the foundational employment agreement from which the subsequent dispute arose. The session does not contain the actual contract text — only the AI's structured response protocol (waiting for section-by-section instruction) — so the full evidentiary content of the contract is not captured here.

## Post-Mortem Interpretation

The two files together show the Agatha agent operating simultaneously across a creative grant application and an employment legal review — a dual-track session pattern. The label "post-mortem" in the folder name most likely refers to a retrospective assessment of how Agatha performed across these two domains, not a project failure autopsy. Neither file contains explicit post-mortem commentary; both are raw capture.
