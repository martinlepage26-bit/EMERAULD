# Delta Closure Frame — Conditions, Actors, Constraints

## Summary

A delta is not an abstract gap. It is a structured discrepancy between a present condition and a target condition inside a field of action.

This frame is the operational doctrine for turning "delta" language into named condition, measure, time, authority, labor, and constraint. It applies to PHAROS method work, vault genealogy, skill-corpus cleanup, evidence gaps, product delivery, client operations, and institutional governance.

## Doctrine

A delta is not an abstract gap. It is a structured discrepancy between a present condition and a target condition inside an institutional field of action. Most talk about "closing deltas" is useless because it treats discrepancy as self-explanatory, as though naming a shortfall were already a mode of acting on it. It is not. A delta becomes intelligible only when the relation between present and target is specified in operational terms: what condition obtains now, what condition would count as closure, by what metric movement is judged, within what time horizon, through whose authority, by whose labor, and under what constraints, dependencies, or veto points. Without that, "delta" is only managerial deodorant for an unanalyzed problem.

The decisive issue is agency. A delta does not close because it has been well described. It closes only if identifiable actors can alter the relevant condition under determinate limits. That is why the language of "ownership" so often obscures more than it reveals. Authority and execution are frequently split; the one who can authorize movement is often not the one who must carry it out, and neither may fully control the dependencies that govern success. Any formulation that collapses these distinctions reproduces the institutional mystification it pretends to clarify.

## Operational Frame

```text
current state -> target state | metric | deadline | decision-maker | responsible actors | constraints/dependencies
```

That frame matters not as a template to be filled ritualistically, but as a discipline against vagueness. It forces the speaker to name condition, measure, time, authority, labor, and obstruction at the level where change either becomes possible or fails.

A delta closes when named actors can move a named condition by a named date under named constraints. Anything less is not analysis, but atmosphere.

## Field Definitions

| Field | Question it forces | Failure if omitted |
|---|---|---|
| current state | What condition obtains now? | The gap becomes mood or complaint. |
| target state | What observable condition would count as closure? | "Done" remains unfalsifiable. |
| metric | By what measure is movement judged? | Revenue, trust, quality, compliance, coherence, and delivery get falsely merged. |
| deadline | By when must movement occur? | The gap becomes indefinitely discussable. |
| decision-maker | Who can authorize or unlock the change? | Diagnosis lacks authority. |
| responsible actors | Who must carry the work out? | Authority is confused with labor. |
| constraints/dependencies | What limits, sequencing conditions, or veto points govern movement? | Obstruction becomes a junk drawer called "blockers." |

## Use When

- A governance gap has been named but no actor, constraint, or consequence has been specified.
- A PHAROS artifact is marked incomplete, blocked, or ready with bounded gaps.
- A vault genealogy delta needs to distinguish current state, lineage state, and closure state.
- A skill-corpus cleanup must separate existence truth, routing truth, and lineage truth.
- A client or product task is described as "almost there" but lacks metric, owner authority, or concrete unblock path.
- An evidence gap is being treated as a writing problem rather than a source, verification, or provenance problem.

## Anti-Patterns

- **Gap language without poles:** naming a delta without current and target conditions.
- **Owner-only language:** hiding authority, labor, veto, and dependency behind one nominal owner.
- **Blocker dumping:** using "blockers" as an undifferentiated container for constraints, dependencies, missing permissions, missing evidence, and institutional resistance.
- **Description-as-action:** treating a well-written diagnosis as if it were itself movement.
- **False singular causality:** pretending one actor can close a delta when authority, execution, and dependency control are distributed.

## PHAROS Interpretation

PHAROS cannot treat closure as rhetorical completion. Closure is a consequence-bound state change. The method's promotion statuses, evidence hierarchy, and governance controls only work if the current condition, target condition, adjudication measure, authority chain, responsible actors, and constraints are all visible.

This is why the skill-corpus genealogy pass matters: the system did not close the delta by choosing one count and erasing the others. It closed the delta by naming the different truths in play: live existence, canonical routing, mirror state, and lineage traces.

The same rule applies to evidentiary gaps, product delivery, publication hardening, client operations, and agent governance. A later state does not erase the earlier one. It changes its authority under named conditions.
