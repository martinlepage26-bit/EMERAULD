# Argus Audit — Phase 3A-3B-3C-3D Relinking Campaign (2026-05-06)

**Independent meta-governance audit** of the EMERAULD vault Phase 3A/3B/3C/3D relinking campaign (23 papers relinked across 4 master TOPICs). Audit verified zero coherence failures, zero narrative-reality gaps, zero charm/fluency capture, and zero authority drift. **Deployment-ready.** Campaign is load-bearing and eligible for Phase 4 (Final Polish) and loop closure.

- **23 relinked papers** (8 Phase 3A Authority, 6 Phase 3B Consent, 10 Phase 3C Controls, 5 Phase 3D Disability)
