# Service Offer Prompts — GPT-Assisted Revenue Design Templates

## Summary

Five structured XML-format prompts designed for GPT-5.2 (or equivalent) to generate freelance offer architecture, 30-day cash plans, paid challenge designs, premium day-rate offers, and three-offer service packages. Each prompt specifies role, context (Martin's background: Independent Researcher, AI Governance Consultant, Clinical Coordinator), task, output verbosity, scope constraints, and a self-check gate. Companion to [[PHAROS AI Governance Service — Offer Architecture and Outreach Templates]].

## Context

These prompts were produced as reusable inputs for revenue-design sessions. They encode Martin's positioning constraints (no big brand, direct outreach, trust-based selling, no paid ads) and connect to the [[PHAROS Commercial Strategy]] intent to build a repeatable AI governance consulting offer ladder. They are not one-time outputs — they are generative templates that can be re-run with updated context.

## Details

**Five prompts:**
1. **Freelance Offer Prompt** — generates 3 offers with pricing, outreach DM/email, and ChatGPT delivery workflow
2. **30-Day Cash Plan Prompt** — day-by-day plan with conversion checkpoints and daily ChatGPT tasks
3. **Seven-Day Paid Challenge Prompt** — challenge concept, transformation, daily breakdown, fastest free-traffic launch plan
4. **AI-Powered Day Rate Prompt** — $1,000 day-rate offer design with no-call sales process and ChatGPT delivery workflow
5. **Three Immediate Service Offers Prompt** — three services ranked by speed to revenue, ease of delivery, and market trust

**Structural convention:** Each prompt uses `<role>`, `<context>`, `<task>`, `<output_verbosity_spec>`, `<design_and_scope_constraints>`, `<uncertainty_and_ambiguity>`, and `<high_risk_self_check>` tags. This is an early version of the structured prompt discipline also seen in [[PHAROS Method — Technical Reference]].
