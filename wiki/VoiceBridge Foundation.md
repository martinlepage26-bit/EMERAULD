# VoiceBridge Foundation

## Summary

VoiceBridge is a minimum-viable nonprofit project providing an AAC (augmentative and alternative communication) bridge fund for people with speech and communication disabilities who face cost, delay, or coverage gaps. Project files at `~/VoiceBridge/`. As of 2026-06, all five minimum tools, a basic website, outreach templates, and an incorporation purposes draft are complete. Connected to [[Host Environment — pharos-corpus-runner-01]] as a live project and to [[Personal and Projects MOC]] as a personal initiative.

## Context

Built on a strict minimum-effort launch model: do not add programs, clinics, training institutes, or national campaigns before pilot evidence exists. The lowest-effort launch is a small bridge fund operated through referral partners covering urgent AAC needs (app licenses, tablet setups, short-term SLP consultations, low-tech boards).

## Launch Status (2026-06)

**Ready to launch.** Blockers are external:
- Engage counsel for incorporation
- Secure first 3 referral partners
- Recruit lived-experience reviewers
- Raise first $25k–$50k

**Rule:** Do not build more structure until the pilot generates evidence.
