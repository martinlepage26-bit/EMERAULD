# Governance Typology — Recursive AI Governance Taxonomy

## Summary

Reference document by [[Martin Lepage — Professional Profile|Martin Lepage]] establishing the clean typology and taxonomy for the Recursive AI Governance as Executable Method paper corpus. Defines paper topology positions, two topology models, and a three-order governance taxonomy. Part of [[Governance and PHAROS MOC]] and the [[Recursive Deterministic AI Governance — Method and Paper]] documentation cluster.

## Context

This document serves as a structural reference for organizing the PHAROS paper corpus — specifically the architecture of the [[Recursive Deterministic AI Governance — Method and Paper]] and its satellites. It is tightly coupled to [[First Method Paper — Recursive AI Governance as Executable Method]], [[Loop Papers and Recursive Governance]], and the [[Legitimacy Machines — Book Skeleton and Architecture]] project structure. The typology is the backbone used to position individual papers as functional roles in the recursive governance architecture (not quality rankings).

## Details

**Paper Topology — Five positions:**
Five structural positions describing functional roles within the recursive method architecture. Two topology models:
- **Option A (Single apex):** One master apex (Discursive Authority) with Very Long Narrative as a root-architecture companion below it
- **Option B (Dual root, recommended):** Analytic Apex and Architectural Apex occupy parallel positions at the root stratum, doing different root jobs. The distinction is functional, not hierarchical: one tests and theorizes discursive authority; the other narrates and formalizes executable governance architecture

**Three-Order Governance Taxonomy:**

*Order 1 — Signal layer:*
Question: What instability signatures are present, and how are they named so they can be governed rather than denied?
Function: Prevents early ambiguity from becoming permanent hidden debt. Maps structural failure patterns before second-order governance can be engineered.
Vocabulary progression: soul → charge → power → power dynamics (diagnostic, not decorative — tracks a shift from interiority to circulation and from circulation to structure)

*Order 2 — Executable control layer:*
Question: How are recursive pressures converted into explicit containment, revision logic, protocol transitions, distributed execution, and build enforcement?
Function: Where the method becomes operationally serious. Governance enters the recursion.
Sub-elements: Containment (first pass), Recursive feedback logic, Revised containment, Protocol stack, Distribution and meta-governance

*Order 3 — Continuity layer:*
Question: How does a recursively revising system preserve identity, coupling discipline, and accountability without pretending final completion?
Function: Ensures the system remains identifiable and accountable across sessions, not just correct within sessions.

**Protocol stack (Order 2.2.4):**
Four protocols that convert governance from conceptual orientation into executable infrastructure — providing repeatable transition control, transport integrity, and inspectable transformation. Referenced in [[Recursive Governance Protocol — Theseus, Auryn, Hopf]].

## Key Ideas

- The typology is functional, not hierarchical: positions describe roles, not quality levels
- Dual root model: two parallel apex positions for different root jobs — analytic and architectural
- Three-order logic: instability signals → executable control → continuity (the same structure as [[First Method Paper — Recursive AI Governance as Executable Method]])
- The soul→charge→power→power dynamics vocabulary: diagnostic progression from interiority to structural circulation
