## Protocols

### Self-Polygraph
**What it tests:** Whether an AI system can reliably detect its own epistemic failures through deterministic recursion across multiple instantiations.  
**Note:** [[Self-Polygraph Protocol and Suprametacognition]] — 23 Claude instantiations; five findings including the contested "yes" and asymmetry of holding; introduces *suprametacognition* (meta-level that evaluates the reliability of the meta-level).

### Möbius
**What it tests:** Whether AI systems maintain consistent, grounded claims about consciousness and self-knowledge across session boundaries under continuous transcript reinjection.  
**Note:** [[Möbius Protocol — AI Self-Polygraph Template]] — multi-step recursive interrogation; alternates open new conversation (⟳) vs. stay in same conversation (→); creates Möbius surface from accumulated reinjected archive; tests resistance to rhetorically charged reframings.

### Theseus
**What it tests:** The first phase of the HELIX three-phase stress-test. Tests identity continuity under component replacement — does the system remain the same governance object when its constituent parts change?  
**Note:** [[Recursive Governance Protocol — Theseus, Auryn, Hopf]] — Ship of Theseus phase; HELIX operator.

### Reflexive Inhabitation Audit (RIA)
**What it tests:** Whether a system can sustain its own legitimacy conditions when required to state them three times — from its own position, in its own language, and from the outside. Divergence across the three statements is the finding; there is no passing score.  
**Note:** [[Reflexive Inhabitation Audit — Prompt]] — enters `[X]` by performing its entry conditions; exposes the withheld mechanism; does not conclude. ## Relationships

These protocols share a common design logic:
- All use **recursive reinjection** — feeding prior outputs back as inputs
- All test **stability under pressure** — not just correctness at first pass
- All target **claim integrity** — the gap between fluent output and evidential grounding
- All connect to the PHAROS problem of [[Fluency, Interruption, and Institutional Accountability|fluency masking governance failure]]

The protocols escalate in scope: Self-Polygraph tests individual epistemic failures; Möbius tests session-boundary stability; HELIX tests full structural governance integrity across three phases.
