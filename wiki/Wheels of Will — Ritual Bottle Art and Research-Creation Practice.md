# Wheels of Will — Ritual Bottle Art and Research-Creation Practice

## Summary

Wheels of Will is Martin Lepage's ritual bottle art and research-creation practice. It uses sealed objects, bottle photographs, residue, and documentation to test how mediation, charging, and legitimacy become visible under scrutiny. In the archive, it functions as the artistic observatory for the Sealed Card, SAKURA, and Phase 1 method clusters.

## Practice

- Ritual bottle photographs
- Sealed and charged objects
- Documentation as method
- Controlled tests of when mediation becomes inferable
- Artistic parallel to governance and proof-regime analysis
