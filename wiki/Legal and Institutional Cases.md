Martin's legal proceedings, institutional accountability work, and boundary protocols: cases where institutional fluency produces harm and formal systems are used to contest it.

## Active Legal Cases

- [[CDPDJ Complaint — Lepage v Calian and Novartis]] — Formal human rights complaint filed 2026-03-20 with the Commission des droits de la personne et des droits de la jeunesse (Québec). Grounds: discrimination based on handicap (stuttering/voice loss) and social condition (precarious contract worker). Four legal arguments, four remedies sought. Martin as both complainant and PHAROS researcher.
- [[TAL Housing Application — Imeson v Esplanades Cartier (2026-05-11)]] — Tenant-side TAL filing package with rent-reduction and damages requests tied to prolonged disturbance, security/theft harm, unresolved maintenance, and lease-termination contestation.
- [[Service Canada EI Delay Letter — Housing Risk Escalation (2026-05-10)]] — Administrative escalation letter documenting prolonged EI delay, weak procedural ownership, and direct housing-stability risk.
- [[Consent, Fraud, and Boundary Protocols]] — Documentation of a bait-and-switch impersonation sexual encounter. Canadian legal analysis of consent. Practical boundary-setting protocols developed in its aftermath.
