# PHAROS LinkedIn April 2026 Publishing Routine

Root Documents contained an April 2026 LinkedIn publishing packet for PHAROS. The packet documents the transition from Govern AI / govern-ai.ca framing to PHAROS / pharos-ai.ca and maps an April thought-leadership sequence into a lower-volume, higher-quality monthly cadence.

## Operational Content

- March 10-20 posts had already gone live under Govern AI framing; the packet recommends no retroactive correction.
- April 7-23 posts 11-24 remained pending at the time of the file.
- May cadence moves to Tuesday / Thursday / Saturday rather than five posts per week.
- The strongest content themes are governance infrastructure, interruptibility, deterministic traceability, recursive workflows, and failure as governance evidence.

## External Signals Named In The Packet

- EU AI Act high-risk compliance deadline on August 2, 2026.
- Enterprise readiness gaps around unclear high-risk classification and missing AI inventories.
- A Gartner forecast that more than 40 percent of agentic AI projects will be canceled by 2027, framed as evidence for upstream governance rather than model-capability marketing.

## Why This Belongs In The Vault

This is not just a social-media calendar. It is a public-surface translation of the PHAROS thesis: governance must be infrastructure, not a downstream ethics gloss. The posts are also a revenue-facing bridge between [[Governance by Denial]], [[AI Society Manuscript — From AI Anxiety to Recursive Governance]], and the PHAROS commercial surface.
