# Slayer Franchise Power — Methodology and Corpus Design

## Summary

Methodology note for the Slayer franchise power study: a qualitative, interpretive close textual analysis of how Slayer power is structured, legitimized, redistributed, and fragmented across television and comics. The method supports [[For Her Alone to Wield — Buffy Power as Infrastructure]] and belongs to [[Buffy Slayer Studies]].

## Research Design

The study uses a qualitative, interpretive design grounded in close textual analysis rather than audience reception or generalizable empirical measurement. It integrates feminist media criticism, discourse-oriented textual analysis, television studies, and transmedia analysis to interpret power as a mediated system rather than an individual character attribute.

## Corpus Design

The corpus is purposive and concept-driven, selected for theoretical relevance rather than representativeness:

- "Get It Done" (S7E15), especially the Shadow Men sequence.
- "Chosen" (S7E22), especially the activation scene.
- Selected issues of *Fray* (2001-2003).
- The crossover arc "Time of Your Life."

These sites foreground the rules, transmission, authorization, and transformation of the Slayer system.

## Analytical Procedure

1. Identify explicit articulations of power, authority, legitimacy, and destiny through dialogue, narrative framing, ritual, and institutional structures.
2. Examine mediation: who authorizes action, who interprets power, and whose body bears the consequences.
3. Compare relations across texts to trace the shift from singular to distributed power and the instability that follows redistribution.
4. Use theory as an interpretive tool rather than an external overlay.

## Theoretical Stack

- Brunsdon / feminist textual analysis: media texts actively construct meanings of gender, authority, and identity.
- Mittell / complex television: serial storytelling accumulates meaning across time rather than isolating episode-level meaning.
- Jenkins / transmedia storytelling: comics are treated as integral narrative components rather than secondary evidence.
- Ahmed / orientation: bodies are directed within systems of power.
- Berlant / cruel optimism: empowerment can remain attached to structures that harm or constrain.
- Taylor / archive and repertoire: continuity breaks down when embodied transmission and archival continuity diverge.

## Methodological Guardrails

- Close reading remains primary; theoretical claims must stay anchored in textual evidence.
- Technological terms such as system, network, and infrastructure are heuristic, not literal equivalence claims.
- The corpus does not claim franchise-wide exhaustiveness.
- The absence of audience research limits the study to textual interpretation.
- Multiple theories must be managed through conceptual coherence to avoid fragmentation.

## Central Method Claim

The method is aligned with the paper's governing claim: power in the Slayer franchise operates not as an individual attribute but as an evolving system of authorization, distribution, and continuity.
