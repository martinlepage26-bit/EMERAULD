## What is EMERAULD

EMERAULD is the second brain and knowledge vault for [[Martin Lepage — Professional Profile|Martin Lepage, PhD]] — researcher, governance architect, novelist, and AI ethicist. It holds working notes, academic papers, governance memos, legal correspondence, fiction manuscripts, and structured project state across every active domain. The vault is organized as a linked knowledge graph: every note participates in the system through backlinks, traversal, and contextual discovery. Start here, or enter through any of the main Maps of Content below.
