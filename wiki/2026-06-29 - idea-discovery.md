# Idea Discovery — 2026-06-29

## Ranking heuristic (stated, auditable)

Three signals, weighted in order:

1. **Pull** — how many vault notes reference or depend on this candidate (backlink count as proxy for accumulated weight).
2. **Momentum** — does anything already build toward it? Includes today's session work, recent daily note activity, and completed prerequisites.
3. **Recency** — when was the candidate last touched or surfaced?

A candidate scores high when all three align. Candidates that have high pull and momentum but no execution are the strongest signals — the vault is ready; what's missing is a decision.
