# Buffy Slayer Studies

Martin's media studies work focused on Buffy the Vampire Slayer as a site of queer power, ritual infrastructure, body politics, and governance theory. A cluster of papers converging on the Slayer as a model of power without institutional legitimacy.

---

## Core Slayer Papers

- [[Martin Lepage Publications — Annotated Bibliography and Verification Leads]] — Public-record bibliography noting the 2025 *Slayage* article "Every Hair a Battle Scar" and the "Martin LePage" name variant.
- [[Slayer Franchise Power — Methodology and Corpus Design]] — Methodology note for the Slayer power-as-infrastructure study: qualitative close textual analysis, feminist media criticism, complex television, and transmedia corpus design.
- [[The Scythe Already in Motion — Buffy, Queer Ritual, and the Politics of Glitch]] — Primary Buffy paper. The Scythe as queer ritual object; glitch as a politics of disruption to institutional power.
- [[For Her Alone to Wield — Buffy Power as Infrastructure]] — Power as infrastructure: the Slayer's power is not exceptional but structural, held as infrastructure rather than individual ability.
- [[Still Running — Willow, Anya, and Queer Ritual Infrastructure]] — Willow and Anya as sustaining ritual infrastructure; still running as a mode of queer persistence.
- [[Every Hair a Battle Scar — Buffy's Hair as Narrative Engine]] — Buffy's hair as a semiotic system tracking narrative arc, power state, and identity through the series.
