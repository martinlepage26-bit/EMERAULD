# HEXA Press — Publishing and Practice Initiative

## Summary

HEXA Press is Martin Lepage's publishing and practice imprint for the creative corpus: novels, monographs, allegory, and research-creation work that runs alongside the scholarly and governance archive. The label appears in the creative notes as `HEX/ADECIMAL Press` and also traces into the research-creation and artistic arms of the archive.

## Creative Method

- The imprint holds the same underlying method as the rest of the archive: repeatable composition, formal insistence, and an unwillingness to collapse into generic voice.
- The publishing label is part of the practice, not just a distribution wrapper.
- The creative corpus remains bilingual, genre-crossing, and tightly tied to the scholarly and governance work.
