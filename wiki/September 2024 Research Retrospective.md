# September 2024 Research Retrospective

See also [[Governance and PHAROS MOC]].
See also [[Pagan and Queer Ritual Studies MOC]].
## Summary
Research program retrospective document covering the full arc of Martin Lepage's active research lanes as of September 2024. Contains 5 research lanes, a chronological development timeline (September 2024–2026), a 16-cluster analytic table synthesizing the research program, a bilingual research statement (EN/FR), and references to active clinical research including the VIGIL-GSK clinical trial, DoxyPEP research, and IRB protocols for digital HIV prevention research for migrants. Author: Martin Lepage, PhD. Related to [[Martin Lepage — Professional Profile]], [[PHAROS Invention Disclosure]], and [[Recursive Deterministic AI Governance — Method and Paper]].

## Context
Source: `September2024.docx`. This is an internal document — a personal retrospective rather than a publication draft. It provides the clearest single-document map of the research program as it existed at the point when the PHAROS methodology was crystallizing. The clinical research references (VIGIL-GSK, DoxyPEP, IRB for migrants) indicate active health research engagement alongside the AI governance and cultural studies work.

## Five Research Lanes

### Lane 1 — AI Governance Methodology (PHAROS)
The core governance methodology work:
- Development of the PHAROS pipeline (at this point approaching formalization)
- Recursive deterministic governance as a publishable method
- The [[Recursive Deterministic AI Governance — Method and Paper]] as the primary output target
- Integration with the [[InfraFabric Architecture]] modules

### Lane 2 — Institutional AI and Algorithmic Power
The applied institutional analysis:
- Algorithmic management, recruitment, and content moderation
- [[Fluency, Interruption, and Institutional Accountability]] as the flagship paper
- [[Governance by Denial]] as the political theory complement
- OECD data on algorithmic workplace governance as empirical anchor

### Lane 3 — Cultural Studies and Popular Culture Governance
The screen objects analysis:
- [[The Scythe Already in Motion — Buffy, Queer Ritual, and the Politics of Glitch]]
- [[For Her Alone to Wield — Buffy Power as Infrastructure]]
- [[Ritual Machine Lab — Legitimacy Machines Introduction]] (book project)
- [[Agatha All Along — Wicca, Digital Mediatization, and Proof Regimes]] (Social Compass Special Issue)

### Lane 4 — Neo-Pagan Spirituality and Queer Theory
The ethnographic and theoretical core:
- Active fieldwork in Montreal Pagan community (ongoing from 2010-2016 dissertation work)
- [[Social Compass Paper — Ritual Authority and Régime de Preuve]] as the flagship publication
- Special Issue co-direction (Social Compass, with Olivier Servais and Lionel Obadia)

### Lane 5 — Clinical AI and Health Governance
Newly active lane as of 2024:
- VIGIL-GSK clinical trial involvement
- DoxyPEP research context (HIV prevention)
- IRB protocols for digital HIV prevention research for migrants
- The [[Healthcare Governance Packet — Recursive Governance for Providers]] as practical output
- The responsible AI evaluation work (see [[Responsible AI Evaluation — Patient Readmission Model]])

## Insights

- The 5-lane structure reveals that the research program is not sequential (cultural studies → AI governance) but genuinely parallel: all five lanes are active simultaneously, each informing the others. The PHAROS methodology emerges from the intersection of the ritual studies, governance, and clinical research lanes — not from a single disciplinary origin
- The clinical research lane (Lane 5) is the newest and potentially most consequential: it connects the governance methodology to actual healthcare outcomes, where governance failures have immediate human costs rather than abstract institutional ones
- The 16-cluster table is the closest thing in the archive to a master conceptual map of the research program — more comprehensive than the individual paper abstracts or the [[Historical Academic Portfolio — Pre-PHAROS Scholarly Work]] index
