# Martin Lepage — Authored Skills

See also [[Manuscript Pipeline MOC]].
## Summary

Multi-function hub for the 26 skills that encode [[Martin Lepage Professional Identity|Martin Lepage]]'s repeatable method. Distinct from the full installed corpus: these are signature practices — used repeatedly, regardless of origin — that compound across projects, sessions, and surfaces. Entry point for identity, method, domain traversal, and provenance. Seeds the [[Skill Ecosystem — Professional Capability Registry]] domain hub architecture.

## Criterion

**Signature** — skills Martin returns to as core repeatable practices, regardless of whether he wrote them from scratch, co-authored them, or adapted them from the corpus. The test: does this skill encode a method that is distinctively his? If yes, it belongs here.

This is not the same as "installed" or "authored from scratch." It is a curated identity artifact.

## The 26 Signature Skills

### Governance and Method (9)

Core [[Recursive Governance Theory|PHAROS governance]] methodology and analytical lenses.

- [[Epistemic Operator — Operational Specification]] — live epistemic governance layer; enforces observation-before-interpretation, tier separation, and anti-fluency discipline across all PHAROS analytical work
- [[Diamond-Eyes — Aesthetic Refinement Skill]] — aesthetic governance gate; structural diagnosis before promotion
- [[recursive-governance-method]] — the PHAROS method itself; recursive analysis of heterogeneous archives
- [[philosopher]] — meta-router of the skill ecosystem; applied to governance dilemmas and ethical trade-offs
- [[fully-rounded-power-analyst]] — maps actors, incentives, constraints, and multidimensional power
- [[trace-investigator]] — traces authority, accountability, and definitions across document packs
- [[intersectionality]] — intersectional analysis of overlapping social position, power, and oppression
- [[hitl-awareness]] — designs and reviews human-in-the-loop oversight, verification, and escalation
- [[ai-governance-workflow]] — staged governance pipeline: intake → bias memo → compliance → framework → red-team → rollout

### Research and Scholarly Work (5)

Methods and modes for [[Martin Lepage — Professional Profile|research practice]] and scholarly output.

- [[qualitative]] — compare, choose, and apply qualitative research methods
- [[grill-me]] — structured decision interview; walks the design tree, resolves dependencies
- [[literature-review]] — systematic literature review and synthesis
- [[scientific-critical-thinking]] — rigorous evaluation of scientific claims and evidence chains
- [[peer-reviewed-paper-writer]] — peer-reviewed manuscript drafting, submission prep, reviewer response

### Writing and Humanization (3)

The writing and communication layer that makes governance and scholarship reach people.

- [[humanize]] — redrafts governance, compliance, and regulatory rules for real human behavior
- [[novelist]] — long-form fiction: planning, drafting, revision, voice, structure
- [[naming-analyzer]] — naming conventions, semantic clarity, terminology consistency

### Vault and Knowledge (5)

Vault synthesis, retrieval, and context management for [[EMERAULD — Vault and Knowledge Graph|EMERAULD]].

- [[Ask Vault — EMERAULD Vault Briefing Skill]] — vault retrieval and briefing; semantic search entry point
- [[Vault Graph Hygiene — Content Title Normalization Skill]] — content title normalization and graph hygiene
- [[genealogy-loupe]] — retraces a work's version lineage, ancestor notes, and provenance chain
- [[boil-the-ocean]] — comprehensive review and synthesis across large, heterogeneous document sets
- [[tabula-rasa]] — blank-slate reset for contaminated context, inherited assumptions, or overfit conclusions

### Skill Architecture (4)

The meta-layer: how [[Skill Development Workflow — TDD, Dual-Layer, and Eval-Iterate|skills are built]], composed, and maintained.

- [[Skill Development Workflow — TDD, Dual-Layer, and Eval-Iterate]] — TDD-based dual-layer skill authorship meta-workflow
- [[Skill-Pairing — Five-Case Test Suite]] — compositional discipline for combining skills across five cases
- [[skill-architect]] — designs, audits, restructures, and composes SKILL.md files using dual-layer architecture
- [[Agatha Unified Skill System — Eight Sovereign Operators]] — eight-operator skill system governing how skills activate and compose

### Operator Wellbeing (1)

These skills operationalize [[Recursive Governance Theory]] and the [[PHAROS Method Map|PHAROS Method]].

- The governance cluster (diamond-eyes, recursive-governance-method, philosopher, fully-rounded-power-analyst, trace-investigator, intersectionality, hitl-awareness, ai-governance-workflow) is the analytical and procedural core of PHAROS governance work.
- The research cluster feeds manuscripts: [[Paper 25 — The Pharos Frame]], [[AI Society Manuscript — From AI Anxiety to Recursive Governance]], [[Recursive Deterministic AI Governance — Method and Paper]].
- The skill architecture cluster (skill-architect, Skill Development Workflow, Skill-Pairing, Agatha) is the meta-layer that produces and maintains all other skills.
- The vault cluster (Ask Vault, genealogy-loupe, Vault Graph Hygiene, boil-the-ocean, tabula-rasa) governs [[EMERAULD — Vault and Knowledge Graph|EMERAULD]] itself.
