# Vault Delta Interconnectivity Atlas — 2026-05-06

## Summary

This is the full interconnectivity pass for the vault’s active deltas. It does not try to make every note identical or every branch collapse into one canon. Instead, it shows how the vault’s recurring material changes shape across six axes: genealogies, themes, functions, failures, logics, and rhetorics. The goal is to make the deltas legible enough that they can be linked, not flattened.

## Delta Axes

## Function Matrix

- **Canonical hubs:** MOCs and TOPIC pages organize the durable structure.
- **Coverage notes:** matrices say what was promoted, what stayed raw, and what is already covered elsewhere.
- **Raw packs:** `raw sources/` preserves originals untouched.
- **Trackers:** `VAULT ADDITIONS TRACKER.md`, `Master Project Tracker — 2026.md`, and `memory.md` keep live state visible.
- **Synthesis notes:** the wiki notes translate source material into linked durable understanding.

## Failure Modes

- **Genealogy collapse:** treating one note as the origin of the whole arc.
- **Alias drift:** old topic names surviving after the canonical hub changed.
- **Raw/durable confusion:** copying source into the wiki without preserving provenance boundaries.
- **Over-completion:** turning a delta into a false final answer instead of a bounded repair.
- **Weak bridges:** themes exist in multiple clusters but do not yet share backlinks.
- **Duplicate variants:** repeated files or near-duplicates stay unmarked until a coverage pass names them.
- **Skill-corpus genealogy drift:** active folders, mirrored Claude/Codex skills, retired aliases, and canonical routing can diverge unless existence truth, routing truth, and lineage truth are tracked separately.

## Logic Patterns

- **Recursion:** notes return to the same question from a new surface.
- **Bounded non-finality:** a note can be useful without pretending to be exhaustive.
- **Charge-persistence:** the same underlying work survives across form changes.
- **Phase transition:** some failures mark a change in state, not a simple bug.
- **Promotion gates:** raw material becomes durable only after inspection and synthesis.
- **Reconstruction from minimal elements:** a note can be rebuilt from fragments without claiming perfect recovery.
- **Delta closure:** a discrepancy closes only when named actors can move a named condition by a named date under named constraints.

## Rhetorical Registers

- **Academic:** papers, method notes, topic indexes, bibliographies.
- **Practitioner:** magical-system notes, lived voice, early belief documents.
- **Product:** GAIA, PHAROS, HELIX, and other public-facing surfaces.
- **Archive:** raw-source packs, manifests, coverage matrices, trackers.
- **Allegorical:** novels and creative work that carry the same logic in another form.
- **Bilingual:** several notes move between EN and FR as a meaning-bearing choice, not a cosmetic one.
