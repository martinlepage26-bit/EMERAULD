## Regional Read

### Montreal / Quebec

Best immediate surface: **insurance and health-adjacent AI operations**, especially Humania/Koios and AlayaCare. The province has a strong cluster of AI builders, but HELIX should not pitch "AI governance" abstractly. It should pitch **one governed diagnostic on one real AI output**.

High-fit names:

- Humania Assurance / Koios Intelligence - strongest known live deployment signal.
- AlayaCare - agents in home-based care, EHR/EMR escalation, HIPAA/PIPEDA compliance.
- Beneva - AI-based group-insurance underwriting; French-language enterprise insurance target.
- Coveo - enterprise generative-answering / third-party model risk surface from the earlier sweep.
- Botpress - agent-governance fit, but source capture was thinner than Ada/AlayaCare.
- CRIM - trusted-AI partner/referral surface.
- Desjardins / National Bank / Beneva - already appear in the PHAROS outreach pack; use this sweep to tighten the evidence basis, not to reclassify them as active prospects.

### Toronto

Toronto has stronger **formal responsible-AI governance language** than the first Quebec sweep. The issue is access: these are larger buyers, so the route probably needs a warm intro or a partner frame.

High-fit names:

- Manulife - strongest formal governance signal: AI Governance team, AI Governance Council, model risk management, independent validation.
- Sun Life - strong official responsible-AI page with governance by design, regulatory compliance, human oversight, and production GenAI use cases.
- RBC - strong official responsible-AI principles and lifecycle requirements across testing, validation, monitoring, fairness, transparency, and regulatory compliance.
- Wisedocs - already captured; strong claims/medical/legal regulated-output fit with human oversight and defensible outputs.
- Cohere - model/vendor ecosystem target; likely partner/credibility route rather than first paid diagnostic.
- ThinkData Works - data-governance / AI-ready data target; useful if the pitch is "your AI governance starts before the model."
- Ada / League - retry needed because sources were blocked, but search results indicate strong agent trust/safety and health-data governance surfaces.

### Ottawa

Ottawa gives the clearest **audit/government/supply-chain compliance** route.

High-fit names:

- MindBridge - best Ottawa target; the source language maps almost directly to HELIX: auditability, explainability, transparency, risk discovery, anomaly detection, and independent AI audit history.
- Kinaxis - strong agentic supply-chain target; AI responses are described as explainable, source-backed, assumption-backed, secure, transparent, and traceable.
- Solace - agentic AI infrastructure target; platform language around observability, control, governance of event flows, and agentic data movement.
- Assent - strong search-result signal for AI-native supply-chain compliance and evidence trails, but raw capture needs retry because the site returned a consent/popup shell.
- Federal/public-sector procurement - not yet scraped in this pass; save for a separate Ottawa public-sector sweep if HELIX moves beyond private-sector first sale.

## Outreach Implications

HELIX should lead with a **diagnostic artifact**, not a platform pitch:

> We run one governed stress test on one AI workflow output, preserve the transcript/evidence packet, and give you a short practitioner ruling on reasoning stability, traceability, and governance failure modes.

Best first email should reference a public source, then ask for one small diagnostic:

> I saw your public material on [specific AI workflow / governance mechanism]. HELIX is a live stress-test instrument for AI reasoning stability and governance failure modes. Would it be useful to run one governed diagnostic on a single output from that workflow and receive the evidence packet plus a short ruling?
