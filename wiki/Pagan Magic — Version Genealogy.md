## Diff Summaries

### v2005 to v2025
The 2025 revision is a major reworking: retitled "Queering Neo-Pagan Magic: Identity, Power, and the Uses of Magic"; trimmed to approximately 3,000 words per editorial request; explicitly excludes "the extended genealogical section" as requested by editors; engages Michael Bailey's "The Meanings of Magic."

### v2025 to v2026
The 2026 version expands on v2025: from 1,577 to 2,956 words (+1,379 words). Likely adds sections cut for the editor's 3,000 word limit or adds new theoretical development.

## Canonical Version
2026 - Pagan Magic Identity, Power, and the Uses of Magic.docx is the most recent version. LEPAGE-MRQ-Queering_NeoPagan_Magic.docx is the original MRQ published version.

## Key Insights from Version History
The gap between the 2005 MRQ article (4,759 words) and the 2025-2026 revisions represents a significant conceptual shift: the later versions explicitly engage Bailey's "Meanings of Magic" and drop the genealogical section, moving from a historically grounded argument to a more theoretical/ethnographic one.
