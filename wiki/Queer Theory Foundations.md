# Queer Theory Foundations

The theoretical grounding for all of Martin's queer scholarship: queer political theory, queer epistemology, and the relationship between queerness and governance, legitimacy, and institutional authority.

---

## Authority and Legitimacy

- [[Authority After Legitimacy — Disenchantment and Queer Political Theory]] — Political theory of authority after disenchantment. When legitimacy structures collapse or are revealed as constructed, what basis remains for authority? The queer political answer.
- [[Authority Without Ethics — The Love Witch]] — Authority without ethical constraint as the dark mirror: what authority looks like when it is exercised without accountability.
- [[Governance by Denial]] — The political mechanism: institutions produce formal rights without access to exercise them. Denial is a governance strategy, not just a failure.
- [[Fluency, Interruption, and Institutional Accountability]] — Fluency as a mode of power that makes authority invisible and uncontestable. Interruption as the queer response.

---

## Epistemology and the Body

- [[Beyond Fluency — Stuttering, Autoethnography, and Unstable Epistemology]] — Stuttering as epistemological ground: knowledge claimed from a marked, non-fluent body. Autoethnography as method.
- [[Stuttering through the Institution — Academic Containment and Queer Knowing]] — Institutional management of queer knowing through containment strategies. The institution absorbs the interruption.
- [[WHEN THE CAT STOPS — Personal Notes on Recursion and Stutter]] — The autobiographical ground. Democratic AI literacy and the institutional denial of stuttering.
- [[Refusing Fixity — Aesthetic Governance and Fugitive Authorship]] — Refusing to be fixed by genre, institution, or identity. Fugitive authorship as queer epistemological practice.

---

## Intersectionality and Social Position

- [[Social Stratification, Intersectionality, and the Why Be King Manuscript]] — Intersectionality as analytical frame for social stratification. How multiply constrained positions produce distinct knowledges.
- [[Mapping the Margins — Crenshaw 1991 (Intersectionality and Violence)]] — Primary intersectionality anchor text (structural + political intersectionality) for governance and queer theory foundations.
- [[Halberstam — The Queer Art of Failure (2011)]] — Failure-as-method theoretical anchor; key to the failure/refusal logic that carries from queer theory into LOTUS and PHAROS.
- [[Se positionner comme chercheuses au prisme des luttes intersectionnelles — Le Gallo & Millette 2019 (Genre, sexualité & société)]] — Allyship + standpoint theory; speaking for/about vs speaking with (research ethics under intersectional struggle).
- [[How French Canadians became White Folks — Scott 2016 (Race in Quebec)]] — Quebec whiteness formation; race assignment drift shaping identity politics and “minority” framing.
- [[LOTUS Model — Agency and Social Positioning]] — Queer theory operationalized: the LOTUS model measures the effects of social position on effective agency.
- [[CDPDJ Complaint — Lepage v Calian and Novartis]] — A lived intersectionality case: disability (stuttering), precarious work, and queer identity combine in a single discrimination complaint.

## Ritual and the Sacred

- [[The Circle Remains Open — Toward a Queer Sociology of the Sacred]] — The sacred as a queer site: the circle remains open because queer temporality refuses closure.
- [[Authority After Legitimacy — Disenchantment and Queer Political Theory]] — Disenchantment and the sacred: what happens to ritual authority after Weber's disenchantment is applied to queer communities?
- [[Sealed Card Protocol — Legitimacy, Glitch, and Charging]] — Legitimacy, glitch, and charging as three modes of ritual authenticity. The sealed card as a queer governance object.
- [[Queer Religiosities — Wilcox (Queer and Transgender Studies in Religion)]] — Field-level bridge reference for queer/trans studies in religion (comparative/thematic).
- [[Queerness and Transgender Identity — Lepage 2017 (Pagan Montreal, Wicca vs Reclaiming)]] — Montreal queer/trans negotiations as a concrete sacred/ritual authority case.
- [[Masculinités et ritualités — La magie chez les Radical Faeries]] — French-language Radical Faeries paper stub; anchors queer masculinity and ritual authority inside the Pagan/ritual research arc.
- [[In the Study of the Witch — Zwissler 2018 (Magic-Religion categories, gender)]] — Gendered genealogy of magic/religion categories; “witch” as a power-laden academic object.
- [[Is Sacred Nature Gendered or Queer — Becci & Grandjean 2022 (Eco-Spiritual Activism Switzerland)]] — Queer ecology lens applied to eco-spiritual activism; essentialist ↔ queer framings of “nature”.
