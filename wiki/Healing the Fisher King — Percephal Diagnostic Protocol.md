# Healing the Fisher King — Percephal Diagnostic Protocol

## Summary
An operational diagnostic and recovery protocol — the **Percephal structure** — for identifying and healing stalled projects: those accumulating material without synthesis, quietly draining continuity across the system. Derived from the Chrétien de Troyes / Wolfram von Eschenbach Perceval archetype: the healing question not asked leaves the Fisher King wounded and the land a Waste Land. The Percephal structure is the [[EMERAULD]] vault's primary organizing lens for all active and stalled projects. See all live instances at [[Fisher King Hub — Project Recovery Map]].

## Context
Several projects in [[Martin Lepage — Professional Profile]]'s work enter the classic Percephal failure mode: the wound (active tensions, unresolved contradictions, lost momentum) is right in front of the operator, but the healing question has not yet been asked. Without intervention, these projects drain continuity across the whole [[EMERAULD]] system.

The Percephal structure connects to [[PHAROS Method — Technical Reference]] and the [[Recursive Governance Method]] in that all three treat structured interrogation as the primary healing act — not more content, but the right question at the right moment. The Trismégiste agent's name itself encodes this: Hermès Trismégiste, the thrice-great, the one who synthesizes rather than merely accumulates. See [[Mythocritique to PHAROS — The 2010 Master's Thesis as Methodological Keystone]].

## Details

### The Mythic Source

The Percephal structure is derived from the Chrétien de Troyes / Wolfram von Eschenbach Perceval archetype. Perceval enters the Grail Castle, witnesses the wounded Fisher King and the Grail procession, but fails to ask "What ails thee?" / "Whom does the Grail serve?" — out of deference, paralysis, or naivety. The land remains a Waste Land. Healing only comes when Perceval returns and finally asks the question.

**Vault translation:**
- **Fisher King** = the stalled project, wounded but still alive, still load-bearing
- **Waste Land** = raw sources accumulating without synthesis; notes without links; actions deferred
- **Grail Castle** = the vault itself — the place of concentrated knowledge and potential
- **The Question** = the project-state template, filled out honestly
- **Healing** = synthesis, a named next action, external contact, or deliberate archival

### The Diagnostic Template (per project)

Each Fisher King project-state note follows this structure:

- **Core Question** — The wound stated plainly. What is unresolved?
- **Why It Matters Now** — Stakes. What drains if this stays unasked?
- **Current Direction** — Heal, archive, or hold. Name the posture.
- **Canonical State & Artifact** — What is the primary canonical artifact? What is its status?
- **Active Tensions** — The real contradictions keeping the project stalled.
- **Contradictions to Preserve** — What must not be collapsed in the healing move.
- **Relevant Materials** — The existing nodes already linked to this project.
- **Recent Moves** — What has already been done; avoids repeating the question without progress.
- **Consequence Binding** — What changes if the next move executes? Real outcome, not aspiration.
- **Blockers** — Named friction. Unnamed blockers are the Waste Land's soil.
- **Next Synthesis Move** — One concrete recoverable action. The healing question answered.

### Percephal Tier Taxonomy

Projects are grouped by wound severity, not by domain. See [[Fisher King Hub — Project Recovery Map]] for the full current map.

**Tier 1 — Primary Wounds** (heal first; load-bearing, blocking external action)
PHAROS, HELIX, Second Brain, Second Self, Papers

**Tier 2 — Second Ring** (significant wounds; important supporting systems)
LOTUS, GAIA, Dr. Sort, Echo, Scripto/Scriptorium, COMPASSai, AurorA

**Tier 3 — Secondary/Downstream** (real wounds, lower urgency)
Glitching the Sacred, Stuttering Machines, Magie sanguine

### Active Tensions in the Protocol Itself

- Tension between forward momentum on new work and the guilt-entropy of abandoned projects
- Naïve Perceval impulse ("I'll just keep riding") vs. disciplined return to ask the question
- Risk of turning the diagnostic into yet another meta-project instead of actual healing

### Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- Running a broad healing pass before completing one recovery move from the Tier 1 list
- Adding new projects to the Fisher King map before executing on existing ones
- Treating the template as complete once filled — the template is the question, the action is the answer

## Consequence Binding
- **What changes if we use this protocol?** → Stalled projects stop silently draining continuity; each gets a bounded recovery path or a deliberate archive decision.
- **What does not change?** → Projects may still take months to heal; the protocol names the wound, it does not conjure energy.
