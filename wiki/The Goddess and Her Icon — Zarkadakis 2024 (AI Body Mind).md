# The Goddess and Her Icon — Zarkadakis 2024 (AI Body Mind)

## Summary
This is an abstract-only routing note from a RIS citation. George Zarkadakis's *AI & Society* piece frames contemporary AI through a long cultural dualism: classical pagan gods and goddesses were transformed into Christian icons, and modern technology inherits a similar split between body and mind, hardware and software, vessel and ghost.

The abstract asks how this inherited narrative shapes social fantasies about coexisting, merging, or uniting with intelligent artifacts. It also asks whether future human transformation will happen through embodied technologies in physical reality or through disembodied minds in virtual worlds.
