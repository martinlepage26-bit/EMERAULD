# Version Genealogy System

The vault's version tracking system: genealogy notes that record how manuscripts evolved across drafts, submissions, rejections, and revisions. Each genealogy is a provenance document for a specific body of work.

---

## What Is a Version Genealogy?

A genealogy note records:
- When a manuscript first existed
- What form it took across iterations
- What forced revision (reviewer response, new evidence, structural rethink)
- Where the current version sits in the evolution

Genealogies enforce the [[Emotional Alliance vs. Evidentiary Discipline in AI|evidence discipline]] requirement that claims be traceable. The genealogy is the trace.

---

## Governance Function

Genealogies enforce the principle that evidence must be traceable. They are governance artifacts, not just organizational convenience:

- A claim made in version 3 that was absent in version 1 requires explanation
- A structural revision forced by reviewer feedback is documented, not erased
- The provenance of an idea is part of its evidentiary status

This connects directly to [[Agatha Unified Skill System — Eight Sovereign Operators|Agatha's authority resolution rule]]: a claim is only authoritative if its reasoning and provenance withstand audit.
