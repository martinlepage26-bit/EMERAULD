# PHAROS Legal Classification — CAE Code Strategy

## Summary

A decision record for how [[PHAROS Company Registration and Security Incidents]] should be classified under the Québec CAE (Code d'activités économiques) system for legal and administrative purposes. The analysis establishes that PHAROS is not a predefined category within that system and must be mapped onto it for compliance. The recommended code is **7771 — Bureaux de conseillers en gestion** (Management Consulting Offices), not the technical services alternatives. This sits in the [[Governance and PHAROS MOC]] as a foundational business structure decision.

## Context

This document is a working note from early April 2026, produced during the [[PHAROS Procurement-Unblock Sprint]] and the [[Phase 1 Governance Formation Sequence]]. It resolves a concrete administrative question — what economic activity code governs PHAROS's registration — with consequences for how PHAROS presents publicly, what regulatory categories it falls under, and how it distinguishes strategic governance consulting from technical services delivery. It connects to [[PHAROS Licensing Prospectus]], [[Founder Charter — Lepage and Stocker]], and [[Martin Lepage — Professional Profile]].

## Details

### The Classification Question

The question asked: which Québec CAE code applies to PHAROS? The CAE system is a registry of economic activity codes for legal and administrative categorization. PHAROS is not a sector, a regulated profession, or a predefined activity code — it is a governance system, methodology, and product ecosystem. The clean distinction: CAE is a classification tool used by the state; PHAROS is a governance method that must be mapped onto that tool for compliance purposes.

### The Options Analyzed

Three candidates were considered:

**7771 — Bureaux de conseillers en gestion (Management Consulting):** The recommended choice. PHAROS sells a framework of analysis, governance, structuration of decision-making, organizational control, and strategic accompaniment. This is management consulting, not technical execution. Choosing 7771 presents PHAROS publicly as a structured governance practice, not a technical service.

**7759 — Autres services techniques (Other Technical Services):** Weaker choice. It is a residual category that describes the strategic and organizational dimension poorly. It risks making PHAROS appear as a vaguely technical service rather than a structured governance practice.

**7799 — Autres services aux entreprises (Other Business Services):** Administrative approximation only; does not capture what PHAROS is.

### The Decision

**Recommended primary code: 7771.**

The rationale: PHAROS is a governance cabinet, not a technical workshop. The content of what it delivers — control frameworks, decision traceability, auditability, organizational supervision of AI — falls in the domain of strategic management consulting rather than specialized technical execution.

### Recommended Business Descriptions

Three formulations for public or administrative use (ranging from formal to distinctive):

**Version 1 (formal):**
"Cabinet de conseil en gouvernance de l'intelligence artificielle, spécialisé dans la conception de cadres de contrôle, la traçabilité décisionnelle, l'auditabilité des systèmes et l'encadrement organisationnel des usages de l'IA."

**Version 2 (technical positioning, optional):**
"Services techniques spécialisés en gouvernance de l'intelligence artificielle, incluant la conception de cadres de contrôle, la structuration de mécanismes de traçabilité, l'auditabilité des systèmes et l'implémentation de dispositifs de supervision organisationnelle."

**Version 3 (precise/distinctive):**
"Cabinet de conseil en gouvernance de l'IA, architecture de contrôle, traçabilité et dispositifs de supervision."

The decision logic: use 7771 if PHAROS is to be positioned publicly as a governance cabinet. Use 7759 only if the deliberate strategic choice is to present PHAROS as a more technically oriented provider than strategically oriented one.

## Key Ideas

- The CAE system classifies what exists; PHAROS must be mapped onto it, not found within it.
- The 7771 / 7759 choice is not just administrative — it determines whether PHAROS presents as a strategic governance actor or a technical services provider.
- The recommended description names control frameworks, decision traceability, auditability, and organizational supervision as the core service — not model development or deployment.

## Insights

- This decision confirms PHAROS's positioning as a governance methodology practice, which is the same claim made in the [[PHAROS AI and Ethics Submission — Architecture Paper]]: PHAROS is not a benchmark, not an interpretability tool, not a red-team protocol. It is a governance-control method.
- The 7771 code aligns with how the [[PHAROS Licensing Prospectus]] positions the offering for institutional clients.
- Choosing "conseil en gestion" over "services techniques" has consequences for procurement eligibility: in many public and institutional procurement categories, management consulting and technical services are evaluated under different frameworks.
