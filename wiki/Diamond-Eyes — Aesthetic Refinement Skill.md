# Diamond-Eyes — Aesthetic Refinement Skill

## Summary
Diamond-Eyes is a [[Claude Code Skill Corpus|Claude Code skill]] for aesthetic refinement of any designed artifact — prose, prompts, bios, taglines, brand language, UX copy, scene descriptions, slide structures — by improving cut, clarity, rhythm, emphasis, contrast, pressure, and light-return. It does not beautify by adding ornament. It governs through structural diagnosis. Core [[Governance Controls and Mechanisms|control mechanism]] in PHAROS (wisdom + care gate). Part of [[Skill Ecosystem — Professional Capability Registry]]. Related to [[Refusing Fixity — Aesthetic Governance and Fugitive Authorship]] and [[Recursive Deterministic AI Governance — Method and Paper|the recursive governance method's]] anti-charm principle.

## Context
Diamond-Eyes is also the operational refinement *skill* that implements the aesthetic dimension of PHAROS’s wisdom-and-care gating. In the governance architecture, the underlying gate/principle is now named [[Consented Frame — Ethics and Wisdom Gate]] (formerly “Diamond-Eyes gate”).
In Codex, the refinement skill is invoked as `diamond-eyes` and lives at `/home/cerebrhoe/.codex/skills/diamond-eyes/`.

**Origin (lived layer).** The personal/lived account of *why* Diamond-Eyes was built — *"love computerized"*, *"to save the last bit of love I had for myself"* — is preserved in [[PHAROS — Origin and Doctrine]] §III. This page documents the mechanics; the Origin doc documents the human reason any of these mechanics exist. Together they form the dual-layer record (technical / lived) of the same instrument.

**Documentary substrate.** Per [[Mythocritique to PHAROS — The 2010 Master's Thesis as Methodological Keystone|the keystone]], Diamond-Eyes operationalizes the doctrine cited in the [[Mort et Naissance et L'Ombre et le Double — MA Thesis on Yvon Rivard (Université Laval, 2010)|2010 MA thesis]] conclusion (p. 84) — Pierre Vadeboncoeur on Rivard: *"L'humanité dont il fait preuve donne la mesure de son caractère. […] La pensée d'un être n'est pas indépendante de la mesure de son âme."* The thought of a being is not independent of the measure of the soul; method without character is dishonest. The 2010 conclusion already names what the 2024–2026 governance stack treats as a non-exceptionable gate.

## Details

### Six-Phase Execution Loop
1. **Read** — identify artifact type, function, intent, and the "live edge" (the sentence or element that already works)
2. **Diagnose** — assess across 8 axes: Cut, Clarity, Flaw, Pressure, Facet, Weight, Brilliance, Light-return
3. **Decide** — select exactly one intervention per problem: Cut / Sharpen / Soften / Condense / Reweight
4. **Refine** — apply only the interventions identified in Phase 3
5. **Check** — does it feel more inevitable? Has any signature irregularity been over-smoothed?
6. **Deliver** — refined artifact + 3–5 line diagnostic note (what was identified, applied, preserved)

### Five Refinement Modes (from `modes.md`)
- **Whisper Cut** — minimal intervention; remove only what actively harms
- **Luminous Restraint** — clarity through reduction; every word earns its place
- **Inhabited Precision** — specificity that creates presence, not connoisseurship
- **Faceted Ground** — structural complexity; multiple surfaces without fragmentation
- **Pressure Core** — concentrated energy; high charge in a small space

### Beauty Logic (Governing Doctrine)
The aesthetic theory underpinning the skill. Five principles:
1. **Selective idealization** — heighten what gives life; do not heighten everything
2. **Strategic subtraction** — what is absent exerts force; every cut concentrates energy
3. **Controlled emphasis** — at most one element carries the highest charge
4. **Disciplined retention of character** — signature irregularities must survive refinement
5. **Softened transitions, sharpened edges** — precision and porosity are applied selectively

Key distinction table navigates: idealization vs. sterilization, elegance vs. emptiness, brilliance vs. glare, flaw vs. signature, attraction vs. attachment.

### Hidden Diagnostic Engine (Four Models of Love)
Four subterranean lenses used when an artifact fails affectively rather than structurally:
- **Poetic** — does the artifact leave space the reader wants to inhabit or resolve?
- **Biological** — does it produce a felt response before interpretation begins?
- **Social** — does it know who it is talking to; does the reader feel recognized?
- **Lived** — does it feel made by a person, or produced by a process?

### Skill Pairs
- `novelist` — scene descriptions, narrative prose, long-form fiction
- `brand-identity-system` — brand language, naming systems, visual directions
- `peer-reviewed-paper-writer` — academic abstracts, argument framing
- `publisher` — jacket copy, author bios, editorial position language

## Key Ideas
- Refinement fails when it performs beauty instead of producing it
- The live edge is the anchor; all interventions must serve it, not replace it
- Overwork is detectable: when three or more signs appear (uniform intensity, no breath, craft noticed before content), reverse the last interventions
