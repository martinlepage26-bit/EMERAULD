# PHAROS-EMERAULD Consolidated Timeline 2024-04-01 to 2026-04-19

> **Pre-2024 substrate anchor:** The timeline as drawn begins 2024-04-01 — but the methodological substrate is older. The 2010 MA thesis at Université Laval (`Mort et Naissance de Christophe Ulric et L'Ombre et le Double d'Yvon Rivard : lecture mythocritique d'une quête spirituelle`) is the documentary substrate of LOTUS, PHAROS, and the EMERAULD vault philosophy; the 2017 PhD at UQAM is the sociocritique extension. See [[Mythocritique to PHAROS — The 2010 Master's Thesis as Methodological Keystone]] and [[Mort et Naissance et L'Ombre et le Double — MA Thesis on Yvon Rivard (Université Laval, 2010)]]. Anyone reading this timeline should understand the substrate predates the consolidated chronology.

## What this is

Operator-requested umbrella chronology for the combined PHAROS research/practice line and the [[Home|EMERAULD vault]] context. It consolidates the strongest timeline anchors from [[September 2024 Research Retrospective]], [[Historical Academic Portfolio — Pre-PHAROS Scholarly Work]], [[PHAROS AI Lineage — Source of Truth]], [[Recursive AI Governance as Executable Method — The Very Long Narrative]], [[Master Project Tracker — 2026]], and the April 2026 PHAROS company/product notes.

Part of: [[Home]] · [[Personal and Projects MOC]] · [[Governance and PHAROS MOC]]

## Why it matters

When the archive is asked for "the full history," the strongest answer was previously distributed across multiple notes. This page is the retrieval-ready bridge note. Use it first for date-order questions, then move to the specialist notes for deeper detail.

## Evidence rule

- `Direct`: explicitly dated or explicitly stated in a source note opened in the vault.
- `Supported`: date range or ordering supported by multiple notes, but not packeted as one direct dated event.
- `Thin`: user-defined boundary or plausible prehistory with weak direct dating inside the currently linked notes.

## Timeline

### 2024-04-01 to 2024-08-31 - Requested start window before the first strong archive checkpoint (`Thin` -> `Supported`)

- No currently opened note gives a direct event on `2024-04-01` itself.
- [[Complete Paper List — Martin Lepage Corpus]] says the active composition window for the current corpus is `2024-2026`, with the 2017 thesis as the only pre-2024 exception. This supports treating spring 2024 as part of the current umbrella-program build rather than as unrelated prehistory.
- [[The Scythe Already in Motion — Buffy, Queer Ritual, and the Politics of Glitch]] is a `2024` prospectus grounded in `2020-2024` fieldwork. It shows the cultural-studies / glitch / recursion lane is already active inside the requested window.
- Working conclusion: the umbrella program is already underway by spring/summer 2024, but the archive does not yet expose a day-by-day PHAROS or EMERAULD chronology for these months.

### September 2024 - First strong archive checkpoint (`Direct`)

- [[September 2024 Retrospective — Version Genealogy]] shows the first explicit retrospective question: "Since September 2024, what have I been working on...". This is the clearest self-declared archive checkpoint for the second-brain / PHAROS system.
- [[September 2024 Research Retrospective]] is the clearest single-document map of the research program as PHAROS is crystallizing.
- [[AI Personas — Agatha, DOTTIE, and MOBI]] dates Agatha to `September 2024-present`.
- [[PHAROS AI Lineage — Source of Truth]] places `September 2024 -> January 2026` inside Phase 0 / epistemic ignition, with anchors including Sept 2024 lab experiments plus 2024 `Violet Gem` and `Master Ethics Methods`.

### September 2024 to December 2024 - Crystallization of the multi-lane program (`Direct` + `Supported`)

- [[September 2024 Research Retrospective]] defines five active research lanes: PHAROS methodology, institutional AI/power, cultural studies, neo-Pagan/queer theory, and clinical AI/health governance.
- The same note says `September-December 2024` covers PHAROS methodology formalization, Social Compass drafting, and AI governance papers in progress.
- This is the point where the archive becomes legible as one coordinated program rather than a loose set of manuscripts.

### Late 2024 to March 2025 - Conception and first operational articulation (`Direct`)

- [[PHAROS Invention Disclosure]] says the PHAROS method's conception runs from `late 2024 to early 2025`.
- The same note dates the first operational articulation to `on or about March 21, 2025`.
- Reading across the retrospective and invention note, this is the shift from research-program crystallization into an explicit governance method.

### March 2025 to August 2025 - Prototype testing and workflow hardening (`Direct`)

- [[PHAROS Invention Disclosure]] dates prototype testing to `March-August 2025`.
- [[AI Personas — Agatha, DOTTIE, and MOBI]] preserves 2025 Agatha memory-rule timestamps, showing language continuity, full-length revision, and workflow commands hardening during this period.
- This phase is still pre-company and pre-formal PHAROS filing, but the operating method is no longer merely conceptual.

### September 2025 to December 2025 - Adversarial pressure and stress testing (`Direct`)

- [[PHAROS Invention Disclosure]] dates adversarial stress testing to `September-December 2025`.
- [[September 2024 Research Retrospective]] adds the external anchor that the Calian/Novartis engagement begins in `October 2025`.
- This is the strongest pre-2026 sign that PHAROS is moving from internal method development toward consequential institutional pressure.

### January 2026 - Late Phase 0 and pre-turn transition (`Direct` + `Supported`)

- [[PHAROS AI Lineage — Source of Truth]] keeps Phase 0 running through `January 2026`.
- Its raw source names `Charging Objects (2017 ms. digitized)` as a January 2026 anchor inside the timeline scaffold.
- This month reads as the end of epistemic ignition and the threshold before explicit containment grammar.

### February 2026 to March 14, 2026 - AI governance turn, first containment, protocol closure (`Direct`)

- [[PHAROS AI Lineage — Source of Truth]] dates the AI Governance Turn to `February 2026 -> March 2026`.
- Its raw source says `AIGOV1` tokens and the constitutional frame appear here.
- [[AI Personas — Agatha, DOTTIE, and MOBI]] dates DOTTIE to `February 2026`.
- The raw PHAROS lineage scaffold dates Phase 1 / protocol closure + first containment to `2026-02-14 -> 2026-03-14`, with anchors including `SEALEDCARD.pdf`, Martin voice specs, `Violet Gem`, `Alambic`, `Agatha v4.1`, peer-facing manuscripts, and the `AI GOV CONSTITUTION`.
- [[Phase 1 Governance Formation Sequence]] gives the conceptual reading of this cluster: charge -> constrained traversal -> rupture recognition -> containment -> recursive correction -> protocol closure.

### March 14, 2026 to March 25, 2026 - Skill distribution, Hephaistos transition, promotion gate (`Direct`)

- The raw PHAROS lineage scaffold dates Phase 2 / governance revision + skill distribution to `2026-03-14 -> 2026-03-23`.
- Anchors include the first `SKILL.md` units, Mobius Protocol, Hephaistos build notes, moving-parts notes, and the governance/skill ecosystem trees.
- [[AI Personas — Agatha, DOTTIE, and MOBI]] dates MOBI to `March 2026`.
- The same scaffold records a `2026-03-25` promotion gate at `ready_full`, but with `promotion_review_required` and manual operator override still required.
- [[Recursive AI Governance as Executable Method — The Very Long Narrative]] confirms the architectural sequence ending in skill distribution, Hephaistos, and implementation surfaces under inherited constraints.

### March 20, 2026 to April 8, 2026 - Institutionalization, filing, and company formation (`Direct`)

- [[September 2024 Research Retrospective]] records the [[CDPDJ Complaint — Lepage v Calian and Novartis]] filing on `2026-03-20`.
- [[PHAROS Invention Disclosure]] is officially filed on `2026-04-01`.
- [[PHAROS Company Registration and Security Incidents]] records Quebec filings on `2026-04-03` and `2026-04-08`, with payment confirmed on `2026-04-08`.
- This is the clearest transition from method / archive / operator stack into legal, corporate, and IP surfaces.

### April 2026 - Archive consolidation and deployment hardening (`Direct`)

- [[PHAROS AI Lineage — Source of Truth]] and [[PHAROS Cross-AI Strategy Matrix]] are both active April 2026 consolidation notes that normalize the lineage and cross-AI method.
