# The Ballad of the Witches' Road — Lyrics

## Summary

Full lyrics to "The Ballad of the Witches' Road" from the Disney+ series *Agatha All Along* (2024), written by Kristen Anderson-Lopez and Robert Lopez. The song functions as a ritual chant within the show and is central to the analysis in [[Agatha All Along — Wicca, Digital Mediatization, and Proof Regimes]] and [[Inductive Literary Discourse Analysis — Witchcraft in Song Lyrics]].

## Context

The lyrics underpin the [[Sociology of Modern Magic — Song Corpus and Queer Ritual Synthesis]] argument about witchcraft as performed community and queer ritual space. Eight versions of the song were released across the series; these are the core lyrics shared across versions. Source metadata and version genealogy at [[Agatha All Along Social Compass — Version Genealogy]].

## Details

Structurally, the lyrics enact the coven logic central to [[Agatha All Along — Wicca, Digital Mediatization, and Proof Regimes]]: collective invocation ("gather sisters"), elemental address, and a triadic close ("Maiden, Mother, Crone"). The repetitive chant structure mirrors ritual function — the song *is* the spell within the diegesis.
