## Diff Summaries

### v1 → v2
Major structural addition: formal academic title "\"For Her Alone to Wield?\": The Infrastructures of Power in the Slayer Franchise" prepended, full abstract inserted. The introduction text is revised but recognizable. The v2 abstract is longer and more detailed than the later coherence-pass version.

### v2 → v3 (coherence pass)
Approximately 30 changed lines. Abstract substantially compressed: removes "key moments—particularly" phrasing; the phrase "the analysis traces" becomes "it traces"; "operates as a form of infrastructural power that depends on" becomes "depends on." The body is tightened throughout with ~377 words cut, repetitions removed, transitions sharpened.

### v3 → v4 (no_image_markers)
201 changed lines. Anonymized header block added at top (`[Author Name]`, `[author@email.com]`). Image markers and references to figures removed from body. Word count increases despite anonymization because some cut material was reinserted.

### v4 → v5 → v6 → v7
Progressive editorial cuts across three passes: v4→v5 (-76 words), v5→v6 (-560 words), v6→v7 (-617 words). These represent significant pruning of redundant passages.

### v7/v8/v9 → v10 → v11 → v12 (FINAL)
Working sequence toward final submission: brackets cleaned, bibliography integrated, cut material reinserted. Final version adds a full 30+ entry selected bibliography (Ahmed, Butler, Fraser, Gill, Halberstam, Jenkins, McRobbie, Muñoz, Taylor, Wilcox, and others).

## Canonical Version
**`BUFFY_FINAL_with_selected_bibliography.docx`** — most complete, includes full selected bibliography, represents the submission-ready manuscript.

## Key Insights from Version History
The revision arc moves from working prose without apparatus → formal academic structure → coherence editing → anonymized submission versions → full bibliography addition. The "coherence pass" stage is notable for producing the tightest prose. The fragmentation of intermediate versions (multiple `no_image_markers` and `reinserted_master` variants) suggests iterative back-and-forth between compression and restoration before the final was locked.
