# Custom GPT Products — PHAROS AI GPT Roster

## Summary

Master inventory of all custom ChatGPT GPTs built by [[Martin Lepage — Professional Profile]] under the [[PHAROS Product Stack]] umbrella. Seven confirmed products; two internal personas (DOTTIE, MOBI) not found as published GPTs. Three products have full specification notes in the vault. Part of the [[Skill Domain — AI and LLM Tools]] digital product surface.

## Context

These GPTs are live products on ChatGPT's platform, distinct from the internal operator personas documented in [[AI Personas — Agatha, DOTTIE, and MOBI]]. They represent the public-facing and product-layer expressions of the PHAROS methodology — turning governance, research, and agency-scoring methods into deployed AI tools. See [[ChatGPT Apps SDK — Planning, Metadata, Deployment, and Operations]] for the technical build layer.
