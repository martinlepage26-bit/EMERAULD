# Governance by Denial — Legibility, Capacity, Classification (Draft)

## Summary
Draft/article text proposing a “democratic hollowing” mechanism family: **narrative control**, **impunity signaling**, and **weak accountability loops**. The core contribution is a governance diagnostic frame that treats **legibility**, **capacity**, and **classification** as measurable levers that determine whether harms can reliably become enforceable institutional consequences. Key paper for [[Authority, Legitimacy, and Post-Sovereignty]] — diagnoses the conditions under which institutional authority hollows out while continuing to perform legitimacy.

## Why it matters here
- **PHAROS governance translation:** a ready-made vocabulary for describing “institutions that exist but don’t bind,” without collapsing into conspiracy language.
- **Audit framing:** suggests indicator-style tracing (“what becomes actionable?” “what enforcement bandwidth exists?” “which classifications pre-sort credibility/risk?”).
- **Cross-link:** connects naturally to framing-process work (see [[Framing Processes and Social Movements — Benford & Snow 2000]]).
- **Power vocabulary:** pairs well with [[Power in International Politics — Barnett & Duvall 2005 (Taxonomy)]] for non-command power (institutional/structural/productive).
- **Transparency paradox:** pairs well with [[Transparency Against Democracy — Paquin 2025 (Sweden Democrats, trust)]] for “accountability tools repurposed” dynamics.

## Key Ideas (Vault-usable)
- Hollowing can proceed through **tempo + legality** rather than visible rupture.
- “Narrative control” as governance of inference rules (what counts as evidence; who counts as credible).
- “Impunity signaling” as patterned nonconsequence that teaches systems/publics what won’t be sanctioned.
- “Weak accountability loops” as procedural repetition that preserves legitimacy while leaving incentives intact.
