# Queer Religiosities — Wilcox (Queer and Transgender Studies in Religion)

> **Downstream applications:** This theoretical anchor is applied across the operator's queer pagan corpus: [[Queering Neo-Pagan Magic — 2026 Paper]], [[Queer Neo-Pagan Spiritualities and the Question of Authenticity in Wicca]], [[The Inner Goddess — Gender Normativity and Gay Identity in Wiccan Ritualities]], [[Why be king Im already a queen — Lepage 2017 (PhD thesis, UQAM)]], and [[The Circle Remains Open — Toward a Queer Sociology of the Sacred]]. Indexed in [[Queer Sociology of Magic and Ritual]].

## Summary
Melissa M. Wilcox’s *Queer Religiosities: An Introduction to Queer and Transgender Studies in Religion* is a comparative, thematic introduction designed to make the queer/trans studies-in-religion field legible to students. For this vault it functions as a **bridge reference**: it supports claims that “queer religiosity” is a defined scholarly domain (not an ad-hoc label) and provides scaffolding for linking Montreal fieldwork to wider comparative frames.

## Why it matters here
- Reinforces the intellectual lane behind [[Queer Sociology of Magic and Ritual]] and the broader queer theory foundations.
- A stable anchor when writing about queer/trans religious practice without collapsing into identity-only framing.
