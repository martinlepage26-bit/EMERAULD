# Skill Domain — Writing and Humanization

## Summary

The Writing and Humanization domain contains the skills that make governance, scholarship, and technical content reach people: long-form fiction, regulatory plain-language rewriting, naming clarity, documentation generation, and voice work. It is the communication layer downstream of both [[Skill Domain — Governance and Method]] and [[Skill Domain — Research and Scholarly Work]], translating technical and analytical outputs into prose that serves human readers. These skills appear throughout [[Martin Lepage — Authored Skills|Martin's signature methodology]] and are central to the [[Novel and Creative Corpus]] and the governance communication work documented in [[AI Governance Manifesto — Upstream Institutional Practice]]. This domain is part of the [[Skill Ecosystem — Professional Capability Registry]].
