# Charge & Circle — Four-Pivot Decision Map (2026-05-24)

## Summary

On 2026-05-24, the Charge & Circle product went through four consecutive reframes in a single day. This note maps each pivot, what triggered it, what survived, and what was killed — serving as a durable strategic decision record and an anchor for the [[Elemental Agents — Productization Plan (2026-05-24)|productization cluster]]. The final landing: exclusively a governance framework with elemental magic as its teaching surface, published as-is at v0.1, no rewrite. Part of [[Charge & Circle — TTRPG Launch (2026)]] lineage and the [[Martin Walks the Witches' Road — Corpus as Charge-Persistence]] corpus doctrine.

## Context

The four-pivot sequence happened within a single long working session. Each pivot cancelled or superseded the previous one, leaving only the final configuration in force. The sequence is useful as a case study in operator discipline: each pivot was a real constraint, not indecision — the pivots trace the work of finding a configuration that survived all three tests (audience, price point, manuscript integrity). The decision to publish-as-is at the end was operator-disciplined refusal of the [[The Lost-Loop Pattern — Avoidance Through System-Building|Lost-Loop pattern]].

## The Four Pivots

### Pivot 1 — TTRPG Launch (morning)
**Trigger:** Operator approval of the TTRPG repackaging plan (plan saved to `luminous-nibbling-volcano.md`). The framework's element-composition system is structurally identical to a Sanderson-class hard-magic spec.
**Product identity:** TTRPG hard-magic-system catalogue at $19–29. Brand: Charge & Circle. Storefront: itch.io.
**What survived to next pivot:** Charge & Circle brand, the element-composition mechanics, the Reddit r/RPGdesign validation angle.

### Pivot 2 — Governance Framework with Magic Surface (afternoon)
**Trigger:** "But it needs to be a framework for governance!!" Three consecutive directives: no waiting, reframe to governance, 10k words max. Path A (governance with ritual surface) selected.
**Product identity:** Governance framework at $299–499 for GRC/compliance/AI-governance buyers. Ritual/elemental vocabulary kept as the teaching surface. Three-chapter manuscript drafted (~9,200 words).
**What survived to next pivot:** $299–499 price band, Charge & Circle brand, manuscript v0.1, validator-as-credibility-artifact.
**What died:** TTRPG-first framing, itch.io storefront, $19–29 price.

### Pivot 3 — Fiction Reframe + Publish-As-Is (evening)
**Trigger:** The [[Epistemic Governance — Canonical Reference|Dimension-F audit]] returned a substantive failure finding (tier-discipline absent throughout; six specific findings). Operator response: "it should be understood as fiction" + "no rewrite publish as is."
**Product identity:** Literary fiction in the false-document / encyclopedic-treatise tradition (Borges, Calvino, Lem, Clarke). Price band reset to $12–18 e-book. Corpus landing: alongside [[Alchemy of the Wound]], not alongside [[Obsidian Agent Vault — Launch Kit]].
**Operator discipline:** Shipping v0.1 with all its imperfections IS the operator-disciplined move. The audit's recommended Brain-level rewrite (~20–30 hours) would be the [[The Lost-Loop Pattern — Avoidance Through System-Building|Lost Loop]] — system-building substituting for the hard concrete action.
**What survived to next pivot:** Publish-as-is mandate, Charge & Circle brand, three-chapter structure, validator.

### Pivot 4 — REFRAME ENTIRELY: Governance Framework Exclusive (late evening)
**Trigger:** "REFRAME ENTIRELY: governance framework with elemental magic surface EXCLUSIVELY." Cancels the fiction reframe and the dual-reading framing. Locks the product as EXCLUSIVELY a governance framework.
**Product identity:** Governance framework at $299–499. Elemental magic vocabulary is the surface teaching layer ONLY — not a halo audience, not a dual surface. No alternate reading invited.
**What died permanently:** Fiction-tradition framing; TTRPG halo audience; dual-reading framing; Bet 2 (TTRPG creators at $19–149); multi-surface Witches' Road framing (collapsed to single-surface charge-persistence — the corpus doctrine is about charge surviving surface CHANGE, not multiple simultaneous surfaces).
**What survived:** Charge & Circle brand; $299–499 governance buyer band; publish-as-is mandate; manuscript v0.1 text (unchanged); validator; companion 165-operation catalogue plan; PHAROS Sprint cross-sell compatibility.

## Unresolved Tension at Publish Time

Chapter 1 contains two passages that explicitly invite multi-surface reading, directly contradicting the EXCLUSIVE governance identity. Under publish-as-is, they stay. Under REFRAME ENTIRELY, they should go. The two directives conflict at these passages. Operator decision required before public launch:
- Cut them (minor text edit, arguably not a "rewrite")
- Keep them (preserve publish-as-is at the cost of internal contradiction)

## What the Four Pivots Demonstrate

The Witches' Road doctrine from [[Martin Walks the Witches' Road — Corpus as Charge-Persistence]] played out in real time: the charge (governance method + elemental vocabulary + validator credibility) persisted through four surface changes. What the four pivots found was NOT the right framing — they found the one framing that survived all the tests. The publish-as-is directive is the final operator discipline that refuses to substitute further refinement for the hard act of shipping.
