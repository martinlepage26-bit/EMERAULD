# Skill Domain — Research and Scholarly Work

See also [[Manuscript Pipeline MOC]].
See also [[Research and Papers MOC]].
## Summary

The Research and Scholarly Work domain contains the methods and modes that drive [[Martin Lepage — Authored Skills|Martin's scholarly output]]: systematic literature review, scientific reasoning, peer-reviewed manuscript production, and structured decision interviews. These skills operationalize the research layer of the [[Recursive Governance Theory|PHAROS method]] and feed directly into the [[Skill Domain — Writing and Humanization]] writing layer. This domain is the primary surface for [[Martin Lepage — Professional Profile|Martin's academic practice]] and is the engine behind the [[Complete Paper List — Martin Lepage Corpus]]. It sits within the broader [[Skill Ecosystem — Professional Capability Registry]] and is managed by HENRY as primary agent.
