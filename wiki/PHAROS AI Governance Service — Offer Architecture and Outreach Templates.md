# PHAROS AI Governance Service — Offer Architecture and Outreach Templates

## Summary

A structured service offer design for the PHAROS AI governance consulting practice targeting small businesses, agencies, consultants, and SaaS founders using AI without controls. Three tiers plus a premium day-rate offer, full outreach scripts, a 30-day $10K cash plan, a seven-day paid challenge design, and a ChatGPT-assisted delivery workflow. Complements [[PHAROS Commercial Strategy]] and [[AI Governance Offer Ladder - Montreal Quebec 90-Day Revenue Plan]].

## Context

This document was produced as a freelance offer design session, generating ready-to-deploy service architecture grounded in the EU AI Act timeline (GPAI obligations August 2025, high-risk obligations August 2026), NIST AI RMF, and Quebec Law 25. Positioning: "AI creates trust debt, and I clean it up fast." Authority stack built on PHAROS method without requiring a large brand. Raw source preserved in root; this wiki note closes the graph gap.

## Details

**Service tiers:**
- **Tier 1 — AI Use Policy Kit:** $750–$1,500. Solo creators, consultants, agencies. Deliverables: usage policy, client disclosure, internal checklist, data handling guide.
- **Tier 2 — AI Trust Sprint:** $2,500–$5,000. Small businesses with teams. Deliverables: AI use inventory, risk register, policy pack, vendor review, staff rules, governance roadmap.
- **Tier 3 — AI Governance Retainer:** $2,000–$6,000/month. Companies deploying AI into operations.
- **Premium day-rate — $1,000 AI Trust Day:** One-day delivery → trust pack + Loom walkthrough.

**30-day cash plan target ($10K):** 4× $2,500 sprints, or 10× $1,000 days, or blended.

**Positioning wedge:** "I help small businesses use AI without looking reckless to clients, partners, platforms, or regulators."

**ChatGPT delivery workflow:** Feed client intake → AI use inventory → risk classification → policy draft → client disclosure → risk register → executive summary. Operator owns: final risk judgment, legal boundary statements, sensitive data decisions.

**Cold outreach scripts:** Both email and DM versions included in raw source.
