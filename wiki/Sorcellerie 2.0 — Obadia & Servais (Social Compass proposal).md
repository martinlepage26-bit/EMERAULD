# Sorcellerie 2.0 — Obadia & Servais (Social Compass proposal)

## Summary
Proposal text for a Social Compass special issue (Obadia/Servais) framing “**sorcellerie 2.0**” as an emerging field parallel to digital religion studies: WitchTok/Instagram and broader digital ecologies are treated as conditions for a renewed, hypermodern witchcraft (aesthetic-romantic, socially/politically engaged, often feminist/ecological), with distinctive sociabilities (online covens, platform dynamics) and hybridizations with national/religious backgrounds. Reference for [[Queer Sociology of Magic and Ritual]] (digital ecologies as ritual sociability) and [[Media Studies and Pop Culture Analysis]] (platform-mediated witchcraft as media-studies object). Submission target tracked under [[Academic Paper Pipeline]].

## Axes named in the proposal (high-signal)
- Digital redeployment of contemporary witchcraft (Wicca and beyond) into platform ecosystems.
- New sociabilities: online covens, generational effects, teen feminist cultures, identity shifts.
- Hybridization with national cultures and “spiritual” systems; politicization and minority claims.
- Political economy: “witchy apps” markets; belief–economy–technology articulation.

## Why it matters here
- Directly supports Martin’s digital witchcraft lane (proof regimes, ritual authority, mediatization), especially where the object is not “religion online” but **witchcraft as a category** under digital conditions.
- Adds a concrete, source-faithful anchor for “WitchTok” framing inside [[Queer Sociology of Magic and Ritual]].
