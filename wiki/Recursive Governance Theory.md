# Recursive Governance Theory

Synthesis of the theoretical core of the PHAROS recursive deterministic governance method: the intellectual history of how the method was built, the three-order taxonomy that organizes it, and the scholarly outputs that formalize it.

**Foundation:** This method IS an evidence discipline system. The entire PHAROS pipeline is built on evidence validation, bounding, and promotion. See [[Evidence Discipline and Epistemics]] for the epistemological grounding.

This is the theoretical branch of the PHAROS graph. Its sibling branches are [[Evidence Discipline and Epistemics]], [[Governance Controls and Mechanisms]], [[HEPHAISTOS Agent Architecture]], [[AI Infrastructure Stack]], [[PHAROS Commercial Strategy]], and [[PHAROS Scholarly Publication Track]].

## Core Theoretical Architecture

- **[[Martin Walks the Witches' Road — Corpus as Charge-Persistence]]** — **Canonical corpus doctrine.** The charge (anti-individualist agency-under-constraint: Bourdieu/Foucault/Butler/Mauss/Honneth/Taylor/Crenshaw/Halberstam) persists by changing surface across every form in the corpus. None is the origin. Martin is simultaneously the cartographer and the traveler. Dr. Sort's failure is phase transition; PHAROS retains the charge by refusing automation. Read this first to understand why the corpus is a single governed arc, not a collection of projects.

- **[[Mythocritique to PHAROS — The 2010 Master's Thesis as Methodological Keystone]]** — Methodological substrate. The Osirian death/rebirth structure (named in the [[Mort et Naissance et L'Ombre et le Double — MA Thesis on Yvon Rivard (Université Laval, 2010)|MA thesis]] p. 81) is the structural ancestor of the recursive deterministic governance pipeline; Thomas's *procès* before the Council (MA pp. 17–19) is the structural ancestor of PHAROS adjudication; Hermetic mentalism (Kybalion principle 1 cited MA p. 80) grounds why governance cannot be sovereign-from-outside. Method-as-form, not method-as-content.
- [[Recursive Deterministic AI Governance — Method and Paper]] — Primary scholarly output. 12-stage pathway, 5-class evidence hierarchy, 6-step adjudication protocol. Target: AI & Society (Springer).
- [[Governance Typology — Recursive AI Governance Taxonomy]] — Defines the three-order taxonomy (soul→charge→power→power dynamics), dual-root topology, and clean typology for the paper corpus.
- [[First Method Paper — Recursive AI Governance as Executable Method]] — Intellectual history. Three-order logic: instability signals → executable governance → bounded non-finality. Documents the governance crises that forced successive architectural revisions.
- [[Loop Papers and Recursive Governance]] — Distinguishes recursiveness (returning to material) from recursivity (structural effects of return). Excluded texts as recursive governance objects.
- [[Phase 1 Governance Formation Sequence]] — Internal paper reconstructing the archive's formation logic. Engineered transition from charge-bearing epistemic material through first containment to protocol closure.
- [[Documents Root Loose Files Intake — 2026-04-28]] — Exact source-preservation pass for root Documents governance/protocol files, including the complete Theseus/Auryn/Hopf packet and Argus arbitration files.
- [[Smallest Building Block — Relation as Rule]] — Cross-domain operator synthesis (DRT7 / EML / lily-of-the-valley) arguing the primitive of reality is "a difference re-entered as a rule"; provides the meta-frame for why recursive constraint (not state assertion) carries governance load.
