# LOTUS Model and Agency

The LOTUS framework: a vectorized model for measuring individual agency and social positioning under constraint. Built by Martin as both a theoretical contribution and a PHAROS governance tool for measuring the conditions under which AI deployment either expands or contracts human agency.

---

## Core Model

- [[LOTUS Model — Agency and Social Positioning]] — Six-dimensional base LOTUS vector: perceptual latitude, regulatory bandwidth, access, social legibility, coherence, shadow remainder. Constraint vector. Scaffold vector. Effective agency computation with nonlinear threshold penalties.
- [[Bonded Intelligence Under Constraint — The LOTUS Processor Framework]] — Scientific grounding of the LOTUS model in coherence theory and constraint theory. Derives governance design constraints: regulation-first scaffolding, anti-coercive oversight.
