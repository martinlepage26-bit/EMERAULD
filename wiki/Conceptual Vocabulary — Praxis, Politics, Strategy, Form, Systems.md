# Conceptual Vocabulary — Praxis, Politics, Strategy, Form, Systems

## Summary

A compact term-selection grid: use **praxis** for lived application, **politics** for power and coordination, **strategy** for directed choice, **form** for design or expression, and **systems** for structure and operations. This belongs to [[Evidence Discipline and Epistemics]] because it constrains concepts before they drift into interchangeable abstractions.

## Context

This note functions as vocabulary discipline for [[Recursive Deterministic AI Governance — Method and Paper]], [[Governance Typology — Recursive AI Governance Taxonomy]], and related work in [[Governance and PHAROS MOC]]. It helps preserve distinctions that can otherwise collapse in governance, media, literary, and institutional analysis. The later [[Ethics Poetics and Praxis — Braided Discipline]] note expands the praxis/form distinction into a full triad of ethics, poetics, and praxis.

## Term Selection Grid

| Term | Use when you mean | Avoid using it when |
|---|---|---|
| Poietics | Making-process, generation, production, emergence, or how something is brought into being | The point is only expressive form, style, or rhetoric |
| Praxis | Lived application; theory as enacted practice | The point is only planning, messaging, or abstract theory |
| Politics | Power and coordination; who can act, align, block, authorize, or contest | The point is only personal preference or general disagreement |
| Strategy | Directed choice; a chosen path under constraints | The point is only broad structure or ongoing operations |
| Form | Design or expression; how something is shaped, staged, made perceptible, or aesthetically organized | The point is only institutional power or operational structure |
| Systems | Structure and operations; how components, rules, roles, and processes interact | The point is only a single decision, expression, or tactic |

## Key Ideas

- The terms are not synonyms for "important." Each one names a different kind of analytic work.
- The grid prevents conceptual overloading: a sentence about power should not hide inside "form," and a sentence about operations should not hide inside "strategy."
- In PHAROS-adjacent writing, this distinction supports [[Emotional Alliance vs. Evidentiary Discipline in AI]] by forcing the claim to name its actual control domain.
