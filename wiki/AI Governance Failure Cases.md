# AI Governance Failure Cases

Applied governance work: real and constructed case studies that demonstrate what happens when AI deployment proceeds without recursive governance controls. Each case maps onto specific PHAROS failure modes or promotion blocks.

**Methodological substrate:** Per [[Mythocritique to PHAROS — The 2010 Master's Thesis as Methodological Keystone|the keystone]], the *Khaibit* (shadow remainder) refusing administration is the structural reason automation of governance fails — failure cases re-instate the substrate's *shout-as-protection* logic from the [[Mort et Naissance et L'Ombre et le Double — MA Thesis on Yvon Rivard (Université Laval, 2010)|2010 MA thesis]] (p. 48): the shadow's refusal of full classification is what protects the system from its own dissolution into automation. [[Dr. Sort Codebase Version Genealogy — Three Iterations of Failed Automation]] is the worked operational case (per operator authorial attestation: *"Dr. Sort is a failed attempt at implementing automation of governance"*); failure across iterations is fidelity to the shadow remainder.
