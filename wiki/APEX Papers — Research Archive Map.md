# APEX Papers — Research Archive Map

## Summary

The APEX Papers are Martin Lepage's primary academic manuscript line, staged and reorganized into numbered subfolders under `/mnt/d/MASTER PACK/APEX_PAPERS_COMMON/` on 2026-03-29. Ten papers constitute the archive (note: slot 07 was merged into 08). Each folder contains a designated final artifact (`**`-marked in the [[MASTER PACK — D Drive Archive Map]] index) plus supporting provisional and placeholder files. This map serves as the retrieval index for the [[AI Society Manuscript — From AI Anxiety to Recursive Governance]] lineage and the broader [[PHAROS]] research program manuscripts.

## Context

The APEX archive was assembled from scattered locations across `/home/cerebrhoe/HEPHAISTOS_BUILD/`, Downloads, and local document folders. A `TRACE_SCAN_2026-03-29.md` and `ARCHIVE_TRIAGE.md` (generated by [[Recursive Governance Method — Skill Corpus Entry|recursive-governance-method]]) accompany the packet as bounded diagnostic artifacts. Status values are `firm`, `provisional`, or `placeholder`; they do not override APEX slot designations.

All paper folders also contain a `superceded/` subfolder holding earlier versions. A `COMPRESS/` folder holds supporting non-paper materials including a voice spec and a publication introduction.
