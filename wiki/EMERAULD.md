# EMERAULD

## Summary

EMERAULD is the local personal-knowledge and operator-continuity vault that holds the project context, persistence surfaces, and second-brain scaffolding for Martin's work. Its retrieval and graph layers are documented in [[EMERAULD Graph Architecture — Link Density and Vector Layer (2026-05-25)]], and its periodic structural-health reports — most recently [[Vault Health — 2026-07-05]] (prior: [[Vault Health — 2026-06-28]]) — track dangling links, orphans, and frontmatter debt. The June 2026 routing audit ([[WIKI-ROUTING-REPORT]]) identified 564 notes and produced destination recommendations for rerouting ~350 notes into the PARA area structure.
