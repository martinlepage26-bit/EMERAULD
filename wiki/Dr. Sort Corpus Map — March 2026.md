# Dr. Sort Corpus Map — March 2026

> **CORRECTION NOTICE (2026-05-03):** This note initially read the masterlist as proof-of-concept that LOTUS-derived classification works. **That framing was wrong.** Per operator authorial attestation: *"Dr. Sort is a failed attempt at implementing automation of governance."* Dr. Sort is the failed automation; PHAROS is the method that emerged from the failure. The masterlist documents the classifier's behavior, including its failures (1909–1942 misclassifications of novel-text-as-correspondence; 1995/2007 dates extracted from document content rather than composition timestamps; duplicate noise across his own self). **Per the [[Mythocritique to PHAROS — The 2010 Master's Thesis as Methodological Keystone|MA thesis frame]]**, the *Khaibit* (shadow remainder) cannot be administered by automation: *shout* in Egyptian is "métaphore de protection" (MA p. 48). The shadow refuses full classification by structural design. Dr. Sort's failure is fidelity to the source material — failure-as-method, in the same Witches' Road logic the operator analyzed in [[The Ballad of the Witches' Road — Analysis]] (canonical doctrine: [[Martin Walks the Witches' Road — Corpus as Charge-Persistence]]). **The masterlist is operational record of failure-as-phase-transition, not autopoietic success.**

## Summary

The 2026-03-08 [[Dr. Sort and LOTUS Ownership Decision — March 2026|Dr. Sort]] masterlist run is the operational record of a **failed attempt at automating governance through LOTUS-derived classification**. Dr. Sort, built by Martin and grounded in the LOTUS-derived vocabulary, was run against Martin's own personal archive of manuscripts, papers, screenplays, novels, and correspondence. The output (1045 lines, `raw sources/Dr_Sort_masterlist_2026-03-08.md`) classifies the corpus under one schema **and surfaces the structural reasons the automation failed**: the *Khaibit* (shadow remainder) refuses administration; novel text gets misread as correspondence; date extraction confuses content-dates with composition-dates; duplicates of his own self proliferate. The classifier's behavior is recursive: it documents the maker through the same apparatus the maker built, and exposes the limits of that apparatus when applied as automation. **Per the [[Mythocritique to PHAROS — The 2010 Master's Thesis as Methodological Keystone|keystone]]**, this failure is what produced PHAROS as method-not-automation. Related to [[LOTUS Model — Agency and Social Positioning]], [[Complete Paper List — Martin Lepage Corpus]], and [[Novel and Creative Corpus]].

## The Recursive Loop

Putting the pieces together:

1. Martin developed a theoretical apparatus for measuring agency under constraint (PhD work, 2007–2017)
2. Martin assembled this into a unified bibliography across PhD-era and PHAROS-era references (`MASTER BIBLIOGRAPHY.txt`)
3. Martin built [[Dr. Sort and LOTUS Ownership Decision — March 2026|Dr. Sort]] — a desktop document classifier — using the LOTUS conceptual vocabulary for content-aware analysis (see [[LOTUS Premium Spec]])
4. Dr. Sort uses the bibliography as cross-reference target — including the layer that contains Martin's own published works
5. Dr. Sort runs against Martin's complete personal archive
6. The output is a topology of Martin's intellectual life, classified by a tool grounded in a framework he derived from his own theoretical work

This is **recursive self-mapping**. The genealogist runs on the genealogist's own corpus. The cross-reference engine cross-references the maker against the canon the maker built. The 1995 horror screenplay sits next to the 2026 governance paper in the same classification schema, because the schema was derived from a theoretical framework that — per [[Bonded Intelligence Under Constraint — The LOTUS Processor Framework]] — sees both as expressions of the same anti-individualist concern with agency under constraint.

## What the Topology Proves

**Three claims, with triangulated evidence:**

1. **The corpus is unified by theoretical concern, not just by author.**
   - Trace A (bibliography): same theorists cited across PhD work and PHAROS work
   - Trace B (vault): [[Bonded Intelligence Under Constraint — The LOTUS Processor Framework]] explicitly names this continuity
   - Trace C (masterlist): Dr. Sort's classifier, agnostic to authorial intent, produces consistent classifications across creative + academic + governance layers

2. **The 2017 PhD dissertation is not a closed historical event.**
   - Trace A (bibliography): the dissertation bibliography is layered into the same working file as current AI ethics and Charmed/Agatha references
   - Trace B (vault): chapter drafts of *Why Be King* exist back to 2013, and the title resurfaces in [[Social Stratification, Intersectionality, and the Why Be King Manuscript]] as 2017 enriched manuscript with Chapter Six "Queering Magic"
   - Trace C (masterlist): "2013 - Why Be King I'm Already a Queen" appears as a CREATIVE manuscript, suggesting the work has continuous draft existence across the PhD and into book project status

3. **The creative work and the academic work are part of one inquiry.**
   - Trace A (bibliography): literary/philosophical scaffolding (Bachelard, Dante, Eliade, Lacan, Frye) sits alongside ethnographic theory (Bourdieu, Mauss, Goffman) in the same merged file
   - Trace B (vault): novels [[Alchemy of the Wound — Novel]], [[The Broken Frequency of the Word — Novel]], [[Le sang qui porte ton nom — Novel]] use the same vocabulary (recursion, agency, ritual, glitch, authority) as governance papers
   - Trace C (masterlist): Dr. Sort's CREATIVE/manuscript category contains novel work, screenplay, academic chapters, and book prospectuses — the classifier does not distinguish them by domain

## Reservations

- The 1909–1942 mis-classifications expose a real classifier limitation: surface features can misroute. Dr. Sort is not yet semantic-grade. This is consistent with [[LOTUS Premium Spec]]'s distinction between "implemented foundation form" and "still premium-roadmap."
- Year labels in the masterlist are unreliable for many CREATIVE entries; per [[Complete Paper List — Martin Lepage Corpus]], "all composition dates are 2024–2026; year labels in source filenames are archiving artifacts or subject-matter reference dates."
- The corpus snapshot is March 8, 2026; Martin's archive has changed since (e.g., Paper 25 added 2026-04-23, Self-Polygraph rewrite 2026-04-30).
