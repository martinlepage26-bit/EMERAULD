# LOTUS Model — Agency and Social Positioning

## Summary
LOTUS is a vectorized model for measuring individual agency and social positioning under constraint. Surfaced within [[Martin Lepage — Professional Profile|Martin's]] PHAROS ecosystem (2024–2026), the model quantifies how structural constraints and scaffolds interact with a person's base capabilities to produce effective agency. **Theoretical genealogy:** see [[MASTER BIBLIOGRAPHY — Theoretical Genealogy of LOTUS]] — LOTUS is documented as the operationalization of a theoretical apparatus developed across Martin's PhD-era ethnographic work on queer/pagan agency in Montreal (Bourdieu, Foucault, Butler, Mauss, Honneth, Charles Taylor, Crenshaw, Halberstam), translated into AI governance domain. **Application layer:** see [[LOTUS Premium Spec]] for the desktop product (Dr. Sort) that operationalizes the model; see [[LOTUS Agency Scoring App GPT — ChatGPT Product Specification]] for the GPT-based scoring interface. **Operational evidence:** see [[Dr. Sort Corpus Map — March 2026]]. Also related to [[PHAROS Recalibration — Unified Governance Architecture]], [[IF.EMOTION — Empathetic AI Architecture]], and the [[Healthcare Governance Packet — Recursive Governance for Providers]].

Sources: `raw sources/LOTUS APP - AGENCY AND SOCIAL POSITIONNING.txt` and `raw sources/Use a vectorized LOTUS model..txt`. Per [[MASTER BIBLIOGRAPHY — Theoretical Genealogy of LOTUS]], they are mappable to specific theoretical sources from Martin's PhD-era work — making LOTUS a case of **methodological isomorphism**: same theoretical apparatus, different domain.

## Details

### The LOTUS Vector
Base LOTUS vector: `L_base = [P, R, A, S, C, Sh]`
- **P** — Perceptual latitude (capacity to perceive one's situation accurately)
- **R** — Regulatory bandwidth (capacity to self-regulate under pressure) — *ba*-function (souffle de vie / breath of life animating the body); see [[Mythocritique to PHAROS — The 2010 Master's Thesis as Methodological Keystone|keystone]] for Egyptian-triad mapping
- **A** — Access (to resources, information, institutions)
- **S** — Social legibility (being readable and recognizable by systems and communities) — *ren*-discipline (the Name pronounced makes the spiritual image legible)
- **C** — Coherence (internal consistency of self-model and narrative)
- **Sh** — Shadow remainder (what cannot be fully captured by the other dimensions) — direct operationalization of the Egyptian *Khaibit* / *shout*. Per [[Mort et Naissance et L'Ombre et le Double — MA Thesis on Yvon Rivard (Université Laval, 2010)|MA thesis]] p. 48: the shadow is paradoxical — *shout* in Egyptian is a "métaphore de protection." The Sh is not statistical noise; it is the structural residue that resists administration and thereby protects the system. *This is also why [[Dr. Sort Corpus Map — March 2026|Dr. Sort failed]] as automation: it tried to administer the Khaibit.*

Each dimension can be decomposed by scale: micro, meso, macro.

### Constraint Vector
`K = [body, time, economic, relational, institutional, stigma, isolation, urgency]`
These are the active suppressors of base LOTUS capacity.

### Scaffold Vector
`G = [relational, material, clinical, cultural, spiritual, organizational, interpretive]`
These are the restoring forces — the supports that counteract constraints.

### Effective LOTUS State
`L_eff = M*L_base - N*K + Q*G`
Weighted combination of base capacity, constraints, and scaffolds.

### Agency Vector
`A_LOTUS = W*L_eff`
Six-dimensional agency output:
- sense reality
- choose under pressure
- act
- protect
- adapt
- coordinate

### Final Scalar Summary
`A_final = w^T * A_LOTUS`
With nonlinear penalties applied when isolation, urgency, overload, or collapse thresholds are crossed.

### Output Report Fields
- Base LOTUS vector
- Constraint vector
- Scaffold vector
- Effective LOTUS vector
- Derived agency vector
- Final scalar score
- Most suppressed dimensions
- Fastest-restoring scaffolds
- Distinction between observation, inference, and symbolic overlay

## Key Ideas
- Agency is not binary — it is a multidimensional vector sensitive to structural conditions
- Constraints and scaffolds interact nonlinearly at threshold conditions (isolation, urgency, collapse)
- The "shadow remainder" dimension acknowledges that full formalization is impossible
- The model explicitly separates observation, inference, and symbolic overlay in its output
