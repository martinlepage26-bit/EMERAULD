# Ethics Poetics and Praxis — Braided Discipline

## Summary

Ethics, poetics, and praxis form a single discipline of responsible making: ethics keeps power answerable, poetics keeps meaning alive, and praxis keeps vision in contact with the real. This note connects [[Martin Lepage — Professional Profile|Martin Lepage]]'s professional identity, [[Conceptual Vocabulary — Praxis, Politics, Strategy, Form, Systems|term-selection discipline]], and the governance logic documented in [[Governance and PHAROS MOC]].

## Governing Claim

Ethics, poetics, and praxis are not separate domains but a braided discipline for producing coherent work. Ethics asks what obligations, refusals, and consequences matter. Poetics asks how meaning should be shaped so that it remains vivid, exact, and inhabitable. Praxis asks whether convictions, forms, and plans survive contact with reality, repetition, constraint, and feedback.

## Core Triad

| Domain | Primary question | Failure mode | Maturing discipline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ethics | What should be done, refused, or repaired? | Appetite, convenience, extraction, harm hidden by abstraction | Responsibility, proportion, memory, answerability |
| Poetics | How should meaning appear and carry relation? | Cliche, ornament, deadened speech, slogans, inflated intensity | Precision, listening, revision, resonant form |
| Praxis | What can be carried forward under real conditions? | Fantasy disguised as planning, busyness, drift, avoidance | Repetition, iteration, maintenance, situated adaptation |

## Key Ideas

- Character, style, and action are accumulated through repetition rather than announced by self-description.
- Values become visible in what one refuses, what one cuts, and what one repeats.
- Culture is shaped by what it admires, what it tolerates, and what it makes easier to repeat.
- Relation is the hidden center of ethics, poetics, and praxis: every artifact, system, sentence, schedule, and threshold forms a relation.
- The mature task is not novelty or nostalgia but faithful transformation: keeping the core while revising the form under pressure.

## Connection to AI Governance

The triad becomes explicitly institutional in [[AI Governance Manifesto — Upstream Institutional Practice]]. AI governance must be ethical because it distributes harm, dignity, and obligation; poetic because it governs language, framing, and public imagination; and practical because principles that cannot survive procurement, product design, regulation, and operations remain decorative.

[[Poiesis Poietics Poetics Praxis — Making and Action Distinctions]] adds a finer distinction: when AI governance is discussed as world-making, institution-making, or condition-making, **poietics** is the stronger term. When governance is discussed as rhetoric, language, framing, symbolic form, or expressive structure, **poetics** is the stronger term.

This is also why [[Recursive Deterministic AI Governance — Method and Paper]] treats governance as workflow-bound control rather than rhetoric. A principle must become a control, a refusal, a threshold, a review path, or a repair mechanism before it becomes praxis.
