# Anti-Charm

## Summary

Anti-Charm is the discipline of refusing polished presentation as a substitute for evidence, structure, or honest limits. It operates as a structural interrupt: when an output looks good, anti-charm asks whether it *is* good — whether surface persuasiveness is backed by actual evidence and bounded claims. Closely paired with [[Diamond-Eyes — Aesthetic Refinement Skill]] (which validates craft) and [[Inner Mind Eye]] (which validates care); the three form the care-clarity-evidence triad at the center of the PHAROS governance gate.

## Context

Anti-charm applies whenever the danger is that fluency substitutes for substance — in governance promotion decisions, peer-reviewed drafts, agent outputs, and operator self-assessments. The paired failure mode is [[Emotional Alliance vs. Evidentiary Discipline in AI]]: the agent that validates the operator's frame rather than testing its evidence is practicing the opposite of anti-charm. Operationalized in the [[Evidence Discipline and Epistemics]] map.
