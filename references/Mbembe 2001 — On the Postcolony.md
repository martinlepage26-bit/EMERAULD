# On the Postcolony

## Citation
Mbembe, A. (2001) On the Postcolony. Berkeley, CA: University of California Press.

## Annotation
Achille Mbembe's On the Postcolony is a landmark work of postcolonial theory analyzing power, subjectivity, and everyday life in postcolonial Africa, developing concepts such as the “banality of power” and the grotesque intimacy between rulers and ruled. It reshaped African studies and critical theory. For this corpus it grounds a critical account of power, violence, and subject-formation under postcolonial conditions.
