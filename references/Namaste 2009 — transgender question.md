# transgender question

## Citation
Namaste, V. (2009). Undoing theory :the« transgender question» and the epistemic

## Annotation
Viviane Namaste's “Undoing Theory” critically interrogates how the “transgender question” is taken up within queer theory, arguing that trans people are often reduced to a theoretical example while their material lives and epistemic authority are ignored. (The canonical citation is truncated.) Namaste is a foundational figure in trans studies. For this corpus it grounds a trans-studies critique of queer theory's treatment of transgender lives.
