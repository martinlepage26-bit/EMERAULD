# Birth of an Industry: Blackface Minstrelsy and the Rise of American Animation

## Citation
Sammond, N. (2015) Birth of an Industry: Blackface Minstrelsy and the Rise of American Animation. Durham, NC: Duke University Press.

## Annotation
Nicholas Sammond's Birth of an Industry makes the provocative argument that American animation's foundational characters and aesthetics emerged from blackface minstrelsy, tracing the racial genealogy of early cartoons. Published by Duke University Press, it is a major intervention in animation and critical race studies. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the analysis of race in the history of animation.
