# Clinical Implementation of Artificial Intelligence Scribes in Health Care: A Systematic Review

## Citation
Hassan, Hadeel, Zipursky, Amy R, Rabbani, Naveed, You, Jacqueline G, Tse, Gabriel, Orenstein, Evan, Ray, Mondira, Parsons, Chase, Shin, Stella, Lawton, Gregory, Jessa, Karim, Sung, Lillian, Yan, Adam P (2025) Clinical Implementation of Artificial Intelligence Scribes in Health Care: A Systematic Review. Applied clinical informatics, 16(4), 1121-1135. doi: 10.1055/a-2597-2017

## Annotation
Hassan and colleagues conduct a systematic review of the clinical implementation of AI scribes — tools using speech recognition and NLP to automate clinical documentation — proposing an evaluation framework and identifying effects on clinicians, patients, and organizations, along with gaps in the literature. Published in Applied Clinical Informatics, it contributes to research on AI in healthcare workflows. For this corpus it grounds the practical governance and evaluation of clinical AI tools.
