# Magic, Witchcraft and the Otherworld: An Anthropology

## Citation
Greenwood, Susan. Magic, Witchcraft and the Otherworld: An Anthropology. Oxford: Berg, 2000.

## Annotation
Susan Greenwood's Magic, Witchcraft and the Otherworld offers an anthropology of contemporary magic, examining how practitioners experience and navigate the “otherworld” and what magical consciousness entails. It contributes to Pagan studies and the anthropology of religion. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation form.)
