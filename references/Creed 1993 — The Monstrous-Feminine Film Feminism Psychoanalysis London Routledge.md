# The Monstrous-Feminine: Film, Feminism, Psychoanalysis. London: Routledge.

## Citation
Creed, B. (1993). The Monstrous-Feminine: Film, Feminism, Psychoanalysis. London: Routledge.

## Annotation
Landmark feminist-psychoanalytic study of the 'monstrous-feminine' in horror cinema, arguing that female monstrosity expresses patriarchal anxieties about the maternal and abject body.
