# Studying complexity in health services research: desperately seeking an overdue paradigm shift

## Citation
Greenhalgh, T., Papoutsi, C. (2018). Studying complexity in health services research: desperately seeking an overdue paradigm shift. BMC Medicine, 16(1). https://doi.org/10.1186/s12916-018-1089-4

## Annotation
Greenhalgh and Papoutsi argue for an overdue paradigm shift in health-services research toward complexity science, contending that linear, reductionist methods fail to capture the dynamic, emergent nature of health systems. They advocate approaches that embrace non-linearity, context, and self-organization. Published in BMC Medicine, it is an influential methodological call. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds complexity-informed inquiry into systems and interventions.
