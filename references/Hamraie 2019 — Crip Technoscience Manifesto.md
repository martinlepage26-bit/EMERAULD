# Crip Technoscience Manifesto

## Citation
Hamraie, A., Fritsch, K. (2019). Crip Technoscience Manifesto. Catalyst: Feminism, Theory, Technoscience, 5(1), 1-33. https://doi.org/10.28968/cftt.v5i1.29607

## Annotation
Hamraie and Fritsch's “Crip Technoscience Manifesto” articulates “crip technoscience” — disabled people's practices of critiquing, altering, and reinventing the material-discursive world toward disability justice. Rejecting design that treats disability as an afterthought, the manifesto centers disabled people as agents of world-building and technological remaking. Published in Catalyst, it is a foundational text linking disability studies and STS. For this corpus it grounds a disability-justice approach to technology design and critique.
