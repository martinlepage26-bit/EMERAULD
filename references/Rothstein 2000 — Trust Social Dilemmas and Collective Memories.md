# Trust, Social Dilemmas and Collective Memories

## Citation
Rothstein, B. (2000). Trust, Social Dilemmas and Collective Memories. Journal of Theoretical Politics, 12(4), 477-501. https://doi.org/10.1177/0951692800012004007

## Annotation
Bo Rothstein's article examines how trust, social dilemmas, and collective memories interrelate, arguing that shared understandings of the past shape whether people can cooperate and trust institutions. Published in the Journal of Theoretical Politics, it contributes to social-capital and institutional theory. For this corpus it grounds the analysis of trust as a condition for collective action and effective governance.
