# Traditions and Trajectories in the Historiography of European Witch Hunting

## Citation
Fudge, T. (2006). Traditions and Trajectories in the Historiography of European Witch Hunting. History Compass, 4(3), 488-527. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1478-0542.2006.00310.x

## Annotation
Thomas Fudge's article reviews the traditions and trajectories in the historiography of European witch hunting from roughly 1450 to 1750, surveying schools of thought from the nineteenth century to the present and focusing on power across social, political, and religious cultures. Published in History Compass, it is a useful synthesizing and teaching resource. For this corpus it grounds the scholarly landscape of witchcraft historiography.
