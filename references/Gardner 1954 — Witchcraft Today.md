# Witchcraft Today

## Citation
Gardner, G., 1954. Witchcraft Today. London: Rider & Company.

## Annotation
Gerald Gardner's Witchcraft Today (1954) is the founding public text of modern Wicca, presenting (with an introduction by Margaret Murray) the claim that a surviving pre-Christian witch-cult persisted into the present. Written after the repeal of the UK Witchcraft Act, it launched Gardnerian Wicca and shaped the entire contemporary Pagan revival. Though its historical claims are now discredited, its influence is foundational. For this corpus it is a primary source at the origin of modern Witchcraft.
