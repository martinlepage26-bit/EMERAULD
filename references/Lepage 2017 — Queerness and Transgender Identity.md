# Queerness and Transgender Identity

## Citation
Lepage, M. (2017). Queerness and Transgender Identity. Studies in Religion/Sciences Religieuses, 46(4), 601-619. https://doi.org/10.1177/0008429817710971

## Annotation
Martin Lepage's “Queerness and Transgender Identity” examines how contemporary Paganism — especially witchcraft — enables queer and transgender Pagans to negotiate gender, contrasting how Montreal's Wicca and Reclaiming Witchcraft traditions develop opposing views on gender. Published in Studies in Religion/Sciences Religieuses (peer-reviewed, DOI verified), it argues that queer negotiations of gender norms position participants in distinct ways within Paganism and society. For this corpus it is a central source on queer/trans gender negotiation in Montreal Paganism.
