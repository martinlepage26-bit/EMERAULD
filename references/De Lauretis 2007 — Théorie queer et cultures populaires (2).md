# Théorie queer et cultures populaires

## Citation
De Lauretis, Teresa. 2007. Théorie queer et cultures populaires. De Foucault à Cronenberg. Translated by Marie-Hélène Bourcier. Paris: La dispute.

## Annotation
Théorie queer et cultures populaires gathers Teresa de Lauretis's essays reading popular culture and film through queer theory, in a French translation by Marie-Hélène Bourcier that helped introduce her thought to francophone readers. De Lauretis's work is foundational to the field she named. (This entry duplicates the same collection in a different citation form.)
