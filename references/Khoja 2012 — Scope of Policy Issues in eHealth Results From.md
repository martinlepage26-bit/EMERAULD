# Scope of Policy Issues in eHealth: Results From a Structured Literature Review

## Citation
Khoja, S., Durrani, H., Nayani, P., Fahim, A. (2012). Scope of Policy Issues in eHealth: Results From a Structured Literature Review. Journal of Medical Internet Research, 14(1), e34. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.1633

## Annotation
Khoja and colleagues conduct a structured literature review of policy issues in eHealth, mapping the scope of governance, regulatory, and implementation concerns raised by digital health. Published in the Journal of Medical Internet Research, it contributes to health-policy and digital-health scholarship. For this corpus it grounds the policy dimension of health technology — adjacent to AI-in-health governance.
