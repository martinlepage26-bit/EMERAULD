# Negotiating the Swamp: The Opportunity and Challenge of Reflexivity in Research Practice.

## Citation
Finlay, Linda. 2002. “Negotiating the Swamp: The Opportunity and Challenge of Reflexivity in Research Practice.” Qualitative Research 2 (2): 209–230.

## Annotation
Linda Finlay's “Negotiating the Swamp” is a widely cited methodological article on reflexivity in qualitative research, mapping five variants — introspection, intersubjective reflection, mutual collaboration, social critique, and discursive deconstruction — and examining how researchers navigate the “swamp” of self-analysis. Published in Qualitative Research, it is a standard reference on reflexive practice. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds a typology of reflexivity.
