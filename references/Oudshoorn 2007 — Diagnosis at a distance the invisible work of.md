# Diagnosis at a distance: the invisible work of patients and healthcare professionals in cardiac telemonitoring technology

## Citation
Oudshoorn, N. (2007). Diagnosis at a distance: the invisible work of patients and healthcare professionals in cardiac telemonitoring technology. Sociology of Health &amp; Illness, 30(2), 272-288. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9566.2007.01032.x

## Annotation
Nelly Oudshoorn's “Diagnosis at a Distance” is an STS study of cardiac telemonitoring that reveals the “invisible work” performed by patients and healthcare professionals to make remote-monitoring technology function. It shows that technologies depend on unacknowledged human labor and reconfigure roles and responsibilities. For this corpus it grounds the analysis of hidden labor and role redistribution in health technology — relevant to how AI systems depend on human work.
