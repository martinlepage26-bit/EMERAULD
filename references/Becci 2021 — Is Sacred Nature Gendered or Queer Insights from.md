# Is Sacred Nature Gendered or Queer? Insights from a Study on Eco-Spiritual Activism in Switzerland

## Citation
Becci, I., Grandjean, A. (2021). Is Sacred Nature Gendered or Queer? Insights from a Study on Eco-Spiritual Activism in Switzerland. Religions, 13(1), 23. https://doi.org/10.3390/rel13010023

## Annotation
Becci and Grandjean's article draws on a 2015–2020 ethnographic study of eco-spiritual activists in French-speaking Switzerland to ask whether “sacred nature” is figured as gendered or queer. They show that across three milieus — New Age/holistic, the transition network, and neo-shamanism — activists articulate gendered notions like “Mother Earth” differently, generating varied emic perspectives that link environmental and spiritual action. Published in Religions, it contributes to the study of gender, ecology, and contemporary spirituality. For this corpus it connects eco-spiritual activism to gender and queer analysis.
