# Les HeeBeeGeeBee Healers au festival Burning Man: trois récits de guérison.

## Citation
Gauthier, François. “Les HeeBeeGeeBee Healers au festival Burning Man: trois récits de guérison.” Ethnologies 33, no. 1 (2011): 191–215.

## Annotation
François Gauthier's article on the “HeeBeeGeeBee Healers” at the Burning Man festival analyzes three healing narratives, examining how alternative healing and spirituality are practiced and experienced in this countercultural gathering. Published in Ethnologies, it contributes to the study of contemporary spirituality, festival, and healing. For this corpus it grounds the ethnography of ritual healing in alternative-cultural settings.
