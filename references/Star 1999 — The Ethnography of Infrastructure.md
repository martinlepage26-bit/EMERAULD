# The Ethnography of Infrastructure.

## Citation
Star, Susan Leigh. "The Ethnography of Infrastructure." American Behavioral Scientist 43, no. 3 (1999): 377–391.

## Annotation
Susan Leigh Star's “The Ethnography of Infrastructure” is a foundational STS essay arguing that infrastructure is typically invisible until it breaks down, and offering methods for studying the embedded, relational, and standardized systems that support work and life. It reframes infrastructure as an analytic object with political and moral dimensions. For this corpus it is highly relevant: it grounds how to study the mostly invisible data and computational infrastructures underlying AI and governance.
