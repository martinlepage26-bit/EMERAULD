# The Meanings of Magic.

## Citation
Bailey, Michael. “The Meanings of Magic.” Magic, Ritual, and Witchcraft 1, no. 1 (2006): 1–23.

## Annotation
Michael Bailey's “The Meanings of Magic” interrogates the category of magic, showing how its definitions have shifted and how the term has been used to police the boundaries between religion, science, and superstition. Appearing as the first article in Magic, Ritual, and Witchcraft, it argues that the instability of “magic” is analytically significant rather than a mere problem to be resolved. It is a foundational reference for the critical study of magic. (This entry duplicates the same article listed in a different citation form.)
