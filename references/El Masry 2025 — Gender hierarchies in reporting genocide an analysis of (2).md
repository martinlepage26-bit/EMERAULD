# Gender hierarchies in reporting genocide: an analysis of the dehumanization of Palestinian men in Western media

## Citation
El Masry, N., Sawaf, Z., King, G., Baroudi, S. (2025). Gender hierarchies in reporting genocide: an analysis of the dehumanization of Palestinian men in Western media. Communication, Culture &amp; Critique, 18(4), 310-321. https://doi.org/10.1093/ccc/tcaf032

## Annotation
El Masry and colleagues analyze gender hierarchies in the reporting of genocide, showing through quantitative analysis of BBC and CNN coverage how Western media dehumanize Palestinian men during the Gaza war. The study combines web scraping with framing analysis to document patterns of erasure and naturalized suffering. Published in Communication, Culture & Critique, it is a critical media-studies contribution. (This entry duplicates the same article in a different citation form.)
