# Counterfactual Explanations Without Opening the Black Box: Automated Decisions and the GDPR

## Citation
Wachter, S., Mittelstadt, B., & Russell, C. (2018). Counterfactual Explanations Without Opening the Black Box: Automated Decisions and the GDPR. Harvard Journal of Law & Technology, 31(2), 841–887.

## Annotation
Wachter, Mittelstadt, and Russell's “Counterfactual Explanations Without Opening the Black Box” (Harvard Journal of Law & Technology) argues that counterfactual explanations can satisfy demands for transparency and contestability in automated decisions while protecting trade secrets and model internals. It has been highly influential in XAI and AI law. (This entry duplicates the same paper in its journal form.)
