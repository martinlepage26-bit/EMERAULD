# The Posthuman

## Citation
Braidotti, Rosi (2013) The Posthuman. Polity Press.

## Annotation
Rosi Braidotti's The Posthuman argues for an affirmative, materialist posthumanism that displaces the humanist subject in favor of a relational, embodied, and non-anthropocentric account of subjectivity. It maps the convergence of post-humanist and post-anthropocentric thought and its consequences for ethics and knowledge. It is a cornerstone of critical posthumanism.
