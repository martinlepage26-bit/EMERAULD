# A Public Management for All Seasons?

## Citation
Hood, Christopher. "A Public Management for All Seasons?" Public Administration 69, no. 1 (1991): 3–19.

## Annotation
Christopher Hood's “A Public Management for All Seasons?” is the canonical article naming and analyzing “New Public Management” (NPM) — the wave of managerialist, market-oriented reforms in the public sector emphasizing performance measurement, competition, and private-sector techniques. It is one of the most cited works in public administration. For this corpus it grounds the analysis of managerialism, metrics, and marketization in governance.
