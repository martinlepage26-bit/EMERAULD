# Trouble dans le Genre: Le Féminisme et la Subversion de l’Identité

## Citation
Butler, Judith. 1990. Trouble dans le Genre: Le Féminisme et la Subversion de l’Identité. Paris: La Découverte.

## Annotation
The French edition of Gender Trouble (Trouble dans le genre) makes Butler's foundational argument available to francophone readers: gender is performatively produced through repeated acts within regulatory norms, and the apparent naturalness of the sex/gender/desire triad is an effect of power. The translation was important for the reception of queer theory in France. (This entry duplicates Gender Trouble in its French edition.)
