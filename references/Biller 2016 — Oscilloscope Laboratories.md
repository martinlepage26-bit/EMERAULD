# Oscilloscope Laboratories

## Citation
Biller, Anna, dir. The Love Witch. 2016. Oscilloscope Laboratories. Film.

## Annotation
Anna Biller's The Love Witch is a stylized feature film that pastiches 1960s Technicolor melodrama and horror to tell the story of a modern witch who uses love magic in pursuit of romance, foregrounding gender, desire, and female fantasy. As an auteur work, it is a rich primary text for feminist film and witchcraft-in-media studies. For this corpus it offers a contemporary cinematic elaboration of the witch figure and the politics of feminine desire.
