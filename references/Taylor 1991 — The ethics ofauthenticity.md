# The ethics ofauthenticity

## Citation
Taylor, C. (1991). The ethics ofauthenticity. Cambridge: Harvard University Press.

## Annotation
Charles Taylor's The Ethics of Authenticity diagnoses the modern ideal of authenticity — being true to oneself — arguing that while it can slide into trivial self-absorption and relativism, it also contains a genuine moral ideal that, properly understood, requires horizons of significance and dialogue with others. It is a widely read work of moral philosophy. For this corpus it grounds a nuanced account of authenticity — relevant to debates on selfhood, spirituality, and identity.
