# Digitalisation, artificial intelligence and algorithmic management in the workplace: shaping the future of work : cost of non Europe

## Citation
(2025). Digitalisation, artificial intelligence and algorithmic management in the workplace: shaping the future of work : cost of non Europe. Publications Office. https://doi.org/10.2861/0136788

## Annotation
This European Union publication, “Digitalisation, Artificial Intelligence and Algorithmic Management in the Workplace: Shaping the Future of Work — Cost of Non-Europe,” assesses the implications of AI and algorithmic management for labour and the potential costs of failing to act at the EU level. As an official policy study, it is a primary source on AI, work, and governance. For this corpus it grounds the labour dimension of AI governance in the EU.
