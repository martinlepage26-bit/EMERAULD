# The History of Sexuality, Volume 1: An Introduction

## Citation
Foucault, Michel. 1978. The History of Sexuality, Volume 1: An Introduction. Translated by Robert Hurley. New York: Pantheon Books.

## Annotation
Michel Foucault's The History of Sexuality, Volume 1 challenges the “repressive hypothesis,” arguing that modern power did not silence sex but incited an explosion of discourse about it, producing sexuality as an object of knowledge and a target of biopower. Foucault introduces the concept of biopower — the administration of life at the level of populations. It is foundational for queer theory, sexuality studies, and biopolitics. For this corpus it grounds the analysis of sexuality, discourse, and the power over life.
