# Audit Cultures: Anthropological Studies in Accountability, Ethics and the Academy

## Citation
Strathern, Marilyn, ed. Audit Cultures: Anthropological Studies in Accountability, Ethics and the Academy. London: Routledge, 2000.

## Annotation
Edited by Marilyn Strathern, Audit Cultures gathers anthropological studies of accountability, audit, and assessment as they reshape institutions, especially the academy. The contributors examine how audit regimes produce new forms of visibility, responsibility, and self-governance — often with perverse effects. It is a key reference on the anthropology of accountability. For this corpus it is highly relevant: it critically analyzes the spread of audit and metrics that also underlie AI-governance and assessment practices.
