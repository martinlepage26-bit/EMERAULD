# Cybercoven : being a witch online

## Citation
Klassen, C. (2002). Cybercoven : being a witch online. Studies in religion/Sciences

## Annotation
Chris Klassen's “Cybercoven” uses a case study of an email discussion group and a selection of Witchcraft websites to argue that the divide between “virtual” and “real” can be rethought so that solitary Witches practice their earth-based religion online, including adapting divination rites to online settings. Published in Studies in Religion, it is an early study of digital Paganism. For this corpus it grounds the practice of Witchcraft in online environments.
