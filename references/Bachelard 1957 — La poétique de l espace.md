# La poétique de l’espace

## Citation
Bachelard, Gaston. 1957. La poétique de l’espace. Paris: Presses universitaires de France.

## Annotation
Gaston Bachelard's The Poetics of Space is a landmark phenomenological study of intimate, lived space — the house, the nest, drawers, corners — as sites of reverie and psychic dwelling. Bachelard argues that certain spaces concentrate memory and imagination, offering a “topoanalysis” of felt interiority. The book has been enormously influential across architecture, literary theory, and geography. For this corpus it provides a phenomenological framework for thinking place, dwelling, and the imagination of space.
