# Le Deuxième Sexe

## Citation
Beauvoir, Simone de. 1949. Le Deuxième Sexe. Paris: Gallimard.

## Annotation
Simone de Beauvoir's Le Deuxième Sexe (1949) argues that femininity is not a natural essence but a social situation into which women are made, encapsulated in her formula that one becomes rather than is born a woman. Ranging across biology, psychoanalysis, history, and myth, Beauvoir analyzes woman's construction as the Other. It is a cornerstone of feminist thought.
