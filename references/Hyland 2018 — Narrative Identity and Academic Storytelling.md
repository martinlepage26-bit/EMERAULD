# Narrative, Identity and Academic Storytelling

## Citation
Hyland, K. (2018). Narrative, Identity and Academic Storytelling. ILCEA, 31. https://doi.org/10.4000/ilcea.4677

## Annotation
Ken Hyland's “Narrative, Identity and Academic Storytelling” examines how academics construct “identity narratives” of themselves in less-studied, less-constrained genres — thesis acknowledgments, bio statements, and personal web pages — where the usual demands of anonymity and depersonalization are relaxed. Published in ILCEA, it contributes to applied linguistics and academic-discourse studies. For this corpus it grounds analysis of self-representation and identity in academic genres.
