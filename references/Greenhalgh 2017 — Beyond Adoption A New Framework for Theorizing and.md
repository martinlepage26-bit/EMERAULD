# Beyond Adoption: A New Framework for Theorizing and Evaluating Nonadoption, Abandonment, and Challenges to the Scale-Up, Spread, and Sustainability of Health and Care Technologies

## Citation
Greenhalgh, T., Wherton, J., Papoutsi, C., Lynch, J., et al. (2017). Beyond Adoption: A New Framework for Theorizing and Evaluating Nonadoption, Abandonment, and Challenges to the Scale-Up, Spread, and Sustainability of Health and Care Technologies. Journal of Medical Internet Research, 19(11), e367. https://doi.org/10.2196/jmir.8775

## Annotation
Greenhalgh and colleagues present the NASSS framework (Non-adoption, Abandonment, and challenges to Scale-up, Spread, and Sustainability) for theorizing and evaluating why health and care technologies succeed or fail in practice. The framework attends to complexity across condition, technology, value proposition, adopters, organization, and wider system. Widely cited, it is a leading tool for studying technology implementation. For this corpus it grounds analysis of why technologies (including AI) are or aren't adopted and sustained in institutions.
