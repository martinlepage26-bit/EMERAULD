# Case Studies and Theory Development in the Social Sciences

## Citation
George, A.L. and Bennett, A. (2005) Case Studies and Theory Development in the Social Sciences. Cambridge, MA: MIT Press.

## Annotation
George and Bennett's Case Studies and Theory Development in the Social Sciences is a foundational methods text codifying case-study research and process tracing for building and testing theory. It sets out structured, focused comparison and standards for causal inference from cases. It is a standard reference in qualitative and mixed-methods research. For method-focused work in this corpus, it anchors rigorous case-based inquiry.
