# La réflexion queer : apports et limites

## Citation
Lamoureux, D. (2005). La réflexion queer : apports et limites. Dans M. N. Mensah

## Annotation
Diane Lamoureux's “La réflexion queer: apports et limites” assesses the contributions and limits of queer theory from within francophone feminist debate, weighing what queer critique offers and where it falls short for feminist politics. Published in a collection on the third feminist wave, it is a thoughtful critical engagement. For this corpus it grounds a francophone feminist appraisal of queer theory.
