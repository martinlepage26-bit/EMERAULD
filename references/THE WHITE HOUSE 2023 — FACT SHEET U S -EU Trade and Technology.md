This White House fact sheet describes how the U.S.–EU Trade and Technology Council (TTC) deepens transatlantic cooperation, including coordination on emerging technologies and AI governance. As an official policy document, it is a primary source on transatlantic technology diplomacy. For this corpus it grounds international coordination on AI and technology governance between the U.S. and EU.
