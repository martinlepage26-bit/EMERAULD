# AI, Cultural Heritage, and Bias: Some Key Queries That Arise from the Use of GenAI

## Citation
Foka, A., & Griffin, G. (2024). AI, Cultural Heritage, and Bias: Some Key Queries That Arise from the Use of GenAI. Heritage.

## Annotation
Foka and Griffin examine bias in the use of generative AI and machine learning to interpret and classify cultural-heritage artifacts, arguing that bias is already inherent in cultural-heritage collections and their digitized versions and that AI pipelines may amplify it. They stress the need for well-annotated datasets and humanities expertise. Published in Heritage, it contributes to critical AI and digital-humanities scholarship. For this corpus it grounds the analysis of AI bias in the cultural and memory domain.
