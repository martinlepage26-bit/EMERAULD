# La fabrique des identités sexuelles

## Citation
Broqua, C. et Eboko, F. (2009). La fabrique des identités sexuelles. Autrepart, 49, 3

## Annotation
Edited by Christophe Broqua and Fred Eboko, La fabrique des identités sexuelles (a special issue of Autrepart) examines how sexual identities are produced across varied social and geographic contexts, including the Global South. The contributors analyze the making and negotiation of sexual categories under differing cultural and political conditions. It contributes to the transnational sociology of sexuality. For this corpus it grounds the constructed and situated character of sexual identity.
