# Witchcraft, Magic and Culture, 1736–1951

## Citation
Davies, O., 2013. Witchcraft, Magic and Culture, 1736–1951. Manchester: Manchester University Press.

## Annotation
Owen Davies's Witchcraft, Magic and Culture, 1736–1951 examines the persistence of witchcraft and magical belief in Britain after the repeal of the witchcraft statutes, showing that belief and practice endured long past the era of the trials. Davies draws on rich archival evidence to document popular magic in the modern period. Published by Manchester University Press, it is a standard scholarly reference. For this corpus it grounds the modern afterlife of witchcraft belief.
