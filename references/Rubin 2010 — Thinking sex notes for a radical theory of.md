# Thinking sex :notes for a radical theory of the politics of sexuality

## Citation
Rubin, G. (2010). Thinking sex :notes for a radical theory of the politics of sexuality.

## Annotation
Gayle Rubin's “Thinking Sex: Notes for a Radical Theory of the Politics of Sexuality” is one of the founding texts of queer theory and sexuality studies, arguing for an analysis of sexuality as its own vector of oppression (distinct from gender) and mapping the “charmed circle” of legitimate sex against the “outer limits” of stigmatized practices. It reshaped feminist and queer thought about sexual hierarchy. For this corpus it is a canonical source on the politics of sexuality.
