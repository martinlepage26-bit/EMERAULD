# Witchcraft and the gay counterculture

## Citation
Evans, A. (1978). Witchcraft and the gay counterculture. Boston: FAG RAG.

## Annotation
Arthur Evans's Witchcraft and the Gay Counterculture is a foundational text of gay radicalism and the Radical Faerie movement, reinterpreting the history of witch persecution as an assault on gay people, women, and nature-based pagan traditions by patriarchal Christianity. Polemical and visionary rather than conventionally historical, it links queer liberation to pre-Christian spirituality. For this corpus it is a primary source in the genealogy of queer paganism and the politicized reclamation of witchcraft.
