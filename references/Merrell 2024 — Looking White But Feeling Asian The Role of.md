# Looking White But Feeling Asian: The Role of Perceived Membership Permeability and Perceived Discrimination in Multiracial-Monoracial Alliances

## Citation
Merrell, W., Vossoughi, N., Kteily, N., Ho, A. (2024). Looking White But Feeling Asian: The Role of Perceived Membership Permeability and Perceived Discrimination in Multiracial-Monoracial Alliances. Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin, 52(1), 141-160. https://doi.org/10.1177/01461672241267332

## Annotation
Merrell and colleagues examine multiracial–monoracial alliances, focusing on how perceived membership permeability and perceived discrimination shape solidarity for people who “look White but feel Asian.” Published in Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin, it belongs to the social psychology of race and identity. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized empirical source on multiracial identity.
