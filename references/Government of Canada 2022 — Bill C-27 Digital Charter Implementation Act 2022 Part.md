# Bill C-27, Digital Charter Implementation Act, 2022, Part 3: Artificial Intelligence and Data Act (AIDA)

## Citation
Government of Canada. (2022). Bill C-27, Digital Charter Implementation Act, 2022, Part 3: Artificial Intelligence and Data Act (AIDA). Proposed legislation.

## Annotation
Canada's Bill C-27 includes, in Part 3, the proposed Artificial Intelligence and Data Act (AIDA) — the federal government's first attempt at binding AI regulation, focused on “high-impact” AI systems, harm mitigation, and enforcement. As proposed legislation, it is a central primary source for Canadian AI governance. For this corpus it anchors analysis of Canada's emerging statutory AI regime.
