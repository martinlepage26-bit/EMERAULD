# What to wear in a vampire film

## Citation
Caws, M. A. (2011). What to wear in a vampire film. In A. Munich (Ed.), Fashion in film (pp. 40-52). Bloomington: Indiana University Press.

## Annotation
Mary Ann Caws's “What to Wear in a Vampire Film,” in Fashion in Film (ed. Adrienne Munich), analyzes how costume and dress construct meaning, gender, and desire in vampire cinema.
