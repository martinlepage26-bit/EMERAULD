# A Manifesto for Performative Research.

## Citation
Haseman, Brad. 2006. “A Manifesto for Performative Research.” Media International Australia 118 (1): 98–106.

## Annotation
Brad Haseman's “A Manifesto for Performative Research” argues that arts, media, and design researchers need a distinct research paradigm — “performative research” (named after Austin's speech-act theory) — alongside the quantitative and qualitative, in which practice itself constitutes inquiry. Published in Media International Australia, it is a foundational statement of practice-led research. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds performative and practice-based research.
