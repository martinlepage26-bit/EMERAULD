# Résumé du chapitre sur le commerce numérique

## Citation
GOUVERNEMENT DU CANADA (2019). Résumé du chapitre sur le commerce numérique. L’Accord Canada– États-Unis–Mexique (ACEUM). En ligne: https://www.international.gc.ca/trade-commerce/trade-agreements-accords-commerciaux/agr-acc/cusma-aceum/digital_trade-commerce_numerique.aspx?lang=fra.

## Annotation
This Government of Canada summary of the digital-trade chapter of the Canada–United States–Mexico Agreement (CUSMA/USMCA) outlines rules on cross-border data flows, data localization, and digital commerce. As an official trade-policy source, it documents the governance of data as an object of international trade law. For this corpus it grounds the trade-and-data-flow dimension of digital and AI governance.
