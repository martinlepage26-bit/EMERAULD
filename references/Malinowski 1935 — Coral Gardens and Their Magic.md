# Coral Gardens and Their Magic

## Citation
Malinowski, Bronislaw. Coral Gardens and Their Magic. London: Allen & Unwin, 1935.

## Annotation
Bronisław Malinowski's Coral Gardens and Their Magic is a detailed ethnography of Trobriand gardening that integrates economics, ritual, and the language of magic, exemplifying his functionalist method. It is a landmark in the anthropology of magic and material life. (This entry duplicates the same work in a different citation form.)
