# Burden of living with HIV among men who have sex with men: a mixed-methods study

## Citation
van Bilsen, W., Zimmermann, H., Boyd, A., Davidovich, U. (2020). Burden of living with HIV among men who have sex with men: a mixed-methods study. The Lancet HIV, 7(12), e835-e843. https://doi.org/10.1016/S2352-3018(20)30197-1

## Annotation
van Bilsen and colleagues use a mixed-methods study to examine the “burden” of living with HIV among men who have sex with men, addressing psychosocial as well as clinical dimensions. Published in The Lancet HIV, it contributes to research on HIV and quality of life. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized health-research source.
