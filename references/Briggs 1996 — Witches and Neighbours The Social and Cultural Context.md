# Witches and Neighbours: The Social and Cultural Context of European Witchcraft

## Citation
Briggs, R., 1996. Witches and Neighbours: The Social and Cultural Context of European Witchcraft. London: Penguin.

## Annotation
Robin Briggs's Witches and Neighbours reconstructs the social and cultural context of European witchcraft, arguing that accusations arose primarily from the strains of village life and interpersonal conflict rather than from centralized campaigns. It foregrounds the communal and psychological dimensions of witch beliefs. It is a leading work in the history of witchcraft. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation form.)
