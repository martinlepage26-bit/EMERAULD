# The Social Psychology of Organizing

## Citation
Weick, Karl E. The Social Psychology of Organizing. 2nd ed. Reading, MA: Addison-Wesley, 1979.

## Annotation
Karl Weick's The Social Psychology of Organizing reframes organization as an ongoing process of “organizing,” introducing influential concepts such as enactment, sensemaking, and loose coupling, and emphasizing how people make sense of ambiguous environments retrospectively. It is a foundational text in organizational theory. For this corpus it grounds how organizations enact and interpret their worlds — relevant to how institutions make sense of and adopt technologies like AI.
