# The Blending of Bending: World-building in Avatar: The Last Airbender and Legend of Korra (2018)

## Citation
Hoey, T. (2020). The Blending of Bending: World-building in Avatar: The Last Airbender and Legend of Korra (2018). figshare. https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.12652460.v1

## Annotation
Timothy Hoey's work analyzes world-building in the animated series Avatar: The Last Airbender and The Legend of Korra, examining how the “bending” system and its lore construct a coherent fictional world. Shared via figshare, it contributes to media and fan studies of animation. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds analysis of world-building in popular animation.
