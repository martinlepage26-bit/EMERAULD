# Importance of sample size on the quality and utility of AI-based prediction models for healthcare

## Citation
Riley, Richard D, Ensor, Joie, Snell, Kym I E, Archer, Lucinda, Whittle, Rebecca, Dhiman, Paula, Alderman, Joseph, Liu, Xiaoxuan, Kirton, Laura, Manson-Whitton, Jay, van Smeden, Maarten, Moons, Karel G, Nirantharakumar, Krishnarajah, Cazier, Jean-Baptiste, Denniston, Alastair K, Van Calster, Ben, Collins, Gary S (2025) Importance of sample size on the quality and utility of AI-based prediction models for healthcare. The Lancet. Digital health, 7(6), 100857. doi: 10.1016/j.landig.2025.01.013

## Annotation
Riley and colleagues examine the importance of sample size for the quality and utility of AI-based prediction models in healthcare, warning that inadequate data undermines model reliability and generalizability. Published in The Lancet Digital Health, it contributes methodological rigor to clinical-AI development. For this corpus it grounds evidentiary standards for trustworthy medical AI.
