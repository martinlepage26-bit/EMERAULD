# Pistes de réflexion pour un encadrement de l’intelligence artificielle

## Citation

## Annotation
This document from Québec's Conseil interprofessionnel offers avenues of reflection for regulating artificial intelligence, particularly as it affects the professional orders and regulated professions. As a stakeholder-consultation source, it contributes a professional-regulation perspective to the Québec AI-governance debate. For this corpus it grounds AI regulation in the context of professional practice and oversight.
