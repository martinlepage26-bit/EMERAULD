## Citation

## Annotation
This Toronto Star article by Nora Underwood gathers four experts' views on what it will take to develop responsible AI, surveying perspectives on ethics, governance, and the shaping of technology toward desirable ends. As a news primary source, it documents public and expert discourse on responsible AI in Canada. For this corpus it grounds the popular framing of the responsible-AI project.
