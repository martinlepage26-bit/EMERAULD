# The insiderloutsider problem in the study of religion

## Citation
McCutcheon, R. T. (dir.) (1999). The insiderloutsider problem in the study of religion.

## Annotation
Edited by Russell McCutcheon, The Insider/Outsider Problem in the Study of Religion is a widely used reader gathering key texts on the methodological debate over whether religion is best understood from within (the believer's view) or from without (the analyst's), and what is at stake in each stance. It is a standard teaching resource. For this corpus it grounds the insider/outsider problem central to studying religion and ritual.
