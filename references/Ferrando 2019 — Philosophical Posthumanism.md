# Philosophical Posthumanism

## Citation
Ferrando, Francesca (2019) Philosophical Posthumanism. London: Bloomsbury Academic.

## Annotation
Francesca Ferrando's Philosophical Posthumanism offers a systematic mapping and articulation of posthumanist philosophy, distinguishing posthumanism from transhumanism and antihumanism and situating it within a genealogy of critiques of the human. Ferrando develops a “philosophical posthumanism” that is post-humanist, post-anthropocentric, and post-dualist. It has become a standard reference and teaching text in the field. For this corpus it grounds the conceptual architecture of posthumanist thought.
