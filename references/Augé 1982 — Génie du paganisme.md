# Génie du paganisme

## Citation
Augé, M. (1982). Génie du paganisme. Paris: Gallimard.

## Annotation
Marc Augé's Génie du paganisme offers an anthropological reflection on paganism not as a defunct religion but as a mode of thought — immanent, plural, and attentive to signs and forces in the world. Augé contrasts pagan logics with monotheistic and modern frameworks, using them to illuminate contemporary symbolic practice. The book is a significant work in the French anthropology of religion. For studies of ritual and contemporary Paganism, it provides a theoretical vocabulary for thinking paganism as a living orientation rather than a survival.
