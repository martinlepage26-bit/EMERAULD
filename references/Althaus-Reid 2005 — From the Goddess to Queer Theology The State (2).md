# From the Goddess to Queer Theology: The State we are in now

## Citation
Althaus-Reid, M. (2005). From the Goddess to Queer Theology: The State we are in now. Feminist Theology, 13(2), 265-272. https://doi.org/10.1177/0966735005051952

## Annotation
Published in Feminist Theology, this article by Marcella Althaus-Reid assesses “the state we are in” as feminist theology confronts queer critique, tracing a shift from Goddess-centered feminist spirituality toward an explicitly queer theology. Drawing on her indecent-theology method, she exposes the normative assumptions that persist even in progressive religious thought. The essay is representative of Althaus-Reid's influential intervention at the intersection of liberation theology, feminism, and queer theory. For studies of religion, gender, and sexuality it is a key text. (This entry duplicates the shorter citation of the same article.)
