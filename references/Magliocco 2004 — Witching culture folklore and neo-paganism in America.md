# Witching culture :folklore and neo-paganism in America

## Citation
Magliocco, S. (2004). Witching culture :folklore and neo-paganism in America.

## Annotation
Sabina Magliocco's Witching Culture examines American Neo-Paganism through folklore studies, analyzing ritual, ecstatic experience, and the creative reinvention of tradition among practitioners. It is a standard reference in the anthropology and folkloristics of modern Paganism. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation form.)
