# Au fondement des sociétés humaines

## Citation
Godelier, M. (2007). Au fondement des sociétés humaines. Paris: Albin Michel.

## Annotation
Maurice Godelier's Au fondement des sociétés humaines investigates what lies at the foundation of human societies, arguing (against reductive accounts) that the sacred, kinship, and the domain of what is kept out of exchange are essential to social life. Godelier synthesizes decades of anthropological theory. It is a significant work in economic and political anthropology. For this corpus it grounds reflection on the sacred and the non-exchangeable in the constitution of society.
