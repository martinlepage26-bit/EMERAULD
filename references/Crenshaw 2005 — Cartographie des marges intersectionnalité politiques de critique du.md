# Cartographie des marges : intersectionnalité, politiques de critique du féminisme

## Citation
Crenshaw, K. (2005). Cartographie des marges : intersectionnalité, politiques de critique du féminisme. Paris :Presses Universitaires de France.

## Annotation
This French translation of Kimberlé Crenshaw's “Mapping the Margins” presents her foundational theory of intersectionality, distinguishing structural intersectionality — how the position of women of color at the intersection of race and gender qualitatively shapes their experience of domestic violence and rape — from political intersectionality, which concerns how single-axis agendas marginalize them. It is the canonical source for intersectionality as an analytic. For this corpus it grounds the argument that single-axis fairness cannot address multiply determined harm — directly relevant to AI bias.
