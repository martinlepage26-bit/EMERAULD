# Chapter 6 Ostracism

## Citation
Williams, K. (2009). Chapter 6 Ostracism. Advances in Experimental Social Psychology, 275-314. https://doi.org/10.1016/S0065-2601(08)00406-1

## Annotation
Kipling Williams's review chapter “Ostracism” synthesizes his influential research program on social exclusion, including the temporal need-threat model of how being ignored and excluded threatens fundamental needs (belonging, self-esteem, control, meaningful existence). Published in Advances in Experimental Social Psychology, it is an authoritative overview. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized social-psychology reference.
