# M

## Citation
Luhrmann, T. M. Persuasions of the Witch’s Craft: Ritual Magic in Contemporary England. Cambridge: Harvard University Press, 1989.

## Annotation
T. M. Luhrmann's Persuasions of the Witch's Craft is a landmark anthropological study of ritual magicians and witches in contemporary England, developing the influential concept of “interpretive drift” — the slow shift in how practitioners perceive and reason as they become absorbed in magical practice. Luhrmann analyzes how otherwise ordinary, educated people come to find magic plausible. It is a classic in the anthropology of religion and belief. For this corpus it grounds a rigorous account of how belief in magic is cultivated.
