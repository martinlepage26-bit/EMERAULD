# Unbearable Weight: Feminism, Western Culture, and the Body

## Citation
Bordo, Susan. Unbearable Weight: Feminism, Western Culture, and the Body. U of California P, 1993.

## Annotation
Susan Bordo's Unbearable Weight is a landmark feminist study of the body in Western culture, analyzing how cultural norms — especially around slenderness, eating disorders, and femininity — inscribe power on women's bodies. Drawing on Foucault, feminism, and cultural criticism, Bordo reads anorexia and the disciplined body as condensations of broader gender ideology. The book is widely cited in feminist theory and body studies. For this corpus it grounds an account of embodiment, gender, and cultural discipline.
