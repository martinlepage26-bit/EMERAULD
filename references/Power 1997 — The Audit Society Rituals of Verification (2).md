# The Audit Society: Rituals of Verification

## Citation
Power, Michael. The Audit Society: Rituals of Verification. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1997.

## Annotation
Michael Power's The Audit Society analyzes the spread of auditing across society as a “ritual of verification,” showing how the demand for accountability generates auditing practices that can be performative and self-referential rather than genuinely assuring quality. It is a key reference on accountability and its rituals. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation form.)
