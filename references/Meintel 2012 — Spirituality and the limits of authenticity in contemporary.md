# Spirituality and the limits of authenticity in contemporary religious life

## Citation
Meintel, D., 2012. Spirituality and the limits of authenticity in contemporary religious life. Culture and Religion, 13(3), pp.257–271.

## Annotation
Deirdre Meintel's “Spirituality and the limits of authenticity in contemporary religious life” examines how notions of authenticity operate — and reach their limits — in contemporary spirituality, drawing on the sociology of lived religion. Published in Culture and Religion, it contributes to debates on authenticity and individualized belief. For this corpus it grounds the analysis of authenticity in contemporary spiritual practice.
