# Algorithmic impact assessments under the GDPR: producing multi-layered explanations

## Citation
Kaminski, M., Malgieri, G. (2020). Algorithmic impact assessments under the GDPR: producing multi-layered explanations. International Data Privacy Law, 11(2), 125-144. https://doi.org/10.1093/idpl/ipaa020

## Annotation
Kaminski and Malgieri's article proposes that GDPR-mandated impact assessments can generate layered explanations of algorithmic decision-making, bridging individual rights to explanation and systemic oversight. It is a key reference in AI-governance and data-protection scholarship. (This entry duplicates the same article in a different citation/year form.)
