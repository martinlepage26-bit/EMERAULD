# Readdressing Spiritual Growth: What Can We Learn From Childhood Education?

## Citation
Polemikou, A., Da Silva, J. (2020). Readdressing Spiritual Growth: What Can We Learn From Childhood Education?. Journal of Humanistic Psychology, 62(3), 334-351. https://doi.org/10.1177/0022167820938612

## Annotation
Polemikou and Da Silva reconsider the concept of spiritual growth by drawing lessons from childhood education, exploring how developmental and educational insights inform understandings of spirituality. Published in the Journal of Humanistic Psychology, it contributes to humanistic and transpersonal psychology. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized source on spirituality and development.
