# Where the Girls Are : Growing Up Female with the Mass Media

## Citation
Douglas, Susan. Where the Girls Are : Growing Up Female with the Mass Media. Crown, 1995.

## Annotation
Susan Douglas's Where the Girls Are examines how mass media shaped the experience of growing up female in postwar America, tracing the contradictory messages about femininity, feminism, and desire delivered by television, music, and advertising. Douglas combines memoir, criticism, and media history. It is a lively reference in feminist media studies. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds analysis of gender, generation, and mass media.
