# Chemsex cultures: Subcultural reproduction and queer survival

## Citation
Florêncio, J. (2021). Chemsex cultures: Subcultural reproduction and queer survival. Sexualities, 26(5-6), 556-573. https://doi.org/10.1177/1363460720986922

## Annotation
João Florêncio's article examines chemsex cultures as sites of subcultural reproduction and “queer survival,” situating the contemporary practice of sexualized drug use within a longer history of gay men's drug and sexual cultures and the conditions (antiretrovirals, dating apps, gentrification) that mediated its rise. Published in Sexualities, it offers a critical-cultural rather than purely epidemiological reading. For work on queer sexuality in this corpus, it grounds chemsex as subculture and survival.
