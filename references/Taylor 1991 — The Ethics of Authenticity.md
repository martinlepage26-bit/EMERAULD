# The Ethics of Authenticity

## Citation
Taylor, Charles. 1991. The Ethics of Authenticity. Cambridge: Harvard University.

## Annotation
Charles Taylor's The Ethics of Authenticity examines the promise and the pitfalls of the modern ideal of self-authenticity, defending it as a moral ideal that must be recovered from its degraded, individualistic forms through connection to shared horizons of meaning. It is a foundational text in the philosophy of modernity. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation form.)
