# Dilemmas in a general theory of planning

## Citation
Rittel, H., Webber, M. (1973). Dilemmas in a general theory of planning. Policy Sciences, 4(2), 155-169. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01405730

## Annotation
Rittel and Webber's “Dilemmas in a General Theory of Planning” is a foundational article introducing the concept of “wicked problems” — problems that are ill-defined, contested, and lack definitive solutions, in contrast to the “tame” problems of science and engineering. It reshaped planning, policy, and design theory. For this corpus it is highly relevant: AI governance is frequently characterized as a wicked problem, and this is the origin of that framing.
