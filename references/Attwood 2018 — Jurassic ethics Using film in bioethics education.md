# Jurassic ethics: Using film in bioethics education

## Citation
Attwood, A. (2018). Jurassic ethics: Using film in bioethics education. Medical Humanities, 44(1), 36–40. https://doi.org/10.1136/medhum-2017-011255

## Annotation
Alison Attwood's article argues for using popular film — exemplified by Jurassic Park — as a pedagogical device in bioethics education. Cinematic scenarios, she suggests, make abstract ethical questions about biotechnology, risk, and hubris vivid and discussable for students. The piece contributes to the medical-humanities literature on narrative and film in ethics teaching. Its relevance here is modest but genuine, illustrating how cultural texts can carry ethical reasoning about science and technology.
