# Factors Associated with General Sexual Functioning and Sexual Satisfaction among People Living with HIV: A Systematic Review

## Citation
Huntingdon, B., Muscat, D., de Wit, J., Duracinsky, M., et al. (2019). Factors Associated with General Sexual Functioning and Sexual Satisfaction among People Living with HIV: A Systematic Review. The Journal of Sex Research, 57(7), 824-835. https://doi.org/10.1080/00224499.2019.1689379

## Annotation
Huntingdon and colleagues conduct a systematic review of factors associated with general sexual functioning and sexual satisfaction among people living with HIV. Published in The Journal of Sex Research, it synthesizes the clinical and psychosocial literature on HIV and sexual well-being. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized health-research source.
