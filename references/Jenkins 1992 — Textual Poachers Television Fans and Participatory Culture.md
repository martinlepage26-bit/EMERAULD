# Textual Poachers: Television Fans and Participatory Culture

## Citation
Jenkins, Henry. Textual Poachers: Television Fans and Participatory Culture. Routledge, 1992.

## Annotation
Henry Jenkins's Textual Poachers is a foundational text of fan studies that reconceives television fans not as passive consumers but as active “poachers” who appropriate and rework media texts to create their own participatory culture. Drawing on de Certeau, Jenkins documents fan writing, vidding, and community. It reshaped media and cultural studies. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the study of fandom, participation, and textual appropriation.
