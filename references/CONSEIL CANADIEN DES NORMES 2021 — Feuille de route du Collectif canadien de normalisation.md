# Feuille de route du Collectif canadien de normalisation en matière de gouvernance des données

## Citation

## Annotation
This Standards Council of Canada document sets out a roadmap for a Canadian data-governance standardization collaborative, mapping needs and priorities for standards in data governance. As a standards-body publication, it is a primary source in the governance of data infrastructure. For this corpus it grounds the standardization dimension of data and AI governance in Canada.
