# The old religion

## Citation
Pearce, M. (2000). Something new, something old : « The old religion » in the

## Annotation
Megan Pearce's MA thesis “Something Old, Something New: ‘The Old Religion' in the Modern World” examines how contemporary Paganism constructs continuity with an imagined ancient “old religion,” analyzing the negotiation of tradition and modernity. It contributes local scholarship to Pagan studies. For this corpus it grounds the study of invented tradition in modern Paganism.
