# Satire TV: Politics and Comedy in the Post-Network Era

## Citation
Gray, J., Jones, J.P. and Thompson, E. (2009) Satire TV: Politics and Comedy in the Post-Network Era. New York: NYU Press.

## Annotation
Gray, Jones, and Thompson's Satire TV examines political satire television — from The Daily Show to South Park — in the post-network era, analyzing how comedy performs political critique and civic engagement. The edited volume is a key reference in media and political-communication studies. For culture- and politics-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the analysis of satire, media, and democracy.
