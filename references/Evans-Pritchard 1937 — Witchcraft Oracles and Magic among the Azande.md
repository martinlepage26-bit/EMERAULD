# Witchcraft, Oracles and Magic among the Azande

## Citation
Evans-Pritchard, E.E., 1937. Witchcraft, Oracles and Magic among the Azande. Oxford: Clarendon Press.

## Annotation
E. E. Evans-Pritchard's Witchcraft, Oracles and Magic Among the Azande demonstrates the coherence of Zande witchcraft belief as a system for interpreting misfortune, profoundly shaping anthropological debates on rationality and explanation. It remains a foundational ethnography of magic and belief. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation form.)
