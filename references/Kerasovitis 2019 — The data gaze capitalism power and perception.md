# The data gaze: capitalism, power and perception

## Citation
Kerasovitis, K. (2019). The data gaze: capitalism, power and perception. Information, Communication &amp; Society, 22(13), 2033-2036. https://doi.org/10.1080/1369118X.2019.1609544

## Annotation
This is a review (by Kerasovitis) of David Beer's The Data Gaze: Capitalism, Power and Perception, which analyzes how data analytics embodies a capitalist “gaze” that shapes perception, value, and power. As a review, it points to Beer's argument that data infrastructures carry political economy. For this corpus it registers critical scholarship on data, capitalism, and the power of the analytic gaze — relevant to AI and datafication.
