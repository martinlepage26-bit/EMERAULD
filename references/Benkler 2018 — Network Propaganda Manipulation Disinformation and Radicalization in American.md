# Network Propaganda: Manipulation, Disinformation, and Radicalization in American Politics

## Citation
Benkler, Y., Faris, R. and Roberts, H. (2018) Network Propaganda: Manipulation, Disinformation, and Radicalization in American Politics. New York: Oxford University Press.

## Annotation
Benkler, Faris, and Roberts's Network Propaganda offers a data-driven analysis of the American political media ecosystem, arguing that asymmetric polarization — particularly a right-wing media sphere insulated from corrective feedback — drives the spread of manipulation, disinformation, and radicalization. Combining network analysis with case studies, the authors challenge simple accounts that blame social-media technology alone. The book is a major reference in the study of misinformation and political communication. For this corpus it grounds analysis of information disorder and its institutional dynamics.
