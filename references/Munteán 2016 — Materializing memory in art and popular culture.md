# Materializing memory in art and popular culture

## Citation
Munteán, L., Plate, L., & Smelik, A. (Eds.). (2016). Materializing memory in art and popular culture. New York: Routledge. https://doi.org/10.4324/9781315472171

## Annotation
An edited collection on how memory is made material in art and popular culture — objects, images, and media as the carriers of remembrance rather than its illustration. Bridges the material-culture and media-analysis clusters, and is the most directly usable of the material-culture acquisitions for work on ritual objects and mediated legitimacy. Peer-reviewed edited volume.
