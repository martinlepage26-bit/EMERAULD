# Epistemology of the closet

## Citation
Sedgwick, E. K. (1990). Epistemology of the closet. Berkeley : University of série( 1 ), 7-40.

## Annotation
Eve Kosofsky Sedgwick's Epistemology of the Closet is a foundational work of queer theory arguing that the homosexual/heterosexual definition is a central, structuring fault line of twentieth-century Western thought, and that “the closet” is a defining feature of gay life shaping knowledge, secrecy, and disclosure. (The canonical entry is garbled at the end.) It transformed literary and queer studies. For this corpus it grounds the epistemology of the closet and the centrality of sexual definition.
