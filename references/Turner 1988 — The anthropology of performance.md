# The anthropology of performance

## Citation
Turner, V. (1988). The anthropology of performance. New York: PAJ Publications.

## Annotation
Victor Turner's The Anthropology of Performance develops his influential theory of “social drama” and the performative dimensions of ritual and social life, extending his work on liminality and communitas toward a performance-centered anthropology. It is a foundational text linking ritual studies and performance studies. (Reconstructed from a canonical entry that had misattributed the work to J. Zipes.) For this corpus it grounds the ritual–performance connection.
