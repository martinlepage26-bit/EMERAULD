# Executive Order 14110: Safe, secure, and trustworthy development and use of artificial intelligence

## Citation
United States Executive Office of the President. (2023). Executive Order 14110: Safe, secure, and trustworthy development and use of artificial intelligence. Federal Register, 88(210), 75191-75226.

## Annotation
U.S. Executive Order 14110 (2023), “Safe, Secure, and Trustworthy Development and Use of Artificial Intelligence,” is a sweeping presidential directive setting requirements across federal agencies on AI safety, security, civil rights, privacy, competition, and innovation, including safety-testing reporting for the most powerful models. As a landmark U.S. governance instrument, it is a central primary source. For this corpus it anchors the U.S. federal executive approach to AI governance.
