# How Things Shape the Mind: A Theory of Material Engagement

## Citation
Malafouris, Lambros. 2013. How Things Shape the Mind: A Theory of Material Engagement. Cambridge, MA: MIT Press.

## Annotation
Lambros Malafouris's How Things Shape the Mind develops Material Engagement Theory, arguing that cognition is not confined to the brain but extends into and is co-constituted by material things and practices. Drawing on archaeology and cognitive science, Malafouris shows how tools and objects participate in thinking. It is a key text in cognitive archaeology and the extended-mind tradition. For this corpus it grounds material and distributed accounts of mind — relevant to human–technology entanglement and posthumanism.
