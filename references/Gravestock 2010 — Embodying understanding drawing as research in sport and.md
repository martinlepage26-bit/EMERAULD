# Embodying understanding: drawing as research in sport and exercise

## Citation
Gravestock, H. (2010). Embodying understanding: drawing as research in sport and exercise. Qualitative Research in Sport and Exercise, 2(2), 196-208. https://doi.org/10.1080/19398441.2010.488028

## Annotation
Helen Gravestock's article explores “drawing as research” in sport and exercise, examining how the act of drawing can embody and generate understanding in qualitative inquiry. Published in Qualitative Research in Sport and Exercise, it contributes to arts-based and embodied research methods. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds drawing as a mode of knowing.
