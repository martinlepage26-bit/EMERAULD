# Sociologie et anthropologie

## Citation
Mauss, M., 1968. Sociologie et anthropologie. Paris: PUF.

## Annotation
Marcel Mauss's Sociologie et anthropologie collects his most influential essays — including “Essai sur le don” (The Gift) and “Les techniques du corps” — foundational for the anthropology of exchange, reciprocity, and the body. It is a cornerstone of French social science. For this corpus it grounds Maussian theories of gift, obligation, and embodied technique.
