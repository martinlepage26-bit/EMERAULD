# The spirit within : gay male culture as a spiritual venue

## Citation
Boisvert, D. L. (2004). The spirit within : gay male culture as a spiritual venue. Dans

## Annotation
Donald Boisvert's essay “The Spirit Within: Gay Male Culture as a Spiritual Venue,” in the collection Gay Religion, argues that gay male culture itself — its rituals, desires, and communal spaces — can function as a site of spirituality. Boisvert reads embodiment and eroticism as potential vehicles of the sacred rather than its opposite. It is a notable contribution to the study of queer religion and spirituality. For this corpus it links gay male culture to spiritual meaning-making.
