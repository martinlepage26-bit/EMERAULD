# Féminin-masculin: mythes et idéologie

## Citation
Vidal, C. (2006). Féminin-masculin: mythes et idéologie. Paris: Belin.

## Annotation
Catherine Vidal's Féminin-masculin: mythes et idéologie critically examines claims about innate sex differences in the brain, arguing that much “neurosexism” rests on ideology rather than solid science and emphasizing brain plasticity. As a neuroscientist's intervention, it contributes to feminist critiques of biological determinism. For this corpus it grounds the critique of gendered biological essentialism.
