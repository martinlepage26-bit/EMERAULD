# Guide de l'enquête de terrain :produire et analyser

## Citation
Beaud, S. et Weber, F. (2003). Guide de l'enquête de terrain :produire et analyser

## Annotation
Beaud and Weber's Guide de l'enquête de terrain is a widely used French manual for conducting and analyzing ethnographic fieldwork, covering observation, interviewing, note-taking, and the interpretation of qualitative data. Practical and rigorous, it has become a standard reference for training francophone social scientists in field method. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the craft of empirical fieldwork.
