# Fiction into religion: imagination, other worlds, and play in the formation of community

## Citation
Cusack, C. (2016). Fiction into religion: imagination, other worlds, and play in the formation of community. Religion, 46(4), 575-590. https://doi.org/10.1080/0048721X.2016.1210390

## Annotation
Carole Cusack's article examines how fiction becomes religion — how imaginative works, other worlds, and play contribute to the formation of religious community, drawing on “invented” or fiction-based religions. Published in Religion, it extends her influential work on new and invented religious movements. For this corpus it grounds the study of imagination, narrative, and play in the making of religious community.
