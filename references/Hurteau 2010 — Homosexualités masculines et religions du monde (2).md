# Homosexualités masculines et religions du monde

## Citation
Hurteau, Pierre. Homosexualités masculines et religions du monde. Paris: L’Harmattan, 2010.

## Annotation
Pierre Hurteau's Homosexualités masculines et religions du monde offers a comparative study of male homosexuality across religious traditions, examining theological and cultural responses to same-sex desire. It is a francophone contribution to religion-and-sexuality scholarship. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation form.)
