# The Authority of Law: Essays on Law and Morality

## Citation
Raz, Joseph. The Authority of Law: Essays on Law and Morality. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1979.

## Annotation
Joseph Raz's The Authority of Law is a foundational work of analytic legal and political philosophy, developing his “service conception” of authority and the idea that authoritative directives provide “exclusionary reasons” that pre-empt one's own deliberation. Raz also elaborates the rule of law as a set of formal virtues. For this corpus it grounds a rigorous account of legal authority and legitimacy — relevant to how governance directives claim to bind.
