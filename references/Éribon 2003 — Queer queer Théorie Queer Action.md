## Annotation
Didier Éribon's dictionary entry distinguishes three senses of “queer” — as an epithet, as theory, and as activist practice — clarifying the term's genealogy and stakes for a francophone readership. Drawn from his Dictionnaire des cultures gays et lesbiennes, it is a lucid conceptual reference. For this corpus it grounds the meanings and history of “queer” across theory and politics.
