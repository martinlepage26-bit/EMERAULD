# La mouvance wiccanne au Québec : un portrait de la sorcellerie

## Citation
Gagnon, M. (2003). La mouvance wiccanne au Québec : un portrait de la sorcellerie

## Annotation
Myriam Gagnon's MA thesis (Université Laval) offers a portrait of the Wiccan movement and contemporary witchcraft in Québec, documenting practitioners, beliefs, and practices in a francophone North American context. It contributes local ethnographic depth to Pagan studies. For this corpus it grounds the study of Wicca and contemporary witchcraft in Québec.
