# Things I do are manifestations of love

## Citation
Lepage, M. (2020). “Things I do are manifestations of love”. Secular Societies, Spiritual Selves?, 216-232. https://doi.org/10.4324/9780429456923-11

## Annotation
Martin Lepage's “‘Things I Do Are Manifestations of Love'” (a chapter in Secular Societies, Spiritual Selves?) examines queer religiosity and secular spirituality, likely among Montreal Pagans, exploring how practitioners frame their practice through love and spiritual selfhood in a secular context. It contributes to the study of lived spirituality and sexuality. For this corpus it grounds the intersection of secular spirituality and queer self-understanding.
