# Individualism and Community in Alternative Spiritual ‘Magic’.

## Citation
Bloch, Jon. “Individualism and Community in Alternative Spiritual ‘Magic’.” Journal for the Scientific Study of Religion 37, no. 2 (1998): 286–302.

## Annotation
Jon Bloch's article on individualism and community in alternative spiritual “magic” examines the tension between personal, self-directed spirituality and the desire for community among practitioners of alternative magical and spiritual paths. Published in the Journal for the Scientific Study of Religion, it contributes to the sociology of alternative religion. For this corpus it illuminates how contemporary magical practice negotiates self and collective.
