# AI anxiety: A comprehensive analysis of psychological factors and interventions

## Citation
Kim, J. J. H., Soh, J. Y., Kadkol, S., Solomon, I., Yeh, H., Srivatsaa, A. V., Nahas, G., Choi, J. Y., Lee, S., & Ajilore, O. (2025). AI anxiety: A comprehensive analysis of psychological factors and interventions. AI and Ethics, 5(4), 3993-4009.

## Annotation
Kim and colleagues offer a comprehensive analysis of AI anxiety, treating it as a multidimensional condition encompassing job loss, privacy, misinformation, bias, and loss of control, and reviewing psychological factors and interventions. Published in AI and Ethics, it connects individual psychological response to wider institutional and technological conditions. For this corpus it supports the claim that AI anxiety functions as a governance signal rather than mere individual pathology.
