# L’éthique de l’intelligence artificielle attend le droit

## Citation
MACLURE, Jocelyn, et MORIN-MARTEL, Alexis (2023). L’éthique de l’intelligence artificielle attend le droit. Options politiques, 27 septembre. En ligne: https://policyoptions.irpp.org/fr/magazines/september-2023/lethique-de-lintelligence-artificielle-attend-le-droit/.

## Annotation
This Options politiques piece by Jocelyn Maclure and Alexis Morin-Martel argues that the ethics of artificial intelligence “awaits the law” — that ethical principles must be translated into binding legal frameworks to be effective. As expert commentary by a prominent philosopher and co-author, it contributes to the francophone AI-governance debate. For this corpus it grounds the argument that AI ethics requires legal enforcement, not just principles.
