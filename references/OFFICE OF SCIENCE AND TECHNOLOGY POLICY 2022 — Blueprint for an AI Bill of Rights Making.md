# Blueprint for an AI Bill of Rights – Making Automated Systems Work For The American People

## Citation
OFFICE OF SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY POLICY (2022). Blueprint for an AI Bill of Rights – Making Automated Systems Work For The American People. En ligne: https://www.whitehouse.gov/ostp/ai-bill-of-rights/.

## Annotation
The White House Office of Science and Technology Policy's Blueprint for an AI Bill of Rights sets out five principles to guide the design and use of automated systems — safe and effective systems, protection from algorithmic discrimination, data privacy, notice and explanation, and human alternatives/fallback. Though non-binding, it is a landmark U.S. rights-framing of AI governance. For this corpus it is a primary source articulating a rights-based approach to automated systems.
