# When guardians become offenders: Understanding guardian capability through the lens of corporate crime*

## Citation
Chan, F., Gibbs, C. (2022). When guardians become offenders: Understanding guardian capability through the lens of corporate crime*. Criminology, 60(2), 321-341. https://doi.org/10.1111/1745-9125.12300

## Annotation
Chan and Gibbs extend routine-activities theory to corporate crime, using qualitative data to examine how “capable guardianship” fails when those tasked with guardianship become offenders themselves — the guardian–offender overlap. Published in Criminology, it advances theory on guardianship and corporate financial fraud. Its relevance to this corpus is more peripheral, but it offers a useful lens on accountability failure and the capture of oversight roles.
