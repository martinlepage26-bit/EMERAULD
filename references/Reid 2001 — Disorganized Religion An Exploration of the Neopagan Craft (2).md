# Disorganized Religion: An Exploration of the Neopagan Craft in Canada.

## Citation
Reid, Susan L. “Disorganized Religion: An Exploration of the Neopagan Craft in Canada.” MA thesis, Carleton University, 2001.

## Annotation
Susan Reid's thesis examines Neopagan Witchcraft in Canada as a “disorganized religion,” analyzing its decentralized organization and practitioner experience. It is a francophone/Canadian contribution to Pagan studies. (This entry duplicates the same thesis in a different citation form.)
