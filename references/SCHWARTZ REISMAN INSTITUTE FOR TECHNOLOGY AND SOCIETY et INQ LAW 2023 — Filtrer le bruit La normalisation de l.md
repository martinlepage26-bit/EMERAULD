## Annotation
This report by the Schwartz Reisman Institute and INQ Law, prepared for the Standards Council of Canada (“Filtering the Noise: The Global Normalization of AI and Its Implications for Canada”), analyzes global AI standardization efforts and their consequences for Canadian policy. As a commissioned policy study, it is a primary source on AI standards governance. For this corpus it grounds the standardization dimension of AI governance from a Canadian vantage.
