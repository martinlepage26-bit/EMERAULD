# sexe

## Citation
Baril, A. (2007). De la construction du genre à la construction du « sexe » : les thèses

## Annotation
Alexandre Baril's article examines Judith Butler's theses on the move “from the construction of gender to the construction of sex,” situating Butler as a central figure of postmodern feminism. Baril reconstructs Butler's deconstruction of the naturalism and foundationalism underlying the categories of sex and gender, and her constructivist case for recognizing plural genders and sexualities. Noting that Butler had been little analyzed in Québec, the article introduces her work to a francophone readership in Recherches féministes. For this corpus it is a lucid French-language entry point to Butlerian gender theory.
