# Her Hidden Children: The Rise of Wicca and Paganism in America

## Citation
Clifton, C., 2006. Her Hidden Children: The Rise of Wicca and Paganism in America. Lanham, MD: AltaMira Press.

## Annotation
Chas Clifton's Her Hidden Children traces the rise of Wicca and contemporary Paganism in America, combining insider knowledge with historical scholarship to chart the movement's development, key figures, and relationship to nature religion. It is a standard reference in Pagan studies. For this corpus it grounds the American history of Wicca and modern Paganism.
