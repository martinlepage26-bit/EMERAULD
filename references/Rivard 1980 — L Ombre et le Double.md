# L’Ombre et le Double

## Citation
Rivard, Yvon. 1980. L’Ombre et le Double. Montréal: Éditions Quinze.

## Annotation
Yvon Rivard's L'Ombre et le Double is a work by the Québécois novelist and essayist, part of his literary exploration of interiority, doubling, and the self. As a work of Québec literature, it belongs to the francophone literary corpus. For this corpus it is a primary literary source in Québec letters.
