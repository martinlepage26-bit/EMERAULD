# The Pagan Religions of the Ancient British Isles: Their Nature and Legacy

## Citation
Hutton, R., 1996. The Pagan Religions of the Ancient British Isles: Their Nature and Legacy. Oxford: Blackwell.

## Annotation
Ronald Hutton's The Pagan Religions of the Ancient British Isles is an authoritative historical survey of pre-Christian religion in Britain and Ireland, carefully distinguishing what can be known from later romantic reconstructions. It is a standard reference on ancient British paganism and its legacy. For this corpus it grounds the historical record against which modern Pagan claims of continuity can be assessed.
