# A community of witches : contemporary neopaganism and

## Citation
Berger, H. A. (1998). A community of witches : contemporary neopaganism and

## Annotation
Helen Berger's A Community of Witches is an ethnographic study of contemporary neopaganism and Witchcraft in the United States, examining covens, ritual practice, and the movement's negotiation of family, community, and modernity. Berger analyzes how practitioners sustain religious community and identity within a secular society. The book is a standard reference in Pagan studies. For this corpus it grounds the sociological study of contemporary Witchcraft in the U.S.
