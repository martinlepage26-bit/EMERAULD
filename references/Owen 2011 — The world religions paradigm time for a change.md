# The world religions paradigm : time for a change

## Citation
Owen, S. (2011). The world religions paradigm : time for a change. Arts and Oxford

## Annotation
Suzanne Owen's “The World Religions Paradigm: Time for a Change” argues that the dominant framework for teaching religion — the “world religions paradigm” — distorts non-Christian traditions by remodeling them along liberal Western Christian lines and widens the gap between research and teaching. She calls for a critical approach treating “religion” as a constructed category. Published in an arts-and-humanities education venue, it is a key intervention in the theory of religious studies. For this corpus it grounds critical reflection on the category of religion.
