# Telemedicine and remote management of patients with heart failure

## Citation
Anker, S., Koehler, F., Abraham, W. (2011). Telemedicine and remote management of patients with heart failure. The Lancet, 378(9792), 731-739. https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(11)61229-4

## Annotation
This clinical review in The Lancet, led by Stefan Anker, examines telemedicine and remote management for patients with heart failure, weighing the evidence on whether remote monitoring improves outcomes. It surveys device-based and structured-telephone approaches and notes mixed trial results regarding mortality and hospitalization. The article belongs to the cardiology and digital-health literatures. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized medical source on remote patient management.
