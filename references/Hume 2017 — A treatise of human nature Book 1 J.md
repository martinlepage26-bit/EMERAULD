# A treatise of human nature, Book 1 (J

## Citation
Hume, D. (2017). A treatise of human nature, Book 1 (J. Bennett, Ed.). Early Modern Texts. (Original work published 1739)

## Annotation
Book 1 of Hume's Treatise, containing the bundle theory of the self — the claim that there is no persisting subject behind experience, only a succession of perceptions. Together with Locke it forms the pair the corpus needs whenever identity, continuity, or the coherence of an agent 'self' is at stake. Held as Bennett's modernised edition; cite a standard scholarly edition in published work.
