# Rapunzel’s Daughters: What Women’s Hair Tells Us About Women’s Lives

## Citation
Weitz, Rose. Rapunzel’s Daughters: What Women’s Hair Tells Us About Women’s Lives. Farrar, Straus and Giroux, 2004.

## Annotation
Rose Weitz's Rapunzel's Daughters examines what women's hair reveals about their lives, analyzing hair as a site of identity, power, conformity, and resistance across women's experiences. It contributes to gender and cultural studies of the body. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the analysis of hair, gender, and embodiment.
