# Bodily citations : religion and Judith

## Citation
Armour, E. T. et St.-Ville, S. M. (dir.) (2006). Bodily citations : religion and Judith

## Annotation
Edited by Ellen Armour and Susan St. Ville, Bodily Citations gathers scholars of religion who put Judith Butler's theory — performativity, materialization, citationality — to work on religious texts, practices, and traditions. The volume tests how far Butler's account of the discursively produced body illuminates ritual, scripture, and theological anthropology, and where religion pushes back on her framework. It helped establish a sustained conversation between queer/feminist theory and religious studies. For this corpus it is a key node linking Butlerian gender theory to the study of religion and ritual embodiment.
