# Religion and healing

## Citation
McGuire, M. B. (1985). Religion and healing. Dans P. E. Hammond (dir.), The sacred

## Annotation
Meredith McGuire's “Religion and Healing” examines how religious and spiritual practices address healing and well-being, situating alternative and non-medical healing within the sociology of religion in a secular age. Published in The Sacred in a Secular Age (ed. Hammond), it is a reference in the study of religion and health. For this corpus it grounds the intersection of religion, ritual, and healing.
