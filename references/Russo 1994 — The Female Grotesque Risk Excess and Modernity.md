# The Female Grotesque: Risk, Excess and Modernity

## Citation
Russo, Mary. 1994. The Female Grotesque: Risk, Excess and Modernity. New York: Routledge.

## Annotation
Mary Russo's The Female Grotesque is an influential feminist study of the grotesque body — its risk, excess, and spectacle — and its relation to modernity, femininity, and transgression, drawing on Bakhtin, the carnivalesque, and figures like the aerialist and the freak. It is a key text in feminist cultural theory. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds analysis of the transgressive female body and spectacle.
