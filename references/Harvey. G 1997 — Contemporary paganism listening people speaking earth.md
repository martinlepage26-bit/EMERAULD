# Contemporary paganism : listening people, speaking earth

## Citation
Harvey. G. (1997). Contemporary paganism : listening people, speaking earth. New

## Annotation
Graham Harvey's Contemporary Paganism: Listening People, Speaking Earth is a foundational study of modern Paganism that emphasizes its animist, nature-centered, and relational character, taking practitioners' beliefs and practices seriously on their own terms. Harvey later developed influential work on the “new animism.” It is a standard reference in Pagan studies. For this corpus it grounds an understanding of contemporary Paganism as relational and earth-centered.
