# Paganism and the ecological crisis

## Citation
White, H., 2002. Paganism and the ecological crisis. Journal for the Study of Religion, Nature and Culture, 6(1), pp.85–101.

## Annotation
Hannah White's “Paganism and the Ecological Crisis” examines how contemporary Paganism engages environmental concern, analyzing the movement's nature-centered spirituality in relation to ecological crisis. Published in the Journal for the Study of Religion, Nature and Culture, it contributes to eco-spirituality scholarship. For this corpus it grounds the environmental dimension of contemporary Paganism.
