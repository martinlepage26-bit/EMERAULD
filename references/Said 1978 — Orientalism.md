# Orientalism

## Citation
Said, E.W. (1978) Orientalism. New York: Pantheon Books.

## Annotation
Edward Said's Orientalism is a foundational work of postcolonial theory, arguing that Western scholarship, art, and administration produced “the Orient” as an object of knowledge and power — a discourse that constructed the East as the West's inferior, exotic Other and legitimated domination. It transformed the humanities and social sciences. For this corpus it grounds a canonical account of how representation, knowledge, and power co-produce the objects they claim merely to describe — relevant to how AI and data classify populations.
