# The Queer Art of Failure

## Citation
Halberstam, J. (2011) The Queer Art of Failure. Durham, NC: Duke University Press.

## Annotation
Jack Halberstam's The Queer Art of Failure reclaims failure as a resource for queer critique, using low theory and popular culture to challenge the disciplinary demands of success, mastery, and heteronormative achievement. It is widely cited across queer and cultural theory. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation form.)
