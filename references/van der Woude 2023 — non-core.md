# non-core

## Citation
van der Woude, M. (2023). Securitizing mobility: Profiling ‘non-core’ Europeans. Security Dialogue, 54(6), 620-638. https://doi.org/10.1177/09670106221125041

## Annotation
Maartje van der Woude's “Securitizing Mobility” analyzes how the profiling of “non-core” Europeans at internal borders securitizes mobility, showing how ostensibly free movement is differentially policed. Published in Security Dialogue, it contributes to critical security and migration studies. For this corpus it grounds analysis of profiling, securitization, and discriminatory mobility control.
