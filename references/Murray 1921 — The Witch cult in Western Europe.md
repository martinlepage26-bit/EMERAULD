# The Witch cult in Western Europe

## Citation
Murray, M. (1921). The Witch cult in Western Europe. Oxford : Oxford University

## Annotation
Margaret Murray's The Witch-Cult in Western Europe advanced the influential but now-discredited thesis that the early-modern witch trials targeted a surviving organized pre-Christian fertility religion. Though rejected by historians, Murray's theory profoundly shaped modern Wicca and popular understandings of witchcraft. For this corpus it is a key (and contested) source in the genealogy of the modern witch-cult myth.
