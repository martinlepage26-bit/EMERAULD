# Staying with the Trouble: Making Kin in the Chthulucene

## Citation
Haraway, D.J. (2016) Staying with the Trouble: Making Kin in the Chthulucene. Durham, NC: Duke University Press.

## Annotation
Donna Haraway's Staying with the Trouble is a major work of the environmental humanities, urging us to “make kin” across species and to think and act within damaged worlds rather than fleeing to techno-fixes or despair. Coining the “Chthulucene,” Haraway develops multispecies, sympoietic modes of living and storytelling. It is central to posthumanism and the ecological humanities. For this corpus it grounds multispecies, more-than-human thought in the Anthropocene.
