# Erectile Dysfunction and Premature Ejaculation in Homosexual and Heterosexual Men: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Comparative Studies

## Citation
Barbonetti, A., D’Andrea, S., Cavallo, F., Martorella, A., et al. (2019). Erectile Dysfunction and Premature Ejaculation in Homosexual and Heterosexual Men: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Comparative Studies. The Journal of Sexual Medicine, 16(5), 624-632. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jsxm.2019.02.014

## Annotation
Barbonetti and colleagues conduct a systematic review and meta-analysis of comparative studies on erectile dysfunction and premature ejaculation in homosexual versus heterosexual men, assessing whether prevalence differs between groups. Using standardized quality appraisal, they synthesize the sparse and previously inconclusive evidence. Published in The Journal of Sexual Medicine, it belongs to the sexual-medicine literature. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized clinical meta-analysis.
