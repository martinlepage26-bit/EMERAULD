# The Aftermath of Feminism: Gender, Culture and Social Change

## Citation
McRobbie, Angela. 2009. The Aftermath of Feminism: Gender, Culture and Social Change. London: Sage.

## Annotation
Angela McRobbie's The Aftermath of Feminism is an influential analysis of postfeminist culture, arguing that feminism has been “taken into account” only to be repudiated — invoked as something already achieved and therefore no longer needed — while new forms of gender regulation emerge through media and consumer culture. It is a foundational text in feminist media and cultural studies. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the critique of postfeminism and gendered neoliberal culture.
