# The Conflict of the Faculties: Perspectives on Artistic Research and Academia

## Citation
Borgdorff, Henk. 2012. The Conflict of the Faculties: Perspectives on Artistic Research and Academia. Leiden: Leiden University Press.

## Annotation
Henk Borgdorff's The Conflict of the Faculties addresses the epistemological and institutional status of artistic research within academia, arguing that art practice can constitute a legitimate mode of knowledge production. Borgdorff examines the tensions between artistic and scientific paradigms of research. The book is a key reference in debates over practice-based research. Its relevance to this corpus is more peripheral, but it bears on questions of method, knowledge, and disciplinary authority.
