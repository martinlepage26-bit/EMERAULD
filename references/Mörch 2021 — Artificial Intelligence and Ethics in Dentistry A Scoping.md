# Artificial Intelligence and Ethics in Dentistry: A Scoping Review

## Citation
Mörch, C M, Atsu, S, Cai, W, Li, X, Madathil, S A, Liu, X, Mai, V, Tamimi, F, Dilhac, M A, Ducret, M (2021) Artificial Intelligence and Ethics in Dentistry: A Scoping Review. Journal of dental research, 100(13), 1452-1460. doi: 10.1177/00220345211013808

## Annotation
Mörch and colleagues conduct a scoping review of the uses of artificial intelligence in dentistry and the ethical concerns they raise, mapping applications and the associated ethical challenges across health-care and computer-science literatures. Published in the Journal of Dental Research, it contributes domain-specific AI-ethics analysis. For this corpus it grounds AI ethics in a concrete clinical field.
