# Actionable Recourse in Linear Classification

## Citation
Ustun, B., Spangher, A., & Liu, Y. (2018). Actionable Recourse in Linear Classification. arXiv.

## Annotation
Ustun, Spangher, and Liu's “Actionable Recourse in Linear Classification” introduces methods to evaluate and ensure “recourse” — the ability of individuals to change algorithmic decisions that affect them through feasible actions — arguing that models should not lock people out of favorable outcomes. This arXiv version is a foundational contribution to contestability and fairness. For this corpus it grounds the concept of recourse as a requirement for just algorithmic decision-making.
