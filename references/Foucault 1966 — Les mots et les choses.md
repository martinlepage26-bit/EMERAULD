# Les mots et les choses

## Citation
Foucault, M. (1966). Les mots et les choses. Paris : Gallimard.

## Annotation
Michel Foucault's Les mots et les choses (The Order of Things) is a foundational work of his “archaeological” period, analyzing the historical epistemes — the deep ordering structures of knowledge — that made possible the human sciences, and famously predicting the “death of man” as an epistemic figure. It reshaped the history and philosophy of knowledge. For this corpus it grounds Foucault's account of how knowledge is historically structured, relevant to critiques of classification and expertise.
