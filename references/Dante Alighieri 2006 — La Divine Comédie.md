# La Divine Comédie

## Citation
Dante Alighieri. 2006. La Divine Comédie. Traduit par Jacqueline Risset. Paris: GF-Flammarion.

## Annotation
Dante Alighieri's Divine Comedy (here in Jacqueline Risset's French translation) is the foundational epic of medieval Christian literature, narrating the pilgrim's journey through Hell, Purgatory, and Paradise as an allegory of the soul's ascent toward God. Its cosmology, moral architecture, and imagery have profoundly shaped Western literature and the imagination of the afterlife. For this corpus it is a primary literary source in traditions of vision, cosmic order, and symbolic journey.
