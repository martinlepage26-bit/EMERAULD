# Collaboration and Trust in Healthcare Innovation: The eDiaMoND Case Study

## Citation
JIROTKA, M., PROCTER, R., HARTSWOOD, M., SLACK, R., et al. (2005). Collaboration and Trust in Healthcare Innovation: The eDiaMoND Case Study. Computer Supported Cooperative Work (CSCW), 14(4), 369-398. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10606-005-9001-0

## Annotation
Jirotka and colleagues present the eDiaMoND case study on collaboration and trust in healthcare innovation, examining how a grid-computing breast-screening project navigated the socio-technical challenges of trust, cooperation, and adoption. Published in CSCW, it contributes to computer-supported cooperative work and the sociology of health technology. For this corpus it grounds analysis of trust and collaboration in the adoption of health IT.
