# Witches and Neighbors: The Social and Cultural Context of European Witchcraft

## Citation
Briggs, Robin. Witches and Neighbors: The Social and Cultural Context of European Witchcraft. New York: Penguin, 1996.

## Annotation
Robin Briggs's Witches and Neighbors situates European witchcraft within the fabric of local community relations, showing how neighbourly obligation, guilt, and conflict shaped who was accused and why. Briggs's emphasis on the social micro-context has been highly influential in witchcraft studies. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation/edition form.)
