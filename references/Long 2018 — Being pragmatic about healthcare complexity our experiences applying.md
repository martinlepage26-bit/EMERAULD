# Being pragmatic about healthcare complexity: our experiences applying complexity theory and pragmatism to health services research

## Citation
Long, K., McDermott, F., Meadows, G. (2018). Being pragmatic about healthcare complexity: our experiences applying complexity theory and pragmatism to health services research. BMC Medicine, 16(1). https://doi.org/10.1186/s12916-018-1087-6

## Annotation
Long, McDermott, and Meadows reflect on applying complexity theory and pragmatism to health-services research, arguing for a pragmatic engagement with the irreducible complexity of healthcare. Published in BMC Medicine, it contributes to methodological debate in health-services research. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds a pragmatic, complexity-aware approach to studying systems.
