# It's all a Giant Web

## Citation
Roberts, R. (2009). « It's all a Giant Web » : Syncretism, agency and (re)connection

## Annotation
Rachel Roberts's “‘It's All a Giant Web'” examines syncretism, agency, and (re)connection in a contemporary Pagan community, analyzing how practitioners weave together diverse traditions and forge relational meaning. It contributes ethnographic depth to Pagan studies. For this corpus it grounds syncretism and agency in contemporary Paganism.
