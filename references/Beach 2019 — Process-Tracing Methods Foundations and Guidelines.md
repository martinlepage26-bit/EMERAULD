# Process-Tracing Methods: Foundations and Guidelines

## Citation
Beach, D. and Pedersen, R.B. (2019) Process-Tracing Methods: Foundations and Guidelines. 2nd edn. Ann Arbor, MI: University of Michigan Press.

## Annotation
Beach and Pedersen's Process-Tracing Methods is a leading methodological text that systematizes process tracing as a within-case method for testing causal mechanisms. The book distinguishes theory-testing, theory-building, and explaining-outcome variants and grounds inference in Bayesian logic and evidential tests (straw-in-the-wind, hoop, smoking-gun, doubly-decisive). Widely used across political science and qualitative methodology, it is a standard reference. For method-focused work in this corpus, it anchors rigorous causal inference from single cases.
