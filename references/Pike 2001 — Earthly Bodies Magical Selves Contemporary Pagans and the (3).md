# Earthly Bodies, Magical Selves: Contemporary Pagans and the Search for Community

## Citation
Pike, Sarah M. 2001. Earthly Bodies, Magical Selves: Contemporary Pagans and the Search for Community. Berkeley: University of California Press.

## Annotation
Sarah Pike's Earthly Bodies, Magical Selves studies contemporary Pagans' search for community through ecstatic, embodied festival ritual, analyzing identity and belonging in these gatherings. It is a standard reference in Pagan studies. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation form.)
