# Cruel Optimism

## Citation
Berlant, Lauren. 2011. Cruel Optimism. Durham, NC: Duke University Press.

## Annotation
Lauren Berlant's Cruel Optimism is a major work of affect theory that names the condition in which people remain attached to objects and fantasies — the “good life,” upward mobility, durable intimacy — that actually obstruct their flourishing. Berlant analyzes how such attachments persist amid economic precarity and the fraying of postwar promises, reading film, literature, and everyday life. The book is widely cited across queer theory, cultural studies, and affect theory. For this corpus it grounds an account of attachment, precarity, and the affective structure of the present.
