# How We Became Posthuman: Virtual Bodies in Cybernetics, Literature, and Informatics

## Citation
Hayles, N. Katherine (1999) How We Became Posthuman: Virtual Bodies in Cybernetics, Literature, and Informatics. Chicago: University of Chicago Press.

## Annotation
N. Katherine Hayles's How We Became Posthuman is a foundational work of posthumanist theory and media studies, tracing the history of cybernetics and information theory to show how “information lost its body” — how the posthuman came to privilege pattern over presence and disembodied information over material embodiment. Hayles critiques this erasure and argues for an embodied posthumanism. It is a canonical reference across posthumanism, literature, and science studies. For this corpus it grounds the intellectual history of the posthuman and the politics of embodiment versus information.
