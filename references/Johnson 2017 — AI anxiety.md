# AI anxiety

## Citation
Johnson, D. G., & Verdicchio, M. (2017). AI anxiety. Journal of the Association for Information Science and Technology, 68(9), 2267-2270. https://doi.org/10.1002/asi.23867

## Annotation
Johnson and Verdicchio's “AI anxiety” is a foundational conceptual article arguing that much public fear about AI rests on misunderstanding — an exclusive focus on AI programs that leaves humans out of the picture, confusion about autonomy, and conflation of AI with science-fiction scenarios. They reframe anxiety as a sociotechnical rather than purely technical problem. Published in JASIST, it is a clear starting point for non-sensational analysis. For this corpus it is a foundational source reframing AI anxiety as a governance signal.
