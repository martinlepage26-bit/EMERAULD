# Research methods in anthropology

## Citation
Bernard, H. R. (2006). Research methods in anthropology

## Annotation
H. Russell Bernard's Research Methods in Anthropology is a comprehensive, widely used textbook covering both qualitative and quantitative approaches, from participant observation and interviewing to sampling, measurement, and analysis. It is a standard training reference for social-science fieldwork. For method-focused work in this corpus, it anchors the practical toolkit of empirical research design.
