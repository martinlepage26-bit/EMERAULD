# Fairy Tales: A New History

## Citation
Bottigheimer, R.B. (2009) Fairy Tales: A New History. Albany, NY: State University of New York Press.

## Annotation
Ruth Bottigheimer's Fairy Tales: A New History offers a revisionist account that challenges the assumption of ancient oral origins for the European fairy tale, arguing instead for the decisive role of print and identifiable authors in shaping the genre. The book provoked significant debate in folklore studies. It is a provocative reference on the history and transmission of fairy tales. For narrative- and folklore-focused work in this corpus, it reframes questions of origin and authorship.
