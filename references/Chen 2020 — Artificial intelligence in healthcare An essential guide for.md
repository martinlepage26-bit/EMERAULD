# Artificial intelligence in healthcare: An essential guide for health leaders

## Citation
Chen, Mei, Decary, Michel (2020) Artificial intelligence in healthcare: An essential guide for health leaders. Healthcare management forum, 33(1), 10-18. doi: 10.1177/0840470419873123

## Annotation
Chen and Décary offer an essential guide to artificial intelligence in healthcare aimed at health-system leaders, surveying AI's applications, benefits, risks, and governance implications for management. Published in Healthcare Management Forum, it translates technical developments into leadership and policy terms. For this corpus it grounds AI governance in the specific setting of health-system administration.
