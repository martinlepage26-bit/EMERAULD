# The Gift: Forms and Functions of Exchange in Archaic Societies

## Citation
Mauss, Marcel. The Gift: Forms and Functions of Exchange in Archaic Societies. Trans. Ian Cunnison. London: Cohen & West, 1925/1954.

## Annotation
Marcel Mauss's The Gift (Essai sur le don) is a foundational anthropological essay arguing that gift exchange in “archaic” societies is a “total social fact” governed by the threefold obligation to give, receive, and reciprocate, binding persons and groups through reciprocity rather than pure self-interest. It reshaped economic anthropology and theories of exchange. For this corpus it grounds the classic theory of the gift, reciprocity, and social obligation.
