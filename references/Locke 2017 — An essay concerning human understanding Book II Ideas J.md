# An essay concerning human understanding, Book II: Ideas (J

## Citation
Locke, J. (2017). An essay concerning human understanding, Book II: Ideas (J. Bennett, Ed.). Early Modern Texts. (Original work published 1690)

## Annotation
Book II of Locke's Essay, where the account of ideas and — critically — of personal identity as continuity of consciousness is set out. This is the origin point for the identity-persistence problem that every argument about machine continuity, memory, and selfhood eventually reaches. Held as Jonathan Bennett's modernised Early Modern Texts edition: convenient and accurate, but cite the standard scholarly edition for published work.
