# La Sorcière dans les séries télévisées américaines: une figure féminine ambiguë de la postmodernité

## Citation
Achouche, A., 2015. La Sorcière dans les séries télévisées américaines: une figure féminine ambiguë de la postmodernité. Paris: L’Harmattan.

## Annotation
Amélie Achouche's French-language monograph examines the recurring figure of the witch in American television series, reading her as an ambiguous emblem of postmodern femininity. Across shows from Bewitched to Charmed and Buffy the Vampire Slayer, the televisual witch oscillates between domestication and transgression, empowerment and containment. Achouche argues that this ambivalence stages broader anxieties about women's power, autonomy, and the boundaries of the normative feminine. The book is a useful entry in the media-studies literature on gender and the supernatural on screen. For work bridging ritual imagery, popular culture, and gender, it connects televisual witchcraft to the politics of representation.
