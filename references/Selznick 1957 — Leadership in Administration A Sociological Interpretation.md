# Leadership in Administration: A Sociological Interpretation

## Citation
Selznick, Philip. Leadership in Administration: A Sociological Interpretation. Evanston: Row, Peterson, 1957.

## Annotation
Philip Selznick's Leadership in Administration is a foundational text of institutional theory, distinguishing “organizations” (technical, expendable tools) from “institutions” (value-infused, distinctive entities) and arguing that leadership's key task is “institutionalization” — infusing an organization with values beyond the technical requirements of the task. For this corpus it grounds a classic account of how organizations acquire character and values — relevant to how governance bodies develop institutional commitments.
