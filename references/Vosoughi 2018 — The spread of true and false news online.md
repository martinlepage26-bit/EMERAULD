# The spread of true and false news online

## Citation
Vosoughi, S., Roy, D., Aral, S. (2018). The spread of true and false news online. Science, 359(6380), 1146-1151. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aap9559

## Annotation
Vosoughi, Roy, and Aral's “The Spread of True and False News Online” is a landmark study analyzing ~126,000 rumor cascades on Twitter (2006–2017), finding that false news spread significantly farther, faster, deeper, and more broadly than the truth — largely because of its novelty and the emotional reactions it provoked, and not primarily because of bots. Published in Science, it is one of the most cited empirical studies of misinformation. For this corpus it grounds the dynamics of online falsehood — highly relevant to AI-amplified information disorder.
