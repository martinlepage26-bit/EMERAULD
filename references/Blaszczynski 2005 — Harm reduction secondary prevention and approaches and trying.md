# Harm reduction, secondary prevention and approaches, and trying to make a machine a safer product

## Citation

## Annotation
Alexander Blaszczynski's article addresses harm reduction and secondary prevention in gambling, including efforts to make gambling machines a “safer product” through design. It contributes to public-health and responsible-gambling scholarship on mitigating harm from machine gambling. Published in the Journal of Gambling Issues, its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized source on gambling harm reduction and product design.
