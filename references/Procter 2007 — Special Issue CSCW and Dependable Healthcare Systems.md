# Special Issue: CSCW and Dependable Healthcare Systems

## Citation
Procter, R., Rouncefield, M., Balka, E., Berg, M. (2007). Special Issue: CSCW and Dependable Healthcare Systems. Computer Supported Cooperative Work (CSCW), 15(5-6), 413-418. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10606-006-9037-9

## Annotation
Procter, Rouncefield, Balka, and Berg introduce a special issue on CSCW and dependable healthcare systems, examining the socio-technical challenges of designing reliable cooperative technologies for health care. Published in Computer Supported Cooperative Work, it frames research on trust, reliability, and cooperation in health IT. For this corpus it grounds socio-technical analysis of dependable health-care systems.
