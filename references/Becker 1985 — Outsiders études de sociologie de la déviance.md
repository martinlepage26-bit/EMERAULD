# Outsiders: études de sociologie de la déviance

## Citation
Becker, H. (1985). Outsiders: études de sociologie de la déviance. Paris : Métaillé.

## Annotation
Howard Becker's Outsiders (here in French translation) is a foundational text of the sociology of deviance and the origin of labeling theory. Becker argues that deviance is not a quality of the act but a consequence of the application of rules and sanctions by others — “the deviant is one to whom the label has successfully been applied.” Through studies of marijuana users and dance musicians, he develops the concepts of moral entrepreneurs and deviant careers. For this corpus it grounds an interactionist account of how norms, labeling, and social control produce “outsiders.”
