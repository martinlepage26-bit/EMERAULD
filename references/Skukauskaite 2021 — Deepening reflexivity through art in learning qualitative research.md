# Deepening reflexivity through art in learning qualitative research

## Citation
Skukauskaite, A., Yilmazli Trout, I., Robinson, K. (2021). Deepening reflexivity through art in learning qualitative research. Qualitative Research, 22(3), 403-420. https://doi.org/10.1177/1468794120985676

## Annotation
Skukauskaite, Yilmazli Trout, and Robinson examine how a doctoral student's engagement with drawing and painting deepened her reflexivity in learning qualitative research, using interactional-ethnographic analysis of the student's art and reflections. Published in Qualitative Research, it contributes to arts-based methods and reflexivity in research training. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds art as a means of developing reflexivity.
