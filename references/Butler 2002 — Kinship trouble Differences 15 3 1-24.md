# Kinship trouble. Differences, 15(3), 1-24.

## Citation
Butler, J. (2002). Kinship trouble. Differences, 15(3), 1-24.

## Annotation
Butler extends her gender theory to kinship, arguing that normative kinship structures are contingent and that queer kinship troubles the boundary between the culturally intelligible and unintelligible.
