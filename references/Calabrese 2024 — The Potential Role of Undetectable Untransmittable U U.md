# The Potential Role of Undetectable = Untransmittable (U = U) in Reducing HIV Stigma among Sexual Minority Men in the US

## Citation
Calabrese, S., Kalwicz, D., Zaheer, M., Dovidio, J., et al. (2024). The Potential Role of Undetectable = Untransmittable (U = U) in Reducing HIV Stigma among Sexual Minority Men in the US. AIDS and Behavior, 28(2), 741-757. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10461-023-04263-1

## Annotation
Calabrese and colleagues examine the potential of the “Undetectable = Untransmittable” (U=U) message to reduce HIV stigma among sexual-minority men in the United States. The study assesses how the scientific fact that people with an undetectable viral load cannot transmit HIV may reshape stigma and self-perception. Published in AIDS and Behavior, it contributes to HIV prevention and stigma research. For work on queer health in this corpus, it addresses the social effects of biomedical HIV messaging.
