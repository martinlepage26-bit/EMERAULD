# New religions in global perspective : a study of religious change

## Citation
Clarke, P. B. (2006). New religions in global perspective : a study of religious change

## Annotation
Peter Clarke's New Religions in Global Perspective offers a comprehensive study of new religious movements and religious change worldwide, examining their emergence, spread, and adaptation across cultures. It is a broad reference work in the sociology and history of religion. For this corpus it provides global context for understanding contemporary and alternative religious movements, including Paganism.
