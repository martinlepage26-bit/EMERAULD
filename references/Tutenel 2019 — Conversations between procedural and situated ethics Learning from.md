# Conversations between procedural and situated ethics: Learning from video research with children in a cancer care ward

## Citation
Tutenel, P., Ramaekers, S., Heylighen, A. (2019). Conversations between procedural and situated ethics: Learning from video research with children in a cancer care ward. The Design Journal, 22(sup1), 641-654. https://doi.org/10.1080/14606925.2019.1595444

## Annotation
Tutenel, Ramaekers, and Heylighen reflect on the relationship between procedural and situated ethics in video research with children on a cancer-care ward, examining how formal ethical procedures meet the messy realities of fieldwork with vulnerable participants. Published in The Design Journal, it contributes to research ethics and design methodology. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the tension between procedural and situated ethics.
