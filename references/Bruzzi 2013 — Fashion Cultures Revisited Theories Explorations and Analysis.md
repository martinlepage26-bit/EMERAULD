# Fashion Cultures Revisited: Theories, Explorations and Analysis

## Citation
Bruzzi, Stella, and Pamela Church Gibson, editors. Fashion Cultures Revisited: Theories, Explorations and Analysis. Routledge, 2013.

## Annotation
Edited by Stella Bruzzi and Pamela Church Gibson, Fashion Cultures Revisited collects theoretical and empirical studies of fashion as a cultural and social phenomenon, spanning identity, representation, consumption, and the body. An updated edition of a foundational reader, it maps the interdisciplinary field of fashion studies. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds fashion as an object of critical analysis.
