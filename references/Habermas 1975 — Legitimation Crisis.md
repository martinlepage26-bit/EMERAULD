# Legitimation Crisis

## Citation
Habermas, Jürgen. Legitimation Crisis. Trans. Thomas McCarthy. Boston: Beacon Press, 1975.

## Annotation
Jürgen Habermas's Legitimation Crisis analyzes the tendencies of late capitalism to generate crises of legitimacy, as states struggle to reconcile economic imperatives with the need for mass loyalty and normative justification. It is an influential early statement of his concerns with legitimacy and the public sphere. For this corpus it grounds the analysis of legitimation and its crises in modern governance.
