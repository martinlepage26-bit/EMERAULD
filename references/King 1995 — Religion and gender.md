# Religion and gender

## Citation
King, U. (dir.) (1995). Religion and gender. Oxford: Blackwell.

## Annotation
Edited by Ursula King, Religion and Gender is a foundational collection examining the relationship between religion and gender across traditions, addressing women's experience, feminist critique, and the gendering of the sacred. It helped establish religion-and-gender as a field. For this corpus it grounds the comparative study of gender and religion.
