# The Iron Cage Revisited: Institutional Isomorphism and Collective Rationality in Organizational Fields.

## Citation
DiMaggio, Paul J., and Walter W. Powell. "The Iron Cage Revisited: Institutional Isomorphism and Collective Rationality in Organizational Fields." American Sociological Review 48, no. 2 (1983): 147–160.

## Annotation
DiMaggio and Powell's “The Iron Cage Revisited” is a foundational text of new institutionalism, arguing that organizations in a field grow similar (isomorphic) not because such structures are functionally optimal but because of coercive, mimetic, and normative pressures. This helps explain why organizations adopt similar practices — including AI-ethics frameworks — under peer and regulatory expectation rather than reasoned choice. It is among the most cited works in organizational sociology. For this corpus it explains why governance convergence need not mean governance improvement.
