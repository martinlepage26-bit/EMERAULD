# Magic in the Modern World: Strategies of Repression and Legitimization

## Citation
Bever, Edward, and Randall Styers, eds. Magic in the Modern World: Strategies of Repression and Legitimization. University Park: Penn State University Press, 2017.

## Annotation
Edited by Edward Bever and Randall Styers, Magic in the Modern World examines the persistence and transformation of magic in modernity, analyzing the strategies of repression and legitimization through which magical practice has been marginalized or defended. The contributors challenge the assumption that modernity simply dispelled magic, showing instead its ongoing negotiation with science, religion, and law. It is a valuable reference on magic and modernity. For this corpus it complements historical accounts with attention to magic's modern status.
