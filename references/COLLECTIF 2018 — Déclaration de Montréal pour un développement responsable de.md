# Déclaration de Montréal pour un développement responsable de l’intelligence artificielle

## Citation
COLLECTIF (2018). Déclaration de Montréal pour un développement responsable de l’intelligence artificielle. En ligne: https://declarationmontreal-iaresponsable.com.

## Annotation
The Montréal Declaration for a Responsible Development of Artificial Intelligence is a multi-stakeholder ethical framework, developed through public deliberation, that sets out principles — well-being, autonomy, justice, privacy, democratic participation, and others — to guide responsible AI. As a landmark participatory governance document, it is widely cited in AI-ethics debates. For this corpus it is a primary source exemplifying deliberative, principle-based AI governance.
