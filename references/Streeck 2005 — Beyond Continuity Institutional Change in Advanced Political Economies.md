# Beyond Continuity: Institutional Change in Advanced Political Economies

## Citation
Streeck, Wolfgang, and Kathleen Thelen, eds. Beyond Continuity: Institutional Change in Advanced Political Economies. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2005.

## Annotation
Edited by Wolfgang Streeck and Kathleen Thelen, Beyond Continuity develops an influential framework for understanding gradual, incremental institutional change — through mechanisms such as layering, drift, conversion, and displacement — challenging accounts that see institutions as static between punctuated ruptures. It is a foundational text in comparative political economy and historical institutionalism. For this corpus it grounds how governance institutions change slowly and cumulatively — relevant to the evolution of regulatory regimes.
