# Parameter-efficient fine-tuning of large-scale pre-trained language models

## Citation
Ding, N., Qin, Y., Yang, G., Wei, F., Yang, Z., Su, Y., Hu, S., & Chen, Y. (2023). Parameter-efficient fine-tuning of large-scale pre-trained language models. Nature Machine Intelligence, 5(3), 220-235. https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-023-00626-4

## Annotation
The reference survey of parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT): why full fine-tuning of large models is prohibitive and how delta-tuning methods achieve comparable performance at a fraction of the trainable parameters. Cite this rather than a blog post when a governance argument depends on who can realistically adapt a frontier model — PEFT is precisely what lowers that barrier, and therefore what redistributes capability. Peer-reviewed, Nature Machine Intelligence.
