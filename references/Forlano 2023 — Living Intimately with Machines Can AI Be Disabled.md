# Living Intimately with Machines: Can AI Be Disabled?

## Citation
Forlano, Laura (2023) Living Intimately with Machines: Can AI Be Disabled?. interactions.

## Annotation
Laura Forlano's “Living Intimately with Machines: Can AI Be Disabled?” reflects, from her standpoint as a disabled cyborg, on how AI and everyday medical technologies blur the natural and the artificial and complicate notions of ability and error. Published in interactions, it contributes a disability-studies perspective to human–AI relations. For this corpus it grounds an intimate, first-person account of living with intelligent machines.
