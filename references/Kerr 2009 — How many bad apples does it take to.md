# How many bad apples does it take to spoil the whole barrel?

## Citation
Kerr, N., Rumble, A., Park, E., Ouwerkerk, J., et al. (2009). “How many bad apples does it take to spoil the whole barrel?”: Social exclusion and toleration for bad apples. Journal of Experimental Social Psychology, 45(4), 603-613. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jesp.2009.02.017

## Annotation
Kerr and colleagues experimentally examine how social exclusion affects toleration for “bad apples” — norm-violating group members — testing when groups expel versus tolerate deviants. Published in the Journal of Experimental Social Psychology, it belongs to the social psychology of groups and exclusion. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized empirical source.
