# Photovoice: A Review of the Literature in Health and Public Health

## Citation
Catalani, C., Minkler, M. (2009). Photovoice: A Review of the Literature in Health and Public Health. Health Education &amp; Behavior, 37(3), 424-451. https://doi.org/10.1177/1090198109342084

## Annotation
Catalani and Minkler present a systematic review of Photovoice, a community-based participatory research method in which participants document their lives and communities through photography. The review defines the Photovoice process, catalogues outcomes, and relates levels of community participation to processes and results across 37 studies. Published in Health Education & Behavior, it is a key methodological reference. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds participatory visual methods.
