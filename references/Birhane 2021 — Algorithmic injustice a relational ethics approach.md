# Algorithmic injustice: a relational ethics approach

## Citation
Birhane, A. (2021). Algorithmic injustice: a relational ethics approach. Patterns, 2(2), 100205. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.patter.2021.100205

## Annotation
Abeba Birhane's “Algorithmic injustice: a relational ethics approach” argues that dominant, individualist framings of AI ethics miss the relational and structural nature of algorithmic harm. Drawing on relational and decolonial thought, Birhane contends that ethics must attend to power, context, and the situated relations between systems and affected communities. Published in Patterns, it is an influential reframing of AI ethics. For this corpus it grounds a relational, justice-centered account of algorithmic harm.
