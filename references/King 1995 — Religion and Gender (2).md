# Religion and Gender

## Citation
King, Ursula, ed. Religion and Gender. Oxford: Blackwell, 1995.

## Annotation
Edited by Ursula King, Religion and Gender is a foundational collection examining the intersection of religion and gender across traditions, addressing women's experience, feminist critique, and the gendering of the sacred. It helped establish religion-and-gender as a scholarly field. (This entry duplicates the same volume listed elsewhere in the canonical list.) For this corpus it grounds the comparative study of gender and religion.
