# Precarious Life: The Powers of Mourning and Violence

## Citation
Butler, J. (2004) Precarious Life: The Powers of Mourning and Violence. London: Verso.

## Annotation
Judith Butler's Precarious Life reflects on mourning, vulnerability, and ethics in the aftermath of September 11, arguing that shared corporeal vulnerability could ground a non-violent ethics and politics rather than a logic of retaliation. Butler examines grief, dehumanization, and the differential grievability of lives. The book is widely cited in ethics and political theory. For this corpus it grounds an ethics of vulnerability and the critique of who is rendered human.
