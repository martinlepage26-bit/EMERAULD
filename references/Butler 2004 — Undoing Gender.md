# Undoing Gender

## Citation
Butler, J. (2004) Undoing Gender. New York: Routledge.

## Annotation
Judith Butler's Undoing Gender collects essays on gender norms, autonomy, and the “human,” engaging trans and intersex experience, kinship, and the New Gender Politics. Butler argues that becoming intelligible as a subject requires conforming to norms that also constrain, and asks how those norms might be remade to make more lives livable. The book is central to gender and queer theory. For this corpus it develops the ethical and political stakes of gender norms and recognition.
