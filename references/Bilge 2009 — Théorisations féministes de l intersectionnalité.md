# Théorisations féministes de l’intersectionnalité.

## Citation
Bilge, Sirma. “Théorisations féministes de l’intersectionnalité.” Diogène 225, no. 1 (2009): 70–88.

## Annotation
Sirma Bilge's “Théorisations féministes de l'intersectionnalité” reviews the development of intersectionality as feminism's major theoretical contribution, from its Anglo-American origins in naming the situation of Black and racialized women toward contemporary uses aimed at reshaping anti-discrimination policy. The article maps the theoretical and political debates the concept generates. Appearing in Diogène, it is a key francophone reference.
