# Informational Autocrats

## Citation
Guriev, S., Treisman, D. (2019). Informational Autocrats. Journal of Economic Perspectives, 33(4), 100-127. https://doi.org/10.1257/jep.33.4.100

## Annotation
Guriev and Treisman's “Informational Autocrats” argues that contemporary authoritarian rule increasingly relies on manipulating information rather than terrorizing populations, using propaganda and selective censorship to manufacture an image of competence. The article documents the shift with cross-national data. (This entry duplicates the same article in a different citation form.)
