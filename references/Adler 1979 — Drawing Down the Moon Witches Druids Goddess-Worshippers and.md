# Drawing Down the Moon: Witches, Druids, Goddess-Worshippers, and Other Pagans in America Today

## Citation
Adler, M., 1979. Drawing Down the Moon: Witches, Druids, Goddess-Worshippers, and Other Pagans in America Today. Boston, MA: Beacon Press.

## Annotation
The 1979 first edition of Margot Adler's Drawing Down the Moon documents the emerging Neo-Pagan and Wiccan movement in America through participant observation of witches, Druids, and Goddess-worshippers. Adler catalogues the movement's plural traditions, ritual practices, and its ecological and feminist currents at an early stage of public visibility. The book helped legitimize contemporary Paganism as an object of serious study and as a coherent religious phenomenon. As both insider account and reportage, it is widely used as a primary source on the movement's self-description. It anchors the historiography of modern nature religion and Goddess spirituality. (Same work as the 1986 edition listed separately.)
