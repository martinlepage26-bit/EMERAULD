# The impacts and governance of artificial intelligence: A transfeminist perspective [Doctoral dissertation, Faculty of Information, University of Toronto]

## Citation
Attard-Frost, B. (2025). The impacts and governance of artificial intelligence: A transfeminist perspective [Doctoral dissertation, Faculty of Information, University of Toronto].

## Annotation
Attard-Frost's doctoral thesis reads AI governance through a transfeminist lens, treating governance regimes themselves as sites where power over gendered and marginalised subjects is distributed. This is the sharpest critical-theory acquisition in this batch and the natural interlocutor for any claim that governance is neutral procedure. Canadian in focus, which makes it doubly useful for Quebec/Canada regulatory work. Dissertation — cite as thesis, not as peer-reviewed article.
