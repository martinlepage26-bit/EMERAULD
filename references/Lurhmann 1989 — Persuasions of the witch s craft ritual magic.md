## Annotation
T. M. Luhrmann's Persuasions of the Witch's Craft studies ritual magic in contemporary England, developing “interpretive drift” to explain how practitioners gradually come to experience magic as real and reasonable. It is a classic anthropological account of belief-formation. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation form.)
