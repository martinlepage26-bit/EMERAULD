# INTERSECTIONALITY

## Citation
Carbado, D., Crenshaw, K., Mays, V., Tomlinson, B. (2013). INTERSECTIONALITY. Du Bois Review: Social Science Research on Race, 10(2), 303-312. https://doi.org/10.1017/S1742058X13000349

## Annotation
Carbado, Crenshaw, Mays, and Tomlinson's “Intersectionality: Mapping the Movements of a Theory” reflects on how intersectionality has traveled across disciplines, issues, and national boundaries since its articulation. The authors revisit its genesis, address debates over its scope and theoretical capacity, and consider where the theory is already at work and where it might go. Published in the Du Bois Review, with Crenshaw among the authors, it is an authoritative reflection on the concept. For this corpus it is a key reference on intersectionality's development and reach.
