# Does trust favor macroeconomic stability?

## Citation
Sangnier, M. (2013). Does trust favor macroeconomic stability?. Journal of Comparative Economics, 41(3), 653-668. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jce.2012.10.002

## Annotation
Marc Sangnier's article asks whether trust favors macroeconomic stability, using cross-country data to test the relationship between social trust and economic volatility. Published in the Journal of Comparative Economics, it belongs to the economics of social capital. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral, though it bears on trust as a societal resource.
