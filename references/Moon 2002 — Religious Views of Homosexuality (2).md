# Religious Views of Homosexuality.

## Citation
Moon, Dawne. “Religious Views of Homosexuality.” In Handbook of Lesbian and Gay Studies, edited by Diane Richardson and Steven Seidman, 313–328. London: Sage, 2002.

## Annotation
Dawne Moon's “Religious Views of Homosexuality” maps the diversity of religious responses to same-sex desire across traditions, from condemnation to affirmation. Appearing in the Handbook of Lesbian and Gay Studies, it is a standard reference. (This entry duplicates the same chapter in a different citation form.)
