# Olsen

## Citation
March, James G., and Johan P. Olsen. Rediscovering Institutions: The Organizational Basis of Politics. New York: Free Press, 1989.

## Annotation
March and Olsen's Rediscovering Institutions is a foundational text of new institutionalism in political science, arguing that political behavior is governed less by rational calculation of consequences than by a “logic of appropriateness” embedded in institutional rules, roles, and identities. It reshaped the study of organizations and politics. For this corpus it grounds an account of how institutions structure behavior through norms and roles — relevant to governance and organizational adoption of AI.
