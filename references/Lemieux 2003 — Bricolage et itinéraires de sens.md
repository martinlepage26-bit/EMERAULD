# Bricolage et itinéraires de sens

## Citation
Lemieux, R. (2003). Bricolage et itinéraires de sens. Religiologiques, 26, 11-34.

## Annotation
René Lemieux's “Bricolage et itinéraires de sens” analyzes religious “bricolage” — the individualized assembling of meaning from diverse spiritual resources — and the trajectories through which contemporary people construct religious meaning. Published in Religiologiques, it contributes to the francophone sociology of contemporary religion. For this corpus it grounds the study of do-it-yourself, bricolage spirituality.
