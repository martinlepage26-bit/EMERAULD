# The Mists of Avalon

## Citation
Zimmer Bradley, M. (1983). The Mists of Avalon. New York: Knopf.

## Annotation
Marion Zimmer Bradley's The Mists of Avalon retells the Arthurian legend from the perspective of its women, foregrounding a Goddess-centered, matriarchal spirituality in conflict with rising Christianity. Enormously popular, it profoundly influenced the feminist Goddess and Neo-Pagan movements. For this corpus it is a primary literary source that shaped contemporary Goddess spirituality (its reception now also complicated by revelations about the author).
