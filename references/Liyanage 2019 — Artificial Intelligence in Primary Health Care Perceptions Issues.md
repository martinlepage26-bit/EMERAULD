# Artificial Intelligence in Primary Health Care: Perceptions, Issues, and Challenges

## Citation
Liyanage, Harshana, Liaw, Siaw-Teng, Jonnagaddala, Jitendra, Schreiber, Richard, Kuziemsky, Craig, Terry, Amanda L, de Lusignan, Simon (2019) Artificial Intelligence in Primary Health Care: Perceptions, Issues, and Challenges. Yearbook of medical informatics, 28(1), 41-46. doi: 10.1055/s-0039-1677901

## Annotation
Liyanage and colleagues survey perceptions, issues, and challenges of applying artificial intelligence in primary health care, mapping opportunities and barriers for clinical adoption. Published in the Yearbook of Medical Informatics, it contributes to health-informatics scholarship on AI. For this corpus it grounds practical and governance considerations for AI in primary care.
