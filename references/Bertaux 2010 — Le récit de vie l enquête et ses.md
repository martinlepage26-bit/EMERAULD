# Le récit de vie: l’enquête et ses méthodes

## Citation
Bertaux, Daniel. 2010. Le récit de vie: l’enquête et ses méthodes. Paris: Armand Colin.

## Annotation
Daniel Bertaux's Le récit de vie sets out the theory and practice of the life-story method, showing how narrated biographies can be collected and analyzed to illuminate social structures and trajectories. It emphasizes moving from singular accounts toward generalizable sociological insight. It is a core francophone methods reference. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation form.)
