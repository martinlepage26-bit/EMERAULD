# Addicted to answers: Need for cognitive closure and the endorsement of conspiracy beliefs

## Citation
Marchlewska, M., Cichocka, A., Kossowska, M. (2017). Addicted to answers: Need for cognitive closure and the endorsement of conspiracy beliefs. European Journal of Social Psychology, 48(2), 109-117. https://doi.org/10.1002/ejsp.2308

## Annotation
Marchlewska, Cichocka, and Kossowska show experimentally that need for cognitive closure (NFCC) fosters endorsement of conspiracy theories, especially for events lacking clear official explanations and when conspiratorial explanations are made salient. Published in the European Journal of Social Psychology, it advances the psychology of conspiracy belief and uncertainty. For work touching on misinformation in this corpus, it grounds the cognitive drivers of conspiratorial thinking.
