# The Challenger Launch Decision: Risky Technology, Culture, and Deviance at NASA

## Citation
Vaughan, Diane. The Challenger Launch Decision: Risky Technology, Culture, and Deviance at NASA. Chicago: University of Chicago Press, 1996.

## Annotation
Diane Vaughan's The Challenger Launch Decision is a landmark sociological study of the 1986 Space Shuttle disaster, developing the influential concept of the “normalization of deviance” — how organizations incrementally come to accept signals of danger as normal — alongside “structural secrecy” and the banality of organizational mistake. For this corpus it is highly relevant to AI safety and governance: a canonical account of how competent organizations drift into catastrophic failure through routine, culturally embedded processes.
