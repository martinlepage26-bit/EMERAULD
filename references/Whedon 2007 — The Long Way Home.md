# The Long Way Home.

## Citation
Whedon, Joss, et al. “The Long Way Home.” Buffy the Vampire Slayer: Season Eight,—The Long Way Home. vVol. 1, Dark Horse, 2007.

## Annotation
This volume of the Buffy the Vampire Slayer Season Eight comic (“The Long Way Home,” Dark Horse), by Joss Whedon and collaborators, launched the canonical comic continuation of the television series. As a franchise text, it is a primary source for fan and media studies of Buffy. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it supports analysis of the Buffyverse's transmedia expansion.
