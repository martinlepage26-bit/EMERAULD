# From the Goddess to queer theology : the state we are in

## Citation
Althaus-Reid, M. (2005). From the Goddess to queer theology : the state we are in

## Annotation
Marcella Althaus-Reid's essay charts a movement “from the Goddess to queer theology,” bringing feminist and liberation theology into dialogue with queer critique. Known for her “indecent theology,” Althaus-Reid challenges the heteronormative and colonial assumptions embedded in both traditional and feminist theologies. The piece exemplifies her project of rethinking the sacred from the margins of sexuality and gender. It is an important reference in queer theology and the study of religion and sexuality. For this corpus it links Goddess spirituality to explicitly queer theological critique.
