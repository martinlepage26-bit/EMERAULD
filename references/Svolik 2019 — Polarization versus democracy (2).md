# Polarization versus democracy

## Citation
Svolik, M.W. (2019) ‘Polarization versus democracy’, Journal of Democracy, 30(3), pp. 20–32. doi: 10.1353/jod.2019.0039.

## Annotation
Milan Svolik's “Polarization versus Democracy” shows, through analysis of voter behavior, that polarization lets elected leaders erode democracy because partisans tolerate undemocratic conduct by their own side. Published in the Journal of Democracy, it is a key reference on democratic backsliding. (This entry duplicates the same article in a different citation form.)
