# The Tacit Dimension

## Citation
Polanyi, Michael. The Tacit Dimension. Garden City: Doubleday, 1966.

## Annotation
Michael Polanyi's The Tacit Dimension develops his influential concept of “tacit knowledge” — the idea that “we know more than we can tell” — arguing that much human knowing is personal, embodied, and not fully articulable in explicit rules. It has been enormously influential across philosophy of science, epistemology, and knowledge management. For this corpus it grounds the notion of tacit knowledge — relevant to expertise, skill, and the limits of formalizing knowledge in AI systems.
