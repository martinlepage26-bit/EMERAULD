# Bureaucracy: What Government Agencies Do and Why They Do It

## Citation
Wilson, James Q. Bureaucracy: What Government Agencies Do and Why They Do It. New York: Basic Books, 1989.

## Annotation
James Q. Wilson's Bureaucracy is a classic of public administration analyzing what government agencies actually do and why, examining organizational culture, incentives, discretion, and the constraints that distinguish public from private organizations. For this corpus it is highly relevant: a canonical account of how bureaucracies behave — essential context for how public institutions design, adopt, and are constrained in governing technology.
