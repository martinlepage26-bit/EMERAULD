# Emotions in storybooks: A comparison of storybooks that represent ethnic and racial groups in the United States

## Citation
Grady, J., Her, M., Moreno, G., Perez, C., et al. (2019). Emotions in storybooks: A comparison of storybooks that represent ethnic and racial groups in the United States. Psychology of Popular Media Culture, 8(3), 207-217. https://doi.org/10.1037/ppm0000185

## Annotation
Grady and colleagues compare how storybooks representing different ethnic and racial groups in the United States portray emotions, examining representation and affect in children's media. Published in Psychology of Popular Media Culture, it contributes to research on media, race, and childhood. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized empirical source on representation in children's books.
