## Annotation
Susan Reid's MA thesis “Disorganized Religion” explores the Neopagan Craft in Canada, examining how practitioners sustain a deliberately non-institutional, decentralized form of religion. It contributes local ethnographic scholarship to Pagan studies. For this corpus it grounds the Canadian study of Neopagan Witchcraft and its anti-institutional character.
