# Les mots, la mort, les sorts: La sorcellerie dans le Bocage

## Citation
Favret-Saada, Jeanne. Les mots, la mort, les sorts: La sorcellerie dans le Bocage. Paris: Gallimard, 1977.

## Annotation
Jeanne Favret-Saada's Les mots, la mort, les sorts (Deadly Words) is a classic anthropological study of witchcraft in the rural French Bocage, arguing that witchcraft operates through speech and that the ethnographer cannot remain a neutral observer but is inevitably “caught” in its circuit of words and forces. The work reshaped debates on participation, language, and reflexivity in fieldwork. (This entry duplicates the same book listed elsewhere in the canonical list.) For this corpus it grounds a reflexive, language-centered account of witchcraft.
