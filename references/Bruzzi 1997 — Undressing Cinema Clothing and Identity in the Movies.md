# Undressing Cinema: Clothing and Identity in the Movies

## Citation
Bruzzi, Stella. Undressing Cinema: Clothing and Identity in the Movies. Routledge, 1997.

## Annotation
Stella Bruzzi's Undressing Cinema analyzes clothing and costume as active agents of meaning and identity in film, rather than mere background. Bruzzi argues that dress can exceed and even resist narrative, expressing gender, sexuality, and subjectivity. The book is influential in film and fashion studies. For media-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the analysis of costume, identity, and cinematic signification.
