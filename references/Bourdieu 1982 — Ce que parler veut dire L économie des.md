# Ce que parler veut dire: L’économie des échanges linguistiques

## Citation
Bourdieu, P., 1982. Ce que parler veut dire: L’économie des échanges linguistiques. Paris: Fayard.

## Annotation
Pierre Bourdieu's Ce que parler veut dire develops his sociology of language, arguing that linguistic exchanges are also exchanges of symbolic power: the value of an utterance depends on the speaker's authority and position within a field, not merely on grammar or meaning. Bourdieu critiques formal linguistics for ignoring the social conditions of legitimate speech. The work grounds his concept of symbolic power. For this corpus it underpins the argument that authoritative-sounding speech can substitute for genuine legitimacy.
