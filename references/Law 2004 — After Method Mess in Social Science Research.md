# After Method: Mess in Social Science Research

## Citation
Law, John. After Method: Mess in Social Science Research. London: Routledge, 2004.

## Annotation
John Law's After Method argues that social-science methods do not simply describe a pre-existing reality but help enact the realities they study, and that much of the world is “messy,” fluid, and multiple in ways standard methods suppress. Law calls for methods that can attend to mess and ontological multiplicity. It is an influential STS methodological intervention. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds a critical, ontologically attentive approach to method.
