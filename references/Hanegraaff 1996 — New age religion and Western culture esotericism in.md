## Citation
Hanegraaff, W. J. (1996). New age religion and Western culture : esotericism in the

## Annotation
Wouter Hanegraaff's New Age Religion and Western Culture is a landmark scholarly study that situates the New Age movement within the longer history of Western esotericism, tracing how esoteric ideas were secularized and reworked in modern culture. It helped establish the academic study of Western esotericism as a rigorous field. For this corpus it is a foundational reference for understanding New Age spirituality and its esoteric lineage.
