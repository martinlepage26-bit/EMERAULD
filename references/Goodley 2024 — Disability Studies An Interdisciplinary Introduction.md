# Disability Studies: An Interdisciplinary Introduction

## Citation
Goodley, Dan (2024) Disability Studies: An Interdisciplinary Introduction. SAGE Publications Ltd.

## Annotation
Dan Goodley's Disability Studies: An Interdisciplinary Introduction is a leading textbook mapping the field across social, cultural, critical, and global models of disability. Goodley synthesizes theory and politics to introduce disability studies as an interdisciplinary project. It is a standard teaching reference. For this corpus it grounds the conceptual landscape of disability studies.
