# Science for the Post-Normal Age.

## Citation
Funtowicz, Silvio O., and Jerome R. Ravetz. "Science for the Post-Normal Age." Futures 25, no. 7 (1993): 739–755.

## Annotation
Funtowicz and Ravetz's “Science for the Post-Normal Age” introduces the influential concept of “post-normal science” for situations where “facts are uncertain, values in dispute, stakes high, and decisions urgent.” They argue that such conditions require extended peer communities and new modes of quality assurance beyond traditional expertise. It is a foundational reference in STS and science-policy. For this corpus it grounds governance approaches for high-stakes, high-uncertainty domains — directly applicable to AI.
