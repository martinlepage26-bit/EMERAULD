# Postfeminist Media Culture: Elements of a Sensibility.

## Citation
Gill, Rosalind. 2007. “Postfeminist Media Culture: Elements of a Sensibility.” European Journal of Cultural Studies 10 (2): 147–166.

## Annotation
Rosalind Gill's “Postfeminist Media Culture: Elements of a Sensibility” is a foundational and much-cited article arguing that postfeminism is best understood not as a theoretical position but as a distinctive media “sensibility” comprising interrelated themes: femininity as a bodily property, a shift from objectification to subjectification, an emphasis on self-surveillance and self-discipline, individualism and “choice,” a makeover paradigm, and a resurgence of ideas about natural sexual difference. Published in the European Journal of Cultural Studies, it reshaped feminist media analysis. For this corpus it grounds the critical concept of the postfeminist sensibility.
