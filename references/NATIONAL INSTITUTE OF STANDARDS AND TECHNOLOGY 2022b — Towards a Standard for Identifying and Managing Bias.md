# Towards a Standard for Identifying and Managing Bias in Artificial Intelligence

## Citation
NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF STANDARDS AND TECHNOLOGY (2022b). Towards a Standard for Identifying and Managing Bias in Artificial Intelligence. NIST Special Publication 1270, mars. En ligne: https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/SpecialPublications/NIST.SP.1270.pdf.

## Annotation
NIST Special Publication 1270, “Towards a Standard for Identifying and Managing Bias in Artificial Intelligence,” analyzes the sources of AI bias — computational, human, and systemic — and proposes a socio-technical approach to identifying and managing it. As an authoritative government standard-setting document, it complements the AI RMF. For this corpus it grounds NIST's treatment of bias as a core AI-governance concern.
