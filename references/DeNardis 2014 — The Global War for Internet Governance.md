# The Global War for Internet Governance

## Citation
DeNardis, Laura. The Global War for Internet Governance. New Haven: Yale University Press, 2014.

## Annotation
Laura DeNardis's The Global War for Internet Governance argues that control over the Internet's technical infrastructure — protocols, standards, domain names, and interconnection — is a central arena of political power, often exercised through seemingly neutral technical decisions. DeNardis shows how internet governance shapes rights, security, and economic power. It is a key reference in internet-governance scholarship. For this corpus it grounds the insight that infrastructure and standards are sites of governance — directly relevant to AI.
