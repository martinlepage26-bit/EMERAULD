# Aligning artificial intelligence with human values: reflections from a phenomenological perspective

## Citation
Han, S., Kelly, E., Nikou, S., Svee, E. (2021). Aligning artificial intelligence with human values: reflections from a phenomenological perspective. AI &amp; SOCIETY, 37(4), 1383-1395. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00146-021-01247-4

## Annotation
Han and colleagues reflect on the problem of aligning artificial intelligence with human values from a phenomenological perspective, arguing that values are lived, contextual, and embodied rather than fixed inputs that can simply be specified for a system. Published in AI & Society, the article critiques technical framings of “value alignment” and calls for richer, situated understandings of human values. For this corpus it grounds a humanities-informed critique of AI alignment.
