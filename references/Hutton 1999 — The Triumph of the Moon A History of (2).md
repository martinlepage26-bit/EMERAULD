# The Triumph of the Moon: A History of Modern Pagan Witchcraft

## Citation
Hutton, Ronald. 1999. The Triumph of the Moon: A History of Modern Pagan Witchcraft. Oxford: Oxford University Press.

## Annotation
Ronald Hutton's The Triumph of the Moon provides the authoritative account of the origins of modern Pagan witchcraft, showing Wicca to be a creative modern construction drawing on Romanticism, folklore, ceremonial magic, and other currents rather than an unbroken ancient tradition. It is foundational to Pagan studies. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation form.)
