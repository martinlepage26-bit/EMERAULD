# Kelly, and Shauna Pomerzntz

## Citation
Currie, Dawn H., Deirdre M. Kelly, and Shauna Pomerzntz. Girl Power : Girls Reinventing Girlhood. Peter Lang, 2004.

## Annotation
Currie, Kelly, and Pomerantz's Girl Power examines how girls negotiate and reinvent girlhood amid postfeminist and consumer-culture messages about empowerment. Drawing on research with girls, the authors interrogate the promises and limits of “girl power.” It is a reference in girlhood and feminist media studies. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds analysis of gender, youth, and empowerment discourse.
