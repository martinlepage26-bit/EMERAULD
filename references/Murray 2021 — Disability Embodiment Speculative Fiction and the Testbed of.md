# Disability Embodiment, Speculative Fiction, and the Testbed of Futurity

## Citation
Murray, Stuart (2021) Disability Embodiment, Speculative Fiction, and the Testbed of Futurity. Journal of Literary & Cultural Disability Studies. doi: 10.3828/jlcds.2021.21

## Annotation
Stuart Murray's article examines disability embodiment through speculative fiction, treating the genre as a “testbed of futurity” where disabled bodies and futures are imagined and contested. Published in the Journal of Literary & Cultural Disability Studies, it connects disability studies, science fiction, and futurity. For this corpus it grounds the imaginative testing of disability futures in speculative fiction.
