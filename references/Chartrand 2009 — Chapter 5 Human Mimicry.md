# Chapter 5 Human Mimicry

## Citation
Chartrand, T., van Baaren, R. (2009). Chapter 5 Human Mimicry. Advances in Experimental Social Psychology, 219-274. https://doi.org/10.1016/S0065-2601(08)00405-X

## Annotation
Chartrand and van Baaren's review chapter on human mimicry synthesizes research on the nonconscious “chameleon effect” — the automatic imitation of others' postures, gestures, and behaviors — and its social consequences for rapport and affiliation. Published in Advances in Experimental Social Psychology, it is an authoritative overview in social psychology. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized reference on nonverbal mimicry and social bonding.
