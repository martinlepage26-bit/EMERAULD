# Rabelais and His World

## Citation
Bakhtin, Mikhail. 1984. Rabelais and His World. Translated by Hélène Iswolsky. Bloomington: Indiana University Press.

## Annotation
Mikhail Bakhtin's Rabelais and His World develops his influential theory of carnival, the carnivalesque, and the grotesque body, reading Rabelais against the popular festive culture of the Middle Ages and Renaissance. Bakhtin argues that carnival laughter and bodily excess temporarily invert official hierarchies and open a utopian, communal mode of life. The work is a cornerstone of literary and cultural theory. For this corpus it supplies a durable framework for analyzing ritual inversion, the body, and popular culture.
