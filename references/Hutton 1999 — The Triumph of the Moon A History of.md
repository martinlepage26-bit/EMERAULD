# The Triumph of the Moon: A History of Modern Pagan Witchcraft

## Citation
Hutton, R., 1999. The Triumph of the Moon: A History of Modern Pagan Witchcraft. Oxford: Oxford University Press.

## Annotation
Ronald Hutton's The Triumph of the Moon is the definitive scholarly history of modern Pagan witchcraft (Wicca), demonstrating that it is a genuinely modern religion synthesized in the twentieth century rather than a survival of an ancient cult, while tracing the cultural currents that shaped it. It transformed the academic study of Paganism. For this corpus it is the canonical historical reference on the origins of modern Witchcraft.
