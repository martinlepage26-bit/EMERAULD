# Postmodern Fairy Tales: Gender and Narrative Strategies

## Citation
Bacchilega, C. (1997) Postmodern Fairy Tales: Gender and Narrative Strategies. Philadelphia, PA: University of Pennsylvania Press.

## Annotation
Cristina Bacchilega's Postmodern Fairy Tales examines how late-twentieth-century retellings rework classic tales to expose and contest their gender and narrative conventions. Drawing on feminist and postmodern theory, she reads literary and cinematic revisions as sites where the ideological work of the fairy tale becomes visible and reworkable. The book is influential in folklore studies, narratology, and gender studies. For media- and narrative-focused work in this corpus, it models how canonical story forms can be critically re-narrated.
