# Normal Accidents: Living with High-Risk Technologies

## Citation
Perrow, Charles. Normal Accidents: Living with High-Risk Technologies. New York: Basic Books, 1984.

## Annotation
Charles Perrow's Normal Accidents is a foundational study of technological risk arguing that in systems with high “interactive complexity” and “tight coupling,” serious accidents are not aberrations but normal, expected features — impossible to fully engineer away. Drawing on nuclear power, aviation, and other domains, Perrow reframes safety as a systemic property. For this corpus it is highly relevant to AI safety: a canonical account of why complex, tightly coupled systems inevitably fail in surprising ways.
