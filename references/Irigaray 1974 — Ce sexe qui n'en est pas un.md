# Ce sexe qui n'en est pas un

## Citation
Irigaray, L. (1974). Ce sexe qui n'en est pas un. Paris : Éditions de Minuit.

## Annotation
Luce Irigaray's Ce sexe qui n'en est pas un (This Sex Which Is Not One) is a foundational text of French feminist philosophy and psychoanalysis, critiquing the phallocentric construction of sexuality and language and theorizing female desire and subjectivity as irreducibly plural and other. It reshaped feminist theory and philosophy of sexual difference. For this corpus it grounds the critique of masculine norms in language and desire.
