# Voices from the Pagan census

## Citation
Berger, H. A., Leah, E. A. et Shaffer, L. S. (2003). Voices from the Pagan census. A

## Annotation
Berger, Leach, and Shaffer's Voices from the Pagan Census reports the first large-scale national survey of Witches and neopagans in the United States, providing demographic and attitudinal data on the movement. The study offers an empirical portrait of practitioners' beliefs, practices, and social characteristics. It is a key quantitative reference in Pagan studies. For this corpus it supplies survey-based grounding on the composition of contemporary Paganism.
