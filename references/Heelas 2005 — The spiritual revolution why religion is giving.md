# The spiritual revolution : why religion is giving

## Citation
Heelas, P. et Woodhead, L. (2005). The spiritual revolution : why religion is giving

## Annotation
Heelas and Woodhead's The Spiritual Revolution reports the influential “Kendal Project,” empirically testing the thesis that congregational religion is giving way to subjective-life “spirituality.” They document a “subjective turn” in the sacred sphere, though with important qualifications. Published by Blackwell, it is a landmark in the sociology of religion and secularization. For this corpus it grounds the empirical study of the shift from religion to spirituality.
