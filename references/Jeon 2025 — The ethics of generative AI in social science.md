# The ethics of generative AI in social science research: A qualitative approach for institutionally grounded AI research ethics

## Citation
Jeon, J., Kim, L., & Park, J. (2025). The ethics of generative AI in social science research: A qualitative approach for institutionally grounded AI research ethics. Technology in Society.

## Annotation
Jeon, Kim, and Park examine the ethics of using generative AI in social-science research, proposing an institutionally grounded, qualitative approach to research ethics for AI-assisted inquiry. Published in Technology in Society, it contributes to emerging debates on responsible AI use in scholarship. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the ethics of generative AI as a research tool.
