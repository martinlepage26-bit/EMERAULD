# La société des identités : éthique et politique dans le monde

## Citation
Beauchemin, J. (2005). La société des identités : éthique et politique dans le monde

## Annotation
Jacques Beauchemin's La société des identités analyzes the ethical and political consequences of the contemporary turn toward identity, arguing that the fragmentation of the social bond into particular identities strains the possibility of a shared political community. Writing from a Québec sociological perspective, Beauchemin reflects on solidarity, the common good, and the limits of identity politics. The book is a notable francophone contribution to debates on identity and citizenship. For this corpus it frames identity politics within a wider question of collective belonging.
