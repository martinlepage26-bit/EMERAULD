# Variations on sex and gender : Beauvoir, Wittig and Foucault

## Citation
Butler, J. (1986). Variations on sex and gender : Beauvoir, Wittig and Foucault.

## Annotation
Judith Butler's early essay “Variations on Sex and Gender” reads Beauvoir, Wittig, and Foucault to interrogate the sex/gender distinction and the constructed character of gendered embodiment, prefiguring the arguments of Gender Trouble. It is an important document in the development of Butler's thought. For this corpus it marks an early stage in the theorization of gender as constructed and performative.
