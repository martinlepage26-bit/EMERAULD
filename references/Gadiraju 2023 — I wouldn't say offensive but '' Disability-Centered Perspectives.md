# ``I wouldn't say offensive but⋯'': Disability-Centered Perspectives on Large Language Models

## Citation
Gadiraju, Vinitha, Kane, Shaun, Dev, Sunipa, Taylor, Alex, Wang, Ding, Denton, Emily, Brewer, Robin (2023) ``I wouldn't say offensive but⋯'': Disability-Centered Perspectives on Large Language Models. Proceedings of the 6th ACM Conference on Fairness, Accountability, and Transparency (FAccT '23), pp. 205–216. doi: 10.1145/3593013.3593989

## Annotation
Gadiraju and colleagues center disabled people's perspectives on large language models, drawing on participant accounts to show how LLMs can produce subtly ableist or othering outputs even when not overtly offensive. Presented at FAccT 2023, the study argues for disability-centered evaluation of generative AI. For this corpus it grounds disability-informed critique of LLM harms and the need for participatory evaluation.
