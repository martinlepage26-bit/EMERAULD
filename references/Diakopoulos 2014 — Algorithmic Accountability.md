# Algorithmic Accountability

## Citation
Diakopoulos, N. (2014). Algorithmic Accountability. Digital Journalism, 3(3), 398-415. https://doi.org/10.1080/21670811.2014.976411

## Annotation
Nicholas Diakopoulos's “Algorithmic Accountability” investigates how journalists and researchers can hold algorithms accountable, including through reverse engineering and investigation of opaque systems. Published in Digital Journalism, it is an influential contribution to computational journalism and critical algorithm studies. For this corpus it grounds the practice of investigating and holding algorithmic systems to account.
