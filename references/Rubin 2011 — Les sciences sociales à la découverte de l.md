# Les sciences sociales à la découverte de l’homosexualité

## Citation
Rubin, G. (2011). Les sciences sociales à la découverte de l’homosexualité. Genre, sexualité et société, Hors-série n° 1. https://doi.org/10.4000/gss.1849

## Annotation
Gayle Rubin's “Les sciences sociales à la découverte de l'homosexualité” (a French-translated essay) retraces the genealogy of social-science research on homosexuality, showing that studies of urban gay communities are older than often assumed and reconstructing the sociological currents that produced foundational texts. Published in Genre, sexualité & société, it is a valuable historiography of the field. For this corpus it grounds the intellectual history of the sociology of homosexuality.
