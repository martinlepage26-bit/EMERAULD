# Mad at School: Rhetorics of Mental Disability and Academic Life

## Citation
Price, M. (2011). Mad at School: Rhetorics of Mental Disability and Academic Life. Ann Arbor: University of Michigan Press. https://doi.org/10.3998/dcbooks.9439946.0001.001

## Annotation
Margaret Price's Mad at School is a foundational work of disability studies examining the rhetorics of mental disability in academic life, analyzing how norms of “rationality,” productivity, and participation exclude people with mental disabilities. Price develops influential concepts such as “kairotic space.” For this corpus it grounds the analysis of mental disability, ableism, and normativity in knowledge institutions.
