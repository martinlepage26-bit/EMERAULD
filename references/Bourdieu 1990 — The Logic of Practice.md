# The Logic of Practice

## Citation
Bourdieu, Pierre. 1990. The Logic of Practice. Translated by Richard Nice. Stanford, CA: Stanford University Press.

## Annotation
Pierre Bourdieu's The Logic of Practice elaborates his theory of practice — habitus, field, capital, and the “feel for the game” — to explain how agents act competently within social structures they have internalized rather than consciously calculated. The work bridges his ethnographic and theoretical concerns and remains foundational across the social sciences. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation form.)
