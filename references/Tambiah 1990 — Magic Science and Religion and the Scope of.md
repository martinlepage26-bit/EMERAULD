# Magic, Science and Religion and the Scope of Rationality

## Citation
Tambiah, S.J., 1990. Magic, Science and Religion and the Scope of Rationality. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press.

## Annotation
Stanley Tambiah's Magic, Science and Religion and the Scope of Rationality examines how Western thought has categorized magic, science, and religion, and interrogates the concept of rationality across cultures, distinguishing “causality” from “participation” as modes of ordering reality. A leading anthropologist, Tambiah offers a nuanced comparative account. For this corpus it grounds the theoretical analysis of magic, rationality, and the boundaries between these categories.
