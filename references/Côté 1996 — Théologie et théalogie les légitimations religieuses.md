# Théologie et théalogie : les légitimations religieuses

## Citation
Côté, P. et Zylberberg, J. (1996). Théologie et théalogie : les légitimations religieuses

## Annotation
Côté and Zylberberg's article examines “theology and thealogy” and the religious legitimations at stake in feminist Goddess spirituality versus traditional theology, analyzing how each grounds religious authority. (The canonical citation is truncated mid-title.) It contributes to the francophone sociology of religion and the study of feminist spirituality. For this corpus it connects thealogy to questions of religious legitimation.
