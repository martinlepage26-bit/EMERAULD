# Frames of War: When Is Life Grievable? London: Verso

## Citation
Butler, J. (2009) Frames of War: When Is Life Grievable? London: Verso.

## Annotation
Judith Butler's Frames of War extends her earlier work on precarity and grievability, asking how the “frames” through which we apprehend life determine whose lives count as grievable and whose deaths are unmarked. Butler analyzes war, photography, and the differential distribution of vulnerability. The book is influential in political theory, ethics, and affect studies. For this corpus it grounds an account of recognition, precarity, and the politics of whose life matters.
