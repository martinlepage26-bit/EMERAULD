# Bias in Judicial Citations: A Window into the Behavior of Judges?

## Citation
Choi, S., Gulati, G. (2008). Bias in Judicial Citations: A Window into the Behavior of Judges?. The Journal of Legal Studies, 37(1), 87-130. https://doi.org/10.1086/588263

## Annotation
Choi and Gulati analyze bias in judicial citations as a window into judges' behavior, using citation patterns to infer how judges evaluate and rely on one another's work. Published in The Journal of Legal Studies, it contributes to empirical legal studies and the quantitative analysis of judicial behavior. Its relevance to this corpus is more peripheral, but it bears on questions of authority, evaluation, and bias in institutional decision-making.
