# Drawing down the moon : witches, druids, goddess-worshippers

## Citation
Adler, M. (1986). Drawing down the moon : witches, druids, goddess-worshippers

## Annotation
Margot Adler's Drawing Down the Moon is a foundational participant-observer survey of contemporary Neo-Paganism, Wicca, Druidry, and Goddess worship in the United States. First published in 1979 and revised in later editions, it maps the movement's beliefs, rituals, internal diversity, and self-understanding at a formative moment of growth. Adler, a journalist and practitioner, combines ethnographic breadth with an insider's sympathy, making the book both a primary and a secondary source. It remains one of the most cited overviews of modern Paganism. For studies of ritual and contemporary spirituality, it is a canonical baseline reference.
