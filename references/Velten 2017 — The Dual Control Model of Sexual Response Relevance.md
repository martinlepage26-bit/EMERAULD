## Annotation
Julia Velten's article reviews the Dual Control Model of sexual response, which frames sexual function in terms of the balance between sexual excitation and inhibition. Published in Current Sexual Health Reports, it belongs to the psychology of sexuality. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized clinical-psychology source.
