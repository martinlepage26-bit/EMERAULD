## Annotation
Hiroki Habuka's CSIS analysis examines Japan's “agile governance” approach to AI regulation and its influence on the 2023 G7 presidency and the Hiroshima AI process. It describes a light-touch, principles-and-disclosure model contrasting with the EU's binding rules. As a policy-analysis primary source, it documents an important national and multilateral governance approach. For this corpus it grounds comparative analysis of AI-governance models.
