# Consommation de substances en contexte sexuel chez des hommes gbHSH de Montréal : 2009-2016

## Citation
Blais, M., Otis, J., Lambert, G., Cox, J., et al. (2019). Consommation de substances en contexte sexuel chez des hommes gbHSH de Montréal : 2009-2016. Drogues, santé et société, 17(2), 76-94. https://doi.org/10.7202/1062117ar

## Annotation
Blais and colleagues examine substance use in sexual contexts among gay, bisexual, and other men who have sex with men in Montreal between 2009 and 2016, contributing to the francophone literature on chemsex and sexual health. The study documents patterns and trends in a specific urban population. Published in Drogues, santé et société, it belongs to public-health scholarship. For work on queer sexuality and health in this corpus, it offers situated Montreal-based evidence.
