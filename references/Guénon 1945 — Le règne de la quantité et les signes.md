# Le règne de la quantité et les signes des temps

## Citation
Guénon, René. 1945. Le règne de la quantité et les signes des temps. Paris: Gallimard.

## Annotation
René Guénon's Le règne de la quantité et les signes des temps is a central work of the Traditionalist school, offering a metaphysical critique of modernity as the “reign of quantity” — the reduction of quality, hierarchy, and the sacred to measurable, materialist uniformity — and reading contemporary decline through cyclical, esoteric doctrines. Highly influential in esoteric and Traditionalist circles, it is also, controversially, a reference for some far-right currents. For this corpus it is a primary source in Western esotericism and critiques of modern quantification.
