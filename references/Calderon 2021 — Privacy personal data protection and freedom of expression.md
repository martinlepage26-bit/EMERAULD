# Privacy, personal data protection, and freedom of expression under quarantine? The Peruvian experience

## Citation
Calderon, A., Gonzales, S., Ruiz, A. (2021). Privacy, personal data protection, and freedom of expression under quarantine? The Peruvian experience. International Data Privacy Law, 11(1), 48-62. https://doi.org/10.1093/idpl/ipab003

## Annotation
Calderón, Gonzales, and Ruiz analyze how privacy, personal-data protection, and freedom of expression were affected by Peru's pandemic quarantine measures, examining the tensions between public-health surveillance and civil liberties. Published in International Data Privacy Law, it is a case study in data governance under emergency conditions. For this corpus it grounds concerns about surveillance, data protection, and rights — directly relevant to governance debates.
