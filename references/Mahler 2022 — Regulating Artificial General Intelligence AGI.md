# Regulating Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)

## Citation
Mahler, T. (2022). Regulating Artificial General Intelligence (AGI). Information Technology and Law Series, 521-540. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-94-6265-523-2_26

## Annotation
Tobias Mahler's chapter “Regulating Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)” examines the legal and governance challenges of regulating hypothetical AI systems with broad, human-level capabilities, considering how existing frameworks might apply and where they fall short. Published in the Information Technology and Law Series, it contributes to AI-governance legal scholarship. For this corpus it grounds the specific regulatory problem posed by AGI as distinct from narrow AI.
