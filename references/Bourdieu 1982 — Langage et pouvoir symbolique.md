# Langage et pouvoir symbolique

## Citation
Bourdieu, P. (1982). Langage et pouvoir symbolique. Paris : Fayard.

## Annotation
Pierre Bourdieu's Langage et pouvoir symbolique gathers his writings on language as a medium of symbolic domination, showing how “legitimate” language and the authority to speak are socially produced and unequally distributed. Bourdieu links linguistic competence to power, markets, and misrecognition. The work is foundational for the sociology of language and symbolic power. (Closely related to, and the basis for, the English Language and Symbolic Power.)
