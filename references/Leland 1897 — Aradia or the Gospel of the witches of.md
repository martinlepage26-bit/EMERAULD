# Aradia, or the Gospel of the witches of Tuscany

## Citation
Leland, C. (1897). Aradia, or the Gospel of the witches of Tuscany. New York :

## Annotation
Charles Leland's Aradia, or the Gospel of the Witches (1899) presents material Leland claimed to have collected from a Tuscan witch tradition, including the figure of Aradia as a messianic goddess of the oppressed. Though its authenticity and sources are disputed, it profoundly influenced modern Wicca and the Goddess movement, notably the “Charge of the Goddess.” For this corpus it is a primary source in the textual genealogy of modern Witchcraft and Goddess spirituality.
