# Witchcraft today

## Citation
Gardner, G. B. (1954). Witchcraft today. London: Rider and Company.

## Annotation
Gerald Gardner's Witchcraft Today is the seminal 1954 work that publicly introduced modern Wicca, asserting the survival of an ancient witch-cult and catalyzing the Gardnerian tradition and the broader Pagan revival. Its historical thesis is rejected by scholars, but the book's role in founding contemporary Witchcraft is undisputed. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation form.)
