# Cultural bias and cultural alignment of large language models

## Citation
Tao, Y., Viberg, O., Baker, R. S., & Kizilcec, R. F. (2024). Cultural bias and cultural alignment of large language models. PNAS Nexus.

## Annotation
Tao, Viberg, Baker, and Kizilcec's “Cultural Bias and Cultural Alignment of Large Language Models” conducts a disaggregated evaluation of five widely used LLMs (the GPT family), comparing their outputs to nationally representative survey data and finding that all exhibit cultural values resembling English-speaking, Protestant-European countries. They test “cultural prompting” as a mitigation. Published in PNAS Nexus, it is a rigorous empirical study. For this corpus it grounds evidence that LLMs carry and universalize particular cultural values — a core AI-bias concern.
