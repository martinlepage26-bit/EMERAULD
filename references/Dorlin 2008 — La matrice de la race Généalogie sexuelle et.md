# La matrice de la race: Généalogie sexuelle et coloniale de la nation française

## Citation
Dorlin, E., 2008. La matrice de la race: Généalogie sexuelle et coloniale de la nation française. Paris: La Découverte.

## Annotation
Elsa Dorlin's La matrice de la race offers a sexual and colonial genealogy of the French nation, showing how race was co-produced with gender and sexuality through medical and political discourses of the body. Dorlin traces how ideas of health, sex, and reproduction underwrote racial and national hierarchies. It is a major work in francophone critical race and feminist theory. For this corpus it grounds an intersectional genealogy of race, gender, and nation.
