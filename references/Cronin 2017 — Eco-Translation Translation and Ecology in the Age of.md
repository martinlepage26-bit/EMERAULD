# Eco-Translation: Translation and Ecology in the Age of the Anthropocene

## Citation
Cronin, Michael (2017) Eco-Translation: Translation and Ecology in the Age of the Anthropocene. Routledge. doi: 10.4324/9781315689357

## Annotation
Michael Cronin's Eco-Translation examines translation through an ecological lens, arguing that translation practices and language politics are entangled with environmental crisis in the age of the Anthropocene. Cronin connects translation studies to ecology, sustainability, and the more-than-human. Published by Routledge, it is a notable contribution to the environmental humanities and translation theory. For this corpus it links language, translation, and Anthropocene/posthumanist themes.
