# The Ethics of Algorithms: Mapping the Debate.

## Citation
Mittelstadt, Brent Daniel, Patrick Allo, Mariarosaria Taddeo, Sandra Wachter, and Luciano Floridi. "The Ethics of Algorithms: Mapping the Debate." Big Data & Society 3, no. 2 (2016).

## Annotation
Mittelstadt, Allo, Taddeo, Wachter, and Floridi's “The Ethics of Algorithms: Mapping the Debate” is a foundational review that organizes the ethical concerns raised by algorithms into a framework — covering inconclusive, inscrutable, and misguided evidence, unfair outcomes, transformative effects, and traceability. It has become a standard map of the field. For this corpus it is a canonical structuring reference for the ethics and governance of algorithmic systems.
