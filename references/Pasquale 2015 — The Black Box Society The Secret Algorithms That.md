# The Black Box Society: The Secret Algorithms That Control Money and Information

## Citation
Pasquale, Frank. The Black Box Society: The Secret Algorithms That Control Money and Information. Cambridge: Harvard University Press, 2015.

## Annotation
Frank Pasquale's The Black Box Society is a landmark analysis of how opaque algorithms in finance, search, and reputation systems exercise enormous power over money and information while escaping scrutiny. Pasquale calls for an “intelligible society” with greater algorithmic accountability and transparency. It is a foundational text in critical algorithm studies and law. For this corpus it is a canonical account of algorithmic opacity and the case for accountability.
