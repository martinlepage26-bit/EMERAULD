# The New Production of Knowledge: The Dynamics of Science and Research in Contemporary Societies

## Citation
Gibbons, Michael, Camille Limoges, Helga Nowotny, Simon Schwartzman, Peter Scott, and Martin Trow. The New Production of Knowledge: The Dynamics of Science and Research in Contemporary Societies. London: Sage, 1994.

## Annotation
Gibbons and colleagues' The New Production of Knowledge is an influential analysis distinguishing “Mode 1” knowledge production (disciplinary, academic, curiosity-driven) from an emerging “Mode 2” (transdisciplinary, context-driven, application-oriented, socially accountable). The book reshaped science-policy debates about how knowledge is produced and governed. For this corpus it grounds analysis of changing modes of knowledge production and their governance — relevant to how AI research is organized.
