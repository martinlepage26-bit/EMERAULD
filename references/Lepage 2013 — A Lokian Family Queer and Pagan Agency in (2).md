# A Lokian Family: Queer and Pagan Agency in Montreal.

## Citation
Lepage, Martin. “A Lokian Family: Queer and Pagan Agency in Montreal.” Pomegranate 15, no. 1/2 (2013): 79–101.

## Annotation
Martin Lepage's “A Lokian Family: Queer and Pagan Agency in Montreal” (Pomegranate) studies Montreal devotees of Loki, analyzing how devotion to a gender-fluid deity supports queer and Pagan agency and alternative kinship. It is part of the author's research corpus on queer Montreal Paganism. (This entry duplicates the same article in a different citation form.)
