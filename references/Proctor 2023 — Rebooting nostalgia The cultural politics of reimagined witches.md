# Rebooting nostalgia: The cultural politics of reimagined witches

## Citation
Proctor, W., 2023. Rebooting nostalgia: The cultural politics of reimagined witches. Popular Culture Review, 34(1), pp.15–29.

## Annotation
William Proctor's “Rebooting Nostalgia” analyzes the cultural politics of reimagined witches in contemporary reboot culture, examining how updated witch figures negotiate nostalgia, gender, and reboot aesthetics. Published in Popular Culture Review, it contributes to media and witchcraft-in-popular-culture studies. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the analysis of the rebooted witch.
