# Detecting digital chameleons

## Citation
Bailenson, J., Yee, N., Patel, K., Beall, A. (2008). Detecting digital chameleons. Computers in Human Behavior, 24(1), 66-87. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chb.2007.01.015

## Annotation
Bailenson and colleagues report virtual-reality experiments on nonverbal mimicry — the “chameleon effect” — showing that people can detect and respond to digital agents that mimic their head movements. The study bears on social influence, embodiment, and trust in avatar-mediated interaction. Published in Computers in Human Behavior, it is an empirical contribution to research on virtual environments and human–agent interaction. For this corpus it connects embodiment and identity to computer-mediated and AI-adjacent contexts.
