# Digital Media and the Entrenchment of Right-Wing Populist Agendas

## Citation
Schroeder, R. (2019). Digital Media and the Entrenchment of Right-Wing Populist Agendas. Social Media + Society, 5(4). https://doi.org/10.1177/2056305119885328

## Annotation
Ralph Schroeder's article argues that discussions of social media's harms have overlooked how right-wing populists have used digital media legitimately and effectively to circumvent traditional gatekeepers, drawing on U.S. and Swedish cases to show divergent paths to electoral success. Published in Social Media + Society, it contributes to research on media and populism. For this corpus it grounds the analysis of how digital platforms enable populist mobilization — relevant to information and AI-mediated politics.
