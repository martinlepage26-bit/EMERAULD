# Stuttering in Film Media - Investigation of a Stereotype

## Citation
Evans, J., Williams, R. (2015). Stuttering in Film Media - Investigation of a Stereotype. Procedia - Social and Behavioral Sciences, 193, 337. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sbspro.2015.03.310

## Annotation
Evans and Williams investigate the stereotyping of stuttering in film media, analyzing how cinematic representations shape perceptions of people who stutter. Published as a Procedia conference paper, it belongs to the media-representation and communication-disorders literatures. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized source on media stereotypes of stuttering.
