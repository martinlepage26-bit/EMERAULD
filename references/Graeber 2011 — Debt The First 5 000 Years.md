# Debt: The First 5,000 Years

## Citation
Graeber, David. Debt: The First 5,000 Years. New York: Melville House, 2011.

## Annotation
David Graeber's Debt: The First 5,000 Years is a sweeping anthropological and historical account arguing that debt and credit — not barter — underlie the history of money and markets, and that debt is fundamentally a moral and political relation entangled with violence and hierarchy. The book was widely influential, including on debates around economic justice. For this corpus it grounds a critical anthropology of debt, obligation, and economic morality.
