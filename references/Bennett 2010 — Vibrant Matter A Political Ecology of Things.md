# Vibrant Matter: A Political Ecology of Things

## Citation
Bennett, J. (2010) Vibrant Matter: A Political Ecology of Things. Durham, NC: Duke University Press.

## Annotation
Jane Bennett's Vibrant Matter is a foundational text of new materialism that argues for the “thing-power” and agentic capacity of nonhuman matter. Drawing on Spinoza, Deleuze, and Latour, Bennett develops a “political ecology of things” in which assemblages of human and nonhuman actants distribute agency, with implications for ethics and environmental politics. The book has been widely influential across the environmental humanities and posthumanist theory. For this corpus it anchors an account of nonhuman agency and material vitality.
