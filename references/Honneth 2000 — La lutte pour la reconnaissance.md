# La lutte pour la reconnaissance

## Citation
Honneth, A. (2000). La lutte pour la reconnaissance. Paris: Cerf.

## Annotation
Axel Honneth's The Struggle for Recognition (here in French translation) is a foundational work of critical theory that reconstructs a theory of recognition across three spheres — love, rights, and solidarity — arguing that misrecognition (disrespect) is a central source of social conflict and moral development. It reshaped debates on justice, identity, and the “politics of recognition.” For this corpus it grounds the analysis of recognition, respect, and legitimacy in social and political life.
