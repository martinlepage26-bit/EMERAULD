# The Diversity of Life

## Citation
Wilson, Edward O. (1992) The Diversity of Life. Penguin.

## Annotation
Edward O. Wilson's The Diversity of Life is a landmark work of popular science by the founder of biodiversity studies, surveying the richness of life on Earth, the processes that generate it, and the mass extinction driven by human activity. It helped bring biodiversity and conservation to public attention. For this corpus it is a foundational source in ecological and Anthropocene thought.
