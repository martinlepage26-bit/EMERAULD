# Chemsex: origins of the word, a history of the phenomenon and a respect to the culture

## Citation
Stuart, D. (2019). Chemsex: origins of the word, a history of the phenomenon and a respect to the culture. Drugs and Alcohol Today, 19(1), 3-10. https://doi.org/10.1108/DAT-10-2018-0058

## Annotation
David Stuart's article traces the origins of the word “chemsex,” the history of the phenomenon, and a call to respect the culture, drawing on his pioneering work in gay men's sexual health. Published in Drugs and Alcohol Today, it is a foundational insider account. For work on queer sexuality and health in this corpus, it grounds the definition and cultural understanding of chemsex.
