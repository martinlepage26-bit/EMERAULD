# blue-ribbon

## Citation
LIMA, Cristiano, et DIMOLFETTA, David (2023). Lawmakers propose ‘blue-ribbon’ AI commission. The Washington Post, 20 juin. En ligne: https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2023/06/20/lawmakers-propose-blue-ribbon-ai-commission/.

## Annotation
This Washington Post article by Lima and Dimolfetta reports that U.S. lawmakers proposed a “blue-ribbon” commission to study and recommend AI regulation, documenting a specific legislative approach to AI governance. As a news primary source, it registers congressional efforts to structure AI policymaking. For this corpus it grounds the institutional politics of forming AI-governance bodies.
