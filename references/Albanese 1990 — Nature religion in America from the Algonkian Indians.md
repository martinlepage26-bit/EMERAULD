# Nature religion in America :from the Algonkian Indians to the

Albanese, C. (1990). Nature religion in America :from the Algonkian Indians to the

## Annotation
Catherine Albanese's Nature Religion in America traces a persistent but often overlooked strand of American religious life in which nature itself becomes the locus of the sacred. Ranging from Algonkian cosmologies through Transcendentalism to twentieth-century New Age and environmental spirituality, she argues that “nature religion” is a recurring American impulse cutting across traditions. The book is a landmark in American religious history and in the study of nature-based spirituality. For research on contemporary Paganism and ecological religion, it provides essential historical depth and an organizing analytic category.
