# The spiral dance : a rebirth of the ancient religion of the great

## Citation
Starhawk (1979). The spiral dance : a rebirth of the ancient religion of the great

## Annotation
Starhawk's The Spiral Dance is one of the founding texts of the modern Goddess and Reclaiming Witchcraft movements, presenting rituals, exercises, and a feminist theology of the Goddess and “power-from-within.” Enormously influential, it helped shape contemporary feminist Paganism. For this corpus it is a primary source at the origin of Reclaiming Witchcraft and Goddess spirituality.
