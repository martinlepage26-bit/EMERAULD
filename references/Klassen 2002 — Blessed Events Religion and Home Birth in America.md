# Blessed Events: Religion and Home Birth in America

## Citation
Klassen, C., 2002. Blessed Events: Religion and Home Birth in America. Princeton, NJ: Princeton University Press.

## Annotation
Chris Klassen's Blessed Events examines religion and the home-birth movement in America, analyzing how women invest childbirth with spiritual meaning and how home birth intersects with religious and feminist commitments. It contributes to the study of religion, gender, and the body. For this corpus it grounds the sacralization of reproduction and embodied ritual around birth.
