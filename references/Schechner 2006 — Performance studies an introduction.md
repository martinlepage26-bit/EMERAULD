# Performance studies : an introduction

## Citation
Schechner, R. (2006). Performance studies : an introduction. New York : Routledge.

## Annotation
Richard Schechner's Performance Studies: An Introduction is the foundational textbook of the field, defining performance broadly to encompass theatre, ritual, play, sports, and everyday life, and developing key concepts such as “restored behavior” and the performance/ritual continuum. It is a standard reference. For this corpus it grounds the analytic vocabulary linking ritual and performance.
