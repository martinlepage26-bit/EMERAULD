# From Eliza to XiaoIce: challenges and opportunities with social chatbots

## Citation
Shum, H., He, X., Li, D. (2018). From Eliza to XiaoIce: challenges and opportunities with social chatbots. Frontiers of Information Technology &amp; Electronic Engineering, 19(1), 10-26. https://doi.org/10.1631/FITEE.1700826

## Annotation
Shum, He, and Li's “From Eliza to XiaoIce” surveys the history and technical evolution of social chatbots, from the 1960s ELIZA to Microsoft's XiaoIce, examining the challenges and opportunities of designing conversational agents for emotional and social engagement. Published in Frontiers of Information Technology & Electronic Engineering, it is a substantive overview from industry researchers. For this corpus it grounds the development of conversational AI and social chatbots.
