## Annotation
The IEEE Global Initiative's Ethically Aligned Design (1st edition) is a major, consensus-driven framework articulating principles and recommendations for prioritizing human well-being in the design of autonomous and intelligent systems. It has been influential in shaping AI-ethics standards and professional practice. For this corpus it is a primary source in the standards-and-principles layer of AI governance.
