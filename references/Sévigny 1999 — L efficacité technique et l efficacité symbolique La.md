# L’efficacité technique et l’efficacité symbolique. La perspective des homéopathes.

## Citation
Sévigny, Odile. “L’efficacité technique et l’efficacité symbolique. La perspective des homéopathes.” Anthropologie et sociétés 23, no. 2 (1999): 41–60.

## Annotation
Odile Sévigny's study of homeopathic practice examines the interplay of technical and symbolic efficacy from practitioners' perspectives, interrogating the social meaning of alternative healing in modernity. Published in Anthropologie et sociétés, it is a reference in the anthropology of healing. (This entry duplicates the same article in a different citation form.)
