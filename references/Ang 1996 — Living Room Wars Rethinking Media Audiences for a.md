# Living Room Wars: Rethinking Media Audiences for a Postmodern World

## Citation
Ang, Ien. Living Room Wars: Rethinking Media Audiences for a Postmodern World. Routledge, 1996.

## Annotation
Ien Ang's Living Room Wars collects influential essays rethinking media audiences for a postmodern, fragmented media landscape. Ang critiques the industry's and the academy's attempts to “know” the audience as a manageable object, arguing that actual viewers are dispersed, contradictory, and resistant to measurement. The book is a key text in cultural studies and reception theory. For media-studies work in this corpus, it supplies a durable critique of audience research and the politics of representing viewers.
