# Model Cards for Model Reporting

## Citation
Mitchell, M., Wu, S., Zaldivar, A., Barnes, P., et al. (2019). Model Cards for Model Reporting. Proceedings of the Conference on Fairness, Accountability, and Transparency, 220-229. https://doi.org/10.1145/3287560.3287596

## Annotation
Mitchell and colleagues' “Model Cards for Model Reporting” (the FAccT conference version) formalizes model cards as a documentation standard requiring disclosure of a model's intended uses, disaggregated performance, limitations, and ethical considerations. It translates abstract accountability into an operational reporting practice. For this corpus it is a canonical reference on making model behavior legible and governable.
