# Human oversight of artificial intelligence: An operations management perspective

## Citation
Salgado-Criado, J. (2025). Human oversight of artificial intelligence: An operations management perspective. Journal of Industrial Engineering and Management.

## Annotation
Salgado-Criado analyzes human oversight of artificial intelligence from an operations-management perspective, drawing on cybernetic and control theory to propose a framework for how operational managers define oversight activities, and comparing it with managers' actual perceptions. Published in the Journal of Industrial Engineering and Management, it treats oversight as both a safety and a governance mechanism. For this corpus it grounds a practical, operations-focused account of the “human oversight” requirement central to AI governance.
