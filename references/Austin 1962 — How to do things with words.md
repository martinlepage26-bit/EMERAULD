# How to do things with words

## Citation
Austin, J. L. (1962). How to do things with words. Oxford : Clarendon Press.

## Annotation
J. L. Austin's How to Do Things with Words is the foundational text of speech-act theory, developed from his William James Lectures. Austin distinguishes constative utterances (which describe) from performatives (which act), then dissolves the distinction into the tripartite analysis of locutionary, illocutionary, and perlocutionary acts. The work reshaped philosophy of language and underwrites later theories of performativity in Butler and beyond. For this corpus it is the philosophical ground for treating governance statements, rituals, and declarations as actions rather than mere descriptions.
