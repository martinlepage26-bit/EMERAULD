## Annotation
Milène Hauri's commentary, from the Laboratoire de cyberjustice, analyzes Canada's Bill C-27 and its proposed Artificial Intelligence and Data Act, offering a legal-scholarly critique of the draft AI legislation. As an expert commentary, it contributes to the francophone debate on Canadian AI regulation. For this corpus it grounds critical legal analysis of AIDA.
