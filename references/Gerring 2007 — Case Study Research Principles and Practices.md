# Case Study Research: Principles and Practices

## Citation
Gerring, J. (2007) Case Study Research: Principles and Practices. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press.

## Annotation
John Gerring's Case Study Research: Principles and Practices is a leading methodological text that defines the case study, clarifies its logic and trade-offs relative to large-N methods, and offers guidance on case selection and inference. It is widely used across the social sciences. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the design and justification of case-study research.
