# Confessing society and epistemic injustice: Toward a queer crip analytic of knowledge production

## Citation
Baril, A. (2020). Confessing society and epistemic injustice: Toward a queer crip analytic of knowledge production. Hypatia, 35(1), 33–56. https://doi.org/10.1017/hyp.2019.31

## Annotation
Alexandre Baril's article brings together queer theory, crip theory, and epistemology to critique the “confessing society” and the epistemic injustices it produces for disabled and non-normative subjects. Baril argues for a queer-crip analytic that challenges dominant regimes of knowledge production and the compulsory disclosure of marginalized identities. Published in Hypatia, it contributes to feminist philosophy, trans studies, and disability studies. For this corpus it advances a critical account of knowledge, testimony, and epistemic injustice at the intersection of queerness and disability.
