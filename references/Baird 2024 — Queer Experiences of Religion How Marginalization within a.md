# Queer Experiences of Religion: How Marginalization within a Religion Affects Its Queer Members

## Citation
Baird, R., Hutchins, C., Kosanovich, S., Dabbs, C. (2024). Queer Experiences of Religion: How Marginalization within a Religion Affects Its Queer Members. Sexes, 5(4), 444-460. https://doi.org/10.3390/sexes5040032

## Annotation
Baird and colleagues use a multi-method study of 626 participants to examine how marginalization within a religion affects the psychological wellness and life satisfaction of its queer members. They find that queer status significantly affects distress and life satisfaction, with strength of faith and experiences of marginalization playing mediating and moderating roles. Published in Sexes, it contributes empirical evidence to research on religion, sexuality, and mental health. For this corpus it grounds the study of queer religious experience in quantitative data.
