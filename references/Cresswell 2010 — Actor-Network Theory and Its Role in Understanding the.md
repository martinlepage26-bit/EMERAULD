# Actor-Network Theory and Its Role in Understanding the Implementation of Information Technology Developments in Healthcare

## Citation
Cresswell, Kathrin M., Worth, Allison, Sheikh, Aziz (2010) Actor-Network Theory and Its Role in Understanding the Implementation of Information Technology Developments in Healthcare. BMC Medical Informatics and Decision Making, 10(1), pp. 67. doi: 10.1186/1472-6947-10-67

## Annotation
Cresswell, Worth, and Sheikh apply Actor-Network Theory to understand the implementation of information-technology developments in healthcare, showing how socio-technical networks of people, devices, and practices shape whether health IT succeeds. Published in BMC Medical Informatics and Decision Making, it is a useful applied-ANT reference. For this corpus it connects STS network analysis to the concrete implementation of technology in institutions.
