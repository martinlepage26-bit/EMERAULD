## Annotation
Sven Erlandson's Spiritual but Not Religious is an early popular articulation and advocacy of the SBNR stance, calling for a “religious revolution” beyond institutional religion toward personal spirituality. As a primary source, it exemplifies the discourse the SBNR category names. For this corpus it documents the self-understanding of spiritual-but-not-religious identity from within.
