# Causal mechanisms in the social sciences

## Citation
Hedström, P. and Ylikoski, P. (2010) ‘Causal mechanisms in the social sciences’, Annual Review of Sociology, 36(1), pp. 49–67. doi: 10.1146/annurev.soc.012809.102632.

## Annotation
Hedström and Ylikoski's “Causal Mechanisms in the Social Sciences” is an authoritative review of mechanism-based explanation, relating it to causation and the covering-law model in philosophy of science and to analytical sociology. They argue that explaining social phenomena means identifying the mechanisms that produce them, not merely establishing correlations. Published in the Annual Review of Sociology, it is a standard methodological reference. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds mechanism-based causal explanation.
