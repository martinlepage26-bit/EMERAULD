# The Wicked Nature of AGI

## Citation
Doker, Y. (2025). The Wicked Nature of AGI. Law, Technology and Humans. https://doi.org/10.5204/lthj.3757

## Annotation
Doker's article frames artificial general intelligence as a “wicked problem” — one whose complexity, contested definitions, and shifting stakes resist tidy technical or regulatory solutions. Published in Law, Technology and Humans, it contributes to legal and governance scholarship on AGI. For this corpus it grounds the argument that governing advanced AI is a structurally wicked challenge rather than a well-defined engineering task.
