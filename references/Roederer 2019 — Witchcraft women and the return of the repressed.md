# Witchcraft, women, and the return of the repressed in popular film

## Citation
Roederer, C., 2019. Witchcraft, women, and the return of the repressed in popular film. Journal of Religion and Film, 23(2), pp.78–92.

## Annotation
Christine Roederer's article examines witchcraft, women, and the “return of the repressed” in popular film, using psychoanalytic and feminist lenses to analyze cinematic witches. Published in the Journal of Religion and Film, it contributes to media and witchcraft-in-popular-culture studies. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the psychoanalytic reading of the witch on screen.
