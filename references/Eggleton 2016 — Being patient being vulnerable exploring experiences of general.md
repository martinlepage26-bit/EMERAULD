# Being patient, being vulnerable: exploring experiences of general practice waiting rooms through elicited drawings

## Citation
Eggleton, K., Kearns, R., Neuwelt, P. (2016). Being patient, being vulnerable: exploring experiences of general practice waiting rooms through elicited drawings. Social &amp; Cultural Geography, 18(7), 971-993. https://doi.org/10.1080/14649365.2016.1228114

## Annotation
Eggleton, Kearns, and Neuwelt explore experiences of general-practice waiting rooms through participant-elicited drawings, examining vulnerability, patience, and the felt experience of clinical space. Published in Social & Cultural Geography, it contributes both to health geography and to arts-based qualitative methods. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the use of visual, drawing-based elicitation.
