# Superintelligence: Fears, Promises and Potentials

## Citation
Goertzel, B. (2015). Superintelligence: Fears, Promises and Potentials. Journal of Ethics and Emerging Technologies, 25(2), 55-87. https://doi.org/10.55613/jeet.v25i2.48

## Annotation
Ben Goertzel's “Superintelligence: Fears, Promises and Potentials” surveys the debate over machine superintelligence, weighing catastrophic-risk arguments against optimistic potentials. Published in the Journal of Ethics and Emerging Technologies, it contributes a researcher's perspective to the superintelligence discourse (in dialogue with Bostrom and others). For this corpus it grounds the long-horizon risk-and-promise framing of advanced AI.
