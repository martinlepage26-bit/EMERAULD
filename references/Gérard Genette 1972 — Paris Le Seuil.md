# Paris, Le Seuil

## Citation
Gérard Genette, Figure III. Paris, Le Seuil, 1972.

## Annotation
Gérard Genette's Figures III (containing “Discours du récit”) is a foundational work of structuralist narratology, systematizing the analysis of narrative through categories of order, duration, frequency, mood, and voice, and distinguishing story, narrative, and narration. It became the standard toolkit for narrative analysis. For narrative-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the formal study of how stories are told.
