# A passage to anthropology : between experience and theory

## Citation
Hastrup, K. (1995). A passage to anthropology : between experience and theory.

## Annotation
Kirsten Hastrup's A Passage to Anthropology reflects on the relationship between experience and theory in anthropological knowledge, developing a reflexive account of how fieldwork experience becomes ethnographic understanding. It is a notable contribution to the theory of anthropological method. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds reflexive reflection on experience and theory in ethnography.
