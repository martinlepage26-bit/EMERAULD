# Les usages anthropologiques de la magie: essai de typologie

## Citation
Keck, F., 2002. Les usages anthropologiques de la magie: essai de typologie. L’Homme, 164(4), pp.141–162.

## Annotation
Frédéric Keck's “Les usages anthropologiques de la magie” proposes a typology of how anthropology has used the category of magic, clarifying the different analytic roles the concept has played. Published in L'Homme, it is a valuable conceptual mapping. For this corpus it disciplines the anthropological category of magic and its uses.
