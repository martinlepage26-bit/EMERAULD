# Le phénomène rituel : structure et contre-structure

## Citation
Turner, V. (1990). Le phénomène rituel : structure et contre-structure. Paris : Presses

## Annotation
Victor Turner's The Ritual Process (here in French translation) is a foundational work of ritual theory, developing the concepts of “liminality” (the ambiguous threshold phase of rites of passage) and “communitas” (the intense, egalitarian bond experienced in that phase). Building on van Gennep, Turner reshaped the anthropology of ritual. For this corpus it is a canonical theoretical anchor for understanding ritual structure, transition, and anti-structure.
