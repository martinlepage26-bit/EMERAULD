# Negotiating Gender Essentialism in Contemporary Paganism

## Citation
Oboler, R. (2011). Negotiating Gender Essentialism in Contemporary Paganism. Pomegranate: The International Journal of Pagan Studies, 12(2). https://doi.org/10.1558/pome.v12i2.159

## Annotation
Regina Oboler's “Negotiating Gender Essentialism in Contemporary Paganism” examines how Pagans grapple with essentialist ideas about gender — such as polarity between “masculine” and “feminine” energies — and how practitioners negotiate, resist, or rework them. Published in Pomegranate, it is a key study of gender debate within Paganism. For this corpus it grounds the negotiation of gender essentialism in Pagan practice.
