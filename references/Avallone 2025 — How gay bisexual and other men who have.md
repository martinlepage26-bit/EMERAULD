# How gay, bisexual and other men who have sex with men living with HIV experience sexual dysfunction: a thematic analysis of interviews in Montreal, Canada

## Citation
Avallone, F., Engler, K., Hickson, F., Cox, J., et al. (2025). How gay, bisexual and other men who have sex with men living with HIV experience sexual dysfunction: a thematic analysis of interviews in Montreal, Canada. Sexual Health, 22(6). https://doi.org/10.1071/SH25150

## Annotation
This qualitative study uses reflexive thematic analysis of interviews with 31 Montreal-based gay, bisexual, and other men who have sex with men living with HIV to explore the perceived causes and effects of sexual dysfunction. The authors document how reduced libido, erectile difficulties, and related problems are experienced and interpreted within this population, an issue they argue is prevalent yet under-addressed. Published in Sexual Health, it contributes to research on HIV, sexuality, and men's sexual well-being. For work on queer embodiment it offers a situated, Montreal-based empirical account.
