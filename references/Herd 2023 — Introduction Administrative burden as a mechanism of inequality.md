# Introduction: Administrative burden as a mechanism of inequality in policy design

## Citation
Herd, P. et al. (2023) ‘Introduction: Administrative burden as a mechanism of inequality in policy design’, RSF: The Russell Sage Foundation Journal of the Social Sciences, 9(5).

## Annotation
Herd and colleagues' introduction frames administrative burden as a mechanism of inequality embedded in policy design, showing how burdens fall unevenly and reproduce disadvantage. Published in the RSF Journal, it extends the administrative-burden research program to questions of equity. For this corpus it grounds the analysis of how procedural design produces unequal outcomes — relevant to automated eligibility and access systems.
