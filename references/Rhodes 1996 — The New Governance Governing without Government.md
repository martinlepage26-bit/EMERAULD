# The New Governance: Governing without Government.

## Citation
Rhodes, R.A.W. "The New Governance: Governing without Government." Political Studies 44, no. 4 (1996): 652–667.

## Annotation
R. A. W. Rhodes's “The New Governance: Governing without Government” is a foundational article defining “governance” as self-organizing, interorganizational networks that operate with significant autonomy from the state — a “hollowing out” of central government. It reshaped public-administration theory. For this corpus it is highly relevant, providing a canonical account of networked, post-sovereign governance that informs how distributed governance (including of AI) is theorized.
