# Des rituels contemporains de première menstruation

## Citation
Houseman, M. (2010). Des rituels contemporains de première menstruation.

## Annotation
Michael Houseman's article describes and analyzes contemporary first-menstruation rituals in the West, arguing that unlike female initiations elsewhere they do not mark accession to adult status or procreative recognition but instead furnish an experience of an original feminine identity — a spiritual being with creative capacities — consecrating the girl as an independent agent within a community of women. Published in a francophone venue, it contributes to the anthropology of ritual and gender. For this corpus it grounds the study of invented, gendered contemporary ritual.
