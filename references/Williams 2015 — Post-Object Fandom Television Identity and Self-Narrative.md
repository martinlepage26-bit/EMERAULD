# Post-Object Fandom: Television, Identity and Self-Narrative

## Citation
Williams, Rebecca. Post-Object Fandom: Television, Identity and Self-Narrative. Bloomsbury, 2015.

## Annotation
Rebecca Williams's Post-Object Fandom examines what happens to fan communities and identities after the object of fandom (e.g., a television series) ends, analyzing mourning, self-narrative, and the ongoing work of identity. It is a key text in fan studies. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the analysis of fandom, loss, and identity after a show concludes.
