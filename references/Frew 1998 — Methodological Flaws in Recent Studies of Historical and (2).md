# Methodological Flaws in Recent Studies of Historical and Modern Witchcraft.

## Citation

## Annotation
Don Frew's “Methodological Flaws in Recent Studies of Historical and Modern Witchcraft” challenges the evidentiary and interpretive methods of several academic accounts of witchcraft, contributing an insider-scholar perspective to historiographical debate.
