# Interview with the creative team

## Citation
Allie, Scott. Interview with the creative team. Buffy the Vampire Slayer: Season Ten—New Rules Deluxe Edition, Dark Horse, 2014.

## Annotation
This interview with editor Scott Allie and the creative team accompanies the deluxe edition of Buffy the Vampire Slayer: Season Ten, part of the Dark Horse comics that continue the television series as canonical “seasons.” As a paratext, it documents authorial intent, production decisions, and the franchise's ongoing world-building. It is a primary source for fan studies and media scholarship on the Buffy transmedia franchise. For work on popular culture and media analysis, it offers behind-the-scenes evidence of how a cult property manages continuity and audience expectations.
