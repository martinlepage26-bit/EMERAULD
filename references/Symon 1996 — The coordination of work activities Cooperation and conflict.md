# The coordination of work activities: Cooperation and conflict in a hospital context

## Citation
Symon, G., Long, K., Ellis, J. (1996). The coordination of work activities: Cooperation and conflict in a hospital context. Computer Supported Cooperative Work (CSCW), 5(1), 1-31. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00141934

## Annotation
Symon, Long, and Ellis examine the coordination of work activities in a hospital, analyzing cooperation and conflict among healthcare workers. Published in Computer Supported Cooperative Work, it contributes to CSCW and the sociology of work. For this corpus it grounds the study of coordination and conflict in complex organizational settings.
