# Artificial Hells: Participatory Art and the Politics of Spectatorship

## Citation
Bishop, Claire. 2012. Artificial Hells: Participatory Art and the Politics of Spectatorship. London: Verso.

## Annotation
Claire Bishop's Artificial Hells is a major critical history of participatory and socially engaged art, interrogating the politics of spectatorship, collaboration, and the “social turn.” Bishop challenges the assumption that participation is inherently ethical or democratic, insisting on aesthetic as well as political judgment. The book is influential in art history and cultural theory. Its place in this corpus is more peripheral, but it offers a rigorous account of participation, spectatorship, and the politics of the aesthetic.
