# Gender Trouble: Feminism and the Subversion of Identity

## Citation
Butler, Judith. Gender Trouble: Feminism and the Subversion of Identity. New York: Routledge, 1990.

## Annotation
Judith Butler's Gender Trouble is one of the founding texts of queer theory, arguing that gender is performative — produced through the stylized repetition of acts rather than expressing a prior inner essence — and that the categories of sex, gender, and desire are effects of regulatory regimes. Butler's critique of the heterosexual matrix and call to subvert identity through parody (e.g., drag) reshaped feminist and queer thought. It is among the most cited works in the humanities. For this corpus it is the cornerstone reference on gender performativity.
