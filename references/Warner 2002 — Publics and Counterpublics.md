# Publics and Counterpublics

## Citation
Warner, Michael. *Publics and Counterpublics*. New York: Zone Books, 2002. Distributed by MIT Press. ISBN 978-1-890951-28-3.

Essay version (abridged title essay): Warner, Michael. 2002. “Publics and Counterpublics.” *Public Culture* 14 (1): 49–90.

## Annotation
Warner distinguishes *the* public (social totality) from *a* public (self-organized through discourse and circulation among strangers). Counterpublics are subordinated or alternative publics that maintain awareness of their subordinate status. Installed for *Fluency After Fracture* Tier C (2026-08-07).
