# The Routledge History of Witchcraft

## Citation
Bever, E. and Styer, R., 2017. The Routledge History of Witchcraft. London: Routledge.

## Annotation
Edited by Edward Bever and Randall Styers, The Routledge History of Witchcraft is a comprehensive scholarly survey covering witchcraft across periods and regions, from ancient and medieval contexts through the early-modern trials to modern and contemporary forms. Its expert contributors synthesize current historiography on belief, persecution, and practice. It serves as an authoritative reference in the history of witchcraft. For this corpus it anchors historical depth on witchcraft as a scholarly subject.
