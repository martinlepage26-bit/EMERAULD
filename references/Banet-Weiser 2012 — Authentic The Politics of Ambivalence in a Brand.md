# Authentic™: The Politics of Ambivalence in a Brand Culture

## Citation
Banet-Weiser, Sarah. Authentic™: The Politics of Ambivalence in a Brand Culture. New York UP, 2012.

## Annotation
Sarah Banet-Weiser's Authentic™ analyzes how “authenticity” has become a central value and marketing logic within contemporary brand culture. She argues that brands increasingly colonize domains once seen as outside the market — self, politics, religion, community — by promising authentic experience, producing an ambivalent politics. The book is influential in media studies, cultural studies, and critical marketing. For this corpus it offers a sharp account of how authenticity and branding structure contemporary culture and identity.
