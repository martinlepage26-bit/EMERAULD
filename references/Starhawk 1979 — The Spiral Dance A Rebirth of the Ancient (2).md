# The Spiral Dance: A Rebirth of the Ancient Religion of the Great Goddess

## Citation
Starhawk, 1979. The Spiral Dance: A Rebirth of the Ancient Religion of the Great Goddess. San Francisco, CA: Harper & Row.

## Annotation
Starhawk's The Spiral Dance presents the beliefs, rituals, and feminist theology of Goddess-centered Witchcraft, becoming a foundational and best-selling text of the modern Pagan and Goddess movements. It is a key primary source in feminist Paganism. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation form.)
