# An Auto-Ethnographic Study of the Disembodied Experience of a Novice Researcher Doing Qualitative Cancer Research

## Citation
Benoot, C., Bilsen, J. (2015). An Auto-Ethnographic Study of the Disembodied Experience of a Novice Researcher Doing Qualitative Cancer Research. Qualitative Health Research, 26(4), 482-489. https://doi.org/10.1177/1049732315616625

## Annotation
Benoot and Bilsen present an autoethnographic account of a novice researcher's “disembodied” experience while conducting qualitative cancer research, reflecting on emotional labor, distance, and embodiment in fieldwork. The article contributes to methodological literature on reflexivity, researcher positionality, and the affective dimensions of qualitative inquiry. Published in Qualitative Health Research, it is a useful case for thinking about the researcher's body and emotions in the field. For method-focused work in this corpus, it foregrounds reflexivity and embodiment.
