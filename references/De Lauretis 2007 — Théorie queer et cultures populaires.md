# Théorie queer et cultures populaires

## Citation
De Lauretis, T. (2007). Théorie queer et cultures populaires. De Foucault à

## Annotation
Teresa de Lauretis's Théorie queer et cultures populaires (translated by Marie-Hélène Bourcier) collects the influential theorist's work bringing queer theory to bear on popular culture, cinema, and desire, ranging “from Foucault to Cronenberg.” De Lauretis is credited with coining “queer theory,” and this francophone collection was important for its French reception. For this corpus it grounds queer theory's engagement with popular culture and its francophone dissemination.
