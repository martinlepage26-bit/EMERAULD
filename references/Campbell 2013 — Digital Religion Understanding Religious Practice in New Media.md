# Digital Religion: Understanding Religious Practice in New Media Worlds

## Citation
Campbell, Heidi A. 2013. Digital Religion: Understanding Religious Practice in New Media Worlds. London: Routledge.

## Annotation
Heidi Campbell's Digital Religion is a foundational text mapping how religious practice, community, and authority are reconfigured in networked, new-media environments. Campbell develops frameworks such as the “networked religion” and studies how offline and online religious life interpenetrate. The book is a standard reference in the study of religion and digital media. For this corpus it grounds analysis of religion, ritual, and authority in digital contexts.
