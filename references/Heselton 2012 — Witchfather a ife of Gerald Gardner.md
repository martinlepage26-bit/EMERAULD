# Witchfather: a !ife of Gerald Gardner

## Citation
Heselton, P. (2012). Witchfather: a !ife of Gerald Gardner. Volume 1 : Into the witch

## Annotation
Philip Heselton's Witchfather is a detailed biography of Gerald Gardner, the founder of modern Wicca, drawing on extensive research to reconstruct his life and the origins of the Craft. This first volume covers Gardner's early life and initiation. It is a key reference for the history of Wicca. For this corpus it grounds the biographical and historical origins of modern Witchcraft.
