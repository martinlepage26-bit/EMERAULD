# The goddess and her icon: Body and mind in the era of artificial intelligence

## Citation
Zarkadakis, G. (2024). The goddess and her icon: Body and mind in the era of artificial intelligence. AI & Society. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00146-023-01764-4

## Annotation
George Zarkadakis’s article in AI & Society uses the ancient relationship between a goddess and her cult icon as a lens on contemporary artificial intelligence, exploring how humans invest artifacts with mind, agency, and the sacred. The piece connects the projection of interiority onto AI systems to a much older religious and cognitive tendency to animate representations. Drawing on the mind–body problem and the history of icons, it treats AI less as a purely technical development than as the latest expression of a human impulse to externalize and venerate mind. The argument bridges AI-governance debates with the anthropology of religion and philosophy of mind. For a bibliography spanning AI, ritual, and embodiment, it is a useful boundary object linking the technical and the sacred.
