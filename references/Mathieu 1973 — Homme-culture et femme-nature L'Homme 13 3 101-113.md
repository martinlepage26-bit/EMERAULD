# Homme-culture et femme-nature? L'Homme, 13(3), 101-113

## Citation
Mathieu, N.-C. (1973). Homme-culture et femme-nature? L'Homme, 13(3), 101-113.

## Annotation
Nicole-Claude Mathieu's “Homme-culture et femme-nature?” is a landmark francophone feminist-anthropology essay critiquing the symbolic mapping of men onto culture and women onto nature, exposing it as an ideological construction rather than a natural fact. Published in L'Homme, it is foundational for French materialist feminism. For this corpus it grounds the critique of the nature/culture gendered dualism.
