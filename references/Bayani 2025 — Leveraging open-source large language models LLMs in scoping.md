# Leveraging open-source large language models (LLMs) in scoping reviews: a case study on disability and AI applications

## Citation
Bayani, Azadeh, Epoh Ewane, Leandre Parfait, Oliveira Dos Anjos, Davllyn Santos, Mac-Seing, Muriel, Nikiema, Jean Noel (2025) Leveraging open-source large language models (LLMs) in scoping reviews: a case study on disability and AI applications. International journal of medical informatics, 204, 106048. doi: 10.1016/j.ijmedinf.2025.106048

## Annotation
Bayani and colleagues present a case study on using open-source large language models to assist scoping reviews, focusing on disability and AI applications. They assess the feasibility, benefits, and limitations of LLMs in evidence-synthesis workflows within a medical-informatics context. Published in the International Journal of Medical Informatics, it contributes to research on AI-assisted review methods. For this corpus it connects LLM tooling to research methodology and disability studies.
