# Les fées dansent sur le Mont-Royal

## Citation
Gauthier, F., Lepage, M. (1969). Les fées dansent sur le Mont-Royal. Magie et négociations queer chez les néo-païens de Montréal. Anthropologica, 58(2), 264-276. https://doi.org/10.3138/anth.582.A02

## Annotation
Gauthier and Lepage's “Les fées dansent sur le Mont-Royal” examines magic and queer negotiations among Montreal neo-Pagans, showing how practitioners work out gender and sexuality through ritual practice. Published in Anthropologica, it contributes to Pagan studies and the anthropology of queer ritual. (Flag: the canonical entry lists the year as 1969, which is erroneous — the article appeared in Anthropologica 58(2), 2016.) For this corpus it is a key source on queer ritual negotiation in Montreal Paganism.
