# Masculinity and Popular Television

## Citation
Feasey, Rebecca. Masculinity and Popular Television. Edinburgh UP, 2008.

## Annotation
Rebecca Feasey's Masculinity and Popular Television surveys how contemporary television constructs and negotiates masculinity across genres, from sport and sitcoms to drama and reality TV. Feasey examines the range of masculine identities on offer and their ideological work. It is a useful reference in media and gender studies. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the analysis of televised masculinity.
