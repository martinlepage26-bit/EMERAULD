# Living a Charmed Life: The Magic of Postfeminist Sisterhood.

## Citation
Sanders, Hannah E. 2014. “Living a Charmed Life: The Magic of Postfeminist Sisterhood.” In Interrogating Postfeminism: Gender and the Politics of Popular Culture, edited by Yvonne Tasker and Diane Negra, 161–178. Durham, NC: Duke University Press.

## Annotation
Hannah Sanders's “Living a Charmed Life” analyzes the television series Charmed as an emblem of “postfeminist sisterhood,” examining how its magical sisters embody the promises and contradictions of postfeminist culture. Published in Interrogating Postfeminism (eds. Tasker and Negra), it connects witchcraft media to postfeminist critique. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the postfeminist reading of the televisual witch.
