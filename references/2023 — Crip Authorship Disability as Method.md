## Annotation
Crip Authorship: Disability as Method (NYU Press, 2023, edited by Mara Mills and Rebecca Sanchez) gathers essays treating disability as a generative method for authorship, publishing, and knowledge-making across media. (The canonical entry lacks editor names.) It is a significant contribution to disability studies. For this corpus it grounds disability-as-method in scholarship and creative work.
