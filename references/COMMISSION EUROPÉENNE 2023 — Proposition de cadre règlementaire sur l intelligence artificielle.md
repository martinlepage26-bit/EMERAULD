# Proposition de cadre règlementaire sur l’intelligence artificielle

## Citation
COMMISSION EUROPÉENNE (2023). Proposition de cadre règlementaire sur l’intelligence artificielle. Bâtir l’avenir numérique de l’Europe, 20 juin. En ligne: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/fr/policies/regulatory-framework-ai.

## Annotation
This European Commission page presents the EU's proposed regulatory framework for artificial intelligence as part of building Europe's digital future, outlining the risk-based approach that became the AI Act. As an official policy source, it documents the EU's flagship AI-governance initiative. For this corpus it is a primary reference for the EU's regulatory model.
