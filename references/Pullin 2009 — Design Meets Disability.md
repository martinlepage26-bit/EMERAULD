# Design Meets Disability

## Citation
Pullin, Graham (2009) Design Meets Disability. Cambridge, MA: The MIT Press.

## Annotation
Graham Pullin's Design Meets Disability argues that disability can be a productive driver of good design, challenging the split between medical “aids” and mainstream design and advocating for inclusive, expressive design informed by disabled people's needs and aesthetics. Published by MIT Press, it is an influential text at the intersection of design and disability. For this corpus it grounds disability-centered design thinking — relevant to how technologies are made for diverse bodies.
