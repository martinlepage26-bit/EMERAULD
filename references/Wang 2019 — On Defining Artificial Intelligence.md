# On Defining Artificial Intelligence

## Citation
Wang, P. (2019). On Defining Artificial Intelligence. Journal of Artificial General Intelligence, 10(2), 1-37. https://doi.org/10.2478/jagi-2019-0002

## Annotation
Pei Wang's “On Defining Artificial Intelligence” critically surveys the many competing definitions of AI, arguing for conceptual clarity about what “intelligence” means and defending a working definition centered on adaptation under limited resources. Published in the Journal of Artificial General Intelligence, it is an influential conceptual contribution. For this corpus it grounds the foundational and consequential problem of defining AI — relevant to how AI is scoped in governance.
