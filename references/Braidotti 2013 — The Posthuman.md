# The Posthuman

## Citation
Braidotti, R. (2013) The Posthuman. Cambridge: Polity Press.

## Annotation
Rosi Braidotti's The Posthuman is a foundational statement of critical posthumanism, arguing for a nomadic, materialist, and non-anthropocentric subject in the wake of humanism's crisis and the blurring of human/nonhuman boundaries. Braidotti develops the “posthuman convergence” of the post-humanist and post-anthropocentric, with implications for ethics, subjectivity, and the university. The book is central to the posthumanities and new materialism. For this corpus it anchors posthumanist theory and its ethical-political stakes.
