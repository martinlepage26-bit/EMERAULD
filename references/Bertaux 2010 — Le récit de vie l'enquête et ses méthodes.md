# Le récit de vie: l'enquête et ses méthodes

## Citation
Bertaux, D. (2010). Le récit de vie: l'enquête et ses méthodes. Paris: Armand Colin.

## Annotation
Daniel Bertaux's Le récit de vie is a classic French methodological guide to the life-story (biographical) method in sociology, setting out how to gather and analyze narrated life histories to reconstruct social processes and trajectories. Bertaux advocates an “ethnosociological” use of life stories to move from individual accounts to structural patterns. Widely used in francophone social science, it is a standard reference. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the disciplined use of biographical narrative as evidence.
