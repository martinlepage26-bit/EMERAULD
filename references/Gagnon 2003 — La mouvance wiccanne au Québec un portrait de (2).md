# La mouvance wiccanne au Québec: un portrait de la sorcellerie contemporaine.

## Citation
Gagnon, Myriam. “La mouvance wiccanne au Québec: un portrait de la sorcellerie contemporaine.” MA thesis, Université Laval, 2003.

## Annotation
Myriam Gagnon's thesis, “La mouvance wiccanne au Québec,” portrays contemporary witchcraft and the Wiccan movement in Québec, examining its adherents and practices. It is a francophone contribution to the study of modern Paganism. (This entry duplicates the same thesis in a different citation form.)
