# States of Knowledge: The Co-Production of Science and Social Order

## Citation
Jasanoff, Sheila, ed. States of Knowledge: The Co-Production of Science and Social Order. London: Routledge, 2004.

## Annotation
Edited by Sheila Jasanoff, States of Knowledge develops the “co-production” idiom in STS — the claim that the production of scientific knowledge and the making of social order are mutually constitutive. The volume's essays show how facts and social arrangements emerge together. It is a foundational STS reference. For this corpus it grounds the co-productionist view that technologies (including AI) and social order are made together.
