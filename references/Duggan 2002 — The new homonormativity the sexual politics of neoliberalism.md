# The new homonormativity : the sexual politics of neoliberalism

## Citation
Duggan, L. (2002). The new homonormativity : the sexual politics of neoliberalism.

## Annotation
Lisa Duggan's “The New Homonormativity” is a foundational essay coining the concept of homonormativity — a politics that does not contest dominant heteronormative institutions but upholds them while promising a privatized, depoliticized gay constituency anchored in domesticity and consumption. Duggan situates this within the sexual politics of neoliberalism. The essay is central to queer critiques of assimilation. For this corpus it grounds analysis of how neoliberalism reshapes sexual politics.
