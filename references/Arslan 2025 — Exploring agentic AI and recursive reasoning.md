# Exploring agentic AI and recursive reasoning

## Citation
Arslan, A. (2025). Exploring agentic AI and recursive reasoning. International Journal of Applied Science and Research, 8(3). https://doi.org/10.56293/IJASR.2025.6505

## Annotation
Arslan provides an architectural account of how agentic and recursive AI systems differ from isolated, single-shot models, foregrounding memory, coordination, multi-step planning, and emergent behavior. The article frames these capacities as governance complications: process supervision becomes more important than inspecting any single output. It functions as a bridge from the AI-anxiety literature toward the governance of recursive workflows. For this corpus it supplies the technical vocabulary connecting agentic architecture to oversight requirements.
