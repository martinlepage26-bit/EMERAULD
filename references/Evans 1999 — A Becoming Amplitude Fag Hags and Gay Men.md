# A Becoming Amplitude: Fag Hags and Gay Men in Fiction and in Theory

## Citation
Evans, Nicola (1999) A Becoming Amplitude: Fag Hags and Gay Men in Fiction and in Theory. Discourse, 21(2), 21-46.

## Annotation
Nicola Evans's “A Becoming Amplitude” examines the figure of the “fag hag” and the relationships between gay men and their women friends in fiction and theory, interrogating how these bonds are represented and theorized. Published in Discourse, it contributes to queer cultural theory and the study of cross-gender intimacy. For this corpus it grounds analysis of queer sociality and its cultural representation.
