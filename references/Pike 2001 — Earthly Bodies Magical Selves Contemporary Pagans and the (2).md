# Earthly Bodies, Magical Selves: Contemporary Pagans and the Search for Community

## Citation
Pike, Sarah. Earthly Bodies, Magical Selves: Contemporary Pagans and the Search for Community. Berkeley: University of California Press, 2001.

## Annotation
Sarah Pike's Earthly Bodies, Magical Selves is an ethnography of American Neo-Pagan festivals, examining how participants forge selfhood, community, and the sacred through embodied ritual in temporary festival settings. It is a key work in Pagan studies. (This entry duplicates the same book listed elsewhere in the canonical list.) For this corpus it grounds embodiment and community in Pagan festival culture.
