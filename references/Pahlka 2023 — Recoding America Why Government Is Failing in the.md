# Recoding America: Why Government Is Failing in the Digital Age and How We Can Do Better

## Citation
Pahlka, J. (2023) Recoding America: Why Government Is Failing in the Digital Age and How We Can Do Better. New York: Metropolitan Books.

## Annotation
Jennifer Pahlka's Recoding America argues that American government fails in the digital age not for lack of technology but because of layered processes, procurement, and cultures that separate policy from implementation. Drawing on her experience founding Code for America and in the U.S. Digital Service, Pahlka calls for delivery-driven, user-centered government. For this corpus it is highly relevant to governance: a practitioner account of why public institutions struggle to build and run digital (and AI) systems well.
