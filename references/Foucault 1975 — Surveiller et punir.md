# Surveiller et punir

## Citation
Foucault, M. (1975). Surveiller et punir. Naissance de la prison. Paris: Gallimard.

## Annotation
Michel Foucault's Surveiller et punir (Discipline and Punish) is a foundational analysis of the shift from spectacular sovereign punishment to modern disciplinary power, using the prison and Bentham's Panopticon to theorize how surveillance, normalization, and the production of “docile bodies” operate through institutions. It is one of the most influential accounts of power in the human sciences. For this corpus it is a canonical anchor for surveillance, discipline, and the power/knowledge nexus — directly relevant to monitoring and AI.
