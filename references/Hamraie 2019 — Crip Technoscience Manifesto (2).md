# Crip Technoscience Manifesto

## Citation
Hamraie, Aimi, Fritsch, Kelly (2019) Crip Technoscience Manifesto. Catalyst: Feminism, Theory, Technoscience, 5(1), pp. 1–33.

## Annotation
The “Crip Technoscience Manifesto” by Hamraie and Fritsch calls attention to the messy, non-innocent, and crucial work of “crip technoscience” — disabled people's critique, alteration, and reinvention of technologies, architectures, and infrastructures toward socially just relations. It positions disabled people as expert agents of design and dismantling. (This entry duplicates the same manifesto in a different citation form.)
