# UK rules out new AI regulator

## Citation
MCCALLUM, Shiona (2023). UK rules out new AI regulator. BBC, 29 mars. En ligne: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/technology-65102210.

## Annotation
This BBC article by Shiona McCallum reports that the UK government ruled out creating a dedicated new AI regulator, opting instead for a “pro-innovation,” principles-based approach spread across existing regulators. As a news primary source, it documents a specific national governance strategy. For this corpus it grounds the UK's decentralized, light-touch model in the comparative landscape of AI governance.
