# Spiritualities of Life

## Citation
Heelas, P. (2008). Spiritualities of Life. Wiley. https://doi.org/10.1002/9781444301106

## Annotation
Paul Heelas's Spiritualities of Life examines contemporary “subjective-life” spirituality — the New Age and holistic milieu — arguing that it expresses a Romantic valorization of inner experience while being entangled with consumer capitalism. Heelas analyzes both the emancipatory and the commodified dimensions of these spiritualities. It is a key reference in the sociology of contemporary spirituality. For this corpus it grounds analysis of New Age/holistic spirituality and its relation to the market.
