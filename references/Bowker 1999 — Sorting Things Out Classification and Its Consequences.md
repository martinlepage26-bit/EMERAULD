# Sorting Things Out: Classification and Its Consequences

## Citation
Bowker, Geoffrey C., and Susan Leigh Star. Sorting Things Out: Classification and Its Consequences. Cambridge: MIT Press, 1999.

## Annotation
Bowker and Star's Sorting Things Out is a foundational study of classification systems and their often-invisible consequences, showing how the categories we build — medical, racial, bureaucratic — shape lives, distribute power, and encode moral and political choices. They argue that classification is infrastructural: consequential precisely because it becomes taken for granted. The book is a cornerstone of information studies and STS. For this corpus it is highly relevant to data governance, showing how categorization and standards carry politics — a direct antecedent to concerns about datasets and AI.
