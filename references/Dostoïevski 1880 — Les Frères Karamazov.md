# Les Frères Karamazov

## Citation
Dostoïevski, Fiodor. 1880. Les Frères Karamazov. Paris: Gallimard (Folio classique), [édition consultée].

## Annotation
Dostoevsky's The Brothers Karamazov (1880), his final novel, dramatizes faith, doubt, freedom, and moral responsibility through the conflicts of a father and his sons, culminating in the celebrated “Grand Inquisitor” parable on freedom and authority. It is one of the deepest explorations in world literature of theodicy, ethics, and the human capacity for both cruelty and grace. For this corpus it is a primary literary source on freedom, authority, faith, and moral order.
