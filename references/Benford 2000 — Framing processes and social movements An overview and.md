# Framing processes and social movements: An overview and assessment

## Citation
Benford, R.D. and Snow, D.A. (2000) ‘Framing processes and social movements: An overview and assessment’, Annual Review of Sociology, 26, pp. 611–639.

## Annotation
Benford and Snow's review synthesizes the literature on collective action frames and framing processes, establishing framing as a central dynamic of social movements alongside resource mobilization and political opportunity. They clarify the characteristic and variable features of collective action frames and the processes — diagnostic, prognostic, motivational — by which movements construct shared meanings. Published in the Annual Review of Sociology, it is a canonical reference. For this corpus it disciplines the concept of “framing” and grounds the analysis of meaning-making in mobilization.
