# What people do with pictures

## Citation
Radley, A. (2010). What people do with pictures. Visual Studies, 25(3), 268-279. https://doi.org/10.1080/1472586X.2010.523279

## Annotation
Alan Radley's “What People Do with Pictures” examines how images are used and made meaningful in social life and research, reflecting on the active, relational work that pictures perform. Published in Visual Studies, it contributes to visual methodology and the sociology of images. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds a nuanced account of the uses of images.
