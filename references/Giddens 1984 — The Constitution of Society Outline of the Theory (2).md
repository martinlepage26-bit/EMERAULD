# The Constitution of Society: Outline of the Theory of Structuration

## Citation
Giddens, Anthony. The Constitution of Society: Outline of the Theory of Structuration. Berkeley: University of California Press, 1984.

## Annotation
Anthony Giddens's The Constitution of Society sets out structuration theory and the “duality of structure,” arguing that social structures are both the medium and outcome of situated social practices, thereby integrating agency and structure. It is a cornerstone of social theory. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation form.)
