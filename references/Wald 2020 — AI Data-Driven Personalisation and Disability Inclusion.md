# AI Data-Driven Personalisation and Disability Inclusion

## Citation
Wald, Mike (2020) AI Data-Driven Personalisation and Disability Inclusion. Frontiers in artificial intelligence, 3, 571955. doi: 10.3389/frai.2020.571955

## Annotation
Mike Wald's article examines AI-driven personalisation and disability inclusion, contrasting “personalisation” with “classification” and arguing that big-data classification struggles with the individual uniqueness of disabled people, so designing for “edge cases” rather than the majority would be more inclusive. Published in Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence, it addresses accessibility, interoperable profiles, and data ethics. For this corpus it grounds disability-inclusive design principles for AI systems.
