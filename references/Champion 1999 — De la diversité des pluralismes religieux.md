# De la diversité des pluralismes religieux

## Citation
Champion, F. (1999). De la diversité des pluralismes religieux. International journal

## Annotation
Françoise Champion's article examines the diversity of religious pluralisms, distinguishing among the different social forms and meanings that pluralism takes in contemporary religious life. It contributes to the francophone sociology of religion's analysis of secularization and pluralization. For this corpus it grounds conceptual distinctions in the study of religious diversity.
