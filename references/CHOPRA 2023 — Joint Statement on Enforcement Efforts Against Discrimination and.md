# Joint Statement on Enforcement Efforts Against Discrimination and Bias in Automated Systems

## Citation
CHOPRA, Rohit et autres (2023). Joint Statement on Enforcement Efforts Against Discrimination and Bias in Automated Systems. 25 avril. En ligne: https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/EEOC-CRT-FTC-CFPB-AI-Joint-Statement%28final%29.pdf.

## Annotation
This joint statement by leaders of the FTC, EEOC, CFPB, and DOJ Civil Rights Division affirms that existing U.S. laws against discrimination and bias apply to automated systems and AI, signaling coordinated enforcement intent. As a government policy document, it is a primary source in U.S. AI governance and civil-rights enforcement. For this corpus it grounds the claim that AI is not a law-free zone and that anti-discrimination authority extends to algorithmic systems.
