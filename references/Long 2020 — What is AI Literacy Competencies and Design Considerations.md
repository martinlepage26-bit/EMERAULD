# What is AI Literacy? Competencies and Design Considerations

## Citation
Long, D., Magerko, B. (2020). What is AI Literacy? Competencies and Design Considerations. Proceedings of the 2020 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems, 1-16. https://doi.org/10.1145/3313831.3376727

## Annotation
Long and Magerko's “What is AI Literacy?” proposes a set of competencies and design considerations for helping non-experts understand and critically engage with artificial intelligence. Presented at CHI 2020, it is a foundational reference for AI-literacy education and public understanding of AI. For this corpus it grounds the capabilities citizens need to participate meaningfully in AI governance.
