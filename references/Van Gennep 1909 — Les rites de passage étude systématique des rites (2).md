## Annotation
Arnold van Gennep's Les rites de passage systematically analyzes rites accompanying life transitions — birth, puberty, initiation, marriage, death — establishing the classic threefold schema of separation, margin/liminality, and aggregation. It is one of the founding texts of ritual theory. (This entry duplicates the same book in a fuller citation form.)
