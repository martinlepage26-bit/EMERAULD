# Art Practice as Research: Inquiry in Visual Arts

## Citation
Sullivan, Graeme. 2010. Art Practice as Research: Inquiry in Visual Arts. 2nd ed. Thousand Oaks, CA: Sage.

## Annotation
Graeme Sullivan's Art Practice as Research argues that studio art practice constitutes a legitimate form of research and knowledge production, developing a framework for understanding inquiry in the visual arts. It is a foundational text in arts-based research methodology. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds practice-based artistic research.
