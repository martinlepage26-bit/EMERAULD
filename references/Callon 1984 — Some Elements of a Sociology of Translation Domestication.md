# Some Elements of a Sociology of Translation: Domestication of the Scallops and the Fishermen of St Brieuc Bay.

## Citation
Callon, Michel. "Some Elements of a Sociology of Translation: Domestication of the Scallops and the Fishermen of St Brieuc Bay." Sociological Review 32, no. S1 (1984): 196–233.

## Annotation
Michel Callon's study of the scallops and fishermen of St Brieuc Bay is a founding text of Actor-Network Theory, introducing the “sociology of translation” and its four moments (problematization, interessement, enrolment, mobilization). Callon shows how heterogeneous actors — including nonhumans like scallops — are enrolled into networks, and how such networks can succeed or fail. It is a canonical STS reference. For this corpus it grounds a relational, network account of how socio-technical arrangements (including AI systems) are assembled.
