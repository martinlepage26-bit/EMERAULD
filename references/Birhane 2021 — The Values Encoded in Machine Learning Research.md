# The Values Encoded in Machine Learning Research.

## Citation
Birhane, Abeba, Vinay Uday Prabhu, and Emma F. Kearns. 2021. “The Values Encoded in Machine Learning Research.” In Proceedings of the 2021 ACM Conference on Fairness, Accountability, and Transparency, 173–184. New York: ACM.

## Annotation
Birhane, Prabhu, and Kearns analyze the values encoded in machine-learning research by systematically examining highly cited papers, showing how the field's stated and enacted values — performance, generalization, efficiency — often go unexamined and serve particular interests. The study reveals ties between prominent ML research and corporate and military funders. Presented at FAccT 2021, it is a notable contribution to critical AI studies. For this corpus it exposes the value commitments embedded in ostensibly neutral technical research.
