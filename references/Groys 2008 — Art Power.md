# Art Power

## Citation
Groys, Boris. 2008. Art Power. Cambridge, MA: MIT Press.

## Annotation
Boris Groys's Art Power examines the entanglement of art and power, arguing that art operates within, and is shaped by, political and institutional forces rather than standing outside them. Groys analyzes how art functions under different regimes, including its propagandistic and market dimensions. It is an influential work in contemporary art theory. Its place in this corpus is peripheral, but it bears on the relationship between aesthetics, power, and legitimacy.
