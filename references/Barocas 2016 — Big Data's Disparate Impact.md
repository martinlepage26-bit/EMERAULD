# Big Data's Disparate Impact

## Citation
Barocas, S., & Selbst, A. D. (2016). Big Data's Disparate Impact. California Law Review, 104(3), 671–732. https://doi.org/10.15779/Z38BG31

## Annotation
Barocas and Selbst's “Big Data's Disparate Impact” is a landmark law-review analysis of how data-mining and algorithmic decision-making can produce discriminatory outcomes even without discriminatory intent. Working through U.S. antidiscrimination law (especially Title VII's disparate-impact doctrine), they show how bias can enter at every stage — from defining the target variable to labeling, feature selection, and proxies for protected traits. The article is foundational for the fairness, accountability, and AI-governance literatures. For this corpus it is the canonical pipeline-level account of algorithmic discrimination.
