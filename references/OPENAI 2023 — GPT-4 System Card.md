# GPT-4 System Card

## Citation
OPENAI (2023). GPT-4 System Card. Publication d’entreprise, 23 mars. En ligne: https://cdn.openai.com/papers/gpt-4-system-card.pdf.

## Annotation
OpenAI's GPT-4 System Card documents the capabilities, limitations, and risks of the GPT-4 model, describing safety evaluations, red-teaming, and mitigations undertaken before deployment. As a corporate model-documentation artifact, it is a primary source on how a leading lab reports and governs a frontier model. For this corpus it grounds industry practices of model documentation and safety disclosure — a real-world instance of “model card”-style governance.
