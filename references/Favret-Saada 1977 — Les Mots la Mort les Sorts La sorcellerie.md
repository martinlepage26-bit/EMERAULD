# Les Mots, la Mort, les Sorts: La sorcellerie dans le bocage

## Citation
Favret-Saada, J., 1977. Les Mots, la Mort, les Sorts: La sorcellerie dans le bocage. Paris: Gallimard.

## Annotation
Jeanne Favret-Saada's Les mots, la mort, les sorts (Deadly Words) is a celebrated anthropological study of witchcraft in the rural French Bocage, arguing that witchcraft is fundamentally a matter of speech and that one cannot study it from outside — the ethnographer inevitably becomes “caught” in its web of words and forces. The work transformed methodological debates about participation, language, and the impossibility of neutral observation. It is a classic of the anthropology of witchcraft. For this corpus it grounds a reflexive, language-centered account of witchcraft and fieldwork.
