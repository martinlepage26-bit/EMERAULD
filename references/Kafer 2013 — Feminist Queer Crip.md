# Feminist, Queer, Crip

## Citation
Kafer, A. (2013) Feminist, Queer, Crip. Bloomington: Indiana University Press.

## Annotation
Alison Kafer's Feminist, Queer, Crip is a foundational text of crip theory that develops a “political/relational” model of disability and imagines “crip futures” that resist the compulsory able-bodiedness embedded in mainstream visions of the future. Kafer weaves disability, feminist, and queer theory to argue that disability is a political rather than merely medical category. For this corpus it grounds crip theory and the politics of disability futures — relevant to how technology imagines “normal” bodies.
