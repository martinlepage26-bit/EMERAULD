# Encoding normative ethics: On algorithmic bias and disability

## Citation
Moura, Ian (2023) Encoding normative ethics: On algorithmic bias and disability. First Monday, 28(1). doi: 10.5210/fm.v28i1.12905

## Annotation
Ian Moura's “Encoding normative ethics: On algorithmic bias and disability” examines how algorithmic systems encode normative assumptions that produce bias against disabled people, connecting machine-ethics debates to disability. Published in First Monday, it contributes to critical disability and AI studies. For this corpus it grounds the analysis of how algorithms embed ableist norms.
