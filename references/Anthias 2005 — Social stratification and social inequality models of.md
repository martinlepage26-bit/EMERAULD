# Social stratification and social inequality : models of

## Citation
Anthias, F. (2005). Social stratification and social inequality : models of

## Annotation
Floya Anthias's essay develops models of intersectionality and identity within debates on social stratification and inequality. She argues for analyzing how class intersects with gender, ethnicity, and other axes of division rather than treating stratification along a single dimension. The piece contributes to the sociology of class and to intersectionality theory. For work on inequality and identity in this corpus, it offers a framework for thinking about how multiple hierarchies combine.
