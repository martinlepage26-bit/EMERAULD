# De la construction du genre à la construction du ‘sexe’.

## Citation
Baril, Alexandre. “De la construction du genre à la construction du ‘sexe’.” Recherches féministes 20, no. 2 (2007): 61–90.

## Annotation
In this Recherches féministes article, Alexandre Baril analyzes Judith Butler's account of how not only gender but “sex” itself is discursively constructed, tracing the constructivist and anti-foundationalist logic of her thought. Baril underscores Butler's influence on feminist, gay, lesbian, and queer studies while noting the scarcity of sustained Québec engagement with her work. The piece is a careful francophone exposition and assessment. For this corpus it links Butler's theory to feminist scholarship in Québec. (Same article as the companion citation.)
