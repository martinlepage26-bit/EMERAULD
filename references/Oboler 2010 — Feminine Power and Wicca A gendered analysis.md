# Feminine Power and Wicca: A gendered analysis

## Citation
Oboler, R., 2010. Feminine Power and Wicca: A gendered analysis. Journal of Religion and Gender, 5(2), pp.45–62.

## Annotation
Regina Oboler's “Feminine Power and Wicca” offers a gendered analysis of Wicca, examining how the tradition constructs and valorizes feminine power and where its gender ideology reproduces or challenges essentialism. It contributes to gender-focused Pagan studies. For this corpus it grounds the analysis of gender and power in Wicca.
