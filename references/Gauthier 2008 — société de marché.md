# société de marché

## Citation
Gauthier, F. (2008). La religion et la« société de marché». Entropia, 5, 93-106.

## Annotation
François Gauthier's article analyzes religion in relation to the “market society,” examining how consumer capitalism reshapes religious forms, meaning, and belonging. Published in Entropia, it contributes to the sociology of religion and critiques of the market. For this corpus it grounds analysis of the entanglement of religion and market logics.
