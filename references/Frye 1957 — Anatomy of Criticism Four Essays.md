# Anatomy of Criticism: Four Essays

## Citation
Frye, Northrop. 1957. Anatomy of Criticism: Four Essays. Princeton: Princeton University Press.

## Annotation
Northrop Frye's Anatomy of Criticism is a landmark of literary theory that proposes a systematic, archetypal account of literature, organizing it into theories of modes, symbols, myths, and genres. Frye sought to make criticism a coherent, quasi-scientific discipline grounded in recurring narrative patterns. It profoundly shaped archetypal and structuralist criticism. For narrative-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the analysis of literary archetypes, genres, and mythic structure.
