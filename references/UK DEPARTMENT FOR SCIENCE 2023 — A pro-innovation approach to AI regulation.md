# A pro-innovation approach to AI regulation

## Citation
UK DEPARTMENT FOR SCIENCE, INNOVATION AND TECHNOLOGY (2023). A pro-innovation approach to AI regulation. 29 mars. En ligne: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ai-regulation-a-pro-innovation-approach/white-paper#executive-summary.

## Annotation
The UK Department for Science, Innovation and Technology's white paper “A Pro-Innovation Approach to AI Regulation” sets out the UK's decision not to create a single AI regulator, instead applying cross-sectoral principles through existing regulators to avoid stifling innovation. As an official policy document, it is a primary source on the UK's distinctive governance model. For this corpus it grounds the UK's light-touch, principles-based approach to AI regulation.
