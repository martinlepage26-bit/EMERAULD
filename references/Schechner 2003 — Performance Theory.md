# Performance Theory

## Citation
Schechner, Richard. 2003. Performance Theory. London: Routledge.

## Annotation
Richard Schechner's Performance Theory is a foundational work developing his influential concepts — “restored behavior,” the braiding of ritual and theatre, and the efficacy/entertainment continuum — that shaped the field of performance studies. For this corpus it grounds the theoretical link between ritual and performance.
