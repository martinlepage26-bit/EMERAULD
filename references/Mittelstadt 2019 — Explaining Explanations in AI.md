# Explaining Explanations in AI

## Citation
Mittelstadt, B., Russell, C., Wachter, S. (2019). Explaining Explanations in AI. Proceedings of the Conference on Fairness, Accountability, and Transparency, 279-288. https://doi.org/10.1145/3287560.3287574

## Annotation
Mittelstadt, Russell, and Wachter's “Explaining Explanations in AI” clarifies what “explanation” means in explainable AI, drawing on philosophy and social science to distinguish types of explanation and argue that useful explanations are contrastive and audience-relative. Presented at FAccT 2019, it is an influential contribution to XAI. For this corpus it grounds the conceptual foundations of explaining automated decisions — central to AI accountability.
