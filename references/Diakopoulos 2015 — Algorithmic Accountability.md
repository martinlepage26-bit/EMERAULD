# Algorithmic Accountability

## Citation
Diakopoulos, N. (2015). Algorithmic Accountability. Digital Journalism, 3(3), 398–415. https://doi.org/10.1080/21670811.2014.976411

## Annotation
Nicholas Diakopoulos's “Algorithmic Accountability” examines how the power of algorithms can be scrutinized and held to account, proposing investigative and reverse-engineering approaches for journalists studying automated systems. It is a foundational reference in algorithmic-accountability reporting. (This entry duplicates the same article in a different citation form.)
