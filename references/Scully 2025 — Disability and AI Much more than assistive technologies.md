# Disability and AI: Much more than assistive technologies

## Citation
Scully, Jackie Leach (2025) Disability and AI: Much more than assistive technologies. Science (New York, N.Y.), 389(6762), eaea4918. doi: 10.1126/science.aea4918

## Annotation
Jackie Leach Scully's “Disability and AI” argues that the relationship between disability and AI extends far beyond assistive technologies, encompassing how AI systems represent, categorize, and affect disabled people across health, media, and everyday life. Published in Science, it calls for centering disability in AI ethics and design. For this corpus it grounds a broad, critical framing of disability and AI.
