# The European Union’s response to transnational repression: Are we moving towards securitisation?

## Citation
Furstenberg, S. (2025) ‘The European Union’s response to transnational repression: Are we moving towards securitisation?’, European Journal of International Security, pp. 1–25. doi: 10.1017/eis.2025.2.

## Annotation
Saipira Furstenberg's article examines the European Union's response to transnational repression — states reaching across borders to silence dissidents — asking whether the EU is moving toward securitizing this issue. Published in the European Journal of International Security, it contributes to critical security studies. For this corpus it grounds analysis of securitization, migration, and cross-border state control.
