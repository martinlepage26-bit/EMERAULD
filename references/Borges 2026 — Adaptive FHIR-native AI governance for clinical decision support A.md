# Adaptive FHIR-native AI governance for clinical decision support: A modular, auditable deployment framework for real-world clinical AI

## Citation
Borges, J. (2026). Adaptive FHIR-native AI governance for clinical decision support: A modular, auditable deployment framework for real-world clinical AI. Research Square. https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-8714776/v1

## Annotation
A deployment framework binding clinical AI governance to the FHIR interoperability standard, so that audit and oversight ride on the health data layer already in production. The design lesson is that governance embedded in an existing standard inherits its adoption; useful precedent for arguing that controls should attach to infrastructure rather than to policy documents. Preprint, healthcare-specific.
