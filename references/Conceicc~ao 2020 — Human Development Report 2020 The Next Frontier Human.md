# Human Development Report 2020: The Next Frontier: Human Development and the Anthropocene

## Citation
Concei\cc\~ao, Pedro (2020) Human Development Report 2020: The Next Frontier: Human Development and the Anthropocene.

## Annotation
The Human Development Report 2020, led by Pedro Conceição for the UNDP, subtitled The Next Frontier: Human Development and the Anthropocene, reframes human development in light of planetary pressures, arguing that people and planet must be advanced together. It introduces experimental indices adjusting for planetary and ecological burdens. As a flagship UN report, it is an authoritative reference on development and the Anthropocene. For this corpus it links posthumanist and Anthropocene themes to global development policy. (The author name appears LaTeX-escaped in the canonical entry.)
