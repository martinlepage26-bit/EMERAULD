# Understanding Illness: Using Drawings as a Research Method

## Citation
Guillemin, M. (2004). Understanding Illness: Using Drawings as a Research Method. Qualitative Health Research, 14(2), 272-289. https://doi.org/10.1177/1049732303260445

## Annotation
Marilys Guillemin's “Understanding Illness: Using Drawings as a Research Method” demonstrates how participant drawings can reveal how people conceptualize and experience illness, offering access to embodied and affective understandings beyond words. Published in Qualitative Health Research, it is a key reference for visual and arts-based methods. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds drawing as a research tool.
