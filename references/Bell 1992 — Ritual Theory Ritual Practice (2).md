# Ritual Theory, Ritual Practice

## Citation
Bell, Catherine. 1992. Ritual Theory, Ritual Practice. New York: Oxford University Press.

## Annotation
Catherine Bell's Ritual Theory, Ritual Practice reframes ritual as “ritualization” — a strategic, embodied practice that differentiates itself from ordinary action and thereby produces authority and social distinction. Bell dismantles the analytic oppositions between thought and action that had structured the field and draws on practice theory to rethink how ritual operates. It remains a core text in ritual studies. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation form.)
