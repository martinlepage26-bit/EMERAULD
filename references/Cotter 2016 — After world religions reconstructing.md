# After world religions : reconstructing

## Citation
Cotter, C. R. et Robertson, D. G. (2016). After world religions : reconstructing

## Annotation
Edited by Christopher Cotter and David Robertson, After World Religions critiques the “world religions paradigm” that has long structured religious studies, arguing that it distorts understanding by imposing a Protestant, Western template. The contributors propose ways to reconstruct the teaching and study of religion beyond this framework. It is an influential intervention in the theory and method of religious studies. For this corpus it grounds critical reflection on how “religion” itself is categorized.
