# Race After Technology: Abolitionist Tools for the New Jim Code

## Citation
Benjamin, Ruha. Race After Technology: Abolitionist Tools for the New Jim Code. Cambridge: Polity Press, 2019.

## Annotation
Ruha Benjamin's Race After Technology introduces the concept of the “New Jim Code” — the ways emerging technologies encode and amplify racial hierarchies while appearing neutral or benevolent. Drawing on science and technology studies and critical race theory, Benjamin analyzes how discriminatory design becomes embedded in everyday tools and offers “abolitionist tools” for contesting it. The book is a foundational text in critical studies of race and technology. For this corpus it grounds the argument that technical systems carry and reproduce social inequality.
