# How Institutions Think

## Citation
Douglas, Mary. How Institutions Think. Syracuse: Syracuse University Press, 1986.

## Annotation
Mary Douglas's How Institutions Think argues that institutions shape cognition itself — the categories, classifications, and memories through which members perceive the world — extending her earlier work on classification and purity. Douglas contends that much of what we take to be individual thought is institutionally patterned. It is a key text in the anthropology of knowledge and institutional theory. For this corpus it grounds the claim that institutions structure how problems are framed and decisions are made.
