# Girls: Feminine Adolescence in Popular Culture and Cultural Theory

## Citation
Driscoll, Catherine. Girls: Feminine Adolescence in Popular Culture and Cultural Theory. Columbia UP, 2002.

## Annotation
Catherine Driscoll's Girls examines feminine adolescence as a cultural and theoretical construct, tracing how “the girl” has been produced across popular culture, psychology, and feminist theory. Driscoll offers both a genealogy and a critical framework for girlhood studies. It is a foundational text in the field. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the theorization of girlhood and feminine adolescence.
