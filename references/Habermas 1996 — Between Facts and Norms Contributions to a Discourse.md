# Between Facts and Norms: Contributions to a Discourse Theory of Law and Democracy

## Citation
Habermas, Jürgen. Between Facts and Norms: Contributions to a Discourse Theory of Law and Democracy. Trans. William Rehg. Cambridge: MIT Press, 1996.

## Annotation
Jürgen Habermas's Between Facts and Norms develops his discourse theory of law and democracy, arguing that legal and political norms gain legitimacy from communicative processes in which those subject to them can genuinely participate in their formation, rather than from mere procedure or coercion. It is one of the most rigorous accounts of democratic legitimacy. For this corpus it grounds the distinction between performative compliance and substantive, participatory legitimacy — central to governance.
