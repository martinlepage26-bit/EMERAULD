# Religion and Healing.

## Citation
McGuire, Meredith. “Religion and Healing.” In The Sacred in a Secular Age, edited by Peter E. Hammond, 268–284. Berkeley: University of California Press, 1985.

## Annotation
Meredith McGuire's “Religion and Healing” analyzes the persistence and forms of religious healing in modern secular society, contributing to the sociology of religion and health. Published in The Sacred in a Secular Age, it is a standard reference. (This entry duplicates the same chapter in a different citation form.)
