## Citation
Grace, K., Stewart, H., Sandkühler, J., Thomas, S., et al. (). Thousands of AI Authors on the Future of AI. arXiv preprint https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.02843

## Annotation
“Thousands of AI Authors on the Future of AI,” led by Katja Grace, reports a large-scale survey of published machine-learning researchers on their forecasts for AI progress, capabilities, timelines, and risks. It is one of the most cited expert-elicitation studies on AI's trajectory, documenting substantial uncertainty and non-trivial probabilities assigned to extreme outcomes. For this corpus it grounds evidence about expert expectations that inform AI-governance and risk debates.
