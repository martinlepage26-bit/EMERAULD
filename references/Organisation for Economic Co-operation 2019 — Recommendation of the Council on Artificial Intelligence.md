# Recommendation of the Council on Artificial Intelligence

## Citation
Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development. (2019). Recommendation of the Council on Artificial Intelligence.

## Annotation
The OECD Recommendation of the Council on Artificial Intelligence (2019) is the first intergovernmental AI standard, articulating principles for trustworthy AI and policy recommendations that influenced the G20 and national frameworks. It is a foundational international governance instrument. (This entry duplicates the OECD AI Recommendation in its English form.)
