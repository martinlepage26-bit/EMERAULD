## Annotation
Fernandez-Crespo and Cordon-Galiano review sexual dysfunction specifically among men who have sex with men, synthesizing prevalence and clinical considerations often neglected in heteronormative research. Published in Current Urology Reports, it belongs to the clinical sexual-medicine literature. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized medical review.
