# When Seconds Turn Into Minutes: Time Expansion Experiences in Altered States of Consciousness

## Citation
Taylor, S. (2020). When Seconds Turn Into Minutes: Time Expansion Experiences in Altered States of Consciousness. Journal of Humanistic Psychology, 62(2), 208-232. https://doi.org/10.1177/0022167820917484

## Annotation
Steve Taylor's “When Seconds Turn Into Minutes” examines “time expansion experiences” in altered states of consciousness, in which subjective time seems to slow or expand. Published in the Journal of Humanistic Psychology, it belongs to transpersonal and consciousness studies. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral, bearing on the phenomenology of altered states.
