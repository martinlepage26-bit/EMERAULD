# Le récit de vie dans les sciences sociales

## Citation
Brun, P. (2003). Le récit de vie dans les sciences sociales. Revue Quart Monde, 188,

## Annotation
Philippe Brun's article discusses the use of the life narrative (récit de vie) in the social sciences, reflecting on its epistemological status and its value for understanding marginalized experience. Published in the Revue Quart Monde, it is oriented toward research on poverty and exclusion. For method-focused work in this corpus, it contributes a francophone perspective on biographical method and its social commitments.
