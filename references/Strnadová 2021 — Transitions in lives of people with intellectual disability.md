# Transitions in lives of people with intellectual disability

## Citation
Strnadová, I., Loblinzk, J., Wehmeyer, M. (2021). Transitions in lives of people with intellectual disability. British Journal of Learning Disabilities, 49(3), 263-270. https://doi.org/10.1111/bld.12413

## Annotation
Strnadová, Loblinzk, and Wehmeyer examine transitions in the lives of people with intellectual disability, addressing self-determination and support across life-course changes. Published in the British Journal of Learning Disabilities, it contributes to disability and inclusion research. For this corpus it grounds attention to disabled people's life transitions and support needs.
