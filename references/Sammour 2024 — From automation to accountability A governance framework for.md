# From automation to accountability: A governance framework for sustainable AI and hybrid intelligence

## Citation
Sammour, G., & Malas, L. (2024). From automation to accountability: A governance framework for sustainable AI and hybrid intelligence. Research Square. https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-5722853/v1

## Annotation
Proposes a governance framework for hybrid human-AI systems, arguing that accountability must be designed at the point where automation displaces human judgment rather than bolted on after deployment. Useful as a contrast case to control-catalogue approaches: it frames accountability as a property of the human-machine division of labour. Preprint; the framework is asserted rather than empirically validated.
