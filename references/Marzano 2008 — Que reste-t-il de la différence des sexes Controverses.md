# Que reste-t-il de la différence des sexes? Controverses, 8, 12-19

## Citation
Marzano, M. (2008). Que reste-t-il de la différence des sexes? Controverses, 8, 12-19.

## Annotation
Michela Marzano's “Que reste-t-il de la différence des sexes?” interrogates what remains of the notion of sexual difference amid contemporary debates over gender, sex, and constructivism. Published in Controverses, it is a philosophical intervention in francophone gender debate. For this corpus it grounds reflection on the status and politics of sexual difference.
