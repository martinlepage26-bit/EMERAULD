# Defining nascent ritual

## Citation
Grimes, R. L. (1982). Defining nascent ritual. Journal of the American Academy of

## Annotation
Ronald Grimes's “Defining Nascent Ritual” is an influential contribution to ritual studies that addresses the problem of defining ritual, especially emergent or newly invented ritual, and the criteria by which ritualization can be recognized. Grimes is a foundational figure in the field. For this corpus it grounds conceptual reflection on what counts as ritual and how new ritual forms arise.
