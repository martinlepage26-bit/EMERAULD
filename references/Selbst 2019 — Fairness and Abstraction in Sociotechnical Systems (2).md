# Fairness and Abstraction in Sociotechnical Systems

## Citation
Selbst, A. D., Boyd, D., Friedler, S. A., Venkatasubramanian, S., & Vertesi, J. (2019). Fairness and Abstraction in Sociotechnical Systems. In Proceedings of the Conference on Fairness, Accountability, and Transparency (FAccT) (pp. 59–68). https://doi.org/10.1145/3287560.3287598

## Annotation
Selbst and colleagues' “Fairness and Abstraction in Sociotechnical Systems” argues that fairness cannot be achieved by treating the algorithm in isolation; abstracting away social context produces “traps” that undermine fairness. It reframes fairness as a sociotechnical rather than purely computational problem. (This entry duplicates the same FAccT 2019 paper in a different citation form.)
