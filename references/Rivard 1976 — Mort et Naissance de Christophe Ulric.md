# Mort et Naissance de Christophe Ulric

## Citation
Rivard, Yvon. 1976. Mort et Naissance de Christophe Ulric. Montréal: Éditions Quinze.

## Annotation
Yvon Rivard's Mort et naissance de Christophe Ulric is an acclaimed Québécois novel exploring interior experience, death, and rebirth through a lyrical, introspective narrative. It is a notable work of Québec literature. For this corpus it is a primary literary source in the francophone Québec corpus.
