# Artificial General Intelligence: Concept, State of the Art, and Future Prospects

## Citation
Goertzel, B. (2014). Artificial General Intelligence: Concept, State of the Art, and Future Prospects. Journal of Artificial General Intelligence, 5(1), 1-48. https://doi.org/10.2478/jagi-2014-0001

## Annotation
Ben Goertzel's survey article on Artificial General Intelligence reviews the concept, state of the art, and prospects of AGI, mapping the research community's approaches — symbolic, emergentist, hybrid, and universalist — and the metrics proposed for general intelligence. Published in the Journal of Artificial General Intelligence, it is an authoritative overview from a leading AGI proponent. For this corpus it grounds the technical framing of AGI as an aspiration and research program.
