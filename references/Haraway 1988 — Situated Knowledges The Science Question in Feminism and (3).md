# Situated Knowledges: The Science Question in Feminism and the Privilege of Partial Perspective.

## Citation
Haraway, Donna J. "Situated Knowledges: The Science Question in Feminism and the Privilege of Partial Perspective." Feminist Studies 14, no. 3 (1988): 575–599.

## Annotation
Donna Haraway's “Situated Knowledges” is a landmark essay proposing situated, partial, and accountable knowledge as a feminist alternative to both the “god trick” of universal objectivity and paralyzing relativism. It reframes objectivity as a matter of location and responsibility. (This entry duplicates the same article in a different citation form.)
