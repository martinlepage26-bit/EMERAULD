# Interrogating Postfeminism: Gender and the Politics of Popular Culture

## Citation
Tasker, Yvonne, and Diane Negra, eds. 2007. Interrogating Postfeminism: Gender and the Politics of Popular Culture. Durham, NC: Duke University Press.

## Annotation
Interrogating Postfeminism, edited by Tasker and Negra, gathers essays analyzing how postfeminist culture operates in popular media, from makeover shows to chick flicks, invoking female empowerment while reinscribing traditional gender norms. It is a key reference in feminist media studies. (This entry duplicates the same volume in a different citation form.)
