# Actionable Recourse in Linear Classification

## Citation
Ustun, B., Spangher, A., Liu, Y. (2019). Actionable Recourse in Linear Classification. Proceedings of the Conference on Fairness, Accountability, and Transparency, 10-19. https://doi.org/10.1145/3287560.3287566

## Annotation
Ustun, Spangher, and Liu's “Actionable Recourse in Linear Classification” (FAccT version) formalizes and provides tools to assess whether people subject to algorithmic decisions have feasible means to alter unfavorable outcomes, making recourse a measurable property of predictive models. It is influential in fairness and accountability research. (This entry duplicates the same paper in a different citation form.)
