# In the Study of the Witch: Women, Shadows, and the Academic Study of Religions

## Citation
Zwissler, L. (2018). In the Study of the Witch: Women, Shadows, and the Academic Study of Religions. Religions, 9(4), 105. https://doi.org/10.3390/rel9040105

## Annotation
Laurel Zwissler's “In the Study of the Witch” examines the historically competing categories of magic and religion and their gendered traces in the history of religious studies, tracing how “magic” was personified with a woman's face and how contemporary Witches reclaim that identification to legitimize religious difference. Published in Religions, it reflexively analyzes the field itself. For this corpus it grounds the gendered genealogy of the categories “magic” and “religion” and the academic study of witchcraft.
