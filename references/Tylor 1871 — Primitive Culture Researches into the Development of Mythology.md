# Primitive Culture: Researches into the Development of Mythology, Philosophy, Religion, Art and Custom

## Citation
Tylor, Edward B. Primitive Culture: Researches into the Development of Mythology, Philosophy, Religion, Art and Custom. London: John Murray, 1871.

## Annotation
Edward Tylor's Primitive Culture defines culture as a complex whole of knowledge, belief, art, and custom, and develops the theory of animism as the origin of religion within an evolutionary framework. It is a founding work of anthropology. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation form.)
