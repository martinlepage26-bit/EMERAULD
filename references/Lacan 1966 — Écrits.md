# Écrits

## Citation
Lacan, Jacques. 1966. Écrits. Paris: Seuil.

## Annotation
Jacques Lacan's Écrits collects the psychoanalyst's major writings, including the essays on the mirror stage, the symbolic order, and the structure of the unconscious “like a language.” Lacan's rereading of Freud through structuralism profoundly influenced critical theory, film theory, feminism, and queer theory. For this corpus it grounds psychoanalytic concepts of subjectivity, desire, and the symbolic that underlie much cultural and gender theory.
