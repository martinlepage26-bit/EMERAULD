# L’imagination créatrice dans le soufisme d’Ibn ‘Arabî

## Citation
Corbin, Henry. 1972. L’imagination créatrice dans le soufisme d’Ibn ‘Arabî. Paris: Flammarion.

## Annotation
Henry Corbin's L'imagination créatrice dans le soufisme d'Ibn 'Arabî is a landmark study of the great Sufi mystic Ibn 'Arabî, developing Corbin's influential concept of the “creative imagination” and the mundus imaginalis — an intermediate imaginal world between sense and intellect. Corbin's phenomenology of Islamic mysticism has deeply influenced the study of esotericism and comparative spirituality. For this corpus it is a foundational reference on imagination, mysticism, and the imaginal in Western esoteric and comparative-religious thought.
