# Les sciences sociales à la découverte de l'homosexualité

## Citation
Rubin, G. S. (2011). Les sciences sociales à la découverte de l'homosexualité. Genre,

## Annotation
Gayle Rubin's essay retraces the genealogy of social-science work on homosexuality, situating the translated texts of a special issue within the sociological currents that produced them and assessing their contributions. Published in Genre, sexualité & société, it is a historiographical reference. (This entry duplicates the same article in a different citation form.)
