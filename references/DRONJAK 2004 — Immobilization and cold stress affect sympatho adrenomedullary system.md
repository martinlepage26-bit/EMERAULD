## Annotation
Dronjak and colleagues report an experimental physiology study on how immobilization and cold stress affect the sympatho-adrenomedullary and pituitary-adrenocortical stress systems in rats under long-term isolation and crowding. Published in Physiology & Behavior, it belongs to the neuroendocrinology of stress. Its relevance to this corpus is negligible/peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized biological source, and its presence may reflect an ingest artifact.
