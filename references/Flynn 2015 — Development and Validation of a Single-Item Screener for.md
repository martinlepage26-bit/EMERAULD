# Development and Validation of a Single-Item Screener for Self-Reporting Sexual Problems in U.S

## Citation
Flynn, K., Lindau, S., Lin, L., Reese, J., et al. (2015). Development and Validation of a Single-Item Screener for Self-Reporting Sexual Problems in U.S. Adults. Journal of General Internal Medicine, 30(10), 1468-1475. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11606-015-3333-3

## Annotation
Flynn and colleagues develop and validate a single-item screener for self-reported sexual problems in U.S. adults, offering a brief instrument for clinical and survey use. Published in the Journal of General Internal Medicine, it belongs to the sexual-health measurement literature. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized measurement tool.
