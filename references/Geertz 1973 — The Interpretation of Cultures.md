# The Interpretation of Cultures

## Citation
Geertz, Clifford. The Interpretation of Cultures. New York: Basic Books, 1973.

## Annotation
Clifford Geertz's The Interpretation of Cultures is a foundational collection in interpretive anthropology, famous for the method of “thick description” and the view of culture as webs of significance that the analyst must read like a text. Its essays, including the celebrated account of the Balinese cockfight, reshaped the human sciences' understanding of meaning and interpretation. For this corpus it grounds interpretive method and the reading of cultural practice as meaningful.
