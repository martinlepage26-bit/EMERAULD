# Réflexions sur le paganisme d’extrême droite

## Citation
François, S. (2018). Réflexions sur le paganisme d’extrême droite. Social Compass, 65(2), 263-277. https://doi.org/10.1177/0037768618768439

## Annotation
Stéphane François analyzes the ethnocentric variant of neo-paganism as an increasingly important spirituality of the radical far right, offering activists a way to reconnect with an ethnic-cultural identity they see as destroyed by Christian universalism, while also enabling transnational synergies among scattered groups. Published in Social Compass, the article maps the intellectual coloring this current gives to far-right identity politics. For this corpus it grounds the study of paganism's appropriation by the far right — a caution against romanticizing nature-religion revival.
