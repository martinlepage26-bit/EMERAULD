# Institutional Entanglements: How Institutional Knots and Reverberating Consequences Burden Refugee Families

## Citation
Sackett, B., Lareau, A. (2023). Institutional Entanglements: How Institutional Knots and Reverberating Consequences Burden Refugee Families. RSF: The Russell Sage Foundation Journal of the Social Sciences, 9(4), 114-132. https://doi.org/10.7758/RSF.2023.9.4.05

## Annotation
Sackett and Lareau's “Institutional Entanglements” examines how “institutional knots” — the compounding, interlocking demands of multiple bureaucracies — burden refugee families, extending the administrative-burden framework to show reverberating consequences across institutions. Published in the RSF Journal, it contributes to the sociology of inequality and bureaucracy. For this corpus it grounds how entangled institutional demands produce disadvantage — relevant to how automated and bureaucratic systems compound burdens.
