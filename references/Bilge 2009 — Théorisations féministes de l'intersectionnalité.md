# Théorisations féministes de l'intersectionnalité

## Citation
Bilge, S. (2009). Théorisations féministes de l'intersectionnalité. Diogène, 225(1),

## Annotation
Sirma Bilge's French-language article surveys feminist theorizations of intersectionality, tracing how attention to the concomitant effects of race, class, and gender elevated intersectionality to a central feminist contribution. Bilge notes the movement beyond the anglophone sphere toward an intersectional instrument for transforming social-justice and anti-discrimination policy, and maps the debates this project provokes. Published in Diogène, it is a valuable francophone synthesis. For this corpus it grounds intersectionality theory for a French readership.
