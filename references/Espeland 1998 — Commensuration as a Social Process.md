# Commensuration as a Social Process.

## Citation
Espeland, Wendy Nelson, and Mitchell L. Stevens. "Commensuration as a Social Process." Annual Review of Sociology 24 (1998): 313–343.

## Annotation
Espeland and Stevens's “Commensuration as a Social Process” is a foundational article analyzing commensuration — the transformation of different qualities into a common metric — as a fundamental and consequential social operation that makes things comparable, often erasing context and meaning. They show how measurement and ranking exercise power. Published in the Annual Review of Sociology, it is widely cited. For this corpus it is highly relevant to metrics, quantification, and the politics of measurement in data and AI systems.
