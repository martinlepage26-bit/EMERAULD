# A Lokian family : queer and pagan agency in Montreal

## Citation
Lepage, M. (2013). A Lokian family : queer and pagan agency in Montreal. The

## Annotation
Martin Lepage's “A Lokian Family: Queer and Pagan Agency in Montreal” examines devotees of the Norse god Loki within Montreal's Pagan community, analyzing how queer and Pagan identities intersect and how ritual devotion to a trickster/shape-shifting deity supports non-normative gender and sexual agency. Published in Pomegranate, the international journal of Pagan studies, it is part of the author's ethnographic work on queer Montreal Paganism. For this corpus it grounds the intersection of queerness, Pagan ritual, and agency.
