# Measuring Individual Differences in Generic Beliefs in Conspiracy Theories Across Cultures: Conspiracy Mentality Questionnaire

## Citation
Bruder, M., Haffke, P., Neave, N., Nouripanah, N., et al. (2013). Measuring Individual Differences in Generic Beliefs in Conspiracy Theories Across Cultures: Conspiracy Mentality Questionnaire. Frontiers in Psychology, 4. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2013.00225

## Annotation
Bruder and colleagues develop and validate the Conspiracy Mentality Questionnaire, a cross-cultural instrument for measuring individual differences in generic belief in conspiracy theories. The paper contributes a widely used psychometric tool to the psychology of conspiracy belief. Published in Frontiers in Psychology, it belongs to social and political psychology. For work touching on misinformation and belief in this corpus, it provides a measurement foundation for conspiracy mentality.
