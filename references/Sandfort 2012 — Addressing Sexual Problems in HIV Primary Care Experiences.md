# Addressing Sexual Problems in HIV Primary Care: Experiences from Patients

## Citation
Sandfort, T., Collier, K., Grossberg, R. (2012). Addressing Sexual Problems in HIV Primary Care: Experiences from Patients. Archives of Sexual Behavior, 42(7), 1357-1368. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10508-012-0009-5

## Annotation
Sandfort, Collier, and Grossberg examine how sexual problems are addressed in HIV primary care from patients' perspectives, contributing to research on sexual health and clinical practice for people living with HIV. Published in Archives of Sexual Behavior, it belongs to the clinical sexual-health literature. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized health-research source.
