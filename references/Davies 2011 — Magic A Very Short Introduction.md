# Magic: A Very Short Introduction

## Citation
Davies, O., 2011. Magic: A Very Short Introduction. Oxford: Oxford University Press.

## Annotation
Owen Davies's Magic: A Very Short Introduction offers a concise, authoritative history of magic across cultures and periods, from ancient ritual to modern occultism, written by a leading historian of witchcraft and the supernatural. Davies treats magic as a persistent and evolving human practice rather than a mere relic. It is a reliable orienting reference. For this corpus it provides scholarly grounding on magic as a historical phenomenon.
