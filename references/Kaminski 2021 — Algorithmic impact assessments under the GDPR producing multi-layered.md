# Algorithmic impact assessments under the GDPR: producing multi-layered explanations

## Citation
Kaminski, M. E., & Malgieri, G. (2021). Algorithmic impact assessments under the GDPR: producing multi-layered explanations. International Data Privacy Law, 11(2), 125–144. https://doi.org/10.1093/idpl/ipaa020

## Annotation
Kaminski and Malgieri analyze algorithmic impact assessments under the GDPR, arguing that data-protection impact assessments can be leveraged to produce “multi-layered explanations” of automated systems that serve both individual and systemic accountability. Published in International Data Privacy Law, it is an influential contribution to algorithmic-accountability law. For this corpus it grounds legal mechanisms for explaining and assessing algorithmic systems.
