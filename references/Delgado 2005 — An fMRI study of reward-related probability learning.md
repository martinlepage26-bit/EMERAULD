## Annotation
Delgado and colleagues report an fMRI study of reward-related probability learning, examining how the brain represents reward and probability during learning. Published in NeuroImage, it belongs to cognitive and affective neuroscience. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized neuroimaging source on reward learning.
