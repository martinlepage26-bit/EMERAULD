# Photovoice: Concept, Methodology, and Use for Participatory Needs Assessment

## Citation
Wang, C., Burris, M. (1997). Photovoice: Concept, Methodology, and Use for Participatory Needs Assessment. Health Education &amp; Behavior, 24(3), 369-387. https://doi.org/10.1177/109019819702400309

## Annotation
Wang and Burris's article is the foundational statement of Photovoice, a participatory-action-research method in which community members use photography to document and reflect on their lives, foster critical dialogue, and reach policymakers. Published in Health Education & Behavior, it defines the method's goals and procedures and remains its canonical reference. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds Photovoice as a participatory visual method.
