# children’s voices

## Citation
Eldén, S. (2012). Inviting the messy: Drawing methods and ‘children’s voices’. Childhood, 20(1), 66-81. https://doi.org/10.1177/0907568212447243

## Annotation
Sara Eldén's “Inviting the messy” reflects on the use of drawing methods to access “children's voices” in research, examining both the promise and the methodological complications of visual and participatory methods with children. Published in Childhood, it contributes to childhood studies and qualitative methodology. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds reflexive use of arts-based methods with young participants.
