# Law as a Social System

## Citation
Luhmann, Niklas. Law as a Social System. Trans. Klaus A. Ziegert. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1993/2004.

## Annotation
Niklas Luhmann's Law as a Social System applies his systems theory to law, treating the legal system as an autopoietic, self-referential system that reproduces itself through the binary code legal/illegal and is “operationally closed” yet “cognitively open” to its environment. It is a major contribution to legal and social theory. For this corpus it grounds a systems-theoretic account of how law operates and maintains itself — relevant to how governance systems process and reproduce norms.
