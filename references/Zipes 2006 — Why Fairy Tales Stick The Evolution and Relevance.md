# Why Fairy Tales Stick: The Evolution and Relevance of a Genre

## Citation
Zipes, J. (2006) Why Fairy Tales Stick: The Evolution and Relevance of a Genre. New York: Routledge.

## Annotation
Jack Zipes's Why Fairy Tales Stick examines the evolution and enduring relevance of the fairy-tale genre, drawing on memetics and cultural evolution to explain why certain tales persist and adapt across time and media. Published by Routledge, it is a key work in folklore and fairy-tale studies. For narrative-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the cultural persistence and adaptation of story forms.
