# The Concept of Law

## Citation
Hart, H.L.A. The Concept of Law. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1961/1994.

## Annotation
H. L. A. Hart's The Concept of Law is a foundational work of analytic jurisprudence that reconstructs legal positivism, defining a legal system as the union of primary rules (of obligation) and secondary rules (of recognition, change, and adjudication), with the “rule of recognition” as its ultimate criterion of validity. Hart's debate with Fuller and later Dworkin is central to legal philosophy. For this corpus it grounds a canonical account of what law is and how legal authority is constituted.
