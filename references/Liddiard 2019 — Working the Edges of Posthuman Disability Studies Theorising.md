# Working the Edges of Posthuman Disability Studies: Theorising with Disabled Young People with Life-Limiting Impairments

## Citation
Liddiard, Kirsty, Runswick-Cole, Katherine, Goodley, Dan, Whitney, Simon, Vogelmann, Emma, Watts, Luke, Tatlow, Emily, Hall, Edward (2019) Working the Edges of Posthuman Disability Studies: Theorising with Disabled Young People with Life-Limiting Impairments. Sociology of Health & Illness, 41(8), pp. 1473–1487. doi: 10.1111/1467-9566.12962

## Annotation
Liddiard and colleagues work at the intersection of posthumanism and disability studies, co-theorizing with disabled young people who have life-limiting impairments and treating them as “theoretical provocateurs” rather than research subjects. Drawing on an ESRC project, the paper explores humanist and posthuman epistemologies to rethink the meaning of life, death, and disability. Published in Sociology of Health & Illness, it advances participatory, posthuman disability scholarship. For this corpus it grounds posthuman disability studies and co-produced theory.
