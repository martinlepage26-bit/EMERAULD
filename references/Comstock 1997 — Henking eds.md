# Henking, eds

## Citation
Comstock, Gary David, and Susan E. Henking, eds. Que(e)rying Religion: A Critical Anthology. New York: Continuum, 1997.

## Annotation
Que(e)rying Religion, edited by Gary David Comstock and Susan Henking, is a critical anthology that applies queer theory and lived queer experience to the study of religion across traditions. It brings together foundational and provocative essays on sexuality and the sacred. It remains a standard reference in queer religious studies.
