# Witching Culture: Folklore and Neo-Paganism in America

## Citation
Magliocco, Sabina. 2004. Witching Culture: Folklore and Neo-Paganism in America. Philadelphia: University of Pennsylvania Press.

## Annotation
Witching Culture: Folklore and Neo-Paganism in America by Sabina Magliocco is a foundational folkloristic ethnography of the American Pagan movement, attending to ritual, myth, and the politics of tradition and identity. It is widely cited in Pagan studies. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation form.)
