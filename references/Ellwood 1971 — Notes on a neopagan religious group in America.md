# Notes on a neopagan religious group in America

## Citation
Ellwood, R. S. (1971). Notes on a neopagan religious group in America. History of empowerment. Thousand Oaks, CA : Altamira Press.

## Annotation
Robert Ellwood's early article offers ethnographic notes on a Neo-Pagan religious group in America, an unusually early scholarly observation of the emerging movement. It documents beliefs and practices at a formative moment in the study of modern Paganism. (The canonical citation appears partly garbled/concatenated.) For this corpus it is an early primary reference in the historiography of American Neo-Paganism.
