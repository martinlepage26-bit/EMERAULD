# Governance of generative AI

## Citation
Taeihagh, A. (2025). Governance of generative AI. Policy and Society, 44(1), 1-22. https://doi.org/10.1093/polsoc/puaf001

## Annotation
Araz Taeihagh's “Governance of Generative AI” introduces a Policy and Society special issue on governing generative AI, laying out its key risks — hallucination, jailbreaking, training-data and validation issues, information leakage, opacity, and control challenges — and mapping the legal, organizational, political, and social challenges of governance. It is a strong public-policy counterpart to narrower technical arguments. For this corpus it grounds a comprehensive account of generative-AI governance and its underdevelopment relative to technical change.
