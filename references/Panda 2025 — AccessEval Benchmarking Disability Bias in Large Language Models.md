# AccessEval: Benchmarking Disability Bias in Large Language Models

## Citation
Panda, Srikant, Agarwal, Amit, Patel, Hitesh Laxmichand (2025) AccessEval: Benchmarking Disability Bias in Large Language Models. Proceedings of the 2025 Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing, pp. 32504–32530. doi: 10.18653/v1/2025.emnlp-main.1653

## Annotation
Panda, Agarwal, and Patel introduce AccessEval, a benchmark for measuring disability bias in large language models, providing a systematic way to evaluate how LLMs treat disability-related content. Presented at EMNLP 2025, it contributes evaluation infrastructure to fairness research on generative AI. For this corpus it grounds the benchmarking of disability bias in LLMs.
