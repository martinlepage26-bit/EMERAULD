# Digital Fandom: New Media Studies

## Citation
Booth, Paul. Digital Fandom: New Media Studies. Peter Lang, 2010.

## Annotation
Paul Booth's Digital Fandom examines how digital and social media reshape fan practices, communities, and creativity, arguing for new-media frameworks to understand participatory fan culture. Booth analyzes phenomena like wikis, blogs, and remix as sites where fans co-produce meaning. The book is a reference in fan and new-media studies. For media-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the analysis of participatory digital fandom.
