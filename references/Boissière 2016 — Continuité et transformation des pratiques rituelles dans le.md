## Annotation
Nathalie Boissière's French-language article examines the continuity and transformation of ritual practice in contemporary neo-Druidism, a movement developed in eighteenth-century Britain and now present across Western countries that seeks to restore the ancient religion of the Celtic peoples. Boissière shows how practitioners rehabilitate Celtic deities and the liturgical calendar while firmly rejecting one antique practice — animal and human sacrifice — revealing how modern Pagans selectively reconstruct tradition. Published via Crossref-indexed francophone scholarship, it contributes to Pagan and ritual studies. For this corpus it illustrates the selective reinvention of ritual in contemporary Paganism.
