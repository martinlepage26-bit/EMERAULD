# Rigorous creativity: AI, art and electronic life

## Citation
Manghani, S., Savage, T. (2025). Rigorous creativity: AI, art and electronic life. Journal of Visual Art Practice, 24(4), 427-449. https://doi.org/10.1080/14702029.2025.2577021

## Annotation
Manghani and Savage's “Rigorous creativity: AI, art and electronic life” examines the relationship between artificial intelligence, artistic creativity, and notions of “electronic life,” reflecting on what AI means for art practice and creative agency. Published in the Journal of Visual Art Practice, it contributes to art-theoretical engagement with AI. For this corpus it grounds humanities and arts perspectives on AI creativity.
