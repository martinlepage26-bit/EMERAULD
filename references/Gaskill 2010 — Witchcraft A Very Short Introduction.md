# Witchcraft: A Very Short Introduction

## Citation
Gaskill, M., 2010. Witchcraft: A Very Short Introduction. Oxford: Oxford University Press.

## Annotation
Malcolm Gaskill's Witchcraft: A Very Short Introduction is a concise, authoritative overview of witchcraft across history, from early-modern trials to modern meanings, written by a leading historian. It balances scholarly rigor with accessibility. It is a reliable orienting reference. For this corpus it provides historical grounding on witchcraft as a phenomenon.
