# The Logic of Practice

## Citation
Bourdieu, Pierre. The Logic of Practice. Trans. Richard Nice. Stanford: Stanford University Press, 1980/1990.

## Annotation
Pierre Bourdieu's The Logic of Practice is a central statement of his practice theory, developing the concepts of habitus, field, and capital to explain how durable dispositions generate action without conscious rule-following. Bourdieu seeks a path between objectivism and subjectivism, showing how social structures are embodied and reproduced through practice. It is a foundational work in sociology and anthropology. For this corpus it grounds accounts of embodied disposition, ritual practice, and social reproduction.
