# Gender hierarchies in reporting genocide: An analysis of the dehumanization of Palestinian men in Western media

## Citation
El Masry, N., Sawaf, Z., King, G. and Baroudi, S. (2025) ‘Gender hierarchies in reporting genocide: An analysis of the dehumanization of Palestinian men in Western media’, Communication, Culture and Critique, 18(4), pp. 310–321. doi: 10.1093/ccc/tcaf032.

## Annotation
El Masry and colleagues investigate how Western media coverage — specifically BBC and CNN language during the first three months of the war on Gaza — dehumanizes Palestinian men, using web scraping to collect and analyze thousands of articles for keyword frequency and framing. The study finds systematic dehumanization through ignoring or naturalizing the suffering and death of Palestinian men. Published in Communication, Culture & Critique, it contributes to critical media analysis of gender, race, and violence. For this corpus it grounds computational-media analysis of dehumanizing coverage.
