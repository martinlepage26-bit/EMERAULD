# Working Girls: Gender and Sexuality in Popular Cinema

## Citation
Tasker, Yvonne. Working Girls: Gender and Sexuality in Popular Cinema. Routledge, 1998.

## Annotation
Yvonne Tasker's Working Girls examines gender and sexuality in popular cinema, analyzing how film represents working women, action heroines, and the intersections of labor, desire, and the body. It is a key text in feminist film studies. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds analysis of gender and work in popular film.
