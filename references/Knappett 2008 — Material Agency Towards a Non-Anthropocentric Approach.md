# Material Agency: Towards a Non-Anthropocentric Approach

## Citation
Knappett, Carl, and Lambros Malafouris, eds. 2008. Material Agency: Towards a Non-Anthropocentric Approach. New York: Springer.

## Annotation
Edited by Carl Knappett and Lambros Malafouris, Material Agency argues for a non-anthropocentric approach in which material things participate in agency, drawing on archaeology, cognitive science, and material-culture studies. The volume develops ideas of distributed and material agency in dialogue with Actor-Network Theory and cognitive archaeology. For this corpus it grounds new-materialist and posthumanist accounts of the agency of objects — relevant to how we attribute agency to technologies.
