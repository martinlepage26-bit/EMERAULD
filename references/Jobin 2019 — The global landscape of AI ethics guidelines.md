# The global landscape of AI ethics guidelines

## Citation
Jobin, A., Ienca, M., Vayena, E. (2019). The global landscape of AI ethics guidelines. Nature Machine Intelligence, 1(9), 389-399. https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-019-0088-2

## Annotation
Jobin, Ienca, and Vayena's “The Global Landscape of AI Ethics Guidelines” is one of the first systematic surveys of published AI-ethics documents, finding convergence around a small set of principles (transparency, justice/fairness, non-maleficence, responsibility, privacy) alongside substantial divergence in how they are defined and operationalized. Their key insight — convergence on labels does not imply convergence on substance — is widely cited. For this corpus it grounds the claim that principles require mechanisms, central to AI governance.
