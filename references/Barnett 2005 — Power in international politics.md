# Power in international politics

## Citation
Barnett, M. and Duvall, R. (2005) ‘Power in international politics’, International Organization, 59(1), pp. 39–75. doi: 10.1017/S0020818305050010.

## Annotation
Barnett and Duvall's “Power in International Politics” is a canonical intervention that breaks the concept of power into four types — compulsory, institutional, structural, and productive — moving beyond the narrow “A gets B to do what B otherwise would not” model. The taxonomy allows analysts to see power operating through institutions, social structures, and the constitution of subjects, not just direct coercion. Published in International Organization, it is widely cited across IR and political sociology. For this corpus it supplies a disciplined vocabulary for analyzing power and authority.
