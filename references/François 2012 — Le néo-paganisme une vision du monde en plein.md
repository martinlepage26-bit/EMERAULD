# Le néo-paganisme: une vision du monde en plein essor

## Citation
François, S. (2012). Le néo-paganisme: une vision du monde en plein essor. Valence

## Annotation
Stéphane François's Le néo-paganisme examines contemporary neo-Paganism as a rising worldview, analyzing its currents, ideas, and cultural and political dimensions, including its sometimes ambiguous relationship to identity politics. (The canonical citation is truncated.) For this corpus it grounds a francophone scholarly account of neo-Paganism as a growing contemporary phenomenon.
