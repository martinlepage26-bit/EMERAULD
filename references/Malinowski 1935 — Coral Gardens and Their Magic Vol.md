# Coral Gardens and Their Magic, Vol

## Citation
Malinowski, B., 1935. Coral Gardens and Their Magic, Vol. 2: The Language of Magic and Gardening. London: Allen & Unwin.

## Annotation
Bronisław Malinowski's Coral Gardens and Their Magic is a classic ethnography of Trobriand horticulture, whose second volume — “The Language of Magic and Gardening” — offers a pioneering analysis of magical language and the pragmatic, context-bound nature of magical utterances. It is foundational for the anthropology of magic and language. For this corpus it grounds the study of magical speech and its social functions.
