# Listening People, Speaking Earth: Contemporary Paganism

## Citation
Harvey, G., 1997. Listening People, Speaking Earth: Contemporary Paganism. London: Hurst & Company.

## Annotation
Graham Harvey's Listening People, Speaking Earth surveys contemporary Paganism, foregrounding its animistic sensibility and its practitioners' relationships with the more-than-human world. It is a key text in the study of modern Paganism. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation/edition form.)
