## Annotation
Quinn-Nilas and colleagues report prevalence and predictors of sexual problems among midlife Canadian adults, drawing on a national survey. Published in The Journal of Sexual Medicine, it belongs to the sexual-health epidemiology literature. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized health-survey source.
