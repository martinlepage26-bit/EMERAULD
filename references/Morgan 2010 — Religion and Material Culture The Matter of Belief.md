# Religion and Material Culture: The Matter of Belief

## Citation
Morgan, David. 2010. Religion and Material Culture: The Matter of Belief. London: Routledge.

## Annotation
David Morgan's Religion and Material Culture: The Matter of Belief foregrounds the material dimensions of religion — objects, images, and embodied practice — arguing that belief is enacted through matter. Morgan is a leading scholar of visual and material religion. (This entry overlaps with the Meyer/Morgan/Paine/Plate edited volume listed elsewhere; the attribution differs.) For this corpus it grounds the material-religion approach.
