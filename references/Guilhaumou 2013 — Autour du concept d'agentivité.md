# Autour du concept d'agentivité

## Citation
Guilhaumou, J. (2013). Autour du concept d'agentivité. Rives méditerranéennes, 41,

## Annotation
Jacques Guilhaumou's article reflects on the concept of “agentivité” (agency), developing a multidimensional paradigm drawing especially on Judith Butler alongside historical and anthropological examples extending to Africa. Published in Rives méditerranéennes, it contributes francophone conceptual clarification of agency across discourse and history. For this corpus it grounds theorization of agency relevant to questions of subjecthood and action.
