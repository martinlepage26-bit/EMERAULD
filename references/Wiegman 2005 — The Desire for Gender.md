# The Desire for Gender.

## Citation
Wiegman, Robyn. "The Desire for Gender." In A Companion to Gender Studies, eds. Philomena Essed, David Theo Goldberg, and Audrey Kobayashi. Oxford: Blackwell, 2005.

## Annotation
Robyn Wiegman's “The Desire for Gender,” in A Companion to Gender Studies, reflects critically on the field of gender studies itself — its attachments, methods, and investments in the category of gender. Wiegman is a leading theorist of identity-based fields. For this corpus it grounds meta-theoretical reflection on gender studies as a field of knowledge.
