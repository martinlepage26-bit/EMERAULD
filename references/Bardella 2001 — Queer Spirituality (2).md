# Queer Spirituality.

## Citation
Bardella, Cristina. “Queer Spirituality.” Social Compass 48, no. 1 (2001): 117–138.

## Annotation
Cristina Bardella's “Queer Spirituality” examines efforts to articulate a queer theology that reinterprets religious traditions from the standpoint of homosexuality, proposing the notion of a Queer Spiritual Discourse. The article situates gay and lesbian theological hermeneutics within the broader field of liberation theology. Appearing in Social Compass, it is a reference for the study of religion and sexuality.
