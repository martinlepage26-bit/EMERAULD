# Stars

## Citation
Dyer, Richard. Stars. 2nd ed., British Film Institute, 1998.

## Annotation
Richard Dyer's Stars is a foundational study in film theory that analyzes the “star image” as a socially produced text encoding ideology, identity, and meaning, rather than a mere reflection of individual charisma. Dyer shows how stars mediate cultural contradictions around class, gender, race, and sexuality. It is a canonical reference in film and media studies. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the critical analysis of stardom and image.
