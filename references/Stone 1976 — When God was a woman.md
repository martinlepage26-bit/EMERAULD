# When God was a woman

## Citation
Stone, M. (1976). When God was a woman. New York : Barnes and Noble.

## Annotation
Merlin Stone's When God Was a Woman is an influential (and contested) work arguing that ancient societies worshipped a great Goddess before patriarchal religions suppressed her, offering a feminist reinterpretation of prehistory and myth. Though critiqued by historians, it profoundly shaped the feminist Goddess movement. For this corpus it is a key (and debated) source in the genealogy of Goddess spirituality, whose influence must itself be analyzed.
