# Language and Symbolic Power

## Citation
Bourdieu, Pierre. Language and Symbolic Power. Ed. John B. Thompson. Cambridge: Harvard University Press, 1991.

## Annotation
Language and Symbolic Power, edited and introduced by John B. Thompson, is the standard English collection of Pierre Bourdieu's writings on language, authority, and symbolic domination. Bourdieu argues that the power of words derives from the delegated authority of the speaker and the social conditions that make speech legitimate — “authorized language.” It is a key reference across sociology, linguistics, and theories of legitimacy. For this corpus it is the theoretical infrastructure behind the critique of hegemonic fluency and authoritative-sounding output.
