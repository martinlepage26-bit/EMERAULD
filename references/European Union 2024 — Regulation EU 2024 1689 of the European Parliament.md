# Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 June 2024 laying down harmonised rules on artificial intelligence (Artificial Intelligence Act)

## Citation
European Union. (2024). Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 June 2024 laying down harmonised rules on artificial intelligence (Artificial Intelligence Act).

## Annotation
This is the final adopted text of the EU Artificial Intelligence Act — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 — the world's first comprehensive, binding, horizontal AI law. It establishes a risk-based framework classifying AI systems (unacceptable, high, limited, minimal risk) with corresponding obligations, and creates governance and enforcement structures. As the culminating legislative instrument of EU AI regulation, it is a central primary source. For this corpus it is the anchor reference for binding AI governance.
