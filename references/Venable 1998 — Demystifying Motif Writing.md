# Demystifying Motif Writing

## Citation
Venable, L. (1998). Demystifying Motif Writing. Journal of Physical Education, Recreation &amp; Dance, 69(6), 32-35. https://doi.org/10.1080/07303084.1998.10605572

## Annotation
Lucy Venable's “Demystifying Motif Writing” explains motif notation, a system for recording movement derived from Labanotation, for dance and movement education. Published in JOPERD, it belongs to dance and movement-notation scholarship. Its relevance to this corpus is negligible/peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized source, likely an ingest artifact.
