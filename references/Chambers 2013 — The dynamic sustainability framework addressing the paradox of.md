# The dynamic sustainability framework: addressing the paradox of sustainment amid ongoing change

## Citation
Chambers, D., Glasgow, R., Stange, K. (2013). The dynamic sustainability framework: addressing the paradox of sustainment amid ongoing change. Implementation Science, 8(1). https://doi.org/10.1186/1748-5908-8-117

## Annotation
Chambers, Glasgow, and Stange propose the Dynamic Sustainability Framework to address the paradox of sustaining health interventions amid ongoing change, arguing against static notions of fidelity in favor of continued learning and adaptation. Published in Implementation Science, it is a widely cited framework in implementation and improvement science. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral but methodological, bearing on how programs are sustained and adapted over time.
