# Preventing Repeated Real World AI Failures by Cataloging Incidents: The AI Incident Database

## Citation
McGregor, S. (2020). Preventing Repeated Real World AI Failures by Cataloging Incidents: The AI Incident Database. arXiv preprint https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.08512

## Annotation
Sean McGregor's paper introduces the AI Incident Database, arguing that — as mature sectors like aviation do with incident databases — intelligent systems need a collective memory of real-world failures to avoid repeating the same harms. The database, an industrial/non-profit cooperative effort, catalogs AI incidents to support avoidance and mitigation. It is a foundational contribution to AI safety infrastructure. For this corpus it grounds the incident-reporting approach to AI governance and accountability.
