# Les théories de la magie dans les traditions anthropologiques anglaise et française.

## Citation
Keck, Frédéric. “Les théories de la magie dans les traditions anthropologiques anglaise et française.” Methodos 2 (2002).

## Annotation
Frédéric Keck's article opposes the French and English anthropological traditions on the problem of magic, addressing the rationality of magical efficacy and the transition to scientific rationality, and contrasting pragmatic (Malinowski, Evans-Pritchard) and structural approaches. Published in Methodos, it is a careful comparative analysis. (This entry duplicates the same article in a different citation form.)
