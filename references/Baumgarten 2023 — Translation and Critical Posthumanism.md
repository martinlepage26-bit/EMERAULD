# Translation and Critical Posthumanism

## Citation
Baumgarten, Stefan (2023) Translation and Critical Posthumanism.

## Annotation
Stefan Baumgarten's Translation and Critical Posthumanism brings translation studies into dialogue with posthumanist thought, questioning humanist assumptions about the translating subject, agency, and the boundaries between human and machine translation. It situates translation within debates on technology, the Anthropocene, and the decentering of the human. The work contributes to critical translation studies and the posthumanities. For this corpus it links language, translation, and posthumanist theory.
