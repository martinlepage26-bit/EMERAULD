# Introduction: Modern Western Magic

## Citation
Bogdan, H. (2012). Introduction: Modern Western Magic. Aries, 12(1), 1-16. https://doi.org/10.1163/147783512X614812

## Annotation
Henrik Bogdan's introduction to a special issue of Aries surveys the study of modern Western magic, mapping how ceremonial magic, esotericism, and occult currents have developed from the nineteenth century to the present. Bogdan situates magic as a serious object of academic inquiry within the field of Western esotericism. Published in a leading esotericism journal, it is a useful orienting reference. For this corpus it frames the scholarly study of modern magical practice and its lineages.
