# Institutions, Institutional Change and Economic Performance

## Citation
North, Douglass C. Institutions, Institutional Change and Economic Performance. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1990.

## Annotation
Douglass North's Institutions, Institutional Change and Economic Performance is a foundational (Nobel-winning) work of institutional economics defining institutions as the “rules of the game” — formal and informal constraints that structure human interaction and shape economic performance. North analyzes how institutions change and why some persist despite inefficiency. For this corpus it grounds a rigorous account of institutions as rule-systems — directly relevant to governance and path dependence.
