# QUAGOL: A guide for qualitative data analysis

## Citation
Dierckx de Casterlé, B., Gastmans, C., Bryon, E., Denier, Y. (2012). QUAGOL: A guide for qualitative data analysis. International Journal of Nursing Studies, 49(3), 360-371. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijnurstu.2011.09.012

## Annotation
Dierckx de Casterlé and colleagues present QUAGOL (the Qualitative Analysis Guide of Leuven), a structured method to support rigorous qualitative data analysis, developed in a nursing-research context. The guide offers a systematic yet flexible process from raw data to interpretation. Published in the International Journal of Nursing Studies, it is a widely used methods reference. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds a concrete approach to qualitative analysis.
