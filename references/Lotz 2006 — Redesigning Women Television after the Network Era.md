# Redesigning Women: Television after the Network Era

## Citation
Lotz, Amanda D. 2006. Redesigning Women: Television after the Network Era. Urbana: University of Illinois Press.

## Annotation
Amanda Lotz's Redesigning Women analyzes the representation of women on U.S. television in the “post-network” era, examining how shifts in the industry reshaped female-centered programming and gender politics on screen. It is a key text in television and gender studies. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds analysis of women, genre, and the changing television industry.
