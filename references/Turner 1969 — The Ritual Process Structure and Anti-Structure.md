# The Ritual Process: Structure and Anti-Structure

## Citation
Turner, Victor. 1969. The Ritual Process: Structure and Anti-Structure. Chicago: Aldine Publishing.

## Annotation
Victor Turner's The Ritual Process: Structure and Anti-Structure develops the influential concepts of liminality and communitas, analyzing how rites of passage move participants through a betwixt-and-between phase that generates powerful, structure-dissolving social bonds. It is one of the most influential works in the anthropology of ritual. For this corpus it grounds liminality and communitas as core ritual concepts.
