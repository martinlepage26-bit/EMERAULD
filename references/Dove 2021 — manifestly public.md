# manifestly public

## Citation
Dove, E., Chen, J. (2021). What does it mean for a data subject to make their personal data ‘manifestly public’? An analysis of GDPR Article 9(2)(e). International Data Privacy Law, 11(2), 107-124. https://doi.org/10.1093/idpl/ipab005

## Annotation
Dove and Chen analyze what it means, under GDPR Article 9(2)(e), for a data subject to make sensitive personal data “manifestly public,” a condition that lifts certain processing restrictions. They clarify the legal ambiguities and risks of this provision for privacy. Published in International Data Privacy Law, it is a focused data-protection analysis. For this corpus it grounds a specific but consequential question in data governance and consent.
