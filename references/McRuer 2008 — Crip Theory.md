# Crip Theory

## Citation
McRuer, R. (2008). Crip Theory. Cultural Signs of Queerness and Disability. Scandinavian Journal of Disability Research, 10(1), 67-69. https://doi.org/10.1080/15017410701880122

## Annotation
This short piece engages Robert McRuer's crip theory, which analyzes the “cultural signs of queerness and disability” and the system of “compulsory able-bodiedness” that parallels compulsory heterosexuality. Published in the Scandinavian Journal of Disability Research, it points to McRuer's influential framework linking queer and disability critique. For this corpus it grounds crip theory's analysis of ability, normativity, and queerness.
