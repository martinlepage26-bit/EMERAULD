# Investigating Charmed: The Magic Power of TV

## Citation
Beeler, Karin, and Stan Beeler, eds. 2006. Investigating Charmed: The Magic Power of TV. London: I.B. Tauris.

## Annotation
Edited by Karin and Stan Beeler, Investigating Charmed collects scholarly essays on the WB series about three sisters who discover they are powerful witches. Contributors read the show's “power of three,” its matrilineal transmission of magic and the Book of Shadows, and its distinctive world of female solidarity against demonic threats. The volume is part of the “Investigating” cult-TV series and contributes to media, gender, and witchcraft studies. For this corpus it links televisual witchcraft to questions of gender, kinship, and popular ritual imagery.
