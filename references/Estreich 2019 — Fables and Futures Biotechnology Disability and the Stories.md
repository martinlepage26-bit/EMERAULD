# Fables and Futures: Biotechnology, Disability, and the Stories We Tell Ourselves

## Citation
Estreich, George (2019) Fables and Futures: Biotechnology, Disability, and the Stories We Tell Ourselves. Cambridge, MA: The MIT Press.

## Annotation
George Estreich's Fables and Futures examines biotechnology and disability through the stories we tell about them, arguing that narratives about genetic technology, normalcy, and the future shape ethical and political choices. Drawing on personal experience and cultural criticism, Estreich foregrounds disabled lives against technological promises of “improvement.” Published by MIT Press, it contributes to disability studies and bioethics. For this corpus it links biotechnology, disability, and narrative.
