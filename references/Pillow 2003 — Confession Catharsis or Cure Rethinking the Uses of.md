# Confession, Catharsis, or Cure? Rethinking the Uses of Reflexivity as Methodological Power.

## Citation
Pillow, Wanda S. 2003. “Confession, Catharsis, or Cure? Rethinking the Uses of Reflexivity as Methodological Power.” International Journal of Qualitative Studies in Education 16 (2): 175–196.

## Annotation
Wanda Pillow's “Confession, Catharsis, or Cure?” critically interrogates the uses of reflexivity in qualitative research, warning against comfortable, redemptive forms of reflexivity and calling for “uncomfortable reflexivity” that unsettles the researcher's authority. Published in the International Journal of Qualitative Studies in Education, it is a widely cited methodological intervention. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds a critical account of reflexivity and its limits.
