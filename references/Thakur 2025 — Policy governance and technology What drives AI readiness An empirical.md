# Policy, governance, and technology: What drives AI readiness? An empirical study of 155 countries

## Citation
Thakur, S. (2025). Policy, governance, and technology: What drives AI readiness? An empirical study of 155 countries. Research Square. https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-6576371/v1

## Annotation
Thakur builds a cross-national empirical model of AI readiness across 155 countries, testing human capital, digital infrastructure, and governance quality as predictors. The value here is comparative and quantitative: it treats 'readiness' as a measurable institutional property rather than a rhetorical claim, which makes it usable as counter-evidence against maturity-model marketing. Preprint, not yet peer reviewed — cite for its dataset and construct design rather than as settled finding.
