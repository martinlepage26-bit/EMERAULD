# Sociologie et anthropologie

## Citation
Mauss, Marcel. Sociologie et anthropologie. Paris: Presses Universitaires de France, 1968.

## Annotation
Marcel Mauss's Sociologie et anthropologie gathers his key essays, notably The Gift and “Techniques of the Body,” which shaped the anthropology of reciprocity, obligation, and embodiment. It is a foundational collection. (This entry duplicates the same volume in a different citation form.)
