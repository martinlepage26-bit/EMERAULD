# Bricolage et itinéraires de sens.

## Citation
Lemieux, René. “Bricolage et itinéraires de sens.” Religiologiques 26 (2003): 11–34.

## Annotation
René Lemieux's “Bricolage et itinéraires de sens” examines how individuals assemble personalized religious meaning from plural sources, tracing the “itineraries of meaning” characteristic of contemporary spirituality. Published in Religiologiques, it is a reference on religious bricolage. (This entry duplicates the same article in a different citation form.)
