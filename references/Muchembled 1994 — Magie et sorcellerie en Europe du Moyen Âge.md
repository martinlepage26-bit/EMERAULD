# Magie et sorcellerie en Europe: du Moyen Âge à nos

## Citation
Muchembled, R. (dir.) (1994). Magie et sorcellerie en Europe: du Moyen Âge à nos

## Annotation
Edited by Robert Muchembled, Magie et sorcellerie en Europe surveys magic and witchcraft in Europe from the Middle Ages to the present, gathering historical studies of belief, practice, and persecution. It is a standard reference on the European history of magic. For this corpus it grounds the long historical arc of magic and witchcraft.
