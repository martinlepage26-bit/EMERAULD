# Power in International Politics

## Citation
Barnett, M., Duvall, R. (2005). Power in International Politics. International Organization, 59(01). https://doi.org/10.1017/S0020818305050010

## Annotation
Barnett and Duvall's article offers a foundational fourfold taxonomy of power — compulsory, institutional, structural, and productive — that expands analysis beyond direct coercion to the diffuse and constitutive workings of power. The framework has become a reference point for thinking about how power operates through rules, positions, and identities. Appearing in International Organization, it is a standard citation in the study of authority and governance. (This entry duplicates the same article in a different citation form.)
