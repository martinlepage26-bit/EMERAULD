# Modern Pagan Festivals: A Study in the Nature of Tradition

## Citation
Hutton, R. (2008). Modern Pagan Festivals: A Study in the Nature of Tradition. Folklore, 119(3), 251-273. https://doi.org/10.1080/00155870802352178

## Annotation
Ronald Hutton's “Modern Pagan Festivals: A Study in the Nature of Tradition” examines how contemporary Pagan festivals construct and legitimate tradition, using them as a case for thinking about invention, continuity, and authenticity in tradition-making. Published in Folklore, it exemplifies Hutton's rigorous historical approach. For this corpus it grounds critical analysis of tradition and invention in modern Paganism.
