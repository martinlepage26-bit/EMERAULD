# If We Build It, Will It Stay?

## Citation
GREENHALGH, T., MACFARLANE, F., BARTON‐SWEENEY, C., WOODARD, F. (2012). “If We Build It, Will It Stay?” A Case Study of the Sustainability of Whole‐System Change in London. The Milbank Quarterly, 90(3), 516-547. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1468-0009.2012.00673.x

## Annotation
Greenhalgh and colleagues present a case study, “If We Build It, Will It Stay?”, on the sustainability of whole-system organizational change in London's health services, analyzing why complex innovations do or do not endure. Published in The Milbank Quarterly, it contributes to implementation science and the study of health-system change. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral but methodological, bearing on the durability of institutional change.
