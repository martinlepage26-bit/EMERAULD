# Superheroes and the sacred: MCU morality and myth

## Citation
Veilleux, A., 2019. Superheroes and the sacred: MCU morality and myth. Theology and Film, 12(3), pp.112–131.

## Annotation
Antoine Veilleux's “Superheroes and the Sacred” analyzes morality and myth in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, reading superhero narratives through theological and mythological lenses. Published in Theology and Film, it contributes to religion-and-popular-culture studies. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the religious reading of superhero media.
