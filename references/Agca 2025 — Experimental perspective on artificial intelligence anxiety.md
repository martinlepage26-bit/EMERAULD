# Experimental perspective on artificial intelligence anxiety

## Citation
Agca, R. K., & Korkmaz, O. (2025). Experimental perspective on artificial intelligence anxiety. International Journal of Technology in Education, 8(1), 22-44. https://doi.org/10.46328/ijte.846

## Annotation
This mixed-methods study examines how an artificial-intelligence training program affects pre-service teachers' anxiety about AI and their views on integrating it into education. Using a quasi-experimental quantitative design alongside qualitative analysis, Agca and Korkmaz find that training does not simply reduce anxiety but redistributes it — lowering learning-related fear while heightening awareness of ethical, professional, and control-related risks. The result complicates the assumption that AI literacy straightforwardly dissolves concern. For AI-governance and AI-anxiety research, it supports treating literacy as something that can sharpen, rather than dissolve, governance-relevant concern.
