# Discipline and Punish: The Birth of the Prison

## Citation
Foucault, Michel. Discipline and Punish: The Birth of the Prison. Trans. Alan Sheridan. New York: Pantheon, 1975/1977.

## Annotation
Discipline and Punish (Surveiller et punir) analyzes the emergence of modern disciplinary power, showing how surveillance, examination, and normalization replace spectacular punishment and produce subjects through institutions like the prison, school, and factory. Foucault's Panopticon has become a defining image for surveillance societies. (This entry duplicates the same work in its English translation.)
