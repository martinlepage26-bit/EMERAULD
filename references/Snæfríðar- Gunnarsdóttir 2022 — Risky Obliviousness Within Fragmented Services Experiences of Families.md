# Risky Obliviousness Within Fragmented Services: Experiences of Families With Disabled Children During the Covid‐19 Pandemic

## Citation
Snæfríðar- Gunnarsdóttir, H., Ólafsdóttir, T., Björnsdóttir, K. (2022). Risky Obliviousness Within Fragmented Services: Experiences of Families With Disabled Children During the Covid‐19 Pandemic. Social Inclusion, 11(1). https://doi.org/10.17645/si.v11i1.5642

## Annotation
Snæfríðar-Gunnarsdóttir, Ólafsdóttir, and Björnsdóttir examine the experiences of families with disabled children during the COVID-19 pandemic, documenting “risky obliviousness” within fragmented services that left such families under-supported. Published in Social Inclusion, it contributes to disability and family-services research. For this corpus it grounds how fragmented systems fail disabled children and their families in crisis.
