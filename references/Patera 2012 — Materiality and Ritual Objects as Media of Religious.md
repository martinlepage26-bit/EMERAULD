# Materiality and Ritual: Objects as Media of Religious Action.

## Citation
Patera, Ioanna. 2012. “Materiality and Ritual: Objects as Media of Religious Action.” Method & Theory in the Study of Religion 24 (4–5): 388–407.

## Annotation
Ioanna Patera's “Materiality and Ritual” examines how objects function as media of religious action, arguing that material things are not mere symbols but active components of ritual practice. Published in Method & Theory in the Study of Religion, it contributes to the material-religion and ritual-studies literatures. For this corpus it grounds the role of objects in ritual efficacy.
