# Artificial intelligence in rare disease diagnosis and treatment

## Citation
Wojtara, Magda, Rana, Emaan, Rahman, Taibia, Khanna, Palak, Singh, Heshwin (2023) Artificial intelligence in rare disease diagnosis and treatment. Clinical and translational science, 16(11), 2106-2111. doi: 10.1111/cts.13619

## Annotation
Wojtara and colleagues review the use of artificial intelligence in the diagnosis and treatment of rare diseases, where limited data and expertise make AI both promising and challenging. Published in Clinical and Translational Science, it contributes to medical-AI scholarship. For this corpus it grounds AI applications and their governance in a specialized clinical domain.
