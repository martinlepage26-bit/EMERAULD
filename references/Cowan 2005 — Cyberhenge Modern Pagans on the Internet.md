# Cyberhenge: Modern Pagans on the Internet

## Citation
Cowan, D.E., 2005. Cyberhenge: Modern Pagans on the Internet. New York: Routledge.

## Annotation
Douglas Cowan's Cyberhenge examines how modern Pagans use the Internet to build community, share ritual, and construct identity, one of the earliest sustained studies of Paganism online. Cowan analyzes both the opportunities and the limits of virtual religious practice. It is a foundational reference in the study of digital Paganism. For this corpus it connects contemporary Paganism to networked and digital religious practice.
