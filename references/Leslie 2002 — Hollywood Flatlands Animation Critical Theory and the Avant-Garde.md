# Hollywood Flatlands: Animation, Critical Theory and the Avant-Garde

## Citation
Leslie, E. (2002) Hollywood Flatlands: Animation, Critical Theory and the Avant-Garde. London: Verso.

## Annotation
Esther Leslie's Hollywood Flatlands connects the history of animation to critical theory and the avant-garde, reading early cartoons through thinkers such as Walter Benjamin and Theodor Adorno to reveal the political and utopian dimensions of animated form. Published by Verso, it is a significant work in animation and cultural theory. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the critical-theoretical analysis of animation.
