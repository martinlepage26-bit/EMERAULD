# Human-AI governance (HAIG): A trust-utility approach

## Citation
Engin, Z. (2026). Human-AI governance (HAIG): A trust-utility approach. Journal of Responsible Technology, 26, 100167. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jrt.2026.100167

## Annotation
Engin argues that as AI systems are deployed across contexts, agency redistributes between humans and machines in patterns that existing governance models fail to track, and proposes a trust-utility framework for analysing that redistribution. Directly relevant to recursive-governance work: it supplies vocabulary for the shifting human/machine authority boundary rather than treating it as fixed. Peer-reviewed and citable.
