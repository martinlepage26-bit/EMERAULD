# The Carbon Diaries 2015

## Citation
Lloyd, Saci (2011) The Carbon Diaries 2015. Hachette UK.

## Annotation
Saci Lloyd's The Carbon Diaries 2015 is a young-adult climate-fiction novel imagining life in a Britain that has imposed carbon rationing, told through a teenager's diary. As a work of cli-fi, it dramatizes climate crisis, adaptation, and everyday life under environmental constraint. For this corpus it is a primary literary source in the cultural imagination of climate and the Anthropocene.
