# Do Artifacts Have Politics?

## Citation
Winner, Langdon. "Do Artifacts Have Politics?" Daedalus 109, no. 1 (1980): 121–136.

## Annotation
Langdon Winner's “Do Artifacts Have Politics?” is a foundational STS essay arguing that technologies can embody political qualities — either by settling issues in a community (as with Robert Moses's low bridges) or by being inherently tied to particular power arrangements. It is one of the most cited texts in the field. For this corpus it grounds the claim that technical artifacts — including AI systems — carry politics and distribute power.
