# AI Risk Management Framework: Initial Draft

## Citation
NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF STANDARDS AND TECHNOLOGY (2022a). AI Risk Management Framework: Initial Draft. 17 mars. En ligne: https://www.nist.gov/system/files/documents/2022/03/17/AI-RMF-1stdraft.pdf.

## Annotation
This is the initial public draft of the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (2022), released for comment as NIST developed its voluntary framework for managing AI risks. As a governance-development document, it shows the framework's evolution toward the final AI RMF 1.0. For this corpus it grounds the drafting process of a central AI-governance instrument.
