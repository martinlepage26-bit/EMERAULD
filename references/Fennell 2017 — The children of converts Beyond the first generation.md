# The children of converts: Beyond the first generation of contemporary Pagans

## Citation
Fennell, J., Wildman-Hanlon, L. (2017). The children of converts: Beyond the first generation of contemporary Pagans. Social Compass, 64(2), 288-306. https://doi.org/10.1177/0037768617704165

## Annotation
Fennell and Wildman-Hanlon study the adult religious retention of children raised in contemporary Paganism, an understudied question for new religious movements. Using an internet sample, they find that a substantial share continue as Pagan or “spiritually Pagan” adults, with childhood religiosity a strong predictor. Published in Social Compass, it contributes empirical data on the intergenerational transmission of Paganism. For this corpus it grounds the sociology of Pagan continuity across generations.
