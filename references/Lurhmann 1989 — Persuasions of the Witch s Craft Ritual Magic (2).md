# Persuasions of the Witch’s Craft: Ritual Magic in Contemporary England

## Citation
Lurhmann, T.M., 1989. Persuasions of the Witch’s Craft: Ritual Magic in Contemporary England. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press.

## Annotation
Persuasions of the Witch's Craft by T. M. Luhrmann is a foundational ethnography of contemporary English ritual magic, showing through the concept of interpretive drift how educated people become absorbed into magical worldviews. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation form.)
