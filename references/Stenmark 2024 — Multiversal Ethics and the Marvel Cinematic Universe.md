## Annotation
Mikael Stenmark's Multiversal Ethics and the Marvel Cinematic Universe uses the MCU's multiverse to examine ethical questions about identity, responsibility, and moral choice across possible worlds. Published by Cambridge University Press, it bridges popular-culture analysis and moral philosophy. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds philosophical reading of the MCU.
