# INTERSECTIONALITY UNDONE

## Citation
Bilge, S. (2013). INTERSECTIONALITY UNDONE. Du Bois Review: Social Science Research on Race, 10(2), 405-424. https://doi.org/10.1017/S1742058X13000283

## Annotation
Sirma Bilge's “Intersectionality Undone” warns that intersectionality, as it has been institutionalized in the academy, risks being depoliticized — “whitened” and disciplined into a diversity ornament stripped of its roots in Black feminist and anti-racist struggle. Bilge calls for reclaiming intersectionality's critical and activist commitments. Published in the Du Bois Review, it is a widely cited intervention. For this corpus it disciplines the use of “intersectionality” and foregrounds its political stakes.
