# Situated Knowledges: The Science Question in Feminism and the Privilege of Partial Perspective

## Citation
Haraway, Donna (1988) Situated Knowledges: The Science Question in Feminism and the Privilege of Partial Perspective. Feminist Studies, 14(3), pp. 575–599. doi: 10.2307/3178066

## Annotation
Donna Haraway's “Situated Knowledges” argues for a feminist objectivity grounded in partial, located, embodied perspectives — knowledge that is accountable to its position rather than claiming a disembodied view from nowhere. It is a cornerstone of feminist epistemology and science studies. (This entry duplicates the same article in a different citation form.)
