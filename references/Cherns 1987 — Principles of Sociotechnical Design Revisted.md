# Principles of Sociotechnical Design Revisted

## Citation
Cherns, A. (1987). Principles of Sociotechnical Design Revisted. Human Relations, 40(3), 153-161. https://doi.org/10.1177/001872678704000303

## Annotation
Albert Cherns's “Principles of Sociotechnical Design Revisited” updates his influential 1976 principles for designing work systems that jointly optimize social and technical requirements. Cherns argues that the principles governing the design process are tightly bound to those governing the design itself, and gives more attention to organizational needs. Published in Human Relations, it is a foundational reference in sociotechnical-systems theory. For this corpus it grounds the idea that technical systems must be designed with their social organization in mind — relevant to AI system design and governance.
