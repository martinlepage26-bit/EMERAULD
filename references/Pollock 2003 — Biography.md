# Biography

## Citation
Pollock, N., Williams, R., Procter, R. (2003). Fitting Standard Software Packages to Non-standard Organizations: The ‘Biography’ of an Enterprise-wide System. Technology Analysis &amp; Strategic Management, 15(3), 317-332. https://doi.org/10.1080/09537320310001601504

## Annotation
Pollock, Williams, and Procter's “Fitting Standard Software Packages to Non-standard Organizations” traces the “biography” of an enterprise-wide system, showing how generic software and particular organizations are mutually adapted over time. Published in Technology Analysis & Strategic Management, it is an STS-informed study of information systems in practice. For this corpus it grounds the negotiated fit between standardized technologies and organizational realities.
