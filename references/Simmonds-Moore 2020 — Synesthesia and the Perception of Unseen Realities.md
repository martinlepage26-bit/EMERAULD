# Synesthesia and the Perception of Unseen Realities

## Citation
Simmonds-Moore, C. (2020). Synesthesia and the Perception of Unseen Realities. Journal of Humanistic Psychology, 62(2), 187-207. https://doi.org/10.1177/0022167820918691

## Annotation
Christine Simmonds-Moore's article examines synesthesia in relation to “exceptional experiences” (paranormal and transpersonal), arguing that synesthetic hyperconnectivity may enable perception of information usually inaccessible — abstract, preconscious, or ostensibly nonlocal. Published in the Journal of Humanistic Psychology, it belongs to parapsychology and transpersonal psychology. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral, bearing on the psychology of anomalous experience.
