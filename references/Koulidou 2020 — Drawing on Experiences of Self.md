# Drawing on Experiences of Self

## Citation
Koulidou, N., Wallace, J., Sturdee, M., Durrant, A. (2020). Drawing on Experiences of Self. Proceedings of the 2020 ACM Designing Interactive Systems Conference, 255-267. https://doi.org/10.1145/3357236.3395513

## Annotation
Koulidou and colleagues' “Drawing on Experiences of Self” explores how drawing can be used in design research to access and represent personal, reflective experience. Presented at the ACM Designing Interactive Systems conference, it contributes to design methods and arts-based inquiry. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds drawing as a reflective design and research method.
