# Sexe, genre et sexualités : introduction à la théorie féministe

## Citation
Dorlin, E. (2008). Sexe, genre et sexualités : introduction à la théorie féministe. Paris :

## Annotation
Elsa Dorlin's Sexe, genre et sexualités is a widely used introduction to feminist theory, clarifying the concepts of sex, gender, and sexuality and mapping the major debates and traditions of feminist thought. It is a standard francophone pedagogical reference. For this corpus it grounds the conceptual vocabulary of feminist and gender theory for French readers.
