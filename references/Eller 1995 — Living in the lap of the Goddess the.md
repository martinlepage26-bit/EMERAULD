# Living in the lap of the Goddess : the feminist spirituality movement

## Citation
Eller, C. (1995). Living in the lap of the Goddess : the feminist spirituality movement

## Annotation
Cynthia Eller's Living in the Lap of the Goddess is a critical yet sympathetic study of the feminist spirituality movement in America, examining its beliefs, rituals, and politics, and the appeal of Goddess religion to feminist women. Eller analyzes the movement's use of history and myth while raising critical questions about its claims. It is a key scholarly reference on feminist spirituality. For this corpus it grounds the study of the Goddess movement and its gender politics.
