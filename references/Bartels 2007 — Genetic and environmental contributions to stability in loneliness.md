# Genetic and environmental contributions to stability in loneliness throughout childhood

## Citation
Bartels, M., Cacioppo, J., Hudziak, J., Boomsma, D. (2007). Genetic and environmental contributions to stability in loneliness throughout childhood. American Journal of Medical Genetics Part B: Neuropsychiatric Genetics, 147B(3), 385-391. https://doi.org/10.1002/ajmg.b.30608

## Annotation
Bartels and colleagues report a twin study on the genetic and environmental contributions to the stability of loneliness across childhood, using behavioral-genetic modeling. They find both heritable and environmental influences shaping how loneliness persists over time. Published in a neuropsychiatric-genetics journal, it belongs to the developmental behavior-genetics literature. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized empirical source on loneliness and heritability.
