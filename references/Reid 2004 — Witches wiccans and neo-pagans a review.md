# Witches, wiccans and neo-pagans: a review

## Citation
Reid, S. L. et Rabinovitch, S. T. (2004). Witches, wiccans and neo-pagans: a review

## Annotation
Reid and Rabinovitch's article surveys the emerging study of the Neo-Pagan movement within new-religious-movement scholarship, reviewing the primary literature and then analyzing the movement through late-modern theorists (Giddens, Maffesoli, Habermas), with attention to the hotly debated question of Neo-Pagan institutionalization. Published via a peer-reviewed venue, it is a useful field survey. For this corpus it grounds the theoretical framing of Neo-Paganism and debates over its institutionalization.
