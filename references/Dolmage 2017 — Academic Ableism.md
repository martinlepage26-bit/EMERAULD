# Academic Ableism

## Citation
Dolmage, J. (2017). Academic Ableism. University of Michigan Press. https://doi.org/10.3998/mpub.9708722

## Annotation
Jay Dolmage's Academic Ableism examines how the structures, spaces, and cultures of higher education systematically exclude and devalue disabled people, tracing ableism through the university's history and design. Dolmage advocates universal design and a rethinking of academic norms. Published by the University of Michigan Press, it is a key text in disability studies and critical university studies. For this corpus it grounds the analysis of ableism in knowledge institutions.
