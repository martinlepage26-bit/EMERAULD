# L’eau et les rêves: essai sur l’imagination de la matière

## Citation
Bachelard, Gaston. 1942. L’eau et les rêves: essai sur l’imagination de la matière. Paris: José Corti.

## Annotation
Gaston Bachelard's L'eau et les rêves develops his phenomenology of the material imagination, focusing on water as an element that structures poetic reverie and image. Part of his series on the four elements, it argues that imagination is organized less by form than by material substance and its dynamic qualities. The work is foundational for the study of poetic imagination and literary phenomenology. For this corpus it grounds an approach to imagery, symbol, and the imaginative substrate of cultural texts.
