# Reassembling the Social: An Introduction to Actor-Network-Theory

## Citation
Latour, Bruno. 2005. Reassembling the Social: An Introduction to Actor-Network-Theory. Oxford: Oxford University Press.

## Annotation
Bruno Latour's Reassembling the Social is the definitive introduction to Actor-Network Theory, reconceiving sociology as the tracing of heterogeneous associations among human and nonhuman actors and rejecting “the social” as a stable substance or cause. It is one of the most influential methodological statements in STS. For this corpus it grounds ANT as a way of following how technologies, people, and things are linked into networks.
