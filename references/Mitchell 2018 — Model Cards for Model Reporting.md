# Model Cards for Model Reporting

## Citation
Mitchell, M., Wu, S., Zaldivar, A., Barnes, P., et al. (2018). Model Cards for Model Reporting. arXiv preprint https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.03993

## Annotation
Mitchell and colleagues' “Model Cards for Model Reporting” (arXiv preprint) proposes standardized short documents accompanying machine-learning models that disclose intended use, performance across relevant groups, limitations, and ethical considerations. It pushes accountability into a repeatable reporting format. It became a foundational reference in responsible AI. For this corpus it grounds the model-documentation layer of AI governance.
