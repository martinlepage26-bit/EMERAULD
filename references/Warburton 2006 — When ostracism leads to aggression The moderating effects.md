# When ostracism leads to aggression: The moderating effects of control deprivation

## Citation
Warburton, W., Williams, K., Cairns, D. (2006). When ostracism leads to aggression: The moderating effects of control deprivation. Journal of Experimental Social Psychology, 42(2), 213-220. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jesp.2005.03.005

## Annotation
Warburton, Williams, and Cairns experimentally examine when ostracism leads to aggression, finding that control deprivation moderates the aggressive response to social exclusion. Published in the Journal of Experimental Social Psychology, it belongs to the social psychology of ostracism. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized empirical source.
