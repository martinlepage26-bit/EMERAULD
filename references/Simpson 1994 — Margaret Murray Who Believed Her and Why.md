# Margaret Murray: Who Believed Her, and Why?

## Citation
Simpson, Jacqueline. “Margaret Murray: Who Believed Her, and Why?” Folklore 105 (1994): 89–96.

## Annotation
Jacqueline Simpson's “Margaret Murray: Who Believed Her, and Why?” examines the surprisingly broad acceptance of Murray's now-rejected witch-cult theory, analyzing the reasons for its influence. Published in Folklore, it is a standard reference on the Murray controversy. (This entry duplicates the same article in a different citation form.)
