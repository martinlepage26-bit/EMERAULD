# Necropolitics

## Citation
Mbembe, A. (2019) Necropolitics. Durham, NC: Duke University Press.

## Annotation
Achille Mbembe's Necropolitics extends and revises Foucault's biopolitics, arguing that sovereignty in the contemporary world often operates as the power to dictate who may live and who must die — deploying “death-worlds” through colonialism, occupation, and states of exception. It is a foundational text in postcolonial theory and critical political thought. For this corpus it grounds a critical analysis of power over life and death — relevant to the politics of who is protected or abandoned by systems.
