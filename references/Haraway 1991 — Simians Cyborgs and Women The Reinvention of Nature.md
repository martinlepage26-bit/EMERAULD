# Simians, Cyborgs, and Women: The Reinvention of Nature

## Citation
Haraway, Donna J. 1991. Simians, Cyborgs, and Women: The Reinvention of Nature. New York: Routledge.

## Annotation
Donna Haraway's Simians, Cyborgs, and Women collects her influential essays, including “A Cyborg Manifesto,” which uses the figure of the cyborg to dissolve the boundaries between human/machine, organism/technology, and nature/culture, offering an ironic, anti-essentialist feminist politics. It is a foundational text of posthumanism, feminist theory, and science studies. For this corpus it grounds the cyborg as a figure for hybridity and the critique of natural/technological dualisms.
