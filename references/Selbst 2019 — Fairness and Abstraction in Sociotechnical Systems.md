# Fairness and Abstraction in Sociotechnical Systems

## Citation
Selbst, A., Boyd, D., Friedler, S., Venkatasubramanian, S., et al. (2019). Fairness and Abstraction in Sociotechnical Systems. Proceedings of the Conference on Fairness, Accountability, and Transparency, 59-68. https://doi.org/10.1145/3287560.3287598

## Annotation
Selbst, Boyd, Friedler, Venkatasubramanian, and Vertesi's “Fairness and Abstraction in Sociotechnical Systems” is a foundational critique arguing that computer-science approaches to fairness fail when they abstract away the social context, identifying five “abstraction traps.” They insist that fairness is a property of sociotechnical systems, not algorithms alone. Presented at FAccT 2019, it is highly influential. For this corpus it grounds why technical fairness fixes fail without attention to social context.
