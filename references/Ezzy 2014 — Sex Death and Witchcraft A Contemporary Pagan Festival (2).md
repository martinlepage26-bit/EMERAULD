# Sex, Death, and Witchcraft: A Contemporary Pagan Festival

## Citation
Ezzy, Douglas. Sex, Death, and Witchcraft: A Contemporary Pagan Festival. London: Bloomsbury Academic, 2014.

## Annotation
Douglas Ezzy's Sex, Death and Witchcraft: A Contemporary Pagan Festival analyzes the Faunalia festival as a site of intense, embodied ritual dealing with sexuality, death, and personal transformation among contemporary Pagans. It contributes to the anthropology of ritual and Pagan studies. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation form.)
