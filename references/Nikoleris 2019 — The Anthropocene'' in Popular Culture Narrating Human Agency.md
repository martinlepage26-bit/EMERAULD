# The ``Anthropocene'' in Popular Culture: Narrating Human Agency, Force, and Our Place on Earth

## Citation
Nikoleris, Alexandra, Stripple, Johannes, Tenngart, Paul (2019) The ``Anthropocene'' in Popular Culture: Narrating Human Agency, Force, and Our Place on Earth. Anthropocene Encounters: New Directions in Green Political Thinking, pp. 67–84. doi: 10.1017/9781108646673.004

## Annotation
Nikoleris, Stripple, and Tenngart examine how the “Anthropocene” is narrated in popular culture, analyzing representations of human agency, force, and humanity's place on Earth in fiction and media. Published in Anthropocene Encounters, it connects environmental humanities and cultural analysis. For this corpus it grounds the cultural storytelling of the Anthropocene.
