# The Mythos of Model Interpretability

## Citation
Lipton, Z. (2018). The Mythos of Model Interpretability. Queue, 16(3), 31-57. https://doi.org/10.1145/3236386.3241340

## Annotation
Zachary Lipton's “The Mythos of Model Interpretability” is an influential critical analysis arguing that “interpretability” in machine learning is an ill-defined, overloaded notion covering distinct desiderata (trust, causality, transferability, fairness). Lipton disentangles what people actually want from interpretable models and questions common assumptions. Published in ACM Queue, it is widely cited in explainable-AI debates. For this corpus it grounds a rigorous critique of interpretability claims central to AI accountability.
