# Burden of Sexual Dysfunction

## Citation
Balon, R. (2015). Burden of Sexual Dysfunction. Journal of Sex &amp; Marital Therapy, 43(1), 49-55. https://doi.org/10.1080/0092623X.2015.1113597

## Annotation
Richard Balon's article surveys the burden of sexual dysfunction, reviewing its prevalence, correlates, and clinical significance across populations. Published in the Journal of Sex & Marital Therapy, it belongs to the sexual-medicine literature and functions as an overview of the epidemiological and clinical stakes of the topic. Its place in this corpus is peripheral to the core themes; it is best treated as a specialized medical reference on sexual dysfunction.
