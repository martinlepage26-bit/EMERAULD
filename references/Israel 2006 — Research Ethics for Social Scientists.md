# Research Ethics for Social Scientists

## Citation
Israel, Mark, and Iain Hay. 2006. Research Ethics for Social Scientists. London: Sage.

## Annotation
Israel and Hay's Research Ethics for Social Scientists is a widely used guide to the principles, regulation, and practice of ethics in social research, covering consent, confidentiality, harm, and institutional review. It is a standard reference for training researchers. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the ethics of social-science inquiry.
