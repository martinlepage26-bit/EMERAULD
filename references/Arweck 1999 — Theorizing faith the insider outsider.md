# Theorizing faith : the insider/outsider

## Citation
Arweck, E. et Stringer, M. D. (dir.) (1999). Theorizing faith : the insider/outsider

## Annotation
Edited by Elisabeth Arweck and Martin Stringer, Theorizing Faith addresses the enduring methodological problem of the insider/outsider distinction in the study of religion. The contributors examine how a researcher's position — believer or non-believer, participant or observer — shapes access, interpretation, and authority in religious ethnography. The volume is a useful reference for reflexive method in religious studies. For research on ritual and lived religion in this corpus, it grounds questions of standpoint and interpretive legitimacy.
