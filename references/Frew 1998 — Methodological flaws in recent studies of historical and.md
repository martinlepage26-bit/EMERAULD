# Methodological flaws in recent studies of historical and modem

## Citation
Frew, D. H. (1998). Methodological flaws in recent studies of historical and modem

## Annotation
Don Frew's article critiques methodological flaws in recent scholarship on historical and modern witchcraft, arguing (from a position sympathetic to contemporary Craft) that some academic studies mishandle evidence about continuity and practice. Published in Ethnologies, it is a pointed intervention in debates over witchcraft historiography. For this corpus it flags methodological stakes in studying the relationship between historical and modern witchcraft.
