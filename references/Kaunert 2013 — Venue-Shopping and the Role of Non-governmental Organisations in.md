# Venue-Shopping and the Role of Non-governmental Organisations in the Development of the European Union Asylum Policy

## Citation
Kaunert, C., Léonard, S., Hoffmann, U. (2013). Venue-Shopping and the Role of Non-governmental Organisations in the Development of the European Union Asylum Policy. Comparative Migration Studies, 1(1), 179-200. https://doi.org/10.5117/CMS2013.1.KAUN

## Annotation
Kaunert, Léonard, and Hoffmann analyze “venue-shopping” and the role of non-governmental organizations in shaping European Union asylum policy, showing how actors move between institutional arenas to advance their aims. Published in Comparative Migration Studies, it contributes to migration and EU-policy studies. For this corpus it grounds analysis of how policy actors exploit institutional venues — relevant to governance dynamics.
