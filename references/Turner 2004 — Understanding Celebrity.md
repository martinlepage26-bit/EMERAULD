# Understanding Celebrity

## Citation
Turner, G. (2004) Understanding Celebrity. London: SAGE Publications.

## Annotation
Graeme Turner's Understanding Celebrity is a foundational textbook in celebrity studies, analyzing celebrity as a cultural and industrial phenomenon — how fame is produced, circulated, and consumed, and what it reveals about media, identity, and value. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the critical study of celebrity.
