# Magie et sorcellerie en Europe: du Moyen Âge à nos jours

## Citation
Muchembled, Robert, ed. Magie et sorcellerie en Europe: du Moyen Âge à nos jours. Paris: Armand Colin, 1994.

## Annotation
Magie et sorcellerie en Europe, edited by Robert Muchembled, offers a historical survey of magic and witchcraft in Europe from the medieval period to modern times, synthesizing scholarship on belief and persecution. It is a key reference collection. (This entry duplicates the same volume in a different citation form.)
