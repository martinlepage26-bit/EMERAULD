# Situated Knowledges: The Science Question in Feminism and the Privilege of Partial Perspective

## Citation
Haraway, D. (1988). Situated Knowledges: The Science Question in Feminism and the Privilege of Partial Perspective. Feminist Studies, 14(3), 575. https://doi.org/10.2307/3178066

## Annotation
Donna Haraway's “Situated Knowledges” is a foundational text of feminist epistemology, arguing against both totalizing objectivity (the “god trick” of seeing everything from nowhere) and pure relativism, in favor of situated, embodied, accountable knowledge from partial perspectives. Haraway reframes objectivity as located and responsible rather than detached. It is among the most cited works in feminist science studies. For this corpus it grounds a critical epistemology of objectivity, perspective, and accountability — relevant to claims of neutral, view-from-nowhere AI.
