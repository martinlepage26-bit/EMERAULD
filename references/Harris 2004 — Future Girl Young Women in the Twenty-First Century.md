# Future Girl: Young Women in the Twenty-First Century

## Citation
Harris, Anita. 2004. Future Girl: Young Women in the Twenty-First Century. New York: Routledge.

## Annotation
Anita Harris's Future Girl analyzes how young women are constructed in the twenty-first century as either “can-do” girls (empowered neoliberal subjects of choice and success) or “at-risk” girls (objects of anxiety and surveillance). Harris links girlhood to neoliberal governance and consumer culture. It is a key text in girlhood and youth studies. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the analysis of gendered youth under neoliberalism.
