# Being a Witch Online: Pagan Pathways on the Internet.

## Citation
Klassen, Chris. 2002. “Being a Witch Online: Pagan Pathways on the Internet.” Journal of Contemporary Religion 17 (1): 83–99.

## Annotation
Chris Klassen's “Being a Witch Online” examines Pagan pathways on the Internet, analyzing how practitioners use online spaces to learn, connect, and practice Witchcraft, and how digital mediation reshapes solitary and communal practice. Published in the Journal of Contemporary Religion, it is a companion study to her cybercoven work. For this corpus it grounds the study of online Pagan practice and community.
