# Chemsex behaviours among men who have sex with men: A systematic review of the literature

## Citation
Maxwell, S., Shahmanesh, M., Gafos, M. (2019). Chemsex behaviours among men who have sex with men: A systematic review of the literature. International Journal of Drug Policy, 63, 74-89. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.drugpo.2018.11.014

## Annotation
Maxwell, Shahmanesh, and Gafos conduct a systematic review of chemsex behaviours among men who have sex with men, synthesizing prevalence, motivations, and associated health risks. Published in the International Journal of Drug Policy, it is a key public-health reference on the topic. For work on queer sexuality and health in this corpus, it grounds the evidence base on chemsex.
