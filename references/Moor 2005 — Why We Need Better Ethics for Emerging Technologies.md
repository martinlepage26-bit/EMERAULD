# Why We Need Better Ethics for Emerging Technologies

## Citation
Moor, J. (2005). Why We Need Better Ethics for Emerging Technologies. Ethics and Information Technology, 7(3), 111-119. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10676-006-0008-0

## Annotation
James Moor's “Why We Need Better Ethics for Emerging Technologies” argues that revolutionary technologies generate “policy vacuums” and conceptual muddles faster than ethics and law can address them, calling for more proactive, collaborative, and dynamic ethical inquiry. Published in Ethics and Information Technology, it is a foundational statement in technology ethics. For this corpus it grounds the argument that emerging technologies like AI outpace existing ethical and governance frameworks.
