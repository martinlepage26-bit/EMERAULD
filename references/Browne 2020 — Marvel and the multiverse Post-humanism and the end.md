# Marvel and the multiverse: Post-humanism and the end of the singular hero

## Citation
Browne, N., 2020. Marvel and the multiverse: Post-humanism and the end of the singular hero. CineAction, 100, pp.34–46.

## Annotation
Nick Browne's article reads the Marvel Cinematic Universe's multiverse through a posthumanist lens, arguing that the proliferation of variant heroes marks the end of the singular, unified hero and reflects posthuman conceptions of identity and multiplicity. Published in CineAction, it contributes to media studies and popular-culture analysis. For this corpus it links superhero media to posthumanist theory and questions of selfhood.
