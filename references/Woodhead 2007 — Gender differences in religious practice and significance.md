# Gender differences in religious practice and significance

## Citation
Woodhead, L. (2007). Gender differences in religious practice and significance. Dans

## Annotation
Linda Woodhead's chapter on gender differences in religious practice and significance examines how women and men differ in religiosity and what those differences mean, a central question in the sociology of religion. (The canonical citation is truncated.) Woodhead is a leading scholar of religion and gender. For this corpus it grounds the analysis of gender in religious practice.
