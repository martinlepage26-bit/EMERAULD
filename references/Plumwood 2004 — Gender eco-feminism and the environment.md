# Gender, eco-feminism and the environment

## Citation
Plumwood, V. (2004). Gender, eco-feminism and the environment. Dans R. White

## Annotation
Val Plumwood's work on gender, ecofeminism, and the environment analyzes how the domination of nature and the domination of women share a common logic of dualistic, hierarchical thinking. A foundational ecofeminist philosopher, Plumwood critiques the reason/nature dualism underpinning environmental crisis. (The canonical citation is truncated.) For this corpus it grounds ecofeminist critique linking gender, nature, and domination.
