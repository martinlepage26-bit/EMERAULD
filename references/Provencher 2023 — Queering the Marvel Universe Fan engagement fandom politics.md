# Queering the Marvel Universe: Fan engagement, fandom politics, and gender deviance

## Citation
Provencher, D.M., 2023. Queering the Marvel Universe: Fan engagement, fandom politics, and gender deviance. Transformative Works and Cultures, 41. doi:10.3983/twc.2023.2200.

## Annotation
Denis Provencher's “Queering the Marvel Universe” examines fan engagement, fandom politics, and gender deviance in relation to the Marvel franchise, analyzing how queer fans and readings engage the superhero universe. Published in Transformative Works and Cultures, it contributes to fan and queer media studies. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds queer fandom and the Marvel Universe.
