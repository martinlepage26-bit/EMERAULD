# Visual Sociology Reframed: An Analytical Synthesis and Discussion of Visual Methods in Social and Cultural Research

## Citation
Pauwels, L. (2010). Visual Sociology Reframed: An Analytical Synthesis and Discussion of Visual Methods in Social and Cultural Research. Sociological Methods &amp; Research, 38(4), 545-581. https://doi.org/10.1177/0049124110366233

## Annotation
Luc Pauwels's “Visual Sociology Reframed” offers an analytical synthesis of visual methods in social and cultural research, providing an integrated framework for producing, analyzing, and using visual materials. Published in Sociological Methods & Research, it is a key methodological reference. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds visual and image-based research methods.
