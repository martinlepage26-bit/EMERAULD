# Exercices spirituels et philosophie antique

## Citation
Hadot, Pierre. 2002. Exercices spirituels et philosophie antique. Paris: Albin Michel.

## Annotation
Pierre Hadot's Exercices spirituels et philosophie antique argues that ancient philosophy was primarily a way of life — a set of “spiritual exercises” for transforming the self — rather than an abstract theoretical system. Hadot's work influenced Foucault's late thought on the care of the self. It is a landmark in the history of philosophy and of practices of self-cultivation. For this corpus it grounds an understanding of philosophy and ritual as transformative practice.
