# Magic, Witchcraft and the Otherworld: An Anthropology

## Citation
Greenwood, S., 2000. Magic, Witchcraft and the Otherworld: An Anthropology. Oxford: Berg.

## Annotation
Susan Greenwood's Magic, Witchcraft and the Otherworld is an anthropological study of contemporary Western magical practice, drawing on fieldwork among practitioners to analyze experiences of the “otherworld” and the logic of magical consciousness. Greenwood takes practitioners' experiences seriously as objects of anthropological analysis. It is a significant reference in the anthropology of magic. For this corpus it grounds ethnographic understanding of magical practice and cosmology.
