# Regulating AI in Canada: A Critical Look at the Proposed Artificial Intelligence and Data Act

## Citation
SCASSA, Teresa (2023). Regulating AI in Canada: A Critical Look at the Proposed Artificial Intelligence and Data Act. La revue du Barreau canadien. Vol. 101 no 1. En ligne: https://cbr.cba.org/index.php/cbr/article/view/4817/4539.

## Annotation
Teresa Scassa's “Regulating AI in Canada” offers a critical legal analysis of the proposed Artificial Intelligence and Data Act (AIDA), examining its scope, definitions, enforcement, and shortcomings. Published in the Canadian Bar Review by a leading law-and-technology scholar, it is a substantive critique of Canada's draft AI law. For this corpus it grounds expert legal assessment of AIDA and its governance design.
