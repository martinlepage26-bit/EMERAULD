# Les limites de l'identité sexuelle

## Citation
Lamoureux, D. (dir.) (1998). Les limites de l'identité sexuelle. Montréal : Remue

## Annotation
Edited by Diane Lamoureux, Les limites de l'identité sexuelle gathers francophone feminist reflections on the limits and instabilities of sexual identity categories, engaging debates on gender, sexuality, and politics. Published by Éditions du Remue-Ménage, it is a reference in Québec feminist thought. For this corpus it grounds critical analysis of sexual identity.
