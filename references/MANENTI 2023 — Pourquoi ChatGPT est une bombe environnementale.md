# Pourquoi ChatGPT est une bombe environnementale

## Citation
MANENTI, Boris (2023). Pourquoi ChatGPT est une bombe environnementale. L’Obs, 18 septembre. En ligne: https://www.nouvelobs.com/economie/20230918.OBS78305/pourquoi-chatgpt-est-une-bombe-environnementale.html.

## Annotation
This L'Obs article by Boris Manenti argues that ChatGPT is an “environmental bomb,” highlighting the large energy and water costs of training and running generative-AI systems. As a technology-journalism primary source, it documents public concern over AI's ecological footprint. For this corpus it grounds the environmental dimension of AI, connecting generative AI to Anthropocene and sustainability concerns.
