# Exiting the Anthropocene? Exploring Fundamental Change in Our Relationship with Nature

## Citation
European Environment Agency (2023) Exiting the Anthropocene? Exploring Fundamental Change in Our Relationship with Nature.

## Annotation
This European Environment Agency report, Exiting the Anthropocene?, explores the possibility of fundamental change in humanity's relationship with nature, examining transitions beyond the extractive, growth-oriented model associated with the Anthropocene. As an official environmental-policy source, it connects Anthropocene discourse to European sustainability governance. For this corpus it links posthumanist/ecological themes to policy horizons.
