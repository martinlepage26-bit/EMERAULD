# The Feminist, the Housewife, and the Soap Opera

## Citation
Brunsdon, Charlotte. The Feminist, the Housewife, and the Soap Opera. Oxford UP, 2000.

## Annotation
Charlotte Brunsdon's The Feminist, the Housewife, and the Soap Opera reflects on the history of feminist television criticism, examining how scholars have positioned themselves in relation to the “ordinary woman” viewer and to devalued genres like soap opera. Brunsdon interrogates the politics of feminist media scholarship itself. The book is a key text in feminist television studies. For media-focused work in this corpus, it grounds reflexive analysis of gender, genre, and audience.
