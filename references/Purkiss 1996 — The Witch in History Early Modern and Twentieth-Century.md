# The Witch in History: Early Modern and Twentieth-Century Representations

## Citation
Purkiss, Diane. 1996. The Witch in History: Early Modern and Twentieth-Century Representations. London: Routledge.

## Annotation
Diane Purkiss's The Witch in History is an influential study contrasting early-modern and twentieth-century representations of the witch, critically examining both historical witch beliefs and modern feminist and Pagan reclamations of the witch figure. Purkiss interrogates the myths scholars and activists have built around witches. For this corpus it grounds a critical historiography of the witch as both historical figure and modern symbol.
