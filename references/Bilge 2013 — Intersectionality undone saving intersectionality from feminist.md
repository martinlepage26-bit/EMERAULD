# Intersectionality undone : saving intersectionality from feminist

## Citation
Bilge, S. (2013). Intersectionality undone : saving intersectionality from feminist

## Annotation
Sirma Bilge's “Intersectionality Undone” argues for saving intersectionality from a feminist and academic mainstream that has diluted its radical, anti-racist origins into a depoliticized rhetoric of diversity. She insists on restoring its grounding in Black feminist critique and social-justice praxis. The article is a key reference in debates over intersectionality's meaning.
