# Earthly Bodies, Magical Selves: Contemporary Pagans and the Search for Community

## Citation
Pike, S.M., 2001. Earthly Bodies, Magical Selves: Contemporary Pagans and the Search for Community. Berkeley, CA: University of California Press.

## Annotation
Sarah Pike's Earthly Bodies, Magical Selves is an ethnography of Neo-Pagan festivals in America, examining how participants forge identity, community, and the sacred through embodied, ecstatic ritual in temporary festival spaces. It is a key work in Pagan studies. For this corpus it grounds the study of embodiment, community, and self-making in Pagan festival ritual.
