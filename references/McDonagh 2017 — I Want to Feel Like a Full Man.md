# I Want to Feel Like a Full Man

## Citation
McDonagh, L., Nielsen, E., McDermott, D., Davies, N., et al. (2017). “I Want to Feel Like a Full Man”: Conceptualizing Gay, Bisexual, and Heterosexual Men’s Sexual Difficulties. The Journal of Sex Research, 55(6), 783-801. https://doi.org/10.1080/00224499.2017.1410519

## Annotation
McDonagh and colleagues conceptualize gay, bisexual, and heterosexual men's sexual difficulties, drawing on the theme captured in their title — “I Want to Feel Like a Full Man” — to show how masculinity norms shape the experience and meaning of sexual problems across orientations. Published in The Journal of Sex Research, it contributes to research on masculinity and sexual health. For work on gender and sexuality in this corpus, it grounds the link between masculinity and sexual difficulty.
