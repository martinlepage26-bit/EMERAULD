# Knowledge in a Social World

## Citation
Goldman, Alvin I. Knowledge in a Social World. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1999.

## Annotation
Alvin Goldman's Knowledge in a Social World is a foundational work of social epistemology, developing a “veritistic” approach that evaluates social practices (testimony, argumentation, institutions, communication technologies) by their tendency to produce true beliefs. Goldman applies this to science, law, education, and democracy. For this corpus it grounds normative analysis of how social and technological systems help or hinder the pursuit of truth — relevant to information environments and AI.
