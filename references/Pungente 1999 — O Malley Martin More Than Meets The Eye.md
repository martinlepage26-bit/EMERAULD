# & O’Malley, Martin, More Than Meets The Eye

## Citation
Pungente, John J. & O’Malley, Martin, More Than Meets The Eye. Watching Television Watching Us. Toronto: McClelland & Stewart. 1999.

## Annotation
Pungente and O'Malley's More Than Meets the Eye is a media-literacy work examining how television watches and shapes its audiences, aimed at fostering critical viewing. As an accessible media-education text, it contributes to media-literacy scholarship. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds critical engagement with television and audiences.
