# Security, Territory, Population: Lectures at the Collège de France, 1977–1978

## Citation
Foucault, M. (2007) Security, Territory, Population: Lectures at the Collège de France, 1977–1978. Trans. G. Burchell. New York: Palgrave Macmillan.

## Annotation
Michel Foucault's Security, Territory, Population collects his 1977–78 Collège de France lectures, in which he develops the concept of “governmentality” — the art of governing populations through security apparatuses, statistics, and the management of life — extending his analysis of power beyond discipline. The lectures are central to studies of biopolitics and neoliberal governance. For this corpus it grounds the analysis of governmental power, population, and the management of risk.
