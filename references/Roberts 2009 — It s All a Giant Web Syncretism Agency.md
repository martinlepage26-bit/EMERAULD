# ‘It’s All a Giant Web’: Syncretism, Agency and (Re)connection in a Contemporary Pagan Community.

## Citation
Roberts, Rachel. “‘It’s All a Giant Web’: Syncretism, Agency and (Re)connection in a Contemporary Pagan Community.” MA thesis, Université de Montréal, 2009.

## Annotation
Rachel Roberts's MA thesis (Université de Montréal) on “‘It's All a Giant Web'” studies syncretism, agency, and (re)connection in a Montreal-area Pagan community, examining how practitioners combine traditions and build relational webs of meaning. It contributes local ethnographic scholarship to Pagan studies. (This entry duplicates the same work in thesis form.)
