# Performativity or performance? Clarifications in the sociology of

## Citation
Brickell, C. (2003). Performativity or performance? Clarifications in the sociology of

## Annotation
Chris Brickell's article intervenes in the sociology of gender and sexuality by clarifying the distinction between “performativity” (in Butler's sense) and “performance,” which are often conflated. Brickell argues for analytic precision about what each concept can and cannot do in explaining gendered social action. Published in a sociology venue, it is a useful conceptual reference. For this corpus it disciplines the use of performativity/performance in gender analysis.
