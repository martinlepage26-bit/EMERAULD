# Security and immigration: Toward a critique of the governmentality of unease

## Citation
Bigo, D. (2002) ‘Security and immigration: Toward a critique of the governmentality of unease’, Alternatives, 27(1), pp. 63–92.

## Annotation
Didier Bigo's article develops a critical-security-studies account of how immigration comes to be governed as a matter of security, producing a “governmentality of unease.” Bigo argues that security professionals and bureaucratic fields construct migration as threat, blurring internal and external security and normalizing surveillance and exception. Published in Alternatives, it is a foundational reference in the Paris School of security studies. For this corpus it grounds analysis of securitization, migration, and the governance of unease.
