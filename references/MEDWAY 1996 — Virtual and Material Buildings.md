# Virtual and Material Buildings

## Citation
MEDWAY, P. (1996). Virtual and Material Buildings. Written Communication, 13(4), 473-514. https://doi.org/10.1177/0741088396013004002

## Annotation
Peter Medway's “Virtual and Material Buildings” offers a semiotic and constructivist account of architectural design as a mode of symbolic production, arguing that design representations do not merely foreshadow but help bring into being objects that are “real in their social effects.” Published in Written Communication, it extends theories of writing and representation to material artifacts. Its relevance to this corpus is more peripheral, but it bears on how representation constitutes social reality.
