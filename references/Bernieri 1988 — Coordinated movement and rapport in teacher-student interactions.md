# Coordinated movement and rapport in teacher-student interactions

## Citation
Bernieri, F. (1988). Coordinated movement and rapport in teacher-student interactions. Journal of Nonverbal Behavior, 12(2), 120-138. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00986930

## Annotation
Bernieri's study examines coordinated movement — interactional synchrony — and its relationship to rapport in teacher-student interactions, contributing to research on nonverbal behavior and social connection. Published in the Journal of Nonverbal Behavior, it belongs to the social-psychology literature on interpersonal coordination. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized empirical source on nonverbal rapport.
