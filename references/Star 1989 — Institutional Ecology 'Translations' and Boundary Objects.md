# Institutional Ecology, 'Translations' and Boundary Objects.

## Citation
Star, Susan Leigh, and James R. Griesemer. "Institutional Ecology, 'Translations' and Boundary Objects." Social Studies of Science 19, no. 3 (1989): 387–420.

## Annotation
Star and Griesemer's “Institutional Ecology, ‘Translations' and Boundary Objects” introduces the influential concept of “boundary objects” — artifacts flexible enough to be shared across different social worlds yet robust enough to maintain identity — that enable cooperation without consensus. Published in Social Studies of Science, it is a canonical STS text. For this corpus it grounds how heterogeneous actors coordinate around shared objects (standards, documents, models) — relevant to governance across communities.
