# Observation participante ou participante observation? Usages et

## Citation
Bastien, S. (2007). Observation participante ou participante observation? Usages et

## Annotation
Sylvie Bastien's methodological article interrogates the distinction and slippage between “participant observation” and “observing participation,” examining the researcher's position and involvement in ethnographic fieldwork. It reflects on the uses and limits of these postures for qualitative inquiry. Published in a francophone qualitative-research venue, it contributes to debates on reflexivity and field method. For method-focused work in this corpus, it clarifies the stakes of observational positioning.
