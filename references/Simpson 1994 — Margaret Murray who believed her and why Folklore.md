# Margaret Murray: who believed her, and why? Folklore, 105, 89

## Citation
Simpson, J. (1994). Margaret Murray: who believed her, and why? Folklore, 105, 89

## Annotation
Jacqueline Simpson's “Margaret Murray: Who Believed Her, and Why?” is an influential folklore-studies article examining why Murray's discredited witch-cult thesis gained such wide acceptance, analyzing the social and disciplinary conditions of its reception. Published in Folklore, it is a key historiographical corrective. For this corpus it grounds critical reflection on the Murray thesis and how erroneous ideas about witchcraft spread.
