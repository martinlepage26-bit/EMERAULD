# Transparency Against Democracy: The Sweden Democrats, Radical‐Right Populism, and Political Trust

## Citation
Paquin, S. (2025). Transparency Against Democracy: The Sweden Democrats, Radical‐Right Populism, and Political Trust. Politics and Governance, 13. https://doi.org/10.17645/pag.9792

## Annotation
Stéphane Paquin's “Transparency Against Democracy” examines how the Sweden Democrats and radical-right populism relate to political trust and transparency, arguing that transparency can be weaponized in ways that erode rather than strengthen democracy. Published in Politics and Governance, it contributes to research on populism and institutional trust. For this corpus it grounds a critical account of transparency's ambivalent political effects.
