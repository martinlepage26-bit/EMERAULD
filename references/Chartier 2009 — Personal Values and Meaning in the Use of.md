# Personal Values and Meaning in the Use of Methamphetamine Among HIV-Positive Men Who Have Sex With Men

## Citation
Chartier, M., Araneta, A., Duca, L., McGlynn, L., et al. (2009). Personal Values and Meaning in the Use of Methamphetamine Among HIV-Positive Men Who Have Sex With Men. Qualitative Health Research, 19(4), 504-518. https://doi.org/10.1177/1049732309333018

## Annotation
Chartier and colleagues present a qualitative, grounded-theory study of the personal values and meanings attached to methamphetamine use among HIV-positive men who have sex with men in the San Francisco Bay area. The study foregrounds participants' own accounts of context, meaning, and impact, contributing to research on HIV, addiction, and sexuality. Published in Qualitative Health Research, it belongs to public-health scholarship. For work on queer health in this corpus, it offers situated qualitative insight.
