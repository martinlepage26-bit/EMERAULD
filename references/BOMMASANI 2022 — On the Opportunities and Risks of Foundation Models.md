# On the Opportunities and Risks of Foundation Models

## Citation
BOMMASANI, Rishi et autres(2022). On the Opportunities and Risks of Foundation Models. arXiv:2108.07258v3 [cs.LG], 12 juillet. En ligne: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.07258.pdf.

## Annotation
The Stanford report On the Opportunities and Risks of Foundation Models, led by Bommasani and many co-authors, is the most comprehensive early mapping of large pre-trained “foundation” models and their implications. Its value lies in the governance taxonomy it generates — homogenization risk, emergent capabilities, opacity, and the concentration of infrastructural power among a few actors. Widely cited, it frames the stakes of the foundation-model paradigm. For this corpus it provides the empirical backdrop for why recursive AI governance requires upstream as well as downstream accountability.
