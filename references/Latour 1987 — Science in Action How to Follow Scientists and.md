# Science in Action: How to Follow Scientists and Engineers Through Society

## Citation
Latour, Bruno. Science in Action: How to Follow Scientists and Engineers Through Society. Cambridge: Harvard University Press, 1987.

## Annotation
Bruno Latour's Science in Action is a foundational STS text that urges analysts to follow “science in the making” — scientists and engineers at work — rather than accept finished “ready-made science.” Latour develops concepts such as black-boxing, immutable mobiles, and centers of calculation. For this corpus it grounds the study of how scientific and technical facts and artifacts are produced and stabilized — directly relevant to how AI claims become authoritative.
