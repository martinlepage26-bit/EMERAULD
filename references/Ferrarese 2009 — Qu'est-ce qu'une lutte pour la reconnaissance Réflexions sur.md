# Qu'est-ce qu'une lutte pour la reconnaissance? Réflexions sur

## Citation
Ferrarese, E. (2009). Qu'est-ce qu'une lutte pour la reconnaissance? Réflexions sur

## Annotation
Estelle Ferrarese's article asks what a “struggle for recognition” actually is — a question she argues recognition theorists (notably Honneth) have left underspecified. By comparing recognition struggles with other conflicts, especially around the figure of the enemy and its designation, she isolates three defining properties. Published in a francophone political-theory venue, it sharpens a key concept in critical social theory. For this corpus it grounds the analysis of recognition, conflict, and legitimacy.
