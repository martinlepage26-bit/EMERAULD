# Dangerous Safe Havens: Institutional Betrayal Exacerbates Sexual Trauma

## Citation
Smith, C., Freyd, J. (2013). Dangerous Safe Havens: Institutional Betrayal Exacerbates Sexual Trauma. Journal of Traumatic Stress, 26(1), 119-124. https://doi.org/10.1002/jts.21778

## Annotation
Smith and Freyd's “Dangerous Safe Havens” introduces and tests the concept of “institutional betrayal,” showing that when trusted institutions fail to prevent or respond to sexual trauma, they exacerbate victims' harm. Published in the Journal of Traumatic Stress, it is a foundational reference on institutional betrayal. For this corpus it grounds how institutions' failures compound harm — relevant to accountability and trust in governance.
