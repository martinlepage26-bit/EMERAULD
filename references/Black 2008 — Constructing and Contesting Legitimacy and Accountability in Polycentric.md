# Constructing and Contesting Legitimacy and Accountability in Polycentric Regulatory Regimes.

## Citation
Black, Julia. "Constructing and Contesting Legitimacy and Accountability in Polycentric Regulatory Regimes." Regulation & Governance 2, no. 2 (2008): 137–164.

## Annotation
Julia Black's article analyzes how legitimacy and accountability are constructed and contested within polycentric regulatory regimes — governance settings with multiple, overlapping regulators rather than a single sovereign authority. Black argues that legitimacy claims are relational and audience-dependent, negotiated among diverse actors. Published in Regulation & Governance, it is a key reference in regulatory-governance scholarship. For this corpus it directly informs analysis of legitimacy, accountability, and multi-actor governance — highly relevant to AI governance.
