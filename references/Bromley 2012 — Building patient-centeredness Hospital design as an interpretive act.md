# Building patient-centeredness: Hospital design as an interpretive act

## Citation
Bromley, E. (2012). Building patient-centeredness: Hospital design as an interpretive act. Social Science &amp; Medicine, 75(6), 1057-1066. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.socscimed.2012.04.037

## Annotation
Elizabeth Bromley's article treats hospital design as an interpretive act, examining how efforts to build “patient-centeredness” into the physical environment encode contested assumptions about care, patients, and healing. Drawing on ethnographic study, she shows that design decisions are value-laden interpretations rather than neutral solutions. Published in Social Science & Medicine, it contributes to medical sociology and the study of the built environment. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral, but it illustrates how material design embeds social meanings.
