# Trust in Numbers: The Pursuit of Objectivity in Science and Public Life

## Citation
Porter, Theodore M. Trust in Numbers: The Pursuit of Objectivity in Science and Public Life. Princeton: Princeton University Press, 1995.

## Annotation
Theodore Porter's Trust in Numbers is a landmark study of how quantification and the pursuit of “mechanical objectivity” became authoritative in science and public life, arguing that reliance on numbers is often a response to distrust and a way to discipline judgment and limit discretion. It is foundational in STS and the history of quantification. For this corpus it is highly relevant to metrics, measurement, and the politics of objectivity in data and AI governance.
