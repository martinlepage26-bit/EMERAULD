# Extraordinary Bodies: Figuring Physical Disability in American Culture and Literature

## Citation
Garland-Thomson, Rosemarie (1997) Extraordinary Bodies: Figuring Physical Disability in American Culture and Literature. New York: Columbia University Press.

## Annotation
Rosemarie Garland-Thomson's Extraordinary Bodies is a foundational text of disability studies in the humanities, analyzing how physical disability is represented and constructed in American culture and literature. She introduces influential concepts such as the “normate” and the cultural work of staring, showing how the “extraordinary body” is produced against a naturalized norm. For this corpus it grounds the cultural analysis of disability, embodiment, and normalcy.
