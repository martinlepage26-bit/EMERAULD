# Administrative Burden: Policymaking by Other Means

## Citation
Herd, P. and Moynihan, D.P. (2018) Administrative Burden: Policymaking by Other Means. New York: Russell Sage Foundation.

## Annotation
Herd and Moynihan's Administrative Burden: Policymaking by Other Means is a foundational study showing how the “burdens” citizens face in accessing programs — learning costs, compliance costs, and psychological costs — function as consequential, often deliberate, policy choices that shape who actually receives benefits. The book reframes bureaucratic friction as politics by other means. For this corpus it is highly relevant to governance and to the design of systems (including automated ones) that ration access through burden.
