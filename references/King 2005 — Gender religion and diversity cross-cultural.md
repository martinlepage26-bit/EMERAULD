# Gender, religion and diversity : cross-cultural

## Citation
King, U. et Beattie, T. (dir.) (2005). Gender, religion and diversity : cross-cultural

## Annotation
Edited by Ursula King and Tina Beattie, Gender, Religion and Diversity: Cross-Cultural Perspectives gathers studies of how gender and religion intersect across cultures, attending to diversity, difference, and power. It advances the comparative study of gender and religion. For this corpus it grounds cross-cultural analysis of gender and the sacred.
