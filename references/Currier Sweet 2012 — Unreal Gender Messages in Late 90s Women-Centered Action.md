# ‘Unreal’ Gender Messages in Late 90s Women-Centered Action Dramas.

## Citation
Currier Sweet, Anne. 2012. “‘Unreal’ Gender Messages in Late 90s Women-Centered Action Dramas.” Series: International Journal of TV Serial Narratives 1 (2): 113–130.

## Annotation
Anne Currier Sweet's article analyzes “unreal” gender messages in late-1990s women-centered action dramas, examining how shows of that era represented female power and agency in complex, contradictory ways. Published in Series: International Journal of TV Serial Narratives, it contributes to television and gender studies. For media-focused work in this corpus, it grounds analysis of gender in the action-heroine television of the period.
