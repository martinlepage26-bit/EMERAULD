# Costume, Makeup, and Hair

## Citation
McLean, Adrienne L. Costume, Makeup, and Hair. Rutgers UP, 2016.

## Annotation
Adrienne McLean's Costume, Makeup, and Hair is a scholarly volume examining these craft and design elements of film as meaningful sites of cultural and industrial analysis rather than mere surface decoration. It contributes to film and star studies. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the analysis of costume and appearance in cinema.
