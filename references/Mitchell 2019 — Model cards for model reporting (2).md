# Model cards for model reporting

## Citation
Mitchell, M., Wu, S., Zaldivar, A., Barnes, P., Vasserman, L., Hutchinson, B., Spitzer, E., Raji, I. D., & Gebru, T. (2019). Model cards for model reporting. In Proceedings of the Conference on Fairness, Accountability, and Transparency (pp. 220-229). https://doi.org/10.1145/3287560.3287596

## Annotation
“Model Cards for Model Reporting” by Mitchell and colleagues (FAccT 2019) establishes the model-card framework for transparent reporting of a model's intended use, evaluation across demographic and environmental conditions, limitations, and ethical considerations. It is widely adopted in responsible-AI practice. (This entry duplicates the same paper in a different citation form.)
