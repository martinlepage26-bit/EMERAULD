# De l'entretien au récit de vie

## Citation
Galligani, S. (2000). De l'entretien au récit de vie. Écarts d'identité, 92, 21-24.

## Annotation
Stéphanie Galligani's short article reflects on the passage “from the interview to the life narrative,” examining how research interviews become biographical accounts and the methodological implications of that shift. Published in Écarts d'identité, it contributes to francophone qualitative methodology. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the relationship between interviewing and life-story method.
