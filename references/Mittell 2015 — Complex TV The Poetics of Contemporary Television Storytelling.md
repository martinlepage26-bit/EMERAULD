# Complex TV: The Poetics of Contemporary Television Storytelling

## Citation
Mittell, Jason. Complex TV: The Poetics of Contemporary Television Storytelling. NYU Press, 2015.

## Annotation
Jason Mittell's Complex TV is a foundational study of narrative complexity in contemporary television, analyzing the poetics of serialized, puzzle-like storytelling and how it engages audiences, industry, and transmedia extension. It reshaped television studies' account of form and narration. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the analysis of complex television storytelling.
