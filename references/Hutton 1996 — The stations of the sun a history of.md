# The stations of the sun : a history of the ritual year in Britain

## Citation
Hutton, R. (1996). The stations of the sun : a history of the ritual year in Britain.

## Annotation
Ronald Hutton's The Stations of the Sun is a comprehensive history of the ritual year and calendar customs in Britain, tracing the origins and transformations of seasonal festivals and folk practices. Meticulously researched, it corrects many popular misconceptions about the antiquity of customs. For this corpus it grounds the history of seasonal ritual and its bearing on modern Pagan festival cycles.
