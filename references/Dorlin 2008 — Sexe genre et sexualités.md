# Sexe, genre et sexualités

## Citation
Dorlin, Elsa. 2008. Sexe, genre et sexualités. Introduction à la théorie féministe. Paris: Presses Universitaires de France.

## Annotation
Elsa Dorlin's Sexe, genre et sexualités provides an accessible introduction to feminist theory, defining its core categories and surveying its principal currents and controversies. It is a standard reference for francophone gender studies. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation/edition form.)
