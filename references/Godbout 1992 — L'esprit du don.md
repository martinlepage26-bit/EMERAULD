# L'esprit du don

## Citation
Godbout, J. T., & Caillé, A. (1992). L'esprit du don. Paris: La Découverte.

## Annotation
Jacques Godbout, with Alain Caillé, extends Marcel Mauss’s theory of the gift into modern society, arguing against the assumption that market exchange and state redistribution have displaced gift relations. Associated with the MAUSS movement (Mouvement anti-utilitariste dans les sciences sociales), the book contends that giving, receiving, and reciprocating remain constitutive of social bonds even in contemporary capitalist societies — in families, friendships, volunteering, and blood or organ donation. The gift is framed not as an archaic survival but as an ongoing “third paradigm” irreducible to market or state logics. Its critique of utilitarian and economistic accounts of social life has been influential across sociology and anthropology. For work on reciprocity, obligation, and the moral economy of ritual, Godbout offers the key modern restatement of the Maussian gift.
