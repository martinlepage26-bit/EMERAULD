# Nature Religion in America: From the Algonkian Indians to the New Age

## Citation
Albanese, Catherine. Nature Religion in America: From the Algonkian Indians to the New Age. Chicago: University of Chicago Press, 1990.

## Annotation
Catherine Albanese's Nature Religion in America argues that a distinctive current of American spirituality has long located the sacred in nature rather than in institutional churches. Following this thread from Native American cosmologies through Transcendentalism to the New Age, Albanese shows how nature has served as symbol, resource, and object of devotion across American history. The book helped establish “nature religion” as an analytic category in religious studies. For studies of modern Paganism, Goddess spirituality, and ecological religion, it is a foundational historical reference. (This entry duplicates the same work listed in a shorter citation form.)
