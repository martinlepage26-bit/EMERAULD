# The Legitimation of Power

## Citation
Beetham, David. The Legitimation of Power. London: Macmillan, 1991.

## Annotation
David Beetham's The Legitimation of Power is a foundational work of political theory that reconstructs legitimacy as a multi-dimensional concept: power is legitimate insofar as it conforms to established rules, those rules are justified by shared beliefs, and there is evidence of consent from the subordinate. Beetham critiques the Weberian reduction of legitimacy to mere belief, insisting on the normative and behavioral dimensions as well. The book is a standard reference in political sociology and legitimacy theory. For this corpus it supplies a rigorous framework for analyzing authority and legitimation.
