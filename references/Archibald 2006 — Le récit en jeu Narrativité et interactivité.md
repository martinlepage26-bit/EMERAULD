# Le récit en jeu. Narrativité et interactivité

## Citation
Archibald, Samuel et Bertrand Gervais. 2006. « Le récit en jeu. Narrativité et interactivité ». En ligne sur le site de l’Observatoire de l’imaginaire contemporain. . Consulté le 12 décembre 2021. Publication originale : (Protée. 2006. vol. 34, p. 27-39).

## Annotation
In this French-language article, Samuel Archibald and Bertrand Gervais analyze the relationship between narrativity and interactivity in emerging interactive forms such as video games, hypertext fiction, and combinatory works. Rather than opposing interactivity and narrative outright — as some theorists do — they seek to articulate the distinction while moving beyond a simple antagonism between experience and representation. The piece is a contribution to francophone narratology and game studies. For media- and narrative-studies work in this corpus, it offers a nuanced account of how interactive forms strain conventional expectations of narrative.
