# Digital Religion, Social Media, and Culture: Perspectives, Practices, and Futures

## Citation
Cheong, Pauline Hope, Peter Fischer-Nielsen, Stefan Gelfgren, and Charles Ess, eds. 2012. Digital Religion, Social Media, and Culture: Perspectives, Practices, and Futures. New York: Peter Lang.

## Annotation
Edited by Cheong, Fischer-Nielsen, Gelfgren, and Ess, Digital Religion, Social Media, and Culture gathers perspectives on how social media reshape religious practice, authority, and community. The volume examines practices and futures at the intersection of religion and networked culture. It is a key reference in the study of digital religion. For this corpus it grounds analysis of religion and ritual in social-media environments.
