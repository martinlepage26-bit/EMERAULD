# AI-Driven Assistive Technology: The Disability Justice Imperative

## Citation
Kundi, Bushra, Taleghani, Sarah, da Silveira Gorman, Rachel, El-Lahib, Yahya, El Morr, Christo (2025) AI-Driven Assistive Technology: The Disability Justice Imperative. Studies in health technology and informatics, 330, 973-985. doi: 10.3233/SHTI251470

## Annotation
Kundi and colleagues examine AI-driven assistive technology through a disability-justice lens, arguing that while AI can enhance communication, mobility, and quality of life for disabled people, its development must center disabled people's lived experience and equity rather than a narrow medical or techno-optimist framing. Published in Studies in Health Technology and Informatics, it contributes to disability and health-informatics scholarship. For this corpus it grounds a disability-justice critique of assistive AI.
