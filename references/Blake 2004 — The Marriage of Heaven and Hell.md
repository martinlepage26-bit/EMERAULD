# The Marriage of Heaven and Hell

## Citation
Blake, William. 2004. The Marriage of Heaven and Hell. New York: Dover Publications.

## Annotation
William Blake's The Marriage of Heaven and Hell (composed c. 1790) is a visionary prophetic work that inverts conventional moral and religious oppositions, celebrating energy, desire, and imaginative excess against restraint and orthodoxy, most famously in its “Proverbs of Hell.” A cornerstone of Romantic literature and of the antinomian imagination, it has profoundly influenced later esoteric and countercultural thought. For this corpus it is a primary literary source for traditions that valorize transgression, vision, and the reconciliation of contraries.
