# Data Ableism: Ability Expectations and Marginalization in Automated Societies

## Citation
Charitsis, Vassilis, Lehtiniemi, Tuukka (2023) Data Ableism: Ability Expectations and Marginalization in Automated Societies. Television & New Media, 24(1), pp. 3–18. doi: 10.1177/15274764221077660

## Annotation
Charitsis and Lehtiniemi introduce the concepts of “data ableism” and “data disablism” to analyze how datafication embeds ability expectations that exclude and marginalize disabled and disadvantaged people. Drawing on critical disability studies, they show how automated societies discriminate against those who fail to meet prescribed data norms. Published in Television & New Media, it is a notable intervention linking data politics and disability. For this corpus it grounds critical analysis of disability, data, and automated systems.
