# Les formes élémentaires de la vie religieuse : le système

## Citation
Durkheim, E. (1968). Les formes élémentaires de la vie religieuse : le système

## Annotation
Émile Durkheim's The Elementary Forms of Religious Life (1912) is a foundational work in the sociology of religion, arguing through study of totemism that religion is fundamentally social — that the sacred is a collective representation and that religious ritual generates social solidarity and “collective effervescence.” It is one of the most influential accounts of ritual and the sacred. For this corpus it is a canonical theoretical anchor for understanding ritual, the sacred, and social cohesion.
