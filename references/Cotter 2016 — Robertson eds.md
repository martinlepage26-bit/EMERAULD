# Robertson, eds

## Citation
Cotter, Christopher R., and David G. Robertson, eds. After World Religions: Reconstructing Religious Studies. New York: Routledge, 2016.

## Annotation
After World Religions, edited by Cotter and Robertson, gathers essays challenging the “world religions” model and offering strategies to reconstruct religious studies on more critical, reflexive foundations. It is a key text in debates over the categories and pedagogy of the field. (This entry duplicates the same volume in a different citation form.)
