# The KKK won't let me play: ostracism even by a despised outgroup hurts

## Citation
Gonsalkorale, K., Williams, K. (2006). The KKK won't let me play: ostracism even by a despised outgroup hurts. European Journal of Social Psychology, 37(6), 1176-1186. https://doi.org/10.1002/ejsp.392

## Annotation
Gonsalkorale and Williams report an experiment showing that ostracism hurts even when the excluding group is a despised outgroup (the Ku Klux Klan), demonstrating the deep automaticity of the pain of social exclusion. Published in the European Journal of Social Psychology, it belongs to the social psychology of ostracism. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized empirical source.
