# Pouvoir, structure et domination

## Citation
Chazel, F. (1983). Pouvoir, structure et domination. Revue française de sociologie,

## Annotation
François Chazel's article examines the concepts of power, structure, and domination, clarifying their relationships within sociological theory. Published in the Revue française de sociologie, it contributes to francophone theoretical debate on how power operates through and is constrained by social structures. For this corpus it grounds conceptual distinctions relevant to analyses of power, authority, and domination.
