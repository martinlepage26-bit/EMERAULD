# Gender and the Media

## Citation
Gill, Rosalind. Gender and the Media. Polity, 2007.

## Annotation
Rosalind Gill's Gender and the Media is a foundational textbook analyzing representations of gender across advertising, news, talk shows, and popular culture, and articulating the concept of a “postfeminist sensibility” in which feminist ideas are simultaneously invoked and repudiated. It is widely used in media and gender studies. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds critical analysis of gender in contemporary media.
