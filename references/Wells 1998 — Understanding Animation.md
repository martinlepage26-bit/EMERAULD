# Understanding Animation

## Citation
Wells, P. (1998) Understanding Animation. London: Routledge.

## Annotation
Paul Wells's Understanding Animation is a foundational textbook analyzing animation as a distinct art form, offering frameworks for reading its aesthetics, narrative, and ideological dimensions. It is a standard reference in animation studies. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the critical analysis of animation.
