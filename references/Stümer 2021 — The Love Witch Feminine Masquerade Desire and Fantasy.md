# The Love Witch: Feminine Masquerade, Desire, and Fantasy.

## Citation
Stümer, Jenny. “The Love Witch: Feminine Masquerade, Desire, and Fantasy.” Feminist Media Studies 21, no. 5 (2021): 737–752.

## Annotation
Jenny Stümer's article analyzes Anna Biller's film The Love Witch through the lenses of feminine masquerade, desire, and fantasy, examining how the film stages and critiques femininity and the male gaze. Published in Feminist Media Studies, it contributes to feminist film analysis of the cinematic witch. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds a psychoanalytic-feminist reading of witchcraft in film.
