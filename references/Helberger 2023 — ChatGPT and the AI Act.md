# ChatGPT and the AI Act

## Citation
Helberger, N., Diakopoulos, N. (2023). ChatGPT and the AI Act. Internet Policy Review, 12(1). https://doi.org/10.14763/2023.1.1682

## Annotation
Helberger and Diakopoulos's “ChatGPT and the AI Act” analyzes how the EU AI Act's risk-based framework struggles to accommodate general-purpose generative systems like ChatGPT, whose wide range of uses cuts across fixed risk categories. Published in Internet Policy Review, it is an influential and timely intervention in AI-governance scholarship. For this corpus it directly addresses the regulatory challenge that generative AI poses for the EU's flagship law.
