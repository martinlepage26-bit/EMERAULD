# Queer Move/ments

## Citation
Bourcier, M.-H. (2002). Queer Move/ments. Mouvements, 2(20), 37-43.

## Annotation
Marie-Hélène Bourcier's “Queer Move/ments” is a French intervention that both introduces and reworks queer theory and activism for a francophone context, emphasizing the political movements and destabilizing gestures the term “queer” names. Bourcier is a key figure in French queer and trans studies. Published in Mouvements, it is a reference for queer theory's francophone reception. For this corpus it grounds queer politics and theory in French-language scholarship.
