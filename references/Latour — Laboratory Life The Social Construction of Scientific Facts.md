# Laboratory Life: The Social Construction of Scientific Facts

## Citation
Latour, Bruno, and Steve Woolgar. Laboratory Life: The Social Construction of Scientific Facts. Princeton: Princeton University Press, 1979/1986.

## Annotation
Latour and Woolgar's Laboratory Life is a pioneering ethnography of a scientific laboratory that analyzes how scientific facts are socially constructed through practices of inscription, credibility, and negotiation, rather than simply discovered. It helped launch the sociology of scientific knowledge and STS. For this corpus it grounds the constructivist analysis of how facts — and by extension authoritative technical outputs — are manufactured.
