# Relational Aesthetics

## Citation
Bourriaud, Nicolas. 2002. Relational Aesthetics. Dijon: Les presses du réel.

## Annotation
Nicolas Bourriaud's Relational Aesthetics coined an influential (and contested) framework for the participatory art of the 1990s, defining art that takes as its subject and medium the sphere of human interactions and their social context. Bourriaud argues that such work produces micro-utopian social relations rather than discrete objects. The book is central to contemporary art theory and its critiques (notably by Claire Bishop). Its place in this corpus is peripheral, but it bears on relationality, participation, and the politics of the social.
