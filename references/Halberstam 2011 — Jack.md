# Jack

## Citation
Halberstam, J. Jack. The Queer Art of Failure. Duke UP, 2011.

## Annotation
Jack Halberstam's The Queer Art of Failure argues that failure — refusing the normative logics of success, productivity, and reproduction — can be a queer and creative mode of living and knowing. Drawing on popular culture, animation, and “low theory,” Halberstam finds subversive potential in losing, forgetting, and not mastering. It is an influential work in queer theory and cultural studies. For this corpus it grounds a queer critique of normative success and achievement.
