# Cyborg-Technology Relations

## Citation
Shew, Ashley, Earle, Joshua (2024) Cyborg-Technology Relations. Journal of Human-Technology Relations, 2(1). doi: 10.59490/jhtr.2024.2.7073

## Annotation
Ashley Shew and Joshua Earle advocate a philosophy of cyborg–technology relations that centers disabled technology users, introducing “technoableism” to name the ableism pervading technology discourse and arguing for a disability-forward approach drawing on disability studies and crip technoscience. Published in the Journal of Human-Technology Relations, it foregrounds disabled cyborgs' own experience. For this corpus it grounds a disability-centered critique of how cyborg and technology relations are theorized.
