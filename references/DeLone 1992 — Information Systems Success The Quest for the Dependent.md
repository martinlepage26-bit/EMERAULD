# Information Systems Success: The Quest for the Dependent Variable

## Citation
DeLone, W., McLean, E. (1992). Information Systems Success: The Quest for the Dependent Variable. Information Systems Research, 3(1), 60-95. https://doi.org/10.1287/isre.3.1.60

## Annotation
DeLone and McLean's “Information Systems Success: The Quest for the Dependent Variable” is a foundational article in information-systems research that proposes a comprehensive model of IS success spanning system quality, information quality, use, user satisfaction, and organizational impact. The DeLone–McLean model became one of the most cited frameworks in the field. For this corpus it grounds the evaluation of information systems and the question of how “success” is defined for technology.
