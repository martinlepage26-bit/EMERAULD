# The Spirit Within: Gay Male Culture as a Spiritual Venue.

## Citation
Boisvert, Donald. “The Spirit Within: Gay Male Culture as a Spiritual Venue.” In Gay Religion, edited by Scott Thumma and Edward R. Gray, 251–366. Walnut Creek: Altamira Press, 2004.

## Annotation
Donald Boisvert's “The Spirit Within,” collected in Gay Religion (eds. Thumma and Gray), argues that gay male culture serves as a spiritual venue in which eros, community, and ritual carry sacred significance. The essay is a reference in queer theology and the study of religion and sexuality. (This entry duplicates the same chapter in a different citation form.)
