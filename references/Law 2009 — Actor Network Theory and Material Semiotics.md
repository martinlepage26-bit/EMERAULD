# Actor Network Theory and Material Semiotics.

## Citation
Law, John. 2009. “Actor Network Theory and Material Semiotics.” In The New Blackwell Companion to Social Theory, edited by Bryan S. Turner, 141–158. Oxford: Blackwell.

## Annotation
John Law's “Actor Network Theory and Material Semiotics” is an authoritative overview of ANT, reframing it as a family of “material-semiotic” approaches that trace how relations among human and nonhuman elements enact realities. Law emphasizes ANT's methodological sensibility over doctrine. Published in a major social-theory companion, it is a standard reference. For this corpus it grounds a relational, material-semiotic understanding of socio-technical assemblages.
