# Disability, Fairness, and Algorithmic Bias in AI Recruitment

## Citation
Bennett, Cynthia L. (2022) Disability, Fairness, and Algorithmic Bias in AI Recruitment. Ethics and Information Technology. doi: 10.1007/s10676-022-09633-2

## Annotation
Cynthia Bennett's article examines disability, fairness, and algorithmic bias in AI-driven recruitment, arguing that hiring algorithms can systematically disadvantage disabled applicants whose profiles diverge from normative training data. It connects fairness scholarship to the specific and often-overlooked axis of disability. Published in Ethics and Information Technology, it contributes to AI-governance and disability-studies debates. For this corpus it grounds concerns about algorithmic exclusion in the context of employment and disability.
