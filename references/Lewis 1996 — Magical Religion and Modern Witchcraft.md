# Magical Religion and Modern Witchcraft

## Citation
Lewis, J.R., 1996. Magical Religion and Modern Witchcraft. Albany, NY: State University of New York Press.

## Annotation
Edited by James R. Lewis, Magical Religion and Modern Witchcraft is a scholarly collection examining contemporary Witchcraft, Wicca, and magical religion from multiple disciplinary perspectives. It was an early academic anthology helping to establish Pagan studies. For this corpus it grounds the scholarly study of modern magical religion.
