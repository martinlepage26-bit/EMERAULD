# Le mythe de l’éternel retour: archétypes et répétition

## Citation
Eliade, Mircea. 1957. Le mythe de l’éternel retour: archétypes et répétition. Paris: Gallimard.

## Annotation
Mircea Eliade's The Myth of the Eternal Return is a foundational work in the history of religions, arguing that “archaic” societies experience time as cyclical and sacred — periodically regenerated through ritual repetition of cosmogonic archetypes — in contrast to the linear, irreversible time of modern historical consciousness. Eliade contrasts the “terror of history” with the consolations of eternal return. Though his comparative method is debated, the book is enormously influential. For this corpus it grounds concepts of sacred time, ritual repetition, and archetype.
