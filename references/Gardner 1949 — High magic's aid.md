# High magic's aid

## Citation
Gardner, G. B. (1949). High magic's aid. London: Michael Houghton.

## Annotation
Gerald Gardner's High Magic's Aid is a historical novel, published under a pseudonym before the repeal of the UK Witchcraft Act, that dramatizes ceremonial magic and witchcraft and encoded ritual material Gardner would later present as Wicca. It is a key primary source in the prehistory of modern Witchcraft, preceding his non-fiction Witchcraft Today. (Reconstructed from an OCR-corrupted canonical entry.) For this corpus it grounds the literary origins of Gardnerian Wicca.
