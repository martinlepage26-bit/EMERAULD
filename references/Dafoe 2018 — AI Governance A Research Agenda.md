# AI Governance: A Research Agenda.

## Citation
Dafoe, Allan. "AI Governance: A Research Agenda." Future of Humanity Institute, University of Oxford, 2018.

## Annotation
Allan Dafoe's “AI Governance: A Research Agenda,” produced at Oxford's Future of Humanity Institute, is a foundational document that maps the field of AI governance, defining its scope, key problems, and research priorities across technical, political, and strategic dimensions. It has been highly influential in framing AI governance as a distinct area of study. For this corpus it is a central reference articulating what AI governance is and what questions it must address.
