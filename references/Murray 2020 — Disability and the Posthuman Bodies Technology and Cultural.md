# Disability and the Posthuman: Bodies, Technology and Cultural Futures

## Citation
Murray, Stuart (2020) Disability and the Posthuman: Bodies, Technology and Cultural Futures. Liverpool: Liverpool University Press. doi: 10.3828/liverpool/9781789621648.001.0001

## Annotation
Stuart Murray's Disability and the Posthuman examines how disability intersects with posthumanist thought, technology, and cultural futures, analyzing representations of disabled bodies in relation to prosthetics, enhancement, and speculative imaginaries. Published by Liverpool University Press, it is a key text linking disability studies and posthumanism. For this corpus it grounds the disability–posthumanism intersection around bodies and technology.
