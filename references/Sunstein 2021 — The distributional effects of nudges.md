# The distributional effects of nudges

## Citation
Sunstein, C. (2021). The distributional effects of nudges. Nature Human Behaviour, 6(1), 9-10. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-021-01236-z

## Annotation
Cass Sunstein's “The Distributional Effects of Nudges” argues that behavioral “nudges” are not distributionally neutral: they can help or harm different groups unequally, and their equity effects deserve explicit attention. Published in Nature Human Behaviour, it is a concise intervention in behavioral public policy. For this corpus it grounds attention to the distributional consequences of choice-architecture and automated decision design.
