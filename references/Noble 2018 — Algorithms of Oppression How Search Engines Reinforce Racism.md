# Algorithms of Oppression: How Search Engines Reinforce Racism

## Citation
Noble, Safiya Umoja. Algorithms of Oppression: How Search Engines Reinforce Racism. New York: NYU Press, 2018.

## Annotation
Safiya Umoja Noble's Algorithms of Oppression is a landmark study showing how search engines — especially Google — reproduce and amplify racism and sexism, notably in the results returned for searches about Black women and girls. Noble argues that algorithmic results are not neutral but reflect commercial and cultural power. It is a foundational text in critical algorithm and race studies. For this corpus it is a canonical demonstration that search and ranking systems encode structural oppression.
