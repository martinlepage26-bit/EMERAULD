# Democracy Report 2025: 25 Years of Autocratization – Democracy Trumped? Gothenburg: V-Dem Institute, University of Gothenburg

## Citation
V-Dem Institute (2025) Democracy Report 2025: 25 Years of Autocratization – Democracy Trumped? Gothenburg: V-Dem Institute, University of Gothenburg.

## Annotation
The V-Dem Institute's Democracy Report 2025, subtitled “25 Years of Autocratization – Democracy Trumped?”, presents the latest global assessment of democracy using the Varieties of Democracy dataset, documenting a wave of autocratization worldwide. As an authoritative index, it is a primary data source on democratic decline. For this corpus it grounds the empirical state of democracy — the governance backdrop for information and AI-enabled threats.
