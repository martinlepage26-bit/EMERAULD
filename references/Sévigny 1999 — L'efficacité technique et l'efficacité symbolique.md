# L'efficacité technique et l'efficacité symbolique

## Citation
Sévigny, O. (1999). L'efficacité technique et l'efficacité symbolique. La perspective

## Annotation
Odile Sévigny's article analyzes “technical efficacy” and “symbolic efficacy” in the context of homeopathic care, drawing on non-medical homeopaths' accounts of their practices and therapeutic relationships, and asking whether such care offers a privileged space of symbolization in advanced modern society. Published in Anthropologie et sociétés, it contributes to the anthropology of healing and alternative medicine. For this corpus it grounds the analysis of symbolic versus technical efficacy in healing.
