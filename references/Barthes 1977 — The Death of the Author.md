# The Death of the Author.

## Citation
Barthes, Roland. "The Death of the Author." In Image, Music, Text. Trans. Stephen Heath. New York: Hill and Wang, 1977.

## Annotation
Roland Barthes's essay “The Death of the Author” (1967) argues that a text's meaning cannot be anchored in its author's intention or biography; meaning is produced in the act of reading, in the “birth of the reader.” Barthes displaces the author as the guarantor of meaning in favor of the text as a tissue of citations without fixed origin. The essay is a touchstone of poststructuralist literary theory. For this corpus it grounds questions of authorship, textuality, and interpretive authority.
