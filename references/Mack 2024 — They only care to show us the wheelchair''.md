# ``They only care to show us the wheelchair'': Disability Representation in Text-to-Image AI Models

## Citation
Mack, Kelly Avery, Qadri, Rida, Denton, Remi, Kane, Shaun K., Bennett, Cynthia L. (2024) ``They only care to show us the wheelchair'': Disability Representation in Text-to-Image AI Models. Proceedings of the CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI '24). doi: 10.1145/3613904.3642166

## Annotation
Mack and colleagues examine disability representation in text-to-image AI models, finding — as their title (“They only care to show us the wheelchair”) suggests — that such systems reproduce narrow, stereotyped depictions of disability centered on visible mobility aids. Presented at CHI 2024, it contributes to fairness research on generative AI. For this corpus it grounds critique of how image-generating AI stereotypes disabled people.
