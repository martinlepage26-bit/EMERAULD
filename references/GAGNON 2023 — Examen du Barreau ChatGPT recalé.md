# Examen du Barreau – ChatGPT recalé

## Citation
GAGNON, Katia (2023). Examen du Barreau – ChatGPT recalé. La Presse, 25 mai. En ligne: https://www.lapresse.ca/actualites/justice-et-faits-divers/2023-05-25/examen-du-barreau/chatgpt-recale.php.

## Annotation
This La Presse article by Katia Gagnon reports that ChatGPT failed Québec's bar examination, a widely noted illustration of the limits of large language models on high-stakes professional tests. As a news primary source, it documents public discourse on AI capabilities and reliability. For this corpus it grounds concrete debate about AI's competence and its implications for professional practice.
