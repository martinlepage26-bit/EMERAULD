# A preference for genuine smiles following social exclusion

## Citation
Bernstein, M., Sacco, D., Brown, C., Young, S., et al. (2010). A preference for genuine smiles following social exclusion. Journal of Experimental Social Psychology, 46(1), 196-199. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jesp.2009.08.010

## Annotation
Bernstein and colleagues report an experiment showing that social exclusion increases people's preference for and sensitivity to genuine (Duchenne) smiles, interpreted as a re-affiliation response to threatened belonging. Published in the Journal of Experimental Social Psychology, it contributes to research on ostracism and social reconnection. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized empirical source on exclusion and affiliation.
