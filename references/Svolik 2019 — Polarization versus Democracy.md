# Polarization versus Democracy

## Citation
Svolik, M. (2019). Polarization versus Democracy. Journal of Democracy, 30(3), 20-32. https://doi.org/10.1353/jod.2019.0039

## Annotation
Milan Svolik's “Polarization versus Democracy” argues that severe partisan polarization enables democratic erosion because voters, when forced to choose between their partisan interests and democratic principles, often sacrifice democracy to keep their side in power. Published in the Journal of Democracy, it is a widely cited analysis. For this corpus it grounds how polarization undermines democratic guardrails — relevant to information environments and governance.
