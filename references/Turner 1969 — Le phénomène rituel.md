# Le phénomène rituel

## Citation
Turner, Victor W., 1969. Le phénomène rituel. Structure et contre-structure. Paris: Presses Universitaires de France.

## Annotation
Victor Turner's Le phénomène rituel (The Ritual Process) presents his theory of liminality and communitas, analyzing the transformative, anti-structural phase of ritual passage. It is a foundational text in ritual studies. (This entry duplicates the same book in a French edition.)
