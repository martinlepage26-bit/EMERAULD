# Organizational Routines as a Source of Continuous Change

## Citation
Feldman, M. (2000). Organizational Routines as a Source of Continuous Change. Organization Science, 11(6), 611-629. https://doi.org/10.1287/orsc.11.6.611.12529

## Annotation
Martha Feldman's article challenges the view of organizational routines as static, proposing instead a “performative” model in which routines carry an internal dynamic toward continuous change because the people enacting them respond to prior outcomes. By keeping agents inseparable from the routine, she reveals routines as sites of ongoing adaptation. Published in Organization Science, it is a foundational reference in organization and practice theory. For this corpus it grounds the idea that institutional practices are enacted and mutable rather than fixed.
