# L'homosexualité comme construction sociale : sur le tournant

## Citation
Broqua, C. (2011). L'homosexualité comme construction sociale : sur le tournant

## Annotation
Christophe Broqua's article introduces a French translation of pioneering North American sociological texts on homosexuality (published 1956–1968) and reconstructs the emergence of a constructionist approach to homosexuality. Broqua shows how these largely forgotten works broke with the then-dominant psychiatric and psychological literature by treating homosexuality as socially constructed. Published in Genre, sexualité & société, it is a valuable francophone genealogy of the field. For this corpus it grounds the constructionist turn in the sociology of sexuality.
