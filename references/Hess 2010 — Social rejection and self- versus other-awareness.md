# Social rejection and self- versus other-awareness

## Citation
Hess, Y., Pickett, C. (2010). Social rejection and self- versus other-awareness. Journal of Experimental Social Psychology, 46(2), 453-456. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jesp.2009.12.004

## Annotation
Hess and Pickett examine how social rejection affects the balance between self- and other-awareness, contributing to the social psychology of exclusion. Published in the Journal of Experimental Social Psychology, it belongs to research on ostracism and social cognition. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized empirical source.
