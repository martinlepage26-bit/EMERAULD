# Autour du Concept d’Agentivité.

## Citation
Guilhaumou, Jacques. 2013. “Autour du Concept d’Agentivité.” Rives méditeranéennes 41: 25–34.

## Annotation
Jacques Guilhaumou's “Autour du concept d'agentivité” examines agency as a multidimensional concept, engaging Butler's account alongside historical and anthropological cases, and considering agency within specific forms of reasoning and argumentation. Published in Rives méditerranéennes, it is a francophone reference on the concept of agency. (This entry duplicates the same article in a different citation form.)
