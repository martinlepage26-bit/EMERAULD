# Human-Centered Disability Bias Detection in Large Language Models

## Citation
Chakour, Habiba, Sadat, Fatiha (2025) Human-Centered Disability Bias Detection in Large Language Models. Proceedings of The First Workshop on Human--LLM Collaboration for Ethical and Responsible Science Production (SciProdLLM), pp. 6–18.

## Annotation
Chakour and Sadat present a human-centered method for detecting disability bias in large language models, contributing to research on fairness and responsible AI. The paper examines how LLMs may encode ableist assumptions and proposes approaches to surface such bias. Presented at a workshop on human–LLM collaboration for ethical science, it sits at the intersection of AI ethics and disability studies. For this corpus it grounds concerns about disability bias in generative AI.
