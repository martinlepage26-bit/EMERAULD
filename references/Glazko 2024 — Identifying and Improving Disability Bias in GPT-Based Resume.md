# Identifying and Improving Disability Bias in GPT-Based Resume Screening

## Citation
Glazko, Kate, Mohammed, Yusuf, Kosa, Ben, Potluri, Venkatesh, Mankoff, Jennifer (2024) Identifying and Improving Disability Bias in GPT-Based Resume Screening. Proceedings of the 2024 ACM Conference on Fairness, Accountability, and Transparency (FAccT '24). doi: 10.1145/3630106.3658933

## Annotation
Glazko and colleagues empirically identify disability bias in GPT-based résumé screening, showing that large language models can systematically disadvantage disabled applicants, and they test interventions to mitigate it. Presented at FAccT 2024, the study contributes to fairness research on generative AI in hiring. For this corpus it grounds concrete evidence of, and remedies for, disability bias in LLM-driven employment tools.
