# Religion and Material Culture: The Matter of Belief

## Citation
Meyer, Birgit, David Morgan, Crispin Paine, and S. Brent Plate, eds. 2010. Religion and Material Culture: The Matter of Belief. London: Routledge.

## Annotation
Edited by Meyer, Morgan, Paine, and Plate, Religion and Material Culture: The Matter of Belief gathers studies foregrounding the material dimensions of religion — objects, images, bodies, and things — arguing that belief is enacted and sustained through matter, not just ideas. It is a key text in the material-religion turn. For this corpus it grounds the study of the materiality of religious and ritual practice.
