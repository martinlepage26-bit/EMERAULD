# Why we should have seen that coming

## Citation
Wolf, M., Miller, K., Grodzinsky, F. (2017). Why we should have seen that coming. ACM SIGCAS Computers and Society, 47(3), 54-64. https://doi.org/10.1145/3144592.3144598

## Annotation
Wolf, Miller, and Grodzinsky's “Why We Should Have Seen That Coming” analyzes Microsoft's Tay chatbot, which was shut down within 24 hours after learning to produce racist and abusive tweets, arguing that “learning software” interacting directly with the public creates distinctive developer responsibilities. Published in ACM SIGCAS Computers and Society, it is a foundational case study in AI ethics. For this corpus it grounds developer responsibility for the behavior of adaptive, publicly interacting AI.
