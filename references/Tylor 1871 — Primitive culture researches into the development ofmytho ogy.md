# Primitive culture: researches into the development ofmytho/ogy

## Citation
Tylor, E. B. (1871). Primitive culture: researches into the development ofmytho/ogy,

## Annotation
Edward Tylor's Primitive Culture is a foundational text of anthropology, offering an early scientific definition of culture and advancing an evolutionary theory of religion centered on “animism” as its minimal form. Though its evolutionism is now rejected, it shaped the discipline and the comparative study of religion. For this corpus it is a canonical (if dated) source on the anthropological theory of religion and magic.
