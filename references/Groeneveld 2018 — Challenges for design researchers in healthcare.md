# Challenges for design researchers in healthcare

## Citation
Groeneveld, B., Dekkers, T., Boon, B., D’Olivo, P. (2018). Challenges for design researchers in healthcare. Design for Health, 2(2), 305-326. https://doi.org/10.1080/24735132.2018.1541699

## Annotation
Groeneveld and colleagues examine the distinctive challenges facing design researchers working in healthcare settings, including ethical, organizational, and methodological complexities. Published in Design for Health, it contributes to design-research methodology in sensitive contexts. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral but methodological, bearing on doing design and research in complex institutions.
