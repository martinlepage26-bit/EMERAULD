# Her hidden children: the rise ofWicca and Paganism in America

## Citation
Clifton, C. (2006). Her hidden children: the rise ofWicca and Paganism in America.

## Annotation
Chas Clifton's Her Hidden Children chronicles the emergence and growth of Wicca and Paganism in America, attending to the movement's nature-religion currents and its distinctive American forms. It is a key historical account in the study of contemporary Paganism. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation form.)
