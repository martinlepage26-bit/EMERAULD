# Age differences in sensation seeking and impulsivity as indexed by behavior and self-report: Evidence for a dual systems model

## Citation
Steinberg, L., Albert, D., Cauffman, E., Banich, M., et al. (2008). Age differences in sensation seeking and impulsivity as indexed by behavior and self-report: Evidence for a dual systems model. Developmental Psychology, 44(6), 1764-1778. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0012955

## Annotation
Steinberg and colleagues report age differences in sensation-seeking and impulsivity, supporting a “dual systems” model of adolescent risk-taking. Published in Developmental Psychology, it is an influential study in developmental and adolescent psychology. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized empirical source.
