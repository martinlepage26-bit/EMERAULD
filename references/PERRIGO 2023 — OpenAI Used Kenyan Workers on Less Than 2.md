# OpenAI Used Kenyan Workers on Less Than $2 Per Hour to Make ChatGPT Less Toxic

## Citation
PERRIGO, Billy (2023). OpenAI Used Kenyan Workers on Less Than $2 Per Hour to Make ChatGPT Less Toxic. Times, 18 janvier. En ligne: https://time.com/6247678/openai-chatgpt-kenya-workers/.

## Annotation
This TIME investigation by Billy Perrigo reveals that OpenAI relied on outsourced Kenyan workers paid less than $2 per hour to label toxic and disturbing content in order to make ChatGPT less harmful. It exposes the hidden, precarious human labor — “ghost work” — underlying generative AI. As a news primary source, it is widely cited in AI-ethics and labor debates. For this corpus it grounds the exploited-labor dimension of AI production, a concrete case of the human costs behind “automated” systems.
