# Occultisme, sorcellerie et modes culturelles

## Citation
Eliade, M. (1978). Occultisme, sorcellerie et modes culturelles. Paris : Gallimard.

## Annotation
Mircea Eliade's Occultism, Witchcraft, and Cultural Fashions collects essays by the influential historian of religions on occult currents, witchcraft, and their recurrence in modern cultural life. Eliade reads these phenomena as expressions of enduring religious and symbolic structures resurfacing in secular guise. Though his comparative method has been critiqued, the work is a significant reference in the history of religions. For this corpus it grounds the study of occultism and witchcraft within broad histories of the sacred.
