# Using Photographs to Capture Women's Experiences of Chemotherapy: Reflecting on the Method

## Citation
Frith, H., Harcourt, D. (2007). Using Photographs to Capture Women's Experiences of Chemotherapy: Reflecting on the Method. Qualitative Health Research, 17(10), 1340-1350. https://doi.org/10.1177/1049732307308949

## Annotation
Frith and Harcourt reflect on using participant-generated photographs to capture women's experiences of chemotherapy, examining the methodological opportunities and challenges of photo-production methods for sensitive health research. Published in Qualitative Health Research, it contributes to arts-based and visual qualitative methodology. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds participant photography as a research method.
