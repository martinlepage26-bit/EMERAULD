# Generative AI could raise global GDP by 7%

## Citation
GOLDMAN SACHS (2023). Generative AI could raise global GDP by 7%. Goldman Sachs Intelligence, 5 avril. En ligne: https://www.goldmansachs.com/intelligence/pages/generative-ai-could-raise-global-gdp-by-7-percent.html.

## Annotation
This Goldman Sachs research note estimates that generative AI could raise global GDP by around 7% over a decade while significantly disrupting labor markets. As an influential industry economic forecast, it shaped public and policy discourse about AI's macroeconomic impact. For this corpus it is a primary source on the economic framing of generative AI and its governance stakes around labor and productivity.
