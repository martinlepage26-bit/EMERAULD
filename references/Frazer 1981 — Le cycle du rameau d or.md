# Le cycle du rameau d’or

## Citation
Frazer, James G. Le cycle du rameau d’or. Paris: Laffont, 1981.

## Annotation
Frazer's Le cycle du rameau d'or (The Golden Bough) offers a monumental comparative survey of magic, ritual, and myth, organized around the priest-king of Nemi and the dying-and-rising god, advancing an evolutionary account from magic to religion to science. Its scholarship is dated and its method criticized, but its cultural influence is immense. (This entry duplicates the same French edition in a different citation form.)
