# Scene

## Citation
Drysdale, K. (2021). ‘Scene’ as a critical framing device: Extending analysis of chemsex cultures. Sexualities, 26(5-6), 604-619. https://doi.org/10.1177/1363460721995467

## Annotation
Kerryn Drysdale proposes “scene” as a critical framing device for analyzing chemsex cultures, arguing that attending to the social and spatial scene extends beyond individualized risk framings toward the collective, cultural dimensions of sexualized drug use. Published in Sexualities, it contributes conceptual tools to the study of gay men's sexual cultures. For work on queer sexuality in this corpus, it offers a framework linking chemsex to community and scene.
