# Healing my Body, Healing the Land

## Citation
Roberts, R. (2012). “Healing my Body, Healing the Land”. Ethnologies, 33(1), 239-256. https://doi.org/10.7202/1007804ar

## Annotation
Rachel Roberts's “Healing my Body, Healing the Land” examines how contemporary Pagans link personal healing to ecological and land-based healing, analyzing the entanglement of body, self, and environment in Pagan practice. Published in Ethnologies, it contributes to Pagan and eco-spiritual studies. For this corpus it grounds the connection between embodiment, healing, and land in Paganism.
