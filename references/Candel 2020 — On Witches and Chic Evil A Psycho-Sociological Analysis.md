# On Witches and Chic Evil: A Psycho-Sociological Analysis of The Love Witch.

## Citation
Candel, Octav Sorin. “On Witches and Chic Evil: A Psycho-Sociological Analysis of The Love Witch.” In International Perspectives on Rethinking Evil in Film and Television, edited by Dilan Tüysüz, 76–87. Hershey, PA: IGI Global, 2020.

## Annotation
Octav Sorin Candel's chapter offers a psycho-sociological analysis of Anna Biller's film The Love Witch, examining how it constructs witchcraft, femininity, and “chic evil” on screen. Appearing in an edited volume on rethinking evil in film and television, it contributes to media and gender studies of the witch figure. For this corpus it complements primary film sources with scholarly analysis of cinematic witchcraft and gendered evil.
