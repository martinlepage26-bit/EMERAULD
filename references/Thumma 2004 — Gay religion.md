# Gay religion

## Citation
Thumma, S. et Gray, E. R. (dir.) (2004). Gay religion. Walnut Creek, CA: Altamira

## Annotation
Edited by Scott Thumma and Edward Gray, Gay Religion is a foundational collection of studies on the religious and spiritual lives of LGBTQ people across traditions, from congregations to alternative spiritualities. It helped establish the study of queer religion. For this corpus it grounds the empirical study of gay religious and spiritual life.
