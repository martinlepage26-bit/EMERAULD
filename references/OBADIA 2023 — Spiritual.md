# Spiritual

## Citation
OBADIA, L. (2023). ‘Spiritual’ witchcraft and magic 2.0 as weapons of resistance: The emergence of a new movement?. Social Compass, 70(4), 549-565. https://doi.org/10.1177/00377686231217194

## Annotation
Lionel Obadia's article examines “spiritual” witchcraft and “magic 2.0” as weapons of resistance, asking whether the politicized, digitally mediated witchcraft of recent years constitutes a new movement. Published in Social Compass, it analyzes the intersection of contemporary witchcraft, activism, and digital culture. For this corpus it grounds the study of politicized, resistance-oriented modern witchcraft.
