# The Fifth Branch: Science Advisers as Policymakers

## Citation
Jasanoff, Sheila. The Fifth Branch: Science Advisers as Policymakers. Cambridge: Harvard University Press, 1990.

## Annotation
Sheila Jasanoff's The Fifth Branch analyzes the role of scientific advisers in regulatory policymaking, showing that the boundary between science and policy is negotiated rather than given, and introducing ideas about “regulatory science” and boundary-work. It is a foundational STS study of expertise in governance. For this corpus it grounds how scientific expertise is mobilized and contested in regulation — relevant to AI advisory bodies.
