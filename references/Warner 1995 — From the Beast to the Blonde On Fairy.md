# From the Beast to the Blonde: On Fairy Tales and Their Tellers

## Citation
Warner, M. (1995) From the Beast to the Blonde: On Fairy Tales and Their Tellers. London: Chatto & Windus.

## Annotation
Marina Warner's From the Beast to the Blonde is a landmark cultural history of fairy tales and their (often female) tellers, examining how these stories encode and contest ideas about gender, femininity, storytelling, and power. Erudite and wide-ranging, it is a key text in folklore and gender studies. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the analysis of fairy tales, gender, and narrative authority.
