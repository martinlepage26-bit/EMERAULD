# Les théories de la magie dans les traditions anthropologiques anglaise et française

## Citation
Keck, F. (2002). Les théories de la magie dans les traditions anthropologiques anglaise et française. Méthodos, 2. https://doi.org/10.4000/methodos.90

## Annotation
Frédéric Keck's article contrasts the English and French anthropological traditions in their treatment of magic, focusing on two problems: what kind of rationality gives magic its efficacy despite offering no prediction, and how one moves from magical to critical-scientific rationality. Reviewing Tylor and Frazer and contrasting Malinowski/Evans-Pritchard's pragmatic view with a structural account, it is a rigorous comparative study. For this corpus it grounds the theory of magic across anthropological traditions.
