# The Division of Labor in Society

## Citation
Durkheim, Émile. The Division of Labor in Society. Trans. W.D. Halls. New York: Free Press, 1893/1984.

## Annotation
Émile Durkheim's The Division of Labor in Society (1893) is a founding text of sociology, arguing that the division of labor transforms the basis of social solidarity from “mechanical” (shared likeness) to “organic” (interdependence of differentiated roles). Durkheim also introduces the concept of anomie. It is a cornerstone of social theory. For this corpus it grounds classical accounts of solidarity, differentiation, and social order.
