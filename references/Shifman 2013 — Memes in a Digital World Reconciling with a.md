# Memes in a Digital World: Reconciling with a Conceptual Troublemaker

## Citation
Shifman, L. (2013). Memes in a Digital World: Reconciling with a Conceptual Troublemaker. Journal of Computer-Mediated Communication, 18(3), 362-377. https://doi.org/10.1111/jcc4.12013

## Annotation
Limor Shifman's “Memes in a Digital World” provides an influential conceptualization of the internet meme, reconciling the term's slipperiness by defining memes as groups of digital items sharing content, form, and stance, spread and imitated by users. Published in the Journal of Computer-Mediated Communication, it is a foundational reference in digital-culture studies. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the analysis of memes and digital circulation.
