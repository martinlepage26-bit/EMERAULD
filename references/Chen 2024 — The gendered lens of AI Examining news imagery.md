# The gendered lens of AI: Examining news imagery across digital spaces

## Citation
Chen, Y., Zhai, X., & Sun, Y. (2024). The gendered lens of AI: Examining news imagery across digital spaces. Journal of Computer-Mediated Communication.

## Annotation
Chen, Zhai, and Sun analyze gender representation in AI-related imagery across 28,199 images from news media, tech sites, and social platforms, finding a prevalence of male faces and consistent underrepresentation and disempowering, stereotyped framing of women. The study exposes how visual narratives around AI reproduce gender bias in the public imagination. Published in the Journal of Computer-Mediated Communication, it contributes to research on AI, media, and gender. For this corpus it grounds analysis of gendered representation in AI discourse.
