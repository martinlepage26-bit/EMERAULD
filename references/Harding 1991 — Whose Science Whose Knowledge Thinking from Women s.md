# Whose Science? Whose Knowledge? Thinking from Women’s Lives

## Citation
Harding, Sandra. 1991. Whose Science? Whose Knowledge? Thinking from Women’s Lives. Ithaca, NY: Cornell University Press.

## Annotation
Sandra Harding's Whose Science? Whose Knowledge? is a foundational work of feminist standpoint theory, arguing that starting inquiry from the lives of marginalized people yields “strong objectivity” — a more, not less, rigorous science because it interrogates the social situatedness of knowledge production. Harding critiques the false neutrality of dominant science. For this corpus it grounds standpoint epistemology and the politics of objectivity — relevant to debates over bias and whose knowledge counts.
