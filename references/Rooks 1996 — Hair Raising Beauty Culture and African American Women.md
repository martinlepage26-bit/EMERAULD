# Hair Raising: Beauty, Culture, and African American Women

## Citation
Rooks, Noliwe M. Hair Raising: Beauty, Culture, and African American Women. Rutgers UP, 1996.

## Annotation
Noliwe Rooks's Hair Raising examines the cultural politics of African American women's hair and beauty, analyzing how hair has been a site of racial and gender meaning, commerce, and identity. It is a key text in Black feminist and cultural studies. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the analysis of race, beauty, and the body.
