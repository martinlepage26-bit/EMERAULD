# Theory Construction in Qualitative Research

## Citation
Timmermans, S., Tavory, I. (2012). Theory Construction in Qualitative Research. Sociological Theory, 30(3), 167-186. https://doi.org/10.1177/0735275112457914

## Annotation
Timmermans and Tavory's “Theory Construction in Qualitative Research” is an influential methodological article arguing that theoretical innovation comes not from pure induction (as classic grounded theory claims) but from “abduction” — the pragmatist logic of forming surprising, testable explanations from anomalous data. Published in Sociological Theory, it reshaped qualitative methodology. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds abductive analysis as a route to novel theory.
