# Autoethnography: An Overview

## Citation
Ellis, C., Adams, T., Bochner, A. (2010). Autoethnography: An Overview. Forum Qualitative Sozialforschung / Forum: Qualitative Social Research, Vol 12, No 1 (2011): The KWALON Experiment: Discussions on Qualitative Data Analysis Software by Developers and Users. https://doi.org/10.17169/fqs-12.1.1589

## Annotation
Ellis, Adams, and Bochner's “Autoethnography: An Overview” is a foundational methodological statement defining autoethnography as research and writing that connects the personal to the cultural, using the researcher's own experience as data. They survey its forms, purposes, and the criteria by which it should be evaluated. Published in Forum: Qualitative Social Research, it is among the most cited references on the method. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds autoethnographic inquiry.
