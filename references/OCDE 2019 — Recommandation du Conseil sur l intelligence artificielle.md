# Recommandation du Conseil sur l’intelligence artificielle

## Citation
OCDE (2019). Recommandation du Conseil sur l’intelligence artificielle. OECD/LEGAL/0449, 21 mai. En ligne: https://legalinstruments.oecd.org/fr/instruments/OECD-LEGAL-0449.

## Annotation
The OECD Council Recommendation on Artificial Intelligence (OECD/LEGAL/0449, 2019) is the first intergovernmental standard on AI, setting out values-based principles for trustworthy AI and recommendations for national policy; it was adopted by OECD members and beyond and shaped the G20 AI Principles. As a foundational instrument, it is a central primary source. For this corpus it anchors the international principles layer of AI governance (French-language record).
