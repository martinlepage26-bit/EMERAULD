# Exclusion, intergroup hostility, and religious fundamentalism

## Citation
Schaafsma, J., Williams, K. (2012). Exclusion, intergroup hostility, and religious fundamentalism. Journal of Experimental Social Psychology, 48(4), 829-837. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jesp.2012.02.015

## Annotation
Schaafsma and Williams experimentally examine links among social exclusion, intergroup hostility, and religious fundamentalism, testing how exclusion affects hostile and fundamentalist responses. Published in the Journal of Experimental Social Psychology, it belongs to the social psychology of exclusion and religion. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized empirical source.
