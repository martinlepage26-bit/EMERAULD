# Gender and power in contemporary

## Citation
Fedele, A. et Knibbe, K. (dir.) (2013). Gender and power in contemporary

## Annotation
Edited by Anna Fedele and Kim Knibbe, Gender and Power in Contemporary Spirituality gathers ethnographic studies of how gender and power operate within alternative and holistic spiritualities. The contributors examine both empowerment and the reproduction of hierarchy in these movements. (The canonical citation is truncated.) For this corpus it grounds gendered analysis of contemporary spiritual practice.
