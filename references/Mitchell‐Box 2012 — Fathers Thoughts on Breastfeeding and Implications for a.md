## Annotation
Mitchell-Box and Braun examine fathers' thoughts on breastfeeding to inform a theory-based intervention, contributing to public-health and nursing research on infant feeding and parental attitudes. Published in JOGNN, it belongs to the maternal-child health literature. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized health-research source.
