# Managing Legitimacy: Strategic and Institutional Approaches.

## Citation
Suchman, Mark C. "Managing Legitimacy: Strategic and Institutional Approaches." Academy of Management Review 20, no. 3 (1995): 571–610.

## Annotation
Mark Suchman's “Managing Legitimacy” is a foundational and much-cited article that synthesizes strategic and institutional approaches to organizational legitimacy, distinguishing pragmatic, moral, and cognitive legitimacy and analyzing how organizations gain, maintain, and repair it. For this corpus it is highly relevant: it provides a rigorous typology of legitimacy directly applicable to how AI-governance institutions and practices seek to be seen as legitimate.
