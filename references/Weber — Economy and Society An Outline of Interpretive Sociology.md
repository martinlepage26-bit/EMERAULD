# Economy and Society: An Outline of Interpretive Sociology

## Citation
Weber, Max. Economy and Society: An Outline of Interpretive Sociology. Eds. Guenther Roth and Claus Wittich. Berkeley: University of California Press, 1922/1968.

## Annotation
Max Weber's Economy and Society is a monumental foundational work of sociology, containing his analyses of social action, the three pure types of legitimate authority (traditional, charismatic, and legal-rational), bureaucracy, class and status, and the sociology of law and religion. It is one of the most influential works in the social sciences. For this corpus it is a canonical anchor for theories of authority, legitimacy, and bureaucratic rationalization — central to governance.
