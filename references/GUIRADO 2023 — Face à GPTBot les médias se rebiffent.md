# Face à GPTBot, les médias se rebiffent

## Citation
GUIRADO, Carole (2023). Face à GPTBot, les médias se rebiffent. La Presse (Agence France-Presse), 30 août. En ligne: https://www.lapresse.ca/affaires/medias/2023-08-30/face-a-gptbot-les-medias-se-rebiffent.php.

## Annotation
This La Presse/AFP article by Carole Guirado reports that news media are pushing back against OpenAI's GPTBot web crawler, blocking it to prevent their content from being scraped for AI training. As a technology-journalism primary source, it documents a concrete conflict over data, consent, and AI training. For this corpus it grounds the contestation between publishers and AI companies over content and scraping.
