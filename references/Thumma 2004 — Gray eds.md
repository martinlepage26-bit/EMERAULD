# Gray, eds

## Citation
Thumma, Scott, and Edward R. Gray, eds. Gay Religion. Walnut Creek: Altamira Press, 2004.

## Annotation
Gay Religion, edited by Thumma and Gray, gathers ethnographic and analytical studies of LGBTQ people's religious and spiritual practices across diverse settings. It is a standard reference in the study of religion and sexuality. (This entry duplicates the same volume in a different citation form.)
