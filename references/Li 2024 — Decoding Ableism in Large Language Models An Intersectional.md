# Decoding Ableism in Large Language Models: An Intersectional Approach

## Citation
Li, Rong, Kamaraj, Ashwini, Ma, Jing, Ebling, Sarah (2024) Decoding Ableism in Large Language Models: An Intersectional Approach. Proceedings of the Third Workshop on NLP for Positive Impact, pp. 232–249. doi: 10.18653/v1/2024.nlp4pi-1.22

## Annotation
Li and colleagues use an intersectional approach to “decode ableism” in large language models, examining how LLM outputs encode disability bias, especially in combination with other axes of identity. Presented at the NLP for Positive Impact workshop, it contributes to fairness research on generative AI. For this corpus it grounds intersectional analysis of ableism in LLMs.
