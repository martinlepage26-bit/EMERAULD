## Annotation
Faith Gordon's book review of Virginia Eubanks's Automating Inequality (in Law, Technology and Humans) summarizes and assesses the book's argument that automated public-service systems profile, police, and punish the poor. As a review, it is a secondary source pointing to a key primary text. For this corpus it registers scholarly reception of Eubanks's influential work on algorithmic harm.
