# Les Disciples à Saïs, et autres fragments

## Citation
Novalis. 1993. Les Disciples à Saïs, et autres fragments. Traduit par Albert Béguin. Paris: Gallimard.

## Annotation
Novalis's Les Disciples à Saïs (The Novices of Sais) is a Romantic philosophical-poetic fragment exploring nature, mysticism, and the unity of the self and the cosmos, emblematic of German Romantic Naturphilosophie. As a literary-philosophical source, it belongs to traditions of nature mysticism and the sacralization of nature. For this corpus it is a primary source in the Romantic imagination of nature — an antecedent of later nature spirituality.
