# The governance of artificial intelligence in Canada: Findings and opportunities from a review of 84 AI governance initiatives

## Citation
Attard-Frost, B., Brandusescu, A., Lyons, K. (2024). The governance of artificial intelligence in Canada: Findings and opportunities from a review of 84 AI governance initiatives. Government Information Quarterly, 41(2), 101929. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.giq.2024.101929

## Annotation
Attard-Frost, Brandusescu, and Lyons review 84 AI-governance initiatives in Canada, mapping the actors, instruments, and gaps in the country's emerging governance landscape. They find fragmentation and uneven coverage across public, private, and civil-society efforts, and identify opportunities for more coherent and accountable governance. Published in Government Information Quarterly, it is an empirically grounded contribution to comparative AI policy. For this corpus it anchors the Canadian case within broader AI-governance debates.
