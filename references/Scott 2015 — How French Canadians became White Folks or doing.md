# How French Canadians became White Folks, or doing things with race in Quebec<sup>†</sup>

## Citation
Scott, C. (2015). How French Canadians became White Folks, or doing things with race in Quebec<sup>†</sup>. Ethnic and Racial Studies, 39(7), 1280-1297. https://doi.org/10.1080/01419870.2015.1103880

## Annotation
Corrie Scott's “How French Canadians Became White Folks” analyzes the construction and “doing” of race in Québec, examining how Frenchness, whiteness, and racial hierarchy have been produced and contested in the province. Published in Ethnic and Racial Studies, it contributes to critical race studies in a Québec context. For this corpus it grounds the analysis of race and whiteness in francophone Canada.
