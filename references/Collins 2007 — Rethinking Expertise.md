# Rethinking Expertise

## Citation
Collins, Harry, and Robert Evans. Rethinking Expertise. Chicago: University of Chicago Press, 2007.

## Annotation
Harry Collins and Robert Evans's Rethinking Expertise proposes a “periodic table of expertises,” distinguishing types of specialist and tacit knowledge and intervening in debates over the role of experts and laypeople in technical decisions. The book is influential in science and technology studies and the sociology of expertise. For this corpus it grounds analysis of expertise, tacit knowledge, and who is authorized to judge technical matters — relevant to AI governance and legitimacy.
