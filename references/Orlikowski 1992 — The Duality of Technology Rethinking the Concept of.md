# The Duality of Technology: Rethinking the Concept of Technology in Organizations

## Citation
Orlikowski, W. (1992). The Duality of Technology: Rethinking the Concept of Technology in Organizations. Organization Science, 3(3), 398-427. https://doi.org/10.1287/orsc.3.3.398

## Annotation
Wanda Orlikowski's “The Duality of Technology” applies structuration theory to technology in organizations, arguing that technology is both shaped by human action and shapes it — a “duality” in which technology is produced and reproduced through use. Published in Organization Science, it is a foundational text in information systems and organizational studies. For this corpus it grounds the mutual constitution of technology and organizational practice — relevant to how AI is enacted in institutions.
