# Les rites de passage : étude systématique des rites de la porte

## Citation
Van Gennep, A. (1909). Les rites de passage : étude systématique des rites de la porte

## Annotation
Arnold van Gennep's Les rites de passage (The Rites of Passage) is the foundational study of transition rituals, introducing the tripartite structure of separation, liminal (transition), and incorporation phases that accompany changes of status. It directly inspired Victor Turner's theory of liminality and shaped the entire field of ritual studies. For this corpus it is a canonical theoretical anchor for understanding ritual structure and transition.
