# Le roi et la sorcière: L’Europe des bûchers, XVe–XVIIIe siècle

## Citation
Muchembled, R., 1994. Le roi et la sorcière: L’Europe des bûchers, XVe–XVIIIe siècle. Paris: Hachette.

## Annotation
Robert Muchembled's Le roi et la sorcière examines the European witch hunts of the fifteenth to eighteenth centuries — “l'Europe des bûchers” — analyzing the social, political, and religious forces behind the persecution. A leading historian of witchcraft and popular culture, Muchembled situates the trials within state- and church-building. For this corpus it grounds the history of European witch persecution.
