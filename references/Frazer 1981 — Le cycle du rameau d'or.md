# Le cycle du rameau d'or

## Citation
Frazer, J. G. (1981). Le cycle du rameau d'or. Paris: Laffont.

## Annotation
This French abridgment of James Frazer's The Golden Bough presents his sweeping comparative study of magic, religion, and myth, famous for the evolutionary schema in which human thought progresses from magic through religion to science, and for the motif of the dying-and-reviving god. Though its methods and evolutionism are now heavily critiqued, the work profoundly shaped twentieth-century literature, anthropology, and the modern imagination of ritual. For this corpus it is a foundational (if contested) reference in the comparative study of magic and myth.
