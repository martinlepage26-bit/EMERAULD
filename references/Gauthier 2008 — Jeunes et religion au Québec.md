# Jeunes et religion au Québec

## Citation
Gauthier, F. et Perreault, J.-P. (dir.) (2008). Jeunes et religion au Québec. Québec :

## Annotation
Edited by François Gauthier and Jean-Philippe Perreault, Jeunes et religion au Québec examines the religious lives of Québec youth in the wake of the Quiet Revolution's rapid secularization, arguing that young francophone Quebecers have inherited a spiritualized, non-institutional religiosity mediated by consumer society rather than being simply “orphans” of the Catholic Church. The volume shows Québec's religious specificity eroding toward Western-European patterns. Published as an edited collection, it is a key reference on youth, religion, and secularization in Québec.
