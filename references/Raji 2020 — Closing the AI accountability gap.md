## Annotation
Raji and colleagues' “Closing the AI Accountability Gap” defines an internal algorithmic-auditing framework to help organizations systematically assess and document AI systems across their lifecycle. It is a key reference in AI-auditing and accountability scholarship. (This entry duplicates the same FAccT 2020 paper in a different citation form.)
