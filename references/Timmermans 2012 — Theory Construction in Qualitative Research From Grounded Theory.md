# Theory Construction in Qualitative Research: From Grounded Theory to Abductive Analysis.

## Citation
Timmermans, Stefan, and Iddo Tavory. 2012. “Theory Construction in Qualitative Research: From Grounded Theory to Abductive Analysis.” Sociological Theory 30 (3): 167–186.

## Annotation
Timmermans and Tavory argue that abduction, drawn from Peirce's pragmatism, rather than induction, is the guiding principle of theoretical innovation in qualitative research, offering an “abductive analysis” alternative to grounded theory. Published in Sociological Theory, it is a key methods reference. (This entry duplicates the same article in a different citation form.)
