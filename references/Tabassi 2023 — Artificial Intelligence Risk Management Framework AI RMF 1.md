# Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)

## Citation
Tabassi, E. (2023). Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0). National Institute of Standards and Technology (U.S.). https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.100-1

## Annotation
This is the NIST Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0), authored under Elham Tabassi, offering a voluntary, rights-preserving framework organized around Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage to help organizations manage AI risks and promote trustworthy AI. It is a central AI-governance instrument. (This entry duplicates the NIST AI RMF listed elsewhere, credited to its lead author.)
