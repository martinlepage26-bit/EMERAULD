# The Fashion System

## Citation
Barthes, Roland. The Fashion System. Translated by Matthew Ward and Richard Howard, University of California Press, 1990.

## Annotation
Roland Barthes's The Fashion System (originally Système de la mode, 1967) applies structuralist semiology to written fashion, analyzing how magazine descriptions of clothing generate meaning and value. Barthes treats fashion as a coded language whose rhetoric transforms garments into signs of the fashionable. The book is a rigorous, if demanding, application of semiotics to a cultural domain. For media- and culture-focused work in this corpus, it exemplifies the structural analysis of a signifying system.
