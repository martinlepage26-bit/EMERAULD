# Why People Obey the Law

## Citation
Tyler, Tom R. Why People Obey the Law. Princeton: Princeton University Press, 1990/2006.

## Annotation
Tom Tyler's Why People Obey the Law is a foundational empirical study showing that people comply with law less from fear of punishment than from a belief in its legitimacy, which in turn depends heavily on “procedural justice” — being treated fairly by authorities. For this corpus it is highly relevant: it grounds why legitimacy and fair process, not just enforcement, secure compliance — directly applicable to the legitimacy of AI-governance regimes.
