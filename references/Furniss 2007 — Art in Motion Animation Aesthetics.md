# Art in Motion: Animation Aesthetics

## Citation
Furniss, M. (2007) Art in Motion: Animation Aesthetics. New Barnet: John Libbey Publishing.

## Annotation
Maureen Furniss's Art in Motion: Animation Aesthetics is a foundational textbook in animation studies, analyzing the aesthetics, techniques, and theory of animation across traditions. It maps a spectrum from mimesis to abstraction and provides tools for critical analysis. It is a standard reference in the field. For media-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the study of animation as an art form.
