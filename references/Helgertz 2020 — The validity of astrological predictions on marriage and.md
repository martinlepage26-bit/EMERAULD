# The validity of astrological predictions on marriage and divorce: a longitudinal analysis of Swedish register data

## Citation
Helgertz, J., Scott, K. (2020). The validity of astrological predictions on marriage and divorce: a longitudinal analysis of Swedish register data. Genus, 76(1). https://doi.org/10.1186/s41118-020-00103-5

## Annotation
Helgertz and Scott empirically test astrological predictions about marriage and divorce using Swedish population-register data, finding no support for astrological theory — a rare large-N test of claims usually too vague or small-sample to evaluate. Published in Genus, it contributes an evidence-based assessment of a popular but unscientific belief system. For this corpus it grounds a critical, data-driven examination of astrology relevant to the study of contemporary esoteric belief.
