# On democratic backsliding

## Citation
Bermeo, N. (2016) ‘On democratic backsliding’, Journal of Democracy, 27(1), pp. 5–19. doi: 10.1353/jod.2016.0012.

## Annotation
Nancy Bermeo's “On Democratic Backsliding” argues that the forms of democratic erosion have shifted since the Cold War: blatant coups and election-day fraud are declining while subtler modes — promissory coups, executive aggrandizement, and strategic electoral manipulation — are rising. Because these newer forms are legitimated through the very institutions democracy promoters prize, they are especially difficult to counter. Published in the Journal of Democracy, it is a widely cited analysis. For this corpus it grounds the study of contemporary threats to democratic governance.
