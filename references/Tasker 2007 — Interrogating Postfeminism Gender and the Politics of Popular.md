# Interrogating Postfeminism : Gender and the Politics of Popular Culture

## Citation
Tasker, Yvonne, and Diane Negra. Interrogating Postfeminism : Gender and the Politics of Popular Culture. Duke UP, 2007.

## Annotation
Edited by Yvonne Tasker and Diane Negra, Interrogating Postfeminism is a foundational collection critically examining postfeminism in popular culture, analyzing how media invoke and neutralize feminism across film, television, and celebrity. It shaped the field of postfeminist media studies. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the critique of postfeminist popular culture.
