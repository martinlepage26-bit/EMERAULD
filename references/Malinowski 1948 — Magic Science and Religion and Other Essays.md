# Magic, Science and Religion and Other Essays

## Citation
Malinowski, B., 1948. Magic, Science and Religion and Other Essays. Boston, MA: Beacon Press.

## Annotation
Bronisław Malinowski's Magic, Science and Religion collects essays advancing his functionalist distinctions among magic, science, and religion, arguing that magic serves to manage anxiety in the face of uncertainty and chance where practical knowledge runs out. It is a foundational text in the anthropology of religion and magic. For this corpus it grounds a classic theory of what magic does socially and psychologically.
