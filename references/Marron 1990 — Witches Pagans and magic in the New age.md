# Witches, Pagans and magic in the New age

## Citation
Marron, K. (1990). Witches, Pagans and magic in the New age. Toronto: Seal Books.

## Annotation
Kevin Marron's Witches, Pagans and Magic in the New Age is a journalistic exploration of contemporary Witchcraft, Paganism, and New Age practice in Canada, documenting practitioners and beliefs for a general readership. As an accessible primary/secondary account, it offers a window into Canadian Paganism of the period. For this corpus it grounds popular documentation of contemporary magical and Pagan movements in Canada.
