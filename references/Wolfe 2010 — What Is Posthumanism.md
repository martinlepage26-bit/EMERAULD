# What Is Posthumanism?

## Citation
Wolfe, Cary (2010) What Is Posthumanism?. Minneapolis: University of Minnesota Press.

## Annotation
Cary Wolfe's What Is Posthumanism? is a foundational theoretical statement defining posthumanism (against transhumanism) through systems theory and deconstruction, arguing for a decentered account of the human that takes seriously animals, embodiment, and the limits of humanist thought. It is central to the posthumanities. For this corpus it grounds a rigorous definition of posthumanism and its stakes.
