## Annotation
NIST Special Publication 800-53 Revision 5, from the Joint Task Force, provides a comprehensive catalog of security and privacy controls for information systems and organizations, widely used as a baseline for cybersecurity and risk management across U.S. government and industry. As an authoritative controls framework, it is a primary governance reference. For this corpus it grounds the security- and privacy-controls dimension of information and AI governance.
