# Certified AI Governance Professional (AIGP): Body of knowledge and exam blueprint, version 1.0.0

## Citation
International Association of Privacy Professionals. (2023). Certified AI Governance Professional (AIGP): Body of knowledge and exam blueprint, version 1.0.0. Portsmouth, NH: IAPP.

## Annotation
The IAPP's formal body of knowledge for the AIGP credential — the closest thing the field has to a consensus statement on what an AI governance professional is expected to know. Useful as evidence of professional-field formation and as a checklist against which to test whether a governance offer covers the recognised domains. Grey literature / certification standard, not scholarship.
