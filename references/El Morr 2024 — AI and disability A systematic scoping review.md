# AI and disability: A systematic scoping review

## Citation
El Morr, Christo, Kundi, Bushra, Mobeen, Fariah, Taleghani, Sarah, El-Lahib, Yahya, Gorman, Rachel (2024) AI and disability: A systematic scoping review. Health informatics journal, 30(3), 14604582241285743. doi: 10.1177/14604582241285743

## Annotation
El Morr and colleagues conduct a systematic scoping review of the relationship between AI and disability, analyzing 45 recent articles and finding that AI research on disability is dominated by a narrow medical model and ableist assumptions, even as AI offers potential benefits. Published in the Health Informatics Journal, it maps a growing but problematic literature. For this corpus it grounds critical analysis of how AI research frames disability and the risks of ableist design.
