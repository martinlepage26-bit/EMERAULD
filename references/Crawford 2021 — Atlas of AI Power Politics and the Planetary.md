# Atlas of AI: Power, Politics, and the Planetary Costs of Artificial Intelligence

## Citation
Crawford, Kate. 2021. Atlas of AI: Power, Politics, and the Planetary Costs of Artificial Intelligence. New Haven, CT: Yale University Press.

## Annotation
Kate Crawford's Atlas of AI is a comprehensive political-economic mapping of AI's material infrastructure, arguing that AI is not an abstract cloud technology but a set of extractive relations — of minerals, labor, data, and energy — that are unevenly distributed and largely ungoverned. Crawford traces these costs from lithium mines to data-labeling labor to planetary energy use. The book is a major reference in critical AI studies. For this corpus it is essential context for any AI-governance argument that would otherwise remain at the level of abstract principle.
