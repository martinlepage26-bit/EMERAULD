# Counterfactual Explanations Without Opening the Black Box: Automated Decisions and the GDPR

## Citation
Wachter, S., Mittelstadt, B., Russell, C. (2017). Counterfactual Explanations Without Opening the Black Box: Automated Decisions and the GDPR. SSRN Electronic Journal. https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.3063289

## Annotation
Wachter, Mittelstadt, and Russell's “Counterfactual Explanations Without Opening the Black Box” proposes counterfactual explanations — statements of what would need to change for an automated decision to differ — as a way to provide meaningful explanations under the GDPR without disclosing a model's inner workings. This SSRN version is a foundational contribution to explainable AI and data-protection law. For this corpus it grounds a legally and practically influential approach to explaining automated decisions.
