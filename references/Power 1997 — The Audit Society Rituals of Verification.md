# The Audit Society: Rituals of Verification

## Citation
Power, M. (1997) The Audit Society: Rituals of Verification. Oxford: Oxford University Press.

## Annotation
Michael Power's The Audit Society is a foundational analysis of the “audit explosion,” arguing that auditing and verification have proliferated as “rituals of verification” that produce comfort and legitimacy but can be decoupled from actual performance, sometimes making organizations less rather than more accountable. For this corpus it is highly relevant: it warns that governance mechanisms (including AI audits and assessments) can become ceremonial rituals rather than substantive accountability.
