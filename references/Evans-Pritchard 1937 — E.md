# E

## Citation
Evans-Pritchard, E. E. Witchcraft, Oracles and Magic Among the Azande. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1937.

## Annotation
E. E. Evans-Pritchard's Witchcraft, Oracles and Magic Among the Azande (1937) is a landmark of social anthropology that shows how Zande beliefs about witchcraft, oracles, and magic form a coherent, internally rational system for explaining misfortune. His analysis reframed debates about rationality, causation, and belief across the social sciences. It is one of the most influential ethnographies ever written. For this corpus it is a canonical theoretical anchor for understanding witchcraft as a logic of explanation.
