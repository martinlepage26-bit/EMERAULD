# La Wicca vivante

## Citation
Cunningham, S. (1998). La Wicca vivante. Montréal : Éditions du Roseau.

## Annotation
This French edition of Scott Cunningham's La Wicca vivante (Wicca: A Guide for the Solitary Practitioner) is a widely used practitioner handbook introducing solitary Wiccan practice, ritual, and ethics to a francophone audience. As a popular insider text, it is a primary source on how Wicca is transmitted and practiced. For this corpus it grounds vernacular Wiccan practice and its francophone dissemination.
