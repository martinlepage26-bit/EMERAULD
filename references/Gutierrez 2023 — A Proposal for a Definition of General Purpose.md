# A Proposal for a Definition of General Purpose Artificial Intelligence Systems

## Citation
Gutierrez, C., Aguirre, A., Uuk, R., Boine, C., et al. (2023). A Proposal for a Definition of General Purpose Artificial Intelligence Systems. Digital Society, 2(3). https://doi.org/10.1007/s44206-023-00068-w

## Annotation
Gutierrez and colleagues propose a definition of “general-purpose AI systems” (GPAIS), arguing that existing proposals — including those informing the EU AI Act — fail to distinguish general-purpose from fixed-purpose systems clearly. They analyze the dimensions of “generality” to sharpen the regulatory category. Published in Digital Society, it is a focused contribution to AI-governance definitional debates. For this corpus it grounds the crucial regulatory problem of defining general-purpose AI.
