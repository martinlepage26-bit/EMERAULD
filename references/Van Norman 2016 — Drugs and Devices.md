## Citation

## Annotation
Gail Van Norman's “Drugs and Devices” examines the regulatory approval processes for pharmaceuticals and medical devices, comparing their evidentiary and oversight requirements. Published in JACC: Basic to Translational Science, it belongs to the medical-regulatory literature. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral, though it bears on how safety-critical products are regulated.
