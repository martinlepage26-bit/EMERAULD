## Citation
Butler, Judith. Trouble dans le genre: Le féminisme et la subversion de l’identité. Paris: La Découverte, 2005. Originally published 1990.

## Annotation
This French edition of Gender Trouble (Trouble dans le genre, La Découverte), originally published in 1990, presents Butler's theory of gender performativity and her critique of identity to a francophone audience. It marks a key moment in the francophone uptake of queer theory. (This entry duplicates Gender Trouble in its French edition.)
