# Recruitment AI Has a Disability Problem: Anticipating and Mitigating Unfair Automated Hiring Decisions

## Citation
Nugent, Selin E., Scott-Parker, Susan (2022) Recruitment AI Has a Disability Problem: Anticipating and Mitigating Unfair Automated Hiring Decisions. The International Handbook of AI and Ethics, pp. 85–96. doi: 10.1007/978-3-031-09823-9_6

## Annotation
Nugent and Scott-Parker's “Recruitment AI Has a Disability Problem” analyzes how automated hiring systems can produce unfair decisions against disabled applicants, and proposes ways to anticipate and mitigate such harms. Published in The International Handbook of AI and Ethics, it contributes to AI-fairness and disability scholarship. For this corpus it grounds concrete analysis of disability bias in recruitment AI and its mitigation.
