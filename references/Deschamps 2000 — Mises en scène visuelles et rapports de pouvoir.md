# Mises en scène visuelles et rapports de pouvoir : le cas des doctorale non publiée)

## Citation
Deschamps, C. (2000). Mises en scène visuelles et rapports de pouvoir : le cas des doctorale non publiée). Université d'Ottawa.

## Annotation
Catherine Deschamps's doctoral ethnography of a Parisian bisexual association analyzes visual self-staging and power relations, comparing communication strategies across Western bisexual organizations and linking them to positions on the scale of social domination. She shows that identity-based bisexuals face both conjunctural and structural difficulties in achieving political visibility through image. It contributes to the sociology of sexuality and minority visibility. For this corpus it grounds the politics of representation and visibility for bisexual communities.
