# PRISMA AI reporting guidelines for systematic reviews and meta-analyses on AI in healthcare

## Citation
Cacciamani, Giovanni E, Chu, Timothy N, Sanford, Daniel I, Abreu, Andre, Duddalwar, Vinay, Oberai, Assad, Kuo, C-C Jay, Liu, Xiaoxuan, Denniston, Alastair K, Vasey, Baptiste, McCulloch, Peter, Wolff, Robert F, Mallett, Sue, Mongan, John, Kahn, Charles E Jr, Sounderajah, Viknesh, Darzi, Ara, Dahm, Philipp, Moons, Karel G M, Topol, Eric, Collins, Gary S, Moher, David, Gill, Inderbir S, Hung, Andrew J (2023) PRISMA AI reporting guidelines for systematic reviews and meta-analyses on AI in healthcare. Nature medicine, 29(1), 14-15. doi: 10.1038/s41591-022-02139-w

## Annotation
Cacciamani and a large international group present the PRISMA-AI reporting guidelines, an extension of the PRISMA standard tailored to systematic reviews and meta-analyses of AI in healthcare. The guidelines aim to improve transparency, completeness, and reproducibility in evidence synthesis on medical AI. Published as a Nature Medicine correspondence, it is a methodological standard-setting document. For this corpus it connects AI evaluation to research-reporting rigor and governance of evidence.
