# Collaboration and Concepts of Authorship.

## Citation
Ede, Lisa, and Andrea Lunsford. "Collaboration and Concepts of Authorship." PMLA 116, no. 2 (2001): 354–369.

## Annotation
Ede and Lunsford's “Collaboration and Concepts of Authorship” challenges the Romantic ideal of the solitary author, drawing on their research into collaborative writing to argue that authorship is often plural and socially distributed. Published in PMLA, it is an influential intervention in composition studies and authorship theory. For this corpus it grounds critical reflection on authorship, collaboration, and the attribution of texts.
