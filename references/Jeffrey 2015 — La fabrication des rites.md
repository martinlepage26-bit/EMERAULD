# La fabrication des rites

## Citation
Jeffrey, D. et Cardita, A. (dir.). (2015). La fabrication des rites. Québec : Presses de

## Annotation
Edited by Denis Jeffrey and Ângelo Cardita, La fabrication des rites examines how rituals are made, invented, and adapted in contemporary contexts, gathering francophone scholars on the creativity and construction of ritual. (The canonical citation is truncated.) It contributes to ritual studies' attention to invented and emergent rites. For this corpus it grounds the study of ritual fabrication and creativity.
