# Le sacré hors religions

## Citation
Champion, F., Nizard, S. et Zawadzki, P. (dir.) (2007). Le sacré hors religions. Paris:

## Annotation
Edited by Françoise Champion, Sophie Nizard, and Paul Zawadzki, Le sacré hors religions examines forms of the sacred that exist outside institutional religion — in politics, ethics, memory, and secular life. The contributors analyze how the sacred migrates and reconstitutes itself beyond confessional boundaries. It is a valuable francophone reference on the sacred and secularity. For this corpus it grounds the study of the sacred beyond religion proper.
