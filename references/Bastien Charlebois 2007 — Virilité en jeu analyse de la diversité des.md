## Annotation
Janik Bastien Charlebois's French-language study analyzes the diversity of attitudes toward masculinity, examining how virility is negotiated and varied rather than monolithic. The work contributes to the francophone sociology of gender and masculinities. (The canonical citation here is truncated mid-title.) For this corpus it addresses the social construction and variability of masculine norms.
