# Le rouge est le noir

## Citation
Houseman, Michel. 2012. Le rouge est le noir. Essais sur le rituel. Toulouse: Presses universitaires du Mirail.

## Annotation
Michael Houseman's Le rouge est le noir gathers his essays on ritual, developing his influential relational and interactional theory in which ritual is understood through the special kinds of relationships it establishes among participants rather than primarily through symbolic meaning. Houseman emphasizes the performative, paradoxical structures that ritual action sets up. It is a significant contribution to the anthropology of ritual. For this corpus it grounds a rigorous relational account of how ritual works.
