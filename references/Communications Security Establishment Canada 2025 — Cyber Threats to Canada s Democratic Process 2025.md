# Cyber Threats to Canada’s Democratic Process: 2025 Update

## Citation
Communications Security Establishment Canada (2025) Cyber Threats to Canada’s Democratic Process: 2025 Update. Ottawa: Government of Canada.

## Annotation
The Communications Security Establishment Canada's Cyber Threats to Canada's Democratic Process: 2025 Update assesses cyber and information threats — including AI-enabled disinformation — to Canadian elections and democratic institutions. As a national-security agency threat assessment, it is a primary source on the intersection of AI, cyber threats, and democratic governance. For this corpus it grounds concerns about AI, disinformation, and threats to democracy.
