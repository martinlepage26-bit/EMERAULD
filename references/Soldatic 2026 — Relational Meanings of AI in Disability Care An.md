# Relational Meanings of AI in Disability Care: An Intersectional, Arts-Based Inquiry

## Citation
Soldatic, Karen, Balram, Rohini, Lee, Mikyung, Santilli, Tommaso, Magee, Liam (2026) Relational Meanings of AI in Disability Care: An Intersectional, Arts-Based Inquiry. Qualitative health research, 36(2-3), 304-316. doi: 10.1177/10497323251401541

## Annotation
Soldatic and colleagues present an intersectional, arts-based inquiry into the relational meanings of AI in disability care, examining how AI is understood and experienced within care relationships. Published in Qualitative Health Research, it centers disabled people's and carers' perspectives on AI. For this corpus it grounds a relational, intersectional account of AI in disability care.
