# Theorising Interventions as Events in Systems

## Citation
Hawe, P., Shiell, A., Riley, T. (2009). Theorising Interventions as Events in Systems. American Journal of Community Psychology, 43(3-4), 267-276. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10464-009-9229-9

## Annotation
Hawe, Shiell, and Riley's “Theorising Interventions as Events in Systems” argues that public-health and social interventions should be understood as events introduced into complex, dynamic systems rather than as fixed, standardized packages, with implications for how fidelity and effectiveness are conceived. Published in the American Journal of Community Psychology, it is influential in complexity-informed evaluation. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral but methodological, bearing on how interventions and change are theorized in systems.
