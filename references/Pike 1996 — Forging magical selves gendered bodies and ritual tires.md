## Citation

## Annotation
Sarah Pike's “Forging Magical Selves” examines gendered bodies and ritual fire at Neo-Pagan festivals, analyzing how participants construct magical selfhood through embodied festival practice. (The canonical citation is truncated.) It is an earlier statement of themes developed in her book Earthly Bodies, Magical Selves. For this corpus it grounds embodied self-making in Pagan festival ritual.
