# Queer spirituality

## Citation
Bardella, C. (2001). Queer spirituality. Social Compass, 48(1), 117-38.

## Annotation
Cristina Bardella's “Queer Spirituality” analyzes the “liberation theology” of the queer — theological efforts to recontextualize the metaphysical dimension of homosexuality. Bardella frames a “Queer Spiritual Discourse” encompassing gay and lesbian re-readings of scripture and of other religious traditions, developed by theologians and intellectuals in recent decades. Published in Social Compass, it contributes to the sociology of religion and queer theology. For this corpus it links queer identity to spiritual and theological reinterpretation.
