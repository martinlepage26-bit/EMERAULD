# The Meanings of Magic

## Citation
Bailey, M. (2006). The Meanings of Magic. Magic, Ritual, and Witchcraft, 1(1), 1-23. https://doi.org/10.1353/mrw.0.0052

## Annotation
Michael Bailey's “The Meanings of Magic,” the inaugural article of the journal Magic, Ritual, and Witchcraft, interrogates “magic” as an analytic category, tracing its shifting and often pejorative definitions across history and scholarship. Bailey argues that the term's instability is itself revealing of the boundary-work by which religion, science, and superstition have been demarcated. The essay is a standard starting point for critical historiography of magic. For this corpus it disciplines the use of the word “magic” and clarifies its scholarly stakes.
