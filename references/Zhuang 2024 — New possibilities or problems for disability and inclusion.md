# New possibilities or problems for disability and inclusion? The case of AI and ADMs across work

## Citation
Zhuang, Kuansong Victor, Goggin, Gerard (2024) New possibilities or problems for disability and inclusion? The case of AI and ADMs across work. Telematics and Informatics, 92, pp. 102156. doi: 10.1016/j.tele.2024.102156

## Annotation
Zhuang and Goggin examine whether AI and automated decision-making systems (ADMs) across the world of work create new possibilities or new problems for disability and inclusion, analyzing risks of exclusion alongside potential benefits. Published in Telematics and Informatics, it contributes to disability and AI studies. For this corpus it grounds the analysis of AI/ADMs, work, and disability inclusion.
