# Standards and Their Stories: How Quantifying, Classifying, and Formalizing Practices Shape Everyday Life

## Citation
Lampland, Martha, and Susan Leigh Star, eds. Standards and Their Stories: How Quantifying, Classifying, and Formalizing Practices Shape Everyday Life. Ithaca: Cornell University Press, 2009.

## Annotation
Edited by Martha Lampland and Susan Leigh Star, Standards and Their Stories collects accessible essays showing how standards — practices of quantifying, classifying, and formalizing — quietly shape everyday life, from blood banks to nursing to seed catalogs. The volume makes visible the political and moral work embedded in mundane standards. It is a key STS reference on infrastructure and classification. For this corpus it grounds the insight that standards (including AI and data standards) organize the world in consequential, often invisible ways.
