# La méthodologie qualitative :postures de recherche et travail

## Citation
Paillé, P. (dir.) (2006a). La méthodologie qualitative :postures de recherche et travail

## Annotation
Edited by Pierre Paillé, La méthodologie qualitative: postures de recherche et travail de terrain gathers francophone contributions on qualitative research stances and fieldwork practice. It is a standard methodological reference in French-language social science. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the varieties of qualitative research posture.
