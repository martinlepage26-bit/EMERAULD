# Datasheets for datasets

## Citation
Gebru, T., Morgenstern, J., Vecchione, B., Vaughan, J. W., Wallach, H., Daumé III, H., & Crawford, K. (2021). Datasheets for datasets. Communications of the ACM, 64(12), 86-92. https://doi.org/10.1145/3458723

## Annotation
The Communications of the ACM version of “Datasheets for Datasets” is the peer-reviewed publication of Gebru and colleagues' proposal to document datasets systematically — provenance, composition, collection, maintenance, and recommended uses — so that governance can begin at the data layer. It reinforces the claim that accountability requires visibility into the evidentiary base of AI systems. For this corpus it is a canonical reference on dataset documentation and data governance.
