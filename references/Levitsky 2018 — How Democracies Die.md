# How Democracies Die

## Citation
Levitsky, S. and Ziblatt, D. (2018) How Democracies Die. New York: Crown.

## Annotation
Levitsky and Ziblatt's How Democracies Die argues that contemporary democracies rarely collapse through coups but erode gradually as elected leaders subvert institutions and violate unwritten norms of mutual toleration and forbearance. Drawing on comparative cases, it offers warning signs of authoritarian drift. It became a widely read reference on democratic backsliding. For this corpus it grounds the analysis of gradual democratic erosion — relevant to information, power, and governance threats.
