# Witchcraft and the reinvention of the self

## Citation
Berger, P., 1998. Witchcraft and the reinvention of the self. Journal of Ritual Studies, 12(2), pp.45–62.

## Annotation
Peter Berger's article in the Journal of Ritual Studies examines how contemporary witchcraft functions as a vehicle for the reinvention of the self, linking ritual practice to identity transformation and self-making. It situates witchcraft within broader debates on modern religiosity and the reflexive self. For this corpus it connects ritual practice to processes of self-construction in contemporary spirituality.
