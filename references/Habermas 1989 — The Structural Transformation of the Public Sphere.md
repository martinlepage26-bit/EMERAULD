# The Structural Transformation of the Public Sphere: An Inquiry into a Category of Bourgeois Society

## Citation
Habermas, Jürgen. *The Structural Transformation of the Public Sphere: An Inquiry into a Category of Bourgeois Society*. Translated by Thomas Burger with the assistance of Frederick Lawrence. Cambridge, MA: MIT Press, 1989. (German original: *Strukturwandel der Öffentlichkeit*, 1962.) Paperback reprint: MIT Press, 1991. ISBN 978-0-262-58108-0.

## Annotation
Habermas’s historical-sociological account of the bourgeois public sphere: its emergence, transformation under commercialized mass media and welfare-state conditions, and stakes for democratic legitimacy.
