# Introduction to Pagan studies

## Citation
Davy, B. J. (2007). Introduction to Pagan studies. Thousand Oaks, CA : Altamira

## Annotation
Barbara Jane Davy's Introduction to Pagan Studies provides a systematic overview of the academic study of contemporary Paganism, covering its worldviews, rituals, and the methods used to study them. It is a standard pedagogical reference in the field. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation form.)
