# Datasheets for Datasets

## Citation
Gebru, T., Morgenstern, J., Vecchione, B., Vaughan, J. W., Wallach, H., Daumé III, H., & Crawford, K. (2018). Datasheets for Datasets. arXiv. https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1803.09010

## Annotation
Gebru and colleagues' “Datasheets for Datasets” proposes that every dataset be accompanied by a datasheet documenting its motivation, composition, collection process, recommended uses, and limitations, to improve transparency and accountability between dataset creators and consumers. This arXiv version is widely cited in AI-governance work. (This entry duplicates the same preprint in a different citation form.)
