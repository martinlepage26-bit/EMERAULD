## Annotation
Tiwari and colleagues examine the impact of artificial intelligence in the workplace and its effects on employees' digital well-being, addressing stress, autonomy, and adaptation as AI reshapes work. Published via EduPub, it contributes to research on AI, labor, and well-being. For this corpus it grounds the workplace and well-being dimension of AI adoption.
