# Witching Culture: Folklore and Neo-Paganism in America

## Citation
Maggliocco, S., 2004. Witching Culture: Folklore and Neo-Paganism in America. Philadelphia, PA: University of Pennsylvania Press.

## Annotation
Sabina Magliocco's Witching Culture is a major ethnography of Neo-Paganism and Witchcraft in America, analyzing the movement through the lens of folklore studies and examining ritual, myth-making, ecstatic experience, and the reclamation of tradition. Magliocco, both scholar and practitioner, offers a nuanced insider-outsider account. It is a foundational text in Pagan studies. For this corpus it grounds the folkloristic study of American Neo-Paganism.
