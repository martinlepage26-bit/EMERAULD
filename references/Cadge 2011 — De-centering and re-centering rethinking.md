# De-centering and re-centering : rethinking

## Citation
Cadge, W., Levitt, P. et Smilde, D. (2011). De-centering and re-centering : rethinking

## Annotation
Cadge, Levitt, and Smilde's article calls for “de-centering and re-centering” the study of religion, urging scholars to move beyond dominant frameworks and geographies and to rethink whose religion and which questions occupy the center. It contributes to the sociology of religion's reflexive and global turn. (The canonical citation is truncated mid-title.) For this corpus it prompts methodological reflection on the framing of religious studies.
