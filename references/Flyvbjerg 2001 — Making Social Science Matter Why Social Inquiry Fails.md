# Making Social Science Matter: Why Social Inquiry Fails and How It Can Succeed Again

## Citation
Flyvbjerg, Bent. Making Social Science Matter: Why Social Inquiry Fails and How It Can Succeed Again. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 2001.

## Annotation
Bent Flyvbjerg's Making Social Science Matter argues that social science should abandon the imitation of natural science and instead cultivate “phronesis” — practical, value-rational, context-dependent wisdom attentive to power. Flyvbjerg contends that social inquiry succeeds when it addresses questions of values and power in concrete cases. It is an influential intervention in social-science methodology and philosophy. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds a phronetic, power-attentive approach to inquiry.
