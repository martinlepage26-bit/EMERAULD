# The language of the Goddess : unearthing the hidden symbols

## Citation
Gimbutas, M. (1989). The language of the Goddess : unearthing the hidden symbols

## Annotation
Marija Gimbutas's The Language of the Goddess catalogues the symbols and imagery she interpreted as evidence of a peaceful, Goddess-centered “Old European” civilization predating patriarchal Indo-European cultures. Though her matristic-prehistory thesis is largely rejected by mainstream archaeology, the book was enormously influential on the feminist Goddess and spirituality movements. For this corpus it is a key (and contested) source in the genealogy of Goddess spirituality, whose influence must itself be analyzed.
