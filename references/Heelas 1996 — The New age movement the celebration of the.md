# The New age movement : the celebration of the self and the

## Citation
Heelas, P. (1996). The New age movement : the celebration of the self and the

## Annotation
Paul Heelas's The New Age Movement is a foundational sociological study defining the New Age through the “celebration of the self” and the sacralization of subjective experience. Heelas maps the movement's beliefs, diversity, and cultural significance. It helped establish the academic study of New Age spirituality. For this corpus it grounds the analysis of self-focused contemporary spirituality.
