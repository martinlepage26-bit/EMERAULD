# The Fashioned Body: Fashion, Dress and Modern Social Theory

## Citation
Entwistle, Joanne. The Fashioned Body: Fashion, Dress and Modern Social Theory. Polity, 2000.

## Annotation
Joanne Entwistle's The Fashioned Body integrates fashion and dress into social theory, arguing that dress is a situated bodily practice through which the social self is presented and the body made culturally meaningful. Drawing on phenomenology and Bourdieu, Entwistle treats fashion as embodied social action rather than mere surface. It is a foundational text in fashion studies and the sociology of the body. For this corpus it grounds the analysis of dress, embodiment, and self-presentation.
