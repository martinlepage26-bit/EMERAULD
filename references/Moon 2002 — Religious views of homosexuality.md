# Religious views of homosexuality

## Citation
Moon, D. (2002). Religious views of homosexuality. Dans D. Richardson et

## Annotation
Dawne Moon's “Religious Views of Homosexuality” surveys how religious traditions and communities have understood and contested homosexuality, mapping the range of theological and social positions. Published in the Handbook of Lesbian and Gay Studies (eds. Richardson and Seidman), it is a reference overview. For this corpus it grounds the intersection of religion and homosexuality.
