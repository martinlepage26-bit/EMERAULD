# Kids Rule!: Nickelodeon and Consumer Citizenship

## Citation
Banet-Weiser, Sarah. Kids Rule!: Nickelodeon and Consumer Citizenship. Duke UP, 2007.

## Annotation
Sarah Banet-Weiser's Kids Rule! examines the children's cable network Nickelodeon as a site where young viewers are addressed simultaneously as citizens and consumers. She shows how the network's rhetoric of empowerment and “kids' rule” blends civic address with brand loyalty and consumer identity. The book is a key text in children's media and consumer-culture studies. For media-studies work in this corpus, it models the analysis of address, empowerment, and commercialization.
