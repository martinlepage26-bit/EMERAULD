# Power: A Radical View

## Citation
Lukes, Steven. Power: A Radical View. London: Macmillan, 1974/2005.

## Annotation
Steven Lukes's Power: A Radical View is a foundational text in political theory that distinguishes three “faces” of power: decision-making power, agenda-setting power, and the third-dimensional power to shape people's very perceptions, preferences, and beliefs so that grievances never arise. Lukes argues the third face is the most insidious. For this corpus it grounds a nuanced analysis of power — including the power to shape wants and frames, directly relevant to media, information, and AI-mediated persuasion.
