# Spiritual but not religious? : Beyond binary choices in the

## Citation
Ammerman, N.T. (2013). Spiritual but not religious? : Beyond binary choices in the

## Annotation
Nancy Ammerman's article interrogates the popular “spiritual but not religious” (SBNR) category, arguing that the binary between spirituality and religion oversimplifies how people actually combine institutional and personal forms of religious life. Drawing on empirical study of everyday religion, she shows that many who claim to be “spiritual not religious” remain entangled with religious institutions and practices. The piece is influential in the sociology of religion's turn toward lived and everyday religion. For research on contemporary spirituality it provides an evidence-based caution against taking the SBNR label at face value.
