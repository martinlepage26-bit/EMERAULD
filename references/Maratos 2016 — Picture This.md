# Picture This

## Citation
Maratos, M., Huynh, L., Tan, J., Lui, J., et al. (2016). Picture This. Qualitative Health Research, 26(8), 1055-1066. https://doi.org/10.1177/1049732316648114

## Annotation
Maratos and colleagues use Photovoice with high-functioning stroke survivors to document their lived experience and identify gaps in community reintegration and rehabilitation services, surfacing themes such as unrecognized disability and emotional impact. Published in Qualitative Health Research, it combines participatory visual method with disability-relevant findings. For method- and disability-focused work in this corpus, it grounds Photovoice as a means of centering disabled experience.
