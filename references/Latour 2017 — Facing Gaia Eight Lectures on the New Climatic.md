# Facing Gaia: Eight Lectures on the New Climatic Regime

## Citation
Latour, Bruno. 2017. Facing Gaia: Eight Lectures on the New Climatic Regime. Cambridge: Polity Press.

## Annotation
Bruno Latour's Facing Gaia presents his lectures on the “new climatic regime,” arguing that the Anthropocene forces a rethinking of politics, science, and the relationship between humans and the Earth understood as Gaia — an active, agential system rather than passive backdrop. It is a major work in the environmental humanities and political ecology. For this corpus it grounds Anthropocene and more-than-human thought in relation to politics and science.
