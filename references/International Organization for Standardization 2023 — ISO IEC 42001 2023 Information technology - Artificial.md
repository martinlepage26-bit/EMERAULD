# ISO/IEC 42001:2023 Information technology - Artificial intelligence - Management system

## Citation
International Organization for Standardization, & International Electrotechnical Commission. (2023). ISO/IEC 42001:2023 Information technology - Artificial intelligence - Management system.

## Annotation
ISO/IEC 42001:2023 is the first international management-system standard for artificial intelligence, specifying requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an AI management system within organizations. It provides an auditable, certifiable framework for responsible AI governance analogous to ISO 9001 for quality. As a foundational governance instrument, it is a central primary source. For this corpus it anchors the organizational-management-system approach to AI governance.
