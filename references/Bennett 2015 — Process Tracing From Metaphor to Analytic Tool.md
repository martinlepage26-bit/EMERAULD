# Process Tracing: From Metaphor to Analytic Tool

## Citation
Bennett, A. and Checkel, J.T. (eds) (2015) Process Tracing: From Metaphor to Analytic Tool. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press.

## Annotation
Edited by Andrew Bennett and Jeffrey Checkel, Process Tracing: From Metaphor to Analytic Tool consolidates process tracing as a rigorous qualitative method for causal inference. The contributors move the method beyond loose metaphor, articulating standards of good practice, tests of evidence, and criteria for evaluating causal-mechanistic claims. It is a standard methodological reference alongside Beach and Pedersen. For method-focused work in this corpus, it codifies best practices for within-case causal analysis.
