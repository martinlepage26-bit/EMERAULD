## Citation

## Annotation
This La Presse opinion piece by Pierre Larouche addresses the regulation of AI, examining “myths, challenges, and avenues,” and offering a legal-scholarly perspective on how AI should (and should not) be governed. As expert commentary, it contributes to the francophone AI-governance debate. For this corpus it grounds critical reflection on the promises and pitfalls of AI regulation.
