# Evidence, Argument, and Persuasion in the Policy Process

## Citation
Majone, Giandomenico. Evidence, Argument, and Persuasion in the Policy Process. New Haven: Yale University Press, 1989.

## Annotation
Giandomenico Majone's Evidence, Argument, and Persuasion in the Policy Process is a foundational work reconceiving policymaking as a process of argument and persuasion rather than mere technical calculation, emphasizing the role of evidence, rhetoric, and deliberation in shaping policy. It is influential in policy analysis and public administration. For this corpus it grounds the view of governance as argumentative and deliberative — relevant to how AI policy is justified and contested.
