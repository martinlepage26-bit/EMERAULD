# Freedom in the World 2025: The Uphill Battle to Safeguard Rights

## Citation
Freedom House (2025) Freedom in the World 2025: The Uphill Battle to Safeguard Rights. Washington, DC: Freedom House.

## Annotation
Freedom House's Freedom in the World 2025 is the latest annual global assessment of political rights and civil liberties, subtitled “The Uphill Battle to Safeguard Rights,” documenting trends in democracy and authoritarianism worldwide. As a widely cited index, it is a primary data source on the state of global freedom. For this corpus it grounds analysis of democratic backsliding and the governance context in which AI and information threats operate.
