# Screen Sorcery: Gender, Magic, and Power in the Age of Streaming

## Citation
Taylor, M.C., 2021. Screen Sorcery: Gender, Magic, and Power in the Age of Streaming. London: Verso.

## Annotation
M. C. Taylor's Screen Sorcery examines gender, magic, and power in witch-themed media in the streaming era, analyzing how contemporary series and films represent witches and what they reveal about gender and cultural anxieties. Published by Verso, it contributes to media and witchcraft-in-popular-culture studies. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the analysis of the witch in streaming media.
