# Wicca: History, Belief, and Community in Modern Pagan Witchcraft

## Citation
Doyle White, Ethan. Wicca: History, Belief, and Community in Modern Pagan Witchcraft. Eastbourne: Sussex Academic Press, 2016.

## Annotation
Ethan Doyle White's Wicca: History, Belief, and Community in Modern Pagan Witchcraft is a scholarly synthesis of the origins, beliefs, ritual practices, and social organization of Wicca, drawing on both historical research and study of the contemporary movement. It is regarded as a reliable and comprehensive academic overview. For this corpus it grounds the study of Wicca as a modern Pagan religion.
