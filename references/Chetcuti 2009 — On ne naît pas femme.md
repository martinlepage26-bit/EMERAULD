# On ne naît pas femme

## Citation
Chetcuti, N. (2009). De « On ne naît pas femme » à« On n'est pas femme ». De

## Annotation
Natacha Chetcuti's article moves from Simone de Beauvoir's “one is not born a woman, one becomes one” to Monique Wittig's “one is not a woman,” interrogating the category of “becoming woman” and the elaboration of sex categories within the social system of normative heterosexuality. By comparing Beauvoir and Wittig, Chetcuti questions the presupposition of sexual difference as a cognitive model in gender and sexuality studies. Published in a francophone venue, it is a rigorous theoretical intervention. For this corpus it deepens the theorization of gender, sex categories, and heteronormativity.
