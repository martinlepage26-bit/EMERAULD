# La religion implicite et son réseau d'études : introduction et

## Citation
Bailey, E. 1. (1996). La religion implicite et son réseau d'études : introduction et

## Annotation
Edward Bailey's article introduces the concept of “implicit religion” and its associated network of study — the idea that religiosity extends beyond formal institutions into secular commitments, rituals, and values that function religiously. Bailey argues for attending to these implicit forms as objects of serious analysis. The piece is a reference point for the implicit-religion research tradition. For studies of contemporary spirituality in this corpus, it broadens the category of the religious beyond confessional boundaries.
