# Homosexualités masculines et religions du monde

## Citation
Hurteau, P. (2010). Homosexualités masculines et religions du monde. Paris

## Annotation
Pierre Hurteau's Homosexualités masculines et religions du monde examines male homosexuality across the world's religious traditions, analyzing how different faiths have understood, condemned, or accommodated same-sex desire. Published by L'Harmattan, it contributes to comparative studies of religion and sexuality. For this corpus it grounds a cross-traditional account of male homosexuality and religion.
