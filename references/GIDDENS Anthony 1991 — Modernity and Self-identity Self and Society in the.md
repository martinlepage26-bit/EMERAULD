# Modernity and Self-identity: Self and Society in the Late Modern Age

## Citation
GIDDENS Anthony (1991), Modernity and Self-identity: Self and Society in the Late Modern Age,

## Annotation
Anthony Giddens's Modernity and Self-Identity examines how the reflexive project of the self is transformed under “late” or “high” modernity, where individuals must continuously construct identity amid abstract systems, risk, and the loss of tradition, seeking “ontological security.” It is a key text in the sociology of modernity and identity. For this corpus it grounds analysis of selfhood, reflexivity, and risk in modern society.
