# Sexuality

## Citation
Nye, R. A. (dir.) (1999). Sexuality. New York: Oxford University Press.

## Annotation
Edited by Robert Nye, Sexuality (an Oxford Reader) gathers key historical and theoretical texts on the history and meaning of sexuality across periods and disciplines. It is a standard teaching anthology in the history of sexuality. For this corpus it grounds the historical study of sexuality.
