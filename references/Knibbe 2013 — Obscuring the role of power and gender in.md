# Obscuring the role of power and gender in contemporary

## Citation
Knibbe, K. (2013). Obscuring the role of power and gender in contemporary

## Annotation
Kim Knibbe's article critiques how scholarship on contemporary spirituality can obscure the role of power and gender, arguing for analyses that foreground these dimensions rather than taking practitioners' egalitarian self-descriptions at face value. (The canonical citation is truncated.) For this corpus it grounds critical attention to power and gender in the study of spirituality.
