# Paganism : a very short introduction

## Citation
Davies, O. (2011). Paganism : a very short introduction. New York

## Annotation
Owen Davies's Paganism: A Very Short Introduction surveys both ancient paganism and its modern revival, distinguishing historical pagan religions from contemporary Neo-Paganism and tracing the connections and discontinuities between them. Concise and authoritative, it is a useful entry point. For this corpus it grounds the historical and conceptual scope of “paganism” across antiquity and the present.
