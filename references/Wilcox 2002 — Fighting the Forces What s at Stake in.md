# Fighting the Forces: What’s at Stake in Buffy the Vampire Slayer

## Citation
Wilcox, Rhonda V., and David Lavery, editors. Fighting the Forces: What’s at Stake in Buffy the Vampire Slayer. Rowman & Littlefield, 2002.

## Annotation
Edited by Rhonda Wilcox and David Lavery, Fighting the Forces is a foundational scholarly collection on Buffy the Vampire Slayer, gathering essays that analyze the show's gender politics, narrative, mythology, and cultural significance. It helped establish “Buffy studies” as a field. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the academic analysis of the Buffyverse.
