# Responsible artificial intelligence governance: A review and research framework

## Citation
Papagiannidis, E., Mikalef, P., & Conboy, K. (2025). Responsible artificial intelligence governance: A review and research framework. Journal of Strategic Information Systems.

## Annotation
Papagiannidis, Mikalef, and Conboy provide a review and research framework for responsible artificial-intelligence governance, synthesizing the literature and identifying dimensions and gaps in how organizations govern AI responsibly. Published in the Journal of Strategic Information Systems, it is a scholarly mapping of the field. For this corpus it grounds an organizational and information-systems perspective on responsible AI governance.
