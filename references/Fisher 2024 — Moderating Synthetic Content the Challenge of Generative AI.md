# Moderating Synthetic Content: the Challenge of Generative AI

## Citation
Fisher, S., Howard, J., Kira, B. (2024). Moderating Synthetic Content: the Challenge of Generative AI. Philosophy &amp; Technology, 37(4). https://doi.org/10.1007/s13347-024-00818-9

## Annotation
Fisher, Howard, and Kira analyze the challenge of moderating synthetic content produced by generative AI — misinformation, propaganda, and non-consensual deepfakes — and argue that platforms should meet this threat through the enforcement of general content rules rather than novel, sui generis synthetic-content policies. Published in Philosophy & Technology, it is a focused contribution to AI-governance and content-moderation debates. For this corpus it grounds platform-governance responses to generative AI.
