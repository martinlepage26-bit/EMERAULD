# Countercultural spiritualists'

## Citation
Bloch, J. (1997). « Countercultural spiritualists' » perceptions of the Goddess.

## Annotation
Jon Bloch's article examines how countercultural spiritualists perceive and construct the Goddess within alternative and New Age spirituality, analyzing the meanings practitioners attach to Goddess imagery and identity. It contributes to the sociology of contemporary alternative religion. For this corpus it grounds the study of Goddess spirituality in practitioners' own perceptions and self-understandings.
