# Les conditions de l'analyse qualitative

## Citation
Paillé, P. (2011). Les conditions de l'analyse qualitative. Réflexions autour de

## Annotation
Pierre Paillé's “Les conditions de l'analyse qualitative” reflects on qualitative data analysis in the age of specialized software, arguing that the essence of qualitative analysis is an interpretive practice that does not require — and can be distorted by — computer tools. Published in a francophone methods venue, it defends a craft understanding of analysis. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds a reflective, non-mechanized approach to qualitative analysis.
