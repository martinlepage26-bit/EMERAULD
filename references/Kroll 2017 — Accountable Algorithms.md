# Accountable Algorithms

## Citation
Kroll, J. A., Huey, J., Barocas, S., Felten, E. W., Reidenberg, J. R., Robinson, D. G., & Yu, H. (2017). Accountable Algorithms. University of Pennsylvania Law Review, 165, 633–705.

## Annotation
Kroll and colleagues' “Accountable Algorithms” is a foundational law-and-technology article arguing that algorithmic accountability requires more than transparency: technical tools (such as cryptographic commitments, zero-knowledge proofs, and procedural regularity) can provide assurances about how automated decisions are made, complementing legal oversight. Published in the University of Pennsylvania Law Review, it bridges computer science and law. For this corpus it grounds mechanisms for making algorithms accountable beyond disclosure.
