# Enchanted feminism : ritual, gender and divinity among the

## Citation
Salomonsen, J. (2002). Enchanted feminism : ritual, gender and divinity among the

## Annotation
Jone Salomonsen's Enchanted Feminism is an ethnographic study of the Reclaiming Witchcraft community in San Francisco, examining how ritual, gender, and conceptions of divinity are worked out in a feminist Pagan tradition. (The canonical citation is truncated.) It is a key work in the study of feminist Paganism. For this corpus it grounds feminist ritual and thealogy in the Reclaiming tradition.
