# Magic: Theories of Magic.

## Citation
Middleton, John. “Magic: Theories of Magic.” In Encyclopedia of Religion, edited by Mircea Eliade. New York: Macmillan and Free Press, 1987.

## Annotation
John Middleton's “Magic: Theories of Magic” entry in the Encyclopedia of Religion surveys the major anthropological and historical theories of magic, from Tylor and Frazer onward, providing an authoritative reference overview. It is a standard starting point for the comparative study of magic. For this corpus it grounds the theoretical landscape of magic as a scholarly category.
