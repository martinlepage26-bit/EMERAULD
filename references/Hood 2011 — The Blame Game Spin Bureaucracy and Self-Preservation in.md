# The Blame Game: Spin, Bureaucracy, and Self-Preservation in Government

## Citation
Hood, C. (2011) The Blame Game: Spin, Bureaucracy, and Self-Preservation in Government. Princeton, NJ: Princeton University Press.

## Annotation
Christopher Hood's The Blame Game analyzes how public officials and organizations engage in “blame avoidance” — through presentational, agency, and policy strategies — often prioritizing self-preservation over effective governance. Hood shows how blame dynamics shape bureaucratic and political behavior. For this corpus it grounds the analysis of accountability, blame, and self-protective institutional behavior — relevant to how organizations respond to AI failures.
