## Annotation
This third volume of the Buffy the Vampire Slayer Watcher's Guide, with contributions from writer Jane Espenson and others, is an official companion documenting the series' episodes, characters, and production. As a franchise paratext, it is a primary source for fan and media studies of Buffy. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it supports analysis of the show's world-building and reception.
