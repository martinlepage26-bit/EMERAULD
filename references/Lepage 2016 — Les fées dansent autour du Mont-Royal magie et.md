## Annotation
Lepage and Gauthier's “Les fées dansent autour du Mont-Royal” examines magic and queer negotiations among Montreal Neo-Pagans, part of the authors' ethnographic study of queer ritual in the city. (The canonical citation is truncated; this is closely related to, or a variant of, their 2016 Anthropologica article.) For this corpus it grounds the study of magic and queer ritual practice in Montreal Paganism.
