# The Age of Surveillance Capitalism: The Fight for a Human Future at the New Frontier of Power

## Citation
Mendoza, C. (2022). The Age of Surveillance Capitalism: The Fight for a Human Future at the New Frontier of Power. Church, Communication and Culture, 7(2), 452-455. https://doi.org/10.1080/23753234.2022.2086891

## Annotation
This is a review (by Mendoza) of Shoshana Zuboff's The Age of Surveillance Capitalism, which argues that a new economic logic — “surveillance capitalism” — claims human experience as free raw material for behavioral data, prediction, and control at a “new frontier of power.” As a review, it points to Zuboff's landmark analysis. For this corpus it registers a central critical account of data extraction and behavioral power — foundational to debates on AI and surveillance.
