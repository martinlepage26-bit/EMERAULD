# Changer de société: Refaire de la sociologie

## Citation
Latour, Bruno. 2006. Changer de société: Refaire de la sociologie. Paris: La Découverte.

## Annotation
Bruno Latour's Changer de société, refaire de la sociologie is the French edition of Reassembling the Social, presenting his reformulation of Actor-Network Theory as a method of “tracing associations” among human and nonhuman actors rather than invoking “the social” as an explanatory force. It is a foundational statement of ANT. For this corpus it grounds a relational, network account of how socio-technical collectives are assembled — directly relevant to AI systems.
