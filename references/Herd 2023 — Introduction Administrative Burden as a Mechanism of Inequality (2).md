# Introduction: Administrative Burden as a Mechanism of Inequality in Policy Implementation

## Citation
Herd, P., Hoynes, H., Michener, J., Moynihan, D. (2023). Introduction: Administrative Burden as a Mechanism of Inequality in Policy Implementation. RSF: The Russell Sage Foundation Journal of the Social Sciences, 9(5), 1-30. https://doi.org/10.7758/RSF.2023.9.5.01

## Annotation
Herd, Hoynes, Michener, and Moynihan's introduction to a special issue analyzes administrative burden as a mechanism of inequality in policy implementation, synthesizing how learning, compliance, and psychological costs are distributed and how they entrench disadvantage. Published in the RSF Journal, it is a key statement of the field. (This entry closely duplicates the companion citation of the same introduction.)
