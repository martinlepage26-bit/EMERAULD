# Belief beyond boundaries : Wicca, ce/tic spirituality and the New

## Citation
Pearson, J. (2002). Belief beyond boundaries : Wicca, ce/tic spirituality and the New

## Annotation
Edited by Joanne Pearson, Belief Beyond Boundaries: Wicca, Celtic Spirituality and the New Age is an Open University text surveying contemporary alternative spiritualities, examining Wicca, Celtic revival, and New Age currents and the porous boundaries among them. It is a useful teaching and reference volume in Pagan and New Age studies. For this corpus it grounds the overlapping landscape of contemporary alternative spirituality.
