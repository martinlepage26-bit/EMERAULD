## Annotation
Helena Blavatsky's Isis Unveiled (originally 1877) is a foundational text of the Theosophical movement, presenting a sweeping esoteric synthesis of ancient wisdom, comparative religion, and occult science as a “master-key” to hidden truths. Though scholarly disputed as history, it was enormously influential on modern occultism, Theosophy, and later New Age and Pagan currents. For this corpus it is a primary source in the genealogy of modern Western esotericism and Goddess spirituality.
