## Annotation
Candi Cann's augmented: Life and Death as a Cyborg explores how digital and biomedical augmentation reshapes human embodiment, identity, and the boundaries of life and death. Published by MIT Press, it engages posthumanist and cyborg themes in relation to mortality and the augmented body.
