# Introduction to Pagan Studies

## Citation
Davy, B., 2007. Introduction to Pagan Studies. Lanham, MD: AltaMira Press.

## Annotation
Barbara Jane Davy's Introduction to Pagan Studies is a textbook overview of contemporary Paganism as a field of study, surveying its history, beliefs, practices, and scholarly approaches. It serves as a teaching reference for the academic study of modern Paganism. For this corpus it grounds Pagan studies as a discipline and orients its key concepts.
