# From Eliza to XiaoIce: Challenges and opportunities with social chatbots

## Citation
Shum, H.-Y., He, X.-D., & Li, D. (2018). From Eliza to XiaoIce: Challenges and opportunities with social chatbots. Frontiers of Information Technology & Electronic Engineering, 19(1), 10-26. https://doi.org/10.1631/FITEE.1700826

## Annotation
Traces conversational systems from Eliza and Parry through to Microsoft's XiaoIce, arguing that social chatbots optimise for emotional connection and session length rather than task completion. Historically valuable: it documents, from inside the industry, the deliberate design of attachment as a product goal — which is the substrate for later work on AI anxiety, parasocial dependence, and companion-model governance. Peer-reviewed.
