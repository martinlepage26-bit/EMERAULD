# The Witch: A History of Fear, from Ancient Times to the Present

## Citation
Hutton, Ronald. 2017. The Witch: A History of Fear, from Ancient Times to the Present. New Haven, CT: Yale University Press.

## Annotation
Ronald Hutton's The Witch: A History of Fear traces beliefs about witches and witchcraft from the ancient world to the present across a global and comparative frame, examining the deep human patterns of fear that underlie witch-figures. It complements his histories of modern Paganism with a longue-durée account of the witch as an object of dread. For this corpus it grounds the long history of witchcraft belief and persecution.
