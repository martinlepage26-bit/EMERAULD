# Racial disparities in automated speech recognition

## Citation
Koenecke, A., Nam, A., Lake, E., Nudell, J., et al. (2020). Racial disparities in automated speech recognition. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 117(14), 7684-7689. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1915768117

## Annotation
Koenecke and colleagues document large racial disparities in five commercial automated speech-recognition systems (Amazon, Apple, Google, IBM, Microsoft), which transcribed speech from Black speakers with roughly twice the error rate of white speakers, attributable largely to unrepresentative training data. Published in PNAS, it is a landmark empirical demonstration of racial bias in widely deployed AI. For this corpus it is a canonical case of how AI systems can systematically underserve marginalized groups.
