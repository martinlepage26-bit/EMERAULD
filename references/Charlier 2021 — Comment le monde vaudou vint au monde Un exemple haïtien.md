# Comment le monde vaudou vint au monde? Un exemple haïtien

## Citation
Charlier, P. (2021). Comment le monde vaudou vint au monde? Un exemple haïtien. Africana Studia, (35), 59-64. https://doi.org/10.21747/0874-2375/afr35a5

## Annotation
Charlier reconstructs a Haitian vodou cosmogony and argues that vodou's creation narratives live in ritual detail — objects, gestures, offerings — rather than in doctrine. Written by a forensic anthropologist and treating vodou as a living syncretic system formed by the Atlantic slave trade, it is a primary-source complement to work on colonial naming and ritual authority. Peer-reviewed, in French.
