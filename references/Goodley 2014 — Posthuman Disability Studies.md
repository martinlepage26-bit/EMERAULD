# Posthuman Disability Studies

## Citation
Goodley, Dan, Lawthom, Rebecca, Runswick-Cole, Katherine (2014) Posthuman Disability Studies. Subjectivity, 7, pp. 342–361.

## Annotation
Goodley, Lawthom, and Runswick-Cole's “Posthuman Disability Studies” brings disability studies into dialogue with posthumanist theory, arguing that disabled embodiment — entangled with technologies, supports, and interdependence — productively troubles the humanist subject. Published in Subjectivity, it is an influential intervention linking the two fields. For this corpus it grounds the intersection of disability and posthumanism.
