# Anthropologie structurale

## Citation
Lévi-Strauss, Claude. Anthropologie structurale. Paris: Plon, 1958.

## Annotation
Claude Lévi-Strauss's Anthropologie structurale gathers his programmatic essays applying structural-linguistic method to kinship and myth, arguing that cultural phenomena are organized by unconscious structural relations. It is a cornerstone of structuralism. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation form.)
