# Culture's Ethical Palette: Cultural Influences in the Moral Machine Era

## Citation
Sun, L. (2024). Culture's Ethical Palette: Cultural Influences in the Moral Machine Era. Journal of Education, Humanities and Social Sciences, 26, 510-515. https://doi.org/10.54097/81t5jr86

## Annotation
Sun's “Culture's Ethical Palette” examines cultural influences on moral judgments in the “Moral Machine” era, addressing how cross-cultural variation shapes the ethics of machine decision-making (as in autonomous-vehicle dilemmas). Published in a humanities-and-social-sciences journal, it contributes to cross-cultural AI ethics. For this corpus it grounds cultural variation in moral expectations for AI.
