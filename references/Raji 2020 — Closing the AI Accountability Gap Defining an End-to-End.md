# Closing the AI Accountability Gap: Defining an End-to-End Framework for Internal Algorithmic Auditing

## Citation
Raji, I. D., Smart, A., White, R. N., Mitchell, M., Gebru, T., Hutchinson, B., Smith-Loud, J., Theron, D., & Barnes, P. (2020). Closing the AI Accountability Gap: Defining an End-to-End Framework for Internal Algorithmic Auditing. In Proceedings of the 2020 Conference on Fairness, Accountability, and Transparency (FAccT). https://doi.org/10.1145/3351095.3372873

## Annotation
Raji and colleagues' “Closing the AI Accountability Gap” proposes SMACTR, an end-to-end framework for internal algorithmic auditing that borrows from aerospace, finance, and other high-reliability industries to give organizations a structured process for assessing AI systems before deployment. Presented at FAccT 2020, it is a foundational contribution to operationalizing AI accountability. For this corpus it grounds concrete auditing practice as an AI-governance mechanism.
