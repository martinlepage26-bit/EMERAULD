# What Is an Author?

## Citation
Foucault, Michel. "What Is an Author?" In Language, Counter-Memory, Practice. Trans. Donald F. Bouchard. Ithaca: Cornell University Press, 1977.

## Annotation
Michel Foucault's essay “What Is an Author?” interrogates the “author function” — the way the author operates as a principle for classifying, grouping, and limiting the proliferation of meaning in discourse, rather than as a simple originating individual. It complements Barthes's “Death of the Author” while historicizing authorship as a discursive construct. For this corpus it grounds critical analysis of authorship, attribution, and the ordering of discourse.
