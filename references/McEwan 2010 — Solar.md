# Solar

## Citation
McEwan, Ian (2010) Solar. Jonathan Cape.

## Annotation
Ian McEwan's Solar is a satirical novel about a dissolute Nobel-laureate physicist who becomes improbably involved in climate-change science and renewable energy, skewering both human self-interest and the politics of climate. As a work of literary fiction, it dramatizes the gap between planetary crisis and human folly. For this corpus it is a primary literary source in the cultural imagination of climate change and the Anthropocene.
