## Annotation
Wu, Duan, and Ni's article surveys the security, privacy, and ethical concerns raised by ChatGPT, cataloguing risks such as data leakage, misuse, and bias. Published in the Journal of Information and Intelligence, it contributes to AI-governance and security scholarship. For this corpus it grounds a systematic overview of generative-AI risks.
