# Artificial agency, consciousness, and the criteria for moral agency: what properties must an artificial agent have to be a moral agent?

## Citation
Himma, K. (2008). Artificial agency, consciousness, and the criteria for moral agency: what properties must an artificial agent have to be a moral agent?. Ethics and Information Technology, 11(1), 19-29. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10676-008-9167-5

## Annotation
Kenneth Himma's article asks what properties an artificial agent must possess to qualify as a moral agent, examining the roles of consciousness, intentionality, and free will in ascriptions of moral agency and responsibility. Published in Ethics and Information Technology, it is an influential contribution to machine ethics. For this corpus it grounds philosophical debate over whether and when AI systems can be moral agents — central to responsibility and accountability in AI.
