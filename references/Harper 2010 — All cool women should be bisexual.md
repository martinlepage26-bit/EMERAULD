## Annotation
Sarah Harper's article, framed by the phrase “All cool women should be bisexual,” examines female bisexual identity and the cultural pressures and meanings surrounding it. It contributes to the sociology of sexuality and bisexuality studies. For work on queer identity in this corpus, it grounds analysis of how female bisexuality is constructed and valued.
