# chemsex

## Citation
Ahmed, A., Weatherburn, P., Reid, D., Hickson, F., et al. (2016). Social norms related to combining drugs and sex (“chemsex”) among gay men in South London. International Journal of Drug Policy, 38, 29-35. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.drugpo.2016.10.007

## Annotation
This public-health study examines social norms around “chemsex” — the use of drugs such as crystal methamphetamine, GHB/GBL, and mephedrone to facilitate or intensify sex — among gay and bisexual men in South London. Drawing on survey data, it situates chemsex within community norms, sexual scripts, and risk environments rather than treating it as individual pathology. The article contributes to sexual-health scholarship on drug use, HIV/STI risk, and gay men's sexual cultures. For work on queer embodiment and sexuality, it offers an empirically grounded account of a distinctive contemporary practice.
