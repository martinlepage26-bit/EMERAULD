# A Community of Witches: Contemporary Neopaganism and Witchcraft in the United States

## Citation
Berger, Helen A. A Community of Witches: Contemporary Neopaganism and Witchcraft in the United States. Columbia: University of South Carolina Press, 1998.

## Annotation
Helen Berger's A Community of Witches examines contemporary neopaganism and Witchcraft in the United States through ethnographic study of covens and practitioners, analyzing how they build community and negotiate their place in modern society. It is a foundational sociological account of American Witchcraft. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation form.)
