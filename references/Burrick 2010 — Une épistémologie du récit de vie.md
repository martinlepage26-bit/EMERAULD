# Une épistémologie du récit de vie

## Citation
Burrick, D. (2010). Une épistémologie du récit de vie. Recherches qualitatives, Hors

## Annotation
Delphine Burrick's article develops an epistemology of the life narrative (récit de vie), reflecting on the kind of knowledge biographical accounts produce in qualitative research and on the status of subjective testimony. Published in Recherches qualitatives, it contributes to francophone methodological debate on narrative inquiry. (This work also appears in the canonical list under a variant author spelling, “Barrick.”) For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the use of life stories as evidence.
