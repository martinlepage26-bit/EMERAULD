# Automated Ableism: An Exploration of Explicit Disability Biases in Sentiment and Toxicity Analysis Models

## Citation
Narayanan Venkit, Pranav, Srinath, Mukund, Wilson, Shomir (2023) Automated Ableism: An Exploration of Explicit Disability Biases in Sentiment and Toxicity Analysis Models. Proceedings of the 3rd Workshop on Trustworthy Natural Language Processing (TrustNLP 2023), pp. 26–34. doi: 10.18653/v1/2023.trustnlp-1.3

## Annotation
Narayanan Venkit, Srinath, and Wilson's “Automated Ableism” empirically explores explicit disability biases in sentiment- and toxicity-analysis models, showing that NLP systems can score disability-related language more negatively or as more “toxic.” Presented at TrustNLP 2023, it contributes to fairness research on language models. For this corpus it grounds evidence of ableism embedded in widely used NLP tools.
