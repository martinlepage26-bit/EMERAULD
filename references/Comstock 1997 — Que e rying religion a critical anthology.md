# Que(e)rying religion : a critical anthology

## Citation
Comstock, G. D. et Henking, S. E. (1997). Que(e)rying religion : a critical anthology.

## Annotation
Edited by Gary David Comstock and Susan Henking, Que(e)rying Religion: A Critical Anthology is a foundational collection that brings queer critique to the study of religion, gathering essays that examine sexuality, faith, and the tensions between LGBTQ life and religious traditions. The volume helped establish queer religious studies as a field. For this corpus it is a key reference at the intersection of queerness and religion.
