# The Archaeology of Knowledge

## Citation
Foucault, Michel. The Archaeology of Knowledge. Trans. A.M. Sheridan Smith. New York: Pantheon, 1969/1972.

## Annotation
Michel Foucault's The Archaeology of Knowledge sets out the methodology underlying his archaeological studies, theorizing “discourse,” the “statement,” and the rules that govern what can be said in a given period, in contrast to histories of ideas centered on authors and continuity. It is a key text in the theory of discourse and method. For this corpus it grounds a discourse-analytic approach to knowledge and its conditions of possibility.
