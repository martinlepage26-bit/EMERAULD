# The ``Anthropocene'' (2000)

## Citation
Crutzen, Paul J., Stoermer, Eugene F., Steffen, Will (2013) The ``Anthropocene'' (2000). The Future of Nature: Documents of Global Change, pp. 483–490. doi: 10.12987/9780300188479-041

## Annotation
This reprint of Crutzen and Stoermer's foundational statement on “the Anthropocene” (with Steffen) marks the proposal that Earth has entered a new geological epoch defined by human impact on planetary systems. The concept has reorganized debates across the sciences and humanities. Appearing in The Future of Nature documentary collection, it is a canonical source for Anthropocene discourse. For this corpus it anchors the Anthropocene concept underpinning posthumanist and ecological thought.
