# Encyclopedia of modern witchcraft and neo

## Citation
Rabinovitch, S. T. et Lewis, J. R. (2002). Encyclopedia of modern witchcraft and neo

## Annotation
Edited by Shelley Rabinovitch and James Lewis, the Encyclopedia of Modern Witchcraft and Neo-Paganism is a comprehensive reference work with entries on the figures, traditions, concepts, and practices of contemporary Witchcraft and Paganism. It is a standard reference in Pagan studies. For this corpus it serves as an encyclopedic anchor for modern Witchcraft and Neo-Paganism.
