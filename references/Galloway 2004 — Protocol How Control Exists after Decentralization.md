# Protocol: How Control Exists after Decentralization

## Citation
Galloway, Alexander R. Protocol: How Control Exists after Decentralization. Cambridge: MIT Press, 2004.

## Annotation
Alexander Galloway's Protocol argues that control on the internet does not disappear with decentralization but is exercised through “protocol” — the technical standards (like TCP/IP and DNS) that govern how networks operate. Galloway shows how protocols are a distinctive, distributed form of power. It is a foundational text in new-media and network theory. For this corpus it grounds the insight that technical standards and protocols are mechanisms of governance and control — directly relevant to AI infrastructure.
