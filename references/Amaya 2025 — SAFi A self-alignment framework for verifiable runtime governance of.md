# SAFi: A self-alignment framework for verifiable runtime governance of large language models

## Citation
Amaya, N. (2025). SAFi: A self-alignment framework for verifiable runtime governance of large language models. Research Square. https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-7675043/v1

## Annotation
Presents SAFi, a runtime governance framework for LLMs built on verifiable self-alignment checks drawn from classical philosophy rather than post-hoc content filtering. Of interest for the control-protocol strand: governance is enforced at inference time as a checkable property, which is the same move as an evidence gate. Preprint; the verifiability claim is the part to pressure-test.
