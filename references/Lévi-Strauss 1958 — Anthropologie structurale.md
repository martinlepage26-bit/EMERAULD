# Anthropologie structurale

## Citation
Lévi-Strauss, C., 1958. Anthropologie structurale. Paris: Plon.

## Annotation
Claude Lévi-Strauss's Anthropologie structurale (Structural Anthropology) is a foundational collection that applies structuralist method — derived from structural linguistics — to kinship, myth, and social organization, seeking the underlying mental structures common to human cultures. It reshaped twentieth-century anthropology and the human sciences. For this corpus it grounds structuralist analysis of myth, symbol, and social structure.
