# Chameleons bake bigger pies and take bigger pieces: Strategic behavioral mimicry facilitates negotiation outcomes

## Citation
Maddux, W., Mullen, E., Galinsky, A. (2008). Chameleons bake bigger pies and take bigger pieces: Strategic behavioral mimicry facilitates negotiation outcomes. Journal of Experimental Social Psychology, 44(2), 461-468. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jesp.2007.02.003

## Annotation
Maddux, Mullen, and Galinsky show experimentally that strategic behavioral mimicry (the “chameleon” effect) in negotiation increases trust and produces better joint and individual outcomes — “bigger pies” and “bigger pieces.” Published in the Journal of Experimental Social Psychology, it belongs to the social psychology of negotiation and mimicry. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized empirical source.
