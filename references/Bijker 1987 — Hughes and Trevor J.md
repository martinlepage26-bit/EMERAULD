# Hughes, and Trevor J

## Citation
Bijker, Wiebe E., Thomas P. Hughes, and Trevor J. Pinch, eds. The Social Construction of Technological Systems. Cambridge: MIT Press, 1987.

## Annotation
Edited by Bijker, Hughes, and Pinch, The Social Construction of Technological Systems is a foundational volume of science and technology studies that launched the SCOT (social construction of technology) program. Its essays argue that technological development is shaped by social choices, interpretive flexibility, and relevant social groups rather than by autonomous technical logic. The book is a canonical STS reference. For this corpus it grounds the claim that technologies — including AI — are socially constructed and politically contingent.
