# Vibrant Matter: A Political Ecology of Things

## Citation
Bennett, Jane. 2010. Vibrant Matter: A Political Ecology of Things. Durham, NC: Duke University Press.

## Annotation
Jane Bennett's Vibrant Matter advances a vital materialism in which matter itself is understood as lively and agentic, redistributing political and ethical agency across human–nonhuman assemblages. Bennett's “political ecology of things” challenges anthropocentric accounts of action and responsibility. It is a cornerstone of new materialism and posthumanist thought.
