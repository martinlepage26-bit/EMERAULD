# On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots

## Citation
Bender, E., Gebru, T., McMillan-Major, A., Shmitchell, S. (2021). On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots. Proceedings of the 2021 ACM Conference on Fairness, Accountability, and Transparency, 610-623. https://doi.org/10.1145/3442188.3445922

## Annotation
Bender, Gebru, McMillan-Major, and Shmitchell's “On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots” is one of the most influential critical interventions on large language models. It argues that ever-larger models risk encoding harm at scale — reproducing biased and toxic patterns from training data, incurring large environmental and financial costs, and concentrating power — while lacking genuine understanding. The paper became a flashpoint in debates over AI ethics and corporate research governance. For this corpus it is a limit case showing what happens when capability scaling outpaces interpretability and accountability.
