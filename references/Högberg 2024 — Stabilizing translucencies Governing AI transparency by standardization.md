# Stabilizing translucencies: Governing AI transparency by standardization

## Citation
Högberg, C. (2024). Stabilizing translucencies: Governing AI transparency by standardization. Big Data & Society.

## Annotation
Charlotte Högberg's “Stabilizing translucencies” analyzes the tension between standardization and transparency in AI governance, arguing that standards seek stability and solidification while transparency is meant to be flexible and see-through, and that black-boxed, fluid AI complicates efforts to standardize transparency. Published in Big Data & Society, it combines conceptual and empirical analysis. For this corpus it grounds a critical account of governing AI transparency through standards.
