# Dreaming the dark : magic, sex and politics

## Citation
Starhawk (1990). Dreaming the dark : magic, sex and politics. London : Unwin

## Annotation
Starhawk's Dreaming the Dark: Magic, Sex and Politics connects feminist witchcraft to political activism, arguing that ritual and “power-from-within” can ground resistance to domination and ecological destruction. A foundational figure in the Reclaiming tradition, Starhawk fuses spirituality and radical politics. For this corpus it is a primary source in feminist, activist Paganism.
