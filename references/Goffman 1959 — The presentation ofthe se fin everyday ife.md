# The presentation ofthe se/fin everyday /ife

## Citation
Goffman, E. (1959). The presentation ofthe se/fin everyday /ife. New York: Anchor

## Annotation
Erving Goffman's The Presentation of Self in Everyday Life is a foundational work of microsociology that analyzes social interaction dramaturgically, treating people as performers who manage impressions through “front stage” and “back stage” behavior. Goffman's account of self-presentation reshaped the study of interaction, identity, and social order. For this corpus it grounds analysis of performance, impression management, and the interactional production of self — relevant to online identity and self-presentation.
