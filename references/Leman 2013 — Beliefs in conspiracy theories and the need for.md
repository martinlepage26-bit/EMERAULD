# Beliefs in conspiracy theories and the need for cognitive closure

## Citation
Leman, P., Cinnirella, M. (2013). Beliefs in conspiracy theories and the need for cognitive closure. Frontiers in Psychology, 4. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2013.00378

## Annotation
Leman and Cinnirella examine the relationship between belief in conspiracy theories and the psychological “need for cognitive closure,” testing whether a desire for definite answers predisposes people to conspiratorial explanation. Published in Frontiers in Psychology, it contributes to the psychology of conspiracy belief. For work touching on misinformation in this corpus, it grounds a cognitive account of conspiracy thinking.
