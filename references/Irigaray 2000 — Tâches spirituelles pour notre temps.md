# Tâches spirituelles pour notre temps

## Citation
Irigaray, L. (2000). Tâches spirituelles pour notre temps. Religiologiques, 21, 17-34.

## Annotation
Luce Irigaray's “Tâches spirituelles pour notre temps” articulates spiritual tasks for the contemporary age, extending her philosophy of sexual difference into a reflection on spirituality, the divine, and becoming. Published in Religiologiques, it contributes to feminist philosophy of religion. For this corpus it connects Irigaray's thought to spirituality and the sacred.
