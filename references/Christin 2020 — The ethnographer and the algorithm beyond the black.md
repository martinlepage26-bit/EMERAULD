# The ethnographer and the algorithm: beyond the black box

## Citation
Christin, A. (2020). The ethnographer and the algorithm: beyond the black box. Theory and Society, 49(5-6), 897-918. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11186-020-09411-3

## Annotation
Angèle Christin's “The ethnographer and the algorithm: beyond the black box” develops a methodological toolkit for studying algorithms ethnographically, arguing that researchers can gain purchase on opaque systems through techniques such as refraction, comparison, and triangulation rather than only demanding transparency. Published in Theory and Society, it is an influential contribution to the sociology of algorithms and critical data studies. For this corpus it grounds methods for empirically investigating algorithmic systems in practice.
