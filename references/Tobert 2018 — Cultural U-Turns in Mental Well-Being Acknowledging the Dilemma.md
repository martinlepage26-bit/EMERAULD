# Cultural U-Turns in Mental Well-Being: Acknowledging the Dilemma

## Citation
Tobert, N. (2018). Cultural U-Turns in Mental Well-Being: Acknowledging the Dilemma. Journal of Humanistic Psychology, 63(2), 173-182. https://doi.org/10.1177/0022167818762916

## Annotation
Natalie Tobert's “Cultural U-Turns in Mental Well-Being” reflects on rising critiques of Western psychiatry and the emergence of alternative frameworks for understanding mental distress and extreme experiences, drawing on cross-cultural perspectives. Published in the Journal of Humanistic Psychology, it contributes to critical and transcultural mental-health scholarship. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral, bearing on cultural framings of well-being.
