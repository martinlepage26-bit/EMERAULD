# A Social History of Truth: Civility and Science in Seventeenth-Century England

## Citation
Shapin, Steven. A Social History of Truth: Civility and Science in Seventeenth-Century England. Chicago: University of Chicago Press, 1994.

## Annotation
Steven Shapin's A Social History of Truth examines how credibility and trust were established in seventeenth-century English science, arguing that the “gentleman's word” and codes of civility underwrote what counted as reliable knowledge. It shows that truth-telling is a social and moral, not merely epistemic, achievement. It is a foundational work in STS. For this corpus it grounds the social construction of credibility and trust — relevant to how authority and reliability are conferred on knowledge claims, including AI outputs.
