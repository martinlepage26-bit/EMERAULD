# Dix-sept auteurs poursuivent OpenAI

## Citation
ITALIE, Hillel (2023). Dix-sept auteurs poursuivent OpenAI. La Presse, 20 septembre. En ligne: https://www.lapresse.ca/arts/litterature/2023-09-20/violation-de-leurs-droits-d-auteur/dix-sept-auteurs-poursuivent-openai.php.

## Annotation
This La Presse article by Hillel Italie reports that seventeen authors (including prominent novelists, via the Authors Guild) sued OpenAI for copyright infringement over the use of their books to train language models. As a news primary source, it documents a major front in the legal contestation over AI training data. For this corpus it grounds the copyright dimension of AI governance and author rights.
