# Munt, and Andrew K

## Citation
Browne, Kath, Sally R. Munt, and Andrew K. T. Yip, eds. Queer Spiritual Spaces: Sexuality and Sacred Places. Farnham: Ashgate, 2010.

## Annotation
Edited by Kath Browne, Sally Munt, and Andrew Yip, Queer Spiritual Spaces gathers studies of how LGBTQ people inhabit, contest, and create sacred places and spiritual practices. The volume examines the spatial and embodied dimensions of queer religion across traditions and sites. It is a significant reference in the study of sexuality and the sacred. For this corpus it grounds the intersection of queerness, spirituality, and place.
