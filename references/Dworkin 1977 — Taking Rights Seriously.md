# Taking Rights Seriously

## Citation
Dworkin, Ronald. Taking Rights Seriously. Cambridge: Harvard University Press, 1977.

## Annotation
Ronald Dworkin's Taking Rights Seriously is a landmark of legal and political philosophy, arguing against legal positivism that law includes principles as well as rules, and that individuals hold rights that function as “trumps” against collective goals. Dworkin defends a rights-based, interpretive conception of law. It is a foundational text in jurisprudence. For this corpus it grounds theories of rights, law, and legitimacy relevant to governance.
