# Material Ecocriticism

## Citation
(2014) Material Ecocriticism. Indiana University Press.

## Annotation
Material Ecocriticism (Indiana University Press, 2014, edited by Serenella Iovino and Serpil Oppermann) is a landmark collection bringing ecocriticism into dialogue with the “material turn,” focusing on the agency of matter, the storied nature of things, and meaning-making in a more-than-human world. (The canonical entry lacks editor names.) For this corpus it grounds material-ecocritical and posthumanist approaches to nature and narrative.
