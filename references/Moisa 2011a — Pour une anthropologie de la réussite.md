# Pour une anthropologie de la réussite

## Citation
Moisa, D. (2011a). Pour une anthropologie de la réussite. Martor, 16, 11-16.

## Annotation
Daniela Moisa's “Pour une anthropologie de la réussite” argues for an anthropology of success, examining how success is culturally defined and pursued. Published in Martor, it contributes to cultural anthropology. Its place in this corpus is more peripheral, but it bears on how cultural values around achievement are constructed.
