# Universal Intelligence: A Definition of Machine Intelligence

## Citation
Legg, S., Hutter, M. (2007). Universal Intelligence: A Definition of Machine Intelligence. Minds and Machines, 17(4), 391-444. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11023-007-9079-x

## Annotation
Legg and Hutter's “Universal Intelligence” proposes a formal, mathematical definition of machine intelligence as an agent's ability to achieve goals across a wide range of environments, synthesizing many informal definitions into a single measure. Published in Minds and Machines, it is a foundational reference in AGI theory. For this corpus it grounds formal attempts to define and measure general intelligence, central to debates about AGI.
