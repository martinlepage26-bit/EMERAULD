# Sexual Dysfunction in Men Who Have Sex With Men

## Citation
Cheng, P. (2022). Sexual Dysfunction in Men Who Have Sex With Men. Sexual Medicine Reviews, 10(1), 130-141. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sxmr.2021.01.002

## Annotation
Cheng's review synthesizes the literature on sexual dysfunction specifically among men who have sex with men, addressing prevalence, correlates, and clinical considerations often overlooked in heteronormative sexual-medicine research. Published in Sexual Medicine Reviews, it belongs to the clinical sexual-health literature. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized medical review on MSM sexual health.
