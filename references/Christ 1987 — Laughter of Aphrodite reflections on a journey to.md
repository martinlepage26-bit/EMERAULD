# Laughter of Aphrodite : reflections on a journey to the Goddess

## Citation
Christ, C. P. (1987). Laughter of Aphrodite : reflections on a journey to the Goddess.

## Annotation
Carol Christ's Laughter of Aphrodite gathers reflections on her journey to Goddess spirituality, articulating a feminist thealogy that affirms the sacredness of women's experience and embodiment against patriarchal religion. Christ is a foundational figure in the feminist Goddess movement. The book blends personal narrative, theology, and feminist critique. For this corpus it is a primary source in the development of Goddess spirituality and feminist thealogy.
