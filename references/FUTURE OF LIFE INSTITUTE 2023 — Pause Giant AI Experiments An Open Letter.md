# Pause Giant AI Experiments: An Open Letter

## Citation
FUTURE OF LIFE INSTITUTE (2023). Pause Giant AI Experiments: An Open Letter. 22 mars. En ligne: https://futureoflife.org/open-letter/pause-giant-ai-experiments/.

## Annotation
The Future of Life Institute's open letter “Pause Giant AI Experiments” (March 2023) called for a six-month moratorium on training AI systems more powerful than GPT-4, citing risks of an uncontrolled race and profound societal impacts; it gathered thousands of signatures, including prominent researchers and technologists. As a widely debated primary source, it crystallized public argument over AI-development governance and precaution. For this corpus it is a key document in the AI-risk and governance debate.
