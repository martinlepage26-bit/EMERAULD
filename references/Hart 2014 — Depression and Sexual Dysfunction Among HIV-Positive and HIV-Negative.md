## Annotation
Hart and colleagues examine depression and sexual dysfunction among HIV-positive and HIV-negative men who have sex with men, testing mediation by antidepressant and recreational-stimulant use. Published in Archives of Sexual Behavior, it belongs to the clinical sexual-health literature. Its relevance to this corpus is peripheral; it is best treated as a specialized medical source.
