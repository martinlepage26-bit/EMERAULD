# Sex, Death and Witchcraft: A Contemporary Pagan Festival

## Citation
Ezzy, D., 2014. Sex, Death and Witchcraft: A Contemporary Pagan Festival. London: Bloomsbury.

## Annotation
Douglas Ezzy's Sex, Death and Witchcraft is an ethnographic study of Faunalia, an intense contemporary Pagan festival, examining how ritual experiences of eros, mortality, and transgression transform participants. Ezzy combines rich description with analysis of embodied, emotional ritual. It is a significant work in Pagan studies and the anthropology of ritual. For this corpus it grounds the study of transformative contemporary Pagan ritual.
