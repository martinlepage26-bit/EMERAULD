# La théorie de la reconnaissance: une esquisse

## Citation
Honneth, A. (2004). La théorie de la reconnaissance: une esquisse. Revue du MAUSS, n o 23(1), 133-136. https://doi.org/10.3917/rdm.023.0133

## Annotation
Axel Honneth's “La théorie de la reconnaissance: une esquisse” offers a concise sketch of his recognition theory, summarizing the three spheres of recognition and the moral grammar of social conflicts. Published in the Revue du MAUSS, it is a compact statement of his framework. For this corpus it grounds the concept of recognition in critical social theory.
