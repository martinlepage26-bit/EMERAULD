# The role of AI anxiety and attitudes toward artificial intelligence in shaping healthcare perceptions among Jordanian children

## Citation
Al-Smadi, S., Al-Smadi, F., Alzayyat, A., & Al-Shawabkeh, J. D. (2025). The role of AI anxiety and attitudes toward artificial intelligence in shaping healthcare perceptions among Jordanian children. The Open Nursing Journal, 19, e18744346417980. https://doi.org/10.2174/0118744346417980250718070018

## Annotation
This cross-sectional study investigates how subcomponents of AI anxiety — learning anxiety, configuration anxiety, job-replacement anxiety, and sociotechnical blindness — shape general attitudes toward artificial intelligence among 400 children in Jordan. By disaggregating AI anxiety into distinct dimensions rather than treating it as a single fear state, the authors show that different components relate differently to attitudes toward AI in healthcare. The study is notable for centering children's psychological responses, an underexamined population. For AI-governance and AI-anxiety research, it strengthens the case that anxiety is multidimensional and can function diagnostically rather than as mere obstruction.
