# Something Old, Something New: ‘The Old Religion’ in the Modern World.

## Citation
Pearce, Megan. “Something Old, Something New: ‘The Old Religion’ in the Modern World.” MA thesis, University of Western Ontario, 2000.

## Annotation
Megan Pearce's thesis examines the modern reinvention of “the old religion” in contemporary Paganism, analyzing claims of continuity with pre-Christian tradition. It contributes to Pagan studies. (This entry duplicates the same thesis in a different citation form.)
