## Annotation
This document from Québec's Conseil de l'innovation presents a collective reflection on the framework for governing AI in the province, focusing on Thematic 1 — the AI governance framework — as part of a broader consultation on responsible AI development and use. As a policy-consultation source, it registers Québec's deliberative approach to AI governance. For this corpus it is a primary francophone reference on subnational AI governance.
