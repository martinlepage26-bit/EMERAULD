# How We Think They Think: Anthropological Approaches to Cognition, Memory, and Literacy

## Citation
Bloch, M., 1998. How We Think They Think: Anthropological Approaches to Cognition, Memory, and Literacy. Boulder, CO: Westview Press.

## Annotation
Maurice Bloch's How We Think They Think gathers essays on anthropological approaches to cognition, memory, and literacy, arguing that much cultural knowledge is non-linguistic, embodied, and practical rather than propositional. Bloch bridges cognitive science and social anthropology to rethink how ideas and traditions are actually transmitted. The book is an influential contribution to cognitive anthropology. For this corpus it grounds an account of ritual knowledge and cultural transmission beyond explicit belief.
