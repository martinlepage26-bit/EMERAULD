# Spiritualities of Life: New Age Romanticism and Consumptive Capitalism

## Citation
Heelas, P., 2008. Spiritualities of Life: New Age Romanticism and Consumptive Capitalism. Oxford: Blackwell.

## Annotation
Paul Heelas's Spiritualities of Life: New Age Romanticism and Consumptive Capitalism analyzes subjective-life spirituality as both a Romantic celebration of inner authenticity and a phenomenon shaped by consumer capitalism. It is a standard sociological account of the holistic milieu. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation form.)
