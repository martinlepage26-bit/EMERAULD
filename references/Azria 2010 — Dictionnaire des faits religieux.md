# Dictionnaire des faits religieux

## Citation
Azria, R. et Hervieu-Léger, D. (dir.) (2010). Dictionnaire des faits religieux. Paris :

## Annotation
Edited by Régine Azria and Danièle Hervieu-Léger, the Dictionnaire des faits religieux is a major French reference work synthesizing contemporary scholarship on religion as social fact across traditions, concepts, and methods. Its entries, written by leading specialists, map the sociology and anthropology of religion for a francophone readership. It is a standard reference for the study of religion in French. For this corpus it serves as an authoritative encyclopedic anchor for concepts in the sociology of religion.
