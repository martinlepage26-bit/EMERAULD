# Process-Tracing Methods

## Citation
Beach, D., Pedersen, R. (2019). Process-Tracing Methods. University of Michigan Press. https://doi.org/10.3998/mpub.10072208

## Annotation
Beach and Pedersen's Process-Tracing Methods provides foundations and guidelines for using process tracing to identify and test causal mechanisms within cases. It codifies the method's variants and its Bayesian logic of evidence, offering practical guidance for design and inference. Published by the University of Michigan Press, it is a core methodological reference. (This entry duplicates the same book in a different citation form.)
