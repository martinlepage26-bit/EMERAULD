# Seeking Guidance from the Spirits: Neo-Shamanic Divination Rituals in Modern Dutch Society

## Citation
Minkjan, H. (2008). Seeking Guidance from the Spirits: Neo-Shamanic Divination Rituals in Modern Dutch Society. Social Compass, 55(1), 54-65. https://doi.org/10.1177/0037768607086498

## Annotation
Hanneke Minkjan's article examines neo-shamanic divination rituals in modern Dutch society, analyzing how practitioners seek guidance from spirits and integrate divinatory practice into contemporary life. Published in Social Compass, it contributes to the study of neo-shamanism and alternative spirituality. For this corpus it grounds the ethnography of contemporary divination and neo-shamanic ritual.
