## Annotation
Alessandro Gribaldo's book review (in Anuac) of Marilyn Strathern's Before and After Gender summarizes and assesses Strathern's posthumously published critique of the sex/gender distinction. As a review, it is a secondary source pointing to Strathern's work. For this corpus it registers scholarly reception of Strathern's intervention on gender categories.
