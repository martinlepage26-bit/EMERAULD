# On Being Included: Racism and Diversity in Institutional Life

## Citation
Ahmed, Sara. On Being Included: Racism and Diversity in Institutional Life. Durham: Duke University Press, 2012.

## Annotation
Sara Ahmed's On Being Included studies the practical work of “diversity” within universities and other institutions, drawing on interviews with diversity practitioners. She argues that institutional commitments to diversity can function as non-performatives — statements that substitute for, rather than produce, real change — and can obscure the persistence of racism. The book develops influential figures such as the institutional “brick wall” and diversity as a form of image management. It is a key text in critical university studies and the sociology of institutions. For governance work it offers a sharp analysis of how formal commitments can perform accountability without enacting it.
