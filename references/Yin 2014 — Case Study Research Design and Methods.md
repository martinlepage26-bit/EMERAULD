# Case Study Research: Design and Methods

## Citation
Yin, Robert K. Case Study Research: Design and Methods. 5th ed. Los Angeles: Sage, 2014.

## Annotation
Robert Yin's Case Study Research: Design and Methods is one of the most widely used methodological texts on case-study research, providing systematic guidance on design, data collection, analysis, and the logic of case-based inference. It is a standard reference across the social sciences. For method-focused work in this corpus, it anchors rigorous case-study methodology.
