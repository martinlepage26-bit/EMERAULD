# The constitution of society : outline of the theory of structuration

## Citation
Giddens, A. (1984). The constitution of society : outline of the theory of structuration. Berkeley et Los Angeles : University of California Press.

## Annotation
Anthony Giddens's The Constitution of Society is the definitive statement of structuration theory, which transcends the agency/structure dualism by treating social structure as both the medium and the outcome of the practices it recursively organizes — the “duality of structure.” It is one of the most influential works in modern social theory. For this corpus it grounds a foundational account of how agency and structure co-produce social order.
