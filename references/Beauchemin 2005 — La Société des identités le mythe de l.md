# La Société des identités: le mythe de l’individu

## Citation
Beauchemin, Jacques. 2005. La Société des identités: le mythe de l’individu. Montréal: Éditions du Boréal.

## Annotation
Jacques Beauchemin's essay on the “society of identities” critiques the myth of the sovereign individual and the fragmentation of collective belonging into particular identities, weighing the consequences for political community and solidarity. The work belongs to Québec debates on identity, citizenship, and the social bond. (This citation lists a variant subtitle and publisher for the same authorial project.) For this corpus it contributes a critical francophone perspective on identity and the common good.
