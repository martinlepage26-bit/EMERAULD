# Witchcraft Myths in American Culture

## Citation
Gibson, Marion. 2012. Witchcraft Myths in American Culture. London: Routledge.

## Annotation
Marion Gibson's Witchcraft Myths in American Culture examines how the figure of the witch has been imagined, retold, and mythologized across American history and popular culture, from Salem to modern media. Gibson traces the cultural work these myths perform. It is a reference in witchcraft and cultural studies. For culture-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the American cultural afterlife of witchcraft.
