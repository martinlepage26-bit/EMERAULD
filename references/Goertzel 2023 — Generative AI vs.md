# Generative AI vs

## Citation
Goertzel, B. (2023). Generative AI vs. AGI: The Cognitive Strengths and Weaknesses of Modern LLMs. arXiv preprint https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.10371

## Annotation
Ben Goertzel's arXiv paper contrasts generative AI (modern large language models) with artificial general intelligence, analyzing the cognitive strengths and weaknesses of LLMs and arguing about what they do and do not achieve relative to general intelligence. As a primary source from a prominent AGI researcher, it contributes to debates over LLM capabilities. For this corpus it grounds the distinction between current generative systems and hypothesized AGI.
