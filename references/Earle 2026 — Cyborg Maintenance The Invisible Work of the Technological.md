# Cyborg Maintenance: The Invisible Work of the Technological Bodymind

## Citation
Earle, Joshua (2026) Cyborg Maintenance: The Invisible Work of the Technological Bodymind. Disability Studies Quarterly, 45(2). doi: 10.18061/dsq.6346

## Annotation
Joshua Earle's article develops the concept of “cyborg maintenance” — the everyday, often invisible work of upkeeping a bodymind that depends on technologies such as prosthetics, wheelchairs, or medications. Focusing on the “everyday cyborg” of disability, Earle argues that this maintenance is fundamentally relational and reveals universal human interdependence. Published in Disability Studies Quarterly, it contributes to disability studies and posthumanism. For this corpus it links cyborg embodiment, disability, and the politics of care and dependence.
