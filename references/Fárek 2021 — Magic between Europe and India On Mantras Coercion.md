# Magic between Europe and India: On Mantras, Coercion of Gods, and the Limits of Current Debates

## Citation
Fárek, M., Horák, P. (2021). Magic between Europe and India: On Mantras, Coercion of Gods, and the Limits of Current Debates. Religions, 12(2), 87. https://doi.org/10.3390/rel12020087

## Annotation
Fárek and Horák's article examines “magic between Europe and India,” analyzing debates over mantras, the coercion of gods, and the categories of spell versus prayer, and critiquing the Christian-theological legacy embedded in Western scholarship on Indian practice. Published in Religions, it contributes to the comparative study of magic and to category-critique in religious studies. For this corpus it disciplines the analytic category of “magic” across cultural traditions.
