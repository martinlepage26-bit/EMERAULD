# Une épistémologie du récit de vie

## Citation
Barrick, D. (2010). Une épistémologie du récit de vie. Recherches qualitatives, Hors

## Annotation
Denis Barrick's French-language article develops an epistemology of the life narrative (récit de vie), examining what kind of knowledge biographical and autobiographical accounts can produce in qualitative research. It reflects on the status of subjective testimony, memory, and narrative as data. Published in Recherches qualitatives, it contributes to francophone methodological debate on narrative inquiry. For method-focused work in this corpus, it grounds the use of life stories as a source of social knowledge.
