# Ritual theory, ritual practice

## Citation
Bell, C. (1992). Ritual theory, ritual practice. New York: Oxford University Press.

## Annotation
Catherine Bell's Ritual Theory, Ritual Practice is a landmark intervention that shifts analysis from “ritual” as a category to “ritualization” as a strategic mode of practice. Bell critiques the thought/action and belief/behavior dichotomies that structure earlier ritual theory and, drawing on Bourdieu and Foucault, treats ritualization as a way of acting that produces distinctions and power relations. The book reshaped the field of ritual studies. For this corpus it is a foundational theoretical reference on how ritual works as practice rather than expression.
