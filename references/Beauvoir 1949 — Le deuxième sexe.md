# Le deuxième sexe

## Citation
Beauvoir, S. de (1949). Le deuxième sexe. Paris: Gallimard.

## Annotation
Simone de Beauvoir's Le deuxième sexe (The Second Sex, 1949) is a founding text of modern feminist philosophy, advancing the thesis that “one is not born, but rather becomes, a woman.” Beauvoir analyzes how woman has been constructed as the Other against a male norm across biology, history, myth, and lived experience. The work is foundational for existentialist feminism and for later theories of gender construction, including Butler's. For this corpus it is the canonical philosophical source on the making of gendered subjectivity.
