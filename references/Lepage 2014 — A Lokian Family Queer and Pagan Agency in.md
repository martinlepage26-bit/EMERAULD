# A Lokian Family: Queer and Pagan Agency in Montreal

## Citation
Lepage, M. (2014). A Lokian Family: Queer and Pagan Agency in Montreal. Pomegranate: The International Journal of Pagan Studies, 15(1-2). https://doi.org/10.1558/pome.v15i1-2.79

## Annotation
Martin Lepage's “A Lokian Family: Queer and Pagan Agency in Montreal” (Pomegranate) analyzes a community of Loki devotees in Montreal, showing how devotion to a gender-fluid trickster deity underwrites queer and Pagan agency and kinship. It is a key article in the author's research on queer Montreal Paganism. (This entry duplicates the same article in a different citation form/year.)
