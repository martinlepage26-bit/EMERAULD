# Social Stratification and Social Inequality: Models of Intersectionality and Identity.

## Citation
Anthias, Floya. “Social Stratification and Social Inequality: Models of Intersectionality and Identity.” In Rethinking Class: Culture, Identities and Lifestyle, edited by Rosemary Crompton, Fiona Devine, Mike Savage, and John Scott, 24–45. London: Palgrave, 2005.

## Annotation
In this chapter from Rethinking Class, Floya Anthias examines models of intersectionality and identity, arguing that class must be understood in articulation with gender, ethnicity, and other social divisions rather than in isolation. She critiques accounts that reduce inequality to a single axis and advances a relational understanding of stratification. The chapter is a substantive contribution to class theory and intersectionality. For studies of inequality and identity it is a useful conceptual reference.
