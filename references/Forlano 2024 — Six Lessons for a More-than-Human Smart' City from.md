# Six Lessons for a More-than-Human `Smart' City from a Disabled Cyborg

## Citation
Forlano, Laura (2024) Six Lessons for a More-than-Human `Smart' City from a Disabled Cyborg. Designing More-than-Human Smart Cities: Beyond Sustainability, Towards Cohabitation, pp. 327–332. doi: 10.1093/9780191980060.003.0018

## Annotation
Laura Forlano offers six lessons for a “more-than-human smart city” drawn from her experience as a disabled cyborg using a smart insulin-pump system, using alert and alarm data to illuminate concepts of cyborg, error, labour, energy, data ownership/privacy, and art. The chapter reframes smart-city discourse around cohabitation and interdependence rather than optimization. For this corpus it links disability, cyborg embodiment, data politics, and urban technology.
