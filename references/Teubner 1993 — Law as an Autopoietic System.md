# Law as an Autopoietic System

## Citation
Teubner, Gunther. Law as an Autopoietic System. Oxford: Blackwell, 1993.

## Annotation
Gunther Teubner's Law as an Autopoietic System applies and extends Luhmann's systems theory to law, analyzing the legal system as a self-producing (autopoietic), self-referential system that constructs its own reality while remaining structurally coupled to its environment. It is a major work in legal theory and sociology of law. For this corpus it grounds a systems-theoretic account of how legal and regulatory systems operate and reproduce — relevant to how governance frameworks process external demands.
