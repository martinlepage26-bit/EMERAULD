## How to Use

- Log decisions when they're made, not after.
- Include rationale - "we chose X because Y" is more useful than "we chose X."
- Mark reversibility. Irreversible decisions deserve more scrutiny before logging.
- Link to the project hub, wiki notes, or people involved.
