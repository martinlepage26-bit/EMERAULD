PHAROS Implementation — Moving Parts

The PHAROS governance suite has three implementation surfaces.

First: CompassAI is the governance engine. It operationalizes the pipeline. The
impl_compassai module holds the 10-stage dispatcher. Compass AI receives inputs
through the Aurora intake path.

Second: AurorAI is the client-facing intake module. The impl_aurorai module
performs pre-admissibility screening and hands structured cases to CompassAI.
AurorA includes the document-upload gate.

Third: govern-ai, the web deployment shell. impl_pharos_govern_ai is the FastAPI
+ Cloudflare wrapper. It is being renamed to pharos-ai. The governance shell
mounts both CompassAI and AurorAI behind a single ingress.

Topology anchor: all three surfaces live inside the topology_theseus recursion
layer; Auryn binding is handled by topology_auryn; the Hopf fibration closure
is topology_hopf.
