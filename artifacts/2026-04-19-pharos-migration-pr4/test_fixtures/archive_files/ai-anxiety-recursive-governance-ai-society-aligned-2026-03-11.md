# From AI Anxiety to Recursive Governance

See also [[Governance and PHAROS MOC]].
Submitted to AI & Society, 2026-03-11.

This manuscript treats AI anxiety as a governance response to weakly inspectable authority.
We introduce **revisability** as the operative concept for deterministic AI governance and
present the recursive governance method architecture grounded in the Theseus → Auryn → Hopf
topology. Evidence discipline is provided throughout.
